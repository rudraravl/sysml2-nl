This model describes a Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) system, a sophisticated medical device designed to non-invasively stimulate specific regions of the brain using powerful, focused magnetic fields. It is used for therapeutic applications, such as treating depression and other neurological conditions.

The system is composed of several key integrated components. At its heart is the **Stimulation Coil**, which is placed on the patient's head and generates the rapid magnetic pulses. This process requires significant power, which is provided by a specialized **Power Supply** capable of delivering high-energy, short-duration electrical discharges. Because generating these fields creates a great deal of heat, a dedicated **Cooling System** circulates coolant through the coil to manage its temperature and allow for sustained operation without overheating.

To ensure the treatment is targeted correctly, a multi-axis robotic **Positioning Arm** precisely holds, orients, and places the stimulation coil over the specific brain region targeted for therapy. The entire operation is managed by a central **Control Unit**, which executes the treatment based on a specific protocol—defining the intensity, frequency, and duration of the magnetic pulses—programmed by a clinician.

Patient safety is paramount, and the system incorporates a dedicated **Safety Monitor**. This component continuously checks critical parameters, such as the coil's temperature and the strength of the magnetic field, and can automatically shut down the system if any predefined safety limits are exceeded.

A typical treatment session involves a clear operational sequence. The system starts in an idle state and first performs a calibration routine to determine the appropriate stimulation intensity for the individual patient. Next, the robotic arm precisely positions the coil. Once positioned and calibrated, the stimulation phase begins. During this active treatment phase, the system works in a coordinated, parallel fashion: the coil generates the magnetic pulses, the cooling system actively manages heat, and the safety monitor continuously oversees the entire process to ensure it remains within safe limits. The session concludes when the treatment protocol is complete, or it is halted immediately if a safety issue is detected.
