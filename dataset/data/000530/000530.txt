This is an Arctic Sun Temperature Management System, a medical device designed for clinical settings like hospitals to precisely control a patient's core body temperature. It is used for therapeutic purposes such as inducing hypothermia, maintaining a specific body temperature (normothermia), or carefully rewarming a patient after events like cardiac arrest, stroke, or traumatic brain injury.

The system works by circulating a temperature-controlled fluid (like water) through a closed loop that includes a set of gel pads applied directly to the patient's skin. This non-invasive method allows for efficient heat transfer to manage the patient's temperature.

The core components work together in a coordinated fluid circuit:
*   A **Fluid Reservoir** holds the cooling fluid, with sensors to ensure the level doesn't drop too low.
*   A **Circulation Pump** actively pushes the fluid from the reservoir through the system. Its speed can be adjusted to control the flow rate.
*   A **Heat Exchanger** is the primary temperature control unit. It can rapidly cool or heat the fluid to a precise setpoint as it passes through.
*   Four **Cooling Pads** are placed on the patient. The temperature-controlled fluid flows through these pads, exchanging heat with the patient's body to either cool them down or warm them up. The fluid then cycles back to the reservoir.

The entire process is managed by a central **Control Module**, which acts as the system's brain. It continuously monitors feedback from two key **Temperature Sensors**—one measuring the patient's core temperature and another measuring the fluid temperature. Based on a target temperature set by a clinician, the module intelligently adjusts the pump's speed and the heat exchanger's output to achieve and maintain the desired therapeutic state.

The system operates through several distinct states, including **standby**, **priming** (to prepare the fluid circuit), and **active** therapy. During an active session, it follows a pre-defined therapy protocol with three phases:
1.  **Induction:** Rapidly bringing the patient's temperature to the target level.
2.  **Maintenance:** Holding the patient's temperature steady at the target for a prolonged period.
3.  **Rewarming:** Slowly and safely returning the patient's temperature to normal.

For safety, the system is equipped with an extensive alarm system that monitors for conditions like temperature deviations, low fluid levels, or pump failure. A **User Interface** allows medical staff to set parameters, monitor progress, and view any alarms. The entire unit is powered by a **Power Supply** that includes a battery backup capable of sustaining operation for at least 30 minutes in case of a power failure.
