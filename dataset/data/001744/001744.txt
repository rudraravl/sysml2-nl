This is a fully automated aquarium system designed to create and maintain a stable and healthy aquatic environment for fish. Its main purpose is to automate all critical life-support functions, minimizing the need for manual intervention.

The system is built around a central tank, which is equipped with a comprehensive life support system. This includes a pump that continuously circulates water out of the tank and through a multi-stage filtration process, consisting of both biological and mechanical filters to remove waste and impurities. The clean water is then returned to the tank.

Environmental conditions are precisely managed by a central control unit. This controller acts as the brain of the system, receiving constant feedback from a suite of sensors that monitor water temperature, pH level, and the overall water level. Based on this data and pre-programmed settings, the controller manages several key components: a heater is switched on or off to maintain the water at a precise target temperature, and an aerator is activated as needed to ensure the water is properly oxygenated.

In addition to water quality, the system also automates daily care routines. A dedicated lighting system is programmed to simulate a natural day-and-night cycle, providing the appropriate spectrum and intensity of light. An automatic feeder dispenses a set amount of food at scheduled times.

The entire system is designed to operate in different modes, including a normal autonomous mode, a maintenance mode that likely allows for manual control during cleaning, and an emergency mode that can be triggered if critical parameters go outside of safe limits.
