This model describes a Forest Fire Detection System (FFDS) designed to identify and report forest fires in a timely manner, allowing authorities to initiate effective counteractions.

The system is being developed with several key objectives. Its primary technical goal is to provide highly reliable fire detection. From a business perspective, the system aims to be affordable for any forest authority and is intended to make its vendor the market leader in this sector.

The FFDS is designed as a product line rather than a single, fixed product. It is based on a core system architecture that can be adapted and configured into different versions or "editions," such as a "Standard Edition," to meet the specific needs and budgets of various customers.
