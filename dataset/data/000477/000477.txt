This is a portable, near-infrared (NIR) vein finder system designed to assist healthcare professionals with venipuncture procedures by providing a real-time visual map of a patient's subcutaneous veins. The device projects this map directly onto the skin, serving as a precise guide for needle insertion.

The system operates on the principle that deoxygenated hemoglobin in venous blood absorbs near-infrared light more strongly than the surrounding tissue. It is composed of several key modules working together to create a seamless, real-time visualization:

*   An **NIR Illumination Module**, consisting of multiple LEDs, emits a safe, low-power beam of near-infrared light (at a wavelength of 850nm) onto the patient's skin.
*   A specialized **NIR Camera Module** captures the light that reflects off the skin. Since veins absorb more light, they appear as darker patterns in the camera's view. This camera operates at 30 frames per second to provide a live video feed.
*   An **Image Processing Unit** acts as the system's brain. It takes the raw, low-contrast images from the camera and applies a series of advanced algorithms to enhance them. It filters out noise, boosts the contrast to make veins distinct, and generates a clean, digital vein map.
*   A **Projection Module** takes this processed vein map and projects it back onto the patient's skin as a visible light overlay, accurately aligned with the actual position of the veins underneath.
*   A rechargeable **Power Management System** provides portable power to all components, allowing the device to run continuously for over three hours on a single charge.
*   A central **System Controller** manages the device's overall operation. It handles user commands like starting and stopping a scan, runs system self-checks upon startup, and manages different operational states.

In practice, the device powers on into a standby mode. When a healthcare professional triggers a scan, the illuminator, camera, processor, and projector all begin working in a continuous, parallel cycle. This provides a live, dynamic overlay that tracks the vein positions even if the patient or the device moves, ensuring high accuracy for the medical procedure. The system also includes safety features to manage optical power output and can enter an error state if a fault is detected.
