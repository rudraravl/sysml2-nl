This model describes the fundamental process of a camera system capturing a photograph. The system's main purpose is to transform a real-world scene into a final, stored picture.

This is accomplished through a specific, two-stage sequence of operations. First, the system performs a "Focus" action. This involves using the camera's optical elements to gather light from the external scene and project a sharp, clear image onto an internal sensor.

Immediately following the focus stage, the system executes a "Shoot" action. This second step takes the focused image from the sensor and captures it, converting the optical information into a final, digital picture that can be stored or displayed. The overall process ensures that a scene is properly focused before the shot is taken.
