This is an integrated ophthalmic examination system designed to automate and ergonomically optimize eye examinations. The system is centered around a motorized "Welner" examination table, which works in coordination with a patient chair and diagnostic instruments.

The core of the system is the Welner table, a platform that features a heavy-duty instrument arm. This arm is designed to hold and precisely position various ophthalmic instruments, such as a phoropter. The table's position is highly adjustable; a linear actuator controls its height, moving it vertically between 0.60 and 1.20 meters, while a separate tilt actuator can angle the instrument arm from -15 to +45 degrees. This allows the clinician to place equipment in the ideal position for different examination procedures and for patients of varying heights.

The system also includes a motorized patient chair that can be raised or lowered to align the patient's eyes perfectly with the instruments mounted on the table's arm. The chair is robust, designed to support patients weighing up to 180 kg.

A key piece of integrated equipment is the phoropter, which is used for refraction tests to determine a patient's eyeglass prescription. It is mounted directly onto the table's instrument arm and can have its spherical and cylindrical lens powers adjusted electronically.

The entire system—the table's height and tilt, the chair's height, and the phoropter's settings—is managed from a central touchscreen control panel. From here, the operator can send commands to each component. The system operates in several states, including an idle mode, a positioning mode for making adjustments, and an active examining mode. For safety, it includes an emergency stop function that can halt all motorized motion instantly. A single power supply unit takes standard wall power and converts it to low-voltage DC to safely run all the system's motors and electronics.
