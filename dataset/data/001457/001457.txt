This model describes a complete humanoid robotic system designed to perform autonomous physical tasks within a defined work zone. The system is managed by a central control center that issues commands and receives status updates.

The core of the system is a 1.7-meter tall, 80-kilogram humanoid robot. This robot is built for both mobility and manipulation. It moves on a wheeled base which also contains a large-capacity rechargeable battery that powers the entire unit. For safety and operational awareness, the robot is designed to operate within a specific area that is monitored by a safety perimeter.

The robot perceives its environment through a high-performance vision system, capable of capturing high-resolution (2048p) video at 60 frames per second. This allows it to detect and precisely locate objects in 3D space. This sensory information is processed by a high-frequency motion controller, which serves as the robot's brain for movement, calculating safe and efficient paths for both the robot's body and its arms.

For physical interaction, the robot is equipped with two highly flexible arms, each featuring seven joints for a wide range of motion. At the end of each arm is a two-fingered gripper that can open, close, and securely hold objects with a controlled amount of force.

A typical task for the robot involves receiving a command from the control center, such as retrieving an object at a specific location. The robot then executes a sequence of actions: it scans the area with its vision system, plans a path with its motion controller, navigates to the target using its mobile base, and then uses one of its arms and grippers to grasp the object. The robot continuously communicates its progress, reporting whether the task was completed successfully or aborted due to an issue. The entire system operates under several modes, including standby, operational, and maintenance, with built-in checks to avoid entering an emergency state and to ensure it always maintains sufficient battery power.
