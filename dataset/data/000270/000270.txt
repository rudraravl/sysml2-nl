This model describes a modular Mining Frigate, a specialized spaceship designed for extracting ore from asteroids. It is a versatile vessel that can be customized for various roles, from pure mining to fleet coordination and self-defense.

The frigate is built around a core system and features numerous slots for configurable modules. For its primary mining function, it can be equipped with one or two powerful mining lasers. Its hauling capacity can be significantly increased by installing up to two optional ore hold expanders in its cargo bay. The ship also carries and controls a small contingent of up to five drones, which can be deployed for support or defense.

For survivability, the frigate relies on a configurable shield module for protection and can be fitted with multiple defense turrets to engage hostile targets. Its operational capabilities are enhanced by a sensor module for targeting and threat detection, a propulsion system for both sub-light and faster-than-light warp travel, and an optional capacitor booster to manage its energy reserves during intense operations. The ship is also designed to act as a command vessel, capable of coordinating a small fleet of other mining ships.

The frigate operates in several distinct states. It can be `Docked` at a space station for unloading ore, resupplying, and refitting its modules. From there, it can undock to enter open space (`InGrid`), where it performs its main functions like mining, navigating, and scanning. For long-distance travel between asteroid belts or stations, the frigate engages its warp drive to enter the `OnWarp` state. The model also defines logistical states where the ship can be `Parked` or even `Packaged` for transport within a station's hangars.
