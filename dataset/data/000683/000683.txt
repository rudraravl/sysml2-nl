This is a medical identification system designed to give emergency responders and medical staff quick, wireless access to a patient's critical health information using RFID technology. The system is composed of two primary components: a personal Medical ID Tag and a handheld Medical Scanner.

The Medical ID Tag is a small, wearable device that securely stores a patient's vital medical data. It contains an RFID chip that handles communication, a secure memory chip for data storage, a small battery for power, and an antenna to transmit and receive signals. The memory is encrypted to protect patient privacy and stores key information such as the patient's ID, blood type, known allergies (like Penicillin or Latex), pre-existing medical conditions (such as Diabetes or Hypertension), current medications, and emergency contact information.

The Medical Scanner is a portable device used by authorized personnel. It is designed to read the tags from a short distance (up to about 15 cm) by sending out a scan request at a specific frequency.

In an emergency, a first responder uses the scanner to read the patient's tag. The tag, which is normally in a power-saving idle state, detects the scanner's signal and activates. It then retrieves the patient's encrypted medical data from its memory and wirelessly transmits it back to the scanner. The scanner receives this information, validates the transmission, and displays the patient's complete medical profile to the user, enabling faster and more informed medical care. The tag also features a low-power mode to conserve energy when the battery level is low.
