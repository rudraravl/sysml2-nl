This is a system model for an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) designed for oceanographic surveys and deep-sea exploration. The vehicle is engineered to operate independently, executing pre-programmed missions without direct human control.

The AUV is comprised of several integrated subsystems housed within a main hull. Power is supplied by a central rechargeable **Battery Pack**, which distributes electricity to all other components. For movement, a **Propulsion System** uses a combination of forward, vertical, and lateral thrusters, allowing the vehicle to precisely control its movement and orientation in three dimensions.

At the heart of its autonomous capability is a **Mission Controller**, which acts as the onboard brain. It manages a mission plan, which consists of a series of waypoints defining a specific path with target depths and speeds. The controller guides the AUV through different operational states: it begins in standby, descends to its operational depth, transits between waypoints, performs survey tasks, and finally ascends to the surface for recovery. It can also execute an emergency ascent if a problem is detected.

To know where it is and where it's going, the AUV relies on a sophisticated **Navigation System**. This system combines data from a suite of sensors, including a GPS receiver for surface positioning, an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) for orientation, a depth sensor, and a Doppler sonar to measure velocity. This allows the vehicle to accurately calculate its position, depth, and heading at all times.

The primary purpose of the AUV is data collection, which is handled by a modular **Sensor Payload**. This payload is equipped with a CTD sensor to measure water conductivity, temperature, and depth (for salinity and pressure data), a high-resolution camera for visual inspection and imaging, and a sonar system for seabed mapping or object detection.

Finally, a dual-mode **Communication System** allows the vehicle to interact with operators. It uses an acoustic modem for low-bandwidth communication while submerged and a satellite transceiver to transmit large volumes of collected data at high speed once it surfaces. The entire system works together to allow the AUV to autonomously navigate a complex underwater route, collect valuable scientific data, and return safely to transmit its findings.
