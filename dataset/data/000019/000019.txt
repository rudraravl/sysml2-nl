This SysML model describes the requirements for a large-scale space mining corporation.  The corporation's core business is the extraction and delivery of ore from space, focusing on potentially dangerous areas such as Low and Null security zones.  The corporation needs to maintain profitability (at least 500 million ISK per operational cycle),  high fleet utilization (at least 80%), and efficient ore extraction (at least 1,000,000 cubic meters per cycle).  Ore must be delivered quickly (within 30 minutes per batch) and stored effectively (minimum 10,000,000 cubic meters storage capacity).

Operational success depends on a fleet of at least 10 mining frigates.  Given the risky environments, a significant portion (at least 30%) of mining operations require security escorts, with a rapid threat response time (under 60 seconds).  The corporation must maintain high operational uptime (at least 85%), successful deployment rates (at least 90%), and control operational costs (under 2 billion ISK per month) while adapting to fluctuating ore market prices (at least 75% adaptability).  The overall success hinges on balancing profitability with efficient resource extraction, secure operations, and responsiveness to market conditions.
