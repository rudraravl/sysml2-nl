This SysML v2 model defines requirements for a Mining Corporation (referred to as "MiningCorporation").  The model is organized into several categories of requirements: Economic and Profitability, Resource Extraction and Logistics, Fleet and Security, and Operational Uptime and Efficiency.  Each requirement specifies a quantitative constraint that the mining corporation must meet.

**Economic and Profitability:**

* **ProfitabilityRequirement (MCRQ01):** The corporation must maintain an average profitability of at least 500 million ISK per operational cycle, considering market value, operational costs, and risk.  This relates to `TotalProfitabilityConcern` and `RiskAdjustedProfitabilityConcern`.
* **FleetUtilizationRequirement (MCRQ02):** The corporation must maintain a fleet utilization rate of at least 80% for operational efficiency. This relates to `FleetDeploymentEfficiencyConcern`.

**Resource Extraction and Logistics:**

* **OreExtractionRequirement (MCRQ03):** The corporation must extract at least 1,000,000 cubic meters of ore per operational cycle. This relates to `OreYieldConcern`.
* **OreDeliveryEfficiencyRequirement (MCRQ04):**  Average ore delivery time per batch must not exceed 30 minutes. This relates to `OreDeliveryEfficiencyConcern`.
* **StorageCapacityRequirement (MCRQ05):** The corporation must maintain a minimum ore storage capacity of 10,000,000 cubic meters. This relates to `ResourceAvailabilityConcern`.

**Fleet and Security:**

* **FleetSizeRequirement (MCRQ06):** The corporation must operate at least 10 mining frigates per operational cycle. This relates to `ScalabilityConcern`.
* **DefenseCapabilityRequirement (MCRQ07):** At least 30% of mining operations in Low Sec and Null Sec must have security escort coverage. This relates to `SecurityConcern`.
* **ThreatResponseTimeRequirement (MCRQ08):** Threat response time in Low Sec and Null Sec must not exceed 60 seconds. This relates to `ThreatNeutralizationConcern`.

**Operational Uptime and Efficiency:**

* **OperationalUptimeRequirement (MCRQ09):** The corporation must maintain an operational uptime of at least 85% per cycle. This relates to `DeploymentEfficiencyConcern`.
* **DeploymentEfficiencyRequirement (MCRQ10):** At least 90% of planned mining deployments must be successfully executed. This relates to `DeploymentEfficiencyConcern`.
* **OperationalCostControlRequirement (MCRQ11):** Operational costs must be minimized (under 2 billion ISK per month) to improve long-term profitability. This relates to `OperationalCostsConcern`.
* **MarketAdaptabilityRequirement (MCRQ12):** The corporation must adapt its operations based on fluctuating ore prices (at least 75% adaptability). This relates to `MarketResponsivenessConcern`.

The model uses a `MiningCorporation` subject for all requirements and includes constraints to define the acceptable ranges or values for each attribute.  The `frame` keyword links each requirement to a named concern, suggesting a traceability mechanism to higher-level concerns or stakeholder needs (defined presumably in the imported packages `Domain`, `ScalarValues`, and `StakeholderConcerns`).  The use of `Real` and `Integer` data types specifies the expected data type for each attribute.
