This model represents a simplified geometric layout of a car, focusing on its basic physical shape and the placement of its engine.

The car's body is represented as a single, large rectangular block measuring 4.8 meters in length, 1.84 meters in width, and 1.35 meters in height. Within this body, there is a single power source: a two-cylinder engine. The engine is specifically positioned towards the rear of the car's main frame.

The engine itself is also modeled with a simplified geometry. It begins as a solid rectangular block of metal. Two identical cylindrical volumes are then subtracted from this block to represent the empty bores where the pistons would be located. This effectively creates a model of a basic engine block with two hollow cylinders running through it.
