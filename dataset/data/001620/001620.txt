This describes an automated vending machine system designed to sell various products without a human cashier. The machine is a self-contained unit that manages the entire sales process from payment to dispensing.

The system is composed of four main physical and logical components that work together. A **Coin Acceptor** handles all monetary transactions; it accepts and validates coins inserted by a user, accumulates the total credit, and dispenses the correct change after a purchase or provides a full refund if a transaction is cancelled. A **Selection Panel** serves as the user interface, allowing a customer to choose a product by entering its specific code. The core of the physical operation is the **Dispensing Mechanism**, which is responsible for both managing the product inventory and physically releasing the selected item to the customer. Finally, a central **Control Module** acts as the brain of the machine, coordinating the other components to ensure a smooth transaction.

A typical vending operation begins with the machine in an idle state. When a customer inserts coins, the machine starts tracking the credit. After the customer makes a selection, the control module validates the transaction by checking two conditions: that the accumulated credit is sufficient for the price of the item, and that the dispensing mechanism has the item in stock.

If both conditions are met, the dispenser releases the product, and the coin acceptor returns any leftover change. If the customer hasn't inserted enough money, the machine waits for more coins. If the selected product is out of stock, the transaction is cancelled, and the system refunds the full amount of money inserted. This ensures that every transaction is complete: the customer either receives their product and correct change, or they get all their money back.
