This model describes a comprehensive bedpan management system for a healthcare facility, designed to handle the entire lifecycle of patient bedpans from storage to sanitation. The system ensures a hygienic, safe, and efficient process for managing waste for bedridden or mobility-impaired patients.

The core of the system is a set of reusable, stainless steel **Bedpan Units**. Each unit consists of an ergonomically contoured basin designed for patient comfort and to prevent spillage, with a capacity of about 2.5 liters. Attached to the basin is a sturdy handle with a textured grip, allowing caregivers to transport it safely and securely. An integrated level sensor monitors the fluid level in the bedpan and can signal when it is nearing capacity.

The broader system includes several key facilities to support the bedpans:

*   A **Sanitation Facility** provides automated cleaning and sterilization. After a bedpan is used and emptied, it is placed into this facility. The system runs a high-temperature, high-pressure wash cycle using sanitizing agents, followed by a disinfection phase (possibly using UV light) to ensure the bedpan is completely sterile for the next use.
*   A dedicated **Storage Rack** is used to keep the clean, sanitized bedpans organized and ready for deployment, ensuring a sufficient inventory of clean units is always available.
*   A central **Waste Disposal** station is designated for emptying the contents of used bedpans before they enter the sanitation cycle.

The system operates in a continuous cycle. A sterile bedpan is retrieved from storage and provided to a patient. After use, the bedpan is considered contaminated. A caregiver transports it to the disposal area, empties it, and then places it in the sanitation facility. Following a successful sanitation and inspection cycle, the bedpan is returned to the sterile state and placed back in the storage rack, ready for the next patient. This closed-loop process is designed to maximize hygiene, prevent cross-contamination, and ensure patient comfort and dignity.
