This is a totally implantable cochlear implant system designed to restore functional hearing in individuals with severe to profound hearing loss, without requiring any external hardware for daily operation. The entire system is housed within a hermetically sealed, biocompatible titanium enclosure designed for long-term implantation within the human body.

The process of restoring hearing begins with a subcutaneous microphone positioned under the skin, which captures ambient sound waves and converts them into an analog electrical signal. This signal is then amplified and filtered by a signal preprocessor before an analog-to-digital converter transforms it into a digital format.

A central digital signal processor, acting as the brain of the implant, analyzes this digital audio. It breaks the sound down into different frequency channels, mimicking the function of a healthy cochlea. Based on this analysis, a stimulus generator creates precise patterns of electrical pulses. These pulses are delivered directly to the auditory nerve via a flexible electrode array, a delicate component containing 22 individual electrodes that is surgically inserted into the cochlea. This electrical stimulation bypasses the damaged parts of the ear, allowing the brain to perceive the signals as sound.

The entire implant is powered by an internal rechargeable battery. To maintain its "totally implantable" nature, the system includes a wireless charging coil that allows the battery to be recharged non-invasively through the skin, eliminating the need for surgical battery replacements and ensuring the system can operate continuously.
