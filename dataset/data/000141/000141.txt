This model represents a vehicle health and maintenance monitoring system designed to track the vehicle's operational status and manage its service schedule. The system is composed of the vehicle itself and an associated vehicle controller.

The vehicle is equipped with a temperature sensor and has predefined properties, including a maximum safe operating temperature, a specific time for its next scheduled maintenance, and a regular interval for future services (e.g., every 6 months).

The system operates in three main states:

1.  **Normal:** This is the standard operating condition where the system continuously monitors temperature.
2.  **Degraded:** If the vehicle's temperature exceeds the maximum safe limit, the system sends an "Over-Temperature" alert to the vehicle controller and enters this state. It will automatically return to the "normal" state once the temperature drops back to a safe level.
3.  **Maintenance:** The system automatically transitions to this state when the scheduled maintenance time is reached. Upon entering this state, it immediately calculates and sets the date for the next service appointment based on its pre-set maintenance interval. The system is designed to return to the "normal" state after a fixed period of 48 hours, which is the assumed duration for the maintenance work.
