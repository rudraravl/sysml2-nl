This model describes a digital thermometer system designed for continuous and accurate environmental temperature monitoring.

The system is composed of four primary electronic components that work together to measure and display temperature. It includes a dedicated **Temperature Sensor** capable of operating in a wide range from -40°C to +100°C. This sensor detects the ambient temperature and generates a raw electronic signal.

This raw signal is passed to a **SignalProcessor**, which acts as the system's brain. The processor applies stored calibration data, including offset and scale adjustments, to convert the raw signal into a precise temperature value. This calibration capability allows the thermometer's accuracy to be fine-tuned over time.

The final, corrected temperature reading is then sent to a **Display** unit, which presents the information to the user. The display is configured to update at least once per second, providing a real-time temperature readout. The entire system is powered by a low-voltage **Power Supply**.

Operationally, the thermometer has several states. When turned on, it enters a continuous measuring loop of sensing, processing, and displaying the temperature. It can also enter a dedicated calibration mode to adjust its accuracy and returns to an idle state when powered off. The system is engineered to meet specific performance requirements, including a measurement accuracy of ±0.5 degrees and a fast response time.
