This model describes an automated system for interpreting clinical 12-lead electrocardiograms (ECGs). Its purpose is to rapidly and accurately analyze a patient's heart rhythm and detect cardiac abnormalities, generating a comprehensive report to aid clinicians in making diagnoses.

The system is composed of several specialized modules that work in a sequential, analytical pipeline:

1.  **Acquisition & Processing:** It begins by acquiring the raw 12-lead electrical signals from the patient. This raw data is immediately sent to a signal processing module that filters out noise and interference (like power line hum or baseline wander) to produce a clean, analyzable waveform. The system also validates the signal quality, checking metrics like the signal-to-noise ratio. If the signal is too poor, it will flag the need for reacquisition.

2.  **Feature Extraction:** Once a quality signal is confirmed, a feature extraction module meticulously measures key clinical parameters from the waveform. This includes calculating the heart rate, the duration of various intervals (PR, QRS, QT), the amplitude and shape of different waves, and the electrical axis of the heart.

3.  **AI-Powered Analysis:** The extracted features and clean signal are then fed into the core analytical components. A **Rhythm Analysis Module**, which uses a deep convolutional neural network, classifies the patient's overall heart rhythm, identifying conditions like normal sinus rhythm, atrial fibrillation, or life-threatening arrhythmias such as ventricular tachycardia. In parallel, an **Abnormality Detection Module** scans for specific waveform anomalies that could indicate conditions like a heart attack (ST elevation), an enlarged heart, or electrical conduction problems (bundle branch blocks).

4.  **Diagnosis & Reporting:** A central **Diagnostic Engine** synthesizes the findings from the rhythm and abnormality modules. It combines this information with the measured features to generate a final diagnosis, complete with a confidence score and an urgency level. Finally, a **Report Generation Module** compiles all this information—the diagnosis, a summary of the measured features, and clinical recommendations—into a structured report. This report is designed to be integrated into electronic health records and explicitly flags whether a physician's review is mandatory, especially for critical findings.

Overall, this is a clinical decision-support system designed to be fast (providing a full interpretation in under 15 seconds) and highly accurate, particularly for life-threatening conditions. It acts as an intelligent assistant to cardiologists and emergency room physicians, providing consistent, data-driven analysis to improve the speed and quality of cardiac care.
