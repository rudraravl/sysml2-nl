This model describes an autonomous entertainment robot system, designed to perform for and interact with an audience. The complete system consists of the mobile robot itself and a dedicated charging station.

The core of the system is the robot, which is built with several integrated subsystems. A motion system allows it to move and perform choreographed dances, while an audio system enables it to play music, tell stories, and deliver comedy routines. The robot is equipped with a sensor system that can detect if an audience is present and even gauge their level of engagement, which helps it decide when to begin a performance.

A critical feature is its autonomous power management. The robot is powered by an internal rechargeable battery and continuously monitors its own charge level. When the battery runs low, it will automatically stop its current activity, locate and navigate to its charging station, and begin recharging without human intervention.

The robot operates through several distinct states. It can be 'idle' while waiting for an audience, 'performing' when actively entertaining, 'interacting' with people, and 'charging' to manage its power. It also includes an 'error' state for self-diagnostics if a problem occurs. The entire system is designed to operate within defined safety constraints, with limits on its maximum movement speed and sound volume.
