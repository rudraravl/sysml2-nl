This is a forest fire detection system (FFDS) designed to provide timely and reliable detection of forest fires, aiming for market leadership through affordability and effectiveness.  The system's core functionality remains consistent across different configurations, focusing on early fire detection to enable prompt countermeasures.  Different editions or configurations of the FFDS are planned, with at least one "Standard Edition" already defined.  The overarching goal is to make the system accessible and effective for forest authorities of all sizes, ensuring rapid detection of any forest fire to facilitate timely intervention and minimize damage.
