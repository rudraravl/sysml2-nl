This is a mining frigate, a spacecraft designed for efficient asteroid mining operations in various levels of security space (High Sec, Low Sec, Null Sec, and Wormholes).  The frigate's primary function is to extract ore from asteroids, transporting it to a docking station for offloading.  It’s designed for both individual operation and fleet coordination, maximizing ore yield and profitability.

Key features include a high ore extraction rate, varying depending on security status (significantly higher in less secure zones).  It boasts a substantial cargo capacity, expandable to accommodate larger hauls.  The frigate incorporates robust shielding to withstand attacks, offering greater protection in more dangerous environments.  To improve both mining and defensive capabilities, the frigate can deploy at least five drones for various tasks.  It also possesses a long-range threat detection system, allowing for early warnings of potential attacks.  For efficient travel between mining sites and docking stations, it features high warp speed and a quick alignment time.  The frigate can simultaneously target and mine up to three asteroids at once to maximize productivity. Docking and offloading at stations or POS (Player Owned Stations) is designed to be swift (under a minute).  Fleet commanders can coordinate fleets of up to ten of these mining frigates for large-scale operations.  While not directly a part of the frigate itself, the model also highlights the role of a Rorqual support vessel, capable of compressing mined ore to reduce its volume by a factor of ten for more efficient transport.
