This SysML model defines requirements for a Mining Frigate and related aspects of fleet operation.  The model is organized within a package named `MiningFrigateRequirementsDef`, importing necessary elements from other packages (`Domain`, `ScalarValues`, `StakeholderConcerns`, `MiningFrigate`).

The core of the model consists of several requirement definitions, each specifying a constraint on the Mining Frigate's capabilities or the broader operational context:

* **MFRQ01 (Ore Extraction Efficiency):** The Mining Frigate must extract ore at a minimum rate of 20 m³/minute in High Sec and 50 m³/minute in less secure environments (Low Sec, Null Sec, Wormholes). This requirement addresses the `OreYieldConcern`.

* **MFRQ02 (Cargo Capacity):** The Mining Frigate needs a minimum cargo capacity of 5000 m³, expandable through modules. This impacts `TotalProfitabilityConcern`.

* **MFRQ03 (Survivability):**  The Mining Frigate must withstand at least 200 DPS in High Sec and 400 DPS in less secure environments. This addresses both `SecurityConcern` and `SurvivabilityConcern`.

* **MFRQ04 (Drone Operations):** The Mining Frigate should support at least 5 drones for mining and combat. This relates to `ThreatNeutralizationConcern` and `OreYieldConcern`.

* **STRQ05 (Threat Detection):** The Mining Frigate's threat detection range must be at least 20 AU.  This addresses `SecurityConcern` and `ThreatNeutralizationConcern`.

* **MFRQ06 (Travel Efficiency):** The Mining Frigate requires a minimum warp speed of 5 AU/second and an align time under 3 seconds. This impacts `DeploymentEfficiencyConcern`.

* **MFRQ07 (Mining Target):** The Mining Frigate can lock and mine up to 3 asteroid targets simultaneously. This requirement addresses `OreYieldConcern`.

* **MFRQ08 (Docking):** The Mining Frigate should dock and offload ore within 1 minute. This relates to `OreDeliveryEfficiencyConcern`.

* **MFRQ09 (Fleet Coordination):** The Fleet Commander must be able to coordinate fleets of up to 10 Mining Frigates. This addresses `FleetDeploymentEfficiencyConcern` and `ScalabilityConcern`.

* **MFRQ10 (Ore Compression):**  This requirement, surprisingly, applies to a Rorqual (a larger ship type) and specifies that it must support onboard ore compression with a factor of 10. This impacts `TotalProfitabilityConcern`.

Each requirement includes a description (`doc`), the subject it applies to (e.g., `miningFrigate`, `fleetCommander`, `rorqual`), relevant attributes (e.g., `miningRateLS`, `cargoCapacity`), constraints defining the minimum or maximum acceptable values, and one or more linked `StakeholderConcerns` which provide context and rationale for the requirement.  The model uses  Real numbers for attributes, implying floating-point values are expected.
