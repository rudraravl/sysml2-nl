This model describes a sophisticated, portable metal detector system designed to locate and identify metallic objects buried underground. The system is composed of several key electronic modules that work together to provide detailed feedback to the user.

At its core, the metal detector operates by using a **Transmitter Coil** to generate a stable electromagnetic field. When this field interacts with a metallic object, it creates a disturbance. A sensitive **Receiver Coil** detects these subtle changes in the field, capturing the distorted signal.

This received signal is then sent to a central **Signal Processing Unit**, which acts as the brains of the device. This unit filters out noise and analyzes the signal's characteristics, such as changes in amplitude and phase. Based on this analysis, it can classify the detected metal into categories like ferrous (iron-based), non-ferrous, or stainless steel. It also estimates the target's depth and calculates a confidence level for the detection. The user can adjust the sensitivity of this processing unit to fine-tune its performance for different ground conditions.

When a target is identified, an **Alerting Subsystem** notifies the user through both audio tones and visual indicators (like an LED). The system provides varied alert levels—from low to high—depending on the signal's strength, with the audio tone likely changing in pitch or frequency as the detector gets closer to the object.

A **Control Module** manages the overall operation of the device. It handles user inputs for powering the system on and off and adjusting sensitivity. The system operates in several states: it starts in a `powerOff` state, moves to a `standby` mode when turned on, and enters an `activeScanning` mode when the user begins a search. During a scan, it transitions from `searching` to `targetAcquired` when a signal is found, and then to a full `alerting` state when the signal is strong.

The entire system is powered by a **Power Management Unit**, which monitors and supplies power from a battery to all the other components, ensuring a long operational duration of over 15 hours on a single charge. The device is also designed to be robust, capable of operating in a wide range of environmental conditions.
