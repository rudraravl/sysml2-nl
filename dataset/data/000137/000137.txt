This model represents the basic operational state management system for a vehicle, such as a modern car with a push-button ignition. It governs how the vehicle transitions between being completely off, in the process of starting up, and fully operational.

Initially, the vehicle is in a powered-down "off" state. When the driver issues a start command (like pressing the ignition button), the system enters a temporary "starting" phase. During this time, the engine cranks and essential electronics boot up. Once the engine is running stably and self-checks are complete, an internal "on" signal is generated, and the vehicle transitions to its fully operational "on" state, ready to be driven. To shut down, another command from the driver returns the system to the "off" state, completing the cycle.
