This model describes a family of configurable vehicles built from a common platform. The fundamental design is a generic vehicle powered by an engine. This engine is itself composed of multiple cylinders, and in its general form, it can have between four and six cylinders.

From this basic template, two specific types of vehicles are defined: a Small Vehicle and a Big Vehicle. The Small Vehicle is a specialized version that is always equipped with a smaller, four-cylinder engine. In contrast, the Big Vehicle is a more powerful variant that consistently uses a larger, six-cylinder engine. This system demonstrates how a standard vehicle architecture can be adapted to create different models by fitting them with specific engine configurations.
