This model describes a foundational framework for creating and managing metadata. In practice, this is a system for adding descriptive information—or "data about data"—to other objects, components, or pieces of information.

Think of it as a sophisticated and standardized digital labeling system. The framework provides the fundamental building blocks to first define your own custom types of labels or tags (e.g., "Author," "Version Number," "Security Clearance," or "Review Status"). Once a type of label is defined, the system then allows you to attach these labels to any item you need to describe. This provides essential context, helps with organization and tracking, and makes it easier to understand and manage complex systems by annotating their parts with relevant, structured information.
