This model represents a corporate mining frigate, a type of spaceship designed for efficient and profitable resource extraction. The frigate's performance is defined by a series of interconnected systems and capabilities that all contribute to its final economic output.

The primary goal is to maximize profitability, which is achieved by improving two key areas: ore extraction and overall operational efficiency.

Ore extraction efficiency is a direct result of how much ore the frigate can gather and how little time is wasted. This is improved by increasing the raw mining rate to collect more ore per minute, and by reducing downtime. Downtime is minimized in two ways: by increasing the cargo hold capacity, which means fewer trips to unload ore, and by upgrading the warp drive for faster travel to and from the mining fields.

Overall operational efficiency is enhanced through better defensive and support systems. Upgrading the frigate's shield strength directly improves its ability to survive attacks or environmental hazards. This survivability is further boosted by better threat detection systems, which enable a faster and more effective response to danger. Efficiency is also gained through better coordination with a larger fleet and by improving the performance of any onboard support drones used for mining or defense.

Ultimately, these two main areas of efficiency—ore extraction and general operations—combine to determine the frigate's success and maximize its profitability.
