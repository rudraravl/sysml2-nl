This is a dynamic performance optimization system designed to autonomously create and manage self-reinforcing cycles of improvement. Its primary purpose is to monitor a target system's performance, identify when it is improving, and then actively intervene to make that improvement happen even faster, resulting in exponential performance gains.

The system is composed of several specialized components that work in a continuous feedback loop. A **Performance Monitor** constantly gathers real-time data on key metrics like throughput and efficiency. This information is fed to a **Change Analyzer**, which acts as the system's intelligence unit. It compares new data with past performance to calculate not just the current rate of improvement, but more importantly, whether that rate is accelerating.

When the analyzer detects a positive acceleration—meaning the system is getting better at getting better—it signals an **Amplification Controller**. This controller then formulates a strategy and issues an optimization command to a **Rate Amplifier**. The amplifier is the engine of the system; it takes the current rate of improvement and boosts it by a calculated factor, effectively "stepping on the gas."

To ensure this process doesn't become unstable or lead to chaotic behavior, a **Feedback Regulator** acts as a governor. It moderates the amplified rate and manages the feedback loop, ensuring the acceleration remains controlled and productive. This regulated output influences the overall system's state, which is then observed by the Performance Monitor, beginning the cycle anew. The entire system is designed to achieve and maintain a target rate of acceleration, turning small, positive changes into significant, rapidly compounding performance growth.
