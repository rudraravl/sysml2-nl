This model represents a vehicle system equipped with an integrated electronic controller. The system is composed of two primary components: the physical vehicle itself and its onboard controller.

The controller acts as the brain of the system, responsible for managing and dictating the vehicle's overall operational state. The system's behavior is explicitly defined by these states, such as being parked, idling, or actively moving. The controller is the component that processes inputs (like a driver's command or an autonomous signal) and orchestrates the transition of the entire vehicle from one state to another.
