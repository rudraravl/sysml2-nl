This model describes a vehicle's core fuel system. The system consists of two primary components: a Fuel Tank Assembly, which serves as the fuel reservoir, and an Engine, which consumes the fuel to generate power.

These two parts are linked by a standardized fuel interface, ensuring a proper connection for fuel delivery. In this arrangement, fuel flows from an output port on the tank assembly directly to an input port on the engine. This represents the fundamental relationship where the tank supplies fuel and the engine consumes it to operate the vehicle.
