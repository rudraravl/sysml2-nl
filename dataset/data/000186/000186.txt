This model describes a passenger vehicle, breaking it down into its main functional areas and classifying specific parts based on their purpose.

The vehicle is composed of two primary sub-systems: an interior and a body assembly. The interior contains components for the occupants, such as two front seats, two seat belts, and a driver's airbag. The body assembly includes the main vehicle body, a bumper, and a keyless entry system for access.

A key aspect of this design is the explicit identification of components that serve as safety and security features. The seat belts, driver's airbag, and bumper are all designated as safety features, designed to protect the vehicle's occupants. The anti-theft alarm (located in the interior) and the keyless entry system are categorized as security features, intended to prevent unauthorized access and protect the vehicle from theft.
