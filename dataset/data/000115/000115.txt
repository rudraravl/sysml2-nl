This model describes the fuel delivery system within a vehicle. The system consists of two main parts: a Fuel Tank Assembly and an Engine.

The Fuel Tank Assembly acts as the fuel supplier, while the Engine is the consumer. A dedicated connection is established between them, featuring a two-way fuel flow. A primary fuel line carries fuel from the tank assembly to the engine for combustion. In addition, a fuel return line allows any unused fuel to be sent from the engine back to the tank. This design, with both supply and return lines, is characteristic of many modern fuel-injected vehicle systems.
