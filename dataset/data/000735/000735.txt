This model describes a complete Plague Doctor Costume System, a historical protective ensemble worn by physicians during plague epidemics. The system's primary function is to protect the wearer from contagion, based on the miasmatic theory that disease was spread through contaminated or "bad air."

The system is a multi-layered defense consisting of several key components that work together. The most iconic piece is the **Beaked Mask**, which serves as a primitive respirator. Its long beak is not for aesthetics but functions as an **aromatic chamber**, designed to be filled with a potent mixture of herbs, spices, and flowers like camphor, mint, and cloves. The idea was that these strong aromatics would purify the foul, disease-carrying air before it was inhaled by the physician. The mask is completed with glass eyepieces for vision and a leather harness to secure it to the head.

For full-body protection, the ensemble includes a long **Waxed Leather Coat**, **Thick Leather Gloves**, and **High Leather Boots**. Together, these items create an impermeable physical barrier intended to prevent any contact with the bodily fluids of infected patients or contaminated surfaces. The wax coating on the leather is crucial for making it water-repellent and non-porous. A **Wide-Brimmed Hat** completes the head-to-toe coverage.

A key tool included in the system is a long **Wooden Cane**. This implement allows the physician to examine patients, move clothing, or interact with people and objects from a distance, enforcing a physical separation to reduce the risk of direct contact and transmission.

The costume operates through different phases. It begins in a stored state before being deployed for duty. The physician goes through a careful process of donning each component to ensure a complete seal. While operational, the costume is actively performing its protective functions: filtering the air, providing a physical barrier, and enabling distancing. The system also requires regular maintenance, such as replenishing the aromatic herbs in the mask to ensure their potency and re-waxing the leather coat to maintain its protective integrity. After a duty period, the costume is carefully removed and stored again.
