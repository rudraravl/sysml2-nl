This model represents the Xanadu House, a highly automated and energy-efficient smart home built with a unique sprayed polyurethane foam structure. Its main purpose is to provide a comfortable living environment while minimizing energy consumption through intelligent automation, renewable energy generation, and superior insulation.

The core of the system is a **Central Controller**, which acts as the brain of the house. It continuously monitors the environment using a network of eight **Environmental Sensors** that detect temperature, humidity, and motion. This data allows the controller to determine if the house is occupied, unoccupied, or if the occupants are sleeping.

Based on the occupancy state, the controller orchestrates all other systems:

*   The **Climate Control Unit** is an advanced HVAC system that can heat, cool, ventilate, or dehumidify the house. The Central Controller sends commands to this unit to maintain a comfortable temperature, automatically adjusting based on sensor readings and the known preferences of the occupants.
*   A **Lighting System** provides smart, adjustable lighting. The controller can change the brightness and color temperature, creating different moods like "normal" or "dim," and ensures lights are turned off in empty rooms to save energy.
*   The house features sixteen **Automated Windows** that can be opened, closed, and even have their tint level adjusted by the controller to manage natural light and heat from the sun.

Energy management is a key feature. The house is equipped with a **Solar Energy System** that generates its own electrical power. This power is used to run the house's systems, and any excess is stored in a large-capacity **Energy Storage System** (a battery). The controller intelligently decides whether to use power from the solar panels, the battery, or the main electrical grid, always prioritizing the most efficient and cost-effective source.

The entire dwelling is constructed from a thick **Foam Structure**, which provides exceptional thermal insulation. This design significantly reduces the amount of energy needed for heating and cooling, forming the foundation of the house's efficiency. The system is designed to learn occupant habits over time, further optimizing comfort and energy use to create a self-sufficient, adaptive, and affordable living space.
