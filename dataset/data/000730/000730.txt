This model describes a comprehensive pedorthic care system designed for the assessment, treatment, and management of patients with foot and lower limb conditions. The system provides a complete workflow for creating and fitting custom foot orthotics (inserts) and therapeutic footwear.

The core of the system is a patient-centered process managed by a Pedorthist. This process begins with a detailed patient assessment using specialized equipment. This equipment captures precise foot measurements (length, width, arch height), analyzes the patient's gait or walking pattern (stride, cadence, pronation), and maps the pressure distribution across the sole of the foot.

Based on this collected data, a biomechanical analysis is performed to diagnose the patient's condition and determine their specific treatment needs. This leads to the development of a formal treatment plan and a prescription for a custom device, which could be a foot orthotic or specialized therapeutic footwear.

The prescription is then sent to a fabrication laboratory, which manufactures the device according to the exact specifications, including material, thickness, and level of support.

Once the device is created, the patient returns for a fitting. The Pedorthist fits the device, and if any discomfort or issues are found, makes necessary adjustments. This fitting and adjustment cycle is repeated until a safe, comfortable, and effective fit is achieved. The process concludes with the Pedorthist educating the patient on the proper use and care of their new device and scheduling a future follow-up appointment to monitor their progress.
