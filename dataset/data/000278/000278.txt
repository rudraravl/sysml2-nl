This model describes the hull of a modular mining frigate, a small, pilot-operated spaceship designed for resource extraction in potentially hazardous space environments. Its primary purpose is to mine ore from asteroids using specialized equipment, such as mining lasers, and then store the materials in its dedicated ore hold.

The frigate is highly configurable, featuring a system of module slots where a pilot can install different equipment to adapt the ship for various needs. This allows for customization with mining tools, defensive systems, or power-enhancing modules like capacitor batteries.

For travel, the frigate can navigate between celestial bodies and engage a warp drive for faster-than-light transit. While its main role is industrial, it is also equipped for self-defense. It has sensors to detect incoming threats from hostile ships and can activate defensive systems to withstand damage and protect the pilot.

The ship's operations are powered by a central energy source known as a capacitor, which passively recharges but can also be actively replenished. The frigate is also designed to dock at space stations, enabling the pilot to transfer mined ore, refuel, and perform maintenance before undertaking another mission.
