This model describes a comprehensive management system for the Asia and South Pacific Design Automation Conference (ASPDAC), an international technical conference focused on electronic design automation. The system is designed to facilitate the entire lifecycle of the conference, from the initial call for papers and peer review process to attendee registration, session scheduling, and the final execution of the event.

The system is composed of several key functional components that work together:

*   A **Paper Management System** is at the core of the academic program. It handles the intake of technical paper submissions from authors, tracking details like titles, author affiliations, and submission dates. It then shepherds these papers through the review process and ultimately records their final status as accepted or rejected.

*   A **Review Committee** system manages the crucial peer-review process. Once a paper is submitted, this component facilitates its assignment to a group of qualified experts. It ensures that each paper receives a minimum number of independent reviews (typically three) and aggregates their feedback and scores to help decide which papers will be accepted for presentation.

*   The **Session Scheduler** takes all the accepted papers and organizes them into a coherent conference program. It groups related papers into technical sessions, assigns session chairs, and builds a detailed schedule, often across multiple parallel tracks, for the duration of the conference.

*   A **Registration System** handles all aspects related to the conference attendees. It processes registrations for individuals, managing different registration types (such as early-bird and regular), and keeps a running count of all participants to ensure the event stays within capacity.

*   Finally, a **Venue Management** component deals with the physical logistics of the conference, keeping track of the venue's name, location, and the number and capacity of available rooms for the technical sessions.

The entire conference operates through a series of distinct phases. It begins with planning and a call for papers, followed by a rigorous reviewing period. Once papers are selected, the system enters a program creation phase where the schedule is built. This is followed by the opening of attendee registration, which all leads up to the final execution of the conference itself. After the event, the system supports post-conference activities like archiving. A specific instance for the 2024 conference is configured to handle approximately 400 paper submissions and 650 attendees in Seoul.
