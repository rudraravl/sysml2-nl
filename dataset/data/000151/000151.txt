This model describes the complete lifecycle of a vehicle, from its creation to its disposal.

The vehicle's life begins with an assembly phase where it is manufactured. Following its construction, there is a specific delivery event, which is recorded with a formal delivery date.

After delivery, the vehicle enters its operational life, which can consist of sequential periods of ownership by different people. Each period of ownership starts with a sale event. Throughout a period of ownership by a specific person, the vehicle can be driven on multiple occasions. The system tracks who is driving the vehicle during each of these periods, recognizing that the driver may be different from the legal owner.

Finally, the vehicle's lifecycle concludes with a final event when it is junked or scrapped, marking its end of service.
