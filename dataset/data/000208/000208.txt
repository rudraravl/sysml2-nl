This model describes the powertrain system of a rear-wheel-drive vehicle. The system's primary function is to convert a fuel command into mechanical power, delivering rotational force (torque) to the rear wheels to propel the vehicle.

The system is composed of several key mechanical components that work in sequence:

1.  An **engine** receives a command for fuel. In response, it combusts the fuel to generate an initial torque.
2.  This torque is then passed to a **transmission**, which modifies or amplifies the force, similar to a gearbox changing gears.
3.  The adjusted torque is transferred from the transmission to the back of the vehicle by a rotating **driveshaft**.
4.  Finally, a **rear axle assembly** takes the torque from the driveshaft and distributes it to the individual left and right rear wheels, causing them to turn and move the vehicle forward.
