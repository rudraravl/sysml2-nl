This model describes an industrial fluid pumping subsystem designed to transfer a liquid from a source tank to a destination tank. The system's core components are the two tanks and a large, powerful pump that facilitates the transfer.

The system's operation begins at the source tank, which holds a supply of the fluid. A high-capacity component, referred to as the "Big Pump," draws this fluid from the source tank through its suction port. This pump is the active heart of the system; it pressurizes the incoming fluid and then expels it at a high flow rate through its discharge port. The fluid then travels from the pump into the destination tank, completing the transfer process.

The pump has several key operational characteristics, including a rated flow rate, a maximum safe discharge pressure, and a specific motor power. It operates through distinct states such as stopped, starting, operating, and faulted, reflecting its operational lifecycle. The entire subsystem is designed to meet specific performance requirements, ensuring it can transfer fluid effectively while also operating safely within its pressure limits.
