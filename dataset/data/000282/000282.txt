This model describes a space-faring Mining Frigate, a versatile spacecraft designed for autonomous and pilot-assisted asteroid mining operations.

The frigate's primary mission is to travel to asteroid belts, identify and target specific asteroids, and extract valuable ore using its mining lasers. Once extracted, the ore is transferred to an internal cargo hold. The entire mining process, from navigation to extraction and storage, is a core operational sequence.

For navigation, a pilot in a command pod sets a destination, such as an asteroid belt or a space station. The frigate's navigation system then plots the optimal course and engages its propulsion systems to travel there, automatically adjusting for obstacles.

Beyond mining, the frigate is equipped for self-defense. It has a sensor array to detect and classify threats like hostile ships. In response to a threat, it can automatically engage its defense turrets and deploy combat drones to neutralize the enemy. The pilot can oversee these engagements and manage drone deployment for different scenarios.

The frigate operates in a cycle. After its cargo hold is full, it navigates to a space station to dock. Once connected, it offloads the mined ore and resupplies its fuel and life support systems before embarking on its next mining expedition. The system is designed to handle various exceptions, such as system failures or a full cargo hold, by alerting the pilot and suggesting alternative actions.
