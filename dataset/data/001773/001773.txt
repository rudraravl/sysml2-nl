This model represents a complete espresso machine system designed to automate the process of brewing high-quality espresso. It also includes a steam wand for frothing milk. The machine takes whole coffee beans and water as inputs and produces a finished espresso shot, managing the critical variables of temperature, pressure, and time to ensure a consistent result.

The system is composed of several key interconnected components that work in a sequence:

*   A **Water Reservoir** holds the cold water supply.
*   A high-pressure **Pump** draws water from the reservoir and pressurizes it for the brewing process.
*   This pressurized water is then fed into a **Boiler**, which heats it to a precise, optimal brewing temperature. The boiler is also the source of high-pressure steam.
*   An integrated **Grinder** takes whole coffee beans and grinds them to a specific fineness, which is then dispensed into the **Portafilter**—a handle with a filter basket.
*   To brew, the user attaches the Portafilter filled with coffee grounds to the **Group Head**. The Group Head is the central component that receives the hot, pressurized water from the boiler and forces it evenly through the coffee grounds.
*   As the water passes through the grounds, it extracts the espresso, which then flows out into a cup.
*   Separately, a **Steam Wand** connected to the boiler can be used to froth milk for beverages like lattes or cappuccinos.
*   The entire operation is managed by a **Control Panel**, which allows the user to power the machine on and select settings such as the desired volume for the espresso shot.

The machine's operation follows a defined sequence of states. When powered on, it enters a `warmup` state until the boiler reaches the target temperature. Once hot, it becomes `ready` for use. Starting a brew cycle initiates the `grinding` of beans, followed by the `brewing` and `dispensing` of the espresso. After the shot is complete, the machine returns to the `ready` state, prepared for the next use or to be powered off.
