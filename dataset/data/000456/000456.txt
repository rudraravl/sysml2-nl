This model represents a comprehensive management system for the Hong Kong International Medical and Healthcare Fair, a large-scale trade exhibition. The system is designed to facilitate all aspects of the event, from initial planning to post-event analysis, with the primary goal of connecting medical and healthcare businesses with professional buyers and partners.

The system manages a physical venue consisting of three large exhibition halls, which are divided into numerous individual exhibition booths. These booths are the core spaces where participating companies, known as exhibitors, showcase their products. Each booth is allocated based on the exhibitor's needs and is equipped with essential utilities like power and network connections.

Two main groups of participants interact with the system: Exhibitors and Visitors.
-   **Exhibitors** are companies from sectors like diagnostic equipment, pharmaceuticals, and digital health. They register through the system, provide a detailed profile of their products and target markets, and are assigned a booth. During the fair, they use the system to collect business leads from interested visitors.
-   **Visitors** are industry professionals, such as buyers and healthcare practitioners. They also register and create a profile outlining their professional role and product interests. This allows them to receive personalized recommendations and efficiently navigate the fair.

The fair's operations are handled by several specialized functional units:
-   A **Registration Center** manages the entire sign-up and check-in process for thousands of exhibitors and visitors.
-   A **Business Matching Center** is a key service that intelligently connects exhibitors with relevant visitors based on their profiles, suggesting potential meetings and business partnerships.
-   An **Event Coordination** unit organizes a schedule of supplementary events, including educational seminars held in dedicated venues and live product demonstrations that take place at exhibitor booths.
-   Multiple **Information Desks** are strategically placed to provide visitors with directions, answer queries, and offer general assistance.

The entire event follows a structured lifecycle, beginning with a preparation phase for registration and setup. It then moves to the active operational phase when the fair is open, during which all business interactions, seminars, and demonstrations occur. The system also includes protocols for handling emergencies. After the fair concludes, it enters a closing phase where it collects feedback from participants and generates detailed analytics reports on attendance, business leads, and the overall return on investment for stakeholders.
