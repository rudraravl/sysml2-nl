This is a videoscope system designed for remote visual inspection of inaccessible or enclosed areas, such as the inside of engines, pipes, or industrial machinery.

The system's core component is a long, thin, and flexible insertion tube, approximately 2.5 meters in length and 6 millimeters in diameter. At the very tip of this tube is a high-definition (1920x1080) CMOS image sensor and a powerful, adjustable LED light source. This allows it to capture clear video footage even in complete darkness. The tip of the tube is also highly maneuverable, featuring an articulation mechanism that allows an operator to bend and steer it up to 180 degrees to navigate complex geometries and get the best viewing angle.

An operator controls the system using a main console. From here, they can issue commands to control the insertion depth, adjust the angle of the articulating tip, and set the brightness of the LED light. The live video feed captured by the insertion tube is sent to a video processing unit. This processor enhances the raw image quality and can automatically perform analysis to detect potential defects.

The processed, high-quality video is then displayed in real-time on a 10-inch monitor, allowing the operator to see exactly what the probe sees. The entire system, including the light, sensor, and processing units, is powered by a central 12-volt power supply. The typical inspection process involves the operator guiding the probe, observing the live feed, and concluding the inspection when the task is complete or if a defect is identified.
