This is an anthropomorphic robot system designed to replicate the upper body of a human for performing complex manipulation tasks. The system is built around a central controller and is equipped with a pair of highly articulated arms and a sophisticated stereo vision system, enabling it to operate with dexterity in human-centric environments.

The robot's perception is handled by a stereo vision system, which acts as its eyes. This system captures and processes visual information to create a 3D map of its surroundings, allowing it to detect and locate objects in its workspace.

The core of its functionality lies in its two independent arm manipulators, a left and a right arm. Each arm is a complete assembly that mimics human anatomy, consisting of a three-axis shoulder joint for wide-ranging movement, a single-axis elbow joint for flexion, and a two-axis wrist for orientation. At the end of each arm is a five-fingered anthropomorphic gripper, designed to grasp objects with adjustable force and dexterity similar to a human hand.

The robot's "brain" is a central motion planner. When given a high-level task, this planner uses data from the vision system to understand the scene. It then calculates a smooth, collision-free trajectory for the arm to reach a target and determines the appropriate grip sequence and force required for the hand.

A dedicated power management unit serves as the heart of the system, distributing electrical power from a main source to all components, including the vision system, the motion planner, and each of the arm and gripper actuators. It monitors the power consumption and battery levels to ensure safe and continuous operation.

Operationally, the robot follows a clear sequence. It begins in an idle state, awaiting commands. Upon receiving a task, it enters a perceiving state to scan its environment. It then transitions to a planning state where it computes the necessary movements. Finally, it enters an executing state, where it physically carries out the coordinated actions of reaching for and grasping an object.
