This is an advanced aquarium filtration system designed to continuously circulate and purify water, maintaining a healthy and stable environment for aquatic life. The system is composed of several specialized components working together in a multi-stage process.

A central water pump provides the core function of drawing water from the aquarium and pushing it through the various filter stages. The filtration process begins with a mechanical filter, which uses physical media like foam and sponges to trap and remove solid waste, debris, and uneaten food.

After the initial mechanical cleaning, the water flows into a biological filter stage. This component contains a medium with a very large surface area designed to cultivate a dense colony of beneficial bacteria. These microorganisms are essential for breaking down toxic ammonia and nitrites (produced from fish waste) into less harmful nitrates through a natural process called nitrification.

The next step is a chemical filtration stage, which uses materials like activated carbon to adsorb and remove dissolved impurities, organic compounds, odors, and discoloration from the water, resulting in crystal-clear water.

To ensure effectiveness, an integrated quality sensor continuously samples the purified water as it exits the final stage. This sensor monitors critical water quality parameters, including levels of ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, and dissolved oxygen. A central control module manages the system's overall operation, maintaining the target water flow rate and tracking operating hours to signal when maintenance is required.

The entire system operates through various states, including startup, shutdown, and maintenance modes. During normal operation, it can even adjust its performance to handle periods of high biological load, ensuring consistent water quality at all times. The system is designed to be reliable and energy-efficient, capable of running for long periods between maintenance cycles while keeping power consumption low.
