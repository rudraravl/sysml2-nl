This model describes the fundamental process of a camera taking a picture. The overall system is designed to transform a real-world scene into a final, captured photograph through a sequence of two distinct steps.

First, the camera performs a "Focus" action. It takes the external scene as its subject and adjusts its optics to create a sharp, clear image on its internal sensor.

Once this focused image is ready, the system immediately proceeds to the "Shoot" action. This step takes that focused image from the sensor and captures it, creating the final, permanent picture. In essence, the system defines a required two-stage workflow for photography: a picture can only be taken after the camera has first successfully focused on the scene.
