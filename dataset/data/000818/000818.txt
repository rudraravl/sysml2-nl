This model represents a complete system for conducting vaginal dilation therapy, a medical treatment for the progressive expansion and rehabilitation of vaginal tissues. The primary purpose of this system is to help patients restore or maintain the capacity and function of the vagina in a controlled, safe, and gradual manner, often following medical procedures, radiation therapy, or to treat conditions like vaginismus.

The system is composed of several key components that work together:

*   **A Set of Vaginal Dilators:** The core of the system is a graduated set of physical dilators, typically five, each with a progressively larger diameter. Each dilator is a single, solid device made from a safe, medical-grade material. It features a smooth, tapered body designed for gentle insertion and an ergonomic handle that allows the patient to control it easily. For patient safety, the dilators have a biocompatible, hypoallergenic coating.

*   **The Patient:** The system is designed for use by a patient who has been medically cleared for this type of therapy. The patient's comfort level and the physical response of their tissues are critical factors that are monitored and guide the entire process.

*   **Medical-Grade Lubricant:** A water-based, pH-balanced lubricant is an essential part of the system. It is applied to the dilator before use to reduce friction, making insertion more comfortable and preventing tissue irritation.

*   **A Clinical Therapy Protocol:** The therapy is not arbitrary; it's guided by a set of clinical instructions. This protocol defines the recommended duration and frequency of each therapy session and provides a schedule for how the patient should progress from the smallest dilator to the largest over time.

The overall therapeutic process involves a sequence of steps for each session:
1.  **Preparation:** The appropriate-sized dilator is selected and prepared by applying lubricant.
2.  **Comfort Assessment:** The patient's readiness is checked. The therapy only proceeds if the patient is comfortable; otherwise, the session is postponed to ensure a positive and pain-free experience.
3.  **Insertion and Dilation:** The lubricated dilator is gently inserted and held in place for a prescribed amount of time, allowing the tissues to stretch gently.
4.  **Removal and Cleaning:** After the session, the dilator is removed, and then thoroughly cleaned and sterilized to ensure it is hygienic and ready for the next use.
