This model describes an autonomous, robotic pool cleaner designed to systematically clean swimming pools without human intervention. The robot is a self-contained unit that navigates the pool floor and walls, scrubbing surfaces, suctioning up debris, and filtering the water.

At its core, the cleaner is a battery-powered vehicle. A **Battery System** provides electrical power to all other components and can be recharged at a docking station. The robot moves using a **Motor Drive** system that controls its wheels for propulsion and steering.

The cleaning process is a coordinated effort between mechanical and hydraulic systems. A **Brush Assembly** with rotating bristles actively scrubs the pool surfaces to dislodge dirt, algae, and other debris. Simultaneously, a powerful **Hydraulic Pump** creates suction, pulling in the surrounding dirty water through an intake port. This water, containing the dislodged debris, is then forced through an internal **Debris Filter** that captures particles, letting only clean, filtered water pass through and exit back into the pool.

To operate autonomously, the cleaner relies on a sophisticated **Navigation Controller** and a suite of **Proximity Sensors**. The sensors continuously scan the environment to detect obstacles and walls, preventing collisions. This data is fed to the navigation controller, which intelligently calculates an efficient cleaning path, tracks the areas already cleaned, and guides the robot's movements.

The system is designed to manage its own operational needs. It constantly monitors its battery charge and the amount of debris collected in its filter. If the battery runs low, or the filter becomes full, the cleaner will automatically stop its cleaning cycle and navigate back to its docking station for recharging or maintenance.
