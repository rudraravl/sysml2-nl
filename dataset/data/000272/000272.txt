This model describes a specialized spaceship, a Mining Frigate, designed for extracting ore from asteroids in various space environments with differing levels of security and danger.

The frigate's primary function is to mine ore efficiently, with performance targets that change based on the operational area; it is expected to mine at a much higher rate in more dangerous, resource-rich regions (like Low Sec or Null Sec) compared to safer, high-security space. To support these operations, it must have a significant cargo hold of at least 5,000 m³ for storing the harvested ore.

Survivability is a key design concern. The frigate is required to have robust shielding capable of withstanding a specific amount of damage per second (DPS), with higher resilience demanded for operations in more hostile territories. To aid in its defense and utility, the ship can deploy and manage a bay of at least five drones, which can be used for both supplementary mining and combat. Situational awareness is provided by a long-range threat detection system, enabling the frigate to identify potential hostiles from as far as 20 Astronomical Units (AU) away.

Mobility is critical for both efficiency and survival. The vessel must be equipped with a fast warp drive for rapid travel across star systems and be agile enough to align and enter warp in under three seconds, allowing for quick escapes. During mining, it can lock and simultaneously extract resources from up to three different asteroids.

This Mining Frigate is designed to operate as part of a larger industrial fleet. A fleet commander can coordinate a squadron of up to ten such frigates. The overall logistical chain also involves support from a capital industrial ship (a Rorqual), which can compress the raw ore on-site, drastically reducing its volume and increasing the profitability of the entire mining operation. Finally, the frigate is designed for efficient logistics, able to dock at stations or structures and offload its cargo within one minute.
