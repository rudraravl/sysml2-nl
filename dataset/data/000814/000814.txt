This is a Vaccine Vial Monitor (VVM) system designed to track the cumulative heat exposure of a vaccine and provide a clear, irreversible visual indicator of its viability. Its primary purpose is to help healthcare workers in the field determine if a vaccine, such as for Measles-Rubella, has been compromised by heat and is safe to administer.

The system consists of several integrated components:

*   **Vaccine Vial**: The standard container holding the vaccine. The monitor is attached to this vial and is sensitive to its temperature.
*   **Thermochromic Label**: This is the core visual component, essentially a smart sticker on the vial. It has a light-colored inner square surrounded by a darker, static-colored outer ring. The inner square darkens irreversibly as it's exposed to heat over time.
*   **Exposure Calculator**: This internal component functions like a tiny computer that continuously monitors the vial's temperature. It only accumulates a "heat damage" score when the temperature exceeds a safe reference point (e.g., 8°C).
*   **Status Evaluator**: This component acts as the decision-maker. It takes the total accumulated heat damage score from the calculator and compares it against two predefined safety thresholds: a "caution" threshold and a "discard" threshold.

In operation, the system provides a simple, at-a-glance status through the label. The inner square's color changes based on the evaluation of its total heat exposure. This results in three distinct, easy-to-understand states:

1.  **Safe**: The inner square is significantly lighter than the outer ring, indicating the vaccine has been stored correctly and is safe to use.
2.  **Caution**: The inner square has started to darken and is approaching the color of the outer ring. This state warns that a significant heat exposure has occurred.
3.  **Discard**: The inner square is as dark as, or darker than, the outer ring. This is a clear signal that the vaccine has exceeded its heat tolerance and must be discarded.

A critical feature of the system is that the color change on the label is permanent and irreversible. Once the vaccine vial has been exposed to damaging heat, the indicator will not reverse back to "safe," even if the vial is cooled down, preventing the use of a potentially ineffective or spoiled vaccine.
