This model describes a specific vehicle and its operation during two consecutive journeys.

The scenario begins with a person named Alice driving the vehicle. At the start of her trip, the vehicle has a mass of 2000 units. By the end of her journey, the mass has decreased to 1500 units. This significant reduction suggests the vehicle's primary purpose during this time was to transport and deliver a heavy cargo weighing 500 units.

Immediately after Alice's trip is complete, a second person, Bob, takes over as the driver for a subsequent journey in the now-lighter vehicle. The model captures this handover, tracking key changes to the vehicle's state (its mass) and its assigned operator over time.
