This model represents the International System of Quantities (ISQ), a foundational framework used globally for physical measurements, based on the ISO 80000 standard. It's not a physical object, but rather a conceptual system that acts as a standard digital library for defining the fundamental properties of the physical world.

The system is composed of the seven base quantities that form the foundation of modern physics and engineering. These are:
*   **Length:** The measure of linear extent in space, with its standard unit being the meter.
*   **Mass:** A body's property of inertia and gravitational attraction, measured in kilograms.
*   **Duration (Time):** The measure of time difference between two events, measured in seconds.
*   **Electric Current:** A base quantity in electromagnetism, measured in amperes.
*   **Thermodynamic Temperature:** A measure related to the internal energy of a system, measured in kelvin.
*   **Luminous Intensity:** The perceived power emitted by a light source in a particular direction, measured in candelas.
*   **Amount of Substance:** A measure of the number of elementary entities (like atoms or molecules) in a sample, measured in moles.

For each of these base quantities, the system provides a formal scientific definition, its standard symbol, and its corresponding base unit of measurement. This framework provides the essential, standardized building blocks needed to describe the physical attributes of any real-world system, ensuring that scientific and engineering models represent physical properties in a consistent and universally understood way.
