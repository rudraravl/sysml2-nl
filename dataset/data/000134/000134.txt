This model describes a digital camera system designed to capture and display photographs. The system consists of two main components: the camera body and a display screen.

The camera's primary function is to take a picture, which is a three-step process. First, the user aims the camera's lens (the viewport) at a real-world scene. The camera then automatically focuses on that scene to create a sharp, internal optical image. Immediately after focusing, the camera's internal mechanism captures that image, converting it into a final digital picture.

Once the picture has been created, the camera body sends it to the attached screen, which then displays the newly taken photograph for the user to review.
