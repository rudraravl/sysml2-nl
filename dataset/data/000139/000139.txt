This model describes the basic operational state of a vehicle, such as a car or a machine. It outlines the fundamental lifecycle from being off, through a startup process, to being fully operational, and back to off.

Initially, the vehicle is in an "off" state. When a user provides a start signal—like turning a key or pressing a power button—the vehicle enters a "starting" phase. This represents the time when the engine is cranking or the system is booting up and running self-checks.

Once the startup process is complete, an internal confirmation signal indicates the vehicle is ready, and it transitions to the fully "on" state. In this state, it is running and operational. Finally, when a user sends an "off" signal, the vehicle shuts down and returns to its initial "off" state.
