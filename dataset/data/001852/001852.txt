This is an industrial safety monitoring and response system designed to protect personnel, equipment, and the environment in a hazardous setting like a factory or chemical plant. Its primary purpose is to continuously monitor conditions, detect potential dangers, and automatically initiate protective measures.

The system is built around a central **Safety Controller**, which acts as the brain of the operation. This controller processes information from a variety of sensors, including **pressure sensors**, **temperature sensors**, and **gas sensors**, which are strategically placed to monitor key operational and environmental metrics. Each sensor constantly checks for conditions that exceed predefined safety thresholds.

Based on the data it receives, the controller continuously evaluates the overall risk level and determines the system's operational state, which can range from `normal` to `warning`, `critical`, or a full-blown `emergency`.

If a low-level risk is detected, the controller activates an **Alarm System** to alert personnel. This system uses both visual alarms, like flashing lights of varying colors and brightness, and audio alarms, such as sirens with different volumes and frequencies, to signal the level of danger. For more severe threats, the system can automatically trigger an **Emergency Shutdown** procedure. This critical safety measure can cut power to equipment and activate emergency systems, like ventilation, to mitigate the hazard and prevent a catastrophic failure. The entire process, from monitoring to response, is designed to be extremely fast and reliable.
