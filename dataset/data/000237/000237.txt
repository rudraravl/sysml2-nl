This model describes a configurable vehicle, likely a car, that is defined by its core powertrain components: an engine and a transmission. The vehicle is available in two distinct and mutually exclusive configurations: a standard version and a high-performance version.

The standard configuration is built with a 4-cylinder engine, which is always paired with a manual transmission.

The high-performance configuration features a more powerful 6-cylinder engine, which is exclusively matched with an automatic transmission.

The system's design strictly links these components together. A customer cannot order a 4-cylinder engine with an automatic transmission, nor can they get a 6-cylinder engine with a manual one. The selection of either the standard or high-performance package determines the complete, pre-defined engine and transmission setup.
