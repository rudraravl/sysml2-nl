This is an Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT) system used for medical imaging.  The system involves a patient, a doctor, and an engineer/technician.  An engineer/technician applies an electrode belt to the patient, ensuring proper contact and placement.  A doctor then initiates the monitoring process, starting the data acquisition. The EIT system itself collects impedance data from the electrodes, processing this raw data to generate EIT images representing the internal electrical conductivity of the patient's body. These images are then analyzed by the doctor to aid in diagnosis.  Periodically, an engineer/technician calibrates the system to maintain accuracy and reliability of the generated images.  The system's primary function is to provide non-invasive, real-time internal imaging for diagnostic purposes.
