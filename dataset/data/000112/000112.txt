This model describes a vehicle's fuel delivery system. The system is built around a single, integrated fuel tank assembly.

This assembly contains two primary components: a fuel tank for storing the vehicle's fuel and a fuel pump. In operation, the fuel pump draws fuel from the tank and supplies it to the rest of the vehicle (presumably the engine). The system is also designed to accommodate a fuel return line, where any unused fuel from the engine is sent directly back into the fuel tank for storage.
