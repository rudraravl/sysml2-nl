This model describes an intelligent battery charging system. The system is composed of a battery and a power system designed to manage the charging process.

The system's primary function is to safely and automatically recharge the battery to its full capacity. The charging process operates as a continuous feedback loop. First, the power system monitors the battery's current state of charge. As long as the charge level is below 100%, the system actively supplies power to the battery. This cycle of monitoring the charge and then adding more power repeats continuously.

Once the system detects that the battery has reached a full charge, it automatically terminates the charging process. This prevents overcharging, ensuring the safety and longevity of the battery.
