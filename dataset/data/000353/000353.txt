This model represents a foundational digital library that defines the standard quantities and units used in the fields of physical chemistry and molecular physics. It is a formal, machine-readable implementation of the international standard ISO 80000-9, designed to provide a precise and unambiguous vocabulary for scientific and engineering applications.

This is not a physical object, but a conceptual framework that catalogs the properties of chemical substances and their interactions. It includes definitions for a wide range of fundamental concepts, such as:

*   **Basic Substance Properties:** Quantities like the amount of a substance (moles), molar mass, and molar volume, which describe the macroscopic properties of materials.
*   **Mixtures and Solutions:** A comprehensive set of definitions for describing the composition of mixtures, including mass fraction, volume fraction, molality, and various types of concentration.
*   **Thermodynamics and Energy:** Key thermodynamic properties like molar enthalpy, molar entropy, and molar Gibbs energy, which are essential for analyzing energy changes during chemical reactions and phase transitions.
*   **Chemical Reaction Dynamics:** Concepts used to describe and quantify chemical reactions, including the stoichiometric number, the extent of a reaction, reaction affinity, and various forms of equilibrium constants.
*   **Transport and Solution Behavior:** Properties that govern how substances behave in solutions and move through a medium, such as diffusion coefficients, chemical potential, osmotic pressure, and activity coefficients.
*   **Electrochemistry:** Definitions for properties of electrolytes, such as ionic strength, electrolytic conductivity, and molar conductivity.

In essence, this library acts as a standardized reference for scientists and engineers. When they build models of systems like chemical reactors, batteries, or biological processes, they can use this library to ensure that all calculations and data are based on consistent, internationally recognized definitions, symbols, and units. This promotes accuracy, clarity, and interoperability between different scientific and engineering models.
