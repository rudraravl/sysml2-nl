This model describes the operational and health status of a vehicle. The system's behavior is defined by two distinct aspects that are tracked simultaneously: its operational state and its health state.

The vehicle's operational cycle begins in an "off" state. Upon receiving a "start" signal, like the turn of an ignition key or press of a start button, it transitions into a "starting" phase. Once the vehicle's systems confirm it is fully powered and running, it moves into the "on" state, ready for operation. An "off" signal will then return the vehicle to its shutdown state.

Crucially, while this operational cycle is occurring, the vehicle is also independently monitoring its own system health. This means the vehicle can be in an "on" state operationally, while its health status might simultaneously be tracked as "normal," "warning," or "faulted," allowing for continuous self-diagnosis during operation.
