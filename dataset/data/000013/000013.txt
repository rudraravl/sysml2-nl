This is a model of a push lawnmower.  The lawnmower consists of a deck, an engine, a blade, and four wheels. The engine, weighing 190 units of mass, powers the blade, which is attached to the deck.  The deck, weighing 50 units of mass, houses the blade and also connects to the four wheels via connecting pins. The handle allows the user to push the mower across the lawn. The engine itself is a complex assembly including a piston, connecting rod, crankshaft, crankshaft bearing, and engine block, all working together to convert fuel into rotational motion which turns the blade. The wheels, each composed of a rim and a tire, allow the mower to move smoothly over the lawn.  The cutting action involves starting the engine, pushing the mower across the grass, and then shutting off the engine.  The connecting rod has the option of two different types of bolts, either steel or aluminum, which are interchangeable components.
