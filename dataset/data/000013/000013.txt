This model describes the automated process for taking a picture with a digital camera, representing the common "half-press, full-press" shutter functionality.

The overall process consists of two distinct stages. First, the camera performs a "focus" action. During this phase, it adjusts the lens to get a sharp image and simultaneously meters the light in the scene to calculate the ideal exposure settings (like shutter speed and aperture).

Once these exposure settings are determined, they are passed to the second stage: the "shoot" action. This final step uses the pre-calculated settings to actually open the shutter and capture the photograph.
