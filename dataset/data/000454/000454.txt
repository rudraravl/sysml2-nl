This model describes a precision Heat Pen, a tool designed for applications like soldering, crafting, and thermal bonding that require controlled heat application.

The system is composed of several integrated components. A central Controller Module serves as the brain, managing the device's overall operation. It receives power from a dedicated Power Supply Unit, which converts standard wall voltage into a safe, low operating voltage for the pen.

The core of the tool is a Heating Element Assembly that, when powered, generates thermal energy. This heat is then conducted to an interchangeable Tip Module, which makes direct contact with the work surface. The tip is made of a thermally conductive copper-iron alloy and is shaped for precision work.

A key feature of the Heat Pen is its pressure-activated operation. A Pressure Sensor Assembly detects when the tip is pressed against a surface, signaling the Controller to begin heating. This ensures the pen only becomes hot during active use, enhancing safety and energy efficiency. The Controller uses feedback from a temperature sensor within the heating element to rapidly reach and precisely maintain the temperature set by the user, creating a closed-loop control system.

The Heat Pen operates through several distinct states. When powered on, it enters a safe `standby` mode. Upon detecting tip pressure, it transitions to `preheating`. Once the target temperature is reached, it becomes `operational`, maintaining a stable heat output. When the user lifts the pen from the work surface, it automatically stops heating and enters a `cooldown` state. A `fault` state is also included to handle potential issues like overheating. A simple User Interface Panel with an LED display allows the operator to select modes and adjust the temperature settings for the task at hand.
