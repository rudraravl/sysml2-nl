This is a follower plate pumping subsystem designed for accurately dispensing high-viscosity fluids, such as grease, sealants, or adhesives, from a bulk reservoir.

The core of the system is a positive displacement pump that uses a linear actuator to drive its operation. Inside a pump cylinder, a precisely-fitted follower plate with a seal moves back and forth. This plate sits directly on top of the fluid in the reservoir, pressing down to prevent air pockets and ensure a consistent, cavity-free supply into the pump.

The pumping action is a two-stroke cycle. During the intake stroke, the actuator retracts the follower plate, creating a vacuum that draws fluid from the reservoir into the cylinder through an inlet check valve. On the discharge stroke, the actuator pushes the plate forward, pressurizing the fluid, closing the inlet valve, and forcing the fluid out through an outlet check valve into a discharge manifold.

The entire subsystem is managed by a pump controller. This controller receives a target flow rate and uses feedback from pressure and flow sensors located at the discharge manifold to precisely regulate the pump's speed. It adjusts the velocity of the linear actuator to ensure the system meets the required output pressure and flow. The system operates through various states, including idle, priming, active pumping, and a fault state, and responds to start and stop commands. It is designed to handle fluids within specific viscosity and temperature ranges to ensure reliable performance.
