This model describes a physical vehicle where mass is a critical design parameter. The central requirement for this vehicle is that its total mass must be less than or equal to 2500 kilograms.

The model also defines a formal test procedure to verify whether a specific vehicle meets this weight requirement. This verification process consists of three distinct steps:

1.  **Data Collection:** The actual mass of the vehicle under test is physically measured.
2.  **Data Processing:** The raw measurement from the previous step is processed, which could involve calibration or other adjustments to get a final, accurate mass value.
3.  **Evaluation:** The final processed mass is compared against the 2500 kg limit to determine if the vehicle passes or fails the requirement, resulting in a formal verdict.
