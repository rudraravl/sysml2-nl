This model describes a Robotic Tech Vest, an intelligent wearable exoskeleton designed to provide real-time postural support and reduce physical strain on the user. The vest is a comprehensive system built to augment human capability during daily activities or physical labor.

The core of the system is a network of components integrated into the vest. It features a sophisticated sensor array, with five sensors positioned along the spine and two at the shoulders, to continuously monitor the user's posture by measuring spinal and shoulder alignment. This data is processed by a central control unit running an adaptive posture control algorithm.

When the controller detects postural deviation, it activates a series of powerful exoskeleton actuators located at the spine (five), shoulders (two), and hips (two). These actuators apply precise torque to the user's joints, gently guiding them back into a correct and ergonomically sound posture. To keep the user informed, the vest also includes six haptic feedback modules that can generate vibrations at specific locations, alerting the user to postural changes or system actions.

The entire system is powered by a rechargeable battery module, designed for at least 8 hours of continuous operation. The vest operates through several states, including a standby mode, an active monitoring and analysis mode, a support/assisting mode, and a low-power mode to conserve energy when the battery is running low. Key design considerations include a rapid response time (under 100 milliseconds from detection to support) and built-in safety limits on the actuator force to prevent any risk of injury to the user.
