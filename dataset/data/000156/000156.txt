This is a model of a car, structured as a composite assembly of individual parts. Its primary purpose is to calculate the vehicle's total mass by "rolling up" the weight of its various components.

The system defines a car as a main structural component (like a chassis and body) that has its own base mass. This car body is identified by a unique Vehicle Identification Number (VIN). It is designed to be assembled with a collection of other distinct parts, such as an engine and a transmission. Each of these individual car parts has its own specified mass and is tracked with a unique serial number.

The total mass of the finished car is determined by adding the base mass of the car's body to the individual masses of all its installed components. This allows for an accurate calculation of the vehicle's final weight based on its specific configuration.
