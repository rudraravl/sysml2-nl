This is a comprehensive robotic system designed for autonomous operation in indoor environments. The core of the system is a versatile Personal Robot capable of performing a variety of tasks such as delivery, cleaning, surveillance, and general assistance. The entire system includes the robot, a dedicated charging station it uses to replenish its power, and a task dispatcher that assigns jobs and receives status reports.

The Personal Robot is a complex machine built from several integrated subsystems. A central control system acts as its brain, managing its operational state and coordinating the other components. For sensing the world, it uses a perception system with six sensors that provide a 360-degree awareness, detecting obstacles up to 10 meters away and gathering data about its environment. This information feeds into its advanced navigation system, which allows the robot to calculate paths and move autonomously to precise 3D locations.

A dedicated task execution system enables the robot to perform the specific actions required by its assigned job. To power all this, a power management system monitors the onboard battery. When the charge gets low, the robot is smart enough to stop its current task, navigate back to its charging station, and recharge itself automatically.

The robot operates through different modes depending on its situation. It typically waits in a **standby** mode. When a task is assigned, it becomes **active**, proceeding through a sequence of planning its route, navigating to the location, executing the task, and reporting the result. If the battery is low, it enters a **charging** mode. The system is also designed for resilience; if a problem is detected, it enters a **fault** mode where it attempts to diagnose the issue and, if possible, recover automatically.

This system is designed with specific safety and performance goals in mind, such as avoiding collisions in human-occupied spaces, operating for at least six hours on a single charge, and successfully completing its assigned tasks with high reliability and accuracy.
