This model describes a test environment and process for verifying the capabilities of a mining frigate.  The frigate itself is the system under test (SUT), and it's subjected to various tests to ensure it meets its design requirements.  The test environment includes a simulated space environment capable of reproducing different levels of threat (from high-security, safe zones to low-security, dangerous zones), a data logger to record performance metrics, and a ship engineer to oversee the testing process.

The mining frigate's performance is verified through several tests:  ore extraction efficiency, cargo capacity, survivability (shield strength under attack), drone deployment capacity, and threat detection range. Each test involves the frigate performing a specific action (e.g., mining ore, deploying drones), collecting data on its performance, and then having that data evaluated against pre-defined requirements.  The results of each test provide a pass/fail verdict indicating whether the frigate meets the specified criteria in different security zones (high-security and low-security environments are explicitly mentioned).  The entire process aims to validate that the mining frigate operates as expected across a range of operational conditions.
