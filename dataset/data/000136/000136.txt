This model describes the basic operational lifecycle of a vehicle's power or ignition system. It defines how the vehicle transitions between its fundamental states of being off, in the process of starting, and fully running.

The system begins in an 'off' state, where it is completely powered down. When it receives a 'start' signal, such as a key turn or a button press, it enters a temporary 'starting' state. This phase represents the process of the engine cranking or the primary systems activating.

Once the startup process is complete and an internal confirmation signal is received, the system transitions to the 'on' state. In this state, the vehicle is fully operational and ready for use. Finally, when an 'off' signal is given, the vehicle shuts down and returns to the 'off' state, ready to begin the cycle again.
