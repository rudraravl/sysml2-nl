This model represents the high-level design specifications for a vehicle, such as a car or truck. The system is defined by its core components and critical performance requirements.

The vehicle itself is subject to specific weight constraints, with defined limits for its mass when both empty and fully loaded. A key component of the vehicle is its engine, which is responsible for generating the torque (rotational force) necessary for propulsion. The engine delivers this power to the vehicle's transmission system through a dedicated physical interface, described as a clutch port. The design also includes specific requirements that govern the engine's ability to produce torque and the characteristics of its power-delivery interface.
