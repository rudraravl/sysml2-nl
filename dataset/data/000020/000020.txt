This model describes a physical battery unit, specifically the LEMS-250W model's housing.

This component has a complex, detailed external shell, the precise geometry of which is defined in an external 3D CAD (STEP) file. For purposes like system layout or high-level planning, the battery unit is also represented by a simple bounding box. This box defines the overall space the unit occupies, measuring 140 mm in length, 148 mm in width, and 90 mm in height.
