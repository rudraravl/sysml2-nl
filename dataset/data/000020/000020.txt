This is a mining frigate, a spaceship designed for autonomous asteroid mining operations.  The frigate has a modular design, allowing for customization based on mission needs.  Its core systems handle essential functions, while configurable modules like mining lasers (one or two can be installed), a shield generator, and a propulsion system determine its capabilities. The ship also features a sensor array for detecting asteroids and threats, and an optional cargo hold expander for increased ore storage. A capacitor booster can be added to enhance energy reserves.  For defense, it can be equipped with an arbitrary number of defense turrets. The frigate can deploy up to five drones to assist with mining or other tasks. It operates in various states: packaged (for transport), parked (in a station for maintenance), docked (in a station for resupply or repairs), in-grid (operating in an asteroid field), and on warp (traveling between locations).  The frigate communicates with stations and other vessels via docking and control ports, and possesses a dedicated port for managing its drones. The frigate can execute a variety of commands, including initiating mining, scanning for threats, deploying drones, warping to a new location, docking, and transferring mined ore to a station.  Its navigation and propulsion systems allow it to travel through space, adjust its course, and navigate to specified destinations.  The ship also includes systems for verifying cargo transfers and resupply actions.
