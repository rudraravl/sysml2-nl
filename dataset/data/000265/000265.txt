This model describes a commercial space mining fleet operated by a corporation. The fleet's primary mission is to extract valuable ore from asteroid belts and hazardous wormholes, process it, and transport it to stations or player-owned structures for sale or further refinement.

The core of the operation consists of specialized mining frigates. These ships are equipped with mining lasers for ore extraction, cargo holds for storage, and in some cases, onboard refining capabilities. A critical feature of these frigates is their self-defense systems, which include sensor arrays to detect hostile ships and defensive armaments like drones and turrets to neutralize threats.

The entire operation is managed by a central command that coordinates the fleet. This command center is responsible for dispatching frigates to various regions of space, which are categorized by their security level and risk (from high-security to dangerous null-security and wormhole space). Central command also oversees fleet maintenance and logistics, ensuring that frigates are resupplied with fuel and life support before being redeployed.

The operational workflow follows a continuous cycle: deployment to a mining site, extraction of ore, transport back to a base, and resupply. The entire system is driven by clear business objectives: maximizing profitability by efficiently harvesting valuable resources, ensuring the safety of pilots and ships from hostile encounters, and maintaining a sustainable and uninterrupted flow of materials. To this end, the corporation has a strict performance requirement to generate a minimum profit of 25,000 ISK per hour to provide sufficient incentive for its pilots.
