This model represents the physical structure of a car, specifically designed to track and calculate its total mass.

The car is defined as a composite assembly made up of various individual parts. Every component, from the main chassis to smaller pieces, is treated as an object with its own distinct mass. Each part is tracked with a unique serial number, while the car as a whole is identified by a Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).

The system's main purpose is to perform a "mass rollup." This means it calculates the vehicle's final, total mass by automatically summing the base mass of the car's body and chassis with the individual masses of all its installed components, such as the engine and transmission.
