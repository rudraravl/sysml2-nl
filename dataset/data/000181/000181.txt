This system represents a configurable, multi-cylinder engine, likely for automotive or industrial applications. The engine's fundamental design is based on a modular architecture composed of two or more cylinders.

The system is designed to be highly customizable, offering specific choices for its core configuration. A user can select the engine's overall size by choosing between two primary variants: a 4-cylinder version or a 6-cylinder version.

Further customization is available for the engine's internal dimensions. The cylinders themselves can be specified with either a small 70 mm diameter or a larger 100 mm diameter, which would directly influence the engine's displacement and performance characteristics.
