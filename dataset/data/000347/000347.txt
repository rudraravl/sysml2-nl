This model describes a family of vehicle configurations based on a common design template. The system specifies a default vehicle as a lightweight model with a mass of 1000.1 units and powered by a 3-cylinder engine.

The second variant is a further specialization of that 4-cylinder model, upgrading it to a more powerful 6-cylinder engine with a mass of 2.1 units. A unique feature of this 6-cylinder vehicle is that it contains an additional, distinct component referred to as an "experiment." This experimental part is characterized as having a single cylinder, suggesting it could be a small auxiliary power unit, a test engine, or another piece of specialized equipment integrated within the main vehicle.
