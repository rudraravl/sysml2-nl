This model represents a complete 2024 Suzuki GSX-S motorcycle, a high-performance vehicle system built around a central, high-tensile steel alloy frame. The system is designed to provide safe, stable, and responsive transportation for a single rider.

The core of the vehicle is its frame, which serves as the structural backbone. It connects all major components and is engineered for specific torsional stiffness and a maximum load capacity of 195 kg, while keeping its own mass low at just over 14 kg to optimize handling.

The motorcycle is powered by a 749cc powertrain (engine) mounted directly to the frame. This engine generates power and torque, which is transmitted to the rear wheel to propel the vehicle. The system is designed to manage and dampen vibrations from the engine to ensure a comfortable ride.

Its suspension system is composed of separate front and rear units. The front suspension consists of forks that connect the front wheel to the frame, absorbing impacts and allowing for steering. The rear suspension uses a swingarm and shock absorber to connect the rear wheel to the frame. Together, they manage the vehicle's load distribution and maintain tire contact with the road for stability and control.

The rider interacts with the motorcycle through a rider station, which includes the seat, handlebars, and footpegs. From here, the rider provides commands like throttle, braking, and steering inputs. The system processes these commands to control the motorcycle's movement and provides feedback forces to the rider.

The motorcycle operates through a clear sequence of states, starting from being `parked`. It then transitions to `starting` as the systems initialize, and then into a `moving` state upon rider command. The moving state is further broken down into `accelerating`, `cruising`, and `decelerating`. Finally, it enters a `stopping` state before returning to `parked`, completing its operational cycle.
