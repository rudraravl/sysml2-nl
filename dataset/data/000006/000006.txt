This is a forest fire observation drone designed for aerial surveillance and monitoring of wildfires.  The drone uses a quadcopter design with four rotors, each driven by an independent engine, alternating clockwise and counter-clockwise rotation for stability.  A central body houses the core components, including the flight control unit, which receives battery status information from a power management module. This module manages power distribution to the engines and monitors the status of a long-distance battery via a charger. The drone is equipped with a comprehensive sensor suite including a GPS module for navigation, an inertial measurement unit (IMU) for orientation and stabilization, a barometer for altitude control, and a camera for visual observation of the fire.  It also has an obstacle avoidance sensor to enhance safety during flight.  Communication with a ground control station is facilitated by a radio transmitter and receiver, using an antenna for signal transmission.  The drone's landing gear allows for safe takeoffs and landings, and the entire system is designed for repeated use in wildfire monitoring operations.
