This model describes a specific, individual vehicle during its assembly process. The vehicle is a particular model designed with dedicated positions for at least a left front wheel and a right front wheel.

The model captures two distinct moments in time to show how the vehicle is put together. In the first stage, a specific type of wheel is installed in the vehicle's left-front position. Following that, in a second stage, another wheel of the same type is mounted in the right-front position.

In essence, the system being represented is this single vehicle as its state changes from being partially assembled (with only one front wheel on) to being more complete (with both front wheels attached).
