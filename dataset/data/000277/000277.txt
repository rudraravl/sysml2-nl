This model describes a configurable Venture-class mining frigate, a type of spaceship designed for asteroid mining operations. The frigate is built around a central, fixed hull that provides the ship's core structure, defenses (shield, armor, and hull integrity), and essential systems. The hull itself includes a dedicated ore hold for storing mined materials, a capacitor for power, and integrated propulsion systems for both standard and faster-than-light (warp) travel.

The key feature of this frigate is its modularity. The hull is equipped with a set of standardized "slots" (high, medium, and low power) that allow a pilot to install a variety of commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) modules to customize its capabilities.

The typical configuration includes:
*   **Mining Lasers:** Two dedicated mining lasers are fitted into the high-power slots, serving as the primary equipment for extracting ore from asteroids.
*   **Propulsion Module:** An optional afterburner can be installed in a medium slot to provide a significant, short-term speed boost for maneuvering or escaping danger.
*   **Shield Module:** A shield extender can be fitted to another medium slot to increase the ship's defensive capacity.
*   **Capacitor Module:** An optional capacitor battery can be added to enhance the ship's available power reserves, allowing modules to run for longer periods.
*   **Sensor Module:** A survey scanner can be equipped to analyze asteroids from a distance, allowing the pilot to identify the most valuable resources efficiently.
*   **Defensive Modules:** Up to two warp core stabilizers can be installed in the low-power slots. These are crucial defensive systems that help the frigate escape if it is being targeted by hostile ships.

In operation, the frigate's integrated systems manage core functions like docking and undocking from stations, plotting and executing travel courses, and transferring ore from its hold. The pilot then uses the installed modules to perform specific tasks like activating the mining lasers, engaging the afterburner, or scanning asteroids. This modular design allows the frigate to be adapted for different scenarios, such as prioritizing defensive capabilities for mining in dangerous territory or maximizing efficiency in secure space.
