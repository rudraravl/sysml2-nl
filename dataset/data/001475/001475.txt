This model represents a comprehensive Conference Management System designed to manage the entire lifecycle of a large academic conference, specifically the International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD). The system facilitates all key processes from the initial call for papers to the final publication of the proceedings, ensuring a well-organized and high-quality academic event.

The system is composed of five major, interconnected components:

1.  **Paper Submission System:** This is the entry point for researchers and authors. They use it to submit their research papers, which include details like the title, authors, abstract, and keywords. The system automatically validates that the submission meets formatting requirements and then sends an acknowledgement to the author. It keeps a central database of all submitted papers and enforces a strict submission deadline.

2.  **Peer Review System:** Once papers are submitted, this component manages the critical quality control process. It assigns each paper to a set of qualified reviewers (typically three) from a pool of experts. These reviewers evaluate the paper based on its technical merit, novelty, and presentation, providing scores and comments. The system then aggregates these independent reviews to make a final decision: accept, reject, or request revisions. This process is governed by a review deadline and is configured to maintain a high academic standard by targeting a specific acceptance rate (e.g., 25%).

3.  **Registration System:** This component handles all aspects of attendee management. Participants, whether they are students, academics, or industry professionals, use this system to register for the conference. It processes payments, checks against the maximum attendee capacity of the venue, and sends out registration confirmations. If the conference is full, it can manage a waitlist. It also handles different registration deadlines, such as for early-bird discounts.

4.  **Session Scheduler:** After the review process is complete, this system takes all the accepted papers and organizes them into a coherent conference program. It groups papers by topic, assigns them to specific time slots and rooms, and organizes them into parallel tracks over the several days of the conference. It also schedules other event types like keynotes, panels, and workshops. The final output is a detailed session schedule for all attendees.

5.  **Publication System:** This final component is responsible for creating the official conference proceedings. It collects all the accepted and finalized papers, compiles them into a single, professionally formatted volume, and assigns permanent identifiers (like a DOI) to each paper. The completed proceedings are then uploaded to a digital library for long-term access and dissemination to the wider academic community.

Together, these components create a seamless workflow. A paper moves from the submission system to the review system. If accepted, it is simultaneously sent to the scheduler to be placed in the program and to the publication system to be included in the proceedings. The registration and scheduling systems work together to provide attendees with a clear and organized conference experience.
