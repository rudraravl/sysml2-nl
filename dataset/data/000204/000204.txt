This system represents a standard vehicle and trailer towing setup, composed of a towing vehicle and a separate trailer.

The vehicle is built on a frame and is equipped with a hitch ball, which serves as the mounting point for towing. The trailer also has its own frame, which features a trailer coupler on its front end.

The core of the system is the physical trailer hitch connection formed between these two components. This is achieved when the trailer's coupler is lowered and secured onto the vehicle's hitch ball. This connection is temporary, allowing the system to exist in either a connected state for towing or a disconnected state.

The system's operation is defined by two sequential actions: first, the trailer is connected to the vehicle to create the complete towing assembly. Later, the trailer is disconnected, separating the two components again.
