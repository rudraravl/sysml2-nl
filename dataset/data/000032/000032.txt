This SysML model describes a mining frigate, a spacecraft designed for asteroid mining and defense.  The frigate operates autonomously but is controlled and monitored by a pilot pod.  Its primary function is to locate and mine asteroids, extracting ore and transporting it back to a space station for processing. This involves navigating to asteroid fields, deploying mining lasers to extract ore, and then transferring the mined ore to internal storage.

The frigate also possesses a robust defense system.  It's equipped with sensor arrays to detect threats, such as hostile ships, and engages defense systems including turrets and deployable drones to neutralize these threats.  The drones are versatile, capable of both defensive and potentially auxiliary mining tasks, depending on the pilot's instructions.

The frigate periodically needs to offload its mined ore and resupply its fuel and life support systems at a space station. This process involves docking with the station, transferring cargo, and receiving necessary supplies.  The entire process, from navigation and mining to defense and resupply, is managed through a sophisticated control system that allows for both autonomous operation and manual overrides by the pilot pod as needed.  The system includes comprehensive error handling and alternate procedures to account for equipment malfunctions and unexpected events.
