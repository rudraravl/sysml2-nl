This system represents a single-use, protective medical gown designed for healthcare workers. Its primary purpose is to create a physical barrier that prevents the transfer of contaminants, such as body fluids and pathogens, between patients and medical staff during clinical procedures.

The gown is constructed from several key components made of a specialized fabric layer. This fabric is characterized by its barrier efficiency against contaminants, resistance to fluid penetration under pressure, and breathability for user comfort. The gown consists of:
*   **A Front and Back Torso Panel:** These panels provide the main coverage for the wearer's body, overlapping at the back to ensure a complete wrap-around shield. The front panel, facing the highest risk of exposure, has a higher fluid resistance.
*   **Two Full-Length Sleeves:** These protect the wearer's arms and are made of the same protective fabric.
*   **Elastic Cuffs:** Located at the end of each sleeve, these provide a snug and secure fit around the wrists, preventing gaps between the gown and gloves.
*   **Tie Closures:** The gown is secured using simple but strong tie strings at the neck and waist to ensure it remains properly in place during use.

The system defines four distinct protection levels (Level 1 to 4), allowing for the selection of an appropriate gown based on the risk of exposure, from minimal-risk situations like basic care to high-risk, fluid-intensive surgical procedures.

The gown follows a strict single-use lifecycle to prevent cross-contamination. It begins in a sterile, `packaged` state. Following a specific procedure, it is `donned` (put on) by the user and is considered `in use` during a medical procedure. If it comes into contact with contaminants, its barrier function is active. The gown's state can change to `contaminated` if its protective barrier is breached or saturated. After the procedure, a careful `doffing` (removal) process is followed, which involves rolling the contaminated exterior inward before the gown is safely `disposed` of in a biohazard container.
