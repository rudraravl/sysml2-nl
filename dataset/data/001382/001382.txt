This model represents a household clothes dryer, a system designed to automatically dry wet laundry by circulating heated air through a tumbling drum. Its core function is to remove moisture from fabrics until they reach a specific dryness level set by the user.

The dryer is composed of several key mechanical and electrical components that work together. A large, rotating **drum** holds the wet clothes. A **drive motor** provides the power to turn this drum, tumbling the laundry to ensure all items are evenly exposed to the hot air.

The air circulation system begins with a **blower**, or fan, which draws in ambient air from the room. This air is then forced across an electric **heating element**, which raises its temperature to the level required by the selected drying cycle. The resulting hot, dry air is ducted into the rotating drum. As this air circulates through the tumbling clothes, it absorbs moisture. The now warm, humid air is then pulled out of the drum, passed through a **lint filter** to capture fabric particles, and finally vented outside the house through an **exhaust duct**.

The entire operation is managed by a central **control module**, which acts as the dryer's brain. This controller receives input from the user via a **control panel**, where different drying cycles (like cotton or delicates) can be selected. During the cycle, the controller continuously monitors data from two critical sensors: a **temperature sensor** to regulate the heating element and prevent overheating, and a **moisture sensor** that directly measures the dampness of the clothes.

Based on the sensor readings, the control module intelligently manages the cycle. When the moisture sensor indicates that the laundry has reached the target dryness, the controller deactivates the heating element and enters a cool-down phase. During this phase, the drum continues to tumble while the blower circulates unheated, room-temperature air to cool the clothes and reduce wrinkling before the cycle ends. The system also includes a safety mechanism in the **door**, which ensures the dryer will not operate unless the door is securely closed and will stop immediately if it's opened mid-cycle.
