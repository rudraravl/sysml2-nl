This model represents a manufacturing company's order fulfillment process.  The company, the "Manufacturer," receives inquiries from "Customers" for custom-made products.  These inquiries lead to order quotations, which, once accepted, become order confirmations.  The Manufacturer's "Sales" department manages this communication.

The "ProductionScheduling" department receives sales orders and generates "ProductOrders" specifying the required materials and production steps.  These orders are sent to the "Production" department, which uses a "ProductionControl" system to manage the workflow.

The production process itself consists of an "AngleProductionLine" that processes raw materials through several stages: heating, bending, cutting, drilling, grinding, and polishing. Each stage has specific parameters (temperature, angle, length, etc.) that determine the final product's characteristics.  The "Production" department also handles material ordering through the "MaterialOrderOut" port.

The required raw materials are procured by the "Procurement" department, which generates "PurchaseOrders" for "Suppliers."  The "Storage" department manages the incoming materials (from suppliers) and outgoing materials (to the production line).  Finished products are then sent to the "Dispatch" department for shipping to the customer.  "ShippingConfirmations" are sent to the customer once the shipment is complete.  The entire process tracks orders from initial inquiry to final shipment, using different order types (sales order, purchase order, etc.) and manages the flow of materials and products within the factory.
