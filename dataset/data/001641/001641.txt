This model represents a Belkin Wemo Smart Plug, a device that allows users to remotely control and monitor household appliances over a WiFi network. The smart plug acts as an intermediary, plugging into a standard wall outlet and providing a controllable power socket for another device, such as a lamp or fan.

The core of the system is a control processor that acts as the device's brain. It receives commands—such as "turn on," "turn off," or "set a schedule"—from a user's mobile app or voice assistant via an integrated WiFi module. Based on these commands, the processor sends a signal to an internal relay switch. This relay is a physical switch that opens or closes the electrical circuit, either cutting off or allowing AC power to flow to the appliance plugged into it.

In addition to control, the smart plug features a built-in power meter. This component continuously measures the voltage and current being drawn by the connected appliance to calculate real-time power consumption and track total energy usage over time. This information is packaged into status reports and sent back over WiFi, allowing the user to monitor their energy habits. An LED indicator on the plug's exterior provides a visual cue of its current state, such as whether it's on, off, or connecting to the network.
