This model describes a therapeutic weighted blanket system designed to provide calming deep pressure stimulation for a user. Its primary purpose is to reduce stress and anxiety, promoting relaxation and a sense of comfort.

The blanket is constructed with a dual-fabric design, featuring a breathable outer layer made of cotton and an inner layer of polyester. Internally, the blanket is sewn into a grid of many small, square compartments. Each of these compartments is filled with a precise amount of weighted material—in this case, glass beads—to ensure that the blanket's total weight is distributed uniformly across its entire surface. This compartmentalized grid is essential for applying consistent, gentle pressure to the user's body.

The system is designed with specific therapeutic goals in mind. Its total weight is carefully calibrated to be a safe and effective percentage (approximately 10%) of the user's body mass. The materials are chosen not only for comfort but also for properties like breathability to prevent overheating during use.

The blanket operates through several distinct states in its lifecycle. It can be stored, made ready for use, be actively providing therapy when a person is under it, or be placed in a maintenance mode for cleaning. The core function, providing a therapeutic effect, is active when the blanket is in use, continuously applying deep pressure to achieve the desired calming outcome for the user.
