This is an autonomous uncrewed ground vehicle (UGV) system designed for executing missions like reconnaissance without direct human intervention. The vehicle is a robust, 150 kg platform engineered to travel up to 50 km on a single charge with a top speed of 15 m/s.

At its core, the UGV is powered by a high-capacity electric battery pack. This power is supplied to a propulsion system consisting of an electric motor to control throttle and a dedicated actuator for steering, allowing it to navigate complex terrain.

To perceive its environment and navigate, the vehicle is equipped with a comprehensive sensor suite. This includes a Lidar sensor for 3D mapping and real-time obstacle detection up to 50 meters away, a high-definition camera for visual data collection, and a GPS receiver for precise global positioning.

The vehicle's "brain" is a combination of a navigation computer and an autonomous controller. The navigation computer processes incoming data from the sensor suite and the mission plan to calculate paths, generating specific throttle and steering commands. The high-level autonomous controller manages the vehicle's overall state and makes critical decisions.

Operationally, the vehicle functions through different states. It begins in standby, awaiting a mission plan, which consists of a series of GPS waypoints. Once a mission is received, it transitions to a navigating state, moving from one waypoint to the next. If the Lidar detects an obstacle, the system enters an obstacle avoidance state to maneuver safely around it before resuming navigation. Upon reaching the final waypoint, it completes its mission and autonomously returns to base. The system also includes emergency states for safe shutdown.

Throughout its operation, a communication module allows the vehicle to transmit telemetry packets—containing its status, position, and sensor data—back to a control station and receive new mission commands if needed. The entire system is designed to meet strict performance and safety requirements, including the ability to avoid obstacles, execute emergency stops, and return home automatically if its battery level becomes critical.
