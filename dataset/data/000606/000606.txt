This is a medical feeding tube system designed to automatically deliver liquid nutritional formula to a patient. The system ensures a controlled and safe administration of nutrition for individuals who cannot eat normally.

The core of the system is a precision pump mechanism that draws a specific nutritional formula from a reservoir, which is typically a bag or bottle. This formula is then pushed through a flexible delivery tube that connects directly to the patient.

A central control module, or the system's "brain," orchestrates the entire process. A healthcare provider uses a user interface to program the controller with a prescribed flow rate and the total volume of formula to be delivered. The controller then commands the pump to start, stop, and adjust its speed to maintain the correct delivery rate.

To ensure patient safety, the system is equipped with a suite of sensors that constantly monitor the delivery process.
*   A **flow rate sensor** verifies that the formula is moving at the correct speed.
*   A **pressure sensor** inside the tubing detects blockages or occlusions. If the pressure rises above a safe threshold, it indicates a problem.
*   An **air detector** checks for bubbles in the line to prevent air from being pumped into the patient.

The system operates through several distinct states. It starts in a **standby** mode. Before delivery begins, it enters a **priming** state to fill the tube with formula and purge any air. During the **delivering** state, the pump is active and the sensors are monitoring. A caregiver can temporarily **pause** the delivery, and the system will automatically enter an **alarmed** state and stop the pump if any of the sensors detect a dangerous condition like an occlusion or air in the line. Once the full prescribed volume has been administered, the system moves to a **completed** state.
