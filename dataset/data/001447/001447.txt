This model represents a smart, integrated home laundry system, designed to automate the entire process of washing and drying clothes. The system consists of two main appliances, a washing machine and a clothes dryer, which work in tandem under the management of a central controller.

The **Washing Machine** is a feature-rich unit equipped with a rotating drum, a motor to spin it, a water heater for temperature control, a detergent dispenser, and a pump for draining water. It connects to the home's water supply to fill the drum and to a drain to dispose of wastewater. The washer is capable of performing various wash cycles, such as normal, delicate, or heavy-duty, by precisely controlling water temperature, drum agitation speed, and cycle duration.

The **Clothes Dryer** component features a large rotating drum, a powerful heating element, and a blower fan to circulate hot air. A key feature is its integrated moisture sensor, which allows it to intelligently determine when clothes have reached the desired level of dryness and automatically end the cycle. This prevents over-drying and saves energy. The dryer also includes a temperature sensor to regulate heat levels, which can be set to low, medium, or high.

Both appliances are managed by a **Central Controller**, which is the "brain" of the smart system. This controller coordinates the entire laundry process, potentially enabling energy-saving features by managing when each high-power component (like heaters) is active to avoid overloading the home's electrical circuit.

The system as a whole operates through a clear lifecycle, starting in a standby mode, moving through washing and drying states, and returning to standby upon completion. It is designed to handle a full laundry cycle: the user loads the washer, which executes a complete wash, rinse, and spin sequence. After the laundry is transferred (manually or automatically), the dryer begins its cycle, heating and tumbling the clothes until the moisture sensor indicates the cycle is finished. The entire system is designed to be connected to standard household utilities, including electrical, water, and drainage systems.
