This model describes a Mining Frigate, a versatile industrial spaceship designed for excavating and transporting valuable ore from asteroids in a space environment.

Its primary function is mining, which is performed using specialized, activatable mining lasers. This mining process is automated to be efficient; the system first checks if an asteroid has been depleted of resources. If it has, a report is sent to the pilot, but if ore is present, the extraction process begins.

For mobility, the frigate is equipped with an advanced propulsion system that allows it to navigate through space and perform "warp" jumps for faster-than-light travel to distant destinations. It is also capable of docking with and undocking from space stations, which serve as hubs for transferring cargo, resupplying, and repairs.

The ship features a dedicated cargo hold for storing mined ore and includes a system for transferring that ore to a station or another vessel. All of its onboard systems are powered by a central energy unit, referred to as a capacitor, which must be actively managed and recharged to keep the ship operational.

To ensure its survival in potentially hostile space, the frigate is equipped with a self-defense system. This system first scans the immediate surroundings to detect potential threats and then automatically engages its defensive measures to withstand any incoming damage. The ship's hull is also designed to support various modules, allowing it to be customized with different types of equipment for mining, defense, or other support roles.
