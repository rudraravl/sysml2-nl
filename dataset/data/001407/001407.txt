This is a model of the Domus Intelligent Home System, a comprehensive home automation system designed to autonomously manage a home's climate, lighting, security, and energy consumption. The system creates a responsive and efficient living environment by adapting to the presence and needs of its occupants.

The core of the system is a Central Controller that acts as the brain, coordinating the various subsystems. The home itself is divided into distinct zones, such as a living room, master bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom, each equipped with environmental and occupancy sensors. These sensors continuously monitor conditions like temperature, humidity, air quality, and whether a room is occupied.

Based on the data from these sensors, the Central Controller makes intelligent decisions. It sends commands to several integrated subsystems:

*   **Climate Control System:** This is the home's heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) system. It can automatically adjust the temperature to a pre-set target, but only in rooms that are currently occupied, saving energy by not heating or cooling empty spaces.
*   **Lighting System:** This system manages the home's dimmable lights across different zones. It can operate automatically, turning lights on when someone enters a room and off when they leave, or adjust intensity based on the time of day and natural light levels.
*   **Security System:** This subsystem provides safety and monitoring with a network of motion detectors, door sensors, and cameras. It can be set to different states like armed, disarmed, or monitoring, and its behavior changes based on whether the home is occupied, unoccupied, or if the residents are sleeping.
*   **Energy Management System:** This component constantly tracks the power consumption of the other subsystems. It provides data back to the central controller to help optimize operations for maximum energy efficiency, aiming to reduce consumption without sacrificing comfort or safety.

The entire system operates through different modes based on the occupants' activities, such as being 'active' at home, 'away', or sleeping in 'night mode'. For example, when the last person leaves, the system can automatically enter the 'away' state, lowering the thermostat, turning off all lights, and arming the security system. Upon arrival, it can return to an 'active' state, restoring a comfortable and welcoming environment.
