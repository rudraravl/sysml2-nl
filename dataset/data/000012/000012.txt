This SysML model describes a configurable internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicle.  The vehicle's core components include an engine (with 4 or 6 cylinders configurable), a transmission, a driveshaft, and an axle assembly (with front and rear axles, each having two wheels). The engine generates torque, which is amplified by the transmission, transferred through the driveshaft, and finally distributed to the wheels via the differential.  The vehicle's mass is calculated based on the mass of its constituent parts, including the fuel tank (with a specified fuel capacity). The model accounts for fuel consumption and allows for the simulation of vehicle dynamics in a straight-line scenario, calculating acceleration, velocity, and position over time based on engine power and vehicle mass.  The vehicle features a software controller that manages various vehicle states, including starting, operating, and shutdown, and monitors the vehicle's health, triggering maintenance alerts based on temperature sensors and scheduled maintenance intervals.  The model also includes requirements related to the vehicle’s mass, fuel economy, and engine performance, allowing for a check against these requirements based on simulation results.  Different configurations of the vehicle are possible, altering attributes like the engine's cylinder count and impacting overall vehicle mass and performance characteristics.
