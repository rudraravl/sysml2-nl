This model describes a medical device known as a Vacuum Bell system, which is used for the non-invasive treatment of chest wall deformities. The system's primary function is to create a controlled vacuum over the chest area to apply a gentle, sustained suction force. This force gradually lifts and remodels the chest tissue over repeated treatment sessions.

The system is composed of several key components working together. At its core is the **Bell Chamber**, a cup-like device that is placed on the patient's chest to form an airtight seal. A **Vacuum Pump** is connected to this chamber and is responsible for removing air to create the necessary negative pressure.

To ensure the treatment is both safe and effective, the vacuum level is precisely managed. A **Pressure Sensor** continuously monitors the pressure inside the bell and sends this data to a central **Control Unit**. This controller then adjusts the pump's operation to maintain the exact therapeutic pressure required for the session. A **Pressure Regulator** further refines the vacuum, ensuring its stability.

Safety is a critical aspect of the design. The system incorporates an automatic **Safety Valve** that constantly monitors the vacuum level and will instantly release the pressure if it exceeds a safe threshold. Additionally, a separate **Release Valve** is used to gradually and comfortably release the vacuum at the end of a normal treatment session or to rapidly release it in an emergency. The entire treatment, including its duration and pressure profile, is managed by the Control Unit to provide a consistent and safe therapeutic experience.
