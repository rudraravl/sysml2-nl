These functions can be linked together into **functional chains** to show how a complex task is accomplished, including how data, energy, or other items flow between them.

*   **Requirements and Verification:** A critical part of the framework is the ability to formally capture **requirements** and provide **justifications** for them. It also provides a structure for defining **verification activities** and scenarios that prove these requirements have been met, ensuring the final system performs as designed.
