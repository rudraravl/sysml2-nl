This is a medical device, specifically a rigid mediastinoscope system, used for performing minimally invasive diagnostic procedures inside the chest cavity. Its primary purpose is to allow a surgeon to visually examine the mediastinum—the area between the lungs—and to take tissue samples (biopsies) from structures like lymph nodes or masses for analysis.

The system consists of several integrated components working together. The main instrument is a rigid `Insertion Tube` that is inserted into the patient. Housed within this tube is an `Optical System` with a lens that captures live video from inside the body and a `Light Source` that provides high-intensity, daylight-colored illumination of the surgical area.

The raw video captured by the optical system is sent to a `Video Processor`, which enhances the image in real-time to high-definition quality. This processed video feed is then displayed on a large screen at the surgeon's `Control Console`. From this console, the surgeon can control the system's power and navigation.

A key feature of the system is the integrated `Biopsy Instrument`, which runs through a channel in the main tube. When the surgeon identifies a suspicious area on the video feed, they can activate this instrument to precisely collect a tissue sample. The system is designed to handle the entire examination workflow, from powering on, inserting the scope, performing the visual examination and biopsy, to finally withdrawing the instrument and shutting down.
