This model represents the FarmWise Autonomous Weeder, a robotic system designed for precision weed control in agricultural fields. Its primary purpose is to autonomously navigate crop rows, identify unwanted weeds using an advanced computer vision system, and mechanically remove them without using chemical herbicides, all while carefully avoiding damage to the valuable crop plants.

The weeder is a self-contained, battery-powered vehicle built around a central control computer that orchestrates its operations. Its core components work together in a continuous cycle:

*   A central **Battery Pack** provides electrical power to all subsystems. The system is designed for high operational autonomy, capable of working for at least 8 hours on a single charge before needing to return to base.

*   As the robot moves along crop rows using its **Drive System**, a high-resolution **Camera System** continuously captures images of the plants below.

*   These images are sent to an onboard **Vision Processor**. This component uses AI to analyze the images in real-time, distinguishing weeds from crops and pinpointing their exact GPS coordinates with high accuracy.

*   A high-precision **GPS Navigation System** manages the robot's location and movement. Once the vision system identifies a weed, the control computer uses the GPS system to guide the robot to the exact location of the target.

*   When positioned over a weed, a **Mechanical Weeder** tool is deployed. This physical tool engages with the soil to uproot or destroy the weed, then retracts. The process is designed to be highly targeted to ensure a crop safety rate of over 98%.

The entire system operates as a cohesive unit, methodically scanning, detecting, navigating, and weeding its way through a field. It continuously monitors its battery life and is programmed to automatically return to a charging station when power is low.
