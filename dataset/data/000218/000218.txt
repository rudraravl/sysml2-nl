This model represents a simplified vehicle, focusing on the breakdown of its mass for engineering analysis. The vehicle is composed of four main systems: an engine, a transmission, a front axle assembly, and a rear axle assembly.

The core purpose of this model is to calculate the vehicle's total mass by adding together the individual masses of these four key components. It's structured to support a formal mass analysis, where the weight of the complete vehicle is determined by this "roll-up" of its parts. This kind of analysis is a fundamental step in automotive design to understand and predict vehicle performance, handling, and fuel efficiency.
