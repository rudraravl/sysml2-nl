This model describes the Ionaco, an advanced air purification and ionization system designed to improve indoor environmental quality. The system is housed in a single unit that draws in ambient air, purifies it through a multi-stage process, and then releases treated, ionized air back into the room.

The process begins when an **intake fan** pulls surrounding air into the unit. This air first passes through a **HEPA pre-filter**, which is designed to capture physical particulates like dust, pollen, and other allergens.

Once filtered, the clean air flows into the core **ionization module**. This component uses a high-voltage electrode to generate a high concentration of ions, which are released into the air stream to neutralize airborne contaminants and further improve air quality.

The system's operation is managed by a central **control processor**. This processor receives continuous feedback from two key sensors: an **air quality sensor** that measures the general purity of the air and an **ion sensor** that specifically monitors the concentration of ions in the treated air. Based on these readings and the selected operating mode, the controller intelligently adjusts the intensity of the ionization module and the speed of the fans.

A dedicated **power management unit** takes standard electrical power from an outlet and safely distributes it to all internal components, including the fans, sensors, ionization module, and the control processor, ensuring efficient and stable operation. Finally, an oscillating **distribution fan** circulates the purified and ionized air throughout the room.

The Ionaco system operates in several distinct states. It can be powered off, in a low-power standby mode, or in active operation. The active mode offers several settings, including low, medium, and high manual levels, as well as a smart auto mode. In auto mode, the system uses its sensors to automatically adjust its performance to maintain optimal air quality without user intervention. It also includes a dedicated state for maintenance procedures.
