This is an Intelligent Home Control System designed to automatically manage a home's environment for comfort, energy efficiency, and security. The system operates by intelligently connecting a network of sensors to the home's climate, lighting, and security hardware.

At its core is a central controller that acts as the brain of the system. It continuously gathers real-time data from a suite of sensors placed throughout the home, including temperature, light, humidity, and occupancy sensors. This information gives the system a complete picture of the current environmental conditions and whether anyone is present.

Based on this sensor data and the homeowner's pre-set preferences (such as a preferred temperature or light level), the central controller makes decisions and sends commands to various actuators. These actuators are the physical components that control the home's functions: a climate actuator manages the heating and cooling system, a lighting actuator controls the intensity and status of lights in different zones, and a security actuator arms or disarms the alarm system.

The system's behavior changes dynamically based on occupancy. When it detects that someone is home, it enters a monitoring state, actively adjusting the temperature and lighting to maintain the user's desired comfort levels. When the home is empty and the user has enabled an "away" mode, the system can automatically arm the security system and switch to an energy-saving mode, reducing heating or cooling to save power. Users can interact with the system to set their preferences and view the overall system status, including current temperature, energy consumption, and security status.
