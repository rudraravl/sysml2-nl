This model describes a structured framework for conducting an investigation, test, or analysis on any given system. It's not a physical object, but rather a blueprint for a process, like a generic test plan or an experimental procedure.

The core of this framework is the "Case," which represents a specific scenario or evaluation. Any Case focuses on a particular "subject"—the system or item being tested or analyzed. The scenario also involves "actors," which are the people, external systems, or environmental elements that interact with the subject during the evaluation.

Every Case is driven by a clear "objective," which is a specific requirement that needs to be checked. The ultimate goal is to determine if the subject satisfies this objective. Upon completion, the Case produces a "result," which is the formal outcome of the investigation, indicating whether the objective was successfully met.

This framework is also designed to be hierarchical. A large, complex analysis can be broken down into a series of smaller, more manageable "subcases," allowing for a structured, step-by-step approach to evaluation.
