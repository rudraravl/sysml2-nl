This model represents a Bodok Seal Gas Connection System, a specialized mechanical interface used to create a safe, high-pressure, leak-tight seal between a medical gas cylinder and a pressure regulator. It is commonly used in medical and emergency settings for gases like oxygen.

The system's core component is the Bodok Seal Assembly, which consists of a deformable nylon washer held within a supportive brass retaining ring. This assembly is designed to be placed between the flat sealing face on the high-pressure Cylinder Valve Outlet (from the gas tank) and the corresponding sealing face of a Regulator Inlet. A large Connection Nut is then used to draw these two components together.

The primary function of the system is to establish a seal by controlled compression. As the connection nut is tightened to a specified torque, it forces the metal faces of the cylinder valve and regulator against the Bodok seal. This pressure deforms the resilient nylon washer, causing it to expand and fill any microscopic imperfections on the metal surfaces, thereby preventing high-pressure gas from escaping.

This connection is engineered to be highly reliable, designed to withstand service pressures up to 200 bar while maintaining an extremely low leak rate. A key feature is its reusability, allowing the seal to be used for multiple connection and disconnection cycles before needing replacement. The system operates through a distinct lifecycle, from being positioned and compressed to achieve a seal, to operating under pressure, and eventually degrading or failing over time. Performance is continuously monitored to ensure seal integrity is maintained for safe gas delivery.
