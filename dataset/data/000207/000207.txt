This model describes an automotive manufacturing process centered around an assembly line. The primary function of this system is to construct a complete vehicle through a specific, sequential set of actions.

The assembly process begins with a basic vehicle structure, into which a transmission is first installed. The resulting sub-assembly then moves to the next stage of the line, where an engine is added. This two-stage process culminates in a fully assembled vehicle.

The completed vehicle is defined by its core components, including the newly installed engine and transmission. Once assembled, these parts work together to enable the vehicle's fundamental capability of providing power for movement.
