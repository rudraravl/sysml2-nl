This SysML model describes a space mining operation conducted by a mining corporation.  The corporation uses a fleet of mining frigates, supported by a mother ship (a Rorqual), to extract ore from asteroid belts.  Each frigate is piloted remotely from a pilot pod, which acts as a central control point.  The pilot pod maintains situational awareness and issues commands to the frigates, including mining commands, warp commands (to move between asteroid belts and stations), and drone deployment commands.

The mining frigates themselves possess varying capabilities, including cargo capacity, warp speed, shield strength, and the ability to lock onto multiple asteroid targets simultaneously.  They also have the capacity to deploy drones for defensive purposes or other tasks.  The Rorqual provides additional support, offering significantly larger ore storage and compression capabilities which increase overall efficiency of the mining operation.

The asteroids within the belts contain varying amounts of ore, which is extracted at a rate dependent on the asteroid's ore content and the frigate's mining equipment.  Hostile ships may also be present in the asteroid belts, posing a threat to the mining operation.  The operation's success depends on the corporation's ability to efficiently locate, mine, and transport ore to nearby stations while mitigating risks from hostile encounters.  The pilot pods use drones to improve overall threat detection and response.  The effectiveness of the entire operation is measured by various key performance indicators (KPIs) tracked by the mining corporation, such as profit margins, fleet utilization, and operational uptime.  These KPIs help the corporation optimize its resource allocation and strategies to maximize profitability while minimizing losses.
