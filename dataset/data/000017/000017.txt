This model describes a complete system centered around a user operating a flashlight to illuminate an object. The system consists of three main parts: the user, the flashlight itself, and an external object or surface that gets illuminated.

The user is the one who initiates the action by sending an on or off command. The flashlight is a device that receives this command and, in response, produces a directed beam of light. The third component is the object or surface that the light is aimed at. This object receives the light from the flashlight and reflects it.

The entire process models the act of "illuminating a region": the user decides to turn on the light, the flashlight creates the light, and the light travels to an object, making it visible.
