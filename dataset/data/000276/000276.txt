This model represents a catalog of commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) modules for outfitting and customizing spaceships, particularly smaller vessels like frigates. These components are designed to be installed into a ship's standardized fitting slots to enhance or specialize its function.

The catalog includes a variety of components for different functions:
*   **Industrial Operations:** For mining, there is a **Mining Laser** used for extracting ore from asteroids and a **Survey Scanner** to assess the mineral content of asteroids from a distance.
*   **Propulsion:** An **Afterburner** module is available to provide a significant, temporary boost to the ship's sublight speed at the cost of high energy consumption.
*   **Defensive Systems:** To improve survivability, a **Shield Extender** increases the ship's total energy shield capacity. A **Warp Core Stabilizer** is also available, which protects the ship's faster-than-light drive from being disabled by enemy electronics, though it comes with a penalty to the ship's targeting systems.
*   **Power Management:** A **Capacitor Battery** can be fitted to increase the ship's onboard energy reserves and improve its recharge rate, allowing other modules to operate for longer.

These modules are designed to be installed into specific standardized slots on a ship's hull—categorized as high, medium, and low slots. Each component has defined mass and power consumption requirements, meaning a shipwright or pilot must balance the desired capabilities with the ship's overall mass limits and power grid capacity. Overall, this collection represents a system of interchangeable hardware that allows a spaceship to be configured for specialized roles like mining, exploration, or increased survivability.
