This model represents a simplified passenger vehicle, focusing on the categorization of its safety and security components. The vehicle is composed of three main assemblies: the interior, the body, and the wheels.

The vehicle's interior contains two front seats, each equipped with a seat belt. For security, it has an anti-theft alarm system. It also includes a driver's airbag as an optional safety feature.

The body assembly includes the main vehicle frame, a bumper, and a keyless entry system. The keyless entry provides secure access, while the bumper is a mandatory safety component designed for impact absorption.

The wheel assembly consists of two wheels and an anti-lock braking system. The anti-lock brakes are an optional safety feature designed to improve control during hard braking.

Overall, the design distinguishes between components that provide safety (protecting occupants from harm) and those that provide security (preventing theft or unauthorized access).
