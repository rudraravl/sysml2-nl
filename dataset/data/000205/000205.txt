This model represents a vehicle and trailer towing system.

The system is comprised of two main physical units: a towing vehicle and a separate trailer. The vehicle is equipped with a frame, onto which a hitch ball is mounted. The trailer has its own frame, which features a corresponding trailer coupler.

The primary function of this system is to physically link the vehicle and trailer for towing. This is achieved when the trailer's coupler is lowered and secured onto the vehicle's hitch ball, creating a strong, articulated connection. The system is also designed for the reverse operation, allowing the trailer to be disconnected from the vehicle by releasing the coupler from the hitch ball.
