This model describes a mining frigate, a type of spaceship used by a mining corporation for asteroid or space-based mineral extraction.  The frigate's effectiveness is analyzed through cause-and-effect relationships.  Improvements in various aspects of the frigate lead to increased operational efficiency and profitability.  For example,  increasing the mining rate results in a higher ore yield.  Larger cargo capacity reduces downtime. Stronger shields and improved threat detection enhance survivability and improve threat response, thus contributing to overall operational efficiency.  Similarly, improved drone operations and faster warp speeds also reduce downtime, further enhancing efficiency.  Finally, better fleet coordination also boosts operational efficiency. The combined effect of increased ore extraction efficiency and operational efficiency leads to maximized profitability for the mining corporation.  The model explores how multiple factors interrelate to impact the overall success of the mining operation.
