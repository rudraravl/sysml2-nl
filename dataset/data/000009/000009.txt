This system represents the relationship between a customer, the products they select, and their shopping cart within an online retail or e-commerce platform.

At its core, the system requires a customer to have an **Account**. This account is the central, mandatory component that uniquely identifies the user and links them to their shopping activity.

A customer using their account can select any number of **Products** they are interested in buying—from choosing none at all to selecting many different items.

These selected products can optionally be gathered into a single **ShoppingCart**. The fact that a shopping cart is optional suggests that a customer's selections are always tied to their account, even if they haven't formally added the items to a cart for immediate checkout, perhaps as a "wishlist" or "saved for later" feature.
