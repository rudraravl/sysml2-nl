This SysML v2 model describes a simplified vehicle configuration system.  It uses a direct, clean approach to modeling variations.

The model defines a base `Vehicle` part that has a variable `roof` which can be one of three types: `StandardRoof`, `SkyRoof`, or `ConvertibleRoof`.  These roof types are all defined as parts, with `StandardRoof`, `SkyRoof`, and `ConvertibleRoof` being specializations (indicated by `:>`) of the general `Roof` part.

Then, the model instantiates two specific vehicles:

* `convertibleVehicle`: A vehicle with a `convertibleRoof`.
* `standardVehicle`: A vehicle with a `standardRoof`.

In essence, the model shows how different vehicle configurations can be created by simply selecting different roof types for the base `Vehicle` part.  The use of parts and variations allows for a clear and concise representation of the different options. The model emphasizes composition – the vehicle *has* a roof – rather than inheritance, which would be less suitable for this scenario.
