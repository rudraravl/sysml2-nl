This model describes a standard plumbing interface for a water delivery system, specifically for connecting a water supply to a mixing faucet.

The system consists of a supply source, called a "Spigot Bank," which has separate spigots (or valves) for hot and cold water. This represents the typical hot and cold water pipes coming out of a wall under a sink.

This supply is designed to connect to one or more faucets. Each faucet has two distinct inlets, one for receiving hot water and another for cold water. The interface mandates a direct connection scheme: the hot water spigot from the supply must connect to the hot water inlet on the faucet, and the cold water spigot must connect to the cold inlet. This ensures that the faucet receives both hot and cold water correctly, allowing it to mix them and deliver water at the desired temperature.
