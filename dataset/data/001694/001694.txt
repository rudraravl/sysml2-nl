This model represents the Bose QuietComfort Active Noise Cancellation Headphones, a wireless audio system designed to deliver an immersive listening experience by actively eliminating ambient environmental noise.

The core of the system is a sophisticated dual-microphone noise cancellation technology. An external "feedforward" microphone on the outside of each ear cup constantly listens to the surrounding environment, capturing noise before it reaches the user's ear. Simultaneously, an internal "feedback" microphone inside the ear cup monitors any sound that leaks in, allowing the system to make fine-tuned corrections.

These two noise inputs, along with the desired audio stream from a wireless Bluetooth receiver, are fed into a central Digital Signal Processor (DSP). This processor acts as the brain of the headphones, analyzing the noise in real-time and generating a precise "anti-noise" signal—a sound wave that is the exact opposite of the unwanted ambient noise.

The DSP then combines this anti-noise signal with the user's music and the correction signal from the internal microphone. This final, combined electrical signal is sent to the high-fidelity speaker driver. The speaker reproduces both the music and the anti-noise wave. This anti-noise wave physically cancels out the incoming environmental sound waves through destructive interference, leaving the listener to hear primarily their intended audio in a quiet environment.

The entire active system, including the processor and Bluetooth receiver, is powered by an internal, rechargeable Lithium-Ion battery. The system also features several user-selectable operating modes, such as being powered off, idle, active noise cancellation (with or without music playing), and a transparency mode that likely uses the microphones to let the user hear their surroundings when needed.
