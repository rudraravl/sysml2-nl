This model represents a car, viewed as a physical assembly of components, with a focus on calculating its total mass.

The car itself is treated as the main composite object, representing the chassis and body. This core structure has its own base mass and is identified by a unique Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).

The car is composed of numerous other parts, such as an engine and a transmission. Each of these individual components is a physical object with its own distinct mass and a serial number for identification and tracking.

The system's primary function is to determine the car's total mass by aggregating the weights of its constituent parts. The final weight is calculated by summing the base mass of the car's main body with the specific masses of its installed components, like the engine and transmission.
