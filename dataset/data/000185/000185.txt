This model describes a mechanical powertrain system, such as one you might find in a vehicle or industrial machinery. Its primary purpose is to generate torque (rotational force) as a means of providing power.

The system is composed of a Powertrain assembly, which contains an Engine as its key functional component. The Engine is the specific part that performs the work of generating torque. In this design, the overall Powertrain is considered the physical realization of the system's need for a "torque generator," with the Engine being the specific component that carries out the task.
