This model represents a portable, battery-powered medical infusion pump system. It is designed to automatically administer medication to a patient at a precisely controlled rate while simultaneously monitoring their vital signs.

The system is centered around a main medical device which contains several key components that work together. A central **Control Unit** acts as the system's brain, processing patient data, managing the therapy schedule, and ensuring safe operation. The entire device is powered by a rechargeable **Battery**, which has its own lifecycle tracking its charge from full to depleted.

For medication delivery, a **Pump Mechanism** physically infuses the medication according to commands it receives from the Control Unit, such as the target flow rate and total volume to be delivered. The pump operates in various states including priming, infusing, and paused.

To monitor the patient, a **Sensor** unit measures vital signs like heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature. This data is sent back to the Control Unit for real-time analysis.

The system's operational flow is state-driven. It starts in a standby mode, transitions to an active therapy state to deliver medication, and can enter an alarm state if the Control Unit detects an unsafe condition—such as abnormal patient vitals or low battery. Any alarms or status updates are displayed on a **User Interface**. Upon successful delivery of the prescribed medication dose, the system transitions to a completed state.

The overall design emphasizes patient safety, with specific requirements for high-precision medication delivery, a long battery life for continuous use, and a rapid alarm response system to alert caregivers to critical issues.
