This is an automated urine collection device designed for the safe and hygienic collection, monitoring, and containment of urine, likely for medical or clinical applications.

At its core, the system consists of a collection reservoir made from medical-grade polymer, capable of holding up to 500 mL of fluid. The device is equipped with a suite of sensors to manage the collection process automatically. A volume monitor continuously tracks the amount of urine collected, while a pressure monitor ensures the internal pressure remains within safe limits to prevent leaks or structural failure. For added safety, an overflow protection system is in place, which activates if the internal pressure exceeds a critical threshold.

A central control module acts as the brain of the system. It processes data from the volume and pressure sensors to make decisions, such as when to activate the sealing mechanism. A key feature is this automated sealing assembly, which hygienically seals the reservoir once collection is complete or if triggered manually, ensuring the contents are securely contained for safe handling and disposal.

The device operates through a distinct lifecycle. It begins in a standby state, ready for use. Once collection starts, it enters an active state, continuously monitoring the fluid level and pressure. As the reservoir fills, the system transitions to a "near full" and then "full" state. At this point, it automatically initiates the sealing process. After the sealing is complete, it enters a sealed state, ready for removal. Once the device is removed and reset, it returns to the standby state, prepared for its next use.
