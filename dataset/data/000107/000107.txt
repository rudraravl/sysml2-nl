This model describes a standard automotive wheel hub assembly, which is the complete unit that connects a vehicle's wheel and tire to its axle. The system is composed of a central hub and a complete wheel assembly.

The wheel assembly itself consists of a metal wheel and a rubber tire. The tire is mounted onto the wheel by seating its two inner edges, known as the beads, against the wheel's rims. This creates an airtight pressure seal that allows the tire to be inflated.

The wheel is designed with five mounting holes. To attach it to the vehicle, the entire wheel assembly is fastened to the central hub, which has five corresponding threaded holes. This connection is achieved using lug bolts, which pass through the wheel's mounting holes and are screwed tightly into the hub, securing the wheel to the car.
