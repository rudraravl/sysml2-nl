This SysML model represents a modular frigate, a type of spaceship used for mining and possibly other operations in space.  The frigate's design allows for customization through the use of interchangeable modules fitted into high, medium, and low slots.  These modules provide different functionalities.  For example, a mining laser extracts ore from asteroids, while an afterburner boosts speed.  A shield extender enhances survivability, a capacitor battery improves energy management, and a survey scanner analyzes asteroids for valuable resources before mining.  A warp core stabilizer increases resistance to warp disruption, albeit at the cost of reduced targeting accuracy and scan resolution.  Each module has specific attributes like mass, power consumption, and performance characteristics (mining yield, velocity bonus, shield strength increase, etc.). The frigate’s overall capabilities are determined by the specific combination of modules installed.  The system functions by using the modules' individual capabilities in a coordinated fashion to accomplish mining and possibly other tasks within a space environment.
