This model represents a complete system for performing a high-risk cardiac procedure known as Protected Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (Protected PCI). This procedure is designed for patients with severe blockages in their heart's arteries and a dangerously weakened heart muscle, for whom a standard stenting procedure would be too risky.

The core of the system is a **Mechanical Circulatory Support (MCS)** device, which acts as a powerful, temporary heart pump. This device is inserted into the patient, typically through an artery in the leg, with its inlet positioned in the heart's main pumping chamber (the left ventricle) and its outlet in the aorta. When activated, it actively pulls blood from the ventricle and pushes it into the aorta, effectively taking over a significant portion of the heart's workload. This ensures that the patient's brain and vital organs receive a stable supply of oxygenated blood, even if their own heart struggles during the procedure. The pump operates in various states, from standby and priming to providing active support, and can be ramped up or weaned down based on the patient's needs.

While the patient is stabilized by this heart pump, the cardiologist uses a set of specialized interventional tools to fix the blocked arteries. These include:

*   A **Guide Catheter**, a hollow tube that serves as a highway from the access point in the leg to the opening of the coronary arteries.
*   A thin, steerable **Guide Wire** that is carefully navigated across the arterial blockage.
*   A **Balloon Catheter** that is threaded over the wire and inflated at the blockage site to compress the plaque and widen the vessel.
*   A **Coronary Stent**, a small, expandable mesh tube, which is deployed by a balloon to prop the artery open permanently.

The entire procedure is visualized using a **Fluoroscopy System**, which is a real-time X-ray machine. By injecting a contrast agent, the medical team can see the blood vessels and precisely guide their tools.

Simultaneously, a **Hemodynamic Monitor** continuously tracks the patient's vital signs, including heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen levels, providing immediate alerts if any parameters become unstable. All this information, along with control over the heart pump, is managed through a central **Control Console**, allowing the medical team to have a complete, real-time view of both the intervention and the patient's physiological status.

In essence, this system combines a life-support pump with advanced cardiac intervention tools and monitoring equipment. It creates a safe, controlled environment that allows cardiologists to perform complex stenting procedures on critically ill patients, restoring blood flow to the heart muscle and preventing hemodynamic collapse.
