This model describes a vehicle's cruise control system, designed to automatically maintain a driver-selected speed. The system involves an interaction between a driver and several key components within the vehicle.

The process begins when the driver initiates the cruise control by setting a desired speed. This command is sent to a central cruise controller unit. The controller then receives feedback on the vehicle's current actual speed from the speedometer. Based on this information, the cruise controller continuously calculates and sends appropriate fuel commands directly to the engine, regulating the vehicle's acceleration or deceleration to maintain the speed set by the driver.
