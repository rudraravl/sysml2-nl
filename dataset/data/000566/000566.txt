This model describes a Carpuject medication delivery system, a pre-filled syringe device used in clinical settings for administering precise doses of medication. It's designed for rapid, safe, and sterile single-use injections with minimal preparation.

The core of the system is a reusable barrel holder that houses a disposable, pre-filled, sterile glass cartridge containing the medication. This cartridge is sealed by a rubber plunger. To deliver the medication, a sterile, single-use needle assembly is attached to the system. The entire assembly is designed to ensure the medication path remains sterile until the point of use.

For safety, the device incorporates a safety mechanism that is initially locked and engaged, preventing accidental activation. A user must perform a deliberate action to unlock this safety feature. The injection is then started by pressing an activation trigger, which requires a specific amount of force to operate, further preventing accidental discharge.

The device operates through a distinct lifecycle. It begins in a "secured" state with the safety on. After being unlocked, it enters a "ready" state and is then primed to remove any air from the needle. Once the trigger is activated, the system enters the "injecting" state, where the plunger advances to expel the medication at a controlled flow rate and pressure. The injection continues until the cartridge is empty, at which point the system transitions to a "complete" state, ready for safe disposal.
