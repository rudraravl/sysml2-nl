This model describes a complete system for performing a medical endotracheal intubation, a procedure used to secure a patient's airway for mechanical ventilation. It encompasses not just the physical equipment but also the patient and the sequence of clinical actions required for a successful and safe procedure.

The system consists of a set of specialized intubation equipment and the patient being treated. The core components include:
*   An **Endotracheal Tube**, which is inserted into the patient's trachea. It features an inflatable cuff to create a seal in the airway, preventing leaks and aspiration.
*   A **Laryngoscope**, a hand-held tool with a light and a blade, used to directly visualize the patient's vocal cords to guide the tube into the correct position.
*   An **Oxygen Source**, such as a tank, which provides high-concentration oxygen.
*   A **Ventilation Bag** (or Ambu bag), which is connected to the oxygen source and the endotracheal tube to manually deliver breaths to the patient.
*   A **Suction Device** used to clear the patient's airway of secretions, ensuring a clear view during the procedure.

The overall process is a carefully orchestrated sequence of steps. It begins with assessing the patient to confirm the need for intubation. The patient is then pre-oxygenated with 100% oxygen to build up reserves, and their head and neck are positioned to provide the best view of the airway.

Using the laryngoscope, the clinician visualizes the larynx. Once the vocal cords are visible, the endotracheal tube is inserted through them into the trachea. The tube's cuff is then inflated to a specific pressure to seal the airway. The most critical step is confirming the tube's placement. This is done by checking for exhaled carbon dioxide, listening for equal breath sounds on both sides of the chest, and observing symmetric chest rise. If placement is not confirmed, the tube is removed, and the attempt is repeated. Once confirmed, the tube is secured in place, and manual ventilation with the bag begins, establishing a secure and functional artificial airway.
