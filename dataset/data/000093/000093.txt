This model describes a configurable vehicle platform designed with a modular architecture. The core design of any vehicle built from this platform consists of a single engine mounted on a chassis assembly.

The platform allows for significant customization, particularly in the powertrain and chassis. The engine can be configured with a variable number of cylinders, ranging from four to eight, to accommodate different performance requirements. The chassis assembly is a standard frame that includes four wheels. For safety and structural integrity, the chassis can be optionally fitted with a single roll bar, with choices available for either a light-duty or heavy-duty version. Each wheel is secured by six to ten lug bolts.

Based on this flexible platform, two distinct vehicle models are defined:

*   **Vehicle Model 1:** A lighter, more economical configuration. It is equipped with a standard 4-cylinder engine and uses exactly 24 lug bolts (six per wheel). This model can be optionally fitted with a light-duty roll bar.
*   **Vehicle Model 2:** A higher-performance or heavy-duty variant. It features a more powerful engine with six to eight cylinders. This model is specifically designed without a roll bar and uses a higher number of lug bolts (24 to 28 total), suggesting it's built to handle greater stress or power.
