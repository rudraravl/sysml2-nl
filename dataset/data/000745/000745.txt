This model describes a medical device known as an automated Rectal Dilator System. It is designed for clinical use to perform controlled, gradual dilation of rectal tissue. The primary purpose of the device is to safely create access for medical examinations, therapeutic treatments, or surgical procedures while minimizing patient discomfort and preventing tissue trauma.

The system is composed of several integrated components. The part that contacts the patient consists of a soft, tapered **Dilator Tip** and an **Expandable Shaft**, both made from biocompatible medical silicone. The tip ensures gentle insertion, while the shaft is the core component that gradually increases in diameter from a minimum of 10 mm up to 40 mm.

Internally, an **Expansion Mechanism** provides the force needed to widen the shaft. This mechanism is precisely managed by a **Control Module**, which allows a medical professional to set the target diameter and procedure mode, such as "gradual" expansion.

For patient safety, the system is equipped with a **Pressure Monitor** that continuously measures the pressure being exerted on the surrounding tissue. If this pressure exceeds a pre-set safe threshold, an alarm is activated. As a further failsafe, a mechanical **Safety Release** is designed to automatically activate and reduce pressure if it reaches a critical level.

The device operates through a carefully managed procedure. It begins with pre-use checks for sterility, followed by lubrication and positioning. The dilation process is not continuous but incremental; the device expands in small steps, pausing after each one to monitor tissue pressure and response. Once the target diameter is safely reached, it is maintained for a specific duration. The procedure concludes with a controlled, gradual retraction of the shaft before the device is withdrawn. In case of excessive pressure at any point, the system is designed to perform an emergency retraction to ensure patient safety.
