This model describes a vehicle and trailer towing system. The system consists of two main components: a vehicle and a detachable trailer.

The vehicle is built upon a frame, and mounted to this frame is a hitch ball. The trailer also has its own structural frame, which is fitted with a trailer coupler on its front end.

The connection between the two is a standard trailer hitch, formed when the trailer's coupler is lowered and locked onto the vehicle's hitch ball. This physical link allows the vehicle to tow the trailer.

The system is designed to be dynamic, with clear operations for both attaching and detaching the trailer. A specific action exists to connect the two, establishing the hitch link. Disconnecting is a two-stage process: first, the physical connection is broken, and then the system recognizes that the trailer is fully separated from the vehicle.
