This is a model of a self-contained, battery-powered residential smoke detector designed to continuously monitor the air and provide an early warning of potential fires.

The device is built around a central control unit that orchestrates the functions of its various components. Its primary sensing component is an optical sensor that draws in air samples to measure the concentration of smoke particles. This measurement is constantly compared against a pre-set safety threshold by the control unit.

If the detected smoke level exceeds this threshold, the control unit activates two types of alerts: a loud audible alarm designed to emit a sound of at least 85 decibels, and a visual alert from a flashing red LED indicator. The entire system is powered by an internal battery pack, and the control unit also monitors the battery's charge.

The smoke detector operates in several distinct states. During normal operation, it is in a quiet "standby" mode, actively monitoring the air. When smoke is detected, it transitions to an "alarming" state, activating the siren and light. The system also includes a "low battery" warning state to signal when the battery needs replacing and supports user interaction through a control interface. This allows a user to manually trigger a self-test to verify that the sensor and alarm are working correctly, or to reset the alarm after an alert has been triggered and the air is clear.
