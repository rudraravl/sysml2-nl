This is a Lung Flute, a medical device designed for respiratory therapy to help patients clear mucus from their airways. The system's main purpose is to convert a patient's exhaled breath into low-frequency acoustic vibrations. These vibrations then travel down into the lungs to loosen and mobilize sticky mucus, making it easier for the patient to cough up and clear.

The device is a handheld, mechanical instrument composed of several key parts that work together. The patient exhales into a biocompatible **Mouthpiece**. This stream of air causes a **Reed Mechanism** inside the device to flutter, generating a consistent acoustic vibration at a specific therapeutic frequency (around 18 Hz). This sound wave then passes into a **Resonance Chamber**, a tube with a precisely defined length and diameter, which amplifies the vibration.

This amplified acoustic energy travels from the device back into the patient's airways, acting directly on the mucus to change its state from adherent to mobilized. To ensure safety and proper operation, the device also includes a **Check Valve**, which permits only one-way airflow, preventing the user from accidentally inhaling through it. The system requires no external power and functions entirely based on the patient's exhalation, which must be strong enough to create a specific backpressure for the therapy to be effective.
