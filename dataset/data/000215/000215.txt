This model describes a configurable rear-wheel-drive vehicle, detailing how different powertrain and wheel components are combined to create two distinct product variants based on performance requirements.

The vehicle's core design includes an engine, a transmission with an integrated clutch, a driveshaft, and a rear axle assembly with two wheels. The system is designed to be offered in two specific configurations: a "normal performance" model and a "high performance" model.

The "normal performance" configuration is built with a 4-cylinder engine. This engine is exclusively paired with a manual transmission and manual clutch system. To complete this package, the vehicle is fitted with narrow-rim wheels on the rear axle.

The "high performance" configuration uses a more powerful 6-cylinder engine. This engine is always matched with an automatic transmission and its corresponding automatic clutch. This variant is also equipped with wider-rim wheels on the rear axle, likely to provide better traction for the increased power.

A key aspect of this system is the strict set of rules that govern how components can be combined. A customer cannot freely mix and match parts; the choice of engine dictates the required transmission and wheel type, ensuring that only these two valid and consistent vehicle configurations can be produced.
