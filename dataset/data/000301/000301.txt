This model represents a foundational digital library for physical quantities and units, based on the International System of Quantities (ISQ) as defined by the ISO/IEC 80000 international standard.

This is not a physical object but a structured collection of data definitions intended to be used within other engineering and scientific models. Its purpose is to provide a standard, unambiguous way to represent physical measurements.

The library is organized into modules that correspond to different domains of physics and science, including space and time, mechanics, thermodynamics, and electromagnetism. It defines fundamental quantities and their units, such as length (meter), mass (kilogram), and thermodynamic temperature (Kelvin). It also defines more complex or specialized quantities, like temperature difference, ensuring they are correctly related back to the fundamental base quantities.

By using this library, engineers and scientists can ensure that their system models are consistent, accurate, and use a shared, standardized understanding of all physical measurements.
