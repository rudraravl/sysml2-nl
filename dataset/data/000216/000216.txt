This model describes the engineering design and requirements for a ground vehicle, such as a car or truck.

The vehicle is defined by its core mechanical components, including an engine, a transmission, and a fuel system. The engine's primary function is to generate torque, which provides the motive power for the vehicle. This power is then transferred to the transmission through a dedicated interface that includes a clutch. The vehicle's key physical attributes, such as its total mass, fuel tank capacity, and current fuel level, are also tracked.

The model details two distinct configurations of this vehicle. One is a lighter version with a specified mass of 2000 kg, while the second is a heavier variant at 2500 kg.

A significant part of the model focuses on the formal engineering requirements that the vehicle's design must meet. These requirements place strict constraints on its physical properties and functional performance. For instance, there are specific mass limitations that depend on the fuel load: the lighter vehicle configuration must not exceed 2000 kg with a full fuel tank, while the heavier configuration has a mass limit of 2500 kg when its tank is empty. Other requirements dictate how components must interact, ensuring the engine properly transfers its torque to the transmission, and define the expected performance of the engine itself.
