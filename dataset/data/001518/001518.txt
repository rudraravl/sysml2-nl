This is a comprehensive home automation system designed to control and monitor various smart devices within a building using the OpenWebNet protocol. The system provides centralized and intelligent management of lighting, climate, and pre-programmed scenarios through a dedicated, low-voltage communication bus that connects all the components.

At the heart of the system is a **Bus Gateway**, which acts as the central translator and bridge. It connects the internal device bus to a standard home network (like Ethernet or Wi-Fi). This allows users to control the system remotely, for example, from a smartphone app. The gateway receives external commands, validates them, and routes them to the correct device on the bus. It also listens for status updates from the devices and sends them back out to the network.

The entire system is powered by a central **Power Supply Unit**. This unit converts standard household voltage into the low voltage required by the bus and all connected devices, distributing both power and data signals over the same set of wires.

The system controls several types of devices:
*   **Lighting Actuators:** These are the physical modules that control the lights. The model shows three specific lights for a living room, kitchen, and bedroom. Each actuator can turn a light on or off, and also supports dimming to adjust the brightness to specific levels.
*   **Temperature Control:** This is a smart thermostat that automatically manages the climate. It continuously monitors the current room temperature and compares it to a desired target temperature. If the room is too cold, it will activate the heating; if it's too warm, it will activate the cooling, ensuring the environment remains comfortable.
*   **Automation Scenarios:** These are pre-programmed routines that execute a series of coordinated actions across multiple devices. For instance, an "Evening Mode" scenario could be triggered to dim the living room lights, turn on the bedroom light, and adjust the thermostat for the night, all with a single command. A "Morning Mode" could do the reverse.

All these components communicate over the shared bus, each identified by a unique address. This architecture allows for a reliable, integrated, and expandable smart home system where devices work together to automate daily routines and improve comfort and efficiency.
