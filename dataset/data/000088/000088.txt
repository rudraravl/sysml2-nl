This model describes a system for tracking the configuration and maintenance history of specific, individual vehicles. The system manages a collection of uniquely identified vehicles, such as 'Vehicle1' with a mass of 1800 kg and 'Vehicle2' with a mass of 1700 kg.

Beyond the vehicles themselves, the system also tracks individual, serialized components that are installed on them, including a specific axle assembly ('AxleAssembly1') and distinct wheels ('Wheel1' and 'Wheel2').

The primary function of this system is to capture snapshots of a vehicle's component configuration at different points in time, effectively creating a digital log of its assembly and service history. The model illustrates a specific maintenance event for 'Vehicle1'. At a starting time (t0), the vehicle's front axle is identified as 'AxleAssembly1', with the specific 'Wheel1' installed on the front-left position. By a later time (t1), the system records a new configuration: the very same 'Wheel1' has been moved from the front-left to the front-right position.

This demonstrates the system's ability to track the physical location and service life of individual parts as they are moved or rotated on a vehicle, which is crucial for detailed maintenance records and managing component lifecycles.
