This model describes requirements for a mining frigate and its supporting infrastructure within a low-security space environment.  The mining frigate itself is a spaceship designed to extract ore from asteroids. Its key capabilities include a high mining rate, substantial cargo capacity for storing the extracted ore,  a strong shield for protection against attacks, and the ability to deploy and manage drones for various tasks.  It also needs efficient travel capabilities including high warp speed and fast alignment times,  advanced threat detection to identify potential dangers,  and the ability to quickly lock onto mining targets.  Finally, it needs to dock efficiently at stations for offloading and maintenance.

Beyond the individual frigate, the requirements also encompass fleet coordination, managed by a fleet commander, who needs to control and organize a sizeable fleet of these frigates.  The extracted ore is processed by a larger vessel, likely a rorqual, which boasts a powerful ore compression unit to reduce volume and increase transport efficiency. The overall system involves a coordinated effort between individual mining frigates, their supporting drones, a fleet commander for strategic management, and a dedicated ore processing vessel to maximize efficiency and profitability.
