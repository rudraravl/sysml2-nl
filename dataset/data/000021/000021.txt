This is a simple quadcopter drone designed for aerial observation. The drone's physical structure consists of a central, cross-shaped main body from which four arms, or struts, extend symmetrically.

Each of the four arms is a rectangular beam with a circular cutout at the end, specifically shaped to house a propulsion unit. Mounted at the end of each arm is a propeller and motor assembly. These four identical assemblies provide the thrust and control for the drone's flight.

A camera is mounted on the front of the drone's main body. It is housed in a small cylindrical case and is tilted to point downwards at an angle, making it suitable for capturing images or video of the ground. The system also defines the camera's conical field of view, representing the area it can see at any given moment.
