This model describes an adjustable medical stretcher system designed for safely transporting patients within a medical facility. The system is built around a robust main frame with a maximum load capacity of 250 kg, ensuring it can support a wide range of patients.

The stretcher features several key components that work together to provide patient comfort, safety, and operational flexibility for medical staff. At its base is a wheel assembly with four wheels and an integrated brake mechanism that can be engaged to lock the stretcher in place for stability during patient transfers. For patient positioning, the stretcher includes two main adjustment systems: a height adjustment mechanism, likely hydraulic, that can raise or lower the entire patient surface between 50 cm and 90 cm, and a backrest mechanism that allows the angle to be adjusted from a flat 0-degree position up to a 75-degree incline.

Patient safety is a primary focus, addressed by a pair of safety rails on the left and right sides. These rails can be raised and locked into place to prevent falls during transport. A central controller provides an interface for operators to command adjustments to the height, backrest angle, and wheel locks. This controller also monitors the overall safety status, verifying that the patient is secured, rails are raised, and wheels are locked before and after movement.

The typical operational workflow involves securing the patient to the stretcher, raising the safety rails, moving the patient to the destination, locking the wheels upon arrival, and then safely transferring the patient.
