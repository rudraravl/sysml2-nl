This is the Osteomed Bone Healing System, a therapeutic medical device designed to accelerate the healing of bone fractures and promote regeneration. It uses a non-invasive technology called low-intensity pulsed ultrasound (LIPUS) to stimulate bone tissue at a cellular level, enhancing the body's natural repair processes.

The system is composed of several integrated modules. At its core is a **Treatment Control Unit**, which serves as the brain, managing the therapy sessions based on pre-defined treatment protocols for each patient. Power is supplied by a dedicated **Power Management Unit** that provides regulated voltage and current to all electronic components.

For treatment delivery, an **Ultrasound Generator Module** creates a precise, low-intensity ultrasonic waveform with a specific frequency and intensity. This signal is sent to a **Transducer Assembly**, which is the part of the device placed in contact with the patient's skin over the fracture site. This assembly converts the electrical signal into physical ultrasound waves and focuses them on the targeted bone tissue.

To track the effectiveness of the therapy, a **Bone Density Sensor** is integrated into the device, likely within the same applicator head as the transducer. This sensor continuously monitors the state of the bone tissue, measuring its density and assessing the rate of healing. This feedback is sent to the control unit, allowing for real-time monitoring and progress assessment.

A typical therapy session involves the system initializing, loading the patient's specific protocol, and then beginning a controlled cycle. It delivers the ultrasound stimulation for a set duration, while simultaneously monitoring the tissue's response to ensure safety and effectiveness. After each session, the system records the data, assesses the healing progress against the baseline, and determines if the treatment plan should continue. Once the therapy is complete, it can generate a comprehensive report detailing the treatment history and final outcome.
