This SysML model describes a mining frigate, a spacecraft designed for asteroid mining and combat in space.  The frigate's primary function is to locate, mine, and transport valuable ore from asteroids. This involves several key processes: identifying suitable asteroid targets, activating mining lasers to extract ore, and then securely storing that ore in the ship's cargo hold.  The mining process can be enhanced by external boosts from support ships, and the system incorporates checks to handle situations like asteroid depletion, laser overheating, insufficient energy, and ECM interference.

Beyond mining, the frigate is equipped for self-defense. It can scan its surroundings for threats, prioritize those threats based on their danger level, and then engage its defense systems, including weapon turrets and deployable drones, to neutralize them.

Navigation is handled via a sophisticated system allowing the frigate to undock from stations, plot optimal courses using the navigation system, engage its propulsion system to travel to its destination, and continuously monitor and adjust its course en route.  The frigate also needs to dock at stations for several reasons: to offload its collected ore, to recharge its systems (capacitor), and to undergo repairs if needed. This docking and undocking is managed by the ship's systems, including verification of cargo transfer and resupply. The system includes reporting mechanisms to keep the pilot constantly updated on the status of various operations, including mining progress, threat detection, navigation, and cargo transfers.  Finally, the frigate can deploy drones for either mining support or defensive operations, selecting the appropriate drones based on the situation.
