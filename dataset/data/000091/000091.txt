This model describes a standard vehicle wheel hub assembly, the complete unit that attaches a tire and wheel to a car's axle. The system is designed to be securely fastened to the vehicle while allowing the wheel to rotate.

The overall assembly consists of a central hub, a complete wheel assembly (which includes the tire and the metal wheel), and a set of five lug bolt joints that fasten the wheel to the hub.

The wheel assembly itself is a combination of a tire and a metal wheel. A critical feature is the airtight "pressure seat" formed where the tire's inner edges, known as beads, press firmly against the wheel's rims. This seal is what allows the tire to be inflated and maintain a specific pressure.

Breaking it down further:
*   The **metal wheel** is defined by its diameter and width. It features the rims for mounting the tire, a single inflation valve for adding or removing air, and five mounting holes for the lug bolts. The design also allows for up to six balance weights to be attached to ensure smooth rotation.
*   The **tire** has its own specifications (like size and type) and features the two beads that form the seal with the wheel.
*   The **hub** is the central component that connects to the vehicle's axle and has five corresponding threaded holes for the lug bolts.

The connection between the wheel and the hub is achieved through the five **lug bolt joints**. Each joint is formed by a lug bolt passing through a mounting hole on the wheel and screwing into a threaded hole on the hub. The model specifies important properties for this joint, such as the required torque and resulting bolt tension, which are critical for ensuring the wheel is safely attached.

Additionally, the system can accommodate an optional **wireless tire pressure monitor**, which is strapped to the wheel and is capable of transmitting pressure data.
