This model describes the core components and interactions of an e-commerce or online retail system, focusing on how a customer selects products to purchase.

The system is centered around an **Online Customer** who interacts with a catalog of available **Products**. Each customer has their own personal **Shopping Cart** which acts as a temporary container for items they intend to buy.

The fundamental process is the "Product Selection." This represents the action of a customer choosing one or more products and adding them to their shopping cart. Every time a selection is made, associated **Selection Information** (like the quantity, color, or size) is captured with it.

The system is designed to handle two distinct types of selection scenarios: a standard multi-item selection, where a customer can add several different products to their cart at once, and a specialized single-item selection, where the action is restricted to choosing only one product at a time. This single-selection capability could be used for things like a "Buy Now" feature or for selecting a single winner from a product giveaway.

In essence, this is a model of the relationship between a customer, their cart, and the products they choose, which forms the basis of a typical online shopping experience.
