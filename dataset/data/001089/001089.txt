This is a precision hydrometer system designed to measure the specific gravity and density of a fluid. The core of the system is the hydrometer itself, which is composed of a calibrated float, a measurement scale, and an integrated temperature sensor.

The system's main component is the hydrometer float, a weighted, sealed instrument with a specific mass and volume, designed to be immersed in a liquid. The float has a thin stem that features a finely graduated measurement scale indicating a range of specific gravity values. To take a measurement, the hydrometer is placed in a sample of the test fluid held within a dedicated fluid container.

Based on the fluid's density, the float will sink to a specific depth and achieve a stable, buoyant equilibrium. The specific gravity is then read directly from the scale at the point where it emerges from the fluid's surface. To ensure high accuracy, an integrated temperature sensor measures the fluid's temperature simultaneously. This reading is crucial because a fluid's density changes with temperature. The system uses this temperature data to correct or compensate the initial reading, providing an accurate specific gravity value referenced against a standard fluid (like water at 20°C). The entire measurement process, from immersion and stabilization to reading and temperature compensation, is designed to be completed quickly and reliably.
