This model describes the operational framework for a large-scale, player-run mining corporation within the virtual universe of the online game EVE Online. The corporation's primary mission is to generate profit by deploying fleets of specialized spaceships to mine, transport, and sell valuable ores.

The core of the operation is a fleet of at least ten "mining frigate" class spaceships. The corporation aims to keep this fleet highly active, with a utilization rate of over 80%, to extract at least one million cubic meters of ore during each operational cycle.

Logistics are critical. Once mined, the ore must be delivered efficiently, with a target delivery time of under 30 minutes per batch. To support these large-scale operations, the corporation must maintain a substantial storage capacity of at least ten million cubic meters.

Because mining often occurs in dangerous regions of space ("Low Sec" and "Null Sec"), security is a major concern. The system requires that at least 30% of these high-risk mining operations are protected by armed security escorts. In the event of an attack by hostile players, a defense force must be able to respond in under 60 seconds.

The entire enterprise is driven by economic goals. It must achieve a profit of at least 500 million ISK (the game's currency) per cycle, while keeping operational costs under control. The corporation is also designed to be adaptable, adjusting its mining priorities based on fluctuating in-game market prices for different types of ore to maximize profitability.
