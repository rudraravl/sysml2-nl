This is a medical ultrasound system specifically designed for the non-invasive detection and analysis of bone fractures. It provides clinicians with a rapid, point-of-care diagnostic tool as an alternative to traditional X-rays.

The core of the system is a handheld **Ultrasound Probe**, which contains a transducer array. This probe generates high-frequency sound waves that are directed into the patient's body and also listens for the returning echoes. The sound waves are electronically focused and steered by a **Beam Former** to precisely target the area of interest.

When the echoes return to the probe, they are sent to a **Signal Processing Unit** which filters and amplifies the raw signals to improve clarity and extract information about the different tissue types. An **Image Reconstruction Engine** then uses this processed data to create a real-time, two-dimensional visual image of the bone and surrounding tissues.

A key feature of this system is an intelligent **Fracture Detection Algorithm**. This AI-based component automatically analyzes the ultrasound images to identify signs of a fracture. It can determine the location and size of a potential break and provides a diagnostic report that includes a confidence score for its findings.

An operator interacts with the system through a **Control Console**, which is used to adjust settings and initiate scans. The live ultrasound image and the final diagnostic report from the AI algorithm are both shown on a high-resolution **Display Monitor**. The entire system is powered by a central **Power Management Unit** that distributes the necessary electrical power to all the components.

The system operates through a series of states, moving from standby and calibration to an active scanning mode. During a scan, it continuously transmits sound, receives echoes, processes the data, and updates the image. Once a scan is complete, it analyzes the data to produce a final diagnostic report for the clinician.
