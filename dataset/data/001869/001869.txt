This model describes a precision static pressure control system designed to accurately measure, monitor, and maintain the pressure of a fluid, such as air, within a contained environment. Its primary purpose is to provide a stable and reliable source of pressurized air for downstream processes or equipment that are sensitive to pressure fluctuations.

The system is built around a core set of components working in a feedback loop. An incoming air supply first passes through a **Pressure Regulator**, which performs the initial control to bring the pressure close to the desired setpoint. This regulated air then fills a **Pressure Vessel**, which acts as a buffer or reservoir, helping to smooth out any remaining fluctuations and create a stable volume of pressurized air.

To ensure precision, the system uses two **Static Pressure Probes** for redundancy and accuracy. These probes sense the ambient pressure within the vessel without being affected by the velocity of the airflow. The physical pressure measurement from the primary probe is then sent to a **Pressure Transmitter**, which converts it into a digital or electrical signal for processing.

This signal is used by the system's control logic to make fine adjustments. If the measured pressure deviates from the target, the system commands a **Control Valve** connected to the vessel's outlet to modulate the flow of air leaving the system. This continuous cycle of measuring, comparing, and adjusting allows the system to maintain the vessel's pressure with high stability.

The system operates through several distinct states. It starts in a safe, unpressurized **standby** mode. Upon receiving a start command, it enters a **startup** sequence, carefully opening valves and building pressure until it reaches the target operational level. During normal operation, it continuously runs its control loop to regulate the pressure. For safety, the system constantly monitors for over-pressure conditions; if a safety limit is breached, it enters an **alarm** state and activates an emergency vent. When finished, a controlled **shutdown** sequence safely vents all pressure before the system returns to standby.
