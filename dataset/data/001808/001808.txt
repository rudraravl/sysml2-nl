This model describes a complete, automated industrial processing machine designed to continuously transform a stream of raw material into a refined, high-quality product.

The system is built around a central **Processing Core**, which is the heart of the machine where the actual material transformation takes place. This core requires a significant amount of electrical power to operate and generates substantial heat, necessitating an active cooling system to maintain a stable and safe operating temperature.

The entire machine is a self-contained unit with several integrated subsystems that work together:

*   A **Material Feeder** automatically supplies the raw material to the processing core at a controlled rate.
*   A dedicated **Power Supply Unit** provides the high-voltage electrical power needed for the transformation process.
*   A **Cooling System** actively circulates a coolant fluid through the processing core to dissipate excess heat and prevent overheating, which is critical for both safety and product quality.
*   A **Product Collector** gathers the finished product as it exits the core, with a capacity to hold a certain amount before it needs to be emptied.
*   An intelligent **Control System** acts as the brain of the operation. It monitors the machine's vital signs, such as its speed, temperature, and power consumption. Based on operator commands and sensor feedback, it adjusts operating parameters in a continuous control loop to ensure efficiency, stability, and safety. It is also responsible for triggering emergency shutdowns in case of a critical fault like overheating or power failure.

The machine operates through a series of distinct states. It doesn't simply turn on and off; it requires a controlled **startup sequence** to warm up to its optimal operating temperature. Once ready, it enters a **processing state** to perform its main function. When a job is complete, it undergoes a managed **shutdown and cooldown sequence** to return to a safe, offline state. This lifecycle ensures the machine's longevity and the consistency of the product.
