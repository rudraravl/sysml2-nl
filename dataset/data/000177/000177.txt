This model describes a vehicle mass verification system, designed for use in a testing or quality control environment.

The system consists of a test station that includes a large-scale measurement device. Its purpose is to perform a specific test procedure to accurately determine the mass of a vehicle. The process involves taking a vehicle, which is the subject of the test, and using the scale to collect a mass measurement.

The model illustrates the system being used to conduct a sequence of two separate tests. In the first test, it measures a vehicle with a mass of 2500 kg. Subsequently, it performs a second test on a different, heavier vehicle with a mass of 3000 kg. This demonstrates the system's ability to be used repeatedly to certify or check different vehicles one after another, such as on a production line or at an inspection facility.
