This model describes a sophisticated, hydraulically-powered operating table system for use in surgical environments. Its primary function is to precisely and safely position a patient for various medical procedures.

The system is built around a central **Table Surface**, which is the platform the patient lies on. This surface can be adjusted in multiple ways: its height can be raised or lowered, and it can be tilted along several axes, including longitudinal (head up/down), lateral (side-to-side), and the specific Trendelenburg position.

Movement is driven by a powerful hydraulic system. A central **Hydraulic Pump** takes in electrical power and generates high-pressure fluid. This fluid is distributed to a set of specialized hydraulic actuators. A dedicated **Height Actuator** controls the vertical movement of the table, while three separate **Tilt Actuators** manage the longitudinal, lateral, and Trendelenburg angles.

A user, such as a surgeon or nurse, interacts with the system through a **Control Panel**. Here, they can input the desired position for the table. This command is processed by a central **Position Controller**, which acts as the brain of the system. The controller compares the desired position with the table's current position (provided by sensors) and sends precise signals to the hydraulic actuators to achieve the target orientation.

Patient safety is a critical aspect of the design. A dedicated **Safety Monitor** continuously checks for hazardous conditions. It monitors the patient's weight to prevent overloading and ensures the table's height and tilt angles stay within predefined safe limits. If an unsafe condition is detected or an emergency stop is triggered, the system immediately halts all movement. A robust **Locking Mechanism** is also included to firmly secure the table in its set position, ensuring stability during a procedure. This lock is automatically engaged once positioning is complete or during an emergency.
