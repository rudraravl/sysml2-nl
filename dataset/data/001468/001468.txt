This model describes a comprehensive Indoor Air Quality System designed to automatically monitor and maintain a healthy and comfortable environment within a building. The system's primary function is to continuously measure key air pollutants and environmental conditions, and then intelligently activate various components to correct any issues.

The core of the system is a central control processor that acts as its brain. It receives real-time data from a set of three integrated multi-sensor units placed throughout the monitored space. These sensors simultaneously measure a wide range of parameters, including carbon dioxide (CO2), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), fine particulate matter (PM2.5), relative humidity, and temperature.

Based on these readings, the controller calculates an overall Air Quality Index. If any parameter exceeds predefined healthy thresholds—for instance, if CO2 levels are too high from occupancy or particulate levels rise—the system automatically responds. It can command a variable-speed ventilation fan to increase the flow of fresh air, activate an air purifier with a HEPA filter to remove particulates and VOCs, and engage a humidity regulator to maintain optimal moisture levels. The system's response is adaptive, meaning the fan speed or purification intensity can scale based on the severity of the air quality degradation.

The system operates through several modes, such as idle monitoring when the air is clean, and active ventilating or purifying when intervention is needed. For occupants, a user interface displays the current air quality status and index. The system is also designed for reliability, featuring sensor redundancy and a failsafe mechanism that defaults to maximum ventilation in case of a control failure. It also tracks the status of its components, such as the air purifier's filter, and can issue alerts when maintenance, like a filter replacement, is required.
