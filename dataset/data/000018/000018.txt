This model represents a simplified physical definition of a car, focusing on its overall external dimensions. The car itself is conceptualized as a single, solid rectangular box that defines the maximum space it occupies.

This "bounding box" has a specific length of 4800 mm (4.8 meters), a width of 1840 mm (1.84 meters), and a height of 1350 mm (1.35 meters). This type of representation is used to define the car's physical footprint and volume for purposes like spatial planning, collision detection, or logistics without needing to model the complex details of the car's actual bodywork.
