This model represents a complete self-balancing electric unicycle (EUC), a personal transportation device. It's designed to carry a single rider, who controls speed and direction hands-free by shifting their body weight.

The core of the system is its ability to dynamically maintain balance. This is achieved through a close partnership between its key components. An Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), acting as a gyroscopic sensor, constantly monitors the unicycle's pitch and roll angles. This orientation data is fed to a central Motor Controller, which serves as the brain of the device.

The Motor Controller processes the sensor data along with input from pressure sensors in the Pedal Assembly, which detect the rider's presence and lean. Based on this information, the controller calculates the precise torque needed to keep the unicycle upright and sends a command to the powerful Electric Motor housed within the single Wheel. To move forward, the rider leans forward; the controller interprets this as a need to accelerate the wheel to "catch up" and maintain balance, resulting in forward motion. Leaning back initiates braking.

The entire system is powered by a high-capacity, rechargeable Battery Pack, which supplies electricity to the motor, controller, sensors, and an LED Display Panel. This display provides the rider with crucial information such as the current operating mode (e.g., eco, comfort, sport), battery level, and system status. All these components are mounted on a durable Chassis Frame, designed to be lightweight yet strong enough to support the rider.

The unicycle operates through several distinct states. It starts in an 'off' state, moves to 'initializing' for a self-check when powered on, and then enters a 'ready' state. When a rider mounts the pedals, it begins actively 'balancing'. A forward lean transitions it to the 'riding' state. It also has dedicated states for 'charging' when connected to a power source and a 'fault' state to safely handle any system errors.
