This model represents a fundamental configurable component or data object that possesses a single, critical numerical property.

A key characteristic of this component is that its defining property must always be a positive value; it cannot be zero or negative. This rule is inherent to its nature, similar to how a physical dimension like length or a quantity like mass must be positive.

The system provides a specific, controlled operation for updating this property. When invoked, this operation takes a new numerical value and applies it to the component, effectively reconfiguring it, and can provide a notification that the change was made.

In practice, this could represent a variety of real-world items, such as a geometric shape where the property is its radius, a component in an electronic circuit where the property is its resistance, or a software setting where the property is a timeout value.
