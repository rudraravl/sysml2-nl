This model describes a vehicle defined by its primary components and their physical properties, specifically their mass.

The system represents a vehicle as an assembly consisting of an engine and a transmission. A key characteristic of this system is that the vehicle's total mass is not a fixed value but is automatically calculated by summing the individual masses of these two core parts.

This demonstrates a system where the properties of the whole (the vehicle's total mass) are derived directly from the properties of its components. The model also extends this concept to represent a small fleet or group of identical vehicles, making it possible to track the total mass of each individual vehicle in the collection.
