This model describes an integrated oncology instrument system for providing comprehensive cancer treatment. It's a sophisticated medical system that combines diagnostic imaging, treatment planning, and the simultaneous delivery of both radiation therapy and chemotherapy, all while continuously monitoring the patient's well-being.

The system is composed of several key components that work in a coordinated sequence. It begins with a **CT Scanner**, which performs high-resolution scans to precisely locate and measure the patient's tumor. This detailed imaging data is then sent to a powerful **Treatment Planning Workstation**. Here, specialized software uses the images to create a customized treatment plan, calculating the optimal radiation dose, beam angles, and energy required to target the tumor while sparing surrounding healthy tissue.

Once the plan is finalized, the patient is treated using a **Linear Accelerator (linac)**, which delivers high-energy radiation beams with extreme positional accuracy. At the same time, a **Chemotherapy Infusion Pump** can be used to administer medication at a controlled and precise flow rate.

Throughout the entire treatment session, a **Patient Monitor** continuously tracks vital signs such as heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation to ensure patient safety. In addition, a **Dosimeter System**, likely an array of sensors, measures the actual radiation dose being delivered in real-time. This provides immediate feedback to verify that the treatment is proceeding exactly according to the plan.

The system operates through a well-defined workflow for each treatment session: it starts with imaging, moves to planning, then patient setup, and finally the concurrent delivery of radiation and chemotherapy. After the therapy is delivered, the system performs a final verification check to ensure the delivered dose matched the plan before concluding the session.
