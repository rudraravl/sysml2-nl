This model represents a vehicle equipped with an automated, time-based maintenance scheduling system. The system is designed to track and manage the vehicle's health status based on a regular maintenance cycle.

The vehicle has a defined maintenance interval (e.g., every six months) and a specific time for its next scheduled service. It operates primarily in a "normal" state until this scheduled maintenance time arrives.

At the exact moment maintenance is due, the vehicle's status automatically and immediately shifts to "in maintenance." The system immediately calculates and sets the date for the *next* service appointment by adding the standard interval to the current date. There is a strict rule that this maintenance work must be completed within 48 hours.

Once the service is physically completed, the system receives a "Maintenance Done" signal, which returns the vehicle to its normal operational state, ready for use until the next service is due. The vehicle also has a defined maximum operating temperature, suggesting it monitors other health parameters, although they are not part of this specific maintenance cycle.
