This model represents a simplified vehicle defined by its physical composition and mass. The system is built around a basic vehicle that is made up of two core components: an engine and a transmission.

A fundamental principle of this system is that the vehicle's total mass is directly calculated by adding together the individual masses of its key parts—the engine and the transmission. Both the complete vehicle and its individual components are treated as physical parts, each having its own measurable mass.

The model also demonstrates its ability to handle a collection or fleet of these vehicles, allowing for the tracking and management of the mass for each individual vehicle in the group.
