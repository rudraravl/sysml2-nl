This is a powered standing wheelchair system designed to enhance user mobility and independence. Its core function is to allow a user to safely transition between a seated driving position and an upright standing position, while also functioning as a standard electric wheelchair.

The wheelchair is built around a main frame that integrates a sophisticated lifting mechanism and a drive system. Mobility is provided by two independent drive motors, one for each main wheel, powered by a central rechargeable battery.

The key feature is its posture-changing capability, managed by a powerful lift actuator that raises the entire seat frame and the user. To ensure stability during this process, the wheelchair is equipped with front and rear stabilizer legs that automatically deploy to the ground before any lifting or lowering begins. As a critical safety measure, the drive motors are also disabled during this transition.

A central control unit acts as the brain of the system, orchestrating all operations. It processes commands from a user interface, which includes dedicated buttons to initiate standing, sitting, and an emergency stop. The control unit continuously monitors a suite of sensors for safety, including a tilt sensor to ensure the wheelchair is on stable, level ground, and a seat pressure sensor to confirm the user is properly positioned before any transition can be attempted.

The wheelchair operates in distinct modes: a 'seated' mode for normal driving, a 'standing' mode, and a temporary 'transitioning' mode where movement is locked for safety. The entire sequence of standing up or sitting down is an automated process, initiated by a single button press, which follows a strict protocol of checking safety conditions, deploying stabilizers, and then performing the lift or descent before re-engaging the drive system.
