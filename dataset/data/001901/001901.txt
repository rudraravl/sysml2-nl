This model represents a complex, mobile humanoid robot system, named Robonaut, designed for dexterous manipulation tasks in environments built for humans.

The Robonaut is built with a humanoid upper body, which includes a head, torso, two robotic arms, and two five-fingered hands, all mounted on a four-wheeled mobility base that allows it to move around its workspace. The entire system is powered by a self-contained, high-capacity battery system that distributes electricity to all its components.

Its head contains a sophisticated vision system with stereo cameras and a depth sensor, enabling it to perceive its surroundings in 3D, detect objects, and create a map of the scene. This visual information is sent to a central control computer, which acts as the robot's brain. This computer processes the data, plans complex actions, and sends control commands to the rest of the body.

The two robotic arms, each with shoulder and elbow joints, provide a long reach and the ability to lift significant weight. Attached to each arm is a highly dexterous, five-fingered hand designed for human-like grasping and fine manipulation of objects and tools. The robot is capable of coordinating both arms to perform complex, two-handed tasks.

Operationally, the Robonaut follows a logical sequence for manipulation. It begins by perceiving its environment to identify a target object. It then plans the motion for its arms, moves them to approach the object, grasps it with the required force, performs the manipulation task, and finally releases the object. Throughout its operation, it sends telemetry data, such as its status and battery level, back to a monitoring system.
