This model describes the physical layout and flow of people and items within a section of a building. The system consists of three distinct areas: a central Hallway, an adjacent Classroom, and a Storeroom.

The Hallway acts as a primary corridor, connecting to both the Classroom and the Storeroom through separate entryways. The system is designed to manage the movement of different entities between these spaces.

Specifically, Students and Teachers can move back and forth between the Hallway and the Classroom. Furniture can be transported from the Hallway into either the Classroom or the Storeroom. A key feature of this layout is that access to the Storeroom is limited; only furniture is intended to move into it, not people. In addition, air is able to circulate between the hallway and both of the connected rooms.
