This model represents a simplified vehicle designed for a mass properties analysis. The vehicle is defined by its major sub-systems: an engine, a front axle assembly, and a rear axle assembly.

The core principle of this system is that the vehicle's total mass is determined by the sum of the individual masses of these key components. Each part—the engine, front axle, and rear axle—contributes its own weight to the overall vehicle. This represents a fundamental mass roll-up calculation common in engineering, where the properties of the whole system are derived from the properties of its constituent parts.
