This model represents a specific assembly of a push lawnmower, identified as "Model A." Its primary function is to cut grass through a sequence of operations: the operator starts the engine, pushes the mower to cut the lawn, and then shuts the engine off.

The lawnmower is constructed around a blue main deck, which serves as the central chassis. Four wheels are attached to the deck via pins, allowing it to roll across the ground. Each wheel is a two-part assembly consisting of a tire with standard tread and a metal rim. A handle is also connected to the deck for the user to push and guide the machine.

The power and cutting system consists of a single-piston engine and a 24-inch cutting blade, both mounted on the deck. The engine is a detailed internal combustion unit with an iron block. Its core components include a piston, a connecting rod, and a crankshaft with bearings, all interconnected to convert the piston's linear motion into the rotational force needed to spin the blade. The design also allows for some configuration, as the engine's connecting rod can be assembled with either standard steel bolts or an alternative, lighter aluminum version.
