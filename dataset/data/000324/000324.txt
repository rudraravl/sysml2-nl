This model describes a formal system for managing and linking project requirements, ensuring that high-level goals are correctly broken down into detailed specifications. The system is built around a fundamental relationship between two types of requirements: "original" and "derived".

An "original requirement" represents a broad, high-level objective or constraint for a project. From a single original requirement, one or more "derived requirements" are created. These are more specific, detailed, and measurable tasks or specifications that logically stem from the higher-level goal.

The core principle of this system is one of logical inheritance. If a product or system successfully meets its original requirement, it is enforced that it must also meet all of the detailed requirements derived from it. This creates a clear, traceable path from a general concept down to its specific implementation details. To ensure logical consistency, the system also includes a rule that prevents a requirement from being derived from itself, avoiding circular definitions.
