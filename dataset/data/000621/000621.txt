This model describes an electric therapeutic heating pad, a device designed to provide controlled and safe heat for pain relief, muscle relaxation, and general comfort.

The system is composed of several key components working together. A **Power Supply** plugs into a standard wall outlet and converts the high voltage AC power into a safer, low-voltage DC current to operate the pad. The user interacts with the system through a **User Interface**, which allows them to select one of several heat levels (off, low, medium, or high) and may also display the current temperature.

At the heart of the system is a **Controller Unit**, which acts as the brain. It receives the desired heat level from the user interface and continuously monitors the pad's temperature using a dedicated **Temperature Sensor**. Based on this feedback, the controller precisely manages the amount of power sent to the **Heating Element**, which is the component that converts electricity into heat. The entire heating assembly is enclosed within a soft **Fabric Cover**, ensuring user comfort and even heat distribution.

The heating pad operates in different states to manage temperature effectively. When first turned on, it enters a `heating` state, applying full power to quickly reach the target temperature. Once near the target, it transitions to a `maintaining` state, cycling power to hold the temperature steady within a very tight tolerance (±1°C). If it slightly overheats, it enters a `cooling` state, cutting power until the temperature falls back into the desired range.

Crucially, the system includes a dedicated **Safety Circuit**. This component independently monitors the temperature and automatically shuts down the heating element if it exceeds a maximum safe limit (60°C) to prevent burns. It also incorporates an auto-shutoff timer that turns the pad off after 60 minutes of continuous use, preventing overheating and reducing fire risk.
