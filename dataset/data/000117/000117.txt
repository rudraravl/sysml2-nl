This model represents the fundamental process of a camera taking a photograph. The system's main function is to capture a real-world "Scene" and produce a final "Picture".

This is accomplished through a two-step sequential process:

1.  First, the camera performs a **Focus** action. This is where the camera's lens system adjusts to project a sharp, clear optical image of the scene onto the camera's internal sensor or film plane.

2.  Once this focused image is successfully created, it is then used in the second step, the **Shoot** action. This represents the moment of capture, where the camera's shutter opens, exposing the sensor to the focused image and digitally recording it to create the final picture.

In essence, the system defines that to take a picture, you must first focus on a scene, and only then can you shoot to capture that focused image. The output of the focusing step is the direct input for the shooting step.
