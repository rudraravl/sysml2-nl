This model represents a comprehensive, standardized digital library of physical quantities and units for the field of electromagnetism. It is not a physical object but rather a foundational reference system based on the International System of Quantities (ISQ), specifically the IEC 80000-6 standard.

The library's purpose is to provide precise, formal definitions for the concepts used in physics and electrical engineering. It covers a vast range of electromagnetic phenomena, starting with fundamental concepts like electric charge, current, voltage, electric field strength, and magnetic flux density. It also defines the properties of materials, such as permittivity (how a material stores electric energy), permeability (how it supports the formation of a magnetic field), and conductivity (how well it conducts electric current).

Furthermore, the system defines concepts essential for analyzing electrical circuits, including resistance, capacitance, and inductance. For alternating current (AC) systems, it specifies more complex quantities like impedance, admittance, reactance, and the relationships between active, reactive, and apparent power. It even includes definitions for electromagnetic energy flow, such as the Poynting vector, and fundamental constants like the speed of light.

In essence, this is a foundational data dictionary that ensures engineers and scientists can build models and perform calculations with a consistent, universally understood set of definitions for every aspect of electromagnetism.
