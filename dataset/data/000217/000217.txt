This model describes a system designed to verify that a vehicle's mass complies with a specific requirement. The core rule being tested is that a vehicle must have a mass less than or equal to 2500 kg.

The verification system itself consists of a scale for measurement and a test operator who oversees the process. To perform a test, a specific vehicle is designated as the "vehicle under test."

The testing process follows a clear sequence of actions:
1.  **Data Collection:** The scale is used to measure the mass of the vehicle under test.
2.  **Data Processing:** The measured mass is processed to get a final value.
3.  **Evaluation:** This final mass is compared against the 2500 kg limit to determine if the vehicle satisfies the requirement. The system then issues a verdict, such as "Pass" or "Fail."

The model also outlines two specific test cases being run in sequence, one for a vehicle named "TestVehicle1" and another for "TestVehicle2," both of which have a mass of exactly 2500 kg.
