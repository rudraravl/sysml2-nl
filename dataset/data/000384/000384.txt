This model describes a European Earth observation satellite system designed for scientific monitoring of our planet's environment, with a particular focus on oceans and coastal regions. The project is supported by major stakeholders like the European Union and is intended to provide critical data to users such as environmental scientists, coastal biologists, and weather forecasters.

The overall system is composed of several major parts: a "Launcher Segment" (the rocket) to get it into space, the satellite itself (the "Space Segment"), and the "ESA/ESOC Ground Segment" which acts as the mission control center on Earth.

Once in orbit, the satellite is actively managed by the ground station. The ground segment sends commands to control the satellite's scientific instruments (the payload) and its Attitude and Orbit Control System (AOCS). This AOCS is responsible for precisely positioning the satellite and pointing its instruments, using thrusters to make orbital adjustments. In return, the satellite sends a continuous stream of telemetry data back to Earth, which includes both scientific observations and information about the satellite's own health and status. This two-way communication ensures the mission's objectives are met and the satellite remains operational.
