This model describes the "Eagle Eye Mission" satellite, an Earth observation spacecraft designed for environmental, coastal, and maritime scientific monitoring. The satellite is the core space-based component (the "Space Segment") of the mission, operating in a complex ecosystem of ground control, launch services, and data users.

Once placed in orbit by a launch vehicle, the satellite is operated and controlled by the ESA/ESOC Ground Segment. This ground control center communicates with the satellite by sending up commands and receiving down both telemetry (health and status data) and the valuable science data the satellite acquires while observing the Earth and oceans.

A key operational function of the satellite is to maintain its solar arrays pointed correctly, likely towards the sun, to ensure a constant power supply. It has the capability to independently calculate the optimal pointing angle for this.

The data collected by the Eagle Eye satellite is primarily intended for use by coastal and maritime biologists and environmental scientists. The overall mission is also subject to oversight from legal and policy actors, who impose requirements such as performing audits on the mission's CO2 emissions.
