This model describes a conventional, rear-wheel-drive pickup truck designed for transporting heavy cargo. The truck is built on a strong chassis capable of supporting a total vehicle weight of up to 3500 kg. Its primary purpose is to haul goods in its open-air cargo bed, which can carry a payload of up to 1200 kg. The cabin is designed to seat up to five people, including the driver.

The vehicle's powertrain starts with a 5.0-liter engine that receives fuel from a 110-liter tank. The engine's power is sent through a multi-gear transmission, which adjusts the torque and speed. From the transmission, a driveshaft transfers the mechanical power to a differential on the rear axle. This differential assembly then distributes the power to the two rear wheels, allowing them to turn at different speeds for smooth cornering. The system also includes four wheels, though only the rear two are powered by the engine.

Operationally, the truck performs transport missions. A typical mission involves loading a specific payload, such as 950 kg of building materials, at an origin like a distribution center. The driver then operates the vehicle to transport the payload to a destination, for example, a construction site. The core function of driving involves the engine generating power based on driver commands (like throttle input), which is then managed and delivered through the transmission and drivetrain to propel the vehicle forward. Once at the destination, the cargo is unloaded to complete the mission.
