This model describes a mechanical powertrain system, where the central component is an engine designed to generate rotational force (torque).

The system is defined by its core function: to act as a torque generator. A fundamental requirement is that it must produce a positive amount of torque, meaning it's capable of actively creating rotational power.

In the physical design, this function is carried out by a powertrain assembly. Within this powertrain, the engine is the specific component that performs the action of generating torque. Essentially, the abstract, functional role of a "Torque Generator" is physically realized by the engine component of the overall powertrain system.
