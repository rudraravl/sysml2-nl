This model describes a battery-powered medical device and the potential consequences of its failure on a patient.

The system consists of the medical device itself, which relies on an internal battery for power, and its interaction with a patient receiving treatment. The device's primary function is to provide some form of therapy.

The model highlights a critical failure pathway. The device's operation is dependent on its battery, which can fail in two ways: it can either be depleted of its charge, or it can degrade to a point where it can no longer be recharged. Either of these battery issues will directly cause the entire medical device to stop working. Consequently, when the device fails, it leads to a delay in the patient's prescribed therapy.
