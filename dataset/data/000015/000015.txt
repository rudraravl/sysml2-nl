This system describes the operational model of a mining corporation's fleet of mining frigates.  The corporation extracts valuable ore from asteroid fields and wormholes in various security zones (High, Low, Null, and Wormhole space).  Each frigate is equipped with mining lasers to extract ore, which is then refined onboard (in some cases) and stored in its cargo hold.  The frigates operate autonomously, but are centrally coordinated from a command center that dispatches them to mining sites based on resource availability and risk assessment.

The frigates are equipped with sensor arrays to detect hostile ships and defensive systems (like drones or turrets) to protect themselves.  Once mining is complete, the ore is transported to nearby stations or player-owned structures for processing or sale.  Before redeployment, the frigates resupply on fuel and life support.  The central command is also responsible for fleet maintenance and optimization to maximize profitability and operational efficiency.  The corporation's overall goal is to maximize profits, ensure the safety of its assets and pilots, and maintain a consistent flow of resources over the long term. A key performance indicator is maintaining a minimum profitability of 25,000 ISK per hour per frigate to incentivize pilot participation.
