This model describes a system for simulating the one-dimensional motion of a vehicle, like a car or a train, along a straight track. Its primary function is to predict the vehicle's position over time based on a predefined power plan.

The simulation requires several key inputs: a constant mass for the vehicle, its starting position and speed, and a "power profile," which is a sequence of power levels to be applied at successive, regular time intervals.

The system operates by stepping through the power profile, one interval at a time. In each step, it uses a core physics calculation to determine the vehicle's new position and speed based on the applied power, its mass, and its state from the previous step. As the simulation progresses, it continuously updates the vehicle's state and records each new position, ultimately producing a complete history of the vehicle's movement from start to finish.
