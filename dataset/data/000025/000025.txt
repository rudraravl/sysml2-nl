This SysML model describes a mining frigate, a spacecraft designed for asteroid mining operations.  The frigate's core functionality centers around several key actions:  mining ore, navigating to and from destinations, managing its energy supply, docking and undocking at stations, and defending itself against threats.

The mining process involves activating a mining laser, which is checked to ensure it is mining a viable asteroid. If the asteroid is depleted, a report is sent to the pilot. Otherwise, the laser continues extracting ore, and the extracted ore is then stored and subsequently transferred to a designated location.

Navigation includes warping to specified destinations and managing the ship's course. The frigate also has defensive capabilities to withstand incoming damage, involving threat detection and the activation of defensive systems.  Energy management is crucial, with the frigate capable of recharging its power supply.  Finally, the frigate can dock and undock at space stations for resource replenishment or repairs. The system supports different types of modules, allowing for customization and flexibility in mining operations.  The ship communicates its status through various reports, providing feedback on its activities and condition.
