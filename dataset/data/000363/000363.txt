This is a comprehensive digital library of the United States Customary system of measurement units, designed to provide a standardized and computable reference for engineering and scientific applications. It acts as a definitive "dictionary" for units commonly used in the U.S.

The system catalogues a wide array of non-metric units, covering fundamental quantities such as length (feet, inches, miles), mass (pounds, ounces, slugs), volume (gallons, barrels), and temperature (Fahrenheit, Rankine). It also includes many specialized and derived units for concepts like pressure (PSI), energy (Btu, horsepower), density (pounds per cubic foot), speed (miles per hour), and thermal conductivity.

A key feature of this system is that every U.S. unit is rigorously defined by its precise mathematical relationship to the International System of Units (SI), the modern metric system. This allows for unambiguous conversion between the two systems, for example, specifying the exact conversion from inches to meters, pounds-force to Newtons, and gallons to cubic meters.

This library is highly detailed, distinguishing between subtle variations like the standard foot versus the U.S. survey foot, or defining multiple types of British Thermal Units (Btu) for different thermal contexts. It also formally defines temperature scales like Fahrenheit, specifying not just the size of a degree but also its zero-point offset relative to the Kelvin scale, ensuring accurate temperature conversions.

Essentially, this is a foundational data model that allows software and engineering systems to correctly understand, calculate with, and convert between U.S. customary units and the global metric standard.
