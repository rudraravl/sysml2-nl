This is an automated system designed for precisely stripping the insulation off the end of tubes. Its purpose is to prepare tubes for further processing, such as fitting connectors or welding, by exposing a specific length of the core material underneath the insulation.

The system is comprised of several key components that work in a coordinated sequence. It includes a **Clamping Assembly** to securely hold the tube in place, a **Rotary Cutting Head** with a blade that can be set to a precise depth, a **Pulling Mechanism** to remove the insulation, and a **Quality Inspection Unit** to verify the result. The entire operation is managed by a central **Controller Unit** and powered by an internal **Power Supply**.

The stripping process is fully automated. First, an insulated tube is fed into the system. The clamping assembly then engages, holding the tube with a specific pressure to prevent it from moving. Next, the rotary cutting head positions its blade to match the thickness of the insulation and scores a clean, circumferential cut without damaging the tube's core. Once the cut is made, the pulling mechanism grips the piece of insulation and pulls it off the end of the tube.

After the insulation is removed, the quality inspection unit automatically checks the stripped tube to ensure the length of the exposed core is accurate and that no surface damage has occurred. Finally, the clamping assembly releases the finished tube, which can then be collected. The piece of removed insulation is ejected into a dedicated **Waste Collection Bin**. An operator can control the system through commands to start, stop, or perform an emergency stop, and can set key parameters like the desired strip length. The system provides real-time status updates on its operation and the results of the quality check.
