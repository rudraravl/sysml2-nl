This model describes a vehicle, such as a car or truck, defined by its core structural and powertrain components. The vehicle consists of a chassis, an engine, and a transmission.

The central focus of this model is on a critical design constraint related to the vehicle's total weight. The system is engineered so that the combined mass of the chassis, the engine, and the transmission must not exceed a limit of 2500 kilograms. This type of weight restriction is a fundamental aspect of vehicle design, influencing everything from material selection and performance to fuel efficiency and safety standards.
