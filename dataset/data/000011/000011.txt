This model describes the core functionality of an online shopping system, focusing on how a customer selects products and adds them to a shopping cart.

The system consists of Online Customers, Products, and a Shopping Cart. Each customer is provided with their own personal shopping cart. The primary function of this system is to allow a customer to browse a collection of available products and select one or more of them to be placed into their cart.

When a customer decides to add a product to their cart, a "Product Selection" event occurs. This event creates a direct link between that specific product and the customer's cart. This selection is not just a simple link; it also carries its own "Selection Info," which could represent real-world details like the date the item was added, the quantity selected, or any chosen configurations (like size or color).

In essence, the system models the dynamic process where a customer populates their personal shopping cart by creating specific, information-rich connections to the products they wish to purchase.
