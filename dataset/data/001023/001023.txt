This is a Dew Warning System designed to monitor environmental conditions and prevent condensation damage on a specific surface. Its primary purpose is to continuously track the risk of dew formation and alert operators before moisture can cause problems for sensitive equipment, materials, or infrastructure.

The system is comprised of several interconnected components that work together to provide real-time alerts. It uses an ambient sensor to measure the surrounding air temperature and relative humidity. Simultaneously, a dedicated surface sensor measures the actual temperature of the surface being protected.

This environmental data is fed into a dew point processor, which calculates the exact dew point—the temperature at which condensation will begin to form under the current atmospheric conditions. A risk analyzer then serves as the system's core logic unit. It constantly compares the temperature of the monitored surface to the calculated dew point.

Based on how close the surface temperature is to the dew point, the system assesses the risk level. It provides a graduated, four-tier alert: "safe" when there's no risk, "caution" as conditions become more favorable for dew, "warning" when the risk is high, and "critical" when dew formation is imminent. These levels are triggered by specific temperature difference thresholds. Finally, an alert generator sends a corresponding signal and a clear text message to a visual indicator, providing operators with a clear and immediate understanding of the current condensation risk.
