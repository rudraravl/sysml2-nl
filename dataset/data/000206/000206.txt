This model describes a vehicle refueling system, designed to transfer fuel from a main storage reservoir to a vehicle.

The system consists of three main components: a large `StorageTank`, a `Pump`, and the `Vehicle` being refueled. The `StorageTank` serves as the primary source of the fuel. The `Pump` is an active component that draws fuel from this storage tank and then dispenses it to the `Vehicle`.

The `Vehicle` is equipped with its own internal `FuelTank`, which is the final destination for the transferred fuel. This onboard `FuelTank` has a defined maximum volume (capacity), and the system keeps track of the current `fuelLevel` based on how much fuel it contains.

In essence, this model represents a complete fueling operation, illustrating the pathway of fuel from a stationary source, through a pumping mechanism, and into a vehicle's tank.
