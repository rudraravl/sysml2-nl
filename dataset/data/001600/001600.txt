This is a system for managing and verifying the maturity of new technologies, ensuring they are ready for operational use. It provides a structured framework for taking a prototype technology through a series of evaluations and tests to advance its Technology Readiness Level (TRL) on the standard 1-to-9 scale.

The system is composed of three main parts: the technology prototype being evaluated, a specialized test environment, and an assessment authority. The **Prototype Subsystem** is the actual piece of hardware or software under development, which has defined performance characteristics like reliability, efficiency, operating temperature, and power consumption. It can receive commands and send back status and telemetry data.

This prototype is tested within a **Test Environment**, which is designed to simulate the conditions the technology will face in the real world, such as specific thermal, vibration, or electromagnetic stresses. The prototype and test environment are connected so that test commands can be sent and results can be measured.

Finally, an **Assessment Authority**, such as a "Technology Review Board," is responsible for evaluating the test results. This authority receives the readiness level data from the prototype and makes an official decision on its maturity.

The overall process is cyclical: the system conducts demonstrations of the prototype within the test environment. The performance metrics and test results are then formally assessed to determine the technology's current TRL. If the technology has not yet reached its target TRL, further development and testing are recommended. This cycle of testing and assessment repeats until the technology successfully meets the requirements for its target readiness level, such as achieving TRL 6 before being approved for integration into a larger system.
