This model describes an integrated medical device for patient monitoring and automated therapy delivery, designed for use in critical care settings and compliant with stringent European regulations.

The system functions as a smart, bedside unit that continuously tracks a patient's vital signs, including heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and respiratory rate. This data is collected by a high-precision vital signs monitor.

The core of the device is an intelligent clinical decision engine. This certified software component analyzes the incoming vital signs in real-time to assess the patient's condition. Based on its analysis, the engine can automatically calculate and command the delivery of a precise medical therapy, such as a specific drug dosage and flow rate, through an integrated infusion pump. If the patient's vitals enter a dangerous range, the engine will automatically trigger alarms of varying severity (info, warning, or critical) to alert medical staff.

The system is built with a strong emphasis on safety, security, and regulatory compliance. It includes a dedicated power supply with a battery backup that can sustain operation for up to six hours during a power outage. A data security module ensures all patient data is encrypted to GDPR standards before being transmitted over a hospital network. Furthermore, a regulatory compliance monitor continuously verifies that the device's operations adhere to European Medical Device Regulations (MDR) and ISO 13485 quality standards.

The device operates through a clear lifecycle, starting from power-up and standby, moving to active monitoring, and then engaging in therapy delivery and compliance checks as needed. The entire system is designed as a single, self-contained unit weighing under 5 kg.
