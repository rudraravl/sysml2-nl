This model describes a physical vehicle, such as a car or truck, focusing on its fundamental mass properties and dynamic behavior.

The vehicle is defined by its core components: a chassis, an engine, and a transmission. A key aspect of this system is how its total mass is determined. The model establishes a rule that the vehicle's overall mass is simply the sum of the individual masses of its chassis, engine, and transmission.

Furthermore, the model defines the physical laws of motion that govern the vehicle. It establishes a dynamic relationship between force, mass, and changes in speed. Specifically, it dictates that the change in the vehicle's speed over a period of time is directly proportional to the force applied to it and inversely proportional to its total mass, adhering to fundamental principles of physics (Impulse-Momentum Theorem).
