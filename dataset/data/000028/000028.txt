This SysML model describes the use cases for a Mining Frigate Hull.  It's structured into two packages: `MiningFrigateHullUseCasesDef` and `MiningFrigateHullUseCasesUsage`.

`MiningFrigateHullUseCasesDef` defines the individual use cases:

* **Withstand Incoming Damage:** The hull must withstand damage from hostile ships while protecting the pilot.  Actors involved are the hull, a hostile ship, and the pilot pod.
* **Navigate and Warp to Destinations:** The hull must navigate between celestial bodies using its navigation system.  Actors are the hull and the pilot pod.
* **Store and Transfer Mined Ore:** The hull must store mined ore and transfer it to a station or hauler.  Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a station.
* **Dock and Undock from Stations:** The hull must support docking and undocking at stations.  Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a station.
* **Manage Energy Supply and Recharge:** The hull must manage its energy supply through capacitor recharge.  Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a small capacitor battery.
* **Support High, Medium, and Low Slot Modules:** The hull must support various modules for different mission profiles.  Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a mining laser.
* **Detect Threat:** The hull must detect incoming threats using sensors and warn the pilot. Actors are the hull and the pilot pod.
* **Engage Defenses:** The hull must activate defensive systems to mitigate threats. Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a hostile ship.
* **Navigate to Destination:** The hull must navigate between waypoints or destinations. Actors are the hull and the pilot pod.
* **Transfer Ore:** The hull must transfer stored ore to a station or hauler. Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a station.
* **Dock at Station:** The hull must dock at a station for refueling, maintenance, and ore transfer. Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a station.
* **Resupply Capacitor:** The hull must replenish its capacitor charge. Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a small capacitor battery.
* **Activate Mining Laser:** The hull must activate its mining laser to extract ore. Actors are the hull, the pilot pod, and a mining laser.


`MiningFrigateHullUseCasesUsage` shows how these individual use cases are combined into higher-level use cases:

* **Withstand Incoming Damage:** This use case includes "Detect Threat" and then "Engage Defenses".  The pilot pod is the actor in both included use cases.
* **Navigate and Warp to Destinations:** This use case includes "Navigate to Destination". The pilot pod is the actor.
* **Store and Transfer Ore:** This use case includes "Transfer Ore". The pilot pod and a station are the actors.
* **Dock and Undock from Stations:** This use case includes "Dock at Station". The pilot pod and a station are the actors.
* **Manage Energy Supply and Recharge:** This use case includes "Resupply Capacitor". The pilot pod and a small capacitor battery are the actors.
* **Support High, Medium, and Low Slot Modules:** This use case includes "Activate Mining Laser". The mining laser is the actor.


In essence, this model provides a hierarchical decomposition of the functionality of a mining frigate hull, defining both the individual capabilities (use case definitions) and how these capabilities are combined to achieve higher-level tasks (use case usage).  The model clearly shows the actors involved in each use case and how they interact.
