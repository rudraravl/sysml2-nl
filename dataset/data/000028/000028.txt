This SysML model describes a mining frigate hull, a spacecraft designed for asteroid mining.  The frigate's primary function is to locate, mine, and transport ore to designated stations.  The hull itself is the subject of numerous use cases, interacting with a pilot pod (the ship's control center and operator interface),  mining lasers (modules for extracting ore),  stations (for ore transfer and resupply), and potentially hostile ships.

The frigate's capabilities include:

* **Mining:** The ship uses mining lasers to extract ore from asteroids.  These lasers are modular, allowing for different configurations depending on the mission.
* **Navigation and Warp:** The frigate can navigate to different celestial bodies, using its onboard navigation system for both conventional and warp-speed travel.
* **Ore Storage and Transfer:**  The hull contains an ore hold for storing extracted materials, and systems allowing for the transfer of this ore to stations or other vessels.
* **Docking and Undocking:** The ship is capable of docking at stations for refueling, maintenance, and ore offloading.
* **Energy Management:** The frigate manages its energy supply, including the use of capacitors and rechargeable batteries (at least one type,  a "small capacity" battery) for powering its systems.
* **Defense:**  The hull can detect incoming threats through its onboard sensors and engage defensive systems, such as shields or warp core stabilizers, to protect itself from hostile actions.


In short, the mining frigate is a self-contained vessel capable of autonomously conducting mining operations, managing its own energy, defending itself from threats, and interacting with other spacecraft and stations within a larger spacefaring context.  The pilot pod acts as the interface between the ship's automated systems and a human operator.
