This is a conceptual model that defines the relationship between people and countries, specifically focusing on the role of a president. The system establishes a set of rules and definitions for these real-world entities.

The model defines two primary concepts: a "Person" and a "Country". A Person is characterized by having an age. The model also recognizes that a person can serve as a president during specific periods of time. A Country is an entity that can have one president, who must be a Person.

The system then models a specific country, the United States, which has more stringent rules. The United States is defined as always having exactly one president. A key constraint is applied: any person serving as the president of the United States must be at least 35 years old.

Finally, the model describes a specific scenario involving an individual named John. It represents the particular periods in time when John is the president of the United States, thereby connecting the specific individual (John) to the specific country (United States) while adhering to its established rules.
