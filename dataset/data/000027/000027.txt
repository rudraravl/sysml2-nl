This is a Venture-class mining frigate, a spacecraft designed for asteroid mining operations.  The frigate has a fixed hull structure containing a warp core, ore hold, and basic defensive systems.  Its core functionality is enhanced through a modular design, allowing for the installation and swapping of commercially-off-the-shelf (COTS) components.

These modules include mining lasers for breaking down asteroids, propulsion modules for faster-than-light travel and maneuvering, shield extenders for protection, capacitor batteries for energy storage, survey scanners for resource detection, and additional warp core stabilizers to improve warp travel stability.  The frigate possesses several slots (high, medium, and low) for these modules; each slot type accepts specific module types.  The hull itself provides essential functions, including navigation (course plotting, course adjustment), docking and undocking procedures, capacitor recharging, ore transfer, and threat detection and defensive engagement. The overall system is designed for autonomous operation, with the ability to accept destinations, scan for resources, and manage its power and defensive systems effectively during mining operations.  The capacity of the ore hold limits the amount of mined material that can be carried before requiring a transfer to a docking station.
