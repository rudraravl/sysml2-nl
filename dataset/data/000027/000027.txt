This model represents a system for analyzing the fuel economy of a vehicle. The system is centered around a vehicle model which is composed of a single engine.

The vehicle's overall power is defined as being equal to the peak power of its engine. The engine itself is characterized by two key attributes: its peak power, measured in horsepower, and a numerical value representing its fuel efficiency.

The primary purpose of this system is to perform a fuel economy analysis. This analysis takes a specific vehicle configuration, uses its power output as an input for a fuel consumption calculation, and determines the vehicle's fuel economy in miles per gallon (MPG).

The model includes a specific instance of this analysis being performed. In this case, a vehicle with a 200-horsepower engine is analyzed, and the system calculates its resulting fuel economy to be 35 miles per gallon.
