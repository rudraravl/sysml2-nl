This model represents a simple physical system of a cart being acted upon by a pusher.

The system consists of two main components: a cart and a pusher. The cart is an object with a specific mass, and its motion is governed by basic physics. Its primary observable characteristic is its velocity. The pusher is an entity that generates a constant, predefined force.

In operation, the pusher continuously applies its force to the cart. This force causes the cart to accelerate, and its velocity changes over time according to Newton's second law (Force = mass × acceleration). The system continuously calculates the cart's acceleration based on the applied force and its mass, thereby determining its changing velocity.
