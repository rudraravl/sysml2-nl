This model represents the ascent trajectory data for a launched vehicle, such as a rocket or projectile. It defines a specific flight path by providing a series of snapshots, or samples, that record the vehicle's state at discrete moments in time.

The system establishes a "Mission Elapsed Time" scale, which starts counting in seconds from the exact moment of launch. This launch time is precisely defined as August 23, 2020, at 22:42:32.924534 UTC.

For each time sample, the model records the vehicle's complete state of motion. This state includes both its 3D spatial position, measured in meters from the launch point, and its 3D velocity, measured in meters per second.

The example ascent profile provided details the vehicle's flight during its first 5.1 seconds. It shows the vehicle starting at rest at the launch point (time 0, position 0, velocity 0), and then traces its path as it rapidly gains altitude and accelerates. For instance, at 5.1 seconds after launch, the vehicle is recorded to be at a position of (0.04, 0.12, 18.6) meters and moving at a velocity of (0.05, 0.03, 25.3) meters per second.
