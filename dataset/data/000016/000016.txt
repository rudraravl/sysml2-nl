This SysML v2 model describes the stakeholders and their concerns in a space mining corporation.  The model is divided into two packages: `StakeholdersDef` and `StakeholderConcerns`.

**StakeholdersDef:** This package defines the roles of key individuals within the corporation:

* **MiningCEO:** Responsible for overall profitability, corporate strategy, and risk management.
* **OperationsManager:**  Focuses on minimizing operational costs and optimizing ore delivery.
* **FleetCommander:** Manages fleet operations, scalability, survivability, and threat neutralization.
* **ShipEngineer:** Concentrates on ship survivability and performance optimization in hostile environments.
* **MarketAnalyst:** Monitors ore market prices and advises on market trends and strategies.


**StakeholderConcerns:** This package defines concerns related to the corporation's mining operations. These concerns are categorized and linked to specific stakeholders based on their responsibilities:

**Concerns derived from the Business Case:** These concerns focus on the overall financial health and strategic direction of the corporation.

* **ProfitabilityConcern:**  Ensuring sufficient ISK/hour profit to justify investments (CEO).
* **OperationalCostsConcern:** Minimizing operational costs to maximize profit (Operations Manager).
* **ScalabilityConcern:**  Assuming the ability to scale mining operations effectively (Fleet Commander).
* **MarketResponsivenessConcern:** Adapting to fluctuating market demands (Market Analyst).


**Concerns derived from the Concept of Operations (ConOps):** These concerns relate to the day-to-day operational aspects of mining.

* **SecurityConcern:** Ensuring the safety and survivability of mining frigates in hostile environments (Fleet Commander, Ship Engineer).
* **ResourceAvailabilityConcern:**  Ensuring access to sufficient high-value resources (Fleet Commander).
* **DeploymentEfficiencyConcern:** Ensuring rapid and efficient movement of mining frigates (Operations Manager).
* **ThreatNeutralizationConcern:** Mitigating risks from pirates and hostile players (Fleet Commander, Ship Engineer).


**Concerns derived from the Measures of Effectiveness (MoEs):** These concerns define how success will be measured.

* **TotalProfitabilityConcern:** Maximizing total ISK/hour while minimizing costs and losses (CEO).
* **OreYieldConcern:** Maximizing ore yield per frigate or fleet (Operations Manager).
* **SurvivabilityConcern:** Ensuring high survivability of mining frigates (Fleet Commander, Ship Engineer).
* **FleetDeploymentEfficiencyConcern:** Ensuring optimal fleet deployment for maximum ore extraction and minimal downtime (Fleet Commander, Operations Manager).
* **OreDeliveryEfficiencyConcern:** Ensuring fast and efficient ore transport (Operations Manager).
* **RiskAdjustedProfitabilityConcern:** Balancing profitability with the risks of operating in hostile environments (CEO, Fleet Commander).


In summary, the model uses SysML to represent the relationships between stakeholders and their respective concerns within the context of a space mining corporation, providing a structured view of requirements and responsibilities.  The use of `require` and `assume` constraints further clarifies the nature of each concern (e.g., a requirement versus an assumption).
