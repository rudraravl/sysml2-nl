This SysML model describes a corporation engaged in space mining operations.  The corporation uses a fleet of mining frigates to extract ore from asteroids and other celestial bodies.  The overall goal is to maximize profitability while mitigating various risks.

Several key roles are involved: the Mining CEO focuses on overall profitability and corporate strategy; the Operations Manager prioritizes cost minimization and efficient ore delivery; the Fleet Commander manages fleet operations, scalability, and threat neutralization; the Ship Engineer ensures the survivability and optimal performance of the mining frigates; and the Market Analyst tracks ore prices and advises on market-driven operational adjustments.

The mining operation faces several concerns: maintaining profitability, controlling operational costs, ensuring the fleet's scalability, adapting to market fluctuations, and ensuring the safety and survivability of the mining frigates in potentially hostile environments (like low-security or null-security space).  Efficient deployment of the fleet to maximize ore extraction and minimize downtime is crucial, as is the efficient delivery of the mined ore to market.  The corporation must also balance the risks inherent in operating in dangerous areas with the potential for higher profits.  Each concern relates to the responsibilities of one or more of the key stakeholders, illustrating the interconnectedness of their roles in the success of the mining operation.
