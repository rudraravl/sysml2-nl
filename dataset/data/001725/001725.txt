This is a model of the QIVICON Smart Home System, an integrated platform for home automation. The system is designed to connect, manage, and control a variety of smart devices within a home, providing users with remote access, security monitoring, and automated routines through a central hub and cloud-based service.

The core of the system is the **QIVICON Home Base**, a physical hub located in the user's home. This base acts as the central coordinator, communicating directly with all the smart devices and connecting to the internet to enable remote functionality. It can operate in different security modes, such as "home," "away," or "night" mode, to manage device behavior and alerts accordingly.

Users interact with the system primarily through a **Mobile App**. This app connects to a **Cloud Backend** service, allowing users to monitor the status of their devices and send commands from anywhere. For example, a user can check if a door is open, adjust the thermostat, or turn on a light remotely.

The cloud backend is the system's brain in the cloud. It authenticates user commands from the app, relays them to the home base, and stores historical data from the devices. Crucially, it also runs automation rules, allowing the system to perform actions automatically based on triggers, such as turning on the lights when a motion sensor detects movement after sunset.

The system supports a diverse ecosystem of smart devices, including:

*   **Heating Thermostats:** These devices monitor the ambient temperature and control the home's heating or cooling systems to maintain a desired setpoint temperature.
*   **Smart Light Bulbs:** These allow for remote control of lighting, including turning them on or off and adjusting their brightness and color temperature.
*   **Door & Window Sensors:** These are battery-powered security devices that detect and report whether a door or window is open or closed, triggering alerts if necessary.
*   **Motion Sensors:** These devices detect movement within a specific area, which can be used to trigger security alerts or automate actions like turning on lights.
*   **Smart Plugs:** These plugs fit into standard wall outlets and allow any connected appliance to be controlled remotely. They also monitor the appliance's real-time power consumption and track its total energy usage over time.

In operation, a command from the mobile app travels to the cloud backend, which then securely sends it to the home base. The home base routes the command to the appropriate device, such as a smart plug or light bulb. Similarly, data from sensors and devices flows back through the home base to the cloud, where it is processed and made available for the user to view on their mobile app.
