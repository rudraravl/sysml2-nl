This model describes a personal transportation system centered around a vehicle, such as a passenger car. Its primary purpose is to transport a driver and up to four passengers from a starting point to a destination.

The system's operation follows a clear sequence. A trip begins with the driver and passengers entering the vehicle. The driver then operates the vehicle, and during the journey, if the fuel level becomes low (for instance, dropping below 10%), the driver can take the vehicle to a fuel station to be refueled. This refueling process can be performed as many times as needed. Once the vehicle reaches its destination, the trip concludes with the driver and passengers exiting.

The overall goal of this system is to provide transportation that is safe, reliable, comfortable, and affordable, while operating within its environment.
