This model describes an autonomous robotic vacuum cleaner system, designed to automatically clean floor surfaces in a home or office environment. The system consists of the main cleaning robot and its companion charging dock.

The robot itself is a compact, circular unit built for navigation and cleaning. Its core cleaning mechanism is multi-stage: two rotating side brushes sweep debris from edges and corners towards the center, where a larger, main rotating brush agitates and lifts dirt from the floor surface. A powerful suction fan then pulls the dislodged dust and debris into an internal, high-efficiency filtered dust container.

For movement, the robot is equipped with two main drive wheels, allowing it to navigate and turn precisely. Power for all its components is supplied by a rechargeable internal lithium battery.

To navigate its environment autonomously, the vacuum relies on a sophisticated suite of sensors. Infrared (IR) sensors on the front and sides detect obstacles from a distance, while a physical bumper sensor provides a secondary detection method upon contact. A set of cliff sensors on the underside of the robot prevents it from falling down stairs or off ledges. A dedicated dirt detection sensor allows the vacuum to identify heavily soiled areas and focus its cleaning efforts there. All this sensor data is processed by a central navigation controller, which creates a path, controls the wheel motors, and manages the cleaning process.

The vacuum operates through several states. When not in use, it rests in an idle state at its charging dock, fully charged. Once activated, it enters an active cleaning mode, systematically navigating the room. If it encounters a particularly dirty area, it can switch to a more intensive spot cleaning mode. It continuously uses its sensors to maneuver around furniture and other obstacles.

A key feature is its autonomous recharging capability. When the battery level drops below a certain threshold, the vacuum will automatically stop its cleaning task, navigate back to the charging dock, and recharge itself. Once the battery is full, it returns to an idle state, ready for the next cleaning cycle.
