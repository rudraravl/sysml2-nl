This model describes a multi-component surgical face mask designed to protect the wearer by filtering airborne particles from inhaled air.

The mask is constructed from three distinct layers that work in sequence to clean the air. The **outer layer**, made of spunbond polypropylene, serves as the first line of defense, filtering larger particles and providing resistance to fluid splashes. Air then passes through the critical **middle layer**, which is an electrostatically charged, meltblown polypropylene filter. This layer is highly efficient, designed to capture over 98% of very fine, sub-micron particles like viruses and bacteria. Finally, the soft **inner layer**, also made of spunbond polypropylene, provides a comfortable surface against the wearer's skin while offering an additional level of filtration.

To ensure the mask is effective, it must fit securely on the user's face. This is achieved with two key components: a flexible **aluminum nose piece** that can be molded to the bridge of the nose to prevent air leakage, and two **elastic ear loops** that hold the mask firmly but comfortably in place.

In operation, the wearer puts on the mask, securing it with the ear loops and shaping the nose piece for a tight seal. As they breathe in, ambient air is drawn through the three-layer filter system, which traps harmful contaminants before the purified air reaches the user's nose and mouth. The overall design is intended to meet high standards for bacterial and particle filtration while remaining lightweight and breathable for extended wear.
