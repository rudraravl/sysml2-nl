This model describes the powertrain of a mechanical system, such as a vehicle. The primary function of this system is to provide power, which it accomplishes by generating torque.

The system consists of a physical powertrain assembly which contains an engine. The engine is the specific, physical component that is responsible for carrying out the function of generating torque for the entire system. Essentially, the engine is designated as the system's "torque generator" within the powertrain.
