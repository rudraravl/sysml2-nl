This model describes a component, likely an electronic controller or signal generator, designed to output a specific, time-varying power command signal. The component operates over a predefined 2-second cycle, controlling the power level of an external system according to a set sequence.

Upon activation, the component begins its cycle by commanding a power level of zero. This state is maintained for the first second. Then, for the next half-second (from 1 to 1.5 seconds into the cycle), the component actively ramps up the power command. During this phase, the commanded power level increases continuously over time. The entire operational sequence concludes precisely at the two-second mark, at which point the component issues a final, steady power level command of 1.
