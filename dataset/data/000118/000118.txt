This model describes the process of taking a photograph. The system takes a real-world 'scene' as its input and produces a finished 'picture' as its final output.

The overall process is broken down into two distinct, sequential steps: focusing and shooting. First, the system performs a "Focus" action, which takes the external scene and creates a sharp, properly-defined 'image' internally (like the image projected onto a camera's sensor by the lens).

Following the focusing step, the system performs a "Shoot" action. This second step takes the internally focused image and captures it, creating the final, permanent 'picture'. The process requires that the focusing action successfully produces an image before the shooting action can capture it.
