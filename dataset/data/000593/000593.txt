This model describes a portable oxygen concentrator, a compact medical device designed for patients who require supplemental oxygen therapy. Its primary purpose is to provide a continuous supply of highly concentrated, medical-grade oxygen, allowing patients greater mobility and independence compared to traditional oxygen tanks.

The device works by drawing in ambient air through a filtered intake port. This air is then pressurized by an internal compressor and fed into a molecular sieve unit. This key component uses a pressure swing adsorption process with two sieve beds containing zeolite material, which selectively traps nitrogen from the air while allowing oxygen to pass through. By alternating between the two beds, the system generates a continuous stream of oxygen-rich air.

This concentrated oxygen is held briefly in a small accumulator tank to ensure a steady, smooth output before being delivered to the patient. A flow regulation system allows the patient or caregiver to adjust the oxygen delivery rate (from 0.5 to 5.0 liters per minute) to match a doctor's prescription.

For versatility and portability, the concentrator features a dual power system. It can be plugged into a standard AC wall outlet for home use or run on a rechargeable battery pack for use on the go. An onboard charging controller manages the battery's health.

The entire system is managed by a central controller board, which acts as the device's brain. It monitors performance using an array of sensors that measure oxygen purity, flow rate, internal pressure, and temperature. A user interface panel with an LCD screen and buttons allows for easy operation. For patient safety, a dedicated alarm subsystem provides both audible and visual alerts for conditions such as low oxygen purity, low battery, high pressure, or a system malfunction.

The device operates through various states, including being powered off, in standby for system checks, fully operational for oxygen delivery, or in an alarm or fault state if a problem is detected. It is designed to be lightweight (under 3 kg) and quiet to minimize disruption to the user's daily life.
