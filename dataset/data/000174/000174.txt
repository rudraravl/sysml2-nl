This model describes a system for analyzing and verifying the fuel economy of a specific vehicle. The system is set up to evaluate the vehicle's performance against two distinct, standardized driving scenarios: one simulating city driving conditions and another for highway driving.

For the vehicle under test, the system conducts two separate fuel economy analyses. The first analysis calculates the vehicle's fuel efficiency based on the city driving scenario, while the second calculates its efficiency for the highway scenario.

The results of these analyses—the specific city and highway fuel economy figures—are then attributed to the vehicle. The ultimate purpose of this system is to use these calculated performance metrics to confirm that the vehicle successfully meets a predefined set of fuel economy requirements.
