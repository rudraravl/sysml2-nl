This model represents an advanced, automated birthing chair system designed to provide adjustable, gravity-assisted positional support for a mother during labor and delivery. The primary purpose of the system is to enable various upright and semi-upright birthing positions, which can help facilitate the birthing process while ensuring the mother's comfort and safety, as well as providing necessary clinical access for medical staff.

The chair is built on a robust, reinforced steel structural frame with stabilizers for maximum load capacity and stability. Its modular design consists of several key, independently adjustable components:

*   **Backrest Module:** A padded backrest that can be electronically reclined or raised. It is powered by a motor and includes a locking mechanism to hold its position securely.
*   **Seat Module:** The main seat features medical-grade foam cushioning and a special opening designed for the delivery process. It can be tilted forward and backward by a dedicated actuator to optimize pelvic alignment.
*   **Leg Support Module:** This consists of two separate, stirrup-equipped leg supports. Each support can be adjusted in angle, and the distance between them can also be widened or narrowed. This allows for a wide range of leg positions to be comfortably and securely maintained.
*   **Armrest Module:** The chair includes adjustable-height armrests on both sides, providing grips for leverage and support.

The entire system is managed by a central control unit, which acts as the brain of the chair. This unit contains a microcontroller, position sensors, and load sensors. It receives commands to adjust the chair to specific pre-set or custom position profiles. Based on these commands, it precisely controls the motors in the backrest, seat, and leg modules.

Operationally, the chair functions in several states, including a **standby** mode, a **positioning** mode where it actively adjusts its configuration, an **active support** mode where it holds a position, and an **emergency** mode. A key feature is its continuous monitoring of weight and pressure distribution across its surfaces, ensuring both patient comfort and safety. If an unsafe load or an emergency stop command is received, the system can immediately halt all movement and lock its components in place.
