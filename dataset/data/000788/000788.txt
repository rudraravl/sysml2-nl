This model describes a Syrette, a type of single-use, pre-filled medication delivery device designed for rapid and simple administration, often in emergency or field settings. It is a self-contained injection system that requires no assembly before use.

The device consists of a main housing body made of medical-grade plastic, which holds several integrated components. The core of the system is a barrel that comes pre-filled with a specific dose of a particular medication, such as Morphine or Atropine. A sterile needle is attached to the end of this barrel, and it is kept protected and clean by a removable safety cap. At the other end is a plunger, which, when pressed, applies pressure to the medication in the barrel.

The Syrette's operation follows a distinct sequence of states. It is initially in a sealed and sterile state with the safety cap securely in place. To use it, an administrator first removes the cap, exposing the needle and putting the device in a "ready" state. After inserting the needle, the user applies pressure to the plunger, which moves the device into a "delivering" state, forcing the medication out of the barrel and through the needle into the patient. Once all the medication has been administered, the device is considered "empty" and is disposed of.

The overall design is a platform that can be adapted for different medications. The model shows examples of a Morphine Syrette and an Atropine Syrette, each configured with a different dose volume, needle length, and needle gauge, tailored to the specific drug and its required administration method. Key requirements for the system include maintaining sterility until use, delivering the correct dose accurately, and ensuring the needle is safely protected by the cap to prevent accidental injury.
