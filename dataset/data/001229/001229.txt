This model describes a Rotary Variable Differential Transformer (RVDT), a high-precision electromechanical sensor designed for contactless measurement of angular position. It is used in applications where a physical shaft's rotational angle needs to be monitored accurately and reliably, such as in aerospace flight control systems, industrial robotics, and precision servo controls.

The core of the system is a transformer with one primary coil, a specially shaped magnetic rotor, and a pair of secondary coils. The entire assembly is designed so that as an external shaft turns, the rotor attached to it also turns. This rotation changes the magnetic relationship between the primary and secondary coils.

Here’s how it works in practice:

1.  **Excitation:** An external AC power source sends a constant voltage and frequency signal to the RVDT's primary coil. This energizes the coil, generating an alternating magnetic field.

2.  **Magnetic Coupling:** The rotor, which is a key component made of a specific ferromagnetic material, is positioned within this magnetic field. As the input shaft rotates the rotor, its unique shape directs or "couples" the magnetic field from the primary coil to the two secondary coils. The amount of magnetic energy transferred to each secondary coil varies depending on the precise angle of the rotor.

3.  **Voltage Induction:** This varying magnetic coupling induces an AC voltage in each of the two secondary coils. When the rotor is at its "zero" position, the voltages in both secondary coils are equal. As the rotor turns, the voltage in one coil increases while the voltage in the other decreases. The difference between these two voltages is directly proportional to the angle of the shaft.

4.  **Signal Processing:** The raw AC voltage outputs from the secondary coils are then processed by internal electronics. A **demodulator circuit** takes these two signals and compares their amplitude and phase to the original excitation signal. This process extracts the essential angular information. An **angle calculator** then takes this demodulated signal, applies calibration and temperature compensation data, and converts it into a final, precise angular position measurement.

The RVDT operates in several states, including an **Idle** state when unpowered, an **Energized** state when power is applied, and a **Measuring** state during active operation. It is designed to be highly robust, capable of operating over a full 360-degree range and across a wide spectrum of temperatures and excitation frequencies, while maintaining a very low linearity error for high-accuracy applications. Its contactless design ensures a long operational life free from mechanical wear.
