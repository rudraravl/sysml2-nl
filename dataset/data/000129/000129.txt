This model describes a digital camera system built from distinct functional components. Its main purpose is to capture a photograph through an ordered sequence of internal actions.

The camera system is composed of two primary sub-components: an **AutoFocus** module and an **Imager** (the image sensor). When the camera is instructed to take a photo, these components work together in a specific order. First, the AutoFocus module performs its function to adjust the lens and achieve a sharp focus on the subject. Once focusing is complete, the Imager then executes its action to capture the light and record the final digital image. This demonstrates a collaborative process where specialized parts are responsible for different steps of a single, high-level operation.
