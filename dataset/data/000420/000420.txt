This is an Alternating Electric Field Therapy System, a medical device designed for cancer treatment. Its primary function is to disrupt the division of cancer cells (mitosis) by applying low-intensity, intermediate-frequency electric fields directly to a tumor. These fields interfere with the formation of critical structures needed for cell division, causing the rapidly dividing tumor cells to die while having minimal effect on healthy, non-dividing tissue. To ensure comprehensive treatment of the tumor, the system continuously rotates the orientation of the electric fields.

The system is composed of several key components working together:

*   A central **Power Module** provides conditioned and reliable power. It converts standard AC wall power to the DC voltage required by the electronics and includes a battery for backup, ensuring therapy can continue without interruption.

*   The brain of the device is a **Control Processor**. This microprocessor manages the entire therapy session according to a pre-configured treatment plan. It controls the timing, duration, and direction of the electric fields and, most importantly, monitors the system for safety.

*   A **Field Generator** receives commands from the processor and synthesizes the precise electrical waveforms needed for the therapy. It generates signals at a specific frequency (e.g., 200 kHz) and directs them to the appropriate transducer arrays to control the field's direction.

*   The electric fields are delivered to the patient's body through several **Transducer Arrays**, which are placed on the skin around the tumor site (e.g., anterior, posterior, left, and right). Each array consists of multiple ceramic disk elements that, when energized, create the therapeutic electric field within the targeted tumor tissue.

*   For patient safety, each transducer array is equipped with a **Temperature Sensor**. This sensor constantly monitors the skin temperature where the array makes contact. This data is fed back to the Control Processor, which will automatically halt the therapy if the temperature exceeds a safe threshold, preventing potential burns.

In operation, the system first runs a self-test and loads the therapy parameters. During a treatment session, which can last for many hours, the Control Processor directs the Field Generator to energize a pair of Transducer Arrays, creating an electric field in one direction. After a short interval, it switches to a different pair of arrays to rotate the field's orientation. This cycle of applying and rotating the field continues for the entire duration of the treatment, all while the system's safety parameters are being continuously monitored.
