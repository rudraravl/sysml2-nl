This model represents a complete household automatic washing machine system, designed to clean laundry by running various selectable wash programs. The system includes the washing machine appliance itself, as well as its necessary connections to a home's water supply, sewer drain, and electrical grid.

The washing machine is a complex assembly of interconnected components. At its core is a rotating **Drum Assembly** where dirty laundry is placed. A powerful **Drive Motor** turns this drum at different speeds for tumbling during the wash phase and high-speed extraction during the spin cycle. A **Water Inlet Valve** controls the flow of fresh water into the machine, while a **Drain Pump** expels used, dirty water at the end of a cycle. An integrated **Water Heater** raises the water temperature to levels appropriate for the selected program.

Operation is managed by a central **Control Module**, which acts as the system's brain. It executes wash programs like "Cotton Heavy," "Delicate," or "Quick Wash." These programs are predefined sequences of actions including filling, heating, washing, rinsing, and spinning. To ensure correct operation, the control module relies on feedback from a **Water Level Sensor** and a **Temperature Sensor**. For safety, a **Door Lock** mechanism engages to prevent the door from being opened while the machine is active, particularly when the drum contains water or is rotating.

A typical wash cycle progresses through a series of distinct states. After being loaded and a program is selected, the machine locks its door, fills with a specific volume of water, and heats it to the target temperature. It then adds detergent and tumbles the clothes for the main wash. This is followed by several draining and rinsing phases to remove soap, and a final high-speed spin cycle to extract excess water from the clean laundry. Once the entire process is complete, the machine unlocks the door, signaling that the cycle has finished.
