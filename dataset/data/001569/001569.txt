This model describes a complete self-making bed system designed to automatically tidy itself after use. The system is built around a standard bed frame and mattress, but is equipped with an integrated set of sensors and mechanical actuators to handle the bedding.

The core of the system is a central controller that orchestrates the entire bed-making process. It receives information from occupancy sensors embedded in the bed. These sensors continuously monitor whether someone is in the bed, and for how long the bed has been vacant. This is a critical safety feature, as the system is designed to only operate when it has been empty for a specific period, such as 60 seconds.

Once the system confirms the bed is empty, the controller initiates a multi-stage making sequence. First, it activates a series of sheet tensioning actuators, which are mechanical devices that pull the top and bottom sheets taut to a predefined tension. Next, a blanket folding actuator engages to neatly fold and position the blanket. Finally, pillow arrangement actuators adjust the pillows, moving them into their proper place at the head of the bed.

The system is designed to meet specific quality standards, ensuring the sheets are sufficiently tight, the blanket is correctly folded, and the pillows are properly aligned. The entire automated cycle, from start to finish, is intended to be completed within a two-minute timeframe.
