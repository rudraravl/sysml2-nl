This SysML model represents a small industrial press shop with three straight-side presses.  Each press is identified by a unique ID, name, manufacturer's model (either Heim or Minster), and tonnage capacity.  The presses are operated by certified press technicians, each with a recorded number of years of experience.  The model shows the assignment of specific technicians (Alice, Bob, and Charlie) to specific presses during a period called "novemberOperation."  For example, Alice operates the Heim S2-200-78 #4 press (200-ton capacity) during this time, while Bob operates the Minster E2-250-60-48 #8 press (250-ton capacity), and Charlie operates the Minster 40-10 #12 press (300-ton capacity).  The system focuses on tracking press operation and technician assignments, not on the detailed mechanical workings of the presses themselves.
