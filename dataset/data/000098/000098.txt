This model describes a conventional, human-operated vehicle, such as a car or truck. The system consists of the main vehicle chassis, which has a specific physical mass, and an engine that provides propulsion.

The vehicle is designed to be controlled by a person, who acts as the driver. The vehicle's operational state is actively monitored, specifically tracking the driver's inputs like the current gear setting and the position of the accelerator. This represents a system where the driver directly manipulates the controls to manage the engine and transmission, thereby controlling the vehicle's movement.
