This model describes an automated fluid pumping system centered around a specific type of reciprocating piston pump, called the Diver's Pump. The system is designed to transfer a fluid from a source reservoir to a destination vessel in a controlled and monitored manner.

The core of the system is the pump itself, which is composed of several key mechanical parts. It features a cylinder chamber that holds the fluid, a piston assembly that moves back and forth within the cylinder, and a drive mechanism that provides the power for the piston's motion. The flow of fluid is managed by two specialized valves: an inlet valve that allows fluid to be drawn into the cylinder from the source, and an outlet valve that allows fluid to be pushed out toward the destination. These valves are designed to open only when a specific pressure threshold is met, ensuring a one-way flow.

The entire operation is managed by a dedicated pump controller. This controller sends start and stop commands to the pump's drive mechanism and continuously monitors its status. To do this, the system is equipped with pressure sensors at both the inlet and outlet, as well as a flow sensor to measure the rate at which fluid is being transferred.

The system operates through a well-defined lifecycle of states. It begins in an `idle` state, awaiting a command. When instructed to start, it first enters a `priming` state, a critical preparation step where the pump cylinder is filled with fluid to ensure there is no air in the system. Once successfully primed, it transitions to its main `pumping` state. During pumping, the pump executes continuous cycles of an intake stroke (drawing fluid in) and a discharge stroke (pushing fluid out).

For safety and control, the system constantly monitors its own operation. If a stop command is issued, it moves to a `stopping` state before returning to idle. If the sensors detect an issue, such as unsafe pressure levels or incorrect flow rates, the system will automatically enter a `fault` state and shut down the pump to prevent damage.
