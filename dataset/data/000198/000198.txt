This model represents the powertrain and drivetrain of a rear-wheel-drive vehicle. It details the mechanical components that generate power and deliver it to the wheels to make the vehicle move.

The system begins with an engine, which creates power based on an external fuel command, like a driver pressing the accelerator. This power is transferred to a transmission, which connects to the engine via a clutch mechanism.

From the transmission, a long rotating driveshaft carries the power to the rear of the vehicle, where it connects to a rear axle assembly. This assembly is a complex unit containing a differential, a rear axle, and two wheels.

The differential is a crucial component that receives power from the driveshaft and splits it, sending it to both the left and right rear wheels. It also allows the wheels to rotate at different speeds, which is necessary for turning corners smoothly. The power is transmitted from the differential to each wheel through a dedicated half-axle.

Finally, the two rear wheels receive the rotational force from the axles. It is the interaction between these two powered wheels and the road surface that ultimately propels the vehicle forward.
