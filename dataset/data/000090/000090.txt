This model describes a complete wheel hub assembly for a vehicle. Its primary function is to securely mount a tire and wheel to a vehicle's hub, allowing for rotation while maintaining proper alignment and air pressure.

The system is composed of two main sub-assemblies: the wheel assembly and the hub.

The wheel assembly consists of a tire mounted onto a metal wheel. The tire itself has a specific text-based specification (like "P225/65R17") and features two beads on its inner circumference. The metal wheel is defined by its diameter and width and includes several key features. It has two rims designed to mate with the tire's beads, creating an airtight "pressure seat" that holds the inflation pressure. The wheel also has a single inflation valve for adding air, five mounting holes for lug bolts, and can accommodate up to six balance weights to ensure smooth rotation.

The hub is the component that would be attached to the vehicle's axle. Its main feature in this model is a set of five threaded holes, perfectly aligned to match the mounting holes on the wheel.

The connection between the wheel assembly and the hub is achieved through five lug bolt joints. Each joint represents a lug bolt passing through a hole in the wheel and being screwed into a threaded hole on the hub. The model specifies that this joint is characterized by a specific torque and the resulting bolt tension, which ensures the wheel is clamped to the hub with the correct force.

Additionally, the design makes a provision for an optional wireless tire pressure monitor, which can be mounted onto the wheel with a band to transmit pressure data.
