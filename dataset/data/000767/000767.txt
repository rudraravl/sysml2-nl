This is a model of a two-piece ostomy appliance system, a medical device designed to securely and hygienically collect bodily waste from a patient with a stoma (a surgical opening in the abdomen). The system is inspired by the innovations of nurse Elise Sørensen, who invented the modern two-piece design.

The core of the system is a modular, two-part design that enhances patient comfort and skin health. It consists of:

*   **Adhesive Baseplate:** This is the foundation of the system. It is a flat, flexible component with a strong, skin-friendly adhesive on one side that sticks to the skin around the stoma. It is designed to be worn for several days, creating a reliable, leak-proof seal. It has a central opening that is cut to fit the patient's stoma.
*   **Collection Pouch:** A lightweight, disposable bag that attaches to the baseplate to collect waste. The pouch is made of a multi-layered film that acts as an effective odor barrier.
*   **Coupling Ring:** This is the key mechanical interface that connects the pouch to the baseplate. It provides a secure, twist-lock connection, allowing the pouch to be easily removed and replaced without having to peel the adhesive baseplate off the skin. This significantly reduces skin irritation compared to one-piece systems.

To manage gas and odor, the pouch is equipped with an integrated **Charcoal Filter**. This filter allows gas to be released slowly, preventing the pouch from ballooning, while neutralizing odor molecules. For convenience, the pouch also features a **Drain Valve** at the bottom, which allows the user to empty the contents without detaching the entire pouch from the baseplate.

In operation, the system transitions through several states. It begins as `Sealed` and clean. As it begins `Collecting` waste, its fill level increases. The system is designed to signal when it is `NearCapacity` or `RequiresChange`, prompting the user to either empty the pouch via the drain valve or detach and replace it with a new one. The entire system is designed to be lightweight, secure against accidental detachment, and discreet under clothing, ultimately aiming to protect skin integrity and improve the patient's quality of life.
