This describes a vehicle system with a configurable engine architecture. The basic system is a vehicle that contains an engine, and the engine itself is made up of a number of cylinders.

The core design is flexible, allowing the engine to be built with anywhere from four to six cylinders. Based on this adaptable blueprint, two specific vehicle models are defined: a 'small vehicle' which consistently uses a four-cylinder engine, and a 'big vehicle' that is equipped with a larger six-cylinder engine.

This represents a common product family where a base vehicle platform can be offered in different versions, distinguished by the size and power of their engines.
