This model describes a satellite, specifically the "Space Segment" that operates in orbit. The satellite is designed with a standard modular architecture, composed of a main Avionics Platform (the satellite bus) and a mission-specific Payload.

The Avionics Platform is the core of the satellite, providing all the essential life-support and operational functions. It includes a **Solar Array** for power generation, an **On-Board Computer** to manage all systems, and a **Communication System** to send and receive data from the ground. For navigation and pointing, the satellite is equipped with an **Attitude and Orbit Control System (AOCS)**, which uses various **Sensors and Actuators** like **Thrusters** and **Magnetorquers** to maintain its correct orientation and orbit. A central **Time Reference** system ensures all operations are precisely synchronized.

The **Payload** is the equipment that performs the actual mission, such as a telescope or an Earth-imaging camera. It is a distinct module that integrates with the main platform.

In operation, the Avionics Platform receives commands from a ground station, including specific instructions for the AOCS and general commands for the entire system. The On-Board Computer then processes these commands, managing the platform's functions while also relaying instructions, control signals, and synchronization timing to the Payload. The Payload, in turn, sends its operational status and mission data back to the platform, which packages it with its own system telemetry to be transmitted down to Earth.
