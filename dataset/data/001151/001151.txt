This describes a motion detection system designed for automated security and surveillance. The system is built to continuously monitor a specific area, detect movement using a passive infrared (PIR) sensor, and intelligently analyze the detected signals to avoid false alarms before issuing an alert.

The system is composed of three main functional components that work in sequence. First, a PIR sensor acts as the primary detector, sensing changes in infrared radiation caused by movement, such as a person walking by. The range and sensitivity of this sensor can be adjusted.

When the sensor detects a potential event, it sends a raw motion signal to a Signal Analyzer. This second component is the brain of the system, responsible for determining if the detected signal is genuine motion or just environmental noise (like a gust of wind). It analyzes the signal's characteristics against a pre-set noise threshold and calculates a confidence level for the detection.

If the analyzer confirms that the motion is real and the confidence level is above a required minimum (e.g., 80%), it triggers the third component, an Alert Generator. This final part creates and sends out a formal alert signal. The alert specifies the time of the event, the confidence in the detection, and a severity level (low, medium, or high).

The entire system operates through different states. It can be in a power-saving `standby` mode, an `active` state where it is actively monitoring, a `detecting` state while processing a signal, and an `alerting` state when it sends a notification. This entire process is designed to be reliable, with a specified operational range of at least 10 meters and a high degree of accuracy in its detections.
