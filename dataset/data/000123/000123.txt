This model describes the process of taking a photograph with an automatic camera, such as a modern digital camera or smartphone. The system is designed to ensure that a picture is only taken after the subject is properly focused.

The overall process is to take a picture of a real-world scene. When initiated, the camera first performs an automatic focusing action on the scene. This creates a temporary optical image on the camera's sensor. The system then internally checks if this image is well-focused.

Crucially, the camera will only proceed to the next step if the focus is successful. If the image is determined to be in focus, the camera then performs the "shoot" action, where the shutter is activated to capture the focused image, resulting in the final picture. If the initial focusing attempt fails, the shooting action is skipped, and no picture is taken, preventing a blurry photograph.
