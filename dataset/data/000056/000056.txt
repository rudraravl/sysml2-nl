This model describes a generic electronic or data processing component designed to act as a "passthrough" or adapter.

At its core, the system contains a fundamental functional unit. This internal unit is equipped with a pair of complementary connection ports: a standard "output" or "provider" port, and a corresponding "input" or "requirer" port (referred to as a conjugated port).

The main component's primary role is to expose these internal connection points to the outside world. It acts as a housing or an extension, wiring the internal unit's provider and requirer ports directly to its own external boundary.

This design allows other systems to connect to the internal functional unit through the main component's exterior. The system is defined with both a direct physical connection path and a formal interface, both of which provide access to the same complementary ports, ensuring a well-defined and structured way to interact with the device.
