This model describes the system and process for providing transportation with a vehicle. The system involves the vehicle itself, a driver, passengers, and external entities like a fuel station and the general environment.

The primary function is to complete a trip. This process begins with the driver and passengers entering the vehicle. The core part of the operation is the driver actively operating the vehicle to travel to a destination. During this journey, the vehicle may need to be refueled. This is an optional step where the driver takes the vehicle to a fuel station to add fuel before continuing. Once the destination is reached, the trip concludes with the driver and passengers exiting the vehicle.
