This model represents the electronic control architecture within a modern vehicle. The system consists of a primary vehicle component and its engine, both of which are managed by their own dedicated computers.

A central Vehicle Control Unit (VCU) is responsible for the overall functions of the vehicle. The engine has its own dedicated Engine Control Unit (ECU) that specifically manages its performance.

These two control units do not communicate directly. Instead, they are connected to a shared communication network called a CAN Bus. This bus acts as a central data highway, allowing the VCU and ECU to exchange information and coordinate their actions, ensuring the vehicle operates as an integrated system. The CAN Bus also features an additional interface for sensors, indicating it's the central network for other electronic components throughout the vehicle.
