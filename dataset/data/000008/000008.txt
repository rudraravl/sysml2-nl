This SysML model represents a home or small office internet network.  The system facilitates data transfer between various devices using different connection types.  Data itself is represented as a fundamental element flowing through the network. Devices, such as computers, smartphones, and other internet-capable equipment, connect to the network and receive power (voltage and amperage specified).  

Two types of routers manage the network: a WiFi router, providing wireless connections (at various frequencies) and possibly ethernet connections (with a variable number of wires); and a DSL router, using a single cable connection (DSL) and multiple ethernet connections for wired devices. Mobile devices connect wirelessly, utilizing multiple connection types such as Bluetooth, WiFi, and cellular data (4G/5G).  All connections, whether wireless or wired, transmit data between the devices and the internet through the routers.  The system's functionality relies on the interplay between these devices and the different types of connections, all driven by the power supplied to each device.
