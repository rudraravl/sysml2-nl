This model represents a Miele G7000 series automatic dishwasher, specifically the G7310 model, designed to automatically clean, sanitize, and dry household dishes. It is a sophisticated appliance that manages water, heat, and detergent through a series of precise, automated cycles.

The dishwasher is comprised of several key physical components that work together. Water enters the system through a controlled **inlet valve** and passes through a **water softener** to reduce mineral hardness, which improves cleaning performance and protects the machine. A powerful **heating system** then brings the water to the precise temperature required for the selected wash program, ranging from cooler eco-washes to high-temperature sanitizing rinses.

Inside the main washing chamber, there are adjustable **dish racks** for loading plates, glasses, and a cutlery basket. A **circulation pump** draws the heated water and propels it at high pressure through two rotating **spray arms** (one upper, one lower) that spray the dishes from multiple angles to ensure complete coverage. At the correct time in the cycle, a **detergent dispenser** automatically releases soap into the main wash.

A central **control module** acts as the brain of the dishwasher, orchestrating the entire process based on the user's program selection (e.g., eco, intensive, quick wash). It relies on a suite of sensors to operate intelligently: a **temperature sensor** to regulate the heating system, a **water level sensor** to ensure the correct amount of water is used, and a **turbidity sensor** to measure how dirty the water is, allowing it to adjust the cycle for optimal efficiency.

The dishwasher executes a multi-stage cleaning process. A typical cycle begins with a safety **door lock** engaging. It then proceeds through a pre-wash rinse, drains that water, and then begins the main wash with hot, detergent-filled water. After the main wash, a **drain pump** expels the soiled water. This is followed by several rinse cycles, with the final rinse at a high temperature for sanitization. After a final drain, a drying phase, likely using condensation, removes moisture from the clean dishes. Once the cycle is finished and the interior has cooled, the door unlocks.

The system is designed to meet high standards of performance, safety, and user convenience. It aims for exceptional cleaning and sanitization while being highly efficient with water (under 10 liters per cycle) and energy (A+++ rating). Safety features like the active door lock and leak protection are critical. For the user, it is designed to be quiet (under 44 dB), flexible with multiple wash programs, and offer a large capacity of 14 place settings.
