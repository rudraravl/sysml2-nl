This is a needle-free jet injector system designed to deliver a precise dose of liquid medication through the skin without using a traditional hypodermic needle. It works by using a compressed gas power source to generate an extremely fine, high-velocity jet of medication that can penetrate the skin and reach the subcutaneous tissue.

The system is comprised of several key components that work in sequence. A disposable, sterile `Medication Ampoule` holds the liquid drug, and a `Compressed Gas Cartridge` provides the energy for the injection. When a user activates the `Trigger Mechanism`, the system first verifies that the device is pressed firmly against the skin using a `Safety Interlock`.

Once safety is confirmed, the compressed gas is released into a `Piston Chamber`. This high-pressure gas drives a piston forward, which in turn applies immense force to the medication. This action forces the liquid medication through a `Micro-Orifice Nozzle`. This precision nozzle has a tiny opening that accelerates the fluid, forming a powerful, ultra-fine jet capable of piercing the skin and delivering the medication accurately. The entire process, from triggering the device to completing the injection, is a single, rapid sequence designed for both safety and effectiveness. The system is engineered to meet strict requirements for dose accuracy and to ensure the medication is delivered to the correct depth within the tissue.
