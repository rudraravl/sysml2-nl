This is a low dead space medical syringe system, designed to deliver precise doses of medication while significantly minimizing waste. The system is particularly valuable for administering expensive drugs or vaccines where maximizing the number of doses from a single vial is critical.

The syringe is composed of four primary physical components that work together. It has a standard cylindrical **Barrel**, made of medical-grade polypropylene, which serves as the chamber to hold the liquid medication. A **Plunger** with a synthetic rubber seal fits snugly inside the barrel; applying force to this plunger creates the pressure needed to expel the fluid. A sharp, hollow **Needle** is used to penetrate the patient's tissue for injection.

The key innovation of this system is its specialized **Low Dead Space Tip**. This component connects the barrel to the needle and is uniquely shaped to reduce the tiny empty space, or "dead space," that typically exists between the fully depressed plunger and the needle hub in a standard syringe. By minimizing this residual volume, the syringe ensures that nearly all the drawn medication is delivered to the patient, thus improving dosing accuracy and reducing waste.

The syringe's operational lifecycle follows a clear sequence of states. It begins as a sterile, idle device ready for use. A clinician then draws medication into the barrel, at which point the syringe is considered primed. To ensure safety and accuracy, any air bubbles are expelled. Once it is ready, the medication is injected into the patient. After the full dose is administered and the injection is complete, the single-use device is safely disposed of. The entire process is designed to guarantee sterility, deliver a highly accurate dose, and achieve a waste reduction of at least 80% compared to standard syringes.
