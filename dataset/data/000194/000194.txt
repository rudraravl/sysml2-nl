This model describes a vehicle, such as a car, that is designed and analyzed based on its component structure. The vehicle is understood as a complete system made up of a hierarchy of numerous individual parts.

A key aspect of this vehicle's design is the specific classification of every component as either a "safety feature" or a "non-safety feature." This allows for a clear distinction between parts critical for occupant safety (like airbags, seat belts, or an anti-lock braking system) and all other components (like the engine, radio, or chassis). The system is organized to allow for different perspectives: one can view the entire breakdown of all parts in the vehicle, or one can generate specific, filtered lists that show only the safety-critical components or, conversely, only the non-safety components.
