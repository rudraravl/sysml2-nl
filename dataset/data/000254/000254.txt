This system represents the complete operational workflow of a manufacturing company, detailing its interactions with customers and suppliers to produce and deliver custom metal products. It models the entire business ecosystem, from initial customer contact to final product shipment.

The system is composed of three primary entities: the Manufacturer, its Customers, and its Suppliers.

The process begins when a **Customer** sends an inquiry to the **Manufacturer's Sales** department. The sales team responds with a quotation. If the customer accepts, they send back an order confirmation, which the sales team formalizes into a sales order.

This sales order triggers two parallel internal processes:

1.  **Production Scheduling**: This department receives the sales order and translates it into a detailed production order, which is an internal command specifying what needs to be built. This production order is then sent to the factory floor.
2.  **Procurement**: This department is responsible for acquiring raw materials. It issues purchase orders to external **Suppliers**, who then deliver the required materials to the manufacturer's **Storage** facility or warehouse.

The **Production** department, or factory floor, receives the production order from scheduling and the necessary raw materials from storage. The core of this model is a specific **Angle Production Line** designed for fabricating metal parts. This line operates as a sequential process where raw material is first heated, then bent to a specific angle, cut to a precise length, drilled with holes, and finally finished through grinding and polishing to meet final specifications.

Once the finished product emerges from the production line, it is sent to the **Dispatch** department. Referencing the original sales order, dispatch packages the product, arranges for its shipment to the customer, and sends a shipping confirmation notice to both the customer and the sales department to close the loop on the order.
