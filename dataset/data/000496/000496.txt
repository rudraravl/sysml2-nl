This is the Revivent Transcatheter Ventricular Enhancement System, a minimally invasive medical device designed to treat heart failure by physically reshaping a patient's damaged heart. The system is used for patients who have non-functional scar tissue on their heart wall, often from a prior heart attack, which has caused the left ventricle to become enlarged and inefficient.

The core of the system consists of three main physical components: a flexible Delivery Catheter, a set of implantable Micro-Anchors, and an external Fluoroscopy Guidance System. The entire procedure is performed without open-heart surgery.

The Delivery Catheter is a long, thin tube that a physician inserts into the patient's vascular system (typically through the leg) and navigates to the left ventricle of the heart. The procedure is visualized in real-time using the Fluoroscopy Guidance System, which provides live X-ray images, allowing the physician to precisely guide the catheter to the area of scarred heart tissue.

The catheter carries the Micro-Anchor Array. These anchors are deployed in pairs, with one anchor placed on each side of the scarred tissue. Once a pair of anchors is securely embedded in the heart wall, a Tensioning Mechanism, controlled via the catheter handle outside the body, is used to pull the anchors together. This action cinches the heart wall, effectively folding the non-functional scar tissue inward and excluding it from the active heart chamber.

The procedure involves the sequential placement and tensioning of several anchor pairs to achieve the desired reshaping. The system operates through distinct phases: navigating the catheter to the target site, deploying an anchor pair, applying tension, and then verifying the result before moving to the next location or completing the procedure. The goal is to reduce the overall volume of the heart chamber and improve its pumping efficiency, measured as ejection fraction. Once the reshaping is complete and verified, the anchors are permanently locked in place, and the delivery catheter is withdrawn from the body.
