This model describes the high-level requirements for a vehicle designed to transport and deliver a payload. The system's primary goal is to successfully carry a payload, which has its own mass limits.

A critical design driver for this vehicle is a strict requirement on its total mass. To successfully achieve its mission of delivering the payload, the vehicle's overall weight must be kept at or below a specified maximum.

From a project management perspective, successfully meeting this mass requirement has been identified as a high-risk challenge. While the schedule risk is low, there are medium technical and cost risks associated with engineering a vehicle that is light enough. The requirement is currently under review, with specific team members assigned to own and manage its development.
