This model represents a generic vehicle, which is broken down into its fundamental components: a chassis and an engine. The primary design focus illustrated here is on weight management and how overall system goals influence the design of individual parts.

The system has a strict requirement for the vehicle's total mass, meaning it cannot exceed a specific weight limit. This top-level constraint is then systematically broken down and allocated to the vehicle's major sub-components. As a result, both the chassis and the engine are given their own individual mass limits. This ensures that when the components are assembled, the final vehicle will successfully meet its overall weight target—a critical factor for performance, safety, and efficiency.
