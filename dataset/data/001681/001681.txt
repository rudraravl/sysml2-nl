This is the SM4All (Smart hoMes for All) ambient assisted living system, designed to support independent living for elderly and disabled individuals in their own homes. The system's primary purpose is to continuously and unobtrusively monitor the resident and their environment, automatically detect potentially hazardous situations, and coordinate an appropriate response.

The system is composed of several interconnected components. Throughout the home, a set of five environmental sensors constantly track conditions such as temperature, humidity, light levels, and motion. Residents are equipped with a wearable device, like a pendant or smartwatch, which monitors their personal vital signs, including heart rate and activity levels. This wearable is also capable of automatically detecting a fall.

All of this data is fed into a central context processing unit, which acts as the brain of the system. This processor analyzes the combined information from the sensors to understand the resident's current situation and activity. It learns their normal routines and patterns, allowing it to identify anomalies that could indicate a problem, such as a period of inactivity after a motion was detected or an unusual heart rate.

If the system detects a critical event like a fall or a severe deviation from normal patterns, it immediately enters an emergency response mode. A communication gateway automatically sends an alert notification, including the location and type of event, to a pre-designated caregiver or emergency contact, ensuring a response within 30 seconds.

Beyond emergency response, the system also manages the home environment for comfort and convenience. It can automatically control smart actuators within the home, such as the HVAC system for climate control, the lighting system, and automated window blinds. For instance, it might adjust the temperature based on the resident's activity level or dim the lights in the evening to prepare for sleep. The entire system is designed to operate continuously while protecting user privacy, with sensitive data being processed locally and communications encrypted.
