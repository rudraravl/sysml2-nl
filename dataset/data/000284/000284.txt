This model represents a system for managing industrial equipment and personnel within a Boeing manufacturing facility. The system centers on a collection of specific heavy-duty machines, known as straight-side industrial presses, which are likely used for metal stamping or forming.

The system tracks three individual presses, each with a unique identifier, manufacturer (Heim or Minster), and a specific force rating (tonnage). These include a 200-ton Heim press, a 250-ton Minster press, and a 300-ton Minster press.

Alongside the machinery, the system also defines and tracks the skilled personnel qualified to operate them. These individuals are classified as Press Technicians, and the system records their name and years of experience. Specifically, it includes three technicians: Alice with 3 years of experience, Bob with 5 years, and Charlie with 7 years.

A key aspect of the system is managing the operational relationship between the technicians and the machines. For a specific time period, documented as the "November Operation," each press is assigned a designated operator. During this period, Alice is assigned to the 200-ton Heim press, Bob operates the 250-ton Minster press, and Charlie is assigned to the 300-ton Minster press.
