This model describes an adjustable underarm crutch, a mobility assistance device designed to support individuals with injuries or lower-body impairments. It is built to be strong, lightweight, and adaptable to a wide range of users.

The crutch is composed of several key components that work together to provide safe and comfortable support. The main structure is a **Telescopic Shaft** that can be adjusted in length to fit the user's height. This adjustment is secured by a **Height Lock Mechanism**, typically a pin system with multiple positions, ensuring the crutch remains at the correct height during use. The shaft itself is made from a durable, lightweight structural material.

For user interaction and comfort, the crutch features a padded **Underarm Cuff** and an ergonomic **Hand Grip**. These components are designed to distribute the user's body weight onto the crutch, minimizing pressure and discomfort during extended use. At the bottom, a **Rubber Foot Tip** with a high-friction tread pattern provides a stable, non-slip connection to the ground.

The crutch operates through several states. It begins in storage, is then configured to the user's specific height, and becomes ready for use. During walking, it enters an active support state, where it bears a portion of the user's weight with each step. The system is designed to maintain stability throughout the walking cycle. After use, it can be returned to a ready state or put back into storage. A maintenance state is also included for periods when inspection or component replacement is needed. The overall design emphasizes safety, with requirements for high load capacity, structural integrity, and excellent ground traction.
