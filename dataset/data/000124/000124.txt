This model represents a digital camera designed to capture photographs of an external scene. The camera is composed of two primary internal subsystems that work in sequence: an autofocus subsystem and an imaging subsystem.

When a picture is taken, the autofocus subsystem first observes the real-world scene the camera is pointed at. Its function is to process this view and produce a sharp, focused optical image. This focused image is then passed directly to the imaging subsystem. The imager, which represents the camera's digital sensor, captures this incoming focused light and converts it into a final digital picture. This newly created photograph is then stored within the camera.
