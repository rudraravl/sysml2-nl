This model describes an intelligent or "smart" first-aid kit system designed to provide guided emergency medical care and automated inventory management.

The system is a portable, physical kit housed in a durable, waterproof container. It contains a comprehensive and diverse range of medical supplies, including different types of bandages (adhesive, gauze, elastic), antiseptics (wipes and spray), pain medication, disposable gloves, and essential tools like scissors, tweezers, and a thermometer.

Unlike a traditional first-aid kit, this system actively assists a user in an emergency. A user reports an injury, specifying its type (e.g., cut, burn, sprain) and severity, through a user interface. The system then assesses this information to generate a step-by-step treatment plan and identify the necessary supplies. It checks its internal stock to ensure the items are available before dispensing them from its storage compartment.

A key feature is its automated inventory management. An internal tracker monitors the quantity of all supplies. After items are used, the system updates its stock count. If any supply level drops below a predefined minimum, it triggers an alert to notify the owner that a restock is needed.

The system operates through a clear sequence of states. It remains in an idle state until an injury is reported. It then moves through states for assessing the injury, selecting and dispensing supplies, providing treatment guidance, and finally updating its inventory before returning to idle. This ensures a structured and reliable response process for providing emergency care.
