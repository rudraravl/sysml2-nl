This is an Unattended Ground Sensor (UGS) system designed for autonomous perimeter surveillance and intrusion detection. The system is a network of self-contained sensor nodes that are deployed in a field to automatically detect, classify, and report ground-based intrusions like people or vehicles to a central command center.

Each sensor node is a sophisticated, standalone device equipped with a suite of four different sensors to provide comprehensive coverage:
*   A **Seismic Sensor** detects ground vibrations from footsteps and vehicle movement.
*   An **Acoustic Sensor** listens for sound signatures, like engines or voices.
*   A **Passive Infrared (PIR) Sensor** detects the body heat of moving people or animals.
*   A **Magnetic Sensor** detects the large metal mass of vehicles.

When a sensor node detects a potential event, its onboard signal processor fuses the data from all its sensors to build a complete picture. This process allows it to classify the target with a certain level of confidence, distinguishing between categories such as personnel, light vehicles, heavy vehicles, or animals. This multi-sensor approach is crucial for reducing false alarms that might be triggered by weather or non-threatening wildlife.

If the processor classifies a target with high confidence, the node’s communication module automatically generates and transmits a detailed alert over a secure RF mesh network. This alert includes the time, location, type of target, and a calculated severity level. The data is also stored locally in a data logger for later retrieval.

The entire system is designed for long-term, autonomous operation in harsh environments. A power management unit in each node manages a battery and solar panel, allowing a node to operate for extended periods (e.g., 90 days or more) without maintenance. The nodes can operate in several modes, including an active surveillance mode, a standby mode, and a low-power mode to conserve energy, automatically managed based on battery levels and activity. A central Command Center receives the alerts from all nodes in the network, allowing operators to monitor the security of a large area.
