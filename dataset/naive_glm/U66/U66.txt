This model describes Gelesis100, a non-systemic, hydrogel-based therapy for weight management. The system is delivered as capsules designed to be taken with water before meals to help a person feel full and reduce their food intake.

The core of the system is a therapeutic capsule containing a superabsorbent material made from cross-linked cellulose and citric acid. When a person swallows these capsules, the outer shell dissolves in the stomach's acidic environment. This releases the cellulose-based particles, which rapidly absorb the surrounding gastric fluids and water.

As the particles hydrate, they swell to approximately 100 times their original size, forming a large, three-dimensional elastic gel matrix. This expanded gel takes up significant space in the stomach and mixes with any food that is eaten, increasing the meal's overall volume and viscosity. This physical presence in the stomach induces a feeling of fullness, or satiety, which helps the user manage their appetite and reduce the amount of food they consume.

The system is designed to work mechanically and locally within the gastrointestinal tract. After its residence in the stomach, the gel matrix passes through the small and large intestines, where it gradually degrades. It is not absorbed into the bloodstream. Finally, the degraded material is naturally eliminated from the body, providing a safe and non-systemic approach to weight management.
