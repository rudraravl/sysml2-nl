This is a personal cooling vest system designed to actively regulate a person's body temperature, particularly during physical activity or exposure to high heat. The system's main goal is to maintain the wearer's thermal comfort by removing excess body heat.

The system is built around a wearable vest garment that contains a network of small channels. A cooling fluid is circulated through these channels to absorb heat directly from the wearer's body. The core of the system is an automated cooling loop powered by a rechargeable battery pack.

Here's how it works together:
*   A **Temperature Sensor**, in contact with the wearer's skin, continuously monitors their body temperature.
*   This temperature reading is sent to a central **Control Unit**, which acts as the brain of the system. The controller compares the wearer's current temperature to a desired target comfort temperature.
*   If the wearer is too warm, the controller activates a **Thermoelectric Cooler** and a **Fluid Pump**.
*   The pump draws fluid from a **Reservoir** and pushes it through the thermoelectric unit, which chills the fluid. The cooler itself dissipates waste heat into the surrounding air.
*   The chilled fluid is then circulated through the **Thermal Distribution Network** of channels within the vest. As it flows, it absorbs the wearer's excess body heat, warming up in the process.
*   The now-warmed fluid returns to the reservoir before being sent back to the cooler to be chilled again, completing the cycle.

This entire process runs continuously and automatically, with the control unit activating and deactivating the cooling system as needed to keep the user within a comfortable and safe temperature range. The entire system is self-contained and powered by its own battery pack.
