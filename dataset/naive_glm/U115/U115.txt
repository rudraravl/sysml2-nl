This model describes a single-use, pre-filled auto-injector, a medical device designed for the rapid and safe delivery of a specific dose of emergency medication.

The device consists of several integrated components housed within a main body. It comes sealed in a sterile package to ensure the medication's integrity and sterility are maintained until the point of use. Inside, a reservoir chamber holds a pre-measured volume of a specific drug. This chamber is connected to a delivery mechanism, which includes a concealed needle of a specific gauge and length.

To use the device, a user first removes it from its packaging and inspects it. Administration is initiated by pressing an activation trigger, which requires a specific amount of force. This action starts the automated injection process: the delivery mechanism extends the needle into the tissue and delivers the full dose of medication from the reservoir at a controlled rate.

A key focus of the design is safety, both for the patient and the user. The device incorporates a safety mechanism that automatically deploys a needle shield after the injection is complete. This shield locks in place, providing a visual indicator that the device has been used and preventing accidental needlestick injuries. Furthermore, an internal usage counter ensures the device can only be activated once, after which it becomes permanently disabled to prevent any possibility of reuse.

After the safety lock is engaged, the entire unit is considered biohazard waste and is designed to be disposed of safely in a designated sharps container.
