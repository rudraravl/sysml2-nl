This model describes an intelligent, automated fluid pumping subsystem designed to transfer fluid from a source tank to a destination tank while precisely and autonomously controlling the flow rate.

The system consists of three main physical components: a source storage tank, a destination storage tank, and an Intelligent Pump that connects them. The pump is the core of the system, designed for self-regulation and operational safety.

The Intelligent Pump is equipped with a suite of sensors at both its inlet and outlet ports. These include flow sensors to measure the rate at which fluid is moving and pressure sensors to measure the fluid's pressure. An integrated Pump Controller acts as the brain of the unit. It continuously receives data from these sensors and compares the actual flow rate at the outlet with a desired target flow rate.

Based on this real-time feedback, the controller automatically adjusts the pump's rotational speed. If the flow is too low, it speeds the pump up; if it's too high, it slows it down. This closed-loop control allows the system to maintain a stable flow rate without manual intervention, even if conditions like pressure change.

The pump also has distinct operational states, including 'off', 'starting', 'running', and 'stopping'. For safety, it includes a 'faulted' state, which it will enter if it detects a problem, causing it to shut down. The entire subsystem is designed to ensure a specific pressure increase is achieved and to operate within defined speed and flow rate limits for efficiency and safety.
