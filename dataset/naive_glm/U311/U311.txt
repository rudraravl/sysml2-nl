This is a microneedle drug delivery system, likely in the form of a smart, wearable patch, designed for the precise and controlled administration of medication through the skin. It serves as an alternative to traditional injections or oral medications.

The system is composed of several key components working in concert. A **Drug Reservoir** holds the liquid drug formulation. This reservoir is connected to a **MicroPump**, which is the active mechanism that pushes the drug through the system at a specific pressure and flow rate. The drug then flows into a **Microneedle Array**, which consists of numerous microscopic needles (e.g., 100 needles, each less than a millimeter long) that painlessly penetrate the outermost layer of the skin. This array serves as the interface for delivering the drug directly to the body.

The entire delivery process is managed by an integrated **Control Module**. This electronic "brain" is programmed with the target dose and desired delivery rate. It receives constant feedback from a **Flow Sensor** and a **Pressure Sensor** to monitor the actual delivery process in real-time.

In operation, a user or healthcare provider applies the patch to the skin. Once an activation command is given, the system enters a preparation phase, ensuring proper skin contact and priming the pump. It then begins the delivery cycle: the pump is activated, and the control module continuously monitors the volume of drug delivered. If the flow rate deviates, the controller adjusts the pump's pressure to maintain accuracy. This feedback loop continues until the prescribed dose has been fully administered, at which point the system automatically stops and signals that the delivery is complete. The system is designed with strict safety and accuracy requirements, ensuring the needles do not penetrate too deeply and that the patient receives the exact prescribed dose.
