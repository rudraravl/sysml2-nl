This is a mobile robotic system designed to perform autonomous "pick and place" tasks. Its primary function is to locate, grasp, transport, and release objects within its operational area.

The system is built on a mobile platform equipped with a four-wheel drive system, which allows it to navigate through its environment. For object interaction, it features a sophisticated six-jointed robotic arm that provides a wide range of motion and a reach of nearly one meter. At the end of the arm is a gripper capable of applying a controlled amount of force to securely hold objects up to 8 kg without crushing them.

The robot's operation is coordinated by a central control module, which acts as its brain. To perceive its surroundings, the robot uses a high-resolution vision sensor to detect and identify target objects up to 5 meters away. It is also equipped with four proximity sensors for close-range awareness and obstacle avoidance.

A typical task sequence begins with the robot scanning for an object. Once a target is identified, the navigation system moves the robot to the object's location while adhering to a safe maximum speed. The manipulation arm then positions the gripper, grasps the object, and transports it to a specified destination before releasing it.

The entire system is powered by an internal battery. A dedicated power module continuously monitors the battery level to ensure the robot has sufficient charge to complete its tasks, and it operates under the constraint that the battery must remain above a minimum threshold.
