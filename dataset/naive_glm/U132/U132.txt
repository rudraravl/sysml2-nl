This model describes a single-use vaginal syringe system designed for the controlled administration of medicinal or cleansing fluids. Its primary purpose is therapeutic irrigation, cleansing, or delivering medication directly into the vaginal cavity in a safe, sterile, and comfortable manner.

The system is an assembly of four main components. It features a main barrel made from medical-grade polypropylene, which holds up to 150 mL of fluid and includes clear graduation markings for accurate volume measurement. A plunger with a reliable seal moves within this barrel, allowing a user to apply force to dispense the contained fluid. At the end of the barrel is a long, slender nozzle with a smooth, rounded, and atraumatic tip, specifically designed to ensure patient comfort and prevent tissue injury during insertion and use. To guarantee sterility and product integrity before use, the nozzle is covered by a tamper-evident safety cap.

The device is designed for a straightforward operational flow. It begins in a sterile, idle state. The process involves preparing the device by verifying its sterility (checking the tamper-evident seal), filling the barrel with the appropriate fluid, positioning the device for use, and finally, dispensing the fluid by pushing the plunger. The entire system is intended for one-time use to ensure hygiene and prevent any risk of cross-contamination.
