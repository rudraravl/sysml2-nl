This model describes a high-precision fluid injection system, designed to deliver accurately metered quantities of a fluid as a fine, atomized spray. It is likely a system such as an automotive fuel injector or a component in an industrial chemical dosing process.

The system is composed of several key components that work in a coordinated sequence. A `FluidReservoir` acts as the source, storing the fluid under an initial supply pressure. From the reservoir, the fluid flows to a `PressureRegulator`, which stabilizes the pressure to a precise operating level, a critical factor for accurate injection.

The "brain" of the system is the `InjectionController`, an electronic unit that calculates the exact timing and duration required for each injection event to meet a target volume. It generates a command signal which is sent to the `InjectorValve`, a fast-acting valve that serves as the system's main actuator. Upon receiving the command, the valve opens for a very specific period, allowing the pressurized fluid to pass through.

As the fluid flows, a `FlowMeter` measures its rate, providing feedback that the controller can use to fine-tune subsequent injections for greater accuracy. Finally, the metered pulse of fluid is forced through a `DeliveryNozzle`, which is designed to atomize the liquid into a specific spray pattern, such as a 45-degree cone, for effective delivery and mixing.

The injector valve operates through a distinct cycle of states for each injection event, moving from `idle` to `priming` (preparing the system), then to `injecting` (delivering the fluid), and finally `purging` before returning to its `idle` state, ready for the next command. The entire system is designed for high reliability and performance, capable of completing millions of injection cycles with high accuracy and a very fast response time, all while operating safely within specified pressure and temperature limits.
