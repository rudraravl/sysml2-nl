This model represents an automated fluid transfer system designed to pump a liquid from a source reservoir to a destination reservoir. The system is engineered for continuous, controlled operation with built-in safety mechanisms.

The physical setup consists of a source tank and a destination tank connected by a main pipeline. The core component is a powerful pump, driven by a 7.5 kW electric motor and an internal impeller, which provides the pressure to move the fluid. The flow of the liquid is managed by several valves: an inlet valve, a primary discharge valve, and a check valve that prevents any backflow.

The system's intelligence resides in a central controller that automates the entire process. This controller relies on a suite of sensors for real-time information: a flow meter and a pressure sensor are installed on the discharge pipe, while level sensors continuously monitor the fluid levels in both the source and destination tanks.

During operation, the controller's main function is to maintain a target flow rate and pressure by sending commands to the pump and adjusting the position of the discharge valve. The system includes critical safety logic: it will automatically shut down the pump if the source tank's level falls below a 10% threshold (to prevent the pump from running dry) or if the destination tank's level exceeds a 90% threshold (to prevent overflow). This ensures safe, reliable, and unattended operation.
