This model describes a comprehensive Medical Scrubs Management System for a healthcare facility, designed to handle the complete lifecycle of scrubs from distribution to cleaning and back again. The system ensures a continuous supply of clean, safe, and decontaminated garments for all healthcare workers.

The process begins at a dispensary unit, which stores and issues clean scrubs of various sizes and colors to staff members. After use, the soiled scrubs are placed in large collection bins strategically located around the facility.

From the collection bins, the used scrubs are gathered and sorted, likely by color and level of contamination. They then enter a decontamination workflow, starting with an industrial washer that uses high-temperature wash cycles and specific detergent solutions to ensure pathogens are eliminated. Following the wash, the wet scrubs are moved to an industrial dryer.

Once dry, the scrubs proceed to a quality inspection station where they are examined for cleanliness, wear, and tear. Any scrubs that fail this inspection are sent back to be re-washed. Garments that pass are then folded, packaged, and placed in a dedicated, environmentally controlled clean storage area. This clean inventory is then used to restock the dispensary units, completing the cycle.

The entire system is designed to operate efficiently, with a goal of processing scrubs from collection to storage within four hours, while adhering to strict hygiene standards, such as maintaining minimum wash temperatures to guarantee effective decontamination.
