This model represents a large-scale Gravitational Energy Storage System, designed to function like a mechanical battery for an electrical grid. Its purpose is to store excess electrical energy during times of low demand and release it back to the grid when demand is high.

The core principle involves converting electrical energy into potential energy. To do this, the system uses a powerful **Hoisting Mechanism** to lift an extremely heavy **Mass Block** (weighing around 500,000 kilograms) to a significant height (approximately 120 meters). When the block is held in its elevated position, the energy is securely stored as gravitational potential energy.

When electricity is needed, the process is reversed. The mass block is lowered in a controlled manner. The force of gravity pulling the mass down drives a **Generator**, which converts the mechanical energy of the descent back into electrical power. This generated electricity is then conditioned by a **Power Converter** (power electronics) and fed back into the electrical grid.

The entire operation is managed by a central **System Controller**. This controller receives commands to either charge (lift the mass), discharge (lower the mass), or remain in standby. It continuously monitors the height of the mass block to ensure it operates within safe limits and manages the flow of energy to and from the grid. This system is designed for high-power applications, capable of delivering over 4 megawatts of continuous power during discharge, with a target round-trip efficiency of at least 75%.
