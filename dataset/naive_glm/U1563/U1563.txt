This model represents the complete system for the Space Shuttle mission STS-123, which was flown by the Orbiter Endeavour. The primary purpose of this mission was to deliver and install critical components to the International Space Station (ISS) and to return its seven-member crew safely to Earth.

The overall system is composed of the full Space Shuttle launch stack. This includes the Space Shuttle Endeavour orbiter, a massive External Fuel Tank carrying cryogenic liquid hydrogen and oxygen propellant for the main engines, and two powerful Solid Rocket Boosters that provide the primary thrust for the first two minutes of flight.

Once in orbit, the Endeavour orbiter operates as an independent spacecraft. It uses its Orbital Maneuvering System (OMS) for major orbital changes like achieving its final orbit and initiating the return to Earth. For finer control, docking, and attitude adjustments, it relies on a network of smaller thrusters in its Reaction Control System (RCS). The entire flight, from launch to landing, is managed by a sophisticated Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC) system.

The core of the mission is the cargo carried within the orbiter's large Payload Bay. For STS-123, this included two main components: the Kibo Japanese Experiment Logistics Module, a pressurized storage unit for the ISS, and Dextre, a highly advanced, two-armed robotic manipulator designed for delicate assembly tasks on the station's exterior. The payload bay provides the necessary mechanical, power, and data connections to support this cargo during flight.

The mission's operational flow is a sequence of distinct phases. It begins with the launch and ascent into a target orbit between 330 and 350 km. The shuttle then performs a carefully choreographed rendezvous and docking with the ISS. While docked, the crew uses robotic arms to transfer and install the Kibo module and the Dextre manipulator. After completing these tasks, the shuttle undocks, performs a deorbit burn, re-enters the atmosphere, and glides to a final runway landing.
