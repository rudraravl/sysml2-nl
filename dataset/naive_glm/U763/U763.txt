This is an industrial Magnetic Level Gauge system designed for continuous, reliable measurement of fluid levels within a process vessel. The system is particularly suited for applications involving high-pressure, high-temperature, or corrosive fluids because its measurement and indication components never come into direct contact with the process liquid.

The core of the system is a **Bypass Chamber**, which is a vertical pipe mounted to the side of the main process vessel. This chamber is connected to the vessel at both the top and bottom, ensuring the fluid level inside the chamber is always identical to the level inside the vessel.

Inside this chamber, a **Magnetic Float** rests on the surface of the liquid. This float is buoyant and contains powerful magnets. As the fluid level in the vessel rises or falls, the float moves up and down with it, precisely tracking the liquid's surface.

The system provides level information through several external components that are magnetically coupled to the float inside the chamber:

*   An **Indicator Assembly** is attached to the outside of the chamber. This is a visual scale, often with colored flippers or a follower, that is magnetically linked to the float. As the float moves, its magnetic field causes the external indicator to move in tandem, providing a clear, at-a-glance visual reading of the fluid level for operators on site.

*   A **Level Transmitter** is an electronic device also mounted externally. It continuously detects the position of the magnetic float and converts this physical height into a standard 4-20 mA analog electrical signal. This signal is sent to a central control system or display, allowing for remote monitoring and process automation.

*   **Alarm Switches** are positioned at specific high and low points on the chamber's exterior. If the float rises to the high-level setpoint or drops to the low-level setpoint, its magnetic field activates the corresponding switch. This sends a discrete alarm signal to alert operators of potentially critical conditions, helping to prevent tank overfills or equipment damage from running dry.

In operation, these components work together to provide a comprehensive and redundant level monitoring solution. The system simultaneously offers a local visual display, a continuous remote signal for the control room, and critical high and low-level alarms, all driven by the simple, reliable movement of the single magnetic float. The entire gauge is typically constructed from robust materials like stainless steel to ensure safety and longevity in demanding industrial environments.
