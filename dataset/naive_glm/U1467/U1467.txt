This is a Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting System designed to capture energy from ambient mechanical vibrations and convert it into stable, usable electrical power for small electronic devices.

The system's core component is a piezoelectric crystal, which generates an alternating electrical current when subjected to physical stress or vibrations. This raw AC power is then sent to a bridge rectifier, which converts it into direct current (DC). From there, a voltage regulator conditions the power, ensuring a stable and clean 5-volt output suitable for sensitive electronics.

To provide a continuous power supply even when vibrations are intermittent, the system includes a storage capacitor. This component acts like a small, rechargeable battery, accumulating the harvested energy. Once sufficiently charged, the capacitor delivers a steady stream of power to the connected load device.

The entire harvester operates through several intelligent states. It remains idle until it detects vibrations, at which point it begins actively harvesting and storing energy. When enough energy is stored, it switches to delivering power. If the energy reserves dip too low or the external device is disconnected, the system adjusts its state accordingly to either prioritize recharging or return to an idle mode. This system is designed to effectively operate with vibrations in the 20 Hz to 500 Hz frequency range and to power a load consuming up to 0.5 watts.
