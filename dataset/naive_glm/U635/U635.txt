This model describes a chemiresistor-based gas detection system, a device designed to continuously monitor the air for hazardous gases like Carbon Monoxide. Its primary purpose is to provide early warnings in environments where toxic or combustible gases might be present, ensuring personnel safety.

The system is built around a core set of interconnected electronic components that work together to detect, measure, and report on gas concentrations. The process begins with the **Chemiresistive Sensor**, which is the heart of the device. This sensor contains a special chemically sensitive material whose electrical resistance changes when it comes into contact with the target gas. The more gas present in the air, the greater the change in the sensor's resistance.

This raw change in resistance creates a weak electrical signal. This signal is immediately sent to a **Signal Processor**. This component's job is to clean up any electronic noise from the signal and amplify it, making it much stronger and more reliable for measurement.

Next, the clean, amplified analog signal is passed to an **Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC)**. The ADC translates the continuous electrical signal into a stream of digital values that the system's "brain," the **Microcontroller**, can understand.

The Microcontroller receives this digital data and uses a built-in calibration profile to accurately calculate the precise concentration of the gas in the air, typically measured in parts per million (ppm). It then compares this calculated concentration against a pre-set safety threshold. If the gas level is dangerously high, the microcontroller sends an activation signal to the **Alarm Indicator**. This unit then produces a clear visual and audible alert, such as a flashing light and a loud siren, to warn anyone nearby. The microcontroller also formats the measurement data, including the concentration level and a timestamp, for logging or display.

The entire system is powered by a dedicated **Power Management** unit, which takes an external power source and provides the stable, regulated voltage required for all the sensitive electronic components to function correctly and reliably.
