This model describes the Segs4Vets platform, an innovative rehabilitation system featuring a two-wheeled, self-balancing mobility device. It is designed to provide controlled balance therapy for veterans with mobility impairments, helping them improve coordination, stability, and confidence.

The core of the system is a self-stabilizing platform that the veteran stands on. This platform is equipped with a sophisticated balance sensor that constantly measures its tilt angle and the user's balance. A central therapy controller acts as the brain, processing this sensor data in real-time. Based on a therapy level set by a healthcare professional, the controller calculates the precise motor torque needed to assist the veteran in maintaining balance. It then sends commands to the motor controller, which drives the wheels to stabilize the platform.

The system is designed for supervised therapeutic use. A therapist initiates a session by registering a veteran and setting safety parameters, such as the maximum allowable tilt angle, and an appropriate therapy level. This allows the difficulty and level of assistance to be tailored to the individual's needs, creating a progressive training program.

For the veteran, there is a display panel that provides feedback and information about the current session. For the therapist, a separate interface allows for monitoring progress and receiving critical safety alerts. A dedicated safety monitor constantly checks for unsafe conditions, like excessive tilting, and can trigger an emergency stop if a safety threshold is breached. The entire platform is powered by a rechargeable battery system, which is monitored to ensure sufficient charge for therapy sessions.

Throughout a session, the system tracks the veteran's performance, generating a balance score. This data is used to monitor progress and is logged in the veteran's profile at the end of each session, providing a detailed record of their rehabilitation journey.
