This model represents a complete medical Cystoscopy System, designed for minimally invasive diagnosis and treatment within the urinary tract, specifically the bladder and urethra. The system integrates several key components to allow a surgeon to visualize the area, manage the environment, and perform procedures like taking tissue samples.

The core of the system is the **Cystoscope**, which is the instrument inserted into the patient. It consists of a long, thin **Sheath** that acts as a conduit, containing channels for irrigation fluid and for the optical components. It can also accommodate tools like the **Biopsy Forceps**, which are used to collect small tissue samples for analysis.

To provide a clear view, the system has two main subsystems: visualization and fluid management.

The **visualization subsystem** starts with a powerful **Light Source** that generates bright, specific-color-temperature light. This light is channeled through a flexible **Fiber Optic Bundle** into the cystoscope, illuminating the internal anatomy. A high-resolution **Camera Head** attached to the cystoscope captures the reflected light and converts it into a digital video signal. This signal is then sent to a **Video Processor**, which enhances the image quality before displaying the live feed in high definition on a large **Display Monitor** for the surgical team.

The **fluid management subsystem** is crucial for maintaining a clear field of view and gently distending the bladder for inspection. A sterile **Irrigation Fluid** is held in a **Fluid Reservoir**. A precision **Irrigation Pump** draws this fluid and pushes it through the cystoscope's sheath into the bladder. The surgeon uses a central **Control Console** to command and monitor the fluid's flow rate and pressure, ensuring patient safety and optimal conditions.

The entire system operates through a series of defined states, moving from **standby** to an **initialization** phase before becoming fully **operational** for a procedure. During operation, it continuously monitors fluid levels and pressure, captures video, and regulates the irrigation flow. This integrated approach ensures a safe and effective platform for urological examinations and interventions.
