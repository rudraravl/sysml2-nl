This is a Z-Wave home automation system designed to wirelessly connect and control various smart devices within a home. The system operates as a secure mesh network, where devices can communicate directly with each other or relay messages to extend range and improve reliability.

The heart of the system is a central controller that manages the entire network. It is responsible for adding or removing devices, maintaining secure, encrypted communication, and optimizing the routes that messages take to travel between devices.

The network includes a variety of smart devices:
*   **Smart Light Switches:** These control home lighting and can report their on/off status and power consumption.
*   **A Smart Thermostat:** This device monitors the current temperature and automatically controls the home's heating and cooling systems to maintain a desired setpoint.
*   **A Battery-Powered Motion Sensor:** It detects movement and sends alerts. To conserve energy, it operates in a low-power sleep mode, waking up periodically or when motion is detected.
*   **A Battery-Powered Smart Door Lock:** This allows for remote locking and unlocking using a secure access code and can report its current state (locked/unlocked).

All devices, or "nodes," in the network can operate in different states, such as listening for commands, transmitting a response, or sleeping to save power. Mains-powered devices, like the light switches, also act as repeaters, helping to route messages for other devices that might be out of direct range of the central controller, creating a robust and self-healing network.
