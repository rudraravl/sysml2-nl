This is a model of an "Ammoniaphone," a unique sound-generating device or musical instrument that creates audible tones by using heated ammonia gas.

The system is built around a sequence of specialized components that work together to convert chemical potential energy into acoustic energy. It begins with an **Ammonia Reservoir**, which stores the ammonia gas that acts as the instrument's fuel. When a tone is to be played, a **Control Unit** translates the desired pitch and volume into a precise electrical signal.

This signal is sent to a **Flow Regulator**, which then releases a controlled stream of ammonia gas from the reservoir. The gas flows into a **Heating Chamber**, where it is brought up to a high operating temperature. This hot, energized gas is then directed into a **Resonance Cavity**. Inside this chamber, the gas is excited in a way that generates a fundamental sound wave at a specific frequency.

Finally, this initial sound wave is channeled through an **Amplification Bell**, similar to the bell of a trumpet or trombone, which makes the sound louder and projects it outwards.

The Ammoniaphone operates through several distinct states. From an **Idle** state, it enters a **Warming** phase after receiving a start command, during which the heating chamber must reach a specific temperature. Once hot enough, it transitions to the **Playing** state where it actively produces sound. Upon receiving a stop command, it enters a **Cooling** phase before returning to the idle state, ready for its next use.
