This is a digital pill adherence system designed to verify that a patient has successfully ingested their medication. The system provides a reliable, automated record of when a dose is taken, which can be monitored by healthcare providers.

The core of the system is a "digital pill," an ingestible capsule that contains both the prescribed medication and a suite of miniature electronics. This pill is designed to be swallowed just like a regular capsule.

Once ingested, the digital pill becomes active upon contact with stomach fluids. It powers itself using a tiny, built-in galvanic cell that generates a small electric charge from the natural environment of the digestive system. This eliminates the need for a pre-charged battery.

After activation, an internal sensor within the pill measures physiological conditions such as body temperature and the pH level of its surroundings. The pill then uses a low-power radio transmitter to send out a telemetry packet. This data packet contains the sensor readings, a unique identifier for that specific pill, a patient identifier, and a precise timestamp of the event.

This signal is captured by a wearable patch worn on the patient's skin, typically on their torso. The patch acts as a receiver and relay, forwarding the data to the patient's mobile application on a smartphone. The mobile app processes the information to confirm that the medication was taken and logs the adherence event. Finally, this confirmation data can be securely uploaded to a cloud platform, allowing doctors or caregivers to access the patient's medication adherence history remotely.

The entire digital pill is made from biocompatible materials, ensuring it can pass safely through the digestive tract and be naturally expelled from the body once its power is depleted.
