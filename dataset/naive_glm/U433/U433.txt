This is an automated, touchless urinal system designed for hygienic liquid waste disposal in public or commercial restrooms. It provides hands-free operation to improve sanitation and conserves water by using a controlled, automated flush cycle.

The system's core components work together to detect a user, collect waste, and then flush it away. An infrared sensor, mounted on the unit, detects when a person is standing in front of the urinal. This sensor communicates with an electronic flush controller, which acts as the system's brain.

Once the person leaves, the sensor signals their departure to the controller. The controller then waits for a brief, pre-set delay before initiating the flush. This is done by sending an electrical command to a solenoid valve, which opens to release a measured amount of fresh water into the main ceramic fixture bowl. The water rinses the bowl, carrying waste down the drain.

The wastewater flows from the bowl into a P-trap, a curved section of pipe designed to hold a small amount of water. This water creates a seal that prevents unpleasant sewer gases from backing up into the restroom. From the trap, the wastewater flows into the main drain pipe and away into the building's sewage system. After a fixed duration, the controller closes the solenoid valve, stopping the water flow and completing the cycle, leaving the system ready for the next user.
