This is an automated, portable medical device designed for closed-loop patient therapy. Its purpose is to continuously monitor a patient's vital signs, automatically deliver a specific therapeutic signal based on a pre-defined treatment protocol, and adjust the treatment based on the patient's real-time response.

The device is composed of several key integrated components:
*   A **Power Management Unit** provides power through a rechargeable battery, and it continuously monitors its charge level, entering a low-power state or signaling when depleted.
*   A **Biometric Sensor** is used to measure the patient's vital signs, such as heart rate and temperature. It ensures the data it collects is accurate and valid before passing it on.
*   A **Therapy Generator** is responsible for creating and delivering the actual therapeutic signal to the patient. It can precisely control the signal's intensity, waveform, and duration, and can modulate this output based on feedback from the patient's vital signs to stay within safe limits.
*   The **Control Processor** acts as the brain of the system. It processes commands from an operator, analyzes the data from the biometric sensor, and determines the appropriate therapy parameters. It also monitors the overall system status, including battery life and errors.
*   A dedicated **Safety Interlock** system provides a crucial layer of patient protection. It constantly monitors for unsafe conditions like excessive temperature or electrical current and can automatically trigger an alarm or shut down the device to prevent harm.

In operation, the device is typically in a standby state. When a command to begin treatment is received, the control processor first performs a safety check. If all conditions are safe, it activates the therapy generator to deliver the treatment. The system then enters a continuous loop of sensing the patient's condition, analyzing the data, delivering therapy, and monitoring the patient's response, making adjustments as needed. This entire process is governed by strict safety and performance requirements for accuracy, reliability, and safe operation.
