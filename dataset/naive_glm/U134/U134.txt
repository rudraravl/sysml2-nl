This is a portable medical device, the Veinoplus, designed to improve blood circulation in the lower legs through neuromuscular electrical stimulation. It is intended for patients with venous insufficiency or those at risk of deep vein thrombosis.

The system consists of a main, battery-powered control unit and a pair of electrode pads that are placed on the patient's calf muscles. The core function of the device is to generate and deliver controlled electrical pulses to the calf muscles, causing them to contract rhythmically. This induced muscle contraction mimics the natural "calf pump" effect of walking, which helps to squeeze the deep veins in the legs and push blood back towards the heart.

The main unit houses several key components:
*   A **Battery Module** provides power to the device.
*   A **Control Module** serves as the brain, managing the device's state (off, standby, stimulating, paused), processing user commands, and running the therapy session timer. Users can select different stimulation programs (e.g., continuous, pulsed, progressive), adjust the intensity of the stimulation for comfort, and start or stop a session.
*   A **Pulse Generator** creates the specific electrical stimulus based on the selected program and intensity settings, defining its waveform, frequency, and pulse width.
*   A **Safety Monitor** continuously checks for safe operating conditions. It monitors the skin's electrical resistance (impedance) through the electrodes and the intensity of the stimulation, and can shut the device down if any parameters go outside of safe limits, such as if an electrode loses proper contact with the skin.

In operation, the electrical pulses are sent from the pulse generator to the **Electrode Pads**. These pads transfer the stimulus to the patient's skin, causing the underlying **Calf Muscles** to contract. This muscle contraction provides the force needed to compress the **Venous Vessels** in the leg, thereby increasing the flow rate and velocity of the venous blood returning to the heart. The entire process is designed to be a safe, effective, and comfortable therapy session for the user.
