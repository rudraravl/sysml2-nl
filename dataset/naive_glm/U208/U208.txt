This model describes an automated medical drug delivery system, commonly known as an infusion pump, designed to administer a precise amount of medication to a patient at a controlled rate.

The system is a self-contained, portable unit powered by an internal battery. Its core components work together to ensure safe and accurate medication delivery. It includes a drug reservoir to hold the liquid medication, an electric pump mechanism that draws the drug from the reservoir, and a delivery catheter for administration to the patient.

The flow of medication is precisely managed and monitored. As the pump moves the drug, it passes through a dedicated flow sensor and a pressure sensor. These sensors provide real-time feedback to a central control unit, which is the brain of the system.

The control unit operates based on a pre-set target dose and delivery rate. It continuously compares the sensor readings to the desired targets and adjusts the pump's speed accordingly to maintain a stable flow. A critical safety feature is the constant monitoring of pressure; if the pressure exceeds a maximum safe limit, which could indicate a blockage in the line, the control unit will immediately halt the pump to prevent harm to the patient. The system will also automatically stop once the total prescribed dose has been delivered.
