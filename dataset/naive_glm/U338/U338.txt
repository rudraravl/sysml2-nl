This is an Oragene DNA Collection and Preservation System, a self-contained kit designed for the simple, non-invasive collection of human saliva samples and the immediate stabilization of the DNA within them. Its primary purpose is to allow for easy self-collection and long-term storage of high-quality DNA at room temperature, eliminating the need for refrigeration or specialized storage conditions.

The physical kit consists of a few key components that work together in sequence. There is a collection tube, often with graduation marks, where the user deposits their saliva. A specially designed collection funnel can be screwed onto the tube to make providing the sample easier and more hygienic.

The most critical component is the sealing cap. This cap is not just a simple lid; it contains a sealed, integrated reservoir holding a liquid DNA stabilization reagent. The collection process is straightforward: a person provides a saliva sample into the tube up to the required volume. They then unscrew and discard the funnel and secure the sealing cap onto the tube. The act of closing the cap pierces the reservoir, automatically releasing the stabilization reagent into the raw saliva.

Once the reagent is released, the contents are mixed (typically by shaking the tube), which creates the final, stabilized DNA sample. This mixture inactivates viruses and prevents bacterial growth, preserving the integrity of the human DNA for years at ambient temperatures. The final sealed tube acts as a secure storage container, preventing contamination and making the sample safe and ready for transport to a lab for genetic analysis.
