This is an advanced, automated hospital bed system designed to provide adjustable patient support, continuous safety monitoring, and proactive care assistance. The system's primary goal is to enhance patient comfort, prevent common hospital-acquired conditions like pressure ulcers, and improve safety by alerting staff to potential risks.

The core of the system is a multi-part, adjustable bed frame. It features distinct head and foot sections, each powered by an electric motor, allowing caregivers to change their angles independently. The entire bed frame is mounted on a linear actuator system that can raise or lower its overall height.

Integrated directly into the bed is a comprehensive sensor suite. The mattress surface contains an array of sixteen pressure sensors that create a real-time "pressure map" of the patient's body. This map is used to identify high-pressure points that could lead to ulcers. The bed frame is also equipped with four load cells, which work together as a scale to continuously measure the patient's weight.

A central control unit acts as the brain of the system. It receives commands from caregivers via a control interface to adjust the bed's configuration (head angle, foot angle, and height). The controller processes information from the pressure sensors and load cells to monitor the patient's status. Based on this data, it performs several key safety functions:

*   **Patient Exit Detection:** By sensing a sudden, significant drop in weight and a change in the pressure distribution, the system can detect if a patient is attempting to get out of bed and issue an immediate alert to staff.
*   **Pressure Ulcer Risk Alert:** The system analyzes the pressure map to identify prolonged pressure on specific areas, alerting caregivers to the need to reposition the patient.
*   **Safety Interlocks:** The controller ensures that all adjustments remain within safe mechanical limits for angle and height. It also monitors the patient's weight against the bed's maximum capacity, generating an alert if the limit is exceeded.

The bed is powered by a standard electrical supply and communicates its status, patient data, and any alerts back to the caregiver through its control interface. In essence, it is an intelligent piece of medical equipment that actively participates in patient care and safety management.
