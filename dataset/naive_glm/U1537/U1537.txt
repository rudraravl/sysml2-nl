This model describes a large-scale, three-bladed horizontal-axis wind turbine system designed to convert wind energy into electricity and supply it to a power grid.

The complete structure consists of a tall, 85-meter steel **Tower** that supports a **Nacelle** (the main housing) and a large **Rotor**. The Rotor itself is comprised of a central hub and three 45-meter **Blades**, creating a total rotor diameter of 90 meters.

Functionally, wind pushes against the blades, causing the entire rotor assembly to spin. This slow, high-torque rotation is transferred into the Nacelle, where it first enters a **Gearbox**. The Gearbox increases the rotational speed significantly (by a factor of 95) to drive a **Generator** more efficiently. The Generator then converts this high-speed mechanical energy into 2.5 megawatts of electrical power.

An integrated **Control System** acts as the brain of the turbine, ensuring both optimal performance and safety. It continuously monitors wind speed and direction. Based on this data, it operates several key subsystems:
*   A **Yaw System** physically rotates the entire Nacelle on top of the tower to ensure the rotor is always facing directly into the wind.
*   A **Pitch System** adjusts the angle of the blades themselves. This is used to control the rotational speed, optimize power capture in varying winds, and can turn the blades to a "feathered" position to stop rotation.
*   A mechanical **Brake System** can be engaged to completely halt the rotor for maintenance or in an emergency.

The electricity from the generator is not yet ready for the grid. It first passes through a **Power Converter** which stabilizes the power and steps up the voltage to match the requirements of the electrical grid it connects to.

The entire system operates automatically within a specific range of wind speeds. It begins generating power at a "cut-in" speed of 3.5 m/s and shuts down for safety if winds exceed the "cut-out" speed of 25 m/s, at which point the brake is engaged and the blades are feathered to prevent damage.
