This is a digital pressure gauge system designed for continuously monitoring the pressure of a fluid and providing real-time, accurate readings with alarm capabilities for industrial or mechanical applications.

The system is composed of several key components working together. At its core is a **pressure sensor** which is in direct contact with the pressurized fluid. This sensor, which requires an external power source to operate, measures the physical pressure and converts it into a raw electrical signal.

This raw signal is then fed into a **signal processor**. This internal component is responsible for refining the measurement. It applies a calibration factor and a zero-offset adjustment to correct the raw signal, ensuring the final reading is highly accurate and compensated for any sensor drift. The processor can also track its own calibration status over time.

The corrected and accurate pressure value is then sent to two other components: a display unit and an alarm controller.

The **display unit** is the user-facing screen of the gauge. It presents the final pressure reading, the current calibration status, and any active alarm information. It is designed to have a specific resolution and to refresh at a high rate, providing an immediate visual representation of the fluid pressure.

Simultaneously, the **alarm controller** compares the pressure reading against pre-set high and low thresholds. If the pressure exceeds the high limit or drops below the low limit, it triggers an alarm. This controller includes a hysteresis function, which prevents the alarm from rapidly switching on and off if the pressure is fluctuating right at the threshold, ensuring a more stable and reliable alert system. The system can be in a normal state, a high-pressure alarm state, or a low-pressure alarm state.

Overall, the pressure gauge system is a complete, self-contained unit that connects to a fluid line, a power supply, and provides a clear display output. It is designed to meet strict requirements for measurement accuracy, fast response time, and reliable alarm functionality within specified operating pressure and temperature ranges.
