This model represents a complete vehicle manufacturing plant designed for the end-to-end production of automobiles, from raw materials to finished, quality-tested cars. The system is structured as a sequential assembly line composed of several specialized departments.

The manufacturing process begins with raw materials, primarily steel sheets and pre-built powertrains (engines and transmissions). The steel is first sent to a **stamping department**, equipped with multiple high-force presses that shape the flat sheets into the various body panels of a car.

These panels are then transported to a **welding department**, which uses a fleet of multi-armed robotic welders to assemble the panels into a complete vehicle chassis. Once the frame is constructed, it moves to the **painting department**, where it enters controlled-environment paint booths to be sprayed and cured to the desired color.

Following painting, the body proceeds to the **final assembly** station. Here, the powertrain is installed into the painted chassis, along with other components, to create a functional vehicle.

The final step is the **quality testing facility**. Each newly assembled vehicle undergoes a series of tests to generate a quality score. A vehicle must meet a specific quality threshold to be approved for shipping. The entire plant operates to meet defined production targets, including the number of vehicles produced per day, a minimum quality standard, and overall operational efficiency. The plant's operational status is managed through different states, such as being offline, fully operational during a production shift, or undergoing maintenance.
