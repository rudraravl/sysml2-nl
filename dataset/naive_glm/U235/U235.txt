This model represents a medical head mirror, a classic diagnostic device worn by healthcare practitioners to provide focused, hands-free illumination for patient examinations. The system is designed to be worn on the head, allowing the user to direct a beam of light into areas like the mouth, throat, or nasal passages, leaving their hands free to use other medical instruments.

The device is composed of four main physical parts:
*   An adjustable **Headband**, typically made of elastic fabric, which securely fastens the entire apparatus to the practitioner's forehead for a stable and comfortable fit.
*   A lightweight but rigid **Mounting Frame** that serves as the structural backbone, holding the mirror assembly in place.
*   The core optical component is a **Concave Mirror**, designed to gather ambient light from the examination room and concentrate it into a bright, focused beam. It is engineered with a specific focal distance (e.g., 25 cm) to provide optimal illumination at a typical working distance from the patient.
*   A **Pivot Joint** connects the mirror to the frame, enabling the practitioner to precisely adjust its vertical and horizontal angle. This allows them to aim the reflected light beam accurately onto the target examination area.

In operation, a practitioner first secures the device to their head. They then adjust the mirror's orientation using the pivot joint until the reflected light is perfectly aimed. The system transitions through several states: from being **stored**, to being **mounted** on the head, to a **positioning** phase for aiming, and finally to an **operating** state during the actual examination. The design prioritizes ergonomic factors, requiring the device to be lightweight, comfortable for extended use, and easy to adjust during a procedure.
