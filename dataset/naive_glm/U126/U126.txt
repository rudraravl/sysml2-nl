This is a Tinnitracks therapeutic audio system, an innovative medical device designed to provide personalized tinnitus relief through customized music therapy. The system's main purpose is to reduce the perceived loudness of a person's tinnitus by processing their own music.

The core of the therapy involves applying a highly specific "notch filter" to a music track. This filter precisely removes a very narrow band of sound frequencies that are centered on the individual's specific tinnitus tone. The theory is that by listening to this modified music, the brain's auditory cortex is stimulated in a way that promotes neuroplastic changes, effectively "turning down the volume" of the tinnitus over time.

The system is composed of several key components that work together:

*   A **Frequency Analyzer** is used to determine the user's exact tinnitus frequency. By listening to test tones, the user identifies their specific pitch, which is used to create a personalized Tinnitus Profile.
*   A **Music Library** stores and provides the music tracks that the user wants to listen to during their therapy session.
*   The central **Notch Filter Processor** is the engine of the system. It takes the music selected from the library and applies the filter based on the user's Tinnitus Profile, creating the final therapeutic audio.
*   A **Therapy Controller** manages the entire session, handling things like the duration of the therapy, starting, and stopping the process.
*   An **Audio Output Device** (like headphones) delivers the processed, therapeutic music to the user.

The overall process is straightforward: a user first has their tinnitus frequency analyzed to create a profile. Then, they select music, and the system processes it in real-time, delivering the filtered audio for a pre-set therapy session duration. The system is designed to be safe, ensuring output volume never exceeds levels that could cause hearing damage, and effective, with high precision in both frequency detection and the filtering process to maintain overall music quality.
