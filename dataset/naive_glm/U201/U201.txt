This is a medical diathermy therapy system designed to deliver controlled deep tissue heating for therapeutic purposes, such as pain relief, improved circulation, and accelerated tissue healing. The system uses high-frequency electromagnetic energy, typically shortwave or microwave radiation, to generate this heat deep within a patient's body tissues.

The core of the system is a Radio-Frequency (RF) Generator, which produces the high-power electromagnetic energy required for treatment. This energy is channeled to an Applicator, a component like a capacitive plate, which is placed directly on the patient's skin over the area to be treated. The Applicator focuses and delivers this energy into the body, causing the underlying tissues to warm up.

To ensure patient safety and treatment effectiveness, the system is equipped with several critical monitoring and control components. A Temperature Sensor continuously measures the tissue temperature at the treatment site, feeding this information back to a central Control Unit. This allows the system to automatically adjust power output to maintain a precise therapeutic temperature (typically 40-45° Celsius) and prevent burns. An Impedance Monitor also checks the electrical contact between the applicator and the skin, ensuring efficient energy delivery and detecting poor coupling that could be unsafe.

The entire operation is managed by the Control Unit, which acts as the system's brain and user interface. A clinician uses it to set treatment parameters like power level, duration, and operating mode (e.g., continuous or pulsed). A Safety Interlock system serves as a master watchdog, monitoring all critical parameters and automatically shutting down the device if it detects a fault, such as overheating or exceeding the set treatment time. Additionally, a Cooling System may be integrated with the applicator to keep the patient's skin surface at a comfortable temperature while the deeper tissues are heated.

The system operates through several states, including idle, warmup, active treatment, and a fault state if an unsafe condition arises. Overall, it is a comprehensive medical device that integrates energy generation, delivery, and a closed-loop feedback system to provide safe and effective deep heat therapy.
