This model describes an industrial fluid pumping system designed to transfer a fluid from a supply tank to a receiving tank.

The core of the system is a screw pump, which is mechanically driven by an electric motor. The pump itself is composed of a helical screw rotor that turns within a tightly fitted casing. This entire assembly is designed to move fluid from a supply tank, which stores the fluid before pumping, to a receiving tank that collects it.

In operation, the electric motor provides rotational power through a drive shaft connected to the pump's rotor. As the screw rotor turns, it traps a volume of fluid at the inlet, carries it along the length of the casing, and then forces it out of the discharge port, increasing its pressure.

To monitor its performance, the system is equipped with instrumentation. A pressure gauge is installed at the pump's inlet (suction side) and another at its outlet (discharge side) to measure the pressure difference the pump generates. A flow meter is also included to measure the actual rate at which the fluid is being transferred. The system is designed to operate within specific performance requirements, ensuring it delivers a consistent flow rate, maintains pressure within its rated limits, and achieves a minimum level of efficiency.
