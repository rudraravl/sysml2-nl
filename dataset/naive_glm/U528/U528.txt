This model describes a complete fluid transfer system built around a Jabsco self-priming positive displacement pump (model 50880-1000 series). The system is designed to automatically draw fluid from a source reservoir, pump it through a series of pipes and components, and deliver it to a destination tank.

The process begins at a 100-liter source reservoir. As fluid leaves the reservoir, it first passes through a strainer filter. This crucial safety component removes debris from the fluid, protecting the pump from damage. From the strainer, the fluid travels through a reinforced rubber suction pipe to the core of the system: the Jabsco pump.

This is a 12-volt DC, self-priming pump featuring a durable bronze housing and a flexible nitrile rubber impeller driven by a permanent magnet DC motor. Once pressurized by the pump, the fluid is pushed out through a discharge pipe. To ensure safety and prevent backflow when the pump is off, the fluid then passes through a check valve.

The system's performance is actively monitored. A flow meter is installed in the line to measure the rate of fluid transfer, and a pressure sensor on the discharge pipe monitors the operating pressure. Finally, the fluid arrives at the 50-liter destination tank. The entire system is powered by a 12-volt DC power supply connected to the pump's motor.

Operationally, the system has a defined lifecycle. It can be idle, go through a self-priming sequence to remove air from the lines, run at its normal operating speed to transfer fluid, and then stop. It also includes a fault state for handling operational errors. The system is engineered to meet specific requirements, including maintaining a nominal flow rate of 15 liters per minute, operating below a maximum pressure of 3 bar, and ensuring reliability and safety through its components.
