This model describes a reusable ice pack designed for sustained cooling, suitable for applications like medical therapy, food preservation, or general temperature control.

The ice pack is composed of three main parts. At its core is a specialized coolant gel, which is the active cooling agent. This gel is engineered with specific thermal properties, including a high capacity to absorb heat as it melts (latent heat of fusion), allowing it to maintain a low temperature for an extended period.

This gel is contained within a durable, flexible pouch made of polyethylene, designed to be leak-proof and hold a specific volume of the coolant. To enhance its performance, the pouch is wrapped in a layer of insulation material. This insulation minimizes the rate at which heat is absorbed from the surrounding warm environment, significantly extending the pack's effective cooling time.

The ice pack operates through a distinct lifecycle based on the physical state of the gel. It starts in a frozen state, ready for use. As it absorbs heat from an object or the environment, it enters a melting state, where it provides its most effective and stable cooling. Once all the gel has melted, it becomes a liquid and gradually warms to the ambient temperature. The system is specifically designed to remain below 4°C (277.15 K) for at least three hours to meet its primary cooling requirements.
