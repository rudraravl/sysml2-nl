This model represents a highly organized and cooperative social animal pack, structured very much like a wolf pack. The system is designed for survival and thriving through collective intelligence and a clear social hierarchy. Its primary functions are to hunt for food, defend its territory, and maintain the social health of the group.

The pack is composed of different members with specialized roles. An Alpha member acts as the leader, possessing strong leadership qualities and the authority to make critical decisions. This leader is supported by Beta members, while specialized Scouts are responsible for reconnaissance and have a long detection range for finding prey. The bulk of the pack consists of Hunters who work together to carry out the hunt.

The pack's core activities are centered around a sophisticated, multi-stage cooperative hunt. The process begins when scouts detect prey within the pack's territory. Once prey is located, the Alpha communicates and coordinates with the members to form a strategic hunting formation. The pack then executes a coordinated approach, encircles the prey, and finally executes the capture. The success of the hunt depends on this seamless cooperation.

Beyond hunting, the pack is constantly engaged in defending its territory from threats. This involves regular patrols and, if a threat is detected, mobilizing the group to confront and repel the intruder.

The system also emphasizes the internal health and social dynamics of the pack. After a successful hunt, food resources are distributed among the members. The pack also engages in social bonding activities to maintain high cohesion, which is critical for their cooperative efforts. The entire pack operates in different behavioral states, such as resting, actively hunting, defending its territory, or nurturing, transitioning between these modes based on external cues like the presence of prey or threats.
