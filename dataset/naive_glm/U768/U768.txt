This model represents a Mass Flow Sensing Unit, an industrial instrument designed to be installed in a fluid pipeline to measure the rate at which mass is flowing through it. In addition to mass flow rate, the unit also measures the temperature and pressure of the fluid.

The system is composed of two primary internal components: a core Mass Flow Sensor and a Signal Processor. In operation, a process fluid enters the unit through an inlet port, flows directly through the sensor, and then exits through an outlet port to continue downstream. While the fluid is inside the sensor, a measurement is taken to determine its properties.

The raw measurement data from the sensor is then internally routed to the Signal Processor. This processor applies a filter to smooth out the raw data, producing a more stable and reliable output. This final, processed data—including mass flow rate, temperature, pressure, and the unit's operational status—is then transmitted through a data interface port to an external monitoring or control system.

The device operates through several states, such as Initializing, Ready, Measuring, and Fault. It is designed to meet specific performance criteria, including measuring a flow range from 0 to 100 kg/s with an accuracy of at least 2% and responding to changes in flow within 100 milliseconds. The model represents a specific instance of this unit with the ID "MFS-2024-001," which was last calibrated on January 15, 2024.
