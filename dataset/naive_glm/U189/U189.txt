This model describes an advanced, intelligent ostomy care system designed for patients with a stoma. The system's primary purpose is to securely and discreetly collect bodily waste while actively protecting the surrounding skin, controlling odor, and providing users with proactive alerts to prevent issues.

The system is composed of several key components that work together. At its core is a two-part appliance: a **Skin Barrier** and a **Collection Pouch**. The Skin Barrier is an adhesive plate that sticks to the skin around the stoma, creating a secure seal to prevent leaks and protect the skin from irritation. The Collection Pouch attaches to this barrier to collect the effluent.

To enhance user confidence and discretion, the system includes an integrated **Charcoal Filter**. This filter allows gas to be released from the pouch, preventing it from ballooning, while neutralizing any associated odors. For patient comfort, the pouch is covered with a soft, breathable **Comfort Layer** that rests against the skin or clothing.

What makes this system advanced is its integrated electronic monitoring. It features a **Leakage Detection Sensor** that constantly checks the integrity of the seal between the barrier and the skin. If it detects moisture or a loss of adhesion that could lead to a leak, it communicates with an **Alert Module**. This module then notifies the user through visual or auditory signals. The alert system also warns the user when the pouch is nearing capacity, when the filter is saturated, or when the recommended wear time has expired.

The system operates through various states in its lifecycle, starting as `unattached`. Once applied to the body, it enters an `attached` and then `collecting` state. As it fills, it may move to a `nearCapacity` state, prompting the user to empty or change it. If a problem like a leak is detected, it enters a `compromised` state, requiring immediate attention. This smart monitoring aims to prevent accidents and improve the quality of life for the user.
