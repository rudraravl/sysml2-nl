This model describes an autonomous mobile robotic system, composed of a versatile, multi-purpose robot and a central control station that issues commands. The system is designed for environments like a factory floor, warehouse, or laboratory where automated, precision tasks are required.

The robot is a modular unit designed to operate with a high degree of autonomy. Its core function is to receive a task command—which includes a priority level and a target location—and execute it. It is equipped with several specialized subsystems that work in concert:

*   A **Sensor Module** constantly scans the environment up to 50 meters away, detecting obstacles and gathering data to ensure safe operation.
*   A **Navigation Module** uses this sensor data to plan the most efficient path to a target location, navigate autonomously at speeds up to 3 m/s, and position itself with an accuracy of 5 centimeters.
*   An **Operation Module** is the "working" part of the robot, capable of performing physical tasks like picking up, placing, inspecting, or measuring objects with a precision of 1 centimeter.
*   A **Communication Module** maintains a link with the control center over a range of 200 meters, allowing it to receive commands and report back the results of its tasks.
*   A **Power Module** manages the onboard battery, supplying electricity to all other components.

The robot operates in several distinct modes: `standby` while awaiting instructions, `active` while carrying out a mission, `maintenance` for servicing, and `fault` if an error occurs. When a task is received, the robot transitions to its active mode and follows a clear operational sequence: it first scans its surroundings, plans its route, travels to the destination, executes the required physical operation, validates that the operation was successful, and finally reports the outcome to the control center before returning to standby.
