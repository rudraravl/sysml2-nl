This model represents a complete, automated industrial riveting machine system. Its primary function is to permanently join metal components together by installing and forming rivets with high precision and force. Designed for a manufacturing environment, the system is composed of several key, interacting sub-assemblies.

The core of the machine is built around a powerful hydraulic system. A central **Hydraulic Power Unit** generates high-pressure fluid, which provides the force needed for both clamping and riveting. This unit supplies controlled pressure to two other components:

1.  A **Clamping Assembly**, which acts like a powerful vice. It uses hydraulic force to securely hold the metal workpieces in place, ensuring they don't move during the high-force riveting operation.

2.  A **Riveting Head Assembly**, which is the primary tool for forming the joint. It receives a rivet and then uses immense hydraulic force (up to 50,000 Newtons) to press and deform it, permanently locking the metal sheets together.

To automate the process, a **Rivet Feeder Unit** with a large magazine (holding 500 rivets) automatically supplies the correct size rivet to the riveting head for each cycle. After the rivet is formed, an integrated **Quality Inspection Unit** immediately examines the joint to ensure it meets a predefined quality threshold (at least 95% accuracy).

The entire operation is orchestrated by a central **System Controller**. This is the brain of the machine, managing the sequence of operations for a complete riveting cycle: loading the workpiece, feeding the rivet, clamping the assembly, applying riveting force, inspecting the result, and finally unloading the finished part. The controller also provides an interface for an operator to start, stop, or perform an emergency shutdown of the machine, and it reports on the status of each cycle and the quality of the finished product. The entire process is optimized for speed, aiming to complete a full cycle in 12 seconds or less.
