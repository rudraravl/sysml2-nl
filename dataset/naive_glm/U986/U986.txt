This model describes a fully automated restaurant system designed to manage the entire dining process from customer ordering to meal delivery without direct human intervention. The system is comprised of several interconnected, automated components that work in concert to provide an efficient food service experience.

The process begins at one of the four customer **Order Kiosks**, where patrons select menu items, specify any special instructions, and complete payment. Once an order is confirmed, it is sent to a central **Order Coordinator**. This coordinator acts as the brain of the operation, managing a queue of incoming orders, scheduling them for preparation based on priority, and coordinating the kitchen and delivery operations. The system is designed to handle up to 60 concurrent customer orders.

The order is then routed to the automated **Kitchen Station**. This is a highly sophisticated unit equipped with six cooking units, twelve automated ingredient dispensers, and three multi-axis robotic arms. The kitchen follows a precise, automated sequence to prepare each meal: it retrieves the necessary ingredients, cooks the items to the correct temperature, assembles the final meal, and performs a quality check. This entire preparation process is optimized to be completed within 12 minutes for a standard meal.

As a meal is finished, the coordinator dispatches a request to the **Delivery Robot** fleet. The system operates with five of these robots, which are responsible for transporting the hot meals from the kitchen to the customer's table. Each robot is equipped with a navigation system and an internal map of the restaurant, allowing it to autonomously travel to the correct destination. The delivery is designed to be very quick, taking less than three minutes, and highly accurate.

Behind the scenes, an **Inventory Manager** continuously monitors the stock levels of all ingredients. When supplies for an item are used by the kitchen, the inventory is updated. If a stock level falls below a predetermined point, the system automatically places a restock order with suppliers to ensure the kitchen never runs out of essential ingredients.

The entire system operates through a cycle of states, from being idle to actively receiving orders, preparing meals, delivering them, and performing inventory restocking tasks as needed, ensuring a seamless and continuous flow of service.
