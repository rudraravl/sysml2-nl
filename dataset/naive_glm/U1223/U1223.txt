This is a high-power, continuous-wave laser system that uses a synthetic diamond as its core amplifying component. It is designed for demanding applications such as industrial materials processing, advanced scientific research, and directed energy systems. The system's primary function is to convert high-voltage electrical energy into a stable, precisely controlled, and powerful 10-kilowatt laser beam.

The system is composed of several key integrated components:

*   **Power Supply Unit:** This serves as the initial energy source, taking in main power and converting it into the high-current electricity needed to run the laser.
*   **Diode Pump Array:** This is an array of 24 smaller laser diodes that are powered by the main supply. Instead of being the final output, their combined light is used to "pump" or energize the central diamond crystal.
*   **Synthetic Diamond Gain Medium:** This is the heart of the system. It's a small, highly pure synthetic diamond crystal doped with nitrogen. The diamond absorbs the light from the diode pumps, and its unique atomic structure allows it to amplify this energy and re-emit it as a much more powerful and coherent beam of light at a specific wavelength (1064 nm). The diamond's exceptionally high thermal conductivity is critical for dissipating the immense waste heat generated.
*   **Resonator Mirrors:** Two specialized mirrors are placed on either side of the diamond crystal, forming an optical cavity. One mirror is almost perfectly reflective, while the other (the output coupler) is partially reflective. This setup traps the light, causing it to pass back and forth through the diamond many times, building in intensity before exiting through the output coupler as the final, powerful laser beam.
*   **Thermal Management System:** To prevent the diamond from overheating and being damaged, a robust cooling system actively circulates a coolant fluid through it, carrying away waste heat to maintain a stable operating temperature.
*   **Laser Controller and Sensors:** A central controller, guided by a temperature sensor on the diamond, manages the entire operation. It handles commands to enable or disable the laser, monitors the system's temperature, regulates the coolant flow, and can trigger an emergency shutdown if temperature limits are exceeded.

The laser operates through several distinct states, including standby, warmup, active lasing, and a controlled cooldown period. During active lasing, the system continuously monitors and adjusts its temperature to ensure stable and safe performance.
