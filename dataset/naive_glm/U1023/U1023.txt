This model describes a comprehensive, full-body cyborg system designed to seamlessly integrate a human's biological functions with advanced cybernetic components. The system's primary purpose is to augment and enhance human capabilities, providing superhuman strength, endurance, sensory perception, and cognitive processing.

The system is composed of several key integrated parts. At its heart is the **Biological Core**, representing the organic human element, which generates neural intents (thoughts) and even contributes bio-electric energy to the system. This core's vital signs, such as heart rate and blood oxygen levels, are constantly monitored to ensure the user's health and safety.

Working in tandem is the **Cybernetic Core**, which serves as the central processing hub and power management unit. It receives neural signals from the brain through a direct interface, decodes them into actionable motor commands, and processes a wide array of sensory information. This core manages power distribution, drawing from both its internal batteries and the biological energy produced by the user.

To enhance physical abilities, the system is equipped with a full set of four powerful **Bionic Limbs**. These robotic arms and legs receive motor commands from the Cybernetic Core to execute precise and forceful movements, far exceeding natural human strength. They also feature integrated tactile sensors that provide sensory feedback, allowing the user to "feel" through the artificial limbs.

A dedicated **Cognitive Processor** further augments the user's mental faculties. This component analyzes the user's biological thought patterns alongside incoming sensory data from the environment, enabling faster decision-making, enhanced problem-solving, and superior situational awareness.

The cyborg operates in various modes depending on the situation. These include a standard `active` mode, a `power-saving` mode to extend endurance, an `enhanced` mode for peak performance, and a `safe` mode that activates if critical errors are detected, prioritizing the user's well-being. Overall, this system represents a complete fusion of human and machine, designed to operate as a single, highly capable entity.
