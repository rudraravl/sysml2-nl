This model describes a medical device, a penis clamp, designed for managing male urinary incontinence. Its primary purpose is to apply controlled external pressure to the urethra to stop or reduce involuntary urine flow, providing a non-invasive solution for men.

The device is composed of several integrated components. The core of the system is a **Compression Mechanism** controlled by an **Adjustment Lever**. The user can set the lever to one of several positions (up to six) to apply a specific, desired amount of compressive force. This force is not applied directly but is distributed through a soft **Pressure Pad**, which ensures the pressure is applied evenly and comfortably over a small area to prevent discomfort or tissue damage.

To ensure both safety and effectiveness, the clamp includes smart monitoring features. A **Flow Sensor** is integrated to detect the rate of any urine flow, providing feedback on how well the device is working. More importantly, a dedicated **Safety Monitor** continuously checks two critical parameters: the level of applied pressure and the duration the device has been in continuous use. This monitor is pre-set with safety thresholds (e.g., a maximum pressure of 45,000 Pascals and a maximum duration of 2 hours) to prevent potential injury.

The device operates through a clear lifecycle. It begins in an `idle` state. When a user puts it on (`positioning`) and activates it, it enters a `compressing` state until the target pressure is achieved. It then stays in an `active` state, maintaining compression while continuously monitoring for safety issues. The user can signal the device to release pressure normally. However, if the Safety Monitor detects that pressure or duration limits have been exceeded, it triggers an `emergency` state, causing the clamp to release immediately to ensure user safety.
