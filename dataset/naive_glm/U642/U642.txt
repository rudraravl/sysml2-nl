This model describes a curb feeler system for a vehicle, designed to help drivers avoid hitting curbs and other low obstacles during parking and slow-speed maneuvers.

The system is composed of two independent assemblies, mounted on the left and right sides of the vehicle. Each assembly features a flexible, 200mm spring-steel rod that extends outwards. This rod is the primary sensor; when it makes physical contact with an object, a force-sensitive contact sensor at its base registers the impact.

Once contact is detected, a dedicated alert controller for that side of the vehicle processes the signal. It calculates the proximity to the obstacle and determines if it is within a predefined safety threshold. If the vehicle is too close, the controller sends a command to the driver's alert interface. This interface then activates an audible alarm inside the vehicle, producing a 2000 Hz tone to immediately warn the driver.

The system is designed to operate in different states, moving from inactive to a continuous monitoring state when the vehicle is maneuvering. It provides a tactile, physical warning that prevents wheel scuffs, tire damage, and scratches to the vehicle's body panels by alerting the driver before the wheels or body make contact with the curb.
