This is a model of a modern, cordless, battery-powered vacuum cleaner designed for cleaning various household surfaces like hard floors, carpets, and upholstery.

The system's core function is driven by a high-speed suction motor powered by a rechargeable battery pack. This motor spins an impeller fan to generate powerful airflow and suction. This suction is channeled through a cleaning head, which is the part that makes contact with the floor. The cleaning head is equipped with a motorized brush roll that agitates surfaces to dislodge embedded dirt and a nozzle that draws the debris-laden air into the vacuum.

Once inside, the dirty air enters a dust chamber where larger debris is separated and collected. The air then passes through a multi-stage filtration system, which includes a pre-filter for larger particles and a high-efficiency HEPA filter to capture fine dust and allergens. After being filtered, clean air is exhausted back into the room.

The entire operation is managed by an electronic control module, which serves as the vacuum's brain. This controller allows the user to select different operating modes, such as eco, normal, or turbo, to balance cleaning power and battery life. The system can also automatically detect the type of surface being cleaned and adjust the motor's power and the brush roll's speed accordingly.

The vacuum operates in several states, including idle, standby, and active cleaning. It constantly monitors its own status, alerting the user when the dust chamber is full, the filter is clogged and requires maintenance, or when the battery is running low and needs to be recharged. The design emphasizes portability, high filtration efficiency, and sufficient runtime for typical cleaning tasks.
