This model describes the Special Sensor Microwave Imager/Sounder (SSMIS), an advanced satellite-based instrument designed for continuous, all-weather environmental monitoring of the Earth. Its primary function is to passively measure the natural microwave radiation emitted and scattered by the planet's atmosphere, oceans, and land surfaces to provide critical data for weather forecasting and climate research.

The core of the system is a highly sensitive multichannel radiometer that detects microwave energy across 24 different frequency channels. This sensor is paired with a conical scanning antenna which constantly rotates to observe a wide, 1707 km swath of the Earth's surface as the satellite orbits. This scanning mechanism allows the instrument to achieve global coverage rapidly.

As the antenna scans, the radiometer collects raw "brightness temperature" data. This information is immediately sent to an onboard signal processor, which uses sophisticated algorithms to convert the raw measurements into a variety of finished environmental data products. These products include detailed profiles of atmospheric temperature and humidity, measurements of ocean surface wind speed and sea ice concentration, and data on precipitation type and intensity.

Once processed, these final data products are passed to a data transmitter, which relays the information at a high data rate to ground stations on Earth. To ensure the accuracy of its scientific measurements, the SSMIS includes a calibration subsystem that uses on-board hot and cold temperature references to regularly calibrate the radiometer.

The entire instrument is supported by a power management unit that draws energy from solar arrays and stores it in batteries, and a thermal control system that maintains all components within their optimal operating temperature range in the harsh environment of space. The SSMIS operates through several distinct modes, including standby, calibration, active imaging, and data transmission, transitioning between them based on commands from ground control.
