This is a mobile, battery-powered patient lift system designed to help caregivers safely transfer individuals with limited mobility. The system is built for use in clinical or home care settings, reducing physical strain on staff and ensuring patient safety.

The lift is constructed on a stable base frame with four caster wheels for maneuverability, two of which are equipped with brakes to secure the unit during a transfer. A vertical mast rises from the base and supports the main lifting mechanism.

At the core of the system is an articulated lifting arm, powered by an electric actuator. A caregiver operates the lift using a simple handheld pendant control with buttons to raise, lower, or stop the motion. This control sends signals to a central control module, which is the brain of the system.

A detachable, full-body patient sling made of polyester mesh attaches to the end of the lifting arm to securely and comfortably hold the patient.

The entire system is powered by a rechargeable battery, allowing it to be used freely without being tethered to a wall outlet. For safety, it is equipped with several critical features: an integrated load cell continuously measures the patient's weight to prevent overloading, a height sensor provides precise positioning feedback to the control system, and a prominent emergency stop button can immediately halt all movement.

The system operates through various states, including standby, raising, lowering, and a locked elevated position, ensuring a smooth, controlled, and safe transfer process for both the patient and the caregiver.
