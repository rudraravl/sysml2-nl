This is a lead shielding system designed for radiation protection in an industrial or medical setting, such as a facility using a Cobalt-60 radioactive source. The system's primary function is to create a safe environment for personnel and equipment by physically blocking harmful ionizing radiation.

The system is built around a core component: a dense lead shield, which is 7.5 cm thick in this configuration. This physical barrier is placed between the high-intensity radiation source and the area that needs to be protected. The lead material is specifically chosen for its ability to absorb, or attenuate, a significant portion of the radiation passing through it.

To ensure safety is actively maintained, the system incorporates a continuous monitoring and alarm subsystem. A dosimeter sensor is installed within the protected zone to constantly measure the level of radiation that manages to penetrate the shield. This real-time data is fed to an alarm controller. The controller compares the measured radiation dose against a pre-defined maximum permissible safety limit.

If the radiation levels remain below the safe threshold, the system operates normally. However, if the dosimeter detects a radiation level that exceeds the safety limit—perhaps due to a problem with the shield or the source—the alarm controller will immediately activate an alarm and can engage a safety interlock, which might shut down equipment or secure the area to prevent exposure. The entire system is designed to work together, combining passive physical blocking with active electronic monitoring to provide a comprehensive radiation safety solution.
