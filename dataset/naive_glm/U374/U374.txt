This model describes an autonomous Scavenger Robot system designed for automatically finding, collecting, sorting, and depositing valuable materials from an environment, such as a waste site or a post-disaster area.

The core of the system is the Scavenger Robot, a self-contained mobile unit. It is equipped with a sensor module that can scan its surroundings up to 100 meters to detect and identify different types of materials, including metal, plastic, and electronics. Once a target item is identified, the robot's navigation system autonomously pilots it to the item's location.

For collection, the robot uses a mechanical arm with a 3-meter reach and significant grasping strength to physically retrieve the items. After collection, an onboard processing module sorts the raw material, separating valuable resources from waste with high accuracy. These processed items are then held in an internal storage compartment, which has a capacity of 1.5 cubic meters.

The robot's operation is governed by its current state and resources. It operates through various modes, including searching, collecting, processing, and transporting. Its decisions are driven by its internal status: if its storage bay is full, it will travel to a designated Deposit Station to unload its cargo. If its battery level, managed by a power management system, drops too low, it will automatically navigate to a nearby Charging Station to recharge.

The entire system is designed to be self-sufficient, operating continuously and making intelligent decisions to efficiently scavenge materials without direct human supervision.
