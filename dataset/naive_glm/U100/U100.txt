This model represents a three-component inflatable penile implant system, a surgically implanted medical device designed to provide on-demand erectile function.

The system is a closed-loop hydraulic circuit that uses a sterile saline solution as its working fluid. It consists of three main parts: a fluid reservoir, a manual pump with an integrated release valve, and a pair of inflatable cylinders. In a real-world application, the reservoir is placed in the abdomen, the pump in the scrotum for easy access, and the two cylinders are implanted within the penis.

To operate the device, the user manually squeezes the pump. Each compression transfers a set volume of saline fluid from the reservoir into the two cylinders. As the cylinders fill with fluid, they inflate, causing their internal pressure and rigidity to increase, ultimately producing an erection. The system transitions from a flaccid state to an erect state once a sufficient level of rigidity is achieved.

To return to a flaccid state, the user activates the release valve. This opens a channel that allows the fluid to flow back out of the cylinders and into the reservoir, deflating the device. The system is designed to ensure a nearly complete return of fluid to achieve a natural flaccid state. The entire operation is user-controlled, functioning within specified pressure safety limits to prevent injury or device failure.
