This is an intelligent emergency medical bag system designed for first responders and medical personnel operating in field conditions. The system provides organized, rapid access to essential medical supplies and includes integrated features to assist with patient assessment and inventory management.

The physical bag is divided into several specialized, temperature-controlled storage compartments. These include a dedicated compartment for medications, another for sterile instruments, and a third for general wound care items like bandages and antiseptics. Each compartment is designed to maintain a specific temperature range to ensure the integrity and effectiveness of the supplies inside.

Beyond simple storage, the system incorporates an electronic Inventory Manager. This component actively tracks every item in the bag, including its quantity, expiration date, and sterility status. It provides alerts for expired or non-sterile items, ensuring the bag is always properly stocked and ready for an emergency.

A key feature is the Diagnostic Module, which acts as a decision-support tool. By inputting a patient's vital signs (such as heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature), the module helps the user assess the severity of the patient's condition and can recommend the appropriate supplies needed for treatment.

The entire system is built around a core operational workflow for providing emergency care. This process involves opening the sealed bag, assessing the patient's condition, retrieving the correct supplies based on the assessed urgency, administering treatment, and then logging the event in a treatment record. The system is designed to be lightweight, portable, and to ensure that all supplies are sterile, effective, and accessible within seconds.
