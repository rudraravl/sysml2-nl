This is a high-precision position measurement system based on a Linear Variable Differential Transformer (LVDT). Its primary function is to accurately convert the linear mechanical movement of an object into a proportional electrical signal, which is then displayed as a precise position reading.

The system is built around a core sensor, the LVDT transformer assembly. This assembly consists of a central primary coil and two secondary coils, all surrounding a movable magnetic ferrite core. The core is the component that physically connects to the object whose position is being measured.

The system's operation begins with a power supply providing DC voltage to an excitation source. This source generates a stable, high-frequency AC voltage that energizes the primary coil of the transformer. This, in turn, induces AC voltages in the two secondary coils.

As the ferrite core moves linearly within the transformer, it alters the magnetic coupling between the primary and the two secondary coils. When the core is perfectly centered, the voltages in the secondary coils are equal and opposite, resulting in a zero net output. As the core moves off-center, one secondary coil produces a stronger voltage while the other produces a weaker one. The difference in these voltages is directly proportional to the core's displacement from the center, and the phase indicates the direction of movement.

A dedicated signal conditioner processes these raw AC outputs. It first calculates the differential voltage between the two secondary coils. This signal is then demodulated (converted from AC to DC), filtered to remove noise, and amplified. The result is a clean, stable DC voltage that directly corresponds to the core's position.

Finally, a display unit receives this conditioned DC voltage, converts it into a calibrated displacement value (e.g., in millimeters), and presents it as a digital position readout for an operator or another system. The entire system is designed to measure displacements over a range of ±25 mm with a resolution of at least 0.01 mm.
