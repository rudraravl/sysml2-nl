This model describes a complete medical Laser Scalpel System designed for performing precise surgical incisions and tissue coagulation. The system delivers controlled, high-power laser energy through a handheld instrument, allowing surgeons to cut and treat tissue with high precision and minimal damage to surrounding areas.

The system is composed of several key, interconnected components:

*   **Laser Generator:** This is the core of the system, responsible for producing the high-energy laser beam. It consists of a laser diode array that generates the initial light, an optical cavity to amplify it, and beam-shaping optics to ensure the quality of the final laser beam.

*   **Control Console:** This is the user interface and central brain of the system. It features a touchscreen display for setting parameters like power and operating mode (e.g., cutting or coagulation) and provides feedback to the operator. A foot pedal connected to the console allows the surgeon to activate and deactivate the laser hands-free.

*   **Optical Delivery System:** This component transports the laser beam from the generator to the surgeon. It uses a combination of a multi-jointed articulated arm and a flexible fiber optic cable, giving the surgeon freedom of movement while ensuring the laser is delivered efficiently.

*   **Surgical Handpiece:** This is the lightweight, pen-like tool that the surgeon holds. It receives the laser light from the fiber optic cable and uses focusing optics to create a tiny, precise spot on the tissue. It also includes a low-power, visible aiming beam for accurate targeting and a sensor to detect when the tip is in contact with tissue.

*   **Thermal Management System:** To handle the significant heat generated by the laser, a dedicated cooling system continuously circulates a coolant. It uses a pump, a reservoir, and a heat exchanger to keep the laser generator and the handpiece within their safe operating temperatures during extended procedures.

*   **Safety Interlock System:** Safety is a critical aspect of the design. This system acts as a master control, preventing accidental laser activation. It integrates multiple safety checks, including sensors on the operating room door, a key-operated switch, an emergency stop button, and other external interlocks. The laser cannot be armed or fired unless all safety conditions are met.

The system operates through a well-defined sequence of states. After being powered on, it performs an initialization and self-test. If successful, it enters a standby mode. To become ready for use, all safety interlocks must be engaged. From the ready state, the surgeon can select a mode and use the foot pedal to activate the laser for cutting or coagulation. The system continuously monitors its own temperature and safety status, and will enter a fault state if any issues are detected.
