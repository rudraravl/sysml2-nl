This model represents a Federal Signal Emergency Warning System, the kind of integrated siren and light-bar system used on emergency vehicles like police cars, fire trucks, and ambulances. Its purpose is to provide powerful, coordinated audible and visual alerts to clear traffic and warn the public during an emergency response.

The system is built around a central **Control Processor**, which acts as the brain. This processor receives commands from an operator, which specify the urgency of the situation and the desired siren and light patterns—for example, an "emergency" priority with a "wail" tone and a "triple flash" light pattern.

Power for the entire system is managed by a dedicated **Power Supply Unit**. This component connects to the vehicle's main electrical system, but it also conditions the power and includes a battery backup. This ensures the warning system can continue to operate reliably for a period even if the vehicle's engine or primary power fails.

Based on the operator's command, the Control Processor sends separate instructions to two specialized modules: a **Siren Driver** and a **Light Controller**.

The Siren Driver is a powerful amplifier that generates the specific electrical signals for different siren tones (such as wail, yelp, or piercer). This signal is then sent to a set of four powerful **Speaker Arrays** mounted on the vehicle, producing the loud, audible warning.

Simultaneously, the Light Controller manages the visual alerts. It takes the command for a flash pattern (like double-flash or rotating) and precisely controls the power sent to six **LED Light Heads**, which are the individual lighting units within the vehicle's light-bar.

The entire system is designed for high reliability, continuously monitoring its own operational health and providing diagnostic feedback to ensure it is always ready for an emergency.
