This is a comprehensive Internet of Things (IoT) system designed for environmental monitoring and automated control. The system's primary function is to collect data from various physical locations, securely process it in the cloud, and then use that information to automatically manage devices like HVAC and lighting systems.

The core of the system consists of several key components working together. At the edge are multiple battery-powered **IoT Sensors**. These devices periodically wake from a low-power sleep state to measure environmental conditions like temperature, humidity, and pressure. After taking a reading, they transmit this data and return to sleep to conserve energy.

All data from the sensors is sent to a central **Network Gateway**. This gateway acts as a router, managing communications for up to 100 connected devices, including both sensors and actuators. It directs all incoming sensor data towards the cloud.

Before the data reaches the cloud, it passes through a dedicated **Security Module**. This component is responsible for ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of the information by authenticating its source and encrypting all communications using strong AES-256 encryption.

The secured data is then received by a **Cloud Platform**, which serves as the brain of the system. Here, the data is aggregated, stored, and analyzed to identify patterns or anomalies. If the cloud's analysis determines that an action is needed (for example, if the temperature rises above a certain threshold), it generates and sends out specific control commands.

These commands are sent back to **Actuator Devices** in the field. These devices are the "hands" of the system and are responsible for performing physical actions. Examples include an HVAC unit that can be told to turn on cooling, or a smart lighting system that can be dimmed or brightened. Upon receiving a command, the actuator executes the requested action and sends back a status report, closing the automated control loop.
