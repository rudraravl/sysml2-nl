This is a multi-channel olfactometer system, a scientific instrument designed for research and clinical applications that require the precise delivery of scents. Its main purpose is to generate and deliver controlled, repeatable puffs of scented air where the odorant's concentration, duration, temperature, and humidity are all carefully managed.

The system is built around a central air-handling pathway. It begins with a supply of clean, filtered air that serves as a neutral carrier stream. This air is first conditioned, passing through a humidity controller and a temperature controller to bring it to a specific, stable state, which is crucial for consistent scientific results.

The olfactometer has four separate channels, allowing it to hold and deliver up to four different liquid odorants from individual reservoirs. To create a scented stimulus, a tiny, precisely measured amount of vapor from one of the selected odorants is mixed into the main carrier air stream. This is achieved using highly accurate mass flow controllers (MFCs), which act like sophisticated valves to regulate the flow of both the carrier air and the odorant vapor.

These two streams are then combined and blended in a mixing manifold. The result is a single, continuous stream of air containing the odorant at the desired concentration. A key feature is a closed-loop feedback mechanism; a concentration sensor is placed at the output to continuously measure the final scent level. This information is sent to a central control computer, which can make real-time adjustments to the flow controllers to ensure the delivered concentration perfectly matches the target set by the researcher.

The entire process is automated by the control computer. An operator defines a stimulus protocol, specifying which scent to use, its final concentration, and how long the puff should last. The system then executes this protocol, which involves stabilizing the flows, delivering the stimulus, and finally purging the system with clean air to clear out any residual scent before the next trial.
