This is a wearable therapeutic device, a "Headache Hat," designed to provide non-pharmaceutical relief from headaches and migraines. It works by combining the principles of targeted cold therapy and gentle compression.

The system is composed of several key components. The main body is a soft, adjustable fabric shell, made from a material like neoprene, that comfortably conforms to the user's head. This shell features four integrated slots designed to hold removable and reusable cooling gel packs. These gel packs are the core of the cold therapy; they must be frozen beforehand to store thermal energy, which is then released as cooling during a session. An adjustable elastic strap is also part of the hat, allowing the user to apply a gentle, consistent pressure for compression therapy.

To ensure safety during use, the hat is equipped with two temperature sensors that monitor the skin's surface temperature at the points of contact. This prevents the skin from getting too cold and avoids potential injury.

A typical therapy session involves first preparing the gel packs by freezing them. The user then inserts the frozen packs into the fabric shell, puts the hat on, and tightens the compression strap to a comfortable level. The hat then simultaneously delivers cold and compression to key areas of the head. The system is designed to operate for a recommended session duration, such as 20 minutes, during which it continuously monitors skin temperature. If the temperature drops to an unsafe level, the therapy is designed to be stopped immediately. The overall goal is to effectively reduce the user's pain level in a safe, comfortable, and reusable manner.
