This model describes the Surgibox, a portable, battery-powered sterile field system. It is designed to create a clean, controlled environment for surgical procedures, particularly in remote, resource-limited, or field settings where a traditional operating room is not available.

The core of the system is a **Sterile Workspace**, a physical enclosure where surgery takes place. The system's primary function is to continuously pump highly filtered air into this workspace to create and maintain positive pressure. This positive pressure ensures that any potential leaks push clean air out, preventing unfiltered, contaminated air from entering the surgical area.

Its operation relies on several integrated components working together:

*   A **Fan Assembly** draws in ambient air from the outside.
*   This air is immediately forced through a medical-grade **HEPA Filter Unit**, which is extremely efficient at removing airborne particles like dust, bacteria, and other contaminants.
*   The clean, filtered air is then managed by a **Pressure Regulation System** which directs it into the Sterile Workspace.
*   An **Environment Monitor** continuously senses the air pressure inside the workspace. This information is used by a central **System Controller**, which acts as the brain of the unit. The controller adjusts the fan's speed to keep the internal pressure at a precise, positive level relative to the outside environment.
*   The entire system is powered by a self-contained **Battery Pack**, allowing it to operate for several hours without needing an external power source.

The Surgibox operates through different states, such as initializing, pressurizing the workspace, a "sterile ready" state for when it's safe to begin, and an active operational state for use during a procedure. It also includes safety states for low battery conditions and a proper shutdown sequence. In essence, the Surgibox is a self-contained "operating room in a box" designed to meet strict requirements for air filtration and sterility.
