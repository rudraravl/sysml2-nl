This is a comprehensive baby monitoring system designed to provide continuous, remote surveillance of an infant's environment, ensuring safety and enabling timely caregiver response. The system consists of two primary components: a Baby Unit placed in the infant's room and a portable Parent Unit for the caregiver.

The **Baby Unit** is a multi-sensor device that captures a complete picture of the room's conditions. It is equipped with a high-definition camera (1920x1080 resolution) that provides a clear, real-time video stream. The camera features automatic night vision for visibility in low-light conditions and has a wide 110-degree field of view. A sensitive microphone continuously captures audio, which is processed by an intelligent sound analyzer. This analyzer can distinguish between general ambient noise and specific distress sounds, such as a baby's cry, by using a predefined sound level threshold (55 dB). In addition to audio-visual monitoring, the unit includes a motion detector to sense activity in the crib and a temperature sensor to monitor the room's climate with high accuracy. All collected data—video, audio, and sensor readings—are then wirelessly transmitted.

The **Parent Unit** is a portable handheld device that receives the data from the Baby Unit. It features a built-in display screen for viewing the live video feed and a speaker for listening to the audio from the baby's room. Its core function is to alert the caregiver. When the Baby Unit detects a significant event like crying, motion, or an unsafe temperature, it sends an alert signal. The Parent Unit receives this signal and notifies the caregiver through a flexible alert system that can use a combination of visual lights, audible alarms, and physical vibrations.

The system is designed to operate in several states, including an active monitoring mode, a low-power state to conserve battery, and an idle/standby mode. Its operational logic is built around continuous monitoring, deciding whether to simply stream normal data or to trigger a specific alert based on inputs from its various sensors.
