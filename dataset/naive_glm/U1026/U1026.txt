This model describes a comprehensive management system for a large-scale technical event, the Design Automation Conference. This system orchestrates the entire lifecycle of the conference, from the initial submission of research papers to the execution of the event and professional networking among attendees.

The system is composed of several integrated components that work together:

A **Submission Management System** is the first point of contact for researchers. It accepts academic paper submissions, validates their format, and tracks their status throughout the process. It operates based on strict deadlines and calculates key metrics like the conference's overall acceptance rate.

Once papers are submitted, a **Peer Review System** manages the critical evaluation process. It maintains a large pool of qualified reviewers and automatically assigns a set of papers to each one based on their expertise. Typically, each paper is reviewed by three experts. The system collects their scores, calculates a final score, and determines whether a paper should be accepted, rejected, or sent for additional review.

For all the accepted papers, a **Program Scheduling System** is responsible for creating the full conference agenda. It intelligently groups papers into related technical tracks and organizes them into specific sessions. These sessions are then allocated to time slots and physical rooms, creating a multi-day program with several parallel tracks running simultaneously.

An **Attendee Registration System** handles all aspects of conference attendance. It allows participants to register under different tiers, such as student, academic, or industry professional, and processes their sign-ups. The system manages the venue's maximum capacity, placing attendees on a waitlist if the event is full.

The system also models the physical **Venue Management System**, which includes facilities like a main auditorium for keynotes, several smaller breakout rooms for technical sessions, an exhibition hall for sponsors, and a dedicated area for poster presentations. This component ensures that the scheduled sessions are assigned to rooms with appropriate capacity and equipment.

Finally, a modern **Networking Platform** is integrated to enhance the attendee experience. It uses algorithms to suggest professional connections between participants based on shared interests and facilitates the scheduling of networking events throughout the conference.
