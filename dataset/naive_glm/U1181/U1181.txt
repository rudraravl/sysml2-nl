This system is an advanced robotic manipulator arm designed to operate autonomously while adhering to a built-in ethical framework. It is specifically engineered to work safely in environments that may include humans or delicate objects, making decisions based not just on efficiency but also on principles like safety and respect for human autonomy.

Physically, the system is a six-axis robotic arm with a maximum reach of 1.5 meters and a payload capacity of 10 kilograms. It is equipped with a gripper for manipulating objects. To perceive its surroundings, the robot is outfitted with a comprehensive sensor suite. This includes four proximity sensors to detect objects and people within a 3-meter radius, a high-resolution vision system for visual analysis, and a force-torque sensor in its end-effector to measure the physical forces it exerts.

The defining feature of this robot is its integrated "Ethical Reasoning Unit." Before executing any command, the system performs a comprehensive ethical evaluation. It analyzes the proposed task and its real-time environmental context, gathered from its sensors. This evaluation assesses the potential for harm (considering factors like proximity to humans and the required force), the potential benefit of the task, and whether the action respects human autonomy. Based on core principles like "do no harm" and "respect for autonomy," the engine generates a decision: approve the action, deny it, or flag it for human review. This decision is then passed to the main control system.

In operation, when the robot receives a task, it doesn't move immediately. First, its ethical engine assesses the situation. If the task is deemed safe and ethically sound (e.g., no humans are nearby and the forces involved are low), it is automatically approved. The control system then calculates the precise movements for the arm and executes the task. However, if the evaluation identifies a potential risk—such as a person entering the workspace—the system will either deny the task or pause and request confirmation from a human operator, ensuring that a human is always in the loop for complex or potentially hazardous situations. This creates a collaborative system where the robot can handle routine tasks independently but defers to human judgment when ethical questions arise.
