This model describes a robotic companion system designed for autonomously performing various tasks like delivery, inspection, and surveillance. The system is composed of a central command unit, a mobile robot, and a dedicated charging station.

The core of the system is the **Companion Robot**, a mobile unit equipped with multiple sensors for navigation and obstacle detection, actuators for movement, and an onboard battery for power. It has its own controller to manage its operations and execute tasks.

The **Main Controller** acts as the command center. It receives high-level task commands—specifying the task type, priority, and target location—and delegates them to the companion robot. After the robot completes its mission, it reports the results, including its success and execution time, back to the Main Controller.

A typical operation involves the robot receiving a command, such as "perform an inspection at location X." Before starting, the robot first checks its own battery level. If the charge is too low (below 20%), it will abort the mission to ensure it can return safely. If the battery is sufficient, it navigates to the target location. Once there, it performs the specific action required, such as delivering an item or completing an inspection. The robot constantly uses its sensors to monitor its position, battery status, and detect any potential obstacles.

The system also includes a **Charging Station**, which the robot uses to recharge its battery between tasks, ensuring it remains operational. The overall design emphasizes autonomous operation, with built-in requirements for successful task execution and intelligent battery management.
