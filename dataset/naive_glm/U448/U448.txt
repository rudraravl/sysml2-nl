This model describes a Wangensteen Suction System, a medical apparatus used for gastric decompression. Its primary purpose is to safely and effectively drain fluid and air from a patient's stomach and intestines, typically after surgery or in cases of intestinal blockage. This process helps prevent abdominal distension, reduces nausea, and promotes healing.

The system is composed of several key components that work together to create a controlled vacuum. At the patient end is a flexible **Nasogastric Tube**, which is inserted through the nose down into the stomach. This tube serves as the primary conduit for drainage.

The tube connects to a large, graduated **Collection Bottle**, where all the withdrawn gastric contents are collected and can be accurately measured. From the collection bottle, the suction path continues to a **Water Seal Chamber**. This crucial component acts as a one-way valve, using a column of water to prevent air from being drawn back into the patient, while also providing a visual confirmation that the system is working correctly.

The negative pressure itself is generated by a **Vacuum Pump**. The intensity of this suction is carefully managed by a **Suction Regulator**, which maintains the pressure within a safe and effective therapeutic range. A visible **Pressure Gauge** constantly displays the current suction level, allowing medical staff to monitor the system's performance. As a critical safety feature, an automatic **Safety Valve** is included to immediately release the vacuum if the pressure becomes dangerously high, thus protecting the patient's delicate tissues from harm.

The entire apparatus is managed by a **Control Unit**. This unit allows clinicians to set the desired pressure and, most importantly, to choose the mode of operation. The system can run in a **continuous** suction mode or an **intermittent** mode, which cycles between active suction and rest periods according to pre-set timers. The system operates through various states, including an initial **priming** phase, a main **operating** state, and an **alarm** state that activates if the collection bottle is full or if pressure levels are outside the safe range.
