The car is equipped with four wheels, each with a specified radius.

Functionally, the automobile is designed to carry up to five passengers in its passenger compartment and up to 500 kilograms of goods in a separate cargo compartment. Its primary mission is to transport this payload from a starting point to a destination.

The vehicle's operation is managed by an integrated control and navigation system, suggesting it has semi-autonomous or autonomous driving capabilities. A navigation system tracks the vehicle's current position and destination, while a control system uses this information to manage speed and direction by sending throttle, brake, and steering commands.

Operationally, the vehicle moves through several states, including being parked, starting up, accelerating, cruising at a target speed, and decelerating to a stop. The entire transport process is defined as a sequence of actions: loading passengers and cargo, planning a route, traveling to the destination while continuously updating its position and consuming fuel, and finally unloading upon successful arrival.
