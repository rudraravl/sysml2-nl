This model describes an industrial pumping subsystem designed for transporting high-density slurries, which are thick, abrasive liquid mixtures containing a high concentration of solid particles (35-70% solids content).

The system is configured to move this slurry from a main **Supply Tank** to a separate **Discharge Tank**. At the core of the system is a specialized **High-Density Solids Pump**, which is a positive displacement type designed with wear-resistant components to withstand the abrasive material it handles.

The slurry's journey begins in the supply tank and flows through a series of connected components: a suction pipe, a control valve, and then into the pump's suction port. The pump pressurizes the slurry and pushes it out through its discharge port into another pipe, which is also fitted with a control valve. Finally, a delivery pipe carries the slurry to its destination at the discharge tank.

To ensure proper and safe operation, the system is equipped with several instruments. **Pressure sensors** are installed at both the inlet and outlet of the pump to monitor its performance and prevent over-pressurization. A **flow sensor** on the discharge side measures the rate at which the slurry is being moved.

The entire operation is managed through a series of states, including stopped, starting, operating, and faulted. A dedicated monitoring function continuously assesses the pump's health by tracking its vibration, temperature, and operating pressures, allowing it to detect degraded performance or potential failures. The system is engineered to meet strict performance requirements, such as maintaining a minimum flow rate and achieving a high discharge pressure, while also adhering to critical safety constraints.
