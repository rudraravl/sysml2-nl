This model describes an automated cash handling system, similar to a sophisticated ATM or a cash recycler used in banks and large retail environments. Its primary purpose is to securely and accurately manage physical currency transactions like deposits and withdrawals without manual intervention.

The system is built from several key hardware components that work together. For deposits, a user inserts cash into a `Bill Acceptor` and a `Coin Acceptor`. This currency is then immediately processed by an internal `Currency Validator`, which scans the bills and weighs the coins to verify their authenticity and denomination. Any counterfeit or unrecognizable currency is rejected and returned to the user.

All validated cash is transported and stored in a `SecureVault`, which is a tamper-resistant safe that keeps a running total of its contents.

For withdrawals, a central `TransactionController` acts as the system's brain. It communicates with an external banking network to authorize the transaction and verify account details. Once approved, the controller instructs the `SecureVault` to prepare the requested amount of cash. This cash batch is then passed to a `CashDispenser`, which presents the bills and coins to the user.

Throughout the process, a `User Display` provides instructions and feedback to the customer. After each transaction is completed, a `Receipt Printer` generates a physical record of the transaction. The `TransactionController` also logs every action, ensuring a complete and auditable history of all operations. The entire system is designed for high accuracy, security, and reliability in processing cash.
