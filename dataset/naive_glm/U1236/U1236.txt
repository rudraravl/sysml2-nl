This model describes a complete telerobotic system that allows a human operator to control a robotic arm in a remote or hazardous environment. The system's primary function is to provide the operator with the ability to perform complex manipulation tasks from a safe distance by mirroring their actions and providing rich sensory feedback.

The system is composed of three main parts: an Operator Station, a Remote Robot, and a Communication System that links them.

The **Operator Station** serves as the human-machine interface. It includes a display screen to show a live video feed from the robot's perspective and an advanced, six-degree-of-freedom input device. This controller allows the operator to intuitively guide the robot's arm and gripper. Crucially, the device provides force feedback, enabling the operator to "feel" the forces the robot encounters, such as when it makes contact with or grasps an object.

The **Remote Robot** is the physical machine performing the work. It consists of a sophisticated six-jointed **robotic manipulator arm**, which provides a high degree of dexterity similar to a human arm. At the end of the arm is a **gripper** capable of opening, closing, and securely holding objects. This gripper is equipped with force sensors to detect when it has made a successful grasp and to measure the force being applied.

To operate effectively, the robot is equipped with an extensive sensor suite. This includes a **vision system** with a depth-sensing camera that streams high-resolution video back to the operator. A **sensor array** provides detailed feedback on the arm's status, measuring the forces and torques at its end-effector, its precise position and orientation in space, and even proximity to nearby objects. A central **robot controller** acts as the robot's onboard brain, translating commands from the operator into precise, smooth trajectories for the arm's joints and continuously monitoring for safety violations that could trigger an emergency stop.

The **Communication System** is the critical link that connects the operator and the robot. It is a robust, encrypted, bidirectional channel that transmits the operator's commands to the robot (uplink) and sends the rich stream of sensor data and live video back to the operator (downlink). This system is designed to be resilient; it can monitor its own connection status and, if the signal weakens, it can automatically reduce the data rate to maintain a stable link. If the connection is lost entirely, it will attempt to re-establish it.
