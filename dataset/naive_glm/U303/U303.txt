This model describes a Medrad Contrast Injection System, a sophisticated medical device used during diagnostic imaging procedures like CT scans, MRIs, and angiography. Its primary function is to inject radiographic contrast media, and often saline, into a patient's bloodstream with high precision to enhance the quality and clarity of the medical images.

The system is built around a dual-head injector, which holds two separate syringes: one for the contrast agent and one for saline. Each syringe is housed within a reinforced pressure jacket to safely handle the high pressures generated during injection. These syringes are driven by individual motor-powered injector heads that precisely control the movement of the syringe plungers, allowing for exact flow rates and volumes to be delivered.

An operator, typically a radiology technologist, interacts with the system through an operator console. This console allows them to program complex, multi-phase injection protocols, defining specific flow rates, volumes, and durations for different stages of the injection. The console also provides a real-time display of the system's status and critical parameters like pressure, flow rate, and the volume of fluid delivered.

Safety is a critical aspect of the system's design. A suite of integrated sensors continuously monitors the fluid pressure, flow rate, and injected volume. This data is fed into a dedicated safety interlock system which constantly compares the real-time values against pre-set safety limits. If any parameter exceeds its limit, the system can automatically trigger an alarm, pause, or perform an emergency stop of the injection to ensure patient safety.

The system operates through a well-defined sequence of states. It starts in a `powerOff` or `idle` state. An operator then loads the contrast and saline syringes and initiates a priming sequence to purge all air from the fluid lines. Before starting, the system is "armed," performing final safety checks. During the `injecting` state, the system executes the programmed protocol, which can be paused and resumed if needed. Upon successful delivery, it enters a `completed` state and logs all injection data. It also has specific states to handle faults or emergency stop conditions, ensuring predictable and safe behavior at all times.
