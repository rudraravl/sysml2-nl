This model describes a comprehensive robotic assistance system designed to support individuals with disabilities in a home or care environment. The system's central component is a sophisticated, autonomous mobile robot built to perform a variety of helpful tasks.

The robot moves around on a four-wheeled mobility base and is equipped with a versatile six-jointed manipulator arm. This arm ends in a gripper capable of securely grasping and carrying objects weighing up to 5 kilograms, such as a bottle of water or a medication container.

For navigation and environmental awareness, the robot relies on an advanced sensor suite. A LiDAR unit provides 360-degree awareness for detecting obstacles and mapping its surroundings, while an IMU helps with balance and orientation. A vision system, consisting of two high-definition cameras and a depth sensor, allows the robot to recognize objects and people.

A user can request assistance through a dedicated interface. The robot is programmed to handle several types of tasks, including retrieving specific objects, delivering medication, providing basic mobility support, or even controlling smart home devices.

The robot operates through a clear set of states. When a task is requested, it transitions from an `idle` state to `navigating` towards the target location. Once it arrives, it enters an `executing` state to perform the action, such as using its arm to `PerformGrasp`. After the task is finished, it returns to its base station. The system is powered by a long-lasting lithium-ion battery designed for over six hours of continuous use. When its power runs low, the robot will automatically navigate back to its charging station to recharge, ensuring it is ready for the next request.
