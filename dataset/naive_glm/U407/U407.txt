This model describes a sterile surgical lubricant delivery system designed for use in operating rooms. Its primary purpose is to automatically apply a specialized, biocompatible lubricant onto surgical instruments and tissue surfaces during procedures. This reduces friction, which helps minimize trauma to the patient's tissues and allows for smoother, more precise manipulation of instruments by the surgeon.

The system is composed of several key components that work in sequence. It uses a disposable, pre-filled lubricant cartridge that contains the sterile, water-soluble lubricating gel. A precision pump mechanism draws the lubricant from the cartridge and pushes it through the system. The flow is managed by an electronic flow controller, which ensures that the lubricant is dispensed at a precise and stable rate as commanded by the operator or a pre-set program. Finally, the lubricant is applied to the target area—either an instrument or a tissue surface—through a specialized application nozzle with a tapered tip for accurate delivery.

Operationally, the system is designed for controlled use during a surgical procedure, such as a laparoscopy. Before use, it performs a priming action to ensure the lines are full and ready for immediate dispensing. During the operation, the system can actively monitor the lubricant's properties, such as its viscosity, and even adjust its temperature to maintain optimal performance. The system continuously evaluates its effectiveness by measuring the reduction in surface friction, ensuring it meets critical performance requirements for patient safety and procedural efficiency. The lubricant itself is a high-performance medical gel, characterized by its sterility, biocompatibility, specific viscosity at body temperature, and water-soluble nature for easy cleaning after the surgery is complete.
