This is an automated Burn Recovery Bed System designed to provide a controlled therapeutic environment for patients with severe burn injuries. Its primary purpose is to accelerate healing and prevent common complications like pressure ulcers and infections by continuously managing the patient's immediate micro-environment.

The system is built around several integrated subsystems that work together, orchestrated by a central Control Unit. These subsystems include:

*   A **Temperature Control System** that maintains the bed's surface at a precise temperature, using integrated sensors and heating/cooling elements to create optimal conditions for skin recovery.
*   A **Pressure Relief System** that constantly monitors and adjusts the pressure between the patient's body and the bed surface. It can redistribute pressure automatically to prevent the development of pressure sores, a critical concern for immobile patients.
*   An automated **Positioning System** that can slowly and gently rotate or reposition the patient at programmed intervals. This is crucial for relieving pressure on different parts of the body over time without requiring manual intervention.
*   A **Humidity Control System** that regulates the moisture level of the air immediately surrounding the patient to prevent skin from drying out and to reduce the risk of infection.

A comprehensive **Monitoring System** acts as the bed's sensory nervous system, collecting continuous readings for temperature, pressure, humidity, and patient position from a suite of sensors. This data is fed to the **Control Unit**, the brain of the system. The Control Unit analyzes the incoming data, compares it to the prescribed therapeutic setpoints, and sends commands to the various control systems to make real-time adjustments.

The system's operation is intelligent and responsive. During normal operation, it makes minor, continuous adjustments to maintain the ideal environment. If it detects a potential issue, such as pressure building up in one area for too long, it can automatically trigger a repositioning cycle. In the event of a critical deviation from safe parameters, it will activate both audible and visual alarms to alert medical staff immediately, while ensuring life-support functions are maintained. The entire system is designed with safety in mind, featuring power reliability through a battery backup and controlled, gentle motion to ensure patient comfort and prevent further injury.
