This is a fuel level sensing system designed for vehicles, which accurately measures the quantity of fuel in a tank using a capacitive sensing method. The system is a complete unit, integrating the physical sensor probe, the electronics to process the signal, and the interfaces to communicate with the vehicle's main computer.

The core of the system is a capacitive sensing element, a probe that is installed inside the fuel tank. This probe works on the principle that its electrical capacitance changes depending on how much of it is submerged in fuel versus exposed to air. Because fuel (like gasoline or diesel) has different electrical properties than air, the system can determine the fuel level by precisely measuring this change in capacitance.

The raw capacitance measurement from the probe is sent to a signal conditioning circuit. This electronic component converts the small, sensitive capacitance reading into a stable and proportional analog voltage signal. This voltage signal is then digitized by an Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC). Finally, a signal processor takes this digital value, applies filtering to smooth out fluctuations caused by fuel sloshing, and uses calibration data to calculate the final, accurate fuel level as a percentage.

The entire unit operates through a series of states. When the vehicle is turned on, the system powers up, performs a self-test to ensure all components are working correctly, and then enters a standby mode. When a measurement is needed, it actively measures the fuel level, processes the data, and transmits the result. The system also supports a dedicated calibration mode to ensure accuracy and will enter a fault mode if any issues are detected, logging error codes for diagnostics.

Designed for automotive use, the system is required to be highly accurate (within ±1.5%), responsive (updating at least 10 times per second), and reliable over a long service life (at least 10,000 hours). It operates on a standard 12V vehicle power supply with low power consumption. The unit communicates the final fuel level percentage, the calculated fuel volume, and its operational status to the vehicle's dashboard or engine control unit through a digital output port. A separate diagnostic port allows technicians to access detailed status information and error codes.
