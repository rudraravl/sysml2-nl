This model describes a personal biometric monitoring system, consisting of a wearable device and a central monitoring hub. The system is designed for continuous, real-time health tracking and alerting.

The core of the system is the **wearable device**, which is powered by a rechargeable battery pack. This device is equipped with a suite of sensors that constantly measure the user's key vital signs: a heart rate sensor, a body temperature sensor, a blood oxygen sensor, and an activity monitor.

An onboard data processor collects the raw measurements from these sensors. It aggregates them into a single, time-stamped data packet that includes a unique ID for the device. The processor also analyzes this data in real-time to detect anomalies, such as a heart rate falling outside of a safe range. If an anomaly is found, it generates an alert. The user can view their current biometric data and any active alerts on a small integrated display.

The wearable device uses a wireless transceiver to continuously transmit these health data packets to a **central monitoring hub**. The hub, which could be a dedicated base station or a computer application, receives and archives all incoming data and alerts. It provides a central point for a caregiver or user to review current and historical health information, and it prominently displays any emergency alerts sent from the wearable device.

The entire system operates through various states, including normal monitoring, data processing, and wireless transmission. It also includes power-management features, automatically entering a low-power mode when the battery is low and eventually shutting down to protect the hardware. The system is designed for reliability, with requirements for high-accuracy sensors, a stable wireless connection of up to 15 meters, and a battery life of at least 48 hours on a single charge.
