This model describes the Aldgate Pumping Subsystem, a system designed to automatically transfer a fluid, likely water, from a source tank to a destination tank.

The core of the system is the Aldgate Pump, which is composed of a motor and an impeller. When active, this pump draws fluid from the 10,000-liter source tank and moves it to the 15,000-liter destination tank.

To ensure safe and efficient operation, the system is equipped with several sensors. A flow meter measures the rate at which the fluid is being transferred, while two pressure sensors monitor the pressure on both the suction (inlet) and discharge (outlet) sides of the pump.

The system's operation is governed by an automated control process. It continuously monitors the fluid levels in both tanks. The pump will only run if the source tank has a sufficient supply (is above its minimum operating level) and the destination tank has enough available space (is below its maximum capacity). If the source tank level drops too low or the destination tank becomes too full, the system automatically stops the pump to prevent damage or overflow. The system is designed to meet specific performance requirements, including maintaining a minimum flow rate and operating below a maximum pressure limit.
