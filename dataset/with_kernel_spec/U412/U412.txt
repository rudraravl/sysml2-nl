This model describes the TGuard system, an autonomous safety device designed to protect industrial fluid processes from dangerous temperature and pressure conditions. Its primary function is to act as an automated safety guard, continuously monitoring a fluid line and intervening to prevent failures or hazardous events.

The system is built from four main components that work together in a closed loop:

1.  A **Temperature Sensor** and a **Pressure Sensor** are placed in the fluid line to provide constant, real-time measurements of the process conditions.
2.  A **Protection Controller** serves as the system's brain. It receives the live data from both sensors and compares these readings against pre-defined maximum safety limits for temperature and pressure.
3.  A **Control Valve** is installed in the fluid line and can physically adjust the flow rate, ranging from fully open to fully closed.

In operation, the TGuard system functions as a continuous monitoring and response loop. The controller analyzes the incoming sensor data to determine the system's current operational state, which can be `normal`, `elevated`, `critical`, or `emergency`. Based on this state, it intelligently decides on the necessary action for the control valve. For example, in a `normal` state, the valve might remain fully open. If conditions become `elevated` or `critical`, the controller will command the valve to a more restricted or partially closed position to mitigate the rising temperature or pressure.

If the safety limits are severely breached and the system enters an `emergency` state, the controller automatically commands the valve to shut completely, halting the fluid flow to ensure the safety of the equipment and environment. This entire process happens autonomously, without the need for manual intervention.
