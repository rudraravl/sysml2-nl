This model represents a therapeutic Bronchitis Kettle, a specialized medical device designed to generate controlled, medicated vapor for respiratory treatments. Its primary function is to heat water mixed with medicinal additives to a specific therapeutic temperature, producing a consistent flow of steam that can be inhaled by a user to treat conditions like bronchitis.

The device is composed of several key components working in concert. It includes a main **Water Reservoir**, which can hold up to 2.0 liters of water and is filled by the user. This reservoir supplies water to a separate, smaller **Boiling Chamber** where the vaporization process occurs. A dedicated port allows the user to introduce medicinal additives directly into this boiling chamber.

An internal electric **Heating Element** provides the thermal energy to heat the water in the boiler. The entire operation is managed by a central **Controller Unit**. This controller uses a **Temperature Sensor** to precisely monitor the temperature inside the boiling chamber. Based on these readings, it regulates the power supplied to the heating element to maintain the water at an optimal therapeutic temperature (around 95°C / 368K) while preventing overheating. Once the medicated water is vaporized, the resulting therapeutic vapor is directed out through a specialized **Vaporization Nozzle** to the user.

Users can interact with the kettle via a control interface to set the target temperature and the duration of the treatment session, which is typically around 20 minutes. Safety is a critical aspect of the design; the system is equipped with a mechanical **Safety Valve** to release excess pressure from the boiler, and the controller provides automatic shutdown in case of overheating or if the water level runs too low. The device is designed to operate on a standard 230V electrical supply.
