This model describes an industrial slurry pumping system designed to transfer an abrasive mixture of solids and liquids from a source tank to a destination tank.

The core of the system is a heavy-duty, electrically-powered slurry pump, specifically designed to handle fluids with high solid content (up to 45%). The entire process begins at a 50 cubic meter source tank, where the slurry is stored. From this tank, the slurry is drawn through a 15-meter section of hardened steel suction piping. Along this path, a flow meter and a pressure sensor continuously monitor the conditions of the slurry entering the pump.

The slurry pump provides the energy to move the fluid, aiming for a flow rate of around 200 cubic meters per hour and generating enough pressure to overcome system resistance. The pump's operation is managed by a dedicated motor controller, which uses a variable frequency drive to precisely adjust the pump's speed based on external commands. Power for the entire system is supplied by a 480-volt industrial power supply that feeds the motor controller, which in turn powers the pump.

After being pressurized by the pump, the slurry is pushed through a 25-meter section of hardened steel discharge piping towards its final destination. Similar to the suction side, this discharge line is equipped with its own flow meter and pressure sensor to monitor the pump's output. The process concludes as the slurry is deposited into a 75 cubic meter destination tank.

The system operates through several distinct states: it can be stopped, in the process of starting up or shutting down, running normally, or in a faulted state. This fault state is a key safety feature, ensuring the pump shuts down if a problem is detected, and it requires a manual reset before it can operate again. The system is built for high reliability, designed to run continuously for 8000 hours between maintenance periods.
