This model describes a complete medical fetoscopy system, designed for performing minimally invasive diagnostic examinations and therapeutic procedures on a fetus inside the uterus. It provides surgeons with real-time visualization and control over the uterine environment.

The system's core component is the **Fetoscope**, a slender, tube-like instrument that is inserted into the amniotic cavity. It contains an advanced optical system with an objective lens and imaging fibers to capture images, as well as a "working channel" that allows for the passage of other surgical tools.

To illuminate the area, a separate **Light Source** generates high-intensity light, which is channeled through the fetoscope. The visual information captured by the fetoscope's optics is then sent to a **Camera Head** attached to it. This camera, featuring a sensitive sensor, converts the optical image into a digital video stream.

This video stream is then processed by a **Video Processor**, which enhances the image quality and converts the signal for display. The final, high-definition video is shown in real-time on a large **Display Monitor** for the surgical team to view.

A critical safety and functional component is the **Fluid Management Unit**. This unit actively circulates amniotic fluid by using an irrigation pump to gently introduce fluid and a suction pump to remove it, ensuring the surgical field remains clear of debris and maintaining a safe, stable pressure inside the uterus.

The entire system is managed through a central **Control Console**. This is the user interface for the surgeon, allowing them to adjust the light intensity, control camera and video settings, and manage the fluid pumps to optimize the view and maintain safety throughout the procedure. The system is designed to operate in distinct states, from initial standby and preparation/calibration to the active operating phase and a final, safe withdrawal phase.
