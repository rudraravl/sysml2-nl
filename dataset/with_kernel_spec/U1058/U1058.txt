This is a femtotechnology system designed for the controlled transmutation of chemical elements. At its core, the machine can reconfigure the nucleus of an atom by precisely adding or removing individual protons and neutrons, effectively changing one element into another, such as turning lead into gold.

The system is composed of several key components working in a coordinated process. The entire operation takes place within a heavily shielded **Containment Chamber** to ensure safety and prevent radiation leakage. A source material is placed into this chamber to begin the process.

First, a **Nuclear Scanner** analyzes the material to determine its exact atomic composition—specifically, the number of protons and neutrons in its nuclei. This information is sent to the central **Process Controller**, which acts as the brain of the system. The controller compares the current element's structure to the desired target element and creates a step-by-step plan for the transformation.

Once the plan is ready, the controller activates a powerful **FemtoField Generator**. This device creates a highly focused and intense energy field within the chamber. This field is then precisely directed by the **Nucleon Manipulator**, which uses the energy to perform the sub-atomic surgery—either adding or removing the exact number of protons and neutrons required to match the target element's configuration.

The system operates through a clear sequence of states: it begins in an idle mode, moves to scanning the source material, plans the manipulation, executes the nuclear changes, and finally verifies that the new material matches the target specifications before completing the process. The entire device is managed through a central control interface and requires a significant power source to operate its high-energy components.
