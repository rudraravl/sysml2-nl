This model describes an autonomous domestic robotic cleaning system, which consists of a mobile cleaning robot and its dedicated charging station. The robot is designed to perform various household cleaning tasks such as vacuuming, mopping, and dusting on different floor surfaces like hardwood, tile, and carpet.

The robot itself has a modular design composed of four primary components that work together:

*   A **Control Module** acts as the central brain, processing task commands, managing the robot's overall behavior, and sending out status updates.
*   A **Navigation Module** is responsible for all movement. It uses sensors to understand its position, scan the environment for obstacles, plan efficient paths to cleaning areas, and guide the robot back to its base.
*   The **Cleaning Module** contains the physical hardware for the job, such as a vacuum suction unit, motorized brushes, and a mopping system, allowing it to execute the specified cleaning task.
*   A **Power Module** manages the robot's onboard rechargeable battery, constantly monitoring the charge level and controlling the recharging process when docked with the station.

The robot operates through a clear cycle of states. It typically starts in **standby** at its charging station. When given a cleaning command, it transitions to **navigating** to the target area, actively avoiding obstacles it detects. Upon arrival, it begins **performing** its cleaning task. During this process, it continuously monitors its battery. If the task is finished or the battery runs low, it automatically starts **returning** to the charging station. Once successfully docked, it enters a **charging** state until its battery is full, then returns to standby, ready for the next command.
