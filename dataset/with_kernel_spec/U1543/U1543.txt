This model describes Dextre, the Special Purpose Dexterous Manipulator (SPDM), a sophisticated dual-armed robotic system designed for performing complex maintenance and replacement tasks on the exterior of the International Space Station (ISS). Its primary purpose is to handle large components, known as Orbital Replacement Units (ORUs), and specialized tools, reducing the need for risky and time-consuming astronaut spacewalks.

The system is built around a central body that houses its core electronics and a tool storage unit. This central body serves as the mounting point for two large, highly articulated robotic arms, a left and a right arm, which work in tandem. Each arm is approximately 3.5 meters long, has seven joints for maximum flexibility, and is strong enough to handle payloads up to 600 kg in the vacuum of space.

At the end of each arm is an "end effector," which acts as a robotic hand. This gripper is equipped with force and proximity sensors, allowing it to securely grasp objects with a precisely controlled force of up to 300 Newtons. It can either grab onto specially designed points on large components or attach to various maintenance tools.

Dextre's operation is guided by a sophisticated vision system composed of four high-resolution cameras and eight LED lights for illumination. This system allows operators on the ground to survey worksites, track targets, and verify that components are aligned with sub-millimeter precision.

The entire system is controlled by a central computer that can operate in several modes: teleoperated (controlled directly by a human), autonomous (executing pre-programmed sequences), or standby. This computer processes commands from ground control, coordinates the movements of both arms for complex bimanual tasks, and monitors the overall health of the system.

A dedicated power system draws electricity from the space station's main power grid, converting and distributing it to the arms, computer, and vision system. The system also includes a tool storage unit with four bays, effectively a tool belt, allowing Dextre to carry, retrieve, and stow the specialized tools needed for its maintenance missions.
