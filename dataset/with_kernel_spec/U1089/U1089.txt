This model describes a collaborative robotic assembly system designed for a human operator and a robot to work together safely and efficiently in a shared workspace. The system is centered around a sophisticated collaborative robot that performs precise assembly tasks under the supervision and with the approval of a human.

The core of the system is a multi-axis robotic arm, equipped with a gripper for manipulating various assembly parts. This robot operates at a dedicated workstation that includes fixtures for holding the assembly in place and storage for the necessary components.

The system's key feature is its deep integration with a human operator. The operator uses a touchscreen interface to issue commands, such as starting a task, pausing, or approving a completed step. The robot communicates its status back to the operator through visual feedback on the screen, audio alerts, and colored LED status indicators.

Functionally, the system operates in a structured, interactive workflow. An operator initiates an assembly process by giving a 'start' command. The robot's control unit, using its vision system (an RGB-D camera), identifies and locates the required parts at the workstation. Before any movement, a comprehensive safety system, comprised of laser scanners and proximity sensors, continuously monitors the workspace to ensure the human operator is at a safe distance. If the operator gets too close, the robot will pause or enter a safe state.

Once safety is confirmed, the robotic arm executes a step of the assembly plan, such as picking up a part and placing it. After completing a critical step, the system pauses and actively requests approval from the human operator via the touchscreen. The task cannot proceed until the operator visually inspects the work and gives an 'approve' command. If the operator rejects the step, the robot may be instructed to retry it. This ensures human oversight and quality control at key points in the process. The operator always has the ability to issue an emergency stop command, which will immediately halt all robotic motion.

Overall, this is an advanced manufacturing system that blends the strength and precision of a robot with the cognitive judgment and flexibility of a human. It operates through a cycle of distinct states, including idle, planning, executing a task, awaiting human approval, and handling emergency stops, to create a safe and productive collaborative environment.
