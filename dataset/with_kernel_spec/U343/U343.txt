This model describes a medical oxygen concentrator system, a device designed to provide a continuous supply of highly concentrated oxygen for patients requiring respiratory therapy. It operates by extracting oxygen from the surrounding ambient air using a process known as Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA).

The system's operation begins when an **Air Compressor** draws in ambient air. This air is then pressurized and sent to one of two **Molecular Sieve Beds**. These beds are filled with a zeolite material that, under pressure, selectively traps nitrogen molecules while allowing oxygen to pass through.

The system employs two sieve beds that operate in a continuous, alternating cycle to ensure an uninterrupted flow of oxygen. While one bed is actively separating oxygen from the compressed air (the adsorption phase), the other bed is depressurized, releasing the trapped nitrogen as waste exhaust and regenerating itself for the next cycle (the desorption phase).

The enriched oxygen produced by the sieve beds flows into a **Product Tank**, which acts as a small reservoir to buffer the supply and ensure a smooth, consistent output pressure. From the tank, the oxygen passes through a **Flow Controller**, which precisely regulates the final output to a specific flow rate prescribed for the patient, typically measured in liters per minute.

A central **PSA Controller** acts as the brain of the system. It receives data from an **Oxygen Sensor** that continuously monitors the purity, pressure, and flow rate of the delivered oxygen. Based on this feedback, the controller manages the entire process, including the compressor speed and the crucial timing for switching between the two sieve beds, to maintain optimal performance.

Finally, a dedicated **Safety System** constantly monitors the system's vital signs. If the oxygen purity falls below a safe threshold, or if the internal pressure or temperature exceeds operational limits, this system will trigger an alarm to alert the user and can initiate a shutdown to ensure patient safety.
