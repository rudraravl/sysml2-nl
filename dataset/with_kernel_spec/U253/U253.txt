This system is a Hyfrecator, a low-power, high-frequency electrosurgical device designed for minor medical procedures involving superficial tissue. Its primary purpose is to deliver controlled electrical energy to biological tissue for therapeutic effects such as desiccation (drying), fulguration (sparking), cutting, and coagulation (stopping bleeding).

The device is composed of several key components that work together. A central **Power Supply** converts standard AC electrical power into the specialized DC power required by the internal circuitry. This DC power is then fed to an **RF Oscillator**, which is the heart of the system, generating the high-frequency electrical signal used for treatment. The oscillator can modulate and amplify this signal according to the specific operational mode and power level selected by the user.

A **Control Unit** serves as the system's brain, managing its overall state, which includes off, ready, active treatment, and fault conditions. This unit receives commands from the user and ensures the system operates correctly. The high-frequency energy is delivered to the patient through a handheld **Handpiece** fitted with a specialized **Electrode** tip.

For patient safety, a dedicated **Safety Monitor** continuously observes critical parameters like tissue temperature, electrical current, and power output during a procedure. If any of these metrics exceed predefined safety limits, the monitor can automatically trigger a shutdown to prevent tissue damage. The entire system is designed to initialize, wait for confirmed tissue contact from the handpiece, and then deliver a precise, monitored dose of energy to perform the intended surgical task safely and effectively.
