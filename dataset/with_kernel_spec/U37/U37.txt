This describes the Angellift system, a non-surgical cosmetic treatment designed to reduce facial wrinkles, specifically those around the mouth (perioral wrinkles). The system's core component is the Angellift Dermal Strip, a small, flexible, and biocompatible medical-grade silicone strip.

The treatment involves a clinician inserting these thin strips just beneath the surface of the skin in the targeted facial region. Each strip is designed with a specific flexibility and an adhesive quality that allows it to gently grip the tissue from within. Once in place, the strip provides a gentle, continuous mechanical lift to the underlying facial tissue. This physical support smooths the skin from the inside out, diminishing the appearance of wrinkles and creating a more youthful look.

The entire treatment follows a structured clinical procedure. It begins with preparing the patient's skin, followed by the precise insertion of the strips. The system then enters a lifting phase, which is monitored to ensure the patient's tissue responds well and without adverse reactions. The effectiveness of the treatment is evaluated by measuring the reduction in wrinkle depth. The system is intended for long-term cosmetic improvement but is designed to be safely removed if necessary. Key requirements for the system include biocompatibility to prevent tissue reactions, sufficient mechanical force to achieve a noticeable lift, and safety protocols to limit insertion depth and pressure on the skin.
