This is a model of an automated lunar propellant production plant. Its purpose is to perform In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) by extracting resources directly from the Moon to create rocket fuel, specifically liquid oxygen and liquid hydrogen. This capability is crucial for future space missions, as it allows spacecraft to refuel on the Moon instead of carrying all their fuel from Earth.

The system is composed of several key interconnected components that work together in a sequential production line:

1.  **Power System:** The entire plant is powered by a large **Solar Power Array** that generates electricity during the long lunar day. This power is managed by a central **Power Distribution Unit**, which allocates the necessary electricity to each of the other subsystems as they operate.

2.  **Excavation and Extraction:** The process starts with an autonomous **Excavation Robot** that digs into the lunar soil, or regolith, to collect ice-rich material. This regolith is then transported to a **Thermal Extractor**, which heats it to sublimate the water ice into vapor. The vapor is then collected and condensed back into liquid water, while the processed, dry soil is discarded.

3.  **Electrolysis:** The extracted water is then pumped into a **Water Electrolyzer**. This unit runs a strong electrical current through the water, splitting the H₂O molecules into their constituent elements: gaseous oxygen and gaseous hydrogen.

4.  **Liquefaction and Storage:** To be used as propellant, these gases must be turned into dense, cold liquids. The system uses two separate cryogenic coolers: an **Oxygen Liquefier** and a **Hydrogen Liquefier**. These units chill the gases down to extremely low temperatures (−183°C for oxygen, −253°C for hydrogen) until they condense. The final liquid products are then transferred to heavily insulated **Oxygen and Hydrogen Storage Tanks** to be kept at cryogenic temperatures until they are needed for fueling rockets.

The entire production cycle is automated and operates through a series of distinct states. The plant starts in a **Standby** mode, moving through an **Initialization** and self-check phase once sufficient power is available. It then enters the main operational loop, cycling through the states of **Excavation**, **Extraction**, **Electrolysis**, **Liquefaction**, and **Storage**. The system constantly monitors its own progress and the levels in the storage tanks. When the tanks are full, it enters a **Production Complete** state, awaiting the next command. If not, it automatically begins a new excavation cycle to continue production.
