This is a smart, wearable therapeutic jacket designed to monitor and treat pneumonia patients, particularly children. The system's main purpose is to provide automated, continuous care by tracking vital signs and applying therapy to reduce fever and improve breathing.

The jacket is a self-contained, battery-powered device that integrates several key components. It is equipped with a suite of sensors, including multiple temperature sensors to measure the patient's body temperature and expansion sensors that wrap around the chest to track breathing patterns and respiration rate.

The therapeutic functions are delivered by two main systems. First, there are several inflatable pneumatic chambers built into the jacket. These chambers apply gentle, intermittent pressure to the patient's chest to help improve lung function and circulation. Second, a liquid-based cooling module actively circulates temperature-controlled fluid through the jacket to help lower the patient's fever.

A central therapy controller acts as the brain of the system. It continuously receives and analyzes data from the temperature and expansion sensors. If it detects a high fever or an abnormal breathing rate, it automatically sends commands to activate the pneumatic chambers and the cooling module, tailoring the therapy to the patient's real-time needs.

The jacket operates in several distinct modes, including a standby mode, a continuous monitoring mode, and an active therapy mode where both pneumatic compression and cooling can be applied simultaneously. For safety, the system is designed to operate within specific limits, such as maximum pressure in the chambers and minimum temperature for the cooling fluid. If a critical condition is detected or the battery runs low, the controller can generate and send an alert to an external monitoring system or caregiver.
