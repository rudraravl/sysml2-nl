This model represents a complete surgical lighting system designed for operating rooms to provide high-quality, adjustable illumination of the surgical field. Its purpose is to enable precise visualization of anatomical structures during medical procedures while ensuring patient and staff safety.

The system's main component is a light head mounted on a long, multi-jointed articulating arm. This arm allows the light to be easily positioned over the patient with a reach of up to two meters and can be locked into a stable position. A key feature is the sterile, autoclavable handle, which allows surgeons to reposition the light during a procedure without compromising the sterile environment.

Inside the light head, a powerful module containing 60 LEDs generates the light. This light then passes through an optical assembly of lenses that focus it into a precise, adjustable beam. To manage the heat produced by the LEDs, the system has a dedicated cooling fan that is controlled based on feedback from a temperature sensor, ensuring the light head's surface remains at a safe temperature.

The entire system is managed by a central control unit. This unit receives commands to turn the light on or off, adjust its brightness, and define its position. It also allows the system to operate in several pre-set modes, including a high-intensity "Surgical Mode," a broader "Examination Mode," and a specialized "Endoscopy Mode," each with specific settings for brightness, color temperature, and beam diameter. The system is powered by an internal power supply that converts standard AC wall power to the low-voltage DC needed by the LEDs and electronics.
