This model describes a complete medical endoscope system designed for minimally invasive internal examinations and therapeutic procedures. The system allows a clinician to visualize internal body structures in real-time, perform diagnostic evaluations, and carry out treatments.

The core of the system is the **Endoscope** itself, which consists of several integrated components. The primary patient-interface is a long, flexible **Insertion Tube**, approximately 1.2 meters long and 9.5 mm in diameter to minimize patient trauma. At the tip of this tube is a high-definition **Image Sensor** that captures video at 1920x1080 resolution and 60 frames per second. This tip is also highly maneuverable, featuring an **Articulation Section** that can bend up to 210 degrees, allowing the operator to navigate and obtain a comprehensive view of the examination area.

Illumination is provided by a powerful external **Light Source**, which sends light through fiber optic bundles within the insertion tube to light up the internal site. The captured raw video from the sensor is sent to a dedicated **Video Processor** that enhances the image quality in real-time before displaying it.

The operator interacts with the system through a **Control Console**. This console displays the live, enhanced video feed and provides the controls for manipulating the articulating tip of the insertion tube. Additionally, the system includes an **Irrigation System** connected to a "working channel" inside the insertion tube. This allows the operator to deliver a fluid (like water) to wash an area for a clearer view, or apply suction to remove fluids and debris.

In operation, a clinician initializes the system, inserts the tube to the target anatomical site, and navigates using the articulation controls while viewing the live feed. They can then conduct the examination, adjust lighting, wash or clear the area as needed, perform therapeutic interventions, and capture images or video for documentation before safely withdrawing the scope.
