This model represents a complete polygraph system, often referred to as a lie detector, designed to aid an examiner in identifying deceptive behavior. It operates by measuring and analyzing a person's involuntary physiological responses while they answer a structured series of questions.

The system is comprised of several physical sensors that are attached to the subject being examined. These include a cardiovascular sensor (like a blood pressure cuff) to measure heart rate and blood pressure, a respiration sensor (typically a band around the chest) to monitor breathing rate, and a galvanic skin response sensor with electrodes to measure changes in skin conductance, which relates to sweating.

During an examination, the data from these sensors is continuously collected. This raw biological data is first sent to an internal signal processor, which cleans up the signals by filtering out noise and amplifying them for accurate analysis.

The core of the system's analytical capability relies on establishing a physiological baseline. Before the primary questioning begins, the system records the subject's normal, resting state to understand their typical bodily signals. Then, as the examiner asks a sequence of control, relevant, and irrelevant questions managed by a question controller, the deception analyzer component continuously compares the subject's live responses to their established baseline. Significant deviations in heart rate, blood pressure, breathing, or skin conductance following a specific question are flagged as potential deception indicators, which include a calculated stress level and a confidence score.

An examiner interacts with the system through a display console that shows the physiological signals in real-time on graphs and also displays the analytical results and deception indicators as they are generated. Throughout the process, a data recorder logs all sensor data, timestamps, and analysis results, linking them to the specific questions asked.

The system's operation follows a formal procedure, moving through states such as calibration, baseline establishment, the active testing phase, and finally, reporting. Once the questioning is complete, the system compiles all the collected information into a comprehensive examination report, detailing the subject's baseline, their responses, the deception indicators found, and an overall assessment for the examiner to make a final judgment.
