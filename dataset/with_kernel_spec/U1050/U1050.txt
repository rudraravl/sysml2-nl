This model describes a system designed to embody the principle of "ephemeralization"—the process of achieving progressively greater results while using progressively fewer physical resources. It represents not a single, static object, but an entire evolutionary framework for a technology or product line that improves over successive generations.

The system is built around three core functional components that work together to drive this "do more with less" philosophy:

1.  **A Technology Evolver:** This is the innovation engine of the system. It is responsible for creating the next generation of the technology. Its goal is to design new versions that are inherently more capable and less resource-intensive than their predecessors, marking a clear step forward in technological progress.

2.  **A Resource Minimizer:** This component is focused purely on efficiency. It analyzes the material and energy required to operate and works continuously to optimize and reduce that consumption. It ensures that the system is always striving to be leaner and more sustainable.

3.  **A Capability Maximizer:** This unit's function is to amplify the system's output. It takes the core function of the current technology and works to enhance its performance, quality, and capacity, ensuring that the service or result delivered is as powerful as possible.

The system operates in a continuous, self-improving cycle. It begins by optimizing its current resource use. Once significant efficiency is gained, the Technology Evolver is triggered to develop and advance to the next generation. This new, improved version then has its capabilities expanded by the Capability Maximizer before the optimization cycle begins again. This entire process is constantly monitored to ensure that the trend is always moving towards greater output with less input, fulfilling the core requirement of scalable, efficient, and dematerialized progress.
