This model describes an advanced fueling station dispenser designed to deliver custom blends of Hydrogen and Compressed Natural Gas (HCNG) to vehicles. It is a complete, self-contained fueling system that manages the entire process from fuel storage to vehicle delivery, ensuring safety and precision.

The system is built around two primary fuel sources: a high-pressure storage tank for hydrogen and another for compressed natural gas (CNG). A central Control Module acts as the brain of the dispenser, orchestrating the entire fueling operation based on user input and sensor feedback.

A typical fueling sequence begins with a user interacting with a customer-facing User Interface to authenticate payment and select a desired fuel blend, such as pure CNG or various HCNG mixtures with up to 30% hydrogen content. Before any fuel flows, the system performs a series of safety checks. It verifies that the Dispensing Nozzle is securely connected and locked to the vehicle and confirms that system pressures and temperatures are within safe operating limits.

Once authenticated and safety-checked, a specialized Blending Valve precisely mixes hydrogen and CNG from their respective storage tanks to create the selected blend. This mixture then passes through a Compression Module to ensure it reaches the target pressure required for efficiently filling the vehicle's tank.

Throughout the dispensing process, a suite of sensors provides real-time data to the Control Module. A Pressure Sensor, Temperature Sensor, and Flow Meter continuously monitor the fuel's condition, while a Leak Detection System stands guard for any potential gas leaks. If any parameter goes outside the safe range or a leak is detected, a Safety Shutoff Valve will immediately stop the flow of gas and trigger an emergency shutdown. The system also includes a Venting System to safely release pressure if necessary.

The dispenser operates through a series of distinct states, moving from idle to authenticating, preparing the blend, dispensing, and finally completing the transaction. In case of any anomaly, it enters a fault state to ensure safety. The entire system is designed to provide a safe, reliable, and flexible fueling experience for vehicles that run on HCNG.
