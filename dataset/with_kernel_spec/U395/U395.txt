This model represents a high-precision digital stadiometer system, an automated device designed for accurately measuring a person's standing height.

The system consists of a stable base platform on which a person stands, and a tall vertical rail that serves as the measurement spine. A sliding headpiece moves up and down this rail. The core of the system is an electronic control unit that orchestrates the entire measurement process, from detecting a person to displaying the final height.

To perform a measurement, a person stands on the base platform. The system detects their presence and automatically lowers the sliding headpiece from its resting position. When the headpiece makes gentle contact with the top of the person's head, a sensor registers the contact, and the headpiece locks in place. A high-resolution position sensor then reads the exact height from the calibrated rail. This raw measurement is processed by the control unit, which applies any necessary calibration adjustments to ensure accuracy.

The final, precise height measurement is then shown on a digital display. After a few seconds, the headpiece automatically retracts to its initial position, and the system returns to a ready state, prepared for the next measurement. The system continually monitors its own status, ensuring the base is level and its calibration is valid, entering an error state if conditions for an accurate measurement are not met.
