This model describes the MitraClip Transcatheter Mitral Valve Repair System, a minimally invasive medical device designed to treat mitral regurgitation, a condition where the heart's mitral valve leaks. The system allows physicians to repair the valve without performing open-heart surgery.

The core of the system is a small, permanent **Clip Implant**, typically made of a cobalt-chromium alloy. This clip has arms that are designed to grasp the anterior and posterior leaflets of the mitral valve, pulling them together to create a more effective seal and reduce the backward flow of blood.

This implant is delivered to the heart using a sophisticated delivery system, which consists of several key components:
*   A **Guide Sheath** is first inserted into a major vein, usually in the patient's leg, to create a stable pathway into the body's vascular system.
*   A long, flexible **Steerable Delivery Catheter** is then passed through this sheath. This catheter holds the clip at its tip and is designed to be navigated through the blood vessels, across the chambers of the heart, and precisely to the location of the mitral valve.
*   An external **Steering Control** handle allows the physician to manipulate the delivery catheter. It has knobs for bending and rotating the catheter's tip to achieve the correct orientation for positioning the clip on the valve leaflets. The handle also includes controls to command the clip to open, grasp the leaflets, and ultimately be released from the delivery system.

The entire procedure is performed under live imaging guidance, typically an echocardiogram, which allows the physician to see the heart's structures and the device in real-time. The operational sequence involves carefully navigating to the valve, aligning the clip, and then deploying it through a series of states: it starts retracted inside the catheter, is opened to prepare for capture, grasps the leaflets, is closed and locked into its final position, and is finally deployed by being released from the delivery catheter. Once the clip is successfully in place and the reduction in valve leakage is confirmed, the entire delivery system is withdrawn from the patient's body, leaving only the small clip implant behind.
