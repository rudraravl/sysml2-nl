This model describes a precision fluid transfer system designed to move a liquid from a source reservoir to a receiving tank at a precisely controlled and stable volumetric flow rate.

The system's core function is to pump a fluid from a storage reservoir, through a series of pipes, and into a destination receiver. The key feature is its automated control loop that ensures the fluid moves at a specific, user-defined rate.

Its physical components include a source reservoir, a pump that provides the initial pressure to move the fluid, and pipes to carry it. Central to its operation are a controllable flow valve and a flow sensor. The flow controller acts as the system's brain. It receives a target flow rate command and constantly compares this target to the actual flow rate being measured by the sensor in the pipeline.

If the measured flow is too high or too low, the controller automatically adjusts the opening of the flow valve to correct the rate. This creates a continuous feedback loop that maintains the flow with high precision, specifically ensuring it stays within a ±5% tolerance of the target rate. The system is also designed to achieve a stable flow within 30 seconds of receiving a new command, making it both accurate and responsive.
