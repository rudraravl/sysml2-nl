This is a Nail Brace System, an orthopedic medical device designed to correct nail deformities such as ingrown toenails or excessively curved nails. The system works by applying a gentle, controlled mechanical force to gradually reshape the nail plate over a period of time, lifting its edges to reduce curvature and relieve painful pressure on the surrounding skin.

The complete system consists of several key components that work together on the patient's nail. It includes two small **Anchor Hooks** that are carefully attached to the left and right edges of the nail. A flexible **Brace Wire**, often made of a memory alloy like Nickel-Titanium, is stretched between these hooks across the surface of the nail. The tension in this wire is controlled by a **Tension Adjuster**, a mechanism that allows a clinician to set and modify the amount of corrective force being applied.

The treatment process follows a defined lifecycle. After the brace is **installed** on the nail, it enters an **active correction** state, where the wire's tension exerts a constant, gentle lifting force. Periodically, the nail's curvature is measured in a **monitoring** phase to assess progress. If needed, the tension is modified in an **adjusting** phase to ensure the correction is proceeding effectively. This cycle of correction and monitoring continues until the nail reaches its target, flatter shape. Once the **treatment is complete**, the brace is safely **removed**.

The system is designed to be both effective and safe, with requirements to achieve a clinically acceptable nail shape without exceeding force limits that could damage the nail or cause the patient excessive pain.
