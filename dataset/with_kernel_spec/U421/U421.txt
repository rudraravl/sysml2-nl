This model describes a Transdermal Continuous Oxygen Therapy System, a portable medical device designed to deliver supplemental oxygen to a patient directly through their skin. It serves as a non-invasive alternative to traditional methods like nasal cannulas or face masks.

The system is a self-contained unit that generates its own medical-grade oxygen from the surrounding air using an internal **Oxygen Concentrator**. This concentrated oxygen is stored in a small **Reservoir Tank** at a controlled pressure. To ensure safe delivery, the oxygen first passes through a **Pressure Regulator Valve** to lower its pressure, and then through a **Flow Control Module** that precisely manages the rate of delivery. The oxygen is administered to the patient via a specialized **Transdermal Delivery Patch** applied directly to the skin, which facilitates oxygen absorption.

A key feature of this system is its intelligent, closed-loop control. A **Physiological Sensor Array** continuously monitors the patient's blood oxygen saturation and skin temperature in real-time. This information is sent to the central **Therapy Control Unit**, which acts as the system's brain. Based on the sensor feedback, the control unit automatically adjusts the oxygen flow and pressure to maintain the patient's blood oxygen saturation within the desired therapeutic range.

The system is designed with safety as a priority, featuring automatic shutdown mechanisms that activate if skin temperature or delivery pressure exceed safe limits. It operates through several states, including standby, active therapy, and a paused mode, and can enter a fault state if a component fails. The entire device is powered by a rechargeable **Power Supply Module**, enabling it to run continuously for extended therapy sessions.
