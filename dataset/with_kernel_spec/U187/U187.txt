This is a medical compression therapy system designed to treat circulatory conditions in a patient's limbs, such as venous insufficiency or lymphedema.

The core of the system is a therapeutic compression garment, similar to a high-tech compression stocking or sleeve. This garment is constructed from multiple layers of specialized compressive fabric, featuring elastic yarns that provide the necessary tension. For added effectiveness, it includes specific reinforcement zones for targeted support and has a moisture-wicking inner liner to ensure patient comfort and breathability.

The system's primary function is to apply a precise, graduated pressure to an affected arm or leg. This means the pressure is strongest at the farthest point of the limb (like the ankle) and gradually decreases as it moves up towards the body. This pressure gradient is crucial for improving blood flow in the venous system and reducing the fluid buildup that causes swelling (edema).

The therapy process is systematic. It starts by assessing the patient's limb to measure the extent of swelling and determine the appropriate garment size and compression level. Once the garment is fitted correctly, it enters an active compression state, delivering sustained therapeutic pressure. The system is designed to monitor the therapy's progress by tracking changes in the limb's circumference and circulation. Based on this monitoring, the treatment can be adjusted or continued until the therapeutic goals of improved circulation and reduced swelling are met. The garment is designed to be durable and retain its compressive properties through repeated use.
