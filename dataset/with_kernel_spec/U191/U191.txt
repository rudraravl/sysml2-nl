This model describes a comprehensive, multi-agency system for combating counterfeit medical products, designed to implement the Council of Europe's MEDICRIME Convention. It's not a single piece of software, but rather a coordinated national framework for protecting public health from the threat of fake medicines.

The system's main purpose is to receive and process reports about suspicious medical products from the public and healthcare professionals. When a report is submitted through a public portal, a central National Competent Authority (NCA) takes charge. The NCA first validates the report and, if the threat is credible, initiates a formal verification process.

This involves sending the suspect product to a Forensic Laboratory for analysis to determine if it is authentic. Based on the lab's findings and an initial risk assessment, a decision is made. If the product is genuine, the case is closed.

If the product is confirmed as counterfeit, a coordinated, multi-pronged response is launched. A formal Investigation Case is opened and assigned to a Law Enforcement Unit to pursue criminal charges. A legal Seizure Order is issued, authorizing Customs and law enforcement to confiscate the counterfeit goods. Simultaneously, a Public Health Agency assesses the danger to the public and issues a Public Alert, warning citizens about the specific product, the associated risks, and the regions where it has been found.

A crucial aspect of this system is its international dimension. The NCA, through an International Cooperation Hub, notifies partner countries and international bodies about the counterfeit threat, sharing case details to enable a global response. The system is designed to handle this entire process efficiently, with a requirement to process high-urgency reports within two hours while maintaining a complete and secure chain of custody for all evidence. The overall operation flows through distinct stages, from initial monitoring and investigation to active response, international coordination, and final case resolution.
