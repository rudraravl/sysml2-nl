This is a non-surgical medical system designed to correct congenital ear deformities in newborn infants. The system leverages the natural flexibility of a baby's ear cartilage in the first few weeks after birth to gently reshape the ear without surgery.

The core of the system is a custom-fitted Auricular Splint Device. This device consists of a soft, anatomically contoured shell made from biocompatible silicone that is placed over the ear to apply a controlled and continuous molding pressure. This shell is held securely in place by two skin-safe adhesive retention bands, one placed in front of the ear and one behind.

The treatment process begins with an assessment of the infant's specific ear deformity to determine the required corrective pressure and estimated treatment time. Based on this, a splint is customized for the patient. Once applied, the device is worn continuously for several weeks. During this period, the system's progress is monitored, and adjustments can be made if necessary. The entire lifecycle of the treatment is managed, from the initial patient intake and splint application through the active correction phase until the device is finally removed once the desired ear shape is achieved. The system is designed to be both safe and effective, using medical-grade materials and ensuring the applied pressure is gentle enough for the infant's delicate skin.
