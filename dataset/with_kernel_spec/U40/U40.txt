This is a medical Aspiration Therapy System designed for weight management in obesity treatment. Its primary function is to remove a portion of gastric contents from a patient's stomach after a meal, which helps reduce caloric absorption.

The system is composed of several key components working together under the direction of a central Therapy Control Unit. A Vacuum Generation Unit acts as a pump to create a controlled and safe level of suction. This vacuum is applied through a specialized Aspiration Catheter Assembly, which provides the interface to the patient. A Pressure Monitoring Subsystem constantly measures the vacuum levels to ensure they remain within strict, physiologically safe limits to prevent tissue damage.

During a therapy session, the aspirated stomach contents are transferred through the catheter into a dedicated Collection Reservoir for containment and later disposal. A User Interface Module allows a clinician or trained user to configure session parameters—such as the target volume to be removed, the maximum session duration, and the precise vacuum pressure—and to monitor the system's status in real-time.

The system operates through a series of defined states. It begins in a Standby mode, moves to a Preparation phase to ensure all connections are secure, and then enters an Active Aspiration state. The therapy is delivered in cycles, with periods of active suction followed by brief waiting intervals. The control unit continuously performs safety checks, monitoring pressure, reservoir fill level, and operating time. A session concludes when the target volume is reached or the maximum time limit is met, at which point the system deactivates safely. If any safety parameter is breached, the device immediately enters a Safety Hold state and alerts the user.
