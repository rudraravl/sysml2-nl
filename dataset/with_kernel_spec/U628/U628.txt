This is a battery-powered carbon monoxide (CO) detector system designed to protect building occupants by continuously monitoring air quality and providing timely warnings of hazardous CO levels.

The system is built around four main components: an electrochemical sensor, a central processing unit, an audio-visual alarm, and a battery power system. The electrochemical sensor's primary job is to constantly sample the ambient air to measure the concentration of carbon monoxide gas.

This data is sent to a central processing unit, which acts as the system's brain. The processor analyzes the CO concentration readings against established safety standards, which consider both the concentration level and the duration of exposure. This prevents false alarms from brief, harmless spikes while ensuring a rapid response to genuinely dangerous conditions. For example, it will trigger an immediate alarm for very high concentrations but requires a longer, sustained exposure for lower levels to be considered a threat.

If the processor determines a dangerous condition exists, it sends a command to activate a combined audio-visual alarm unit. This unit uses a loud piezoelectric buzzer and a bright LED indicator to alert anyone nearby. The entire system is powered by a 9V battery, which has its own management function to monitor its charge level. When the battery runs low, it alerts the main processor, which then triggers a distinct low-battery warning so the user knows to replace it, ensuring continuous and reliable operation.
