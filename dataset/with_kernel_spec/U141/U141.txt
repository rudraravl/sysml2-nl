This is an intelligent air purification system designed to continuously monitor and clean indoor air. Its primary function is to remove airborne contaminants like particulate matter, allergens, dust, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and odors to maintain a healthy environment.

The system is built around a multi-stage filtration process driven by an adjustable-speed fan. When operating, the fan draws contaminated room air into an intake port. The air first passes through a **Pre-Filter** which captures larger particles like dust and hair. Next, it flows through a high-efficiency **HEPA Filter** that is responsible for removing at least 99.97% of fine particles, such as allergens and pollutants. The final stage is an **Activated Carbon Filter**, which adsorbs and neutralizes gases, VOCs, and odors. The fan then expels the purified air back into the room.

The purifier's intelligence comes from its **Control Unit** and an integrated **Air Quality Sensor**. The sensor continuously samples the incoming air and provides a real-time air quality reading. Based on this data, the control unit automatically adjusts the fan speed in its "Auto Mode": if the air quality is poor, the fan runs at high speed for maximum cleaning power, and as the air becomes cleaner, the fan speed is reduced to a medium or low setting to save energy and reduce noise.

The system operates in several states, including **Off**, a **Standby** mode with a low fan speed, and the main **Auto Mode**. It also includes a crucial **Filter Maintenance** state. The system tracks the operational time of the HEPA filter and calculates its remaining life. When the filter life drops below 10%, the system enters this maintenance mode and alerts the user through its interface that a replacement is needed.

A simple user interface allows a person to turn the device on or off and view the current air quality level and the filter's remaining life percentage. The entire system is powered by a standard electrical connection.
