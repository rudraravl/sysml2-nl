This model describes an autonomous robotic lawn mower system designed to automatically maintain a lawn. The system consists of two main physical components: the mower itself and a dedicated charging dock station.

The robotic mower is a self-propelled vehicle equipped with the necessary components to navigate and cut grass without human intervention. Its core components include a rechargeable battery system that powers a drive system and a cutting deck. The drive system uses two independently controlled wheels, allowing the mower to move forward and make precise turns. The cutting deck contains rotating blades set to a specific height to ensure a uniform grass length across the lawn.

To operate autonomously, the mower is outfitted with a comprehensive sensor array. This includes ultrasonic sensors to detect obstacles from a distance, allowing it to plan avoidance maneuvers. A boundary wire sensor detects a perimeter signal, ensuring the mower stays within the designated lawn area. It also has contact-based bump sensors as a backup and a sensor to measure the height of the grass.

The system's intelligence lies in its navigation and control unit, which manages its operational modes such as mowing, navigating, docking, and obstacle avoidance. The mower's primary function is to execute an autonomous mowing sequence. During this operation, it continuously scans for obstacles and checks its position relative to the boundary. If it detects an obstacle, it will stop, turn, and find a clear path. When it reaches the boundary wire, it turns around to continue mowing within the defined area.

A key feature of the system is its self-sufficiency. The mower constantly monitors its own battery level. When the charge drops below a certain threshold (e.g., 15%), it automatically stops mowing, navigates back to its charging dock, and initiates a recharge cycle. Once fully charged, it can resume its mowing task.
