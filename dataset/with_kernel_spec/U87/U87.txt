This model represents the miraDry medical system, a non-invasive device designed to permanently reduce underarm sweat, odor, and hair. The system works by delivering precisely controlled microwave energy to the specific depth beneath the skin where sweat glands reside, heating and eliminating them.

The system is composed of three primary physical components:

1.  **Console Unit:** This is the main stationary part of the system that acts as the control center. It contains the power supply that generates the microwave energy, a coolant reservoir and pump for the cooling system, and a control processor with a user interface. A medical professional uses this console to set treatment parameters, initiate the procedure, and monitor its progress.

2.  **Handpiece Assembly:** This is the handheld applicator that the operator uses to perform the treatment on the patient. It is connected to the console by a set of cables and hoses. The handpiece contains the microwave antenna that emits the energy, a cooling plate that makes contact with the skin to protect it, and a vacuum chamber. It is also equipped with various sensors to measure skin temperature and ensure proper contact.

3.  **BioTip Applicator:** This is a disposable, single-use tip that attaches to the end of the handpiece. It serves as the sterile interface between the device and the patient's skin. The BioTip has a special ceramic window that allows the microwave energy to pass through efficiently while its surface transfers the cooling effect from the handpiece directly to the skin, ensuring the outer skin layers are protected from the heat.

The treatment process involves a carefully orchestrated sequence of actions. First, the handpiece, with a new BioTip attached, is placed on the treatment area. A vacuum is activated, which gently suctions the skin and underlying tissue, holding it stable and positioning the sweat glands at the optimal depth for treatment. Simultaneously, the cooling system circulates coolant from the console through the handpiece to chill the skin's surface.

Once the skin is stabilized and cooled, the system delivers a controlled dose of microwave energy. This energy passes through the protected outer skin layer and is absorbed by the tissue below, where it generates heat that destroys the sweat glands. Throughout this process, temperature sensors in the handpiece continuously monitor the skin to prevent burns, and the control processor in the console can automatically shut down the energy delivery if temperatures exceed safe limits. After the energy cycle is complete, the vacuum is released, and the operator moves the handpiece to the next adjacent area to repeat the process until the entire underarm is treated. The system operates through distinct states, including initialization, ready, active treatment, and cooling, with built-in safety checks at each stage.
