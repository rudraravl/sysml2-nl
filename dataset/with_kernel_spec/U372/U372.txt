This model represents the SAHRA (Simple Affordable High-quality Respirator for All), a mechanical respirator system designed to provide life-sustaining breathing support for patients with respiratory insufficiency. Its core function is to replace or assist a patient's natural breathing by delivering controlled breaths of oxygen-enriched air.

The system works by taking in ambient room air, compressing it, and then blending it with a supply of pure oxygen to deliver a precise gas mixture. The concentration of oxygen can be adjusted from 21% (normal air) up to 100% based on the patient's needs. This mixture is then delivered to the patient through a breathing circuit, which consists of tubes connecting the machine to the patient.

Its operation is managed by a central controller, which is the brain of the system. This controller orchestrates a precise breathing cycle based on settings from a medical professional, such as the volume of air per breath (tidal volume) and the number of breaths per minute. A set of inspiratory and expiratory valves are controlled to manage the two main phases of breathing: delivering the air (inspiration) and allowing the patient to breathe out (expiration).

For safety, the system is equipped with an extensive monitoring and alarm system. Integrated pressure and flow sensors continuously measure the conditions in the patient's airway. If the pressure becomes too high (posing a risk of lung injury) or a disconnection is detected, a dedicated alarm module will trigger immediate audible and visual alerts. The system operates in different states, including standby, the active ventilation cycle (inspiration and expiration), and a critical alarm state that is entered when safety limits are breached.
