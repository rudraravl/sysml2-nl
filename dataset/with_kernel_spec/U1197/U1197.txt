This model describes an autonomous robotic vacuum cleaner system, designed for automated cleaning of indoor floor surfaces. The system consists of a mobile robot and a stationary charging dock.

The robot itself is a self-contained unit powered by a rechargeable battery. Its primary function is to remove dust and debris using a combination of rotating brushes (a main brush and two side brushes) and a vacuum fan that provides suction. All collected dirt is stored in an internal dustbin of a fixed capacity.

For movement, the robot is equipped with two independently controlled wheel motors, allowing it to drive forward, backward, and execute turns. Its navigation and awareness of the environment are handled by a suite of sensors, including six obstacle sensors that can detect objects within a 15 cm range to avoid collisions, and a dirt sensor to identify areas needing more thorough cleaning.

The robot's operation is governed by dedicated navigation and cleaning controllers. The navigation controller interprets data from the obstacle sensors to intelligently steer the robot around the room. The cleaning controller uses feedback from the dirt sensor to adjust the brush speed and suction power for optimal performance.

The system operates through a complete, autonomous lifecycle. It typically begins in an idle, fully charged state at its dock. When a cleaning cycle starts, it undocks and begins navigating the room. If it detects an obstacle, it changes its path. When its battery level falls below 20%, it automatically stops cleaning, locates its charging dock, and returns to recharge. Once fully charged, it waits for the next cleaning command.
