This system is a smart, wearable microneedle patch for transdermal drug delivery. It is designed to administer therapeutic drugs painlessly and automatically through the skin over a set period, providing a controlled and minimally invasive alternative to traditional injections or pills.

The patch consists of several integrated components. At its core is a **Microneedle Array**, a grid of hundreds of microscopic needles short enough to painlessly penetrate the skin's outermost layer without reaching the nerves. This array creates tiny channels for the drug to enter the body for rapid absorption. An **Adhesive Backing** holds the entire patch securely against the patient's skin for its intended wear duration, which could be up to 24 hours.

The medication is stored in a single-use, non-refillable **Drug Reservoir**. A **Microfluidic Pump** actively draws the drug from this reservoir and pushes it through the microneedles at a precise, controlled rate.

The patch operates as an intelligent, automated system. A central **Flow Controller** acts as the brain, managing the entire delivery process. It receives information from a **Pressure Sensor** to ensure the patch is applied with enough force for the needles to penetrate the skin correctly. Based on a pre-set dose and flow rate, the controller sends commands to the microfluidic pump to start, regulate, and stop the delivery.

The operational lifecycle of the patch begins when it is applied to the skin. After confirming successful skin penetration, the system enters a 'delivering' state, where the pump is activated. The controller continuously monitors the volume of drug delivered until the full prescribed dose is complete. Once finished, the delivery process is safely terminated, and the patch can be removed and disposed of.
