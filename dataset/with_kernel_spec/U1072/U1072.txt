This model describes a fully autonomous agricultural harvesting system, likely a self-driving robot or vehicle, designed to operate in fields with minimal human intervention.

The system's primary function is to identify, travel to, and harvest ripe crops. It is composed of several integrated components. A sophisticated vision system acts as the machine's "eyes," continuously scanning the field to locate crops using GPS coordinates. This vision system can also analyze each detected crop to determine its ripeness level with a high degree of confidence.

Once a suitably ripe crop is identified, the system's navigation controller autonomously plans a path and drives the machine to the crop's location. A robotic harvesting arm, with a specific reach and maximum grip force, is then commanded to position itself, gently grip the crop, and pick it. This process is carefully controlled to avoid damaging the produce.

After being picked, the crop is transferred to an internal conveyor system, which transports it into a large-capacity storage bin on the vehicle. This entire cycle repeats as the system moves through the field, harvesting crops one by one.

The entire operation is managed by a central mission controller that makes high-level decisions, such as which crop to target next and when the harvesting goal (e.g., a specific total weight) has been met. The system is powered by a rechargeable battery pack, designed for long operational periods.

The harvester operates through various states. It has an active autonomous mode for harvesting, a charging mode for when the battery runs low, and an unloading mode for when the storage bin becomes full. It also includes standby, initialization, and fault states to manage its operational readiness and handle any errors.
