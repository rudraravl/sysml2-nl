This model represents a specialized mobile sanitation vehicle, commonly known as a "honeywagon." It is designed to service portable toilets, holding tanks, and temporary sanitation facilities by collecting, transporting, and disposing of liquid waste, as well as providing fresh water for replenishment.

The system is built on a standard truck chassis and drivetrain, which provides mobility and power. Its core components include a large main storage tank for holding collected waste and a separate, smaller reservoir for fresh water. The primary function of waste collection is performed by a powerful vacuum pump system, which is powered by the vehicle's engine. An operator deploys a long, wide suction hose from the vehicle and uses the vacuum system to draw waste into the main storage tank.

The vehicle is managed from an operator control station, which displays critical information like the waste tank level and allows the operator to switch the vehicle between different operational modes. These modes include `idle` when waiting, `collecting` when the pump is active, `transporting` while driving to a disposal site, `discharging` when emptying the tank, and `waterDispensing` for refilling facilities.

After collecting waste, the vehicle transports it to an authorized disposal facility. There, the operator opens a large discharge valve, allowing the waste to be drained from the tank. The system is designed to handle specific capacities and pressures to ensure safe and compliant transport and disposal. Finally, the vehicle can use its onboard fresh water supply to clean and replenish the portable toilets it services before returning to a standby state or proceeding to the next job.
