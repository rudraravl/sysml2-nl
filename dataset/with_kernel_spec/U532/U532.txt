This is an industrial liquid ring pumping system designed to transfer process gas from a source vessel to a discharge receiver. The core of the system is an electrically-driven liquid ring pump, which uses a rotating impeller and a circulating sealing liquid to compress and move the gas.

The system is a complete, closed-loop subsystem that includes all the necessary auxiliary equipment to support the pump's operation. The main process flow involves drawing gas from a source vessel into the pump, where it is compressed and then sent to a discharge receiver.

Crucially, the system features a dedicated circuit to manage the pump's sealing liquid. After exiting the pump, the mixture of compressed gas and warm sealing liquid is sent to a separator. Here, the main process gas is separated and continues to the discharge receiver, while the recovered sealing liquid enters its own conditioning loop. This liquid is first sent through a heat exchanger to be cooled down, and then returned to a storage tank. From this tank, the cooled liquid is supplied back to the pump, ensuring continuous and efficient operation.

The entire subsystem is heavily instrumented with a suite of sensors, including pressure, temperature, flow, and level transmitters, to continuously monitor its performance and safety. The pump operates through various states, including stopped, starting, priming, and running. It also has built-in protective states to handle adverse conditions like high temperature or cavitation, and includes an emergency shutdown capability to ensure operational safety.
