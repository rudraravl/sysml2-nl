This model describes an integrated and automated security system for a property, such as a home or commercial building. Its purpose is to provide comprehensive protection through surveillance, intrusion detection, and a graduated, automated response to threats.

The system is built around a central controller that acts as the brain, processing information from a variety of components. A network of sensors, including motion detectors and sensors on doors and windows, continuously monitors the premises for any unauthorized activity. These are complemented by multiple cameras placed at key locations (e.g., front, rear, and interior) that provide constant video surveillance.

Users interact with the system through a control panel to arm or disarm it. When armed, the system enters a monitoring state. If a sensor is triggered, the central controller immediately begins to analyze the situation, using data from the sensors and corresponding video feeds to assess the severity and confidence of the threat.

The system's response is tailored to the assessed threat level. For a minor event, it may simply log the incident and record video evidence. For a more serious threat, it will activate both interior and exterior sirens and send alert notifications to designated recipients, such as the owner or a security company. In the event of a critical threat, the system executes its full range of countermeasures: it sounds the alarms, automatically engages all electronic door locks to secure the premises, records video evidence, and immediately notifies the authorities.

The entire process, from detection and analysis to response, is designed to be very rapid. The system operates in clear states, transitioning from a passive "disarmed" state to an active "armed" and "monitoring" state. When a threat is detected, it enters an "alert" state where it assesses the situation and executes its response protocol before returning to monitoring.
