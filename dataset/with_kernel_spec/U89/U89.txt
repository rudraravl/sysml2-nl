This model represents an N95 respirator, a personal protective device designed to protect the wearer from inhaling hazardous airborne particles. Its primary function is to filter contaminated air before it reaches the user's respiratory system while ensuring a secure fit to the face.

The respirator is constructed with a multi-layer filtration system. Air first passes through an outer mechanical filter layer, then through a primary electrostatic filter layer, and finally through an inner mechanical layer. The core of the system is the electrostatic layer, which uses an electrical charge to capture at least 95% of very small particles (down to 0.3 micrometers). The mechanical layers provide structural support and pre-filter larger particles.

To be effective, the respirator must form a tight seal against the wearer's face. This is achieved by a facial seal assembly, which has distinct sections for the nose, cheeks, and chin. An adjustable metal nose clip allows the user to conform the mask to their facial contours. The entire device is held firmly in place by a retention strap system, typically two elastic headbands, which apply a specific tension force.

The system also includes an exhalation valve. This is a one-way valve designed to make breathing out easier by opening when the user exhales, reducing heat and moisture buildup inside the mask. It automatically closes when the user inhales to ensure all incoming air passes through the filter media. The respirator's overall performance is defined by its ability to filter particles efficiently while maintaining low resistance to both inhalation and exhalation, making it breathable for the user.
