This is a comprehensive Medical Equipment Management System designed for a healthcare facility to track, maintain, and allocate critical medical devices. The system manages a fleet of equipment, including 15 ventilators, 25 infusion pumps, and 20 patient monitors, ensuring they are safe, operational, and available for patient care.

At its core, the system features a central **Inventory Database** that maintains a complete, real-time record of every piece of equipment. This includes its unique ID, current location, operational status (e.g., available, in use, under maintenance), usage history, and maintenance records.

The system's key functions are handled by several integrated components:

*   A **Location Tracker** monitors the physical position of each device within the facility, enabling staff to quickly find needed equipment and preventing loss.
*   An **Allocation Manager** handles requests from clinical staff. When a device is needed for a patient, this manager finds the nearest available unit, assigns it, and coordinates its delivery.
*   The **Maintenance Scheduler** and **Calibration Manager** proactively manage the health of the equipment. Based on usage data (like operating hours) or time-based schedules, these components automatically generate work orders for preventive maintenance and calibration, assign tasks to technicians, and track them to completion. This ensures the devices remain compliant with safety standards and function accurately.
*   A **Utilization Analyzer** continuously collects usage data to generate reports on how efficiently the equipment is being used. This helps hospital administration make informed decisions about purchasing new devices or reallocating existing ones.
*   An integrated **Alert System** acts as a watchdog, monitoring all equipment for critical issues. It automatically sends notifications to relevant staff for events like equipment malfunctions, low battery levels on an infusion pump, overdue maintenance, or the unavailability of a requested device.

Overall, this system automates the entire lifecycle of medical equipment management, from initial registration in the inventory to ongoing monitoring, allocation for patient use, routine maintenance, and eventual retirement, all while ensuring traceability and compliance with regulatory requirements.
