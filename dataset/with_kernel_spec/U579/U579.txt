This is an automated water pumping system designed to transfer water from a source reservoir to a destination tank. Its main purpose is to maintain the water level in the destination tank within specific low and high limits, ensuring a reliable water supply while preventing overflows.

The system is composed of several key components that work together. A **Source Reservoir** acts as the water supply, and a **Destination Tank** is where the water is stored and its level is managed.

A powerful **Water Pump** is responsible for transferring the water. This pump doesn't just turn on and off; it transitions through distinct operating states, including starting, running, and stopping, to manage the flow of water from the reservoir's outlet to the tank's inlet.

To monitor the water level, a **Level Sensor** is installed in the destination tank. It continuously measures the water level with a defined accuracy and sends this data to the central control unit.

The "brain" of the system is the **Pump Controller**. This controller receives the real-time water level readings from the sensor. It is configured with low and high setpoints that correspond to the desired water level thresholds in the tank. Based on these readings, the controller makes decisions: if the water level drops below the low setpoint, it sends a command to activate the pump. Once the pump has filled the tank to the high setpoint, the controller sends another command to shut it off. This creates a fully automated and self-regulating control cycle.
