This is a smart environment control system designed to autonomously manage the climate and lighting within an indoor space, such as a room or an office. Its main purpose is to optimize occupant comfort while minimizing energy consumption.

The system is built around a central controller that acts as its brain. This controller is connected to a suite of sensors that constantly monitor the environment: a temperature sensor, a humidity sensor, a light sensor to measure ambient brightness, and a motion sensor to detect whether the space is occupied or vacant.

Based on the data gathered from these sensors, the controller sends commands to two main hardware units: an HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) system and a lighting system. The HVAC unit can regulate the temperature, while the lighting unit can adjust its brightness to several predefined levels (off, low, medium, high, and full).

The system operates in different user-selectable modes, such as 'Comfort', 'Eco', 'Away', 'Sleep', and 'Manual'. Each mode has its own set of rules for temperature and lighting. For example, 'Comfort' mode will maintain a pleasant temperature (around 22°C), while 'Eco' mode will allow for a wider temperature range to save energy. The 'Away' mode, often triggered automatically by the motion sensor, sets the system to a minimal energy state.

A key feature of this system is its intelligent adaptation. It doesn't just follow a rigid schedule; it reacts to real-time conditions. For instance, it uses the motion sensor to know when people are present and adjusts the environment accordingly. It also considers the amount of natural light available before turning on the artificial lights, further conserving energy. The entire system is designed to operate within a set energy budget, ensuring it remains efficient over time.
