This is an autonomous robotic vacuum cleaning system designed to automatically navigate and clean floor surfaces within a home or office environment. It can operate independently, cleaning designated areas and automatically returning to its charging station when its battery is low or the cleaning task is complete.

The system consists of the robot vacuum itself and a stationary charging station that serves as its home base.

The robot's core components work together to perform its mission:
*   **Control Processor:** This is the central brain of the robot, managing its overall operation and deciding its current mode, such as standby, active cleaning, returning to base, or docked for charging.
*   **Navigation System:** For movement and mapping, the robot is equipped with a comprehensive sensor suite. This includes multiple infrared sensors to detect obstacles and walls, physical bump sensors that register contact, and cliff sensors on its underside to prevent it from falling down stairs. This system allows the robot to navigate spaces, avoid collisions, and clean methodically.
*   **Drive System:** Propulsion is handled by a two-wheel drive system. Two main drive wheels can be controlled independently to steer and maneuver the robot, while a front caster wheel provides stability and balance.
*   **Cleaning Mechanism:** The actual cleaning is performed by a multi-part system. It features a main rotating brush to agitate and lift debris from floors, two smaller side brushes to sweep dust from edges and corners into the vacuum's path, and a powerful vacuum motor that creates suction to collect everything. The system can adapt its cleaning intensity based on the floor type (e.g., hardwood or carpet).
*   **Dust Container:** Collected dirt and debris are stored in an internal dust container. The robot is aware of how full the bin is and can recognize when it needs to be emptied.
*   **Battery Module:** The entire unit is powered by an internal, rechargeable battery. The robot continuously monitors its own charge level and knows when it has enough power to continue cleaning or if it needs to return to its dock.

The **Charging Station** not only charges the robot but also emits an infrared beacon signal. The robot uses this signal to locate and navigate back to the station for precise, autonomous docking when a recharge is needed.

In a typical operation, the robot leaves its dock and enters a cleaning state. It navigates the designated area, switching between normal, intensive (for dirtier spots), and edge-cleaning modes. If the battery runs low, the dustbin becomes full, or the cleaning mission is complete, it will automatically stop cleaning and begin navigating back to its charging station. Once docked, it recharges until it is ready for the next mission. The system also includes an error state to handle situations like getting stuck or a critical system fault.
