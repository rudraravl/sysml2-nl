This model describes a penis extender system, a mechanical device designed for therapeutic or cosmetic enhancement. Its primary function is to apply sustained and controlled mechanical traction to penile tissue, encouraging gradual tissue expansion and growth over extended periods of use.

The system is a physical assembly consisting of several key components that work together. A **Base Ring**, made of certified biocompatible medical-grade silicone, serves as a stable anchor at the base of the user's body. Extending from this base are two parallel **Extension Rods** which are adjustable in length. At the other end of these rods is a **Glans Cradle**, also made from padded, biocompatible material, which securely and comfortably holds the head of the penis.

To operate the device, the user manipulates a **Tension Adjuster** on each rod. This mechanism lengthens the rods, creating a gentle and consistent pulling force (traction) along the shaft. The system includes an integrated **Force Sensor** to continuously monitor this tension, ensuring it remains within safe, physiologically acceptable limits to prevent injury. For user comfort during multi-hour wear sessions, the device incorporates several pieces of **Comfort Padding** at key contact points.

The device operates through a clear lifecycle: it starts in an `idle` state, moves to `fitting` as the user puts it on, then to `tensioning` where the desired extension is set. It then enters an `active wear` state for the duration of the session, after which it is removed and returns to an `idle` state. The entire system is designed with safety, comfort, and gradual, effective application of force as primary requirements.
