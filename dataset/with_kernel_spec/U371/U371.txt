This model describes a single-use medical safety syringe designed to deliver a precise dose of medication while automatically protecting healthcare workers from accidental needlestick injuries.

The system is composed of several key components that work together. It includes a standard **barrel** that holds the medication, a **plunger** that is pushed to administer the dose, and a sharp, sterile **needle** for injection. The defining feature is an integrated **safety shield**, a mechanism designed to cover the needle after use.

In practice, a healthcare worker uses the syringe in a normal manner to inject medication. As the plunger is fully depressed, completing the injection, it automatically and passively triggers the safety shield. This shield extends over the needle and permanently locks into place, securely covering the sharp tip.

The syringe operates through a distinct lifecycle, beginning in a sterile, ready-to-use state. After the medication is delivered and the safety mechanism has been activated, the needle status changes from "exposed" to "shielded" and then "locked." This ensures the syringe cannot be reused and can be safely handled for disposal, fulfilling its primary goal of preventing both needlestick injuries and the potential for cross-contamination.
