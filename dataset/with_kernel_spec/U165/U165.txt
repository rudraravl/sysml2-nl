This model describes a Biophone, an integrated, wearable biomedical communication device designed for comprehensive remote patient monitoring. Its primary purpose is to continuously track a patient's vital signs and transmit this information wirelessly to healthcare providers, while also enabling two-way voice communication.

The device is composed of several key components that work together. A **biomedical sensor array** is the core data-gathering unit, responsible for continuously measuring heart rate, body temperature, blood pressure, and blood oxygen saturation. This raw data is then sent to an internal **signal processing unit**, which filters and analyzes the information in real-time to ensure accuracy and prepare it for transmission.

For communication, the Biophone includes a **wireless communication module**. This module transmits the processed vital signs to a remote monitoring station and also handles bidirectional audio, allowing the patient to speak directly with their healthcare provider through the device. The system is powered by an internal **rechargeable battery**, which is designed to support at least 24 hours of continuous operation. A simple **user control interface** provides the patient with status updates and audio communication capabilities.

The Biophone operates through various states depending on the situation. It can be in an `idle` standby mode, an active `monitoring` and `transmitting` state, or a `communicating` state during a voice call. Critically, if the device detects abnormal vital signs, it automatically enters an `alerting` state, notifying both the patient and the remote provider to enable a rapid medical response. It also features a `low power` mode to conserve energy when the battery is running low.
