This model describes an autonomous mobile service robot designed to perform a variety of tasks like delivery, cleaning, inspection, and general assistance. The robot is a self-contained unit built on a wheeled mobile platform, equipped with a sophisticated manipulator arm and an extensive suite of sensors for navigating and interacting with its environment.

Physically, the robot stands about 1.2 meters tall and weighs 85 kg. Its mobility comes from a platform with two main drive wheels and a steering system, allowing it to move at speeds up to 2 meters per second. Mounted on this base is a highly flexible, six-axis robotic arm that can reach up to 0.8 meters and lift objects weighing as much as 15 kg. The arm is fitted with a gripper capable of securely holding items with a controlled force.

The robot perceives its surroundings through a comprehensive sensor suite. A LIDAR scanner provides a 360-degree view for mapping and long-range obstacle detection (up to 30 meters). A high-resolution front-facing camera aids in navigation and object recognition, while a second camera mounted on the gripper provides a close-up view for precise manipulation tasks like grasping objects. Proximity sensors offer additional safety for close-range interactions.

Operationally, the robot functions autonomously through several states. It begins in an `idle` state, awaiting commands. Upon receiving a task, it enters a `navigating` state, using its sensors to plot a course and move to a target location. If it reaches its destination, it transitions to an `executing` state to perform the specific task with its manipulator arm. Once the job is finished, it returns to `idle`. The robot is intelligent about its power needs; if its battery level drops below 20%, it will automatically pause its work and enter a `charging` state, returning to a docking station until it's sufficiently recharged. An `error` state handles unexpected issues, like a blocked path or a failed task, ensuring safe operation. The entire system is powered by a high-capacity 48-volt battery, which supplies electricity to its controllers, motors, and sensors.
