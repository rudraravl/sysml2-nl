This model describes a Charcot Shower Hydrotherapy System, a specialized therapeutic device designed to deliver contrast water therapy by alternating high-pressure streams of hot and cold water onto a user. The primary purpose of the system is to provide treatments that stimulate circulation, reduce inflammation, and provide pain relief through controlled thermal cycling.

The system is built around several key components that work together to control the water's properties. It takes a standard water supply and splits it into two paths: one leading to a powerful **Water Heater** and the other to a **Water Chiller**. These components create separate, readily available sources of hot and cold water.

The core of the system is a **Therapy Control Unit**, which acts as the brain, orchestrating the entire treatment process. An operator uses this unit to program specific therapy parameters, such as the exact temperatures for the hot and cold phases (e.g., 40°C and 15°C), the duration of each phase, and the total number of cycles for the session.

Once a treatment is started, the control unit commands a **Mixing Valve Assembly**. This valve precisely blends the hot and cold water to achieve the target temperature for the current phase of the therapy. The mixed water then flows through a **Safety Shutoff Valve**, a critical component that automatically cuts the flow if the temperature exceeds a maximum safe limit (e.g., 45°C) to prevent scalding.

After the safety check, the water passes through a **Pressure Regulator** to ensure the stream is delivered at a consistent, effective therapeutic pressure. The final, conditioned water is then expelled through a **Jet Nozzle Array**, which creates multiple high-pressure streams directed at the user.

Throughout the operation, **Temperature and Pressure Sensors** continuously monitor the output water, providing real-time feedback to the control unit to ensure the therapy is delivered accurately and safely. The system operates through distinct states, beginning in **Standby**, moving to **Initializing** when a command is given, and then cycling through **Hot Phase Active** and **Cold Phase Active** states for the prescribed duration and number of cycles. If a fault is detected, it enters an **Emergency Shutdown** state. Once the therapy is finished, it returns to standby.
