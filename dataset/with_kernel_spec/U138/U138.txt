This is a medical device known as a hip abductor wedge system, specifically designed for patients recovering from orthopedic procedures such as total hip replacement surgery. The primary purpose of this system is to position and securely maintain a patient's legs in a separated, or "abducted," position to prevent the new hip joint from dislocating during the healing process.

The system consists of three main physical components: a central wedge, a protective cover, and securing straps.

The core of the device is a triangular foam wedge, engineered with a specific angle (e.g., 25 degrees) to achieve the required leg separation. The foam itself is hypoallergenic and has a defined firmness and density to provide both stable support and patient comfort.

This foam core is enclosed within a protective cover made from a waterproof and antimicrobial material. This cover is crucial for hygiene, preventing contamination and making the device easy to clean, while also providing a safe, non-irritating surface for direct contact with the patient's skin.

To hold the wedge securely in place between the patient's legs, the system uses two adjustable securing straps. These straps wrap around each leg and the wedge, allowing for tension to be customized to fit different patient sizes and to ensure the wedge does not slip, all while maintaining pressure within safe limits to prevent skin damage.

The operation of the system involves placing the wedge between the patient's thighs, securing it with the straps, and adjusting the tension until the desired therapeutic angle is achieved and stable. The system is designed to maintain this position for extended periods, with its state being monitored to ensure the angle remains correct and that pressure on the legs is safe. This entire process ensures the patient's hip remains in the correct orientation, which is critical for a successful recovery.
