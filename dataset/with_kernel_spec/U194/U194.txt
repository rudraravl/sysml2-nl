This is an automated grain sampling system designed to collect representative samples from large, flowing streams of grain, such as in silos, grain elevators, or industrial processing lines. The system's primary purpose is to ensure quality control by obtaining small, accurate samples that reflect the characteristics of a much larger batch.

The core of the system is the **Crosby-Kugler Capsule**, a specialized sampling container that can be opened and closed. This capsule is lowered into the moving grain by a **Winch Assembly**, which consists of a motor, cable, and drum. The winch can precisely deploy the capsule to depths of up to 10 meters, allowing samples to be taken from different points within the grain stream.

The entire process is managed by a **System Controller**. In a typical sampling cycle, the controller commands the winch to lower the capsule to a target depth. Once in position, an inlet valve on the capsule opens, allowing it to fill with the flowing grain for a specific duration. After collecting a sufficient amount (at least 500 grams), the valve closes to seal the sample inside. The winch then retrieves the capsule. Once it's back at its starting position, the capsule empties the collected grain into a **Sample Receptacle** for storage and later analysis.

This automated system is designed to perform these sampling cycles repeatedly and reliably, ensuring that the collected samples are statistically representative of the entire grain batch, with a bias of less than 5%.
