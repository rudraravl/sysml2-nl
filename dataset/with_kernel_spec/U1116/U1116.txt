This model describes an automated laundry folding machine designed to reduce manual labor and improve the consistency of folding clothes. The system is a self-contained unit that processes one garment at a time through a coordinated, multi-step process.

The machine's operation begins when an unfolded laundry item is placed on an input conveyor belt, which transports it into the main processing area. A vision system, acting as the machine's eyes, then scans the garment to identify its category (such as a t-shirt, pants, or towel) and measure its precise dimensions.

This information is fed to a central process controller, the brain of the machine. The controller analyzes the garment's type and size to determine the most effective folding sequence. Based on this plan, it sends commands to two robotic folding arms. These arms work together on a dedicated folding surface to grip, lift, and execute the precise folds required for the specific item. This surface can also adjust its temperature, likely to help smooth out wrinkles during the process.

Once the folding is complete, the neatly folded garment is transferred to an output stacker. This component collects and neatly stacks the finished laundry, with the capacity to hold a significant number of items before needing to be emptied. The entire system is engineered to operate continuously, aiming to fold at least 15 garments per hour with a high degree of precision and quality.
