Create a closed-loop one-degree-of-freedom robot arm. Isaac Sim owns the rigid-body dynamics of a two-kilogram link that is 0.6 meters long with a 0.1 meter square cross-section, and the Modelica FMU owns a PD controller. The controller receives shoulder angle in radians and angular velocity in radians per second and outputs shoulder torque in N.m. Use proportional gain 12 N.m/rad, derivative gain 2 N.m.s/rad, a 30 degree target, and a 5 N.m torque limit. The OpenUSD scene contains a base fixed to the world and the link connected by a Y-axis revolute joint limited from -90 to 90 degrees. Use zero gravity for this integration oracle. Run from 0 to 3 seconds at 100 Hz with two physics substeps per communication step. The arm must stay within its joint limits and finish within 3 degrees of the target.
