Create a closed-loop two-joint pick-and-place arm. Newton Physics owns the rigid-body dynamics and a Modelica FMU owns independent PD controllers for both joints. A fixed base supports a 2 kg box arm that is 0.5 m long, 0.12 m wide, and 0.12 m deep. A 1 kg box slider that is 0.3 m long, 0.1 m wide, and 0.1 m deep attaches to the arm. The shoulder is a Y-axis revolute joint from the base to the arm with limits from -60 to 60 degrees. The extension is an X-axis prismatic joint from the arm to the slider with limits from -0.15 to 0.15 m. The shoulder controller receives position in rad and velocity in rad/s and outputs torque in N.m with proportional gain 10 N.m/rad, derivative gain 1.5 N.m.s/rad, target 20 degrees, and torque limit 4 N.m. The extension controller receives position in m and velocity in m/s and outputs force in N with proportional gain 50 N/m, derivative gain 8 N.s/m, target 0.08 m, and force limit 12 N. Use zero gravity. Run from 0 to 2.5 seconds at 100 Hz with two physics substeps. Both joints must always stay within their limits. At the end, the shoulder must be within 4 degrees of its target and the extension must be within 0.015 m of its target.
