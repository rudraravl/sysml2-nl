Create a closed-loop branching three-joint inspection mechanism. Newton Physics owns all rigid-body dynamics and one Modelica FMU owns three independent PD control channels. A fixed base supports a 1.5 kg cylindrical left link with radius 0.06 m and height 0.4 m and a separate 1.25 kg capsule right link with radius 0.05 m and height 0.35 m. A 0.5 kg spherical tool with radius 0.07 m attaches to the left link. The left shoulder is an X-axis revolute joint from the base to the left link with limits from -45 to 45 degrees. The right shoulder is a Z-axis revolute joint from the base to the right link with limits from -40 to 40 degrees. The tool slide is a Y-axis prismatic joint from the left link to the tool with limits from -0.1 to 0.1 m. The left shoulder PD controller has target 15 degrees, proportional gain 8 N.m/rad, derivative gain 1.2 N.m.s/rad, and torque limit 3 N.m. The right shoulder PD controller has target -12 degrees, proportional gain 0.5 N.m/rad, derivative gain 0.07 N.m.s/rad, and torque limit 0.15 N.m. The tool slide PD controller has target 0.05 m, proportional gain 40 N/m, derivative gain 6 N.s/m, and force limit 8 N. Each controller receives its joint position and velocity in canonical SI units and outputs joint effort. Use zero gravity. Run from 0 to 3 seconds at 100 Hz with two physics substeps. Every joint must always stay within its limits. At the end, each shoulder must be within 4 degrees of its target and the tool slide must be within 0.015 m of its target. Both shoulder speeds must finish below 0.05 rad/s in magnitude and the tool speed must finish below 0.01 m/s in magnitude.
