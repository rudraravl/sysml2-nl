Create a portable one-degree-of-freedom robot arm. The Modelica plant owns the revolute joint dynamics and exposes joint angle in radians and angular velocity in radians per second. Use rotational inertia 0.5 kg.m2, proportional stiffness 8 N.m/rad, and viscous damping 2 N.m.s/rad. The OpenUSD scene contains a fixed base and a two-kilogram link connected by a Y-axis revolute joint limited from -90 to 90 degrees. Drive the plant toward 30 degrees, run it from 0 to 3 seconds at 50 Hz, and animate the USD link from the FMU joint-angle output. The arm must stay within its joint limits and settle within 2 degrees of the target by 3 seconds.
