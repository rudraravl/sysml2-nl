This contract is a decentralized reserve currency protocol that custodies a basket of stablecoin assets and a native protocol token. It maintains a global treasury balance for each stablecoin asset, a total supply of the native protocol token, and individual user balances for both stablecoins and the native token. Callers can deposit supported stablecoins to mint native protocol tokens, and they can burn native protocol tokens to redeem stablecoins. Callers can also stake their native protocol tokens to earn rewards, and unstake them. A designated operator can adjust the redemption fee, add new supported stablecoin assets, and update the oracle price for each asset. The redemption fee for stablecoins is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed amount. The maximum number of supported stablecoin assets is 5. The contract announces when native protocol tokens are minted or burned, and when stablecoins are deposited or redeemed.
