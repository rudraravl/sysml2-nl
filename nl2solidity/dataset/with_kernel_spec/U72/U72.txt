This contract manages a tokenized fund representing fractional ownership of short-term government debt instruments, holding an equivalent amount of a stablecoin in reserve. It maintains each investor's balance of fund shares and the total stablecoin held by the fund. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint new fund shares, redeem fund shares to withdraw stablecoins, and transfer their fund shares to other addresses. A designated operator can pause and unpause all deposit and redemption operations, and can update the stablecoin reserve balance to reflect rebalancing activities. The fund charges a 0.05% fee on all redemptions, which is deducted from the stablecoin amount received by the investor. All deposits must be at least 100 stablecoins. The contract emits an event when shares are minted, redeemed, or transferred, and when the fund's stablecoin reserve balance is updated.
