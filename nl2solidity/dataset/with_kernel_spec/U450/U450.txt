This contract facilitates private, cross-chain asset transfers by holding deposited assets in escrow. It maintains a record of each deposit's unique identifier, the asset deposited, the deposit amount, and the recipient's shielded address. Callers can deposit assets, provide proofs to withdraw assets to a shielded address, and emergency withdraw their own assets if a withdrawal fails. A designated operator can pause deposits and withdrawals, and set the withdrawal fee. The withdrawal fee is fixed at 0.05 units of the deposited asset. The maximum deposit amount for any single transaction is 10,000 units of the deposited asset. The contract emits an event upon each successful deposit, withdrawal, and emergency withdrawal, detailing the transaction's unique identifier, the asset, and the amount.
