This contract facilitates a prediction market, holding collateral deposited by users to back their predictions. It maintains records of individual user positions, the total collateral for each prediction outcome, and global configuration parameters such as the market's resolution status. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit collateral into an existing market, make predictions on market outcomes, redeem their winnings after a market resolves, and withdraw their initial collateral if a market is canceled. Only a designated operator can create new prediction markets and resolve existing markets by declaring an outcome. A market must have at least 100 units of collateral deposited before it can be resolved. A 0.5% fee is applied to all winnings redeemed from resolved markets. The contract announces market creation, prediction submissions, and market resolution events to observers.
