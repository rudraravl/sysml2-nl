This contract manages unique digital pet tokens, serving as a staking vault for these non-fungible tokens (NFTs). It maintains individual records of staked pets per user, along with global configurations for staking rewards and pet attributes. Callers can stake their pet NFTs, claim accrued rewards, and withdraw their staked pets. A designated operator can adjust the daily reward rate for staked pets, and the owner can pause all staking and withdrawal operations. The maximum number of pets a single user can stake is 5, and the daily reward rate for staking cannot exceed 0.01 units of the reward token per pet. The contract announces events for pet staking, reward claims, and pet withdrawals.
