This contract is a risk hedging mechanism that custodies a designated collateral asset. It maintains a global configuration for each unique asset pair, including the current minting ratio, and per-user records of deposited collateral and minted tokens. Callers can deposit collateral to mint principal and swap tokens, redeem principal tokens for the collateral asset at expiry, and swap the reference asset for the collateral asset using swap tokens before expiry. A designated operator can adjust the minting ratio within predefined bounds and set the expiry time for new asset pairs. The initial minting ratio for principal and swap tokens is always 1:1, and the swap fee is fixed at 0.1% of the swapped amount. The contract announces successful minting events, redemptions, and swaps, including the quantities involved.
