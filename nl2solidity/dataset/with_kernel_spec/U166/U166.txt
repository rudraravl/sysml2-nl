This contract manages a leveraged yield farming vault, holding deposited base tokens and borrowed quote tokens. It maintains per-user records of deposited base tokens and borrowed quote tokens, and global configuration for available farming strategies and associated risk parameters. Callers can deposit base tokens, borrow quote tokens against their collateral, open a farming position, close a farming position, and withdraw their base tokens and any accrued yield. A designated operator can add new farming strategies, update strategy parameters, and pause all new position openings. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 5x the deposited collateral. A 0.5% fee is charged on all profits realized from closed positions. The contract emits an event when a new position is opened, when a position is closed, and when a strategy's parameters are updated.
