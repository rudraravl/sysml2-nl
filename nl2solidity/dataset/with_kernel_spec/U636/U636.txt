This contract manages a prediction market where users speculate on real-world event outcomes, holding user deposits in an escrow until market resolution. It maintains records of individual user positions, the total liquidity for each outcome, and market-specific configuration parameters such as resolution status and payout multipliers. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit funds to back an outcome, withdraw funds from an open market, and claim winnings after a market has resolved. A designated operator is privileged to resolve markets, setting the winning outcome and initiating payouts; only the operator can finalize a market. Each market has a maximum deposit limit of 1000 units of the base currency per user, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all winning payouts. The contract emits an event when a new market is created, when funds are deposited or withdrawn, and when a market is resolved.
