This contract facilitates a global mobile money transfer system, holding a reserve of a stablecoin to back user balances. It maintains individual user accounts, each storing a stablecoin balance and a record of recent transactions. Callers can deposit stablecoins, transfer stablecoins to other registered users, and withdraw their stablecoin balance to a designated external wallet. A designated operator can register new users, set transfer fees, and pause or unpause all transfer operations. The maximum transfer fee is 0.5% of the transfer amount, and each user account has a daily withdrawal limit of 1000 stablecoins. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit, transfer, or withdrawal occurs, and when the operator changes the transfer fee or pauses/unpauses transfers.
