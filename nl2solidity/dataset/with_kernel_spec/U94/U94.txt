This contract facilitates continuous, time-based token transfers from a sender to a recipient, holding the transferred tokens in escrow until claimed. It maintains individual stream configurations for each sender-recipient pair, including the total amount to be streamed, the start time, and the end time, along with the amount already streamed and the last time a stream was updated. Callers can create new streams, deposit tokens into existing streams, withdraw available tokens from their streams, and cancel their own streams. Only the contract owner can pause all streaming activity or update the protocol-wide streaming fee. A stream must have a minimum duration of 60 seconds, and the protocol charges a 0.1% fee on all tokens withdrawn by recipients. The contract announces stream creations, stream cancellations, and successful token withdrawals.
