This contract manages an incentivized action market, holding deposited collateral tokens and distributing rewards. It maintains individual account balances for deposited collateral, accrued rewards, and active action commitments, alongside global configuration for reward rates and action definitions. Callers can deposit collateral, commit to specific actions, claim earned rewards, and withdraw their collateral. A designated administrator can define new actions, adjust reward rates, and pause or unpause the contract's core functionality. Each action commitment requires a minimum collateral deposit of 100 base units of the collateral token, and a 5% fee is applied to all claimed rewards, directed to a treasury address. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, action commitments, and reward claims, including the involved account, action identifier, and amounts.
