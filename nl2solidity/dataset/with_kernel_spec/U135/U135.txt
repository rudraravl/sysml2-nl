This contract manages a marketplace for unique digital rooms, custodying an ERC-20 token used for purchases and a unique ERC-721 token representing room ownership. It maintains records of room ownership, room prices, and a global fee recipient. Callers can create new rooms, buy existing rooms, and set the price for rooms they own. A designated operator can set the creation fee and the marketplace fee percentage. The creation fee for a new room is fixed at 100 of the ERC-20 token, and the marketplace fee is 5% of the sale price, deducted from the seller. The contract announces when a room is created, when a room is bought, and when a room's price is updated.
