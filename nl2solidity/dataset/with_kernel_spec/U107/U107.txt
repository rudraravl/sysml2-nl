This contract facilitates fast, non-custodial transfers of tokens between different blockchain domains, holding transferred tokens in escrow until a corresponding release on the destination domain. It maintains records of each transfer's unique identifier, the amount transferred, the token type, the sender's address, the recipient's address on the destination domain, and the status of the transfer (e.g., pending, completed, disputed). Callers can initiate a transfer by depositing tokens and specifying destination details, and designated operators can confirm transfer completions on the destination domain or dispute invalid transfers. Only the owner can set the transfer fee, which is fixed at 0.05% of the transfer amount, and only designated operators can approve a transfer for release after a minimum 10-block confirmation period on the origin domain. The contract announces the initiation of each transfer, its unique identifier, and the final resolution status of each transfer to observers.
