This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, holding staked Ether deposit receipts as collateral. It maintains individual user collateral balances, minted stablecoin amounts, and a global system configuration including a collateralization ratio and various fees. Callers can deposit staked Ether deposit receipts, mint stablecoins against their collateral, repay minted stablecoins, and withdraw their collateral. A designated operator can adjust system parameters like the collateralization ratio and fees, and can pause or unpause the contract. The minimum collateralization ratio is 150%, meaning for every 100 stablecoins minted, at least 150 units of collateral value must be held. A 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin minting operations. The contract announces events for every deposit, mint, repayment, and withdrawal, including the user's address and the amounts involved.
