This contract facilitates collateralized stablecoin loans against a liquid staking token, holding both deposited liquid staking tokens as collateral and stablecoins for lending. It maintains individual loan positions, tracking collateral amount, borrowed stablecoin amount, and a cumulative interest accrual for each borrower, alongside a global pool of available stablecoins for lending. Callers can deposit liquid staking tokens to open a loan, borrow stablecoins against their collateral, repay their stablecoin loans, and withdraw their liquid staking token collateral once their loan is fully repaid. A designated operator can adjust the interest rate and liquidate undercollateralized loans. The maximum loan-to-value ratio is 70%, and a 0.5% origination fee is charged on all new stablecoin borrowings. The contract announces loan origination events, loan repayment events, and liquidation events.
