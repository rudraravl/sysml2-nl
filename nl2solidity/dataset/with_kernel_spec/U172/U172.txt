This contract manages a decentralized lottery, holding deposited funds and distributing prizes. It maintains a record of each participant's ticket purchases, the current lottery round number, the total prize pool for the current round, and a list of winning numbers for past rounds. Participants can purchase tickets for the current round, and a designated operator can initiate new lottery rounds and draw winning numbers. Only the owner can set the ticket price and the operator address. The ticket price is fixed at 0.01 ether per ticket, and a new lottery round can only be initiated if at least 100 tickets have been sold in the previous round. The contract announces when a new round begins, when tickets are purchased, and when winning numbers are drawn.
