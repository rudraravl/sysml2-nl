This contract manages the tokenization of real-world assets, specifically private credit facilities, and custodies the underlying asset tokens representing fractional ownership in these facilities. It maintains a record of each user's deposited asset tokens, their corresponding ownership shares in the credit facilities, and a global configuration for fees and operational parameters. Callers can deposit asset tokens to acquire ownership shares, redeem ownership shares to withdraw asset tokens, and transfer their ownership shares to other eligible users. A designated operator can pause or unpause deposits and redemptions, and an owner can set the redemption fee and add or remove approved asset tokens. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed amount, and a maximum of 10 distinct asset tokens can be approved for deposit at any time. The contract announces when asset tokens are deposited, ownership shares are redeemed, and the redemption fee is updated.
