This contract is a yield aggregation vault for a single ERC-20 token, holding deposited user funds and their accumulated yield. It maintains per-user deposit balances, the total deposited amount, and configuration parameters for its yield-generating strategy. Callers can deposit the specified ERC-20 token, withdraw their deposited amount plus any earned yield, and claim any available rewards tokens. The owner can update the strategy contract address, adjust the fee percentage for withdrawals, and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The withdrawal fee is capped at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount, and the strategy contract can only be changed once every 24 hours. The contract announces deposit and withdrawal events, including the user's address and the amount involved.
