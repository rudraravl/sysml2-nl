This contract is a perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral deposited by traders and liquidity providers. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and oracle addresses. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close long or short positions, add or remove margin from existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated administrator can adjust trading fees, update the oracle address, and pause trading in emergencies. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x. A 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing operations. The contract emits events for all position openings, closures, and significant collateral movements.
