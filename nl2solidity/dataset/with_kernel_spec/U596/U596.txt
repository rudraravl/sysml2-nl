This contract manages a stablecoin, USDz, backed by tokenized real-world assets held in custody. It maintains each user's USDz balance, the total supply of USDz, and a registry of approved real-world asset tokens and their current valuations. Callers can mint USDz by depositing approved real-world asset tokens, redeem USDz for an equivalent value of approved real-world asset tokens, and transfer USDz to other users. A designated operator can approve new real-world asset tokens for backing, update the valuation of approved real-world asset tokens, and pause all minting and redemption operations. Minting requires a minimum deposit value of 1000 USDz, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all redemption transactions. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and transfer events, including the amount and the involved parties.
