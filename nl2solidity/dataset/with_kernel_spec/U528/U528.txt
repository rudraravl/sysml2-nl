This contract manages a gaming platform's native utility tokens, enabling users to stake them to earn rewards and participate in governance. It maintains individual user balances of staked tokens, accumulated rewards, and a global pool of undistributed rewards. Users can stake tokens, unstake tokens, claim their earned rewards, and delegate their voting power to another address. Only a designated operator can deposit new rewards into the global pool and set the reward distribution rate, which must be between 0.01% and 1% of the total staked tokens per day. The contract emits an event when tokens are staked, when tokens are unstaked, and when rewards are claimed.
