This contract manages a gaming guild's treasury, holding various fungible tokens and non-fungible tokens representing in-game assets. It maintains a record of each member's delegated voting power and a configurable list of whitelisted game asset contracts. Members can deposit fungible tokens, request withdrawals of fungible tokens, and propose the purchase or sale of non-fungible tokens. The owner can add or remove whitelisted game asset contracts, approve or reject non-fungible token proposals, and initiate fungible token transfers from the treasury. A member's delegated voting power is capped at 10% of the total outstanding governance tokens. All withdrawal requests for fungible tokens are subject to a 72-hour delay before funds are released. The contract announces when new non-fungible token proposals are created and when fungible token withdrawals are processed.
