This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, specifically the minting and burning of a metastable token and a superstable token. It maintains a record of the total supply of both the metastable token and the superstable token, and a global configuration for minting and burning parameters. Callers can mint the metastable token by depositing a base asset, burn the metastable token to redeem the base asset, mint the superstable token by depositing the metastable token, and burn the superstable token to redeem the metastable token. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio for minting the superstable token and set the stability fee for burning the superstable token. The collateralization ratio for minting the superstable token must always be between 100% and 150%, inclusive. A stability fee of 0.5% is applied when burning the superstable token. The contract announces the current total supply of both the metastable token and the superstable token, and the current collateralization ratio and stability fee.
