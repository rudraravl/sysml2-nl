This contract serves as a wrapper for a base asset, enabling its use within a broader protocol by issuing a wrapped representation and managing its redemption. It maintains a record of each user's wrapped asset balance and the total supply of wrapped assets. Callers can deposit the base asset to mint wrapped tokens, and they can burn wrapped tokens to redeem the base asset. Only a designated operator can pause or unpause the wrapping and unwrapping functions, and only the owner can update the designated operator. A minimum deposit of 100 base asset units is required for minting, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all redemptions. The contract announces events for each deposit, withdrawal, and changes to the operator or pause status.
