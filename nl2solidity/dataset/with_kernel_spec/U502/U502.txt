This contract manages collateralized debt positions (CDPs) for minting a synthetic stablecoin, holding deposited collateral tokens and minted stablecoin tokens. It maintains individual CDP records including collateral amount, minted stablecoin amount, and liquidation status, along with system-wide configuration parameters like liquidation thresholds and fees. Callers can open a CDP by depositing collateral, mint stablecoin against their collateral, repay stablecoin to reduce their debt, withdraw excess collateral, and liquidate undercollateralized CDPs. A designated operator can adjust system parameters such as the liquidation threshold, the liquidation penalty, and the stablecoin minting fee. The maximum collateralization ratio for minting stablecoin is 150%, meaning for every 1.5 units of collateral value, at most 1 unit of stablecoin value can be minted. A 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin minting operations. The contract announces events for CDP openings, stablecoin mints, repayments, collateral withdrawals, and liquidations.
