This contract mints and burns a stablecoin, maintaining a reserve of a volatile collateral token. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin, a total supply of the stablecoin, and the total amount of collateral held in reserve. Callers can mint stablecoins by depositing collateral, burn stablecoins to redeem collateral, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio and pause or unpause minting and burning. The initial collateralization ratio is 150%, meaning 1.5 units of collateral token are required to mint 1 unit of stablecoin. A 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin transfers, which is sent to a treasury address. The contract emits events for all minting, burning, and transfer operations, including the sender, recipient, amount, and any fees.
