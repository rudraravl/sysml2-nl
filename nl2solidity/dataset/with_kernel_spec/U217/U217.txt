This contract is a synthetic dollar minting and staking vault, holding collateral in an internal reserve. It maintains a total supply of the synthetic dollar, a total staked amount of the yield-bearing synthetic dollar, and for each user, their individual synthetic dollar balance and their individual staked synthetic dollar balance. Callers can mint synthetic dollars by depositing collateral, transfer synthetic dollars, stake their synthetic dollars to receive yield-bearing synthetic dollars, unstake yield-bearing synthetic dollars to receive synthetic dollars, and burn synthetic dollars. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio and initiate a rebalance of the collateral. The minimum collateralization ratio for minting is 1.05:1, meaning 1.05 units of collateral are required for every 1 unit of synthetic dollar minted. A 0.1% fee is applied to all unstaking operations, deducted from the returned synthetic dollars. The contract announces all minting, burning, staking, unstaking, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
