This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers by holding wrapped tokens representing assets locked on a source chain. It maintains a mapping of each user's balance of wrapped tokens and a global count of total wrapped tokens in circulation. Callers can deposit native tokens to mint wrapped tokens, and redeem wrapped tokens to initiate a withdrawal of native tokens on the source chain. A designated operator can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The maximum number of wrapped tokens that can be minted for a single deposit is 10,000 units. A 0.1% fee is applied to all withdrawal amounts. The contract announces events for successful deposits, withdrawals, and when the operator pauses or unpauses operations.
