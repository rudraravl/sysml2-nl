This contract is a vault for tokenized precious metals, holding a single type of tokenized gold. It maintains individual balances for each user and tracks the total supply of tokenized gold within the vault. Users can deposit tokenized gold into the vault, withdraw their deposited tokenized gold, and transfer their vault balance to another user. A designated operator can pause and unpause deposits and withdrawals, and can set the withdrawal fee. The contract charges a 0.5% fee on all withdrawals, and the maximum daily withdrawal amount for any single user is capped at 100 tokenized gold. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit or withdrawal occurs, and when the withdrawal fee is updated.
