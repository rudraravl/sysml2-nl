This contract is a vault for wrapping non-smart contract assets, holding a reserve of a stablecoin to back the wrapped assets. It maintains individual balances of wrapped assets for each user, a total supply of the wrapped asset, and a configuration for various operational parameters. Callers can mint new wrapped tokens by depositing the stablecoin, redeem wrapped tokens for the stablecoin, and transfer wrapped tokens to other users. A designated operator can pause all minting and redeeming operations in an emergency. The minimum amount of stablecoin that can be deposited for minting is 100 units, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all redemption operations. The contract announces events for successful minting, redemption, and transfers, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
