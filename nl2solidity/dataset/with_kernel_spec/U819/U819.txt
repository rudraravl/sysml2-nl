This contract is a yield aggregator that custodies user-deposited tokens and manages their deployment into external yield-generating strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balances, the total amount of each token held by the contract, and a configuration of approved external strategy contracts. Callers can deposit supported tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim accumulated rewards. Only the owner can approve new external strategy contracts, set the fee percentage for claimed rewards, and initiate emergency withdrawals of all funds. A 0.5% fee is applied to all claimed rewards, and a maximum of 10 external strategy contracts can be approved at any given time. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claim events, including the user's address, the token involved, and the amount.
