This contract manages a crowdfunding campaign, holding deposited tokens from participants. It maintains a record of each participant's deposited amount and a total accumulated amount for the campaign. Callers can deposit tokens into the campaign, claim their allocated tokens after the campaign concludes, and withdraw their initial deposit if the campaign fails. Only the owner can start the campaign, set the funding goal, and finalize the campaign. The campaign has a minimum funding goal of 100,000 tokens and a maximum duration of 30 days. The contract emits an event when a deposit is made and when the campaign status changes.
