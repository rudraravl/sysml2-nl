This contract is a bridge for transferring assets between a Layer-1 network and a Layer-2 rollup, holding deposited tokens in escrow. It maintains a record of each user's deposited tokens awaiting transfer to Layer-2, and a configuration for the bridge's operational parameters. Callers can deposit tokens on Layer-1 for transfer to Layer-2, initiate withdrawals from Layer-2 back to Layer-1, and claim withdrawn tokens after a waiting period. A designated operator can pause deposits and withdrawals, and upgrade the Layer-2 bridge contract. Deposits have a minimum value of 0.001 Ether, and withdrawals incur a fixed fee of 0.0005 Ether. The contract announces successful deposits, initiated withdrawals, and claimed withdrawals.
