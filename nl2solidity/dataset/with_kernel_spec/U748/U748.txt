This contract manages a perpetual futures exchange, holding user collateral in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual user accounts tracking their collateral balances, open positions, and unrealized profit/loss, alongside global configuration parameters like funding rates and maximum leverage. Callers can deposit collateral, withdraw available collateral, open new long or short positions, close existing positions, and liquidate undercollateralized accounts. A designated operator can update global configuration parameters, adjust funding rates, and pause trading in emergencies. New positions require an initial margin of at least 10% of the notional value, and a 0.05% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing trades. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, position closures, and liquidations.
