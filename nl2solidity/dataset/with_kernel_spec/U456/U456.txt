This contract is a liquidity book automated market maker that custodies pairs of fungible tokens, allowing users to swap between them. It maintains per-pool records of token reserves, a list of active price bins, and per-user liquidity positions within those bins. Callers can deposit liquidity into specific price bins, withdraw their deposited liquidity, and swap one token for another. Only the owner can set the swap fee, which is initially 0.25% and can be changed within a range of 0.01% to 1.00%. The contract emits events for all liquidity deposits, withdrawals, and token swaps, including the amounts involved and the affected price bins.
