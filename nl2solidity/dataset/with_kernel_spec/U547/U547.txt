This contract facilitates efficient trading of non-fungible tokens (NFTs) by allowing users to deposit NFTs into shared pools and trade fungible tokens representing fractional ownership. It maintains a mapping of each NFT collection to its associated pool, tracking the total fungible tokens issued for each pool, and the individual fungible token balances for each user. Callers can deposit NFTs into a pool, withdraw NFTs from a pool, buy fungible tokens from a pool, sell fungible tokens to a pool, and redeem fungible tokens for a specific NFT. The owner can set the trading fee for each pool, which is initially 0.5% and can be adjusted between 0% and 5%, and can also designate an operator to manage pool parameters. The contract announces when an NFT is deposited, when an NFT is withdrawn, and when fungible tokens are bought or sold.
