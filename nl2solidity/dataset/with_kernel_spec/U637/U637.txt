This contract provides automated, on-chain coverage for digital assets, holding deposited collateral in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual coverage positions for each user, tracking their deposited collateral, active coverage amount, and a unique coverage ID, alongside a global configuration for coverage terms. Callers can deposit collateral, activate coverage for a specified duration, extend existing coverage, and withdraw their available collateral. Only a designated operator can adjust the global coverage fee percentage and the maximum coverage duration. The coverage fee is fixed at 0.5% of the covered amount per month, and the maximum coverage duration for any single activation or extension is 12 months. The contract announces events for coverage activation, coverage extension, and collateral withdrawal, including the user's address, the amount involved, and the coverage ID where applicable.
