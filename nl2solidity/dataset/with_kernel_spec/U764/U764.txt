This contract is a bridge for a wrapped Bitcoin asset, custodying the wrapped asset on the destination chain. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped asset, a mapping of user balances, and a record of the current bridge fee. Callers can deposit the wrapped asset to mint a corresponding amount of a liquid staking derivative, and they can redeem the liquid staking derivative to burn it and receive the wrapped asset back. A designated operator can adjust the bridge fee and pause or unpause deposits and redemptions. The bridge fee is initially 0.1% and cannot exceed 1.0%. The contract emits an event when wrapped assets are deposited and when liquid staking derivatives are redeemed.
