This contract facilitates the creation and management of token launch events, holding deposited tokens for distribution. It maintains a record of each launch event's configuration, including the token being sold, the fundraising token, soft cap, hard cap, and individual participant allocations. Callers can create a new launch event, contribute fundraising tokens to an active event, claim purchased tokens after a successful event, or withdraw their contributions if an event fails. A designated owner can set the platform fee percentage, cancel an ongoing launch event, or withdraw accumulated platform fees. The platform fee is fixed at 2% of all raised funds. Each launch event must have a minimum soft cap of 100 fundraising tokens and a maximum hard cap of 10,000 fundraising tokens. The contract emits events when a new launch event is created, a contribution is made, tokens are claimed, or an event is cancelled.
