This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, holding reserve tokens and issuing a pegged token. It maintains a global reserve pool of a volatile asset, a global supply of the pegged stablecoin, and per-account balances for both the reserve and stablecoin. Callers can deposit the volatile reserve asset to mint the stablecoin, redeem stablecoin for the reserve asset, and transfer stablecoin between accounts. The owner can set the redemption fee and pause all minting and redemption operations. Redemption operations incur a 0.5% fee, deducted from the redeemed reserve asset. The maximum stablecoin supply is capped at 1,000,000 units. The contract announces stablecoin minting and burning events, and reserve asset deposits and withdrawals.
