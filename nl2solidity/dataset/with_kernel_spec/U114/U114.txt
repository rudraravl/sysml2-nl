This contract manages a token launchpad, holding deposited tokens from participants and the project tokens allocated for distribution. It maintains a record of each participant's deposited amount and their claimable project token allocation, alongside a global configuration for the launch event. Callers can deposit a specified token, claim their allocated project tokens after the launch, and withdraw any unspent deposited tokens if the launch fails. An owner role can set the launch parameters, including the project token address and the total allocation, and initiate or cancel the launch. The maximum individual deposit is 1000 units of the deposit token, and the project token claim period begins exactly 24 hours after the launch is initiated. The contract emits an event when a deposit is made, when project tokens are claimed, and when the launch status changes.
