This contract manages a treasury of a base cryptocurrency, enabling strategic deployment and governance. It maintains a total balance of the base cryptocurrency, a record of designated investment allocations, and a list of approved investment strategies, each with a maximum allocation percentage. Callers can propose new investment strategies, vote on proposed strategies, and view the current treasury balance and investment allocations. Only the owner can approve new investment strategies, set the maximum allocation percentage for an approved strategy, and initiate the transfer of funds to an approved investment strategy. A proposed strategy requires at least 51% of the total voting power to be approved. The maximum allocation for any single investment strategy is 25% of the total treasury balance. The contract announces when a new investment strategy is proposed, when a vote is cast, and when funds are transferred to an investment strategy.
