This contract facilitates confidential transfers of the native blockchain currency by acting as a mixer, holding user deposits in a shared pool. It maintains a Merkle tree of deposit commitments and a separate Merkle tree of nullifiers, along with a record of the current deposit fee. Callers can deposit native currency, providing a zero-knowledge proof of a valid commitment, and can withdraw native currency to a specified recipient, providing a zero-knowledge proof of a valid nullifier and commitment. The owner can update the deposit fee and the designated operator can update the Merkle tree roots. The deposit fee must be between 0% and 5% of the deposited amount, inclusive, and the initial deposit amount must be exactly 1 unit of the native currency. The contract emits an event upon each successful deposit and each successful withdrawal, including the amount and the relevant Merkle tree root.
