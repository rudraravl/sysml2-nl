This contract is for managing reputation scores and is the canonical source of truth for user credibility within the ecosystem. It maintains a mapping of unique user identifiers to their current credibility score, a history of score adjustments, and a record of staked tokens supporting those scores. Callers can register a new user identifier, stake tokens to support their own or another user's credibility score, transfer staked tokens between users, and challenge a user's credibility score. Only a designated operator can adjust a user's credibility score, resolve a challenge, or set the staking token address. A user must stake a minimum of 100 tokens to register a new user identifier, and a challenge incurs a 5% fee on the staked amount, which is distributed to successful challengers. The contract announces when a user's credibility score changes and when a challenge is initiated or resolved.
