This contract is a decentralized lending pool for various crypto assets, holding deposited tokens as collateral. It maintains individual account balances for each user's deposits and borrows, a global record of total liquidity and outstanding debt per asset, and configuration parameters like interest rates and collateral factors. Callers can deposit supported tokens, borrow available assets against their collateral, repay their loans, and withdraw their excess collateral. A designated operator can update interest rate models, add new supported assets, and pause borrowing for specific assets. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 75%, and a 0.05% protocol fee is charged on all new borrows. The contract emits events for every deposit, borrow, repayment, and withdrawal, including the user's address, the asset, and the amount.
