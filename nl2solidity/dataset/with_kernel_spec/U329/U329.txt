This contract manages a decentralized exchange-traded fund (dETF) and custodies the underlying asset tokens that compose the fund. It maintains a registry of dETFs, each with its own token composition and weightings, and tracks individual user balances of each dETF token. Callers can create new dETF tokens by depositing the required underlying assets, redeem dETF tokens for their proportional share of underlying assets, and transfer their dETF tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can update the composition of an existing dETF, and a governance committee can approve new dETF creations. A 0.2% fee is charged on all dETF redemptions, and new dETF compositions must be approved by the governance committee within 72 hours of proposal. The contract announces when a new dETF is created, when an existing dETF's composition is updated, and when dETF tokens are minted or burned.
