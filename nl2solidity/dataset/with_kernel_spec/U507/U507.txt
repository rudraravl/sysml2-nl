This contract manages a decentralized launchpad for new token projects, holding deposited tokens from participants and distributing new project tokens. It maintains per-participant records of deposited funds, allocated project tokens, and vesting schedules, alongside global configuration for launchpad rounds. Callers can deposit funds into active rounds, claim vested project tokens, and withdraw their original deposits if a round fails. A designated operator can create new launchpad rounds, configure vesting schedules, and initiate token distributions. The maximum number of participants in any single round is capped at 500. A 2% fee is applied to all successful project token distributions, payable to the contract owner. The contract announces the creation of new launchpad rounds, the completion of funding targets, and the distribution of project tokens.
