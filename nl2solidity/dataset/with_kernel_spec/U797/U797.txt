This contract serves as a token launchpad, allowing creators to issue new tokens and establish initial liquidity. It maintains a record of each launched token project, including its creator, the total supply of the new token, and the amount of base currency committed for liquidity. Callers can initiate a new token launch, contribute base currency to an ongoing launch, and claim their share of newly created tokens after a successful launch. Only the contract owner can set the platform fee for new token launches and pause all new launch initiations. A platform fee of 0.5% of the total base currency committed for liquidity is charged for each successful launch. Each token launch must raise a minimum of 1000 base currency to be considered successful. The contract announces the initiation of a new token launch and the successful completion of a launch.
