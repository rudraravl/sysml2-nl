This contract manages a social token for a decentralized social network, holding user-deposited base tokens in escrow. It maintains a record of each user's social token balance, a global supply of the social token, and a configuration for the conversion rate between the base token and the social token. Callers can deposit base tokens to mint social tokens, transfer social tokens to other users, and burn social tokens to withdraw their proportional share of the underlying base tokens. Only a designated operator can adjust the social token minting and burning rate, which is initially set to 1 base token per 100 social tokens. The contract enforces a maximum transfer amount of 1,000,000 social tokens per transaction. The contract emits an event whenever social tokens are minted, transferred, or burned.
