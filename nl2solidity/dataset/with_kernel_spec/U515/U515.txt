This contract manages a tokenized real-world asset, specifically a pool of fractionalized debt instruments, and custodies the underlying stablecoin collateral. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance and their corresponding share of the tokenized asset pool. Callers can deposit stablecoins to acquire asset tokens, redeem asset tokens for stablecoins, and transfer their asset tokens to other addresses. A designated operator can adjust the stablecoin redemption fee and the maximum total supply of asset tokens. The stablecoin redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed amount, and the maximum total supply of asset tokens is capped at 10,000,000 units. The contract announces a Deposit event when stablecoins are added and a Redemption event when stablecoins are withdrawn.
