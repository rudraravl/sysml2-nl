This contract manages a marketplace for non-fungible tokens, holding tokens offered for sale and the proceeds from successful sales. It maintains a record of listed items including their price and seller, and tracks account balances for native currency and approved non-fungible tokens. Callers can list a non-fungible token for sale, purchase a listed non-fungible token, cancel their own listing, and withdraw their native currency proceeds from sales. The owner can set the marketplace fee percentage and designate specific accounts as operators who can pause all trading activity. The marketplace fee is fixed at 2.5% of the sale price, and a listed non-fungible token must be approved by the marketplace contract before it can be purchased. The contract announces when a non-fungible token has been listed, when a non-fungible token has been purchased, and when a listing has been canceled.
