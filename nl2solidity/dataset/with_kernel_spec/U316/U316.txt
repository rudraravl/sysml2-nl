This contract manages a decentralized prediction market, holding user deposits and payouts in an escrow. It maintains a record of open prediction markets, the total liquidity available for each market, and individual user positions within those markets. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit funds into existing markets to provide liquidity, place bets on market outcomes, and withdraw their winnings or unused liquidity. A designated operator is privileged to resolve market outcomes and set the fee percentage for all market interactions. A market creation fee of 0.5% of the initial liquidity must be paid, and the maximum number of active prediction markets at any given time is capped at 100. The contract announces market creation, bet placements, and market resolutions to observers.
