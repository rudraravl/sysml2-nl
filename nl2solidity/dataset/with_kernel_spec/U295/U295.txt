This contract manages a restaking pool for real-world asset backed tokens, holding deposited collateral tokens and issuing liquid restaking tokens. It maintains a mapping of user addresses to their deposited collateral amounts and issued liquid restaking token balances, along with a total supply of liquid restaking tokens and a current exchange rate between collateral and liquid restaking tokens. Callers can deposit collateral tokens to receive liquid restaking tokens, and redeem liquid restaking tokens for collateral tokens. A designated operator can adjust the exchange rate and pause deposits, while the owner can set the designated operator and upgrade the contract. A 0.5% fee is applied to all redemptions, and the exchange rate cannot be adjusted by more than 1% in a single operation. The contract announces deposit events, redemption events, and exchange rate updates.
