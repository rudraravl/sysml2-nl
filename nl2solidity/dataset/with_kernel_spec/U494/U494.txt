This contract is a vault for tokenized U.S. Treasury Bills, holding a fungible token representing ownership of these bills. It maintains individual account balances for each depositor and a total supply of the tokenized bills. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint new tokenized bills, and redeem tokenized bills to withdraw stablecoins. A designated operator can pause and unpause deposits and redemptions, and set the redemption fee. Only the owner can upgrade the contract logic. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.1% of the redemption amount, and the minimum deposit amount is 100 stablecoins. The contract announces deposit events, redemption events, and changes to the redemption fee.
