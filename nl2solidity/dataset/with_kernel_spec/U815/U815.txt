This contract mints and burns a token representing physical gold, custodying no assets itself, but tracking the supply of the token. It maintains a total supply of the token and individual account balances. Callers can transfer tokens to other accounts, approve spending by another account, and transfer tokens on behalf of another account. A designated operator can mint new tokens, burn existing tokens, and pause or unpause all token transfers. The total supply of tokens cannot exceed 1,000,000 grams. A transfer fee of 0.1% is applied to all token transfers. The contract announces token transfers, approvals, minting, and burning events.
