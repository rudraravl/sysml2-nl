This contract manages a virtual farming game, holding player-owned land parcels and various in-game resources. It maintains individual player inventories of resources, a global registry of land parcels with their ownership and current crop status, and a configuration for resource generation rates and adventure difficulty. Players can plant seeds on their land, harvest mature crops, send their characters on adventures to acquire resources, and craft new items from existing resources. A designated game administrator can adjust resource generation rates, introduce new adventure types, and mint new land parcels up to a total supply cap of 10,000 parcels. Harvesting crops incurs a 5% tax on the harvested resources, which is sent to a treasury address. Players must wait at least 24 hours between consecutive adventures with the same character. The contract emits events when crops are planted, resources are harvested, and items are crafted.
