This contract manages a tiered NFT staking system, holding staked non-fungible tokens and distributing rewards. It maintains individual records for each staker, tracking their staked NFTs, accumulated rewards, and current staking tier, along with a global configuration for reward rates and tier thresholds. Callers can stake their non-fungible tokens, claim accumulated rewards, and unstake their non-fungible tokens. Only a designated operator can adjust the reward rates for each tier and set the non-fungible token collection that can be staked. The maximum staking duration for any non-fungible token is 365 days, and a 5% unstaking fee is applied to any non-fungible token unstaked before 30 days. The contract emits events when non-fungible tokens are staked, rewards are claimed, and non-fungible tokens are unstaked.
