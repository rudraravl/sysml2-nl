This contract is a liquid staking pool that custodies a base token and issues a liquid staking token. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, a total amount of staked base tokens, and a per-user balance of liquid staking tokens. Callers can deposit base tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw base tokens by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim rewards. A designated operator can stake deposited base tokens with an external validator and unstake base tokens from an external validator. The owner can set the staking fee and upgrade the contract. The staking fee is fixed at 5% of all staking rewards. Withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period. The contract emits an event when tokens are deposited, when tokens are withdrawn, and when rewards are distributed.
