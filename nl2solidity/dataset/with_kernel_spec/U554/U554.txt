This contract is a treasury for a decentralized autonomous organization, holding a stablecoin and managing its allocation across various yield-generating strategies. It maintains a record of the total stablecoin balance, the allocation to each approved strategy, and configuration parameters for risk limits and strategy approvals. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the treasury, and a designated operator can initiate stablecoin transfers to approved strategies, redeem funds from strategies, and update strategy allocations. Only the designated operator can approve new strategies, set risk limits for each strategy, and pause or unpause the treasury's operations. The maximum allocation to any single strategy is 30% of the total treasury balance, and a 24-hour delay is enforced before any new strategy approval becomes active. The contract announces events for stablecoin deposits, stablecoin withdrawals, strategy allocations, and changes to strategy approvals or risk limits.
