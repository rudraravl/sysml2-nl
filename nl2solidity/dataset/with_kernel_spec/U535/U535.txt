This contract is a tokenized representation of real-world assets, holding an equivalent amount of a stablecoin in reserve. It maintains a total supply of the token, individual user balances, and a record of the current stablecoin reserve address. Callers can mint new tokens by depositing stablecoins, redeem tokens for stablecoins, and transfer tokens to other accounts. Only a designated operator can update the stablecoin reserve address, and only the owner can pause all minting and redemption operations. The minimum minting amount is 100 stablecoins, and a 0.1% redemption fee is applied to all redemptions. The contract announces token transfers, minting events, and redemption events.
