This contract is a liquid staking vault for a base asset, holding deposited base assets and issuing liquid staking tokens representing a claim on those assets plus accrued yield. It maintains a total supply of liquid staking tokens, a total amount of base assets deposited, and per-account balances of liquid staking tokens. Callers can deposit base assets to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw base assets by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim rewards from their staked position. A designated operator can initiate withdrawals from an underlying yield-generating protocol and deposit earned rewards back into the vault. The owner can set the operator address and adjust the withdrawal fee, which is capped at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount. The contract emits an event when base assets are deposited, when base assets are withdrawn, and when rewards are added.
