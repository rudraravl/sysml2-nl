This contract facilitates the launch of new tokens via a bonding curve, holding the newly issued tokens and the underlying collateral until the launch concludes. It maintains a record of each token launch's current bonding curve parameters, the total amount of collateral deposited, and the amount of new tokens minted for each participant. Callers can initiate a new token launch, deposit collateral to purchase new tokens, and claim their purchased tokens once the launch is complete. Only a designated operator can approve new token launches and set the initial bonding curve parameters. Each token launch has a hard cap of 10,000,000 new tokens that can be minted, and a 0.5% fee on all collateral deposits is directed to the protocol treasury. The contract announces events for new token launches, collateral deposits, and token claims.
