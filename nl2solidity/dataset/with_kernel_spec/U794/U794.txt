This contract manages a token-pegged algorithmic stablecoin system, holding a reserve of a base asset to back the stablecoin. It maintains the current supply of the stablecoin, the amount of the base asset held in reserve, and a record of each user's stablecoin balance. Callers can mint stablecoins by depositing the base asset, redeem stablecoins for the base asset, and transfer stablecoins to other users. A designated operator can adjust the target peg ratio and initiate emergency shutdowns. The minimum minting amount is 100 base units, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all redemption operations. The contract announces stablecoin minting events, stablecoin burning events, and transfers of stablecoins.
