This contract facilitates the launch of new tokens, holding deposited funds and the newly minted tokens for distribution. It maintains a record of each launch's configuration, including the token being launched, its total supply, the fundraising goal, and individual participant contributions. Callers can deposit funds into an active launch, claim their allocated tokens after a successful launch, or withdraw their deposited funds if a launch fails. Only a designated operator can initiate a new token launch, set its parameters, or finalize a launch. Each launch has a minimum fundraising goal of 100,000 units of the designated stablecoin, and a 2% fee is applied to all successful fundraises, directed to a treasury address. The contract emits an event upon a new launch being created, and another when a participant successfully deposits funds.
