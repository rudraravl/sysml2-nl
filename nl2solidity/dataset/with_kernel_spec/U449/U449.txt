This contract is a pool-based lending facility that custodies supplied digital assets. It maintains records of individual user deposits, outstanding loans, collateral ratios, and global pool-specific configuration parameters like interest rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can supply assets to earn interest, borrow against their supplied collateral, repay loans, and withdraw their unencumbered deposits. A designated operator can adjust interest rate models and liquidation parameters. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrowed asset is 75%, and a 0.05% origination fee is applied to all new loans. The contract announces all supply, borrow, repay, and withdraw actions, including the affected user, asset, and amount.
