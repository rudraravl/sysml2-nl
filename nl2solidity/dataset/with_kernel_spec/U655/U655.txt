This contract manages the issuance and transfer of tokenized private company shares, custodying a registry of share ownership. It maintains a record of each shareholder's balance of each share class, along with a total supply for each share class and a configuration for each share class including its nominal value and transfer restrictions. Callers can subscribe to new share offerings, transfer shares to other approved holders, and redeem shares if a buyback is initiated. Only a designated issuer can create new share classes, mint new shares into circulation, approve or revoke transfer eligibility for specific addresses, and initiate share buybacks. Share transfers are subject to a 0.1% transfer fee, and new share subscriptions have a minimum investment of 100 units of the payment token. The contract announces share issuance events, share transfer events, and changes in share class configurations.
