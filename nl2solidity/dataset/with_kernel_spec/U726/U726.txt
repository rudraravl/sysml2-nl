This contract facilitates direct stablecoin payments between users and merchants, holding no funds itself. It maintains a record of each merchant's configured payment links, including the designated recipient address and the associated stablecoin and amount. Callers can create new payment links, update existing link details, and initiate payments through a link. Only the contract owner can set the platform fee percentage and designate new stablecoin addresses for use. A payment link must specify a stablecoin from an approved list, and the platform fee is fixed at 0.2% of the payment amount. The contract announces when a new payment link is created and when a payment is successfully processed.
