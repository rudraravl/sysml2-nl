This contract manages a gaming platform's reward system, custodying a fungible reward token. It maintains individual player balances of the reward token, a global pool of tokens available for distribution, and a configuration for various game parameters. Callers can play a game to earn tokens, spend tokens to mint unique digital collectibles, and stake those collectibles to earn additional yield. The owner can set the reward rate for playing games, adjust the staking yield percentage, and pause all token transfers in an emergency. Each game play costs 0.01 units of the native blockchain currency, and the maximum number of unique digital collectibles a single player can mint is 10. The contract announces when a player earns tokens, when a collectible is minted, and when a collectible is staked.
