This contract manages a yield-bearing stablecoin, holding underlying collateral tokens that back its value. It maintains a total supply of the stablecoin, the amount of collateral deposited by each user, and a global exchange rate between the stablecoin and its underlying collateral. Callers can deposit collateral to mint stablecoins, redeem stablecoins for the underlying collateral, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the global exchange rate and set a redemption fee. The redemption fee is capped at 0.5% of the redeemed amount, and the exchange rate can only be increased, never decreased. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and transfer events, along with any changes to the exchange rate or redemption fee.
