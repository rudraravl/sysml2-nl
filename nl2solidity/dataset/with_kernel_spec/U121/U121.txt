This contract manages user deposits of a stablecoin, enabling them to fund spending accounts for real-world purchases. It maintains individual user balances of deposited stablecoin, a total supply of spending account tokens, and a global fee percentage. Callers can deposit stablecoin, withdraw their stablecoin balance, and convert stablecoin to spending account tokens. Only a designated operator can mint new spending account tokens for users, burn spending account tokens, and update the global fee percentage. The maximum deposit per user is 10,000 stablecoins, and the fee for converting stablecoin to spending account tokens is fixed at 0.5%. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, and conversions to observers.
