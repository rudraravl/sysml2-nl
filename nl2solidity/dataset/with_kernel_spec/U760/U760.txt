This contract manages curated investment vaults, holding various ERC-20 tokens deposited by users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount per vault and the current configuration parameters for each vault, including its associated strategy. Callers can deposit tokens into an active vault, withdraw their principal from a vault, and claim any accrued rewards. A designated operator can update a vault's strategy, initiate a harvest of rewards, and roll over a vault's position to a new strategy. The owner can add new vaults, pause and unpause deposits for a specific vault, and set the operator. A vault must have a minimum deposit amount of 100 base units of its underlying token. All withdrawals incur a 0.5% fee, which is directed to a designated treasury address. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and strategy updates.
