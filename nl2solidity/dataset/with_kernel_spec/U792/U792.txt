This contract manages automated investment strategies, holding user-deposited Bitcoin-backed tokens. It maintains individual user balances for each strategy, a global configuration for active strategies, and records of current allocations within external DeFi protocols. Callers can deposit Bitcoin-backed tokens into a chosen strategy, withdraw their share from a strategy, and claim earned rewards. A designated operator can add new strategies, adjust strategy parameters, and initiate rebalancing operations across strategies. Each strategy has a maximum deposit cap of 1000 Bitcoin-backed tokens, and a 0.5% performance fee is applied to all earned rewards before distribution. All deposits and withdrawals emit an event detailing the user, strategy, and amount.
