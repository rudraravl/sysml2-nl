This contract manages a token representing fractional ownership of short-term US Treasury bills and reverse repurchase agreements, holding no assets directly but reflecting an off-chain portfolio. It maintains each account's current token balance and a global daily interest rate. Callers can mint new tokens by depositing US Dollars, redeem tokens for US Dollars, and transfer tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can update the daily interest rate and pause all transfers. The daily interest rate cannot exceed 0.001 (0.1%) and redemptions incur a 0.1% fee. The contract announces token transfers, minting events, redemption events, and daily interest rate updates.
