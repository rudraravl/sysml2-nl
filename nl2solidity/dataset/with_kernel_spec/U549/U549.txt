This contract facilitates a decentralized news market by enabling users to stake funds on the veracity of specific claims, acting as a truth bounty system. It custodies user deposits in an escrow for each claim. The contract maintains a record of each claim, including its current status (open, resolved true, resolved false), the total staked amount for and against the claim, and individual user stakes for each side. Callers can create new claims, stake funds on either the "true" or "false" outcome of an existing claim, withdraw their stake if a claim is unresolved, and claim rewards if their staked side is declared correct. A designated operator is privileged to resolve claims as either true or false, and to set the fee percentage for successful claims. New claims must have an initial staking period of at least 7 days. A 2% fee is deducted from the total rewards of correctly predicted claims, which is then distributed to the designated operator. The contract announces the creation of new claims, all staking actions, and the final resolution of claims, including the winning side and the total rewards distributed.
