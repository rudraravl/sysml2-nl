This contract facilitates a prediction market where users can bet on the outcome of future events, holding user stakes in escrow until event resolution. It maintains records of active markets, individual user positions within each market, and a global configuration including fees and administrative addresses. Callers can create new prediction markets, place bets on market outcomes, claim winnings from resolved markets, and withdraw their initial stakes from unresolved markets under specific conditions. A designated operator is exclusively permitted to resolve markets by declaring a winning outcome and to adjust the market creation fee. The market creation fee is fixed at 0.01 units of the native currency, and users cannot withdraw their stakes from a market within 24 hours of its scheduled resolution time. The contract emits events when a new market is created, a bet is placed, a market is resolved, and winnings are claimed.
