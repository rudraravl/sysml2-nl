This contract manages a sports prediction market, holding USDC deposits for active predictions. It maintains individual user balances, open prediction positions, and market configurations for each sports event. Callers can create new prediction markets, place bets on existing markets, claim winnings from settled markets, and withdraw their available USDC balance. A designated operator can settle markets by providing the final outcome, and only the owner can adjust the market creation fee or pause all betting activity. The market creation fee is fixed at 10 USDC, and a minimum of 5 USDC is required for any single bet. The contract emits an event when a new market is created, when a bet is placed, and when a market is settled.
