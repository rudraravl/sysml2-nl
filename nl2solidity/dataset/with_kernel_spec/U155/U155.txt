This contract facilitates cross-chain asset swaps without wrapped tokens, holding deposited assets in escrow until a swap is completed. It maintains a global configuration for swap parameters, a record of active swap requests including source and destination chain details, and a balance for each supported asset. Callers can initiate a swap by depositing funds, claim swapped funds after a successful cross-chain transfer, and cancel their own pending swap requests. A designated operator can register new supported assets, update swap fees, and finalize cross-chain swap outcomes. The maximum swap fee is 0.5% of the swapped value, and a swap request automatically expires if not finalized within 24 hours. The contract emits events for every initiated swap, successful swap completion, and cancelled swap.
