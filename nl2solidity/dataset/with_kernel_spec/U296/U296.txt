This contract manages a gamified savings pool for fitness challenges, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing earned yield. It maintains individual user balances, a total staked pool, weekly challenge progress for each participant, and a configurable challenge goal. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their principal, register weekly challenge completion, and claim their share of redistributed yield. A designated operator can set the weekly challenge goal, mark users as non-compliant, and initiate the weekly yield distribution. Each user must deposit a minimum of 100 stablecoins to participate, and the weekly challenge goal is a minimum of 5 kilometers. The contract emits an event when a user deposits, when a user withdraws, and when weekly yield is distributed.
