This contract serves as a decentralized money market, holding deposited collateral tokens and issuing corresponding debt tokens. It maintains per-account records of deposits and borrows, global pools of available liquidity for each supported asset, and configurable interest rate models. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can add new supported collateral and borrow assets, adjust interest rate parameters, and pause borrowing for specific assets. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 75%, and a 0.5% origination fee is charged on all new borrows. The contract announces deposit events, borrow events, and repayment events.
