This contract manages a strategic game where players stake fungible tokens into designated country vaults, forming alliances and competing in recurring elimination epochs. It maintains a record of each player's staked token balance per country, the current epoch number, and the total token supply held within each country vault. Players can stake tokens into an available country vault, transfer their staked tokens between allied country vaults, and withdraw their staked tokens from any country vault they are part of. A designated operator can initiate a new epoch, declare a country eliminated, and distribute a portion of the eliminated country's vault tokens to its conquerors. Staking into a country vault requires a minimum of 100 tokens. A 5% fee is applied to all token withdrawals, which is directed to a treasury address. The contract emits an event when tokens are staked, transferred, or withdrawn, and when a country is eliminated.
