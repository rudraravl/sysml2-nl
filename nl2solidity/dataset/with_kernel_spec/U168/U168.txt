This contract is a bridge for a wrapped Bitcoin token, custoding native Bitcoin deposits in an off-chain vault and issuing an equivalent amount of wrapped tokens on-chain. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped token and individual balances for each account, along with a mapping of Bitcoin deposit addresses to user accounts. Callers can deposit native Bitcoin to a generated address to mint wrapped tokens, transfer wrapped tokens to other accounts, and redeem wrapped tokens to receive native Bitcoin from the off-chain vault. A designated operator can pause all minting and redeeming operations, and can update the Bitcoin deposit address generation logic. The minimum amount of native Bitcoin that can be deposited or redeemed is 0.0001 BTC. A redemption fee of 0.1% is applied to all redeemed amounts. The contract announces token transfers, minting events, and redemption requests to observers.
