This contract manages token launches, holding deposited tokens from participants and newly minted project tokens. It maintains a record of each participant's deposited amount and their allocation of project tokens, along with the current launch phase and the total project tokens available for distribution. Callers can deposit tokens during the funding phase, claim their allocated project tokens during the distribution phase, and withdraw any unspent deposited tokens after the launch concludes. A designated operator can initiate new launches, set the project token address and total supply, advance the launch phase, and set the funding phase duration. The funding phase duration must be between 1 day and 7 days. A 2% fee on all deposited tokens is automatically transferred to a treasury address upon deposit. The contract announces the start and end of each launch phase, and when participants claim their project tokens.
