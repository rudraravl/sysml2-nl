This contract facilitates private transfers of the native blockchain currency by acting as a mixer, holding user deposits in a pool. It maintains a list of deposited commitment hashes and a nullifier set to prevent double-spending. Callers can deposit the native currency with a commitment hash, and later withdraw their deposited amount by providing a valid proof and a nullifier. The owner can update the fee percentage charged on withdrawals and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. A withdrawal fee of 0.1% of the withdrawn amount is enforced. Each deposit must be a minimum of 0.01 native currency. The contract emits an event upon successful deposits, withdrawals, and fee updates.
