This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding user collateral in various tokens. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and supported collateral types. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close leveraged positions, adjust margin on existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update global configuration parameters, liquidate undercollateralized positions, and pause trading in emergencies. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x, and a 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position openings and closings. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, position closings, and liquidations.
