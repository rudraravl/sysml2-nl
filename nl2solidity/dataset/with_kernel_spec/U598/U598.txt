This contract manages a decentralized lending pool, holding deposited collateral tokens and issued loan tokens. It maintains individual loan positions, tracking collateral amounts, outstanding debt, and accrued interest for each borrower, alongside a global configuration for interest rates and liquidation parameters. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow loan tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can adjust the base interest rate and liquidation thresholds, and can initiate liquidations of undercollateralized positions. The maximum loan-to-value ratio is fixed at 75%, and a 0.5% liquidation fee is applied to all liquidated positions. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, including the affected accounts and amounts.
