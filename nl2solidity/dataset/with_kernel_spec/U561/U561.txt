This contract facilitates decentralized prediction markets, holding deposited collateral in escrow for active markets. It maintains records of open markets, participant positions within each market, and the total collateral locked per market, alongside a global configuration for fees. Callers can create new markets, place bets on market outcomes, claim winnings from resolved markets, and withdraw their unspent collateral. A designated operator is privileged to resolve market outcomes and update the global fee percentage. A market creation fee of 0.5% of the initial collateral must be paid in the market's collateral token. The maximum number of outcomes for any market is 10. The contract announces market creation, bet placement, market resolution, and winning claims to observers.
