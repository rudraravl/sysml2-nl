This contract facilitates the trading of unique digital collectibles representing vaulted physical assets, holding no fungible tokens itself but tracking ownership of these non-fungible tokens. It maintains individual records for each collectible, including its current owner, a listing status, and an optional sale price, alongside a global configuration for platform fees. Callers can list a collectible for sale, purchase a listed collectible, delist a collectible they own, and transfer ownership of a collectible to another account. Only a designated operator can set the platform fee percentage and withdraw accumulated fees. The platform fee is fixed at 2.5% of the sale price for every successful purchase, and a collectible cannot be listed for sale if it is already actively listed. The contract announces collectible listings, purchases, and transfers to observers.
