This contract facilitates decentralized token exchanges and liquidity provision, holding deposited tokens within its pools. It maintains records of each liquidity provider's deposited amounts and their corresponding shares in specific liquidity pools, along with global configuration settings for fees and protocol parameters. Callers can deposit tokens to provide liquidity, withdraw their liquidity, swap tokens between supported pairs, and claim accrued trading fees. A designated administrator can set the base trading fee, add new token pairs, and update the protocol's treasury address. The maximum trading fee is 0.5% of the swapped amount, and a minimum of 100 base tokens must be deposited to create a new liquidity pool. The contract emits events for every token swap, liquidity deposit, and liquidity withdrawal, detailing the tokens involved and their quantities.
