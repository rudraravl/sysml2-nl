This contract is for a synthetic dollar, which is a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar, and it custodies all of the underlying collateral backing the issued tokens. It maintains the total supply of issued tokens, individual account balances, and a record of approved allowances for token transfers. Callers can mint new tokens by depositing approved collateral, burn tokens to redeem collateral, transfer tokens to other accounts, and approve other accounts to spend their tokens. A designated operator is privileged to pause all token transfers and minting, and to unpause them. The contract enforces a minimum collateralization ratio of 100% for all issued tokens, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all token redemptions. The contract announces when tokens are transferred, when tokens are minted, and when tokens are burned.
