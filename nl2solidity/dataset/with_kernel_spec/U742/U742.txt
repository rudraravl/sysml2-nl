This contract manages a novel stablecoin system, holding a reserve of a base asset to back a newly minted stablecoin. It maintains the current reserve ratio, the total supply of the stablecoin, and individual account balances for both the stablecoin and any base asset deposited. Callers can mint stablecoins by depositing the base asset, redeem stablecoins for the base asset, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the target reserve ratio and pause or unpause minting and redemption operations. The initial target reserve ratio is 100%, and the redemption fee is 0.5% of the stablecoins redeemed. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and transfer events, including the amounts and involved accounts.
