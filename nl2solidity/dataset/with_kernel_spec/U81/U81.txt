This contract is a yield aggregator that custodies user-deposited ERC-20 tokens. It maintains individual user balances for each deposited token and tracks the total amount of each token held within the contract. Callers can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim earned yield. A designated operator can add new supported ERC-20 tokens and update the underlying yield-generating strategies for each token. The contract imposes a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all withdrawals, and a maximum of 10 distinct ERC-20 tokens can be supported at any given time. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, including the user's address, the token involved, and the amount.
