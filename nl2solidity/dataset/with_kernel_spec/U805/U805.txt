This contract is a cross-chain bridge for transferring a specific wrapped asset between two networks, holding custody of the wrapped asset on this network. It maintains a record of each user's pending withdrawals and a global counter for total wrapped assets bridged. Callers can initiate a transfer of wrapped assets to the other network, claim wrapped assets received from the other network, and cancel a pending withdrawal before it is finalized. A designated operator can approve a transfer from the other network and finalize a withdrawal to the other network. The maximum amount that can be transferred in a single transaction is 1000 wrapped assets. A 0.1% fee is charged on all transfers initiated from this network. The contract announces when a transfer is initiated, when a transfer is approved, and when a withdrawal is finalized.
