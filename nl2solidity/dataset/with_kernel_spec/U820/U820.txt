This contract facilitates decentralized token swaps and custodies user-provided liquidity in token pairs. It maintains a record of each liquidity pool, including the reserves for each token in the pair, the total outstanding liquidity tokens for that pool, and each user's balance of liquidity tokens. Callers can add liquidity to a pool, remove liquidity from a pool, and swap one token for another within a pool. A designated operator can set the swap fee percentage, which defaults to 0.3% and cannot exceed 1%. The contract announces liquidity additions, liquidity removals, and token swaps, including the tokens involved and the amounts.
