This contract manages the tokenization of real-world assets, specifically fractional ownership of real estate properties, and custodies the underlying asset tokens. It maintains a record of each property's total token supply, the current owner of each token, and a mapping of property identifiers to their associated legal documentation hashes. Callers can purchase fractional property tokens, transfer their owned tokens to another address, and redeem tokens for their underlying real-world asset share upon property sale. Only a designated operator can register new properties, mint new tokens for a property, or initiate a property sale. A maximum of 10,000 tokens can be minted for any single property. A 0.5% fee is applied to all token transfers, payable to the contract owner. The contract announces property registration events, token transfer events, and property sale initiation events to observers.
