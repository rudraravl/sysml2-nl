This contract facilitates a non-custodial, fee-free native staking mechanism for a base layer asset, allowing users to delegate their assets to a set of validators without relinquishing control. It maintains a registry of user-initiated staking requests, each linked to a specific validator, and tracks the total amount of the base asset delegated to each validator. Callers can initiate a new delegation, request to undelegate their assets, or claim their undelegated assets after a waiting period. A designated operator can add or remove validators from the approved list, and set the minimum delegation amount for any new delegation. The minimum delegation amount is fixed at 100 base assets. Undelegated assets become claimable after a 7-day cooldown period. The contract emits an event when a new delegation is initiated, when an undelegation request is made, and when undelegated assets are successfully claimed.
