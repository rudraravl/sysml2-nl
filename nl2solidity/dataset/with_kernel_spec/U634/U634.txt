This contract manages a basket of diverse tokens, automatically rebalancing their proportions to maintain a target composition. It maintains the current token balances for each user's deposited basket, the target proportion for each token in the basket, and a global rebalancing fee. Callers can deposit tokens into a basket, withdraw their proportional share of tokens from a basket, and trigger a rebalance operation. Only a designated operator can adjust the target token proportions and the rebalancing fee. The rebalancing fee is set to 0.5% of the value rebalanced, and the contract must hold at least 100 units of the primary base token before a rebalance can be initiated. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, and rebalance completions, including the tokens involved and their quantities.
