This contract manages a stablecoin system, holding collateral assets and issuing stablecoins pegged to various foreign exchange rates. It maintains a global configuration for each stablecoin type, including its collateral ratio and associated oracle, and tracks each user's stablecoin balances and collateral deposits. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing approved collateral, redeem stablecoins for their underlying collateral, transfer stablecoins to other accounts, and swap one type of stablecoin for another. A designated operator can pause minting and redemption for all stablecoins, and the owner can update the collateral ratio for a specific stablecoin type. The minimum collateral ratio for any stablecoin is 110%, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all stablecoin swaps. The contract emits an event whenever stablecoins are minted, redeemed, or transferred, and when a collateral ratio is updated.
