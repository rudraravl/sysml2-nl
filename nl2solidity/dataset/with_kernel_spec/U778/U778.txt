This contract manages a pooled investment fund, holding various ERC-20 tokens deposited by participants. It maintains a record of each participant's share balance within the fund and the total amount of each held asset. Participants can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens to receive fund shares, redeem their shares for a proportional amount of the underlying assets, and view their current share balance. A designated fund manager can add or remove supported ERC-20 tokens, adjust the fund's investment strategy, and initiate rebalancing operations. A 0.5% fee is applied to all redemptions, and a maximum of 10 supported tokens can be held in the fund at any time. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit or redemption occurs, detailing the participant, amount, and token involved.
