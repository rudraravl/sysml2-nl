This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers by holding wrapped tokens on the source chain and releasing native tokens on the destination chain, or vice-versa, effectively custodying wrapped tokens. It maintains a record of each user's wrapped token balances, a total supply of wrapped tokens, and a global transfer fee percentage. Callers can deposit native tokens to mint wrapped tokens, burn wrapped tokens to withdraw native tokens, and initiate a cross-chain transfer by burning wrapped tokens on one chain to trigger a release on another. A designated operator can approve cross-chain transfer requests and update the transfer fee. The maximum transfer fee is 0.5% of the transferred amount. All cross-chain transfer requests must be approved by the designated operator within 24 hours of initiation. The contract announces when tokens are deposited, withdrawn, or transferred across chains, including the sender, recipient, amount, and any associated fees.
