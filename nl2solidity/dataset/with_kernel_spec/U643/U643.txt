This contract manages the issuance and redemption of custom debt instruments, holding deposited collateral tokens until maturity or default. It maintains records for each issued bond, including its principal amount, collateral type, interest rate, and maturity date, along with a global configuration for supported collateral types and fees. Callers can issue new bonds, deposit collateral, withdraw collateral if the bond is repaid, and claim defaulted collateral. A designated operator can pause or unpause the contract and update the list of supported collateral tokens. The maximum number of collateral tokens supported is 10, and a 0.1% issuance fee is applied to the principal of each new bond. The contract emits an event upon bond issuance, collateral deposit, and bond redemption.
