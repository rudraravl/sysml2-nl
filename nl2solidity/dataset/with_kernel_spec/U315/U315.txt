This contract is a yield-generating vault that custodies deposited tokens. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount and the total shares issued, along with a global configuration for allowed investment strategies. Callers can deposit tokens into the vault, withdraw their principal and accrued yield, and claim any available rewards. Only a designated operator can initiate rebalancing of funds across approved investment strategies, and only the owner can approve new investment strategies or adjust the management fee. A minimum deposit of 100 tokens is enforced, and a 0.5% management fee is applied to all yield generated. The contract announces deposit and withdrawal events, including the user's address and the amount involved.
