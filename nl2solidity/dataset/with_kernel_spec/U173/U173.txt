This contract establishes and manages non-custodial vaults for executing automated trading strategies, holding user-deposited base tokens and strategy-generated profit tokens. It maintains a record of each vault's current strategy, its allocated capital, its profit and loss, and a global registry of approved trading strategies and their associated parameters. Callers can create a new vault, deposit base tokens into an existing vault, withdraw their share of vault tokens, and initiate a strategy execution cycle. A designated operator can approve new trading strategies, update the parameters of existing strategies, and pause or unpause the entire system. A vault creation requires a minimum initial deposit of 100 base tokens. A 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, directed to a designated treasury address. The contract announces the creation of new vaults, all deposits and withdrawals, and any changes to strategy parameters.
