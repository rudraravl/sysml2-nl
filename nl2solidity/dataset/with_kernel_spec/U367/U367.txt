This contract facilitates anonymous transfers of a single, specified ERC-20 token by allowing users to deposit tokens and later withdraw them to a different address without linking the two. It maintains a list of unspent commitment hashes, a list of spent nullifier hashes, and a configurable fee percentage for withdrawals. Callers can deposit tokens by providing a commitment hash, and they can withdraw tokens by providing a valid proof, a nullifier hash, and a recipient address. The owner can update the withdrawal fee percentage and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The withdrawal fee is fixed at 1% of the withdrawn amount, and the maximum deposit amount is 1000 of the specified ERC-20 token. The contract announces the commitment hash upon a successful deposit and the nullifier hash upon a successful withdrawal.
