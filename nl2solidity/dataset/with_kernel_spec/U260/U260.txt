This contract facilitates fair token launches by holding deposited funds and newly minted tokens in escrow until a launch concludes. It maintains a registry of active launch campaigns, each with its own pool of deposited funds, allocated tokens, and participant contributions, alongside a global configuration for fees and operational parameters. Callers can initiate new launch campaigns, contribute funds to open campaigns, claim their allocated tokens and any refunded contributions after a campaign ends, and withdraw their initial campaign creation deposit. A designated owner can set the global fee percentage, pause or unpause the contract, and designate operators who can approve or reject new launch campaigns. Each launch campaign requires a minimum of 100 units of the base currency to be deposited by the creator, and the contract charges a 2% fee on all successfully raised funds, deducted before tokens are distributed. The contract emits an event whenever a new launch campaign is created, funds are contributed, or tokens are claimed.
