This contract manages a staking pool for a single fungible token, allowing users to lock their tokens for rewards. It maintains individual user stake balances, reward accruals, and chosen lock-up periods, along with a global reward rate and a penalty schedule. Callers can deposit tokens to stake, claim accumulated rewards, and initiate withdrawals of their staked principal. A designated operator can adjust the reward rate and the penalty schedule. The minimum lock-up period is 30 days, and early withdrawals incur a 10% penalty on the principal. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal requests, and reward claims.
