This contract facilitates peer-to-peer predictions on real-world events, holding deposited funds in escrow until event resolution. It maintains a record of each event, including its potential outcomes, the total funds staked on each outcome, and the event's current status (open, closed, resolved). Callers can create new prediction events, place a bet on an outcome for an existing event, cancel an open bet they placed, and claim winnings once an event is resolved. A designated operator is exclusively allowed to close an event to further bets and to resolve an event by declaring the winning outcome. The minimum bet amount is 0.01 ether. A 1% fee is deducted from all winning payouts. The contract announces new event creations, bet placements, bet cancellations, and event resolutions, including the winning outcome and total payout.
