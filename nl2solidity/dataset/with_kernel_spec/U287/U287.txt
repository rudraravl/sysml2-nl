This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding user collateral in various approved tokens. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters for each trading pair. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close positions, adjust leverage, and withdraw their available balance. Only a designated operator can list new trading pairs, adjust trading fees, or initiate emergency shutdowns. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 20x, and a fixed trading fee of 0.05% is applied to all executed trades. The contract emits events upon every deposit, withdrawal, position opening, and position closing, detailing the affected account, asset, and amount.
