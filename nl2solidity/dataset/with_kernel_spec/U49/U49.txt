This contract manages fractionalized ownership shares of real estate assets, holding no direct assets itself but representing claims on off-chain property. It maintains a record of each user's total shares across all properties, along with a registry of all tokenized properties and their current valuations. Callers can purchase fractional shares in available properties, sell their existing fractional shares, and transfer fractional shares to other users. A designated administrator can add new properties to the registry, update the valuation of existing properties, and pause all share trading for a specific property. The minimum purchase amount for any property share is 0.01 units. A 0.5% fee is applied to all share sale transactions. The contract announces when new properties are added, when property valuations are updated, and when shares are purchased, sold, or transferred.
