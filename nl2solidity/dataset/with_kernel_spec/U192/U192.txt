This contract manages the creation, distribution, and opening of digital mystery boxes, holding deposited tokens and non-fungible tokens (NFTs) as potential box contents. It maintains a registry of defined box types, each with its probability distribution of contents, and tracks the quantity of each box type owned by individual users. Callers can purchase boxes using an accepted token, open owned boxes to receive a random item, and claim any physical assets associated with opened boxes. Only a designated operator can define new box types, add items to existing box types, deposit tokens and NFTs into the contract for box contents, and withdraw unallocated assets. A box purchase costs 100 units of the accepted token, and the probability distribution for items within a box must sum to exactly 10,000 basis points. The contract announces when a box type is created, when a box is purchased, and when a box is opened revealing its contents.
