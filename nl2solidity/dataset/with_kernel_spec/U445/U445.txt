This contract facilitates leveraged long and short positions on an underlying asset, holding the collateral for these positions. It maintains individual position records, each tracking the collateral amount, the leverage ratio, the position's direction (long or short), and its opening price. Callers can open new positions, add collateral to existing positions, close positions, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can update the external price oracle's address and adjust the liquidation threshold. New positions require a minimum collateral of 0.01 units of the base asset, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all position opening and closing operations. The contract announces position openings, closures, and liquidations, including the position identifier and relevant financial details.
