This contract manages a decentralized reserve currency protocol, holding various reserve assets to back a native stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin and various staked derivatives, a global treasury of reserve assets, and configurable parameters for bonding and staking. Callers can mint the stablecoin by depositing approved reserve assets, stake their stablecoin for yield, unstake their staked stablecoin, and claim rewards. A designated operator can adjust bonding terms, add or remove approved reserve assets, and initiate treasury disbursements. The maximum annual inflation rate for the stablecoin is capped at 10%, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin transfers. The contract emits events for all stablecoin transfers, staking and unstaking operations, and changes to treasury assets.
