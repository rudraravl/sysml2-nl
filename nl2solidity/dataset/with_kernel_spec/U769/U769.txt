This contract is for a decentralized identity registry, custodying unique identity tokens that represent verified human identities. It maintains a record for each registered identity, storing its unique identifier and a timestamp of its last verification, alongside a global count of total registered identities. Callers can register a new identity by providing proof of uniqueness, update their identity's verification timestamp, and query the verification status of any identity. A designated operator can pause new identity registrations and unpause them, and can also revoke an identity if it is found to be fraudulent. New identity registrations are limited to 100 per day, and a successful registration emits an event containing the new identity's unique identifier and the registration timestamp. The contract announces identity registration, identity verification updates, and identity revocations to observers.
