This contract facilitates peer-to-peer cryptocurrency to fiat currency swaps, holding cryptocurrency deposits in escrow during the swap process. It maintains individual user balances for deposited cryptocurrency, a global configuration for swap parameters, and a list of active swap orders. Callers can deposit cryptocurrency, create new swap orders specifying a fiat amount and desired cryptocurrency price, accept existing swap orders, confirm fiat receipt for an accepted order, and withdraw their cryptocurrency deposits. Only a designated operator can adjust the global swap fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of the cryptocurrency amount, and set the maximum allowed cryptocurrency deposit per order, which is capped at 1000 units of the deposited cryptocurrency. The contract announces when new swap orders are created, when orders are accepted, and when fiat receipt is confirmed.
