This contract manages a content creator's social token, enabling fans to purchase and trade shares representing access to exclusive content. It maintains individual fan balances of the creator's social token, a total supply of the token, and a bonding curve configuration for pricing. Callers can purchase tokens from the bonding curve, sell tokens back to the bonding curve, and transfer tokens to other fans. The creator is privileged and can set the initial bonding curve parameters and withdraw accumulated protocol fees. A 5% protocol fee is applied to all token purchases and sales, and the total supply of the creator's social token is capped at 1,000,000 units. The contract announces when tokens are purchased, sold, or transferred.
