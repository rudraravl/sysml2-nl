This contract manages a system for issuing and redeeming portable tokens backed by isolated credit positions, holding no custodied assets itself. It maintains records of each user's credit limit, their current outstanding balance, and the total supply of issued tokens. Callers can request to mint new tokens up to their available credit limit, repay outstanding balances to reduce their debt, and redeem tokens to decrease their outstanding balance. A designated operator can adjust individual user credit limits and pause or unpause the entire token issuance and redemption system. The maximum credit limit for any single user is 1,000,000 units of the underlying credit, and a 0.1% fee is applied to each token minting operation. The contract announces events for token mints, token redemptions, and credit limit adjustments.
