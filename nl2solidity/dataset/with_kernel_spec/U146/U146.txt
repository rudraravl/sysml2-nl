This contract manages a competitive gaming platform, holding deposited game tokens and non-fungible hero tokens. It maintains individual player records including hero token ownership, hero statistics, equipped item identifiers, and accumulated training points, alongside global configuration for battle schedules and reward distribution. Callers can deposit game tokens, register hero tokens, equip items to their heroes, allocate training points, enter heroes into battles, and claim earned rewards. A designated operator can initiate battles, determine battle outcomes, and distribute rewards based on results. The contract enforces a maximum of 5 equipped items per hero and a 2% fee on all claimed rewards, deducted at the time of claim. The contract announces battle commencements, battle results, and reward distributions to observers.
