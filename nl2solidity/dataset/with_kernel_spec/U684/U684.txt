This contract establishes a peer-to-peer lending market for any pair of ERC-20 tokens, holding deposited tokens as collateral and lent tokens awaiting repayment. It maintains records of each borrower's outstanding debt, collateral, and accrued interest, as well as global pool balances for each token and market configuration parameters. Callers can deposit tokens to lend, borrow tokens against collateral, repay outstanding loans, withdraw available liquidity, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can adjust market parameters like interest rates and collateralization ratios, and pause certain market operations in emergencies. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any loan is 80%, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all loan originations, payable in the borrowed token. The contract announces all loan originations, repayments, liquidations, and parameter changes to observers.
