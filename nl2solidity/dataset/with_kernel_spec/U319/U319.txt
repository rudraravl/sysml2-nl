This contract is a tokenized real-world asset representing ownership of physical gold, and it custodies no assets directly, but rather tracks balances representing gold held by a custodian. It maintains individual account balances for the token, a total supply, and a configurable transfer fee percentage. Callers can transfer tokens to other accounts, approve spending by other accounts, and burn their own tokens. Only a designated operator can mint new tokens, pause all transfers, or unpause transfers. A transfer fee of 0.1% is applied to all transfers, and the maximum total supply of tokens is capped at 1,000,000,000. The contract announces token transfers, approvals, minting, and burning events to observers.
