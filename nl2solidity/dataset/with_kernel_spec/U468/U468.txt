This contract is a marketplace aggregator for non-fungible tokens representing vote-escrowed positions, holding deposited non-fungible tokens in escrow. It maintains a global configuration for marketplace fees, a record of all listed non-fungible tokens including their current asking price, and a record of all outstanding global buy offers for specific non-fungible token types. Callers can list their non-fungible tokens for sale, make global buy offers for any non-fungible token type, accept an offer for their listed non-fungible token, and withdraw their non-fungible tokens if unlisted or their offer funds if unmatched. The owner can set the marketplace fee percentage and designate operators who can pause or unpause trading activity. The marketplace fee is fixed at 0.5% of the sale price, and global buy offers must be at least 0.01 ether. The contract announces when a non-fungible token is listed, when a global buy offer is made, and when a sale is completed.
