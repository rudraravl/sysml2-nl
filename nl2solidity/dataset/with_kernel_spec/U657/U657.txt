This contract manages the minting of 10,000 unique digital collectibles, each represented by an on-chain SVG image and associated metadata, and does not custody any fungible tokens. It maintains a record of which collectibles have been minted, the current total supply, and a mapping of collectible identifiers to their current owner. Callers can mint a new collectible, transferring ownership to their account, and can retrieve the SVG image and metadata for any minted collectible. A designated operator can set the base URI for the collectible metadata and can pause or unpause the minting process. The total supply of collectibles is capped at 10,000, and a maximum of 5 collectibles can be minted per transaction. The contract announces when a new collectible has been minted, including its unique identifier and the new owner.
