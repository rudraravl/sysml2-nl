This contract manages the creation and distribution of unique in-game items, specifically weapon skins, and holds the underlying digital assets representing these skins. It maintains a registry of all minted weapon skins, including their unique identifiers, current owner, and a set of immutable attributes like rarity and visual effects. Callers can mint new weapon skins, transfer ownership of their owned skins to another address, and burn their owned skins. A designated operator is privileged to mint new weapon skins, set the maximum supply for each skin type, and pause all transfer operations. The maximum supply for any single weapon skin type is capped at 10,000 units, and a 2% fee is applied to all successful transfers, payable to the contract owner. The contract announces the minting of new skins, the transfer of skin ownership, and the burning of skins to observers.
