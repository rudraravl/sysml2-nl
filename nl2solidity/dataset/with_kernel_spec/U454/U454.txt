This contract manages tokenized real-world assets, specifically fixed-income debt instruments, and custodies the underlying stablecoin collateral. It maintains a record of each user's bond holdings, the total outstanding tokenized bonds, and a configuration for accepted collateral types. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint new bond tokens, transfer bond tokens to other accounts, and redeem bond tokens for the underlying stablecoin collateral. A designated operator can pause minting and redemption, and the owner can set the collateralization ratio and update the list of approved collateral tokens. The collateralization ratio for minting new bonds is fixed at 100%, and a 0.1% redemption fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces events for bond token minting, redemption, and transfers.
