This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers by holding wrapped tokens in escrow on the source chain until they are minted on the destination chain. It maintains a record of each pending transfer, including the destination chain identifier, recipient address, asset type, and amount, along with a global configuration for fees and supported chains. Callers can initiate a transfer by depositing wrapped tokens, cancel a pending transfer if it has not been processed, and claim refunded tokens from a failed transfer. Only a designated operator can mark a transfer as completed, refund a transfer, or update the list of supported chains and their associated transfer fees. Each transfer incurs a flat fee of 0.05 wrapped tokens, and a transfer must be claimed within 7 days of being refunded or the funds are returned to the contract's treasury. The contract emits an event upon the initiation of a new transfer, the completion of a transfer, and the refunding of a transfer.
