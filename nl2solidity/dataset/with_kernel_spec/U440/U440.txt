This contract manages a stablecoin pegged to a fiat currency, holding a reserve of a major cryptocurrency to back its value. It maintains individual stablecoin balances for each user, a total reserve balance of the backing cryptocurrency, and a global configuration for fees and parameters. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing the backing cryptocurrency, redeem stablecoins for the backing cryptocurrency, and transfer stablecoins between accounts. Only a designated operator can adjust the stablecoin's peg ratio to the backing cryptocurrency and set the maximum daily minting limit. The minting fee is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited amount, and the maximum daily minting volume is capped at 1,000,000 stablecoins. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
