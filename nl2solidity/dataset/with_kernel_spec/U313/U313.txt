This contract manages a game where participants contribute funds to a growing prize pool, with a single winner claiming a portion of the pot. It custodies the contributed funds, denominated in the native blockchain currency. The contract maintains the current total pot size, the current game round number, the address of the last participant to contribute, and a timestamp for when the current round began. Callers can contribute to the pot, extending the round timer, and claim the prize if they are the last contributor when the round ends. A designated operator can set the minimum contribution amount and the duration of the round timer extension. The minimum contribution to the pot is 0.01 native currency. The round timer extends by 10 minutes with each valid contribution. The contract announces the start of a new round, a new contribution, and the winner of a round.
