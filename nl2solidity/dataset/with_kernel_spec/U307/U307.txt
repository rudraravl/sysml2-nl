This contract facilitates the transfer of a specific fungible token between an origin chain and a destination chain, holding deposited tokens in escrow on the origin chain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balance awaiting withdrawal on the destination chain. Callers can deposit tokens on the origin chain, initiating a transfer, and later claim their tokens on the destination chain once the transfer is finalized. A designated operator can finalize transfers by providing a signed message attesting to the deposit on the origin chain, and can also set the fee for transfers. The transfer fee is fixed at 0.0005 of the transferred amount, and a minimum deposit of 0.01 tokens is required for any transfer. The contract announces a deposit event when tokens are received and a withdrawal event when tokens are disbursed.
