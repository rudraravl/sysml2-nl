This contract manages a decentralized lottery where users predict the outcome of a random number. It custodies user deposits in the native blockchain currency. The contract maintains a record of each user's current prediction, their deposited amount, and the total prize pool for the current round. Callers can deposit funds to make a prediction, withdraw their prediction and deposit before the round closes, and claim their winnings after a round concludes. Only a designated operator can initiate a new round, set the winning number for a closed round, and withdraw a portion of the prize pool as a platform fee. A minimum deposit of 0.01 native currency is required for each prediction. The platform fee is fixed at 5% of the total prize pool. The contract announces when a new round begins, when a round closes, and the winning number for a concluded round.
