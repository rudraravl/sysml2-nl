This contract is a vault for aggregating multiple liquid restaking tokens (LRTs) into a single, diversified position. It maintains a record of each user's deposited LRTs, the total amount of each LRT held in the vault, and the current configuration parameters. Callers can deposit supported LRTs into the vault, withdraw their deposited LRTs, and redeem their share of the aggregated position for underlying LRTs. Only a designated operator can add new supported LRTs, update the redemption fee, or pause deposits. The vault charges a 0.5% fee on all redemptions, and a maximum of 10 different LRTs can be supported at any given time. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, redemptions, and changes to the supported LRTs or redemption fee.
