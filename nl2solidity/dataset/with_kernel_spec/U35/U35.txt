This contract facilitates collateralized lending and borrowing of various fungible tokens, holding deposited collateral tokens and borrowed tokens in escrow. It maintains individual account records for each user's deposits, borrows, and collateralization ratios, along with global pools for each supported asset and configuration parameters like interest rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw excess collateral; designated liquidators can liquidate undercollateralized positions. The contract owner can add or remove supported collateral and borrow tokens, adjust interest rate models, and set liquidation parameters. A borrower's collateralization ratio must always exceed 120% to avoid liquidation, and a liquidation fee of 5% of the liquidated amount is applied. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, including the affected user, asset, and amount.
