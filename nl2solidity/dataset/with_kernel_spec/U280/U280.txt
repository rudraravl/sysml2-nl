This contract manages a decentralized options trading platform, holding collateral tokens deposited by users and minted option tokens. It maintains individual user balances of collateral and option tokens, global pools of available collateral, and configuration parameters for each option type. Callers can deposit collateral, mint option tokens against their deposited collateral, exercise option tokens, and withdraw their unencumbered collateral. A designated operator can create new option types, adjust margin requirements, and pause or unpause trading for specific option types. The contract enforces a maximum collateralization ratio of 150% for minting new options and charges a 0.1% fee on all option exercise operations, payable in the underlying collateral token. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, option mints, and option exercises, including the user's identifier, the token amount, and the option type.
