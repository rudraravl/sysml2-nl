This contract manages a decentralized marketplace for staked Ether liquidity, holding deposited Ether and various liquid staking derivative tokens. It maintains a registry of approved liquid staking derivative tokens, per-user balances of deposited Ether and earned rewards, and a global record of the total Ether allocated to each liquid staking derivative. Callers can deposit Ether, withdraw their deposited Ether and accumulated rewards, and swap their accumulated rewards for other liquid staking derivative tokens. A designated operator can add new liquid staking derivative tokens to the approved list, update the allocation of deposited Ether to different liquid staking derivatives, and set the platform fee. The platform charges a 0.5% fee on all reward swaps, and a minimum of 1 Ether must be deposited at a time. The contract emits events when Ether is deposited, withdrawn, rewards are swapped, and when the allocation of Ether to a liquid staking derivative changes.
