This contract serves as a secure, on-chain registry for user-defined spending policies, enabling automated transaction execution and custodying no assets directly. It maintains a record of each user's configured policies, including associated spending limits and designated beneficiary addresses. Callers can create new policies, update existing policy parameters, and revoke policies entirely. A designated operator can pause all policy execution in an emergency, and only the owner can change the designated operator. Each policy must specify a maximum daily spending limit, which cannot exceed 10 ether, and a minimum of 24 hours must pass between any two updates to the same policy's spending limit. The contract announces the creation, update, and revocation of any spending policy.
