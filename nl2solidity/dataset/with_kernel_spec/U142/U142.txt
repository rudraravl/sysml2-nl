This contract enables liquid staking for a base asset, holding deposited base assets and issuing a corresponding liquid staking token. It maintains per-account records of deposited base assets and issued liquid staking tokens, along with a global exchange rate between the base asset and the liquid staking token. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive liquid staking tokens, and they can redeem liquid staking tokens for the base asset. A designated operator can update the exchange rate to reflect staking rewards and can pause deposits and redemptions. The initial exchange rate is 1 base asset per 1 liquid staking token, and a 0.5% redemption fee is applied to all redemptions. The contract announces deposit and redemption events, including the amount of base asset and liquid staking token involved.
