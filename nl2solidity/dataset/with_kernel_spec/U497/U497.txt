This contract is a multi-chain NFT marketplace and aggregator, holding no assets itself but facilitating the exchange of non-fungible tokens. It maintains a registry of listed NFTs with their asking prices, a record of active orders, and per-user trading statistics. Callers can list an NFT for sale, buy a listed NFT, cancel their own listings, and accept offers on their NFTs. A designated operator can set the marketplace fee and pause all trading activity. The marketplace fee is fixed at 0.5% of the sale price, and a listed NFT must have a minimum asking price of 0.001 ether. The contract announces successful NFT sales, new listings, and cancelled orders.
