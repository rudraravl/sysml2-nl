This is a perpetual futures trading contract, holding collateral deposited by traders and funds for the insurance pool. It maintains individual trader accounts with their collateral balances and open positions, a global insurance pool balance, and system-wide configuration parameters like funding rates and fees. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close long or short positions, add margin to existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update funding rates and adjust system parameters, and the owner can pause all trading operations. A maximum leverage of 50x is enforced for all positions, and a 0.1% trading fee is charged on each position's notional value at opening. The contract announces position openings and closings, and collateral deposits and withdrawals.
