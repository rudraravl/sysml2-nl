This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral deposited by users to back their trading positions. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each user on various trading pairs, and global configuration parameters such as funding rates and margin requirements. Callers can deposit collateral, open new positions, close existing positions, modify their leverage, and withdraw available collateral. Only a designated operator can adjust trading pair parameters, pause trading for specific pairs, or initiate emergency settlements. A minimum initial margin of 10% of the position value is required for all new positions, and a 0.05% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing actions. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, and position closures, including the user's address, the asset involved, and the quantity.
