This contract manages a decentralized social network's native token, facilitating user engagement and content creation. It maintains individual user balances, a global content creator fund, and configuration parameters for network operations. Callers can transfer tokens, stake tokens to support creators, claim rewards from staked tokens, and withdraw unstaked tokens. A designated administrator can adjust the staking reward rate, pause token transfers, and initiate upgrades. Staking rewards are distributed proportionally to staked amounts, with a minimum staking period of 7 days before rewards can be claimed. A 0.5% fee is applied to all token transfers, which is directed to the content creator fund. The contract announces token transfer events, staking events, and reward claim events.
