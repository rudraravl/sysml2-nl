This contract manages a decentralized, algorithmic stablecoin system, holding a reserve of a collateral asset to back the stablecoin. It maintains a total supply of the stablecoin, a collateral reserve balance, and a redemption rate for the stablecoin against the collateral. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing collateral, burn stablecoins to redeem collateral, and transfer stablecoins between accounts. A designated operator can adjust the redemption rate within a predefined range and pause or unpause minting and burning operations. The redemption rate can be adjusted by a maximum of 0.5% in any 24-hour period. A 0.1% fee is applied to all stablecoin redemption operations, payable in the collateral asset. The contract emits events for all minting, burning, and transfer operations, and for changes to the redemption rate.
