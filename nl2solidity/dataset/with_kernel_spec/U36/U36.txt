This contract manages user trading strategies and holds user-deposited tokens for automated execution. It maintains individual user balances, active strategy configurations including token pairs and trade parameters, and a global fee structure. Callers can deposit tokens, create new trading strategies, modify existing strategies, pause or resume their strategies, and withdraw their available balance. Only a designated operator can adjust the global trading fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of each successful trade, and pause all trading activity across the platform. A user's maximum number of active strategies is capped at 5. The contract announces strategy creations, modifications, and successful trade executions.
