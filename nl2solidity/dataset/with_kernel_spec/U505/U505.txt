This contract manages a vault for tokenized real-world collectibles, holding ERC-721 tokens as collateral for issued debt. It maintains individual debt positions for each borrower, a global interest rate, and a list of approved collateral types. Callers can deposit approved ERC-721 tokens as collateral, borrow stablecoins against their collateral, repay their outstanding debt, and withdraw their collateral once debt is fully repaid. A designated operator can adjust the global interest rate and add new approved ERC-721 collateral types. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any collateral type is 50%, and the interest rate cannot exceed 10% annually. The contract announces when a new loan is issued and when a loan is fully repaid.
