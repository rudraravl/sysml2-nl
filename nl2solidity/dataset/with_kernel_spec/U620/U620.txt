This contract manages a stablecoin system, holding collateral in a designated stable asset. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin, the collateral, and records of outstanding loans, along with system-wide configuration parameters like the collateralization ratio. Callers can deposit collateral, mint stablecoins against their collateral, repay loans, and withdraw excess collateral or stablecoins. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio and pause or unpause minting. The minimum collateralization ratio is 150%, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin minting operations. The contract announces stablecoin minting and burning events, and changes to the collateralization ratio.
