This contract issues a stablecoin that is collateralized by tokenized short-term government debt and money market fund shares. It maintains each account's stablecoin balance and the total supply of the stablecoin, along with a record of approved collateral tokens and their current valuation. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing approved collateral, burn stablecoins to redeem collateral, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. Only a designated operator can approve new collateral tokens, update the valuation of existing collateral, and pause or unpause minting and burning operations. The contract must always maintain a collateralization ratio of at least 100%, and a maximum of 1,000,000 stablecoins can be minted per day. The contract emits events for all minting, burning, and transfer actions, as well as for changes in collateral approval or valuation.
