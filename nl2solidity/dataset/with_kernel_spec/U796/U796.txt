This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers by holding wrapped tokens on the source chain and releasing native tokens on the destination chain, or vice-versa. It maintains a record of deposited amounts for each user, a total supply of wrapped tokens, and a configuration for each supported token including its destination chain identifier and associated bridge agent. Callers can initiate a transfer by depositing tokens, redeem tokens on a destination chain after a successful transfer, and claim refunds for failed transfers. A designated operator can pause and unpause transfers for specific tokens, and update the bridge agent for a given token. The contract imposes a 0.1% fee on all transfers, and a minimum transfer amount of 100 units for any supported token. The contract emits an event upon successful deposit, successful redemption, and when a transfer is refunded.
