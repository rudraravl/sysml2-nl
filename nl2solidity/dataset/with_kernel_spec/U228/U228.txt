This contract is a Bitcoin-backed reserve token, designed to custody wrapped Bitcoin and issue an equivalent amount of a fungible ERC-20 token. It maintains a total supply of the issued token, a transparent reserve balance of wrapped Bitcoin, and individual user balances of the issued token. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin to mint new tokens, withdraw wrapped Bitcoin by burning tokens, and transfer their issued tokens to other accounts. Only a designated operator can pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals, and only the contract owner can update the operator address or set the withdrawal fee. A withdrawal fee of 0.1% is applied to all withdrawals, and the maximum daily withdrawal limit for any single address is 10 wrapped Bitcoin. The contract announces deposit, withdrawal, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
