This contract manages a multi-asset liquidity pool for various liquid staking tokens, holding deposited liquid staking tokens and their underlying assets. It maintains records of each liquid staking token's individual pool balance, its exchange rate to the underlying asset, and global configuration parameters like fees. Callers can deposit liquid staking tokens, withdraw liquid staking tokens, and swap between different liquid staking tokens within the pool. Only the owner can adjust the swap fee, add new supported liquid staking tokens, or pause all deposit and swap operations. The swap fee is fixed at 0.05% of the swapped amount, and a maximum of 200 distinct liquid staking tokens can be supported at any given time. The contract emits events for every deposit, withdrawal, and swap operation, including the assets involved and the amounts transferred.
