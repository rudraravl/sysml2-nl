This contract facilitates the creation and management of non-fungible token collections, holding no custodied assets itself. It maintains a registry of deployed collection contracts, their associated creators, and configurable launch parameters for each. Callers can initiate the deployment of a new collection contract, update the metadata for a collection they own, or pause/unpause minting for their collections. A designated operator can set a global platform fee percentage and approve or reject new collection deployments. The platform fee for any successful primary sale mint must be between 0% and 10%. A collection creator can only deploy a maximum of 5 collection contracts per calendar month. The contract announces when a new collection is deployed and when a collection's minting status changes.
