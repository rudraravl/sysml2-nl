This contract is a liquid staking pool that custodies staked tokens and issues liquid staking tokens representing proportional shares of the pool. It maintains a registry of approved validator addresses, a total staked amount, a total liquid token supply, and per-user balances of liquid tokens. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive liquid tokens, withdraw native tokens by burning liquid tokens, and claim rewards. A designated operator can add or remove validators from the approved list and update the fee percentage. The maximum number of approved validators is 25. The withdrawal fee is fixed at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount. The contract emits an event whenever native tokens are deposited, liquid tokens are minted or burned, or the fee percentage is updated.
