This contract is for issuing an EVM-compatible wrapped asset representing a staked asset, and it custodies the staked asset. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped asset, a record of each user's wrapped asset balance, and the total amount of the underlying staked asset held. Callers can deposit the underlying staked asset to mint the wrapped asset, transfer their wrapped asset to another account, and redeem their wrapped asset for the underlying staked asset. A designated operator can pause all transfers and redemptions, and can initiate a forced redemption of a specific amount of the underlying asset from the contract. The minting ratio is strictly 1:1, meaning one unit of the underlying staked asset yields one unit of the wrapped asset. A redemption fee of 0.1% of the redeemed amount is applied to all redemptions. The contract announces all deposit, mint, transfer, and redemption events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
