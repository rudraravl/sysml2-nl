This contract serves as a decentralized identity registry, custodying no assets directly but managing attestations of human uniqueness. It maintains a record for each registered identity, storing a boolean indicating its verified status and a timestamp of its last verification. Callers can register a new identity, submit proof for verification, and query the verification status of an identity. A designated operator is privileged to approve or reject verification proofs, and to revoke an identity's verified status. A maximum of 10,000 identities can be registered in total. Each identity verification is valid for 365 days. The contract announces an event when a new identity is registered, when an identity's verification status changes, and when an identity's verification is revoked.
