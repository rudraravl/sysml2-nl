This contract manages a decentralized insurance fund, holding a reserve of a stablecoin to cover claims. It maintains individual user balances of stablecoin deposits, records of active coverage policies, and a global pool of stablecoin for payouts. Callers can deposit stablecoins to become underwriters, purchase coverage for specified assets, and submit claims against active policies. A designated operator can approve or reject claims, and an owner can adjust system parameters. The maximum coverage period for any policy is 365 days, and a 1% fee is applied to all policy purchases, directed to the protocol treasury. The contract emits an event upon each successful policy purchase and claim payout.
