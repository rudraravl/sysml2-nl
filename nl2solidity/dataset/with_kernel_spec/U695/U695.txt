This contract is a liquid staking contract that custodies deposited base tokens. It maintains a global total of deposited base tokens, a global total of issued liquid staking tokens, and a per-account record of each user's liquid staking token balance. Callers can deposit base tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw their deposited base tokens by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim rewards. A designated operator can update the exchange rate between base tokens and liquid staking tokens, and can pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The contract charges a 0.5% fee on all rewards claimed, and the minimum deposit amount for base tokens is 10 units. The contract announces deposit events including the depositor's address and the amount deposited, withdrawal events including the withdrawer's address and the amount withdrawn, and reward claim events including the claimant's address and the amount of rewards claimed.
