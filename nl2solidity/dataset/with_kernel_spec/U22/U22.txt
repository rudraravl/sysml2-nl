This contract manages a yield-generating vault that accepts and custodies a single designated base asset. It maintains a total amount of the base asset deposited, a total amount of vault shares minted, and for each user, their individual share balance. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive vault shares, withdraw their base asset by burning vault shares, and redeem their shares for the underlying base asset. A designated operator can initiate a rebalance of the vault's underlying strategy, and the owner can set the designated operator. Deposits are capped at 1,000,000 units of the base asset per transaction, and withdrawals incur a 0.5% fee on the withdrawn amount. The contract announces deposit events including the depositor and amount, and withdrawal events including the withdrawer and amount.
