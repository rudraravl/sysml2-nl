This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral deposits and tracking open positions. It maintains individual user account balances, active perpetual positions including margin and unrealized profit/loss, and global configuration parameters for each trading pair. Callers can deposit collateral, withdraw available collateral, open new perpetual positions, close existing positions, and modify their leverage on open positions. A designated operator role can add new perpetual trading pairs, adjust trading fees, and pause all trading activity. A maximum leverage of 100x is enforced for all trading pairs, and a trading fee of 0.05% of the notional value is applied to all position opening and closing operations. The contract emits events upon successful deposits, withdrawals, position openings, position closures, and any changes to trading pair configurations.
