This contract manages a decentralized exchange for perpetual futures, holding collateral in various approved digital assets. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each account, and global funding rates for each trading pair. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close positions, adjust leverage, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can add new trading pairs, update oracle prices, and adjust risk parameters like initial margin requirements. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x. A trading fee of 0.1% of the notional value is charged on all trades. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, and position closures.
