This contract manages the creation and distribution of unique digital collectibles representing in-game assets, holding no custodied value itself. It maintains a record of each collectible's unique identifier, its current owner, and its specific attributes, along with a global counter for the total number of collectibles minted and a configuration for the maximum supply. Callers can mint new collectibles, transfer ownership of their owned collectibles to another account, and burn collectibles they own. A designated operator is privileged to set the base URI for collectible metadata and to pause or unpause all minting operations. The maximum total supply of collectibles is capped at 100,000, and a minting fee of 0.01 ether is charged for each new collectible created. The contract announces when a new collectible is minted, when a collectible is transferred, and when a collectible is burned.
