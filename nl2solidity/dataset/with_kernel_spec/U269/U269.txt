This contract manages a liquid staking derivative token, representing staked Ether and accruing yield, and custodies deposited Ether. It maintains each user's deposited Ether amount, their share of the total staked Ether, and the current exchange rate between the derivative token and Ether. Callers can deposit Ether to mint the derivative token, redeem the derivative token for Ether, and transfer their derivative tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can update the Ether balance representing staked assets and adjust the exchange rate. The contract imposes a 0.5% fee on all redemptions, and a minimum deposit amount of 0.01 Ether is required. The contract emits an event when Ether is deposited, when Ether is redeemed, and when the exchange rate is updated.
