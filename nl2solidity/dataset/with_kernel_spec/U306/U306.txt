This contract is a liquid staking derivative token representing staked Ether, holding deposited Ether and minting an equivalent amount of the derivative token. It maintains a total supply of the derivative token, the Ether balance of the contract, and a per-account balance of the derivative token. Callers can deposit Ether to receive derivative tokens, withdraw their staked Ether by burning derivative tokens, and transfer derivative tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can initiate withdrawals from the underlying staking protocol and process received staking rewards. The contract imposes a 0.1% fee on all withdrawals, and a minimum deposit amount of 0.01 Ether is required. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
