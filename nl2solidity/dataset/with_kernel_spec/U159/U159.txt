This contract manages the staking of a base asset, enabling users to lock their holdings to secure a proof-of-stake chain and earn rewards, without custodying the underlying base asset directly. It maintains individual user staking positions, tracking the amount staked and accumulated rewards for each staker, along with a global pool of available rewards and a configurable unbonding period. Callers can initiate a stake by locking their base asset, claim accumulated rewards, and request an unbonding of their staked amount, which becomes withdrawable after a delay. Only a designated operator can deposit rewards into the contract's reward pool and update the unbonding period. The unbonding period is fixed at 14 days, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all claimed rewards. The contract emits events when a stake is initiated, rewards are claimed, an unbonding request is made, and when unbonded funds are withdrawn.
