This contract manages a treasury-backed reserve currency, holding various stablecoin and liquidity provider tokens. It maintains individual account balances for staked tokens, a global total supply of the reserve currency, and configuration parameters for rebase rewards. Callers can stake their tokens to receive the reserve currency, unstake their reserve currency to redeem underlying assets, and claim periodic rebase rewards. Only a designated operator can initiate rebase events, which distribute newly minted reserve currency to stakers, and only the owner can adjust the rebase reward rate or add new treasury assets. The rebase reward rate is fixed at 0.05% per 4-hour period, and unstaking incurs a 0.5% fee on the redeemed amount. The contract announces rebase events, new treasury asset additions, and changes to the rebase reward rate.
