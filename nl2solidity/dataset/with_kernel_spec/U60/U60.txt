This contract is a decentralized exchange for token pairs, holding reserves of two distinct fungible tokens for each pair. It maintains a record of each trading pair's token reserves, an accumulated fee for each pair, and a global protocol fee percentage. Callers can add liquidity to a trading pair, remove liquidity from a trading pair, swap one token for another within a pair, and initiate a time-weighted average swap for larger orders. A designated operator can update the global protocol fee percentage, but only within a range of 0% to 10% inclusive. The contract charges a 0.3% fee on all swaps, with a portion of this fee (determined by the global protocol fee percentage) accruing to the protocol and the remainder to liquidity providers. The contract emits an event whenever liquidity is added or removed, or when a swap occurs, detailing the tokens involved and their amounts.
