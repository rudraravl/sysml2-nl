This contract facilitates the trading of non-fungible tokens, holding deposited NFTs and associated payments in escrow. It maintains a record of listed NFTs with their seller, price, and status, alongside user balances for native currency. Callers can list an NFT for sale, cancel a listing, and purchase an NFT from a listing. A designated operator can pause all trading activity and update the platform fee. The platform fee is fixed at 2.5% of the sale price, and listings expire after 30 days if not purchased or canceled. The contract announces when an NFT is listed, when a listing is canceled, and when an NFT is successfully purchased.
