This contract manages a basket of collateral assets to back a new stable token, holding the specified collateral tokens in escrow. It maintains the current collateral basket composition, the total supply of the stable token, and each user's balance of the stable token. Callers can mint new stable tokens by depositing the required collateral, redeem stable tokens for their underlying collateral, and transfer stable tokens to other users. A designated operator can adjust the collateral basket composition and update the oracle addresses used for pricing. The maximum number of collateral tokens in the basket is 10. A 0.1% fee is applied to all redemption operations, payable in the redeemed collateral assets. The contract announces events for all minting, redemption, and transfer operations, as well as changes to the collateral basket or oracle configurations.
