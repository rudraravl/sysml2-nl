This contract is a token and liquidity pair locker, custodying deposited fungible tokens and liquidity provider tokens. It maintains records of each lock's start time, end time, locked amount, and the depositor, along with a global fee percentage and a designated owner address. Callers can deposit tokens to create a new lock, extend an existing lock's duration, or withdraw their locked tokens once the lock period expires. The owner can set the global fee percentage and designate a new owner. A lock duration must be at least 30 days and cannot exceed 1095 days. A 1% fee is applied to all withdrawals, deducted from the withdrawn amount. The contract announces lock creation, lock extension, and token withdrawal events to observers.
