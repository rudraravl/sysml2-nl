This contract manages a decentralized prediction market, holding user deposits and payouts in a base currency. It maintains a record of each market's state, including its event question, possible outcomes, resolution time, total staked amount per outcome, and the market's current status (open, resolved, or canceled). Callers can create a new market, place a bet on an outcome, redeem their winnings after a market resolves, or withdraw their initial stake if a market is canceled. A designated operator can resolve a market by declaring the winning outcome or cancel a market before its resolution time. New markets require a minimum initial stake of 100 units of the base currency to be created. A 2% fee is applied to all winning payouts, deducted before distribution. The contract emits an event when a new market is created, when a bet is placed, when a market is resolved, and when a market is canceled.
