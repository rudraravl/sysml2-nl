This contract manages a decentralized lending pool for a single stablecoin, holding user deposits and distributing yield from external strategies. It maintains individual user deposit balances, the total deposited amount, and configuration parameters for yield strategies. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the pool, withdraw their deposited principal and accrued yield, and claim their share of governance tokens distributed to depositors. A designated operator can add or remove approved yield strategies, adjust the interest rate spread, and pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The maximum deposit per user is 100,000 stablecoins, and a 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, yield distributions, and changes to the operator or strategy configurations.
