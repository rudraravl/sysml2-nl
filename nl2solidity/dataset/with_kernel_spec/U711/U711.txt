This contract is a decentralized asset management vault, designed to custody various ERC-20 tokens deposited by users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited tokens, the total value locked for each asset, and a set of approved investment strategies. Callers can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens if not actively invested, and claim profits from successful strategies. A designated operator can initiate new investment strategies with deposited funds, rebalance existing strategies, and pause deposits or withdrawals in emergencies. The owner can approve new investment strategies, set the management fee, and upgrade the contract. A management fee of 0.5% is applied to all profits generated by investment strategies before distribution. Each investment strategy must allocate at least 10% of its capital to a stablecoin for liquidity. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, strategy initiations, and profit distributions to observers.
