This contract manages a marketplace for unique digital assets, holding deposited assets awaiting sale or withdrawal. It tracks ownership of listed assets, active listings with their prices, and the balance of marketplace-specific tokens for each user. Callers can list an asset for sale, purchase an asset from an active listing, cancel their own active listing, and withdraw their marketplace tokens. A designated operator can set the marketplace fee percentage and pause all trading activity. The marketplace charges a 2.5% fee on all successful sales, deducted from the seller's proceeds. The maximum number of active listings per user is 50. The contract emits an event when an asset is listed, when an asset is purchased, and when a listing is cancelled.
