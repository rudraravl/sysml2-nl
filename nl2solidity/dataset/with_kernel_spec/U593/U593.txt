This contract is a fixed-rate borrowing market for a stablecoin, holding deposited collateral tokens and stablecoin liquidity. It maintains per-account records of collateral deposited, stablecoin borrowed, and a borrowing right token balance, along with global configuration for interest rates and collateral factors. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow stablecoins against their collateral, repay stablecoin loans, withdraw excess collateral, and transfer borrowing right tokens. Only a designated administrator may adjust interest rate models, set collateral factors for approved collateral types, and pause borrowing or depositing. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any collateral type is 75%. All stablecoin loans incur a fixed annual interest rate of 5%. The contract announces events for deposits, borrows, repays, withdrawals, and administrator actions.
