This contract provides decentralized coverage against smart contract vulnerabilities, holding deposited collateral tokens. It maintains a global pool of collateral, individual user coverage policies with associated premium payments, and a configuration for coverage types and pricing. Callers can deposit collateral, purchase coverage for a specified smart contract, file a claim against an active policy, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can approve or reject claims, update coverage parameters, and pause new policy purchases. Claims must be filed within 90 days of a reported vulnerability, and approved claims pay out 90% of the covered amount. The contract announces policy purchases, premium payments, claim filings, and claim resolutions.
