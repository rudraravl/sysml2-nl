This contract is a yield aggregator for stablecoins, holding user-deposited stablecoins and managing their deployment into various yield-generating strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance, their share of the total yield-bearing pool, and the current allocation of funds across approved strategies. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their deposited stablecoins and accrued yield, and redeem their pool shares for the underlying stablecoins. A designated operator can add new yield strategies, remove existing strategies, and adjust the allocation of funds among active strategies. The contract enforces a maximum of ten active yield strategies at any given time, and a 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals made within 72 hours of deposit. The contract announces when a new strategy is added, when a strategy is removed, and when the allocation of funds to a strategy changes.
