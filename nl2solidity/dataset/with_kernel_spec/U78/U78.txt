This contract is a yield-generating liquid staking token, holding Ether and various yield-bearing tokens from external strategies. It maintains a total supply of its own token, the total amount of Ether and other assets it controls, and a per-account balance for each user's share of the total supply. Callers can deposit Ether to mint new tokens, redeem their tokens for Ether, and approve other accounts to spend their tokens. A designated operator can rebalance assets between different external yield strategies, and update the addresses of approved external strategies. The contract imposes a 0.5% fee on all redemptions, and a maximum of 75% of the total assets can be allocated to any single external strategy. The contract emits an event when tokens are minted, when tokens are redeemed, and when assets are rebalanced between strategies.
