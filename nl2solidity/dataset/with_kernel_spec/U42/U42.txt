This contract manages a lending pool for a specific collateral asset and a specific borrowable asset, holding deposited collateral from lenders and borrowed assets awaiting repayment. It maintains individual records for each user's deposited collateral, borrowed amount, and accrued interest, along with a global pool balance for both assets and a configuration for interest rates. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow assets against their collateral, repay borrowed assets, and withdraw their available collateral. Only a designated operator can adjust the interest rate model parameters and pause or unpause borrowing. A minimum collateralization ratio of 150% must be maintained for all active loans, and a fixed fee of 0.1% is applied to all borrowed amounts at the time of borrowing. The contract announces events for every deposit, borrow, repayment, and withdrawal, including the user's address, the asset amount, and the transaction type.
