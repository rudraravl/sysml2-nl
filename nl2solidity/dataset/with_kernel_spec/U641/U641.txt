This contract is for liquid restaking, holding deposited native blockchain currency and issuing a liquid restaking token in return. It maintains individual user deposit balances, the total amount of native currency staked, and the current exchange rate between the native currency and the liquid restaking token. Callers can deposit native currency, withdraw their deposited native currency and accrued rewards, and claim their share of protocol fees. A designated operator can update the exchange rate based on external oracle data, and set the protocol fee percentage. The maximum protocol fee percentage is 10%. Withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and exchange rate updates.
