This contract establishes a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) for collective governance, custodying a treasury of arbitrary ERC-20 tokens. It maintains a record of proposals, including their state (pending, active, defeated, executed), and the voting power associated with each member's staked governance tokens. Callers can create new proposals, stake governance tokens to gain voting power, delegate their voting power to another address, vote on active proposals, and execute successful proposals. Only the DAO itself, through a successfully executed proposal, can modify core configuration parameters like the proposal creation fee or the minimum voting quorum. A proposal requires a minimum of 100,000 staked governance tokens to be created, and active proposals remain open for voting for exactly 72 hours. The contract emits events when new proposals are created, when votes are cast, and when proposals are successfully executed.
