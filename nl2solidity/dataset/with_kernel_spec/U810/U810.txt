This contract is a stablecoin protocol that mints a digital gold token, backed by a reserve asset. It maintains individual balances of the digital gold token for each user, a total supply of the digital gold token, and the current balance of the reserve asset held within the contract. Callers can mint new digital gold tokens by depositing the reserve asset, redeem digital gold tokens for the reserve asset, and transfer digital gold tokens to other users. A designated operator can adjust the minting fee and the redemption fee. The minting fee is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited reserve asset, and the redemption fee is fixed at 0.75% of the redeemed digital gold tokens. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
