This contract is a cross-chain portfolio vault, designed to manage and rebalance a basket of diverse digital assets across multiple EVM networks. It maintains per-vault records of deposited assets, current allocations, and historical performance data for each participating user. Callers can deposit assets into a vault, withdraw their share of assets, propose rebalancing strategies, and vote on proposed strategies. A designated operator is privileged to approve new rebalancing strategies and update the list of supported assets. The contract imposes a maximum of 10 distinct assets per vault and charges a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all asset withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and successful rebalancing operations to observers.
