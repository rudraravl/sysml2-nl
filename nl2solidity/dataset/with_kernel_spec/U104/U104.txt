This contract serves as a decentralized price feed aggregator, storing and distributing validated financial market data. It maintains a registry of data providers, the latest submitted price for each asset from each provider, and a consolidated aggregate price for each asset. Callers can fetch the latest aggregated price for any listed asset, and designated operators can submit new price updates for assets they are authorized to cover. The owner can add or remove data providers, set their authorized assets, and adjust the aggregation parameters, including a minimum of 3 valid provider submissions required for an aggregate price to be considered valid. A fee of 0.01 ether is charged for each price submission, which is directed to a treasury controlled by the owner. The contract announces new aggregate price updates for each asset.
