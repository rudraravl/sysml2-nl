This contract manages a vault for tokenized derivatives strategies, holding deposited base tokens and issuing vault shares representing a pro-rata claim on the vault's assets. It maintains records of each user's deposited base token balance and their corresponding vault share balance, along with the total supply of vault shares and the current strategy configuration. Callers can deposit base tokens to mint vault shares, withdraw base tokens by burning vault shares, and redeem their vault shares for the underlying assets. An owner role can update the strategy parameters, pause deposits, and set the management fee. A designated operator can initiate rebalancing of the vault's assets according to the current strategy. The management fee is fixed at 0.5% of the vault's total value annually. The minimum deposit amount for base tokens is 100 units. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, and strategy parameter updates.
