This contract manages a yield-bearing index token representing a diversified portfolio of yield-generating strategies. It custodies various base tokens deposited by users and the yield-bearing tokens received from external strategies. The contract maintains a registry of approved strategies, each with its allocated capital, and tracks the total supply of the index token along with each user's share. Callers can deposit approved base tokens to mint new index tokens, redeem their index tokens for a proportional share of the underlying base tokens, and transfer their index tokens to other users. A designated operator can add new strategies to the approved list, update the capital allocation for existing strategies, and rebalance capital between strategies. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 100 base tokens for minting index tokens and charges a 0.5% fee on all redemptions, which is directed to a treasury address. It announces events for index token minting, index token burning, and strategy capital reallocations.
