This contract is a privacy mixer for a fungible token, holding user deposits of that token. It maintains a Merkle tree of deposit commitments, a mapping of nullifiers to booleans indicating spent commitments, and a configuration for deposit sizes and fees. Callers can deposit a specific amount of the fungible token, providing a zero-knowledge proof of a secret and a commitment, and can withdraw a previously deposited amount by providing a zero-knowledge proof of a nullifier and a Merkle proof. The owner can update the fee percentage and add new deposit sizes. Each deposit must be one of the pre-configured amounts, and a withdrawal incurs a 0.5% fee on the withdrawn amount. The contract announces deposit events including the commitment hash, and withdrawal events including the nullifier hash and recipient.
