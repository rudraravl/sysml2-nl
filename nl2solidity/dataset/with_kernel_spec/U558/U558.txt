This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding user collateral in various approved tokens. It maintains individual account balances for each collateral token, open positions for each trading pair including leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters such as minimum initial margin requirements and liquidation thresholds. Callers can deposit collateral, withdraw available collateral, open new positions, close existing positions, modify leverage on open positions, and initiate liquidations of under-collateralized accounts. A designated operator can add new trading pairs, adjust trading fees, and pause all trading operations in an emergency. The minimum initial margin for any new position is 10% of the position's value, and a liquidation fee of 0.5% of the liquidated position's value is applied. The contract emits an event for every deposit, withdrawal, position opening, position closing, and successful liquidation.
