This contract manages a marketplace for unique digital assets, holding deposited funds and escrowed assets during sales. It maintains a registry of listed assets with their prices, ownership records for minted assets, and balances for each user. Callers can list an asset for sale, purchase an asset, mint a new asset, or withdraw their earned funds. The contract owner can set the platform fee and designate approved minters. The platform fee is fixed at 2% of each sale. A user can only mint a maximum of 100 assets. The contract emits an event when an asset is listed, when an asset is sold, and when a new asset is minted.
