This contract manages the creation and distribution of unique digital collectibles, custodying the collectible tokens themselves. It maintains a record of each collectible's unique identifier and its current owner, along with a global count of total collectibles minted and a mapping of pack identifiers to their contents. Callers can open a pack to receive collectibles, transfer a collectible they own, and view the contents of any pack. Only a designated operator can create new packs of collectibles and set the probability distribution for collectible rarity within those packs. A maximum of 10,000 unique collectibles can ever be minted, and opening a pack costs a fixed fee of 0.01 units of the native blockchain currency. The contract announces when a new pack has been created and when a collectible has been transferred.
