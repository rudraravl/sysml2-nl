This contract facilitates institutional lending and borrowing, holding deposited collateral tokens and issued loan tokens. It maintains records of each borrower's outstanding loan principal, accrued interest, and collateral provided, alongside a global pool of available liquidity and configuration parameters like interest rates. Callers can deposit collateral, request a loan, repay a loan, or withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can adjust interest rates and approve new loan requests. A borrower's collateralization ratio must always be at least 120% of their outstanding loan value, and the maximum loan term is 180 days. The contract announces loan origination, loan repayment, and collateral liquidation events.
