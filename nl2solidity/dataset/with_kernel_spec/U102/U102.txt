This contract enables fractional ownership of a single, specified non-fungible token (NFT) by issuing fungible tokens representing shares in that NFT. It maintains the total supply of fractional tokens, the current price and reserve of the NFT, and a record of each user's fractional token balance. Callers can deposit an amount of the underlying NFT to create new fractional tokens, redeem fractional tokens for a proportional share of the underlying NFT, and participate in a Dutch auction to purchase the entire NFT. Only a designated operator can set the initial NFT and its reserve price, initiate an auction for the entire NFT, and withdraw the proceeds from a successful auction. The initial reserve price for an NFT must be at least 100 units of the underlying currency, and the auction duration is fixed at 24 hours. The contract announces when an NFT is fractionalized, when fractional tokens are minted or burned, and when an auction for the entire NFT begins or ends.
