This contract is a cross-chain asset bridge that custodies wrapped tokens representing assets on other chains. It maintains a record of each user's wrapped token balances, the total supply of each wrapped token, and a mapping of original chain asset identifiers to their corresponding wrapped token addresses on this chain. Callers can deposit native assets on a connected chain to mint wrapped tokens on this chain, transfer wrapped tokens to other users, and burn wrapped tokens on this chain to withdraw native assets on a connected chain. A designated operator is privileged to register new wrapped token types, set the fee for cross-chain transfers, and pause or unpause the bridge for specific wrapped token types. The maximum fee for any cross-chain transfer is 0.5% of the transferred amount. All cross-chain transfers incur a flat fee of 0.01 units of the wrapped token, in addition to the percentage fee. The contract announces events for wrapped token minting, burning, and cross-chain transfer completions.
