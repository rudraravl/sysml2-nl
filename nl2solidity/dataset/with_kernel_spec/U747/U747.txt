This contract is a concentrated liquidity management vault, custodying deposited tokens for users. It maintains per-user records of deposited assets, active liquidity positions within external concentrated-liquidity pools, and configuration parameters for rebalancing strategies. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their share of tokens, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can adjust rebalancing parameters and update approved external concentrated-liquidity pool addresses. The contract imposes a 0.1% withdrawal fee on all withdrawals and limits the maximum number of active concentrated-liquidity positions per vault to 10. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and rebalancing operations to observers.
