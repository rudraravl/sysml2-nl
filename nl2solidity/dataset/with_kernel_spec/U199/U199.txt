This contract manages a basis trading vault, holding user-deposited stablecoins and a wrapped version of a major cryptocurrency. It maintains individual user balances for deposited stablecoins and wrapped cryptocurrency, as well as a global pool of each asset, and records for open basis positions including their size and collateralization. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their stablecoin deposits, deposit wrapped cryptocurrency, and withdraw their wrapped cryptocurrency deposits. An owner can set the stablecoin deposit fee and the wrapped cryptocurrency withdrawal fee, and can initiate and close basis trading positions. A designated operator can adjust the collateral ratio of open basis positions. The stablecoin deposit fee is 0.1% of the deposited amount. The minimum stablecoin deposit is 100 units. The contract emits an event when stablecoins are deposited, when wrapped cryptocurrency is deposited, when a basis position is opened, and when a basis position is closed.
