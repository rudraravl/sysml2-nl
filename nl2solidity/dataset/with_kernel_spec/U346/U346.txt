This contract manages a principal-protected yield strategy, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing yield to different risk tranches. It maintains records of each user's deposited principal, their allocated risk tranche, and the current balance of stablecoins available for distribution to each tranche. Callers can deposit stablecoins into a chosen risk tranche, withdraw their principal and accumulated yield from a tranche, and claim their share of yield distributions. A designated operator can initiate new yield distribution rounds and configure the risk parameters for each tranche. The contract enforces a minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins for any new deposit. A 0.5% fee is applied to all principal withdrawals, deducted from the withdrawn amount. The contract announces when new yield distribution rounds begin and when a user successfully deposits or withdraws.
