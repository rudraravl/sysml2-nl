This contract is a bridge for transferring assets between a Layer-1 and a Layer-2 network, holding deposited tokens securely on Layer-1 until they are released on Layer-2. It maintains a record of each user's deposited Layer-1 tokens awaiting withdrawal on Layer-2, and a configuration for the minimum deposit amount and withdrawal delay. Callers can deposit Layer-1 tokens, initiate a Layer-2 withdrawal, and claim Layer-1 tokens after a Layer-2 withdrawal. A designated operator can pause all deposits and withdrawals, and set the minimum deposit amount and the withdrawal delay. The minimum deposit amount is 0.001 ETH. A withdrawal from Layer-2 to Layer-1 has a mandatory 7-day delay before Layer-1 tokens can be claimed. The contract announces when a deposit occurs and when a withdrawal is initiated.
