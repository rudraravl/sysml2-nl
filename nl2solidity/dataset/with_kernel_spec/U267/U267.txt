This contract facilitates a social token exchange, holding Ether and custom social tokens. It tracks the price curve for each social token, the Ether balance of each social token, and the Ether balance of each user. Callers can buy social tokens, sell social tokens, and withdraw their Ether. A designated operator can set the base price curve parameters and withdraw accumulated fees. The initial supply of each social token is zero, and the price increases quadratically with each token bought. A 5% fee is applied to all buy and sell transactions, split between the social token creator and the protocol. The contract emits events for all buy and sell actions, fee withdrawals, and changes to the base price curve.
