This contract manages a pooled lending facility, holding deposited tokens and issuing corresponding yield-bearing receipt tokens. It maintains records of each user's deposits, borrows, and collateral, along with global pool liquidity, interest rates, and configuration parameters for each supported asset. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their deposits, borrow tokens against collateral, repay borrowed tokens, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. Only the owner can add new supported assets, adjust interest rate models, or pause borrowing for specific assets. A borrower's health factor must remain above 1.05 to avoid liquidation, and the liquidation penalty is fixed at 5% of the liquidated collateral value. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, indicating the user, asset, and amount.
