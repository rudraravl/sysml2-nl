This contract defines and enforces the operational parameters for a capital allocation vault, holding various fungible tokens. It maintains a configuration for each vault instance, detailing permitted integrations, allowed actions, and capital flow rules. Callers can deposit tokens into a vault, initiate approved capital movements between integrated protocols, and withdraw their share of tokens. Only a designated operator can update the vault's configuration, which includes adding or removing approved integrations and modifying capital allocation strategies. A vault must always maintain at least 10% of its total capital in a designated reserve pool, and any single capital movement action cannot exceed 50% of the vault's current non-reserve capital. The contract announces events for all deposits, withdrawals, and changes to the vault's operational configuration.
