This contract is a lending pool for various collateral types, holding deposited tokens and tracking outstanding loans. It maintains per-user records of deposited collateral, borrowed assets, and accumulated interest, alongside global pools for each supported asset and configuration parameters for each market. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow assets against their collateral, repay loans, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can add new collateral and borrowable assets, adjust interest rate models, and set liquidation thresholds. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset pair is 75%, and a 0.5% origination fee is applied to all new loans. The contract announces deposit, borrow, repay, and withdrawal events, including the user, asset, and amount.
