This contract manages a prediction market where users can bet on the outcome of future events using a designated stablecoin. It maintains records of each market's parameters, the total stablecoin staked on each outcome, and individual user positions within each market. Callers can create new markets, stake stablecoin on a specific outcome, withdraw their staked stablecoin before market resolution, and claim their winnings after market resolution. The owner can pause all market activity and designate a market resolver who alone can declare a market's final outcome. A market must remain open for staking for at least 24 hours after creation, and a 1% fee is applied to all winnings, which is directed to the owner. The contract announces market creation, staking events, withdrawals, and market resolutions.
