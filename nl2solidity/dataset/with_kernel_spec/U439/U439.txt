This contract facilitates donations to open-source projects and automatically distributes a portion of those donations to their dependencies. It custodies donated cryptocurrency tokens. The contract maintains a registry of projects, each with a designated wallet address, a list of dependent projects, and a configurable distribution ratio for each dependency. Callers can donate to a project, register a new project, update a project's wallet address, add or remove a dependency for a project, and adjust the distribution ratio for a dependency. Only the owner can register new projects, update project wallet addresses, or modify dependency relationships and ratios. A project's distribution ratio to any single dependency must be between 1% and 50%, and the sum of all distribution ratios for a project cannot exceed 80%. The contract announces when a donation is received and when funds are distributed to a project or its dependencies.
