This contract manages the minting and ownership of unique in-game character NFTs, representing various species and classes within the game world. It maintains a record of each character's species, class, and current owner, along with a total count of characters minted for each species. Callers can mint new characters, transfer ownership of their characters to another account, and burn characters they own. Only a designated operator can set the base minting fee for new characters and approve new species for minting. A maximum of 10,000 characters can be minted for any single species, and the initial minting fee for any character is 0.05 units of the native blockchain currency. The contract announces when a new character is minted, when a character's ownership changes, and when a character is burned.
