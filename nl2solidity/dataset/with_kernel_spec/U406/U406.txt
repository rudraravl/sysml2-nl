This contract is a vault for managing automated liquidity positions in concentrated liquidity pools, holding deposited tokens on behalf of users. It maintains individual user balances for each deposited token, records of active liquidity positions including their range and pool identifier, and global configuration settings such as fees and allowed pools. Callers can deposit tokens, create new liquidity positions within a specified range, adjust existing positions by adding or removing liquidity, and withdraw their deposited tokens or earned fees. Only a designated operator can set the management fee percentage, add or remove allowed concentrated liquidity pool addresses, and pause all new position creation. The maximum management fee is 10% of earned fees. A user must deposit at least 0.01 ETH worth of tokens to open a new position. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, position creations, and position adjustments.
