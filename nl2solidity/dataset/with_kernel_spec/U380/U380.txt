This contract mints a liquid, index-backed token representing a diversified basket of wrapped Bitcoin assets, and it custodies the underlying wrapped Bitcoin tokens. It maintains a total supply of the index token, a balance of the index token for each user, and a record of the underlying wrapped Bitcoin tokens held. Callers can deposit supported wrapped Bitcoin tokens to mint new index tokens, and they can redeem their index tokens to receive a proportional share of the underlying wrapped Bitcoin tokens. A designated operator can add or remove supported wrapped Bitcoin tokens from the basket, and they can adjust the weighting of each wrapped Bitcoin token in the basket. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 0.001 wrapped Bitcoin tokens. Redemptions incur a 0.1% fee, deducted from the redeemed wrapped Bitcoin tokens. The contract announces the total supply of the index token and the current composition of the underlying wrapped Bitcoin basket.
