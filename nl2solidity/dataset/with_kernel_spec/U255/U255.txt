This contract manages a parimutuel prediction market for asset price movements, holding user deposits in an escrow. It maintains records of active prediction pools, each with a designated asset, a target price, an expiry timestamp, and the total amount staked on "up" or "down" outcomes, alongside individual user stakes within each pool. Callers can create a new prediction pool, deposit funds into an existing pool, choose an outcome (up or down), and withdraw their winnings after a pool has expired and been settled. Only a designated operator can settle an expired prediction pool by providing the final asset price, and only the owner can set the operator address or adjust the fee percentage. A new prediction pool must have a minimum deposit of 100 units of the base currency to be considered active, and a 1% fee is applied to all winnings. The contract emits an event when a new prediction pool is created, when a deposit is made, and when a pool is settled, including the final outcome.
