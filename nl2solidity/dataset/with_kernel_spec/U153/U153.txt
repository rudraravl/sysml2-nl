This contract is a digital art registry and marketplace, holding non-fungible tokens representing unique artworks. It maintains per-artwork records including creator, ownership, royalty percentages, and sale listings, along with a global fee configuration. Callers can mint new artwork tokens, transfer ownership of tokens, list tokens for sale at a fixed price, purchase listed tokens, and delist their own tokens. Only the designated owner can set the global platform fee, which is capped at 5%, and designate approved minters. Each artwork token must specify a royalty percentage between 0% and 15% for its creator, which is enforced on all secondary sales. The contract announces all token mints, transfers, listings, and purchases to observers.
