This contract is a liquid staking vault for a base asset, holding deposited base asset tokens and issuing a liquid staking token representing staked positions. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, the amount of base asset deposited by each user, and a global staking ratio determining the conversion between base asset and liquid staking tokens. Callers can deposit base asset tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, and they can redeem liquid staking tokens to withdraw their proportional share of the base asset, subject to a 7-day withdrawal delay. A designated operator can initiate staking of deposited base assets and manage validator keys, while the contract owner can update the staking ratio and set a protocol fee, which is capped at 0.5% of all redemptions. The contract announces all deposits, redemptions, and staking ratio updates as events.
