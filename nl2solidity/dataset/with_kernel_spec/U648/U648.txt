This contract is a yield-generating vault that custodies user-deposited ERC-20 tokens. It maintains individual user balances, a total deposited balance for each supported token, and a configuration for each vault strategy. Callers can deposit tokens into a vault, withdraw their deposited tokens and accrued yield, and claim rewards. Only a designated operator can update vault strategies, adjust strategy parameters, and initiate emergency withdrawals from a compromised strategy. A deposit fee of 0.5% is applied to all deposits, and a minimum deposit amount of 100 of the deposited token is enforced. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claims.
