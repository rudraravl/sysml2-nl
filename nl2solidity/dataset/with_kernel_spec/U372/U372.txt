This contract is for a gold-backed stablecoin, custodying no assets directly but representing a claim on physical gold held by a designated custodian. It maintains per-account balances of the stablecoin and a global total supply, alongside a configurable minting fee and redemption fee. Callers can mint new stablecoins by providing an equivalent value in a specified stablecoin, transfer their stablecoin balances to other accounts, and redeem their stablecoins for the equivalent value in the specified stablecoin. Only a designated operator can pause and unpause minting and redemption, and only the owner can update the designated operator, set the minting fee, and set the redemption fee. The minting fee is fixed at 0.1% of the minted amount, and the redemption fee is fixed at 0.2% of the redeemed amount. The contract announces all minting, transfer, and redemption events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
