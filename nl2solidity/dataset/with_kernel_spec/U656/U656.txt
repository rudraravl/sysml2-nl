This contract facilitates a prediction market where users can stake a base currency on the outcome of future events, and it custodies the staked base currency. It maintains a record of each user's staked amount for each prediction market, the total staked amount for each outcome, and the current odds for each outcome, along with global configuration parameters like the prediction market creation fee. Callers can create new prediction markets, stake currency on an outcome, withdraw their stake if a market is cancelled, and claim their winnings after a market resolves. A designated operator can resolve a prediction market to a specific outcome, cancel a market, or update the prediction market creation fee. The prediction market creation fee is 0.1 units of the base currency, and a market must remain open for staking for at least 24 hours before it can be resolved. The contract announces when a new prediction market is created, when a stake is placed, when a market is resolved, and when winnings are claimed.
