This contract is a governance delegation platform, holding a specific governance token to enable delegated voting. It maintains per-user records of deposited governance tokens and delegated voting power, along with a global delegation target address and a platform fee percentage. Callers can deposit governance tokens, delegate their voting power to the platform's designated target, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim any accumulated yield. The owner can set the delegation target address and adjust the platform fee. A minimum deposit of 10 governance tokens is required, and the platform fee is capped at 5% of the yield. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the delegation target.
