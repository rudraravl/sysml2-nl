This contract issues and manages a token representing physical gold held in secure vaults. It maintains each user's token balance and a global record of total gold ounces represented by all tokens, along with a configurable fee percentage for redemptions. Callers can mint new tokens by depositing gold, transfer tokens to other users, and redeem tokens for physical gold. A designated operator can pause and unpause all transfers and redemptions, and only the contract owner can update the redemption fee percentage. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed gold amount, and redemptions are limited to a maximum of 1000 ounces per transaction. The contract announces all token transfers, minting events, and redemption events.
