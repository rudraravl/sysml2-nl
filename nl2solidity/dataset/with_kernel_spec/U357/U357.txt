This contract manages a player-versus-player gaming system, custodying wrapped ether for battle rewards. It maintains individual player balances of wrapped ether, a global game configuration, and a record of active battles. Players can initiate attacks against other players, claim rewards from won battles, and withdraw their wrapped ether balance. A designated operator can set the attack cost, adjust battle parameters, and pause the system. Each attack costs 0.001 wrapped ether, and a player can only initiate one attack every 5 minutes. The contract announces when a battle is initiated, when a battle concludes, and when a player claims rewards.
