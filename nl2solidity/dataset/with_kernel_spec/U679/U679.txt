This contract facilitates a decentralized marketplace for unique digital assets, holding deposited assets and escrowed payments during transactions. It maintains a registry of listed assets with their prices and sellers, records of active bids, and balances of platform fees collected. Callers can list an asset for sale, make an offer on a listed asset, accept an offer, cancel a listing, and withdraw their owned assets or collected proceeds. A designated operator can set the platform fee percentage and pause all trading activity. The platform fee is fixed at 2.5% of the sale price, and a listed asset cannot be priced below 0.01 units of the accepted payment token. The contract announces asset listings, accepted offers, and completed sales to observers.
