This contract facilitates the creation and management of token presales, holding deposited funds and newly minted tokens. It maintains a record of each presale's configuration, including the token being sold, its soft cap and hard cap, and the contribution limits for individual participants, alongside a mapping of participant addresses to their contributed amounts and allocated tokens. Callers can create new presales, contribute funds to an active presale, claim their purchased tokens after a successful presale, or withdraw their contributions if a presale fails. Only the contract owner can set the global presale creation fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of the hard cap for any presale, and only the presale creator can finalize their specific presale. Each presale must have a minimum soft cap of 100 base currency units and a maximum hard cap of 10,000 base currency units. The contract announces when a new presale is created, when a contribution is made, and when a presale is finalized or cancelled.
