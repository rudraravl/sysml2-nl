This contract facilitates synthetic asset creation and redemption, holding deposited collateral tokens to back the synthetic assets. It maintains individual user balances for each synthetic asset, a global collateral pool for each accepted collateral type, and configuration parameters for each synthetic asset such as its collateralization ratio and associated oracle. Callers can deposit collateral, mint synthetic assets against their deposited collateral, redeem synthetic assets to reclaim collateral, and transfer synthetic assets to other users. A designated owner can add new synthetic assets, update oracle addresses, adjust collateralization ratios, and pause all minting and redemption operations. The minimum collateralization ratio for any synthetic asset is 150%, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all synthetic asset minting operations. The contract emits an event whenever synthetic assets are minted, redeemed, or transferred, and when new collateral is deposited or withdrawn.
