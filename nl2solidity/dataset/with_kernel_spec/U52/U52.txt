This contract facilitates the issuance and redemption of a wrapped Bitcoin token, holding native Bitcoin as collateral in a designated multi-signature wallet. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped token and a record of each user's wrapped token balance. Callers can mint new wrapped tokens by providing proof of a Bitcoin deposit to the multi-signature wallet, and they can burn wrapped tokens to initiate a Bitcoin withdrawal from the multi-signature wallet. A designated operator is privileged to approve minting requests after verifying Bitcoin deposits and to initiate Bitcoin withdrawals from the multi-signature wallet upon burning of wrapped tokens. Each minting operation requires a minimum deposit of 0.001 Bitcoin, and a 0.1% fee is levied on all redemptions, payable in the wrapped token. The contract announces the total supply of the wrapped token and the current wrapped token balance of any user.
