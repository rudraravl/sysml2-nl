This contract manages a decentralized investment strategy vault, holding various liquid tokens and their interest-bearing derivatives. It maintains a record of each user's deposited tokens, their share of the vault, and the current configuration of the active investment strategy. Callers can deposit supported tokens, withdraw their share of the vault, and propose new investment strategies. A designated governance committee can approve or reject proposed strategies, and only they can adjust the vault's operational fee, which is capped at 0.5% of all withdrawals. New strategies, once approved, have a mandatory 48-hour timelock before activation. The contract emits events upon successful deposits, withdrawals, and strategy changes.
