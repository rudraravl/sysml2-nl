This contract manages a vault for depositing and earning yield on wrapped Bitcoin, holding wrapped Bitcoin tokens and an interest-bearing receipt token from an external yield-generating protocol. It maintains a record of each user's deposited wrapped Bitcoin balance and their corresponding share of the vault's total interest-bearing tokens, along with a global configuration for the external yield protocol's address. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin into the vault, withdraw their deposited wrapped Bitcoin and accrued yield, and claim any accumulated rewards from the external protocol. Only a designated owner can update the external yield protocol's address or adjust the vault's operational parameters. A deposit fee of 0.5% is applied to all new deposits, and withdrawals are subject to a 24-hour time lock after the initial deposit before any funds can be removed. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and any changes to the external yield protocol's address.
