This contract manages a private, programmable stablecoin, backed 1:1 by a designated reserve stablecoin held in an external, off-chain reserve. It maintains each account's balance of the private stablecoin and a global total supply. Callers can mint new private stablecoin by depositing the designated reserve stablecoin, transfer private stablecoin to other accounts, and redeem private stablecoin for the designated reserve stablecoin. A designated operator can pause and unpause all transfers and redemptions. The owner can set the designated reserve stablecoin address and upgrade the contract. The maximum total supply of the private stablecoin is 1,000,000,000 units. All redemptions incur a 0.1% fee, paid in the designated reserve stablecoin, which is sent to a predefined treasury address. The contract announces Transfer and Mint events for all balance changes.
