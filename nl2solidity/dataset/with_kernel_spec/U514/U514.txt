This contract manages prediction markets, holding deposited collateral for active markets. It maintains individual user balances for each market, the current state of each market (open, closed, resolved), and global configuration parameters like fees. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit collateral into an open market, make predictions on market outcomes, and withdraw their share of resolved market winnings. The owner can set the global market creation fee and designate operators who can resolve markets. A market creation fee of 0.01 units of the native currency is charged for each new market. Predictions must be made before a market's specified closing time. The contract emits events when a new market is created, when collateral is deposited, when a prediction is made, and when a market is resolved.
