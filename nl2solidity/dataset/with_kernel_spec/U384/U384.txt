This contract serves as a central registry and escrow for digital collectibles, holding non-fungible tokens on behalf of users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited non-fungible tokens and their associated metadata, along with a global configuration for fees and operators. Callers can deposit their non-fungible tokens into the contract, withdraw their previously deposited non-fungible tokens, and transfer ownership of deposited non-fungible tokens to another user within the system. A designated operator can register new non-fungible token collections for deposit and update the metadata associated with registered collections. The owner can set the deposit fee, which is fixed at 0.01 ether per deposit, and can designate or revoke operator privileges. All withdrawals are subject to a 24-hour time lock after initiation. The contract announces when a non-fungible token is deposited, withdrawn, or transferred, and when a new collection is registered.
