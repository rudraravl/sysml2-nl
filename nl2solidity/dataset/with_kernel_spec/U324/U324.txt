This liquid staking contract allows users to deposit a base asset and receive a liquid staking derivative token in return, which represents their share of the total staked assets and accrued rewards. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking derivative, a mapping of user balances for this derivative, and a configuration for the current staking ratio and withdrawal fees. Callers can deposit the base asset, withdraw their staked base asset plus rewards by burning the derivative, and claim any accumulated rewards. A designated operator can update the staking ratio, set the withdrawal fee, and initiate unstaking requests with the underlying validator network. The withdrawal fee is fixed at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount, and a minimum deposit of 100 base asset units is required. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the staking ratio.
