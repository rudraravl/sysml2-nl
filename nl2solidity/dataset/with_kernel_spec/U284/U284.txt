This contract manages a yield-bearing stablecoin, holding deposited stablecoins as collateral. It maintains individual user deposit balances, a total supply of the yield-bearing token, and a global profit distribution rate. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint the yield-bearing token, withdraw their deposited stablecoins by burning the yield-bearing token, and claim accumulated profits. Only a designated operator can update the profit distribution rate and initiate profit distributions. The maximum deposit amount per transaction is 10,000 stablecoins, and a 0.1% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, profit claims, and profit distribution rate updates.
