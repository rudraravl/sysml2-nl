This contract manages and custodies tokenized real-world assets, representing fractional ownership in a diversified portfolio of private credit investments. It maintains per-investor records of their asset holdings, a total value locked for the portfolio, and configuration parameters for asset eligibility. Callers can deposit stablecoins to acquire asset tokens, redeem asset tokens for stablecoins, and transfer asset tokens to other investors. A designated operator can add new real-world assets to the portfolio and approve or reject investor redemption requests. The maximum individual investor holding is capped at 10,000,000 stablecoins, and a 0.5% redemption fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces successful deposits, redemptions, and asset additions through public events.
