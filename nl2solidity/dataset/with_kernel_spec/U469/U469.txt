This contract facilitates automated, continuous token streams between accounts, holding deposited tokens in escrow. It maintains per-sender records of active streams, including the recipient, the token being streamed, the total amount to stream, the start time, and the per-second rate, along with a total balance of each token held in the contract. Callers can create new streams, deposit tokens into existing streams, and recipients can withdraw their accrued stream amounts. Only the contract owner can pause all streaming activity or update the global streaming fee. A stream cannot be created for less than 100 units of the streaming token, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces stream creation, stream deposits, and stream withdrawals.
