This contract facilitates the bridging and restaking of a specific wrapped Bitcoin asset, holding custody of all deposited wrapped Bitcoin and any generated yield. It maintains individual account balances for wrapped Bitcoin deposits, staking positions, and accrued rewards, along with global parameters like the current staking rate and a list of approved external restaking operators. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin, initiate a restake operation by selecting an approved operator, claim earned rewards, and withdraw their original wrapped Bitcoin deposit. A designated owner role can approve or revoke restaking operators, adjust the staking rate, and set a protocol fee. The maximum staking rate is capped at 10% per annum, and a 0.5% protocol fee is applied to all reward claims. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, restaking operations, reward claims, and changes to the staking rate or approved operators.
