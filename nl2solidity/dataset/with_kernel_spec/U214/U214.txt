This contract manages a collateralized debt position system, holding deposited collateral tokens and issuing stablecoin tokens against them. It maintains individual user positions tracking deposited collateral, issued stablecoin debt, and a global system configuration including the collateralization ratio and stability fee. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow stablecoins, repay stablecoins, and withdraw collateral. A designated operator can adjust the stability fee and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The minimum collateralization ratio is 150%, and the stability fee is 0.5% per year, applied continuously. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, withdrawals, and liquidations.
