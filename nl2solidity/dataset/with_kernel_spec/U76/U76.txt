This contract facilitates gasless token swaps, holding a reserve of a stablecoin and a native network token. It maintains a stablecoin balance for each user, a total stablecoin reserve, and a configurable exchange rate between the stablecoin and the native token. Callers can deposit stablecoins, request a native token swap for their deposited stablecoins, and withdraw their stablecoin balance. A designated operator can update the exchange rate, replenish the native token reserve, and withdraw excess stablecoins. The exchange rate is fixed at 100 native tokens per 1 stablecoin. A 0.5% fee is applied to all native token swaps, deducted from the stablecoin amount. The contract announces when a deposit occurs, when a swap is executed, and when the exchange rate is updated.
