This contract manages a liquid restaking vault, holding deposited base tokens and issuing liquid restaked tokens representing proportional shares of the vault's assets. It maintains a total supply of liquid restaked tokens, the total amount of base tokens held, and per-user balances of liquid restaked tokens. Callers can deposit base tokens to receive liquid restaked tokens, and redeem liquid restaked tokens for their share of base tokens. A designated operator can initiate rebalancing strategies, which involve transferring base tokens to external restaking protocols and receiving corresponding yield-bearing tokens. The owner can set the operator address and update the redemption fee, which is initially 0.1% and cannot exceed 1%. The contract announces deposit and redemption events, including the depositor, amount, and resulting liquid restaked token balance.
