This contract facilitates the bridging of a specific external digital asset to a fungible token on the Ethereum network, holding the external digital asset in custody. It maintains a record of each user's deposited external asset amount and their corresponding minted token balance, alongside a global configuration for fees and minimum deposit sizes. Callers can deposit the external asset to mint tokens, redeem tokens to withdraw the external asset, and approve token transfers. A designated operator can adjust the deposit fee and the minimum deposit amount, and can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The deposit fee is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited amount, and the minimum external asset deposit is 0.01 units. The contract announces events for all successful deposits, withdrawals, and fee updates.
