This contract manages a decentralized reserve currency, holding various digital assets as treasury backing. It maintains a treasury balance for each accepted asset, a total supply of the reserve currency, and individual user balances of the reserve currency. Callers can deposit accepted assets to mint the reserve currency, stake their reserve currency to earn rewards, and unstake their reserve currency to withdraw it. The owner can add new accepted assets to the treasury, adjust the minting ratio for new deposits, and initiate bond offerings. A minimum of 10 units of an accepted asset must be deposited to mint reserve currency, and the staking reward rate is fixed at 0.05% per block. The contract announces all deposits, withdrawals, staking events, and unstaking events.
