This contract facilitates decentralized prediction markets, holding deposited collateral in an escrow. It maintains a record of each market's parameters, including its question, resolution source, and outcome states, along with individual user positions for each market and the total collateral pooled per outcome. Callers can create new markets, deposit collateral into an existing market to take a position on an outcome, withdraw their collateral if the market is unresolved, or claim their winnings after a market has been resolved. A designated market creator can resolve a market by providing the final outcome, and only the owner can set the market creation fee or pause all market activity. New markets require a minimum collateral deposit of 100 units of the designated stablecoin, and a 1% fee is charged on all market winnings, deducted at the time of claiming. The contract announces market creation, collateral deposits, position withdrawals, market resolutions, and winning claims to observers.
