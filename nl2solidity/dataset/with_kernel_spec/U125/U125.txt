This contract manages the deposit and withdrawal of a wrapped Bitcoin token, serving as a vault for user-deposited assets. It maintains individual user balances of the wrapped Bitcoin token, a total deposited amount, and a configurable fee percentage. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and initiate a withdrawal of their restaked assets from an external restaking protocol. Only a designated operator can set the withdrawal fee, which is capped at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount, and only the owner can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 0.0001 wrapped Bitcoin. The contract emits events for every deposit, withdrawal, and fee update.
