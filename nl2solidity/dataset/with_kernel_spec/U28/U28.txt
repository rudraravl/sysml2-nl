This contract manages a fixed-rate lending pool, holding deposited collateral tokens and issued stablecoin tokens. It maintains individual loan positions, tracking collateral deposited, stablecoins borrowed, and accrued interest for each borrower, alongside a global configuration for interest rates and collateralization requirements. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow stablecoins against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can adjust the fixed interest rate and the minimum collateralization ratio. The fixed interest rate for new loans is 0.5% APR, and the minimum collateralization ratio is 150%. The contract announces events for loan creation, repayment, and collateral adjustments.
