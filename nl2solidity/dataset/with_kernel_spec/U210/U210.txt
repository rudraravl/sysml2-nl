This contract serves as a risk pool for insurance policies, holding stablecoins deposited by investors to cover potential claims. It maintains individual investor balances, a total pool balance, and a registry of active insurance policies including their coverage amount and premium. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the pool, withdraw their available balance, and propose new insurance policies for underwriting. A designated operator can approve or reject proposed policies, and disburse funds for approved claims. The contract enforces a minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins for new investors and a maximum coverage limit of 50,000 stablecoins for any single policy. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, policy approvals, and claim payouts.
