This contract manages user deposits of a stablecoin and facilitates spending from associated external accounts. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance and their corresponding external account identifier. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their stablecoin balance, and initiate a spending transaction from their external account. A designated operator can approve or reject spending transactions, and set the fee for spending transactions. The maximum stablecoin deposit per user is 10,000 units. The fee for a spending transaction is 0.5% of the transaction amount. The contract announces when a deposit occurs, when a withdrawal occurs, and when a spending transaction is initiated, approved, or rejected.
