This contract manages a decentralized reserve currency, holding a basket of diverse digital assets as backing. It maintains a total supply of the reserve currency, the balance of each user's reserve currency, and the current value of each backing asset as reported by an external price oracle. Callers can mint new reserve currency by depositing approved backing assets, burn reserve currency to redeem a proportional share of backing assets, and transfer reserve currency to other users. A designated operator can add new backing assets to the approved list and adjust the minting fee. The minting fee is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited value, and a minimum of 100 units of reserve currency must be minted in any single transaction. The contract emits an event when reserve currency is minted, burned, or transferred, and when a new backing asset is approved.
