This contract facilitates a non-custodial marketplace for unique digital assets, holding only the specific digital assets offered for sale and the proceeds from their successful sale. It maintains a record of each listed asset, including its current seller, its asking price, and whether it has been sold, along with a configurable marketplace fee percentage. Callers can list a digital asset for sale, purchase a listed digital asset, and cancel their own active listings. Only a designated marketplace administrator can adjust the marketplace fee percentage. The marketplace fee is fixed at 2.5% of the sale price for every successful transaction, and a listed asset's price cannot be changed once it is active; it must be delisted and relisted to adjust the price. The contract announces when an asset is listed, when an asset is purchased, and when a listing is cancelled.
