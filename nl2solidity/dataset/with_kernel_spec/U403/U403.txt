This contract is a personal asset management vault, designed to hold and manage a user's various digital assets including fungible tokens and non-fungible tokens. It maintains a record of each user's deposited assets, their current balance, and a history of their transactions within the vault. Callers can deposit fungible tokens, deposit non-fungible tokens, withdraw fungible tokens, withdraw non-fungible tokens, and delegate spending authority for specific assets to other addresses. A designated operator can set a global withdrawal fee and pause all withdrawal operations. The maximum number of unique non-fungible tokens a single user can deposit is 100, and any withdrawal of fungible tokens incurs a 0.1% fee. The contract announces asset deposit events, asset withdrawal events, and delegation of spending authority events.
