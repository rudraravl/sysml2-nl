This contract manages a decentralized exchange for non-fungible tokens, holding deposited non-fungible tokens and fungible tokens within liquidity pools. It tracks individual liquidity provider positions, including deposited amounts and accumulated fees, and maintains global configuration parameters such as trading fees and royalty percentages. Callers can add liquidity to a pool, remove liquidity from a pool, swap non-fungible tokens for fungible tokens, and claim accumulated fees. A designated operator can pause all trading activity and update the trading fee percentage. The maximum trading fee is 2.5%, and a royalty fee of 0.5% is always applied to non-fungible token sales. The contract emits events when liquidity is added or removed, when a swap occurs, and when fees are claimed.
