This contract is a launchpad for new web3 projects, custodying project tokens deposited by project creators and funds contributed by participants. It maintains a list of approved projects, each with its funding goal, current contributions, and a record of individual participant contributions per project. Callers can contribute funds to an active project, and project creators can initiate a project launch, withdraw their project tokens after a successful launch, or reclaim them if the launch fails. The owner can approve new projects, set the platform fee, and designate operators. A project launch requires a minimum funding goal of 100 units of the contribution currency, and a 5% platform fee is applied to all successful project fundraises. The contract announces when a project is launched, when a project fails to meet its funding goal, and when a participant contributes to a project.
