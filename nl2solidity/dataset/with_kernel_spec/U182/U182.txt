This contract manages an algorithmic stablecoin pegged to a target value of 0.1 units of a base asset, minting and burning the stablecoin to maintain its peg. It maintains the total supply of the stablecoin, the current target price of the base asset, and a record of each user's stablecoin balance. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing the base asset, burn stablecoins to redeem the base asset, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the target price of the base asset, but only by a maximum of 0.001 units per 24-hour period. The contract charges a 0.5% fee on all stablecoin minting operations, deducted from the base asset deposit. The contract announces all stablecoin minting, burning, and transfer events to observers.
