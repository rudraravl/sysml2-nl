This contract manages a decentralized exchange's liquidity pools, holding deposited token pairs. It maintains records of each liquidity provider's deposited amounts and accrued fees within specific token pair pools, along with global configuration settings for fees and emissions. Callers can deposit token pairs to provide liquidity, withdraw their liquidity and accumulated fees, swap one token for another within a pool, and claim accumulated emissions. A designated administrator can adjust the base swap fee, which is initially 0.05%, and update the emission schedule for new tokens. All swaps incur a fee, with a minimum of 0.01% and a maximum of 1%, and the contract ensures that a user cannot withdraw more liquidity than they have provided. The contract announces successful token swaps, liquidity additions, and liquidity removals through events.
