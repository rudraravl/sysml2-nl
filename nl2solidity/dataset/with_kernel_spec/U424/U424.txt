This contract manages player NFT assets and their associated training progress within a strategic gaming ecosystem. It maintains individual player records including their unique identifier, current statistics (e.g., speed, strength), and accumulated training points, alongside a global configuration for training costs and experience thresholds. Callers can acquire new player NFTs, initiate training sessions for their players, and transfer player NFTs to other accounts. Only a designated operator can mint new player NFTs and adjust the global training cost, which is capped at 0.05 units of the platform's native token per training session. Training sessions require a minimum of 10 accumulated training points to be consumed. The contract announces player NFT transfers and the completion of training sessions.
