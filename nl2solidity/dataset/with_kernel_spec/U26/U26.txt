This contract manages a treasury of various fungible tokens and distributes a portion of its holdings to eligible participants. It maintains a total balance for each held token, a record of each participant's share of the distribution pool, and a configuration for distribution parameters. Callers can deposit supported tokens into the treasury, withdraw their accumulated distributions, and stake a specific token to earn a share of future distributions. Only a designated operator can add new supported tokens, adjust the distribution rate, or initiate a distribution event. The maximum distribution rate is 5% of the treasury's total value per distribution event, and a minimum of 24 hours must pass between distribution events. The contract announces when a new token is supported and when a distribution event has occurred, including the total amount distributed.
