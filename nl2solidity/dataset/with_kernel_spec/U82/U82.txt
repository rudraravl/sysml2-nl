This contract is a token launchpad facilitating fair-launch meme-token distribution and custodying KUB and newly minted tokens. It maintains a record of each launched token's current bonding curve parameters, the total KUB contributed to each token's launch, and the amount of newly minted tokens held for each contributor. Callers can contribute KUB to a token's bonding curve, claim their newly minted tokens, and withdraw their tokens from the subsequent liquidity pool. A designated operator can initiate a new token launch and transition a token from its bonding curve phase to a sealed liquidity pool. The initial bonding curve phase for any token must last exactly 24 hours, and 14.3% of all trading fees generated in the subsequent liquidity pool are shared back to the token's creator. The contract announces when a new token launch begins, when KUB is contributed, and when a token transitions to a liquidity pool.
