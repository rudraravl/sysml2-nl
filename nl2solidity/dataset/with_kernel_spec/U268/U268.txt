This contract manages a yield-bearing stablecoin, custodying deposited stablecoins and distributing earned yield. It maintains individual account balances for each user, a total supply of the yield-bearing stablecoin, and a global exchange rate between the deposited stablecoin and the yield-bearing stablecoin. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint the yield-bearing stablecoin, redeem their yield-bearing stablecoin for the underlying stablecoin, and transfer their yield-bearing stablecoin to other accounts. A designated operator can update the exchange rate based on external yield accrual, and can pause all deposit and redemption operations in an emergency. The minimum deposit amount is 10 deposited stablecoins. A 0.1% fee is applied to all redemption transactions, deducted from the redeemed amount. The contract announces deposit, redemption, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
