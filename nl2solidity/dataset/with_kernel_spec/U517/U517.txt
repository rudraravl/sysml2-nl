This contract manages a yield-generating vault, holding deposited stablecoins and investing them into various strategies. It maintains individual account balances for each depositor, a total value locked, and configuration parameters for each approved investment strategy. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their principal and accrued yield, and claim any available rewards. A designated operator can approve new investment strategies, adjust allocation percentages across strategies, and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. A minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins is enforced, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, directed to a treasury address. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to strategy allocations.
