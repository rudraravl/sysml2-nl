This contract manages unique digital assets representing social network profiles, enabling users to trade ownership of these profiles. It maintains a mapping of profile identifiers to their current owner, a price curve for each profile based on its total shares outstanding, and a global fee percentage for all trades. Callers can buy shares of a profile, sell shares of a profile, and withdraw their accumulated earnings from selling shares. The contract owner can update the global fee percentage, set a new beneficiary for collected fees, and pause all trading activity. A buyer must purchase at least 1 share, and the global fee percentage is fixed at 5%. The contract emits an event whenever shares are bought, shares are sold, or the fee percentage is updated.
