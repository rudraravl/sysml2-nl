This contract facilitates decentralized parlay predictions, custodying user deposits in a designated stablecoin. It maintains records of active parlays, including their constituent predictions, user stakes, and payout multipliers, along with a global configuration for fees and operator addresses. Callers can create new parlays by specifying a series of outcomes and a stake, join existing open parlays, and claim winnings for settled parlays. A designated operator is solely responsible for settling individual prediction outcomes within a parlay and updating parlay statuses. A 5% protocol fee is deducted from all winning payouts, and a parlay must contain a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 10 individual predictions. The contract announces parlay creation, outcome settlement, and payout events to observers.
