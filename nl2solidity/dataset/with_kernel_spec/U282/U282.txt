This contract facilitates the launch of new tokens by managing a bonding curve and price-gated escrows, holding deposited native currency and newly issued tokens. It maintains a record of each launched token's configuration, including its bonding curve parameters and price milestones, alongside individual user purchase positions detailing their locked token amounts and unlock eligibility. Callers can purchase new tokens by depositing native currency, claim unlocked tokens once price milestones are met, and the designated operator can register new token launches and update their price milestones. Only the owner can set the protocol fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of all native currency purchases, and pause all purchase and claim operations. Purchases are limited to a maximum of 100 native currency units per transaction. The contract announces token purchases, token claims, and changes to token launch configurations.
