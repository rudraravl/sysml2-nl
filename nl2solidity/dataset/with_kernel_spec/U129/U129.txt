This contract is a decentralized exchange for token swaps and liquidity provision, custodying deposited tokens within various trading pools. It maintains records of individual liquidity positions, global pool configurations including swap fees, and per-user token balances. Callers can add liquidity to a pool, remove their liquidity, swap tokens between pools, and collect earned fees. Only the designated owner may create new trading pools, update the protocol-wide swap fee, or pause all trading activity. The maximum swap fee is 0.5%, and new pools can only be created for ERC-20 compatible tokens. The contract announces all liquidity additions, removals, and token swaps to observers.
