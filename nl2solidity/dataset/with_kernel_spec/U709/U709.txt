This contract facilitates the creation of a decentralized stablecoin, backed by various approved collateral assets. It maintains individual user positions, tracking deposited collateral, borrowed stablecoin amounts, and accrued interest, alongside global configuration parameters for each collateral type. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow stablecoin against their collateral, repay stablecoin, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can add new collateral types, adjust interest rates, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any collateral type is 75%, and a 0.5% stablecoin origination fee is charged on all new borrowings. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, and liquidations.
