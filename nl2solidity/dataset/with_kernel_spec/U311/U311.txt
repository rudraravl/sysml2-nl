This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding user collateral in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each trading pair, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and margin requirements. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close positions, modify existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can adjust trading pair parameters, update oracle prices, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage for any trading pair is 50x, and a 0.05% trading fee is applied to all position openings and closures. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position changes, and liquidations.
