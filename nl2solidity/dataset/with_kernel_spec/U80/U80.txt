This contract is a liquid staking token for a base asset, custodying deposited base assets and issuing a corresponding receipt token. It maintains a global record of the total base assets staked, the total receipt tokens minted, and a per-user balance of receipt tokens. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive receipt tokens, and redeem receipt tokens to withdraw their proportional share of the staked base asset. A designated operator can initiate staking of deposited base assets with an external staking provider and claim staking rewards. The owner can set the operator address and adjust the redemption fee, which is initially 0.1% and cannot exceed 1%. The contract emits an event upon each deposit, redemption, and reward claim, detailing the amount and the involved addresses.
