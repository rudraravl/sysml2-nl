This contract manages a decentralized lending pool for a single specified collateral asset and a single specified borrowable asset, holding all deposited collateral and borrowed assets. It maintains individual user deposit balances, outstanding loan amounts, and accrued interest for each user, along with global pool-level reserves and interest rate parameters. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow assets against their collateral, repay loans, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated administrator can adjust interest rate models, pause borrowing, and withdraw excess reserves. The maximum loan-to-value ratio is 75% for all loans, and a 0.5% origination fee is applied to all new loans. The contract emits an event upon each deposit, borrow, repay, and withdrawal operation, detailing the user, asset, and amount.
