This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, holding collateral in a designated asset to back the issuance of a stable token. It maintains a global supply of the stable token, a collateral reserve balance, and per-account balances for both the stable token and the collateral asset. Callers can deposit collateral, mint stable tokens against their deposited collateral, redeem stable tokens for collateral, and withdraw their unencumbered collateral. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio and pause or unpause minting and redemption. The initial collateralization ratio is 150%, meaning 1.5 units of collateral are required to mint 1 unit of stable token, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stable token redemptions. The contract announces stable token minting and redemption events, as well as changes to the collateralization ratio.
