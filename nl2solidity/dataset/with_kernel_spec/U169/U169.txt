This contract is a liquid staking mechanism that custodies a base asset and issues a liquid staking token representing staked positions. It maintains individual user balances of the base asset deposited, the corresponding liquid staking token minted, and a global record of the total base asset staked and the total liquid staking token in circulation. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive liquid staking tokens, redeem liquid staking tokens for the base asset, and transfer liquid staking tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can initiate staking of the base asset with an external validator set and claim rewards from those validators. The contract charges a 1% fee on all redeemed base assets, and the redemption queue processes a maximum of 100 requests per block. The contract emits events for all deposits, redemptions, and transfers of liquid staking tokens.
