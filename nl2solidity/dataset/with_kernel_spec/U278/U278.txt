This contract manages a token launchpad, holding deposited tokens from participants and the project tokens being distributed. It maintains a record of each participant's deposited funds, their allocated project tokens, and the current phase of the launch. Callers can deposit funds during the funding phase, claim their allocated project tokens after the distribution phase begins, and withdraw any unspent deposited funds if the launch fails to meet its minimum target. A designated operator can set the launch parameters, advance the launch phase, and initiate the distribution of project tokens. The maximum deposit per participant is 1000 units of the deposited token, and a 2% fee is applied to all successfully claimed project tokens, deducted from the project token amount. The contract announces when a new launch phase begins and when project tokens are successfully claimed.
