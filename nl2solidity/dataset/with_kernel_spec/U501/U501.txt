This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding user collateral in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for various trading pairs, and global configuration parameters such as funding rates and margin requirements. Callers can deposit collateral, open new long or short positions, close existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update trading pair configurations, adjust global margin parameters, and settle funding payments. Each trading pair has a maximum leverage of 50x, and all trades incur a 0.01% taker fee. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, and position closures, including the account involved, the asset, and the amount.
