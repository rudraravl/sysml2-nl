This contract facilitates a cross-chain bridge for a wrapped version of an external asset, holding the native external asset in custody. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped asset, a mapping of account balances for the wrapped asset, and a record of all pending withdrawal requests. Callers can deposit the external asset to mint the wrapped asset, transfer the wrapped asset to other accounts, and request a withdrawal of the external asset by burning the wrapped asset. A designated operator is solely responsible for approving and executing withdrawal requests, and for pausing or unpausing the contract's deposit and withdrawal functions. The contract imposes a 0.1% fee on all withdrawals, and a maximum of 100,000 units of the wrapped asset can be minted per day. The contract emits an event upon every deposit, withdrawal, and transfer, announcing the sender, recipient, and amount.
