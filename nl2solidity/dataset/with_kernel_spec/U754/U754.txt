This contract manages a system for issuing and transferring unique, customizable digital identifiers, holding no custodial assets directly. It maintains a registry of identifiers, their associated owners, and configurable metadata for each, alongside a global configuration for pricing and operational parameters. Callers can register new identifiers, transfer ownership of identifiers they possess, update metadata for their owned identifiers, and revoke identifiers they own. A designated operator can set the base registration fee, pause new registrations, and update the contract's metadata schema. A new identifier registration costs a base fee of 0.01 ether and each identifier can have a maximum of 256 bytes of associated metadata. The contract emits events upon identifier registration, ownership transfer, and metadata updates.
