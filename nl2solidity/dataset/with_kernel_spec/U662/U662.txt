This contract is a decentralized exchange for stablecoins, custodying various stablecoin tokens deposited by users. It maintains a pool for each supported stablecoin pair, tracking reserves, and per-user balances for deposits and liquidity provider shares. Callers can deposit stablecoins into pools, withdraw their share of stablecoins, and swap one stablecoin for another. A designated administrator can add new stablecoin pairs and adjust swap fees. Each swap incurs a 0.05% fee, and the maximum slippage allowed for any swap operation is 1%. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, and swaps, including the amounts involved and the user's address.
