This contract manages a social impact fund, holding deposited base tokens and distributing them to approved projects. It maintains a record of each user's total deposited amount, the current balance of the fund, and a list of approved project addresses with their allocated funding caps. Callers can deposit base tokens into the fund, propose new projects for funding, and claim their allocated funds if they are an approved project. Only the owner can approve new projects, set the funding cap for an approved project, and pause or unpause deposits. A minimum deposit of 10 base tokens is required for any deposit operation. Approved projects can claim up to their allocated funding cap, and a 1% fee is applied to all claimed funds, which is sent to a designated treasury address. The contract announces when new projects are approved, when funds are deposited, and when funds are claimed.
