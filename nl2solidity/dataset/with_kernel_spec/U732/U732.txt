This contract provides a decentralized insurance marketplace, holding deposited collateral in a designated stablecoin to cover potential smart contract vulnerabilities for specified protocols. It maintains a record of each user's deposited collateral, active coverage policies, and claims status, alongside a global pool of available coverage, and a configuration for coverage parameters per insured protocol. Callers can deposit collateral, purchase coverage for a specified protocol and duration, file a claim against an active policy, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can add new protocols for coverage, adjust coverage parameters for existing protocols, and approve or reject claims. The maximum coverage duration for any policy is 365 days. A 2% fee is applied to all coverage purchases, directed to a treasury address. The contract announces when new coverage policies are purchased, when claims are filed, and when claims are approved or rejected.
