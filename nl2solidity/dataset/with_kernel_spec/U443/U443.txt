This contract manages a public goods funding round, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing them to approved projects. It maintains a registry of approved projects, their funding requests, and the total stablecoin balance available for distribution, along with a record of each contributor's stablecoin deposits. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the round, register a new project for consideration, and claim their allocated stablecoin funds if their project is approved. A designated operator can approve or reject project registrations, set the maximum funding amount for any single project, and initiate the distribution of funds to approved projects. The maximum funding amount for any single project is 100,000 stablecoins. A 2% fee is automatically deducted from all stablecoin deposits and directed to a pre-configured treasury address. The contract announces when a new project is registered, when a project is approved or rejected, and when funds are distributed to a project.
