This contract facilitates peer-to-peer non-fungible token lending and borrowing, holding non-fungible tokens offered as collateral and the principal amounts of loans. It maintains individual loan records including the non-fungible token collateral, the principal amount, the interest rate, and the repayment due date, alongside a global configuration for default loan terms and supported non-fungible token collections. Callers can offer non-fungible tokens for collateralized loans, take out loans against offered collateral, repay outstanding loans, and reclaim collateral upon full repayment or loan default. A designated operator can pause all lending and borrowing activity and update the list of supported non-fungible token collections. The maximum loan duration is 30 days, and a 0.5% protocol fee is applied to all interest payments. The contract emits events for new loan originations, loan repayments, and collateral reclamations.
