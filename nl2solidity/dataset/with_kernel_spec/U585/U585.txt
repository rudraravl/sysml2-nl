This contract manages a decentralized exchange's liquidity pools, holding pairs of fungible tokens provided by users. It maintains records of each liquidity pool's token reserves and the total supply of its corresponding liquidity provider tokens, along with each user's balance of these liquidity provider tokens. Callers can add liquidity to a pool, remove liquidity from a pool, swap tokens between pools, and approve spending of their tokens by other addresses. A designated administrator can set the swap fee percentage and add new token pairs to be traded. The swap fee is fixed at 0.3% of the swapped amount, and the minimum initial liquidity required to create a new pool is 100 units of each token in the pair. The contract emits an event whenever liquidity is added or removed from a pool, and whenever a token swap occurs, detailing the tokens involved and their amounts.
