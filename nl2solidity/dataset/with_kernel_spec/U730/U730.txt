This contract manages subscriptions for premium data feeds, holding no custodial assets itself. It maintains a record of each subscriber's active feeds and their subscription end dates, alongside a global configuration of available data feeds and their associated monthly fees. Callers can subscribe to a data feed by paying its monthly fee, renew an existing subscription, and query the status of their subscriptions. The owner can add new data feeds, update the monthly fee for an existing feed, and pause or unpause a specific data feed. A subscription for any feed costs exactly 100 units of the designated payment token per month, and a subscriber can renew for a maximum of 12 months at a time. The contract emits an event when a subscription is initiated or renewed, and when a data feed's status or fee is updated.
