This contract manages a gaming ecosystem's core utility token, holding a reserve of the token for distribution and staking. It maintains individual account balances for staked tokens, a global total supply of staked tokens, and a configuration for staking rewards. Callers can deposit tokens to stake them, withdraw their staked tokens, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can adjust the staking reward rate and initiate new reward periods. The maximum staking reward rate is 10% annually, and a withdrawal incurs a 0.5% fee. The contract emits events when tokens are staked, unstaked, or rewards are claimed.
