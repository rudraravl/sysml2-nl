This contract manages a decentralized exchange for synthetic assets, holding deposited collateral tokens. It maintains a record of each user's collateral balance, open positions for each synthetic asset, and global configuration parameters for each market. Callers can deposit collateral, open a long or short position on a synthetic asset, close an existing position, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can adjust market parameters, including the maximum leverage, and pause all trading operations in an emergency. The maximum leverage for any synthetic asset market is 50x. A 0.1% fee is applied to the notional value of every trade. The contract announces all position openings, closures, and collateral deposits or withdrawals.
