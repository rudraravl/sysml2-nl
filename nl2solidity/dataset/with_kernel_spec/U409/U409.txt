This contract manages a basket of ERC-20 tokens, allowing users to deposit and withdraw from the basket. It maintains the current composition of the basket, the total supply of basket tokens, and each user's balance of basket tokens. Callers can deposit a specific set of ERC-20 tokens into the basket to mint new basket tokens, or redeem basket tokens to withdraw a proportional share of the underlying ERC-20 tokens. A designated operator can adjust the target weights of the underlying ERC-20 tokens within the basket, but only after a 24-hour time lock has passed since the proposed change was announced. The owner can set a deposit fee, which is currently 0.1% of the value deposited, and can also pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The contract announces when new basket tokens are minted, when basket tokens are burned, and when the target weights of the underlying tokens are updated.
