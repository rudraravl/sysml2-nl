This contract is a yield optimizer designed to maximize returns on deposited tokens by strategically allocating them across various external yield-generating strategies. It custodies deposited tokens and their corresponding yield. The contract maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, the specific strategy their funds are allocated to, and their accumulated yield, alongside a global configuration for each available strategy. Callers can deposit supported tokens, withdraw their principal and accumulated yield, and claim accumulated yield without withdrawing principal. A designated operator can add new yield strategies, update existing strategy parameters, and pause or unpause deposits for specific strategies. The contract imposes a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all principal withdrawals. Each yield strategy has a maximum allocation cap of 10,000 units of the underlying token. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, including the user's address, the amount, and the strategy identifier.
