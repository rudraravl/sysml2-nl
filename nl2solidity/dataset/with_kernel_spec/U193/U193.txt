This contract manages a decentralized lending protocol, holding deposited collateral tokens and issued stablecoin tokens. It maintains individual user accounts tracking deposited collateral, borrowed stablecoin, and accrued interest, along with global configuration parameters like interest rates and collateral factors. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow stablecoin against their collateral, repay stablecoin loans, and withdraw their collateral. A designated operator can adjust the interest rate model and add new collateral types, while an owner can pause all borrowing and lending activity. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any loan is 75%, and the stablecoin borrowing annual interest rate is fixed at 5%. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, borrows, and repayments, including the user's address, asset, and amount.
