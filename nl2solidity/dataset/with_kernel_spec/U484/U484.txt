This contract manages user balances of a stablecoin for a debit card spending platform, holding deposited stablecoin tokens in escrow. It maintains individual stablecoin balances for each user and stores a global spending limit configuration. Users can deposit stablecoin into their account, withdraw available stablecoin, and initiate a spending transaction that debits their balance. A designated operator can approve or reject pending spending transactions and set the global daily spending limit. Each user has a daily spending limit of 1,000 stablecoins, and a fee of 10 basis points is applied to each successful spending transaction. The contract emits an event upon every deposit, withdrawal, and approved spending transaction, including the user's address and the amount involved.
