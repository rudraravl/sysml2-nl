This contract is for managing a decentralized network of compute providers and their staked collateral, which it custodies. It maintains records of each provider's staked amount, their current compute power rating, and their accumulated rewards, alongside a global configuration for staking parameters and reward rates. Callers can stake tokens to become a provider, unstake their tokens after a cooldown period, claim earned rewards, and delegate their compute power to other entities. Only a designated operator can adjust the global reward rate, which is capped at 0.05% per block, and set the minimum staking amount, which must be at least 1000 units of the staked token. The contract announces when a provider stakes new tokens, when rewards are claimed, and when the global reward rate is updated.
