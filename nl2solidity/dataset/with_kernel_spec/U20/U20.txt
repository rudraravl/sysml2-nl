This contract manages fixed-rate, fixed-term lending and borrowing positions, holding deposited collateral and borrowed assets. It maintains individual user position data, including collateral amounts, borrowed amounts, and maturity dates, along with global configuration parameters for interest rates and available liquidity pools. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow assets against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw their collateral once loans are repaid or mature. A designated operator can adjust interest rate curves and add or remove supported collateral and borrowable assets. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any position is 75%, and a 0.5% origination fee is charged on all new loans, deducted from the borrowed amount. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, and withdrawals, including the user's identifier, asset type, and amount.
