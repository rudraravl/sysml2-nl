This contract facilitates the on-chain representation and management of tokenized environmental assets, specifically carbon credits, and custodies the underlying fungible carbon credit tokens. It maintains a registry of all deposited carbon credit tokens, tracking their associated metadata such as vintage and project type, and also stores a mapping of each user's balance of wrapped carbon tokens. Callers can deposit approved carbon credit tokens to receive wrapped carbon tokens, redeem wrapped carbon tokens for the underlying carbon credit tokens, and transfer their wrapped carbon tokens to other addresses. A designated operator is privileged to approve new types of carbon credit tokens for deposit and to set the redemption fee. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed amount, and a minimum deposit of 100 carbon credit tokens is required for each deposit operation. The contract announces events for deposits, redemptions, and transfers of wrapped carbon tokens, including the amount and the involved addresses.
