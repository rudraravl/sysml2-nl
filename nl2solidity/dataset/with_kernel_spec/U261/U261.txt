This contract manages a prepaid crypto card system, holding user-deposited stablecoins to back card spending. It maintains individual user balances, a total stablecoin reserve, and a configurable transaction fee. Users can deposit stablecoins to increase their card balance, and initiate spending transactions which deduct from their balance. A designated operator can approve or reject spending transactions, and set the transaction fee. The maximum daily spending limit per user is 1000 stablecoins, and a 0.5% transaction fee is applied to all approved spending. The contract emits events for deposits, approved spending transactions, and rejected spending transactions.
