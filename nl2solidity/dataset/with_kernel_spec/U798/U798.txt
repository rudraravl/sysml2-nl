This contract is a bridge for a wrapped asset, custodying the underlying asset in a vault. It maintains a record of each user's wrapped asset balance and the total supply of the wrapped asset. Callers can deposit the underlying asset to mint the wrapped asset, and they can redeem the wrapped asset to withdraw the underlying asset. A designated operator can pause or unpause all minting and redeeming operations. The redemption fee is 0.1% of the amount redeemed, and a minimum deposit of 0.001 units of the underlying asset is required for minting. The contract announces to observers when assets are deposited, when assets are redeemed, and when the operator changes the contract's paused status.
