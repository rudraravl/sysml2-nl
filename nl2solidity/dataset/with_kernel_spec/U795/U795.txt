This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral in a stablecoin for all open positions. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters for trading pairs. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close positions, modify existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update oracle prices, liquidate undercollateralized positions, and adjust trading pair parameters. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 20x. A 0.1% fee is charged on all trades, deducted from the collateral. The contract announces position openings, position closures, liquidations, and collateral deposits and withdrawals.
