This contract is a lending pool for various digital assets, holding deposited tokens as collateral and issuing new tokens representing debt. It maintains per-account records of deposits, borrowed amounts, and collateralization ratios, along with global pool configurations such as interest rates and liquidation thresholds for each supported asset. Callers can deposit tokens, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can pause borrowing or liquidations, and the owner can add new supported assets, adjust interest rate models, or change the operator. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 75%, and a 5% liquidation penalty is applied to the borrower's collateral during a liquidation event. The contract announces deposit events, borrow events, repayment events, and liquidation events, including the involved accounts, asset types, and amounts.
