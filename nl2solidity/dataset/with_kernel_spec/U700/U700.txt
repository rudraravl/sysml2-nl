This contract facilitates fixed-rate yield exchange and custodies wrapped yield-bearing tokens. It maintains per-account records of deposited principal, active fixed-rate positions, and configuration parameters for each yield-bearing token. Callers can deposit wrapped yield-bearing tokens, open a fixed-rate position specifying a term and desired yield, close an existing position to redeem principal and earned yield, and withdraw their available principal. A designated operator can adjust the maximum allowable fixed-rate yield for new positions, which is capped at 15% annual percentage rate. The contract emits an event when a new fixed-rate position is opened, including the principal amount, the fixed rate, and the maturity timestamp.
