This contract serves as a secure, non-custodial wallet for managing digital assets across multiple chains, specifically holding fungible tokens and non-fungible tokens. It maintains a record of each user's owned tokens, approved spending allowances for other contracts, and a list of authorized linked accounts for cross-chain interactions. Users can deposit tokens, transfer tokens to other addresses, approve spending by other contracts, and initiate cross-chain transfers to their linked accounts. Only the owner can upgrade the contract's logic or pause all transfers in an emergency. A transfer fee of 0.1% is applied to all outgoing fungible token transfers, and a maximum of 10 linked accounts can be associated with a single wallet. The contract emits an event whenever tokens are deposited, transferred, or a cross-chain transfer is initiated.
