This contract tokenizes fractional ownership of physical uranium concentrate, acting as a ledger for these ownership shares. It maintains individual account balances representing ownership shares, a total supply of tokenized shares, and a record of the current custodian's address. Callers can acquire new shares by depositing funds, transfer their existing shares to another account, and redeem their shares for physical uranium concentrate. Only a designated operator can mint new shares, burn existing shares, and update the custodian's address. A minimum redemption amount of 100 shares is enforced, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all share transfers. The contract announces share transfers, share minting, and share burning events.
