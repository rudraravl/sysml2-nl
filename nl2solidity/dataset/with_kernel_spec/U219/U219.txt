This contract manages a tokenized representation of boosted yield positions, holding deposited yield-bearing tokens and their associated voting power. It maintains a mapping of user addresses to their deposited token balances and their corresponding liquid wrapper token balances, along with a global configuration for fees and operator addresses. Callers can deposit yield-bearing tokens to receive liquid wrapper tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens by burning liquid wrapper tokens, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can adjust the fee percentage for withdrawals and update the address of the underlying yield-bearing token contract. Withdrawal fees are capped at 5% of the withdrawn amount, and a minimum deposit of 100 of the yield-bearing token is required. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, and fee updates.
