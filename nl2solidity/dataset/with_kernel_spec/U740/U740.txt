This contract facilitates a decentralized marketplace for non-fungible tokens, holding deposited NFTs and accepted bids in escrow. It maintains individual account balances for marketplace tokens, records of active listings and offers, and global configuration parameters like fees. Callers can list NFTs for sale, make offers on listed NFTs, accept offers, cancel listings or offers, and withdraw their marketplace tokens or NFTs. A designated operator can adjust the marketplace fee and pause all trading activity. The maximum marketplace fee is 2.5%, and all accepted offers must be settled within 24 hours. The contract announces successful NFT sales, new listings, and cancelled offers to observers.
