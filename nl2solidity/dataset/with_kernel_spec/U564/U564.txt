This contract manages a decentralized lending pool, holding deposited collateral tokens and issued loan tokens. It maintains individual account balances for deposited collateral, borrowed loan amounts, and accrued interest, alongside global pool parameters such as interest rates and collateral factors. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow loan tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can adjust interest rates and collateral factors, and initiate liquidations of undercollateralized positions. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrower is 75%, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all loan originations. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, including the affected account, asset, and amount.
