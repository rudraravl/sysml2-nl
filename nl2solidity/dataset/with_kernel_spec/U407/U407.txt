This contract manages a decentralized reserve-backed stable asset, holding various reserve tokens and a native governance token. It maintains a treasury balance for each reserve token, a total supply of the stable asset, and individual balances of the stable asset for each account. Callers can deposit approved reserve tokens to mint the stable asset, stake the stable asset to earn rewards, and unstake the stable asset to reclaim it. A designated operator can adjust the minting rate, distribute rewards to stakers, and add or remove approved reserve tokens. The maximum minting rate for the stable asset is 1000 units per unit of reserve token, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stable asset unstaking operations. The contract announces all minting, staking, and unstaking events, including the account involved and the amount.
