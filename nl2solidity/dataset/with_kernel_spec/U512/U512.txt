This contract is a lending pool for a single specified digital asset, holding deposited tokens and tokens borrowed from it. It maintains individual deposit balances, individual borrow balances, and a global interest rate model configuration. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay their outstanding loans, and liquidate undercollateralized loans. Only a designated administrator can adjust the interest rate model parameters and pause deposits or borrows. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrow is 75%, and a liquidation penalty of 8% is applied to the borrower's outstanding debt. The contract announces deposit events, borrow events, repayment events, and liquidation events.
