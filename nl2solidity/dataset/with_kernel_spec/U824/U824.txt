This contract is a liquid staking pool for a base asset, holding deposited base assets and issuing a corresponding liquid staking token. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, a total amount of staked base assets, and a mapping of each user's liquid staking token balance. Callers can deposit base assets to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw base assets by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim staking rewards proportional to their share of the pool. A designated operator can update the exchange rate between the base asset and the liquid staking token based on staking rewards and penalties, and can pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The minimum deposit amount is 0.1 base assets, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract emits an event upon each deposit, withdrawal, and exchange rate update.
