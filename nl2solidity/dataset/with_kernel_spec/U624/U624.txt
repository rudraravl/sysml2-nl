This contract manages a stablecoin backed by a basket of yield-bearing assets, holding the underlying collateral tokens. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin, a global reserve pool of collateral, and a configuration for various operational parameters. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing approved collateral, redeem stablecoins for the underlying collateral, and transfer stablecoins between accounts. A designated operator can pause all minting and redemption operations, and a separate owner can update the list of approved collateral tokens and adjust the redemption fee. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.1% of the redeemed amount, and the contract enforces a minimum collateralization ratio of 105% for all minted stablecoins. The contract emits events for all minting, redemption, and transfer actions, as well as for changes to the operational parameters.
