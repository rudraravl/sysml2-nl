This contract is a vault for tokenized real-world assets, holding various ERC-20 tokens representing fractional ownership in yield-generating strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited assets, their share of the vault, and the total value of assets under management, along with configuration parameters for each strategy. Users can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their share of the underlying assets, and claim accrued yield. A designated operator can add new yield strategies, update strategy parameters, and initiate rebalancing operations. The vault imposes a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all redemptions, and a maximum of 10 new strategies can be added per month. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, yield claims, and strategy additions.
