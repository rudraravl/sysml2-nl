This contract manages a social media platform's core token, which represents a user's stake in creator communities. It maintains individual user balances of the platform token, a global supply of the token, and a mapping of creators to their associated community tokens. Callers can acquire platform tokens, transfer platform tokens to other users, stake platform tokens with a creator to receive community tokens, and unstake platform tokens to redeem their community tokens. Only a designated operator can mint new platform tokens, up to a maximum of 1,000,000 tokens per month. The staking fee is fixed at 0.5% of the staked amount, deducted from the platform tokens. The contract emits an event whenever platform tokens are transferred, staked, or unstaked.
