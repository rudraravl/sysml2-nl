This contract manages token streaming agreements, holding deposited tokens for scheduled distribution. It maintains individual stream records detailing recipient, token type, start time, end time, and total amount, alongside a global configuration for fees. Callers can create new streams, deposit tokens into existing streams, cancel their own streams, and withdraw tokens from streams where they are the recipient. A designated operator can pause all new stream creation and update the fee percentage. Each stream must have a duration of at least 24 hours. A 0.1% fee is applied to all tokens streamed, deducted at the time of withdrawal. The contract announces when a new stream is created, when tokens are deposited into a stream, and when tokens are withdrawn from a stream.
