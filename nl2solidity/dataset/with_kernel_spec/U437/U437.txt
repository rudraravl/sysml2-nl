This contract facilitates the trading of non-fungible tokens (NFTs) from designated collections, holding listed NFTs in escrow during active listings. It maintains a record of all active listings, including the seller, the NFT identifier, the asking price, and any active bids for each listed NFT. Callers can list an NFT for sale, make an offer on a listed NFT, accept an offer, cancel their own listing, and withdraw an NFT they previously listed if it has not been sold. Only a designated operator can add or remove approved NFT collections for listing. A listing fee of 0.02 ether is charged upon successful sale, and the maximum number of active listings per seller is 100. The contract announces when an NFT is listed, when an offer is made, when an offer is accepted, and when a listing is canceled.
