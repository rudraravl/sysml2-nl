This contract manages the issuance and ownership of unique digital collectible tokens, each representing a physical trading card, and custodies the associated physical card inventory. It maintains a record of each digital token's owner, its unique identifier, and its corresponding physical card's status (e.g., "vaulted", "redeemed"). Callers can open digital packs to receive new tokens, transfer tokens to other accounts, redeem tokens for their physical counterparts, or sell tokens back to the contract. Only a designated operator can mint new digital packs and update the physical card inventory status, while only the contract owner can set the pack opening fee. A pack opening always costs 0.05 units of the native currency, and a maximum of 10,000 unique digital tokens can ever be minted. The contract announces when a new pack is opened, when a token is transferred, and when a token is redeemed.
