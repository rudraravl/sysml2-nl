This contract manages a no-loss creator funding platform, holding staked Ether and distributing native yield. It maintains records of individual user deposits, creator pools, and the allocation of yield to each pool. Callers can create a new creator pool, deposit Ether into an existing pool, withdraw their deposited Ether, and claim their accumulated yield and bonus rewards. A designated operator can set the bonus reward rate and update the external yield source address. A newly created pool must have a minimum initial deposit of 0.1 Ether, and a 5% fee is applied to all claimed yield. The contract emits events when Ether is deposited, Ether is withdrawn, yield is claimed, and a new pool is created.
