This contract manages a vault for depositing and staking a specific yield-bearing token, holding deposited tokens and their accumulated yield. It maintains individual account balances for deposited tokens, the current total staked amount, and a configurable fee percentage. Callers can deposit the yield-bearing token, withdraw their deposited tokens plus any accrued yield, and claim rewards generated from staking activities. Only a designated operator can initiate staking of deposited tokens into an external yield-generating protocol and unstake them, and only the contract owner can set the staking fee, which must be between 0% and 10% inclusive, and update the address of the external yield-generating protocol. Withdrawals are subject to a 24-hour unbonding period after an unstake request is made. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the staking fee.
