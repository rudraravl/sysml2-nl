This contract manages a social token distribution and bonding curve, holding the native blockchain currency and the issued social tokens. It maintains a record of each user's purchased social tokens, their corresponding native currency contributions, and the current price and supply of the social tokens. Callers can purchase social tokens by sending native currency, sell social tokens to receive native currency, and claim any native currency from sales of their social tokens. Only a designated operator can set the initial price of the social token and adjust the bonding curve parameters. The initial price of a social token is 0.001 native currency, and a 5% fee is applied to all social token sales, which is directed to a treasury address. The contract announces social token purchases and sales, including the buyer, seller, amount of social tokens, and amount of native currency exchanged.
