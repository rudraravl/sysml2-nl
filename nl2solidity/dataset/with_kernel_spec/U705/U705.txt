This contract manages a decentralized investment fund, holding various digital assets contributed by investors. It maintains individual investor balances, a record of the total assets under management, and a configuration for investment strategies. Callers can deposit funds into the contract, withdraw their share of the fund, and propose new investment strategies for approval. A designated operator can approve or reject proposed strategies, execute approved investment strategies, and adjust certain fund parameters. The fund charges a 0.5% management fee on all deposits, and a minimum deposit amount is 100 units of the base currency. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, and strategy approvals.
