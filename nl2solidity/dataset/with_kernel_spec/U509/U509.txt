This contract is a cross-chain asset bridge for a yield-bearing stablecoin, holding deposited tokens on one chain to mint wrapped representations on another. It maintains a total supply of wrapped tokens minted per destination chain, a per-user record of deposited and withdrawn amounts, and a configurable fee structure. Callers can deposit the native stablecoin, initiate a withdrawal of wrapped tokens from a specific destination chain, and claim their native stablecoin after a successful withdrawal. A designated operator can pause deposits and withdrawals, update the withdrawal fee percentage, and approve new destination chains for bridging. The maximum withdrawal fee is 0.5% of the withdrawn amount, and a minimum of 100 native stablecoin tokens must be deposited for any cross-chain transfer. The contract emits an event upon every deposit, withdrawal initiation, and successful claim.
