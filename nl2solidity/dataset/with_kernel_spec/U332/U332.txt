This contract issues a transferable digital token representing an indirect fractional non-voting economic interest in a venture fund. It maintains each account's token balance and a global total supply. Callers can transfer tokens between accounts, approve another account to spend their tokens, and spend approved tokens on behalf of another account. A designated operator can mint new tokens up to a hard cap of 1,000,000,000 tokens and burn tokens from any account. Only the owner can pause all token transfers, and only the owner can unpause them. The contract announces token transfers, approvals, and changes in the total supply.
