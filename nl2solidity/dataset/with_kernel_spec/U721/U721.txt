This contract manages a liquid staking derivative (LSD) system, holding staked base tokens and issuing corresponding liquid tokens. It maintains a global total of staked base tokens, the total supply of liquid tokens, and for each user, their balance of liquid tokens. Callers can deposit base tokens to receive liquid tokens, redeem liquid tokens for base tokens, and transfer liquid tokens to other users. A designated operator can pause deposits and withdrawals, and set the fee for redemptions. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.1% of the redeemed amount, and the maximum redemption amount per transaction is 1000 base tokens. The contract announces events for deposits, redemptions, and liquid token transfers.
