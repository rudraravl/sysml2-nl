This contract is for an NFT marketplace, facilitating the creation, listing, and sale of unique digital assets. It maintains a registry of all listed NFTs, their current owner, their listed price, and any associated royalty percentages. Callers can list an NFT for sale, purchase a listed NFT, delist their own NFT, and transfer ownership of an unlisted NFT. Only a designated administrator can set the platform's listing fee and the maximum royalty percentage. The platform charges a 2% fee on all successful NFT sales, and the maximum royalty an NFT creator can set is 10%. The contract announces when an NFT is listed, when it is sold, and when its ownership is transferred.
