This contract manages a decentralized insurance pool, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing coverage payouts. It maintains individual user deposit balances, active coverage policies with their associated premiums and coverage limits, and a global configuration for fees and payout thresholds. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the pool, request new coverage policies by specifying a coverage amount and duration, submit claims against active policies, and withdraw their available stablecoin balance. A designated operator can approve or reject claims, and only the owner can update the global configuration parameters. A minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins is required to activate a coverage policy, and a 5% fee is charged on all approved claim payouts. The contract announces when new coverage policies are issued, when claims are submitted, and when claims are approved or rejected.
