This contract facilitates peer-to-peer over-the-counter trades of non-fungible tokens, holding deposited NFTs and associated payment tokens in escrow during the trade process. It maintains records of active offers, including the NFT identifier offered, the requested payment token and amount, and the expiration time for each offer. Callers can create new offers to sell an NFT, accept an existing offer by providing the requested payment, or cancel their own active offers. A designated operator can pause all trading activity, preventing new offers or acceptances, and can update the fee percentage applied to successful trades. A trade fee of 0.5% of the payment token value is deducted from the seller's proceeds upon successful acceptance of an offer. Offers are valid for a maximum of 7 days from their creation. The contract announces when an offer is created, when an offer is accepted, and when an offer is cancelled.
