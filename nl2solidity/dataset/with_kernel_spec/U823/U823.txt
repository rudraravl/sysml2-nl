This contract facilitates efficient, customizable NFT trading by managing liquidity pools for various NFT collections and their associated fungible tokens. It maintains state for each pool, including its NFT collection address, fungible token balance, NFT inventory, and configurable trading parameters like curves and fees. Callers can create new pools, add liquidity to existing pools by depositing NFTs or fungible tokens, remove liquidity, and execute trades by buying or selling NFTs. A designated operator can adjust pool trading curves, update royalty recipient addresses, and set the platform-wide trading fee percentage, which defaults to 0.5% and cannot exceed 2%. The contract announces successful trades, liquidity additions, and liquidity removals to observers.
