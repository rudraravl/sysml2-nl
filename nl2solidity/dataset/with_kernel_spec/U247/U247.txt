This contract is a portfolio management vault, designed to custody various ERC-20 tokens representing diversified investment strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited assets, their proportional share of the vault's total assets, and the current configuration of the underlying investment strategies. Callers can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their proportional share of the vault's assets, and claim any accrued rewards. The owner can add or remove supported tokens, adjust the allocation of assets among strategies, and set the management fee. A management fee of 0.5% of the total vault assets will be charged annually, and a minimum deposit amount of 100 units of any supported token is enforced. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the management fee.
