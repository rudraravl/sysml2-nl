This contract manages the collateralization of real-world assets, holding a designated stablecoin as backing for issued debt. It maintains a record of each asset's collateralization ratio, its current valuation, and the total outstanding debt associated with it. Callers can deposit stablecoin collateral, withdraw excess collateral, draw debt against collateral, and repay debt. A designated operator can approve new real-world assets for collateralization, update their valuations, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The minimum collateralization ratio for any asset is 150%, and a 0.5% liquidation penalty is applied to the liquidated amount. The contract announces when new assets are approved, when collateralization ratios change, and when liquidations occur.
