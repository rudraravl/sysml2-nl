This contract manages a pool of stablecoin funds for lending, holding deposited stablecoins and tracking individual lender balances. It maintains a record of each lender's deposited amount, accrued interest, and any pending withdrawals, alongside an overall pool balance and configuration parameters like interest rates and fees. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the pool, withdraw their available balance, and claim accrued interest. A designated operator can adjust the interest rate within a predefined range, pause deposits, and initiate bulk disbursements to borrowers. The maximum deposit amount per transaction is 100,000 stablecoins, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all interest payments before distribution to lenders. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and interest rate updates.
