This contract manages a collection of unique, non-fungible digital assets representing collectible game cards, holding the cards themselves and the associated in-game currency used for purchases. It maintains individual balances of the in-game currency for each player, records of card ownership, and a global configuration for card pack prices. Players can purchase card packs using the in-game currency, open purchased packs to receive random cards, and transfer cards they own to other players. A designated operator can mint new cards, set the price of card packs, and distribute in-game currency to players. The maximum number of cards a player can hold is 500. The fee for transferring a card between players is 10 units of in-game currency. The contract emits an event when a card pack is purchased, when a card is minted, and when a card is transferred.
