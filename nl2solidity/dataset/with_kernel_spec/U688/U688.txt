This contract facilitates the creation and launch of new fungible tokens, holding the initial supply of the newly created token. It maintains a record of each launched token's configuration, including its total supply and the address of its creator, and a mapping of creator addresses to the number of tokens they have launched. Callers can create a new token by providing a name, symbol, and initial supply, and they can participate in a "meme battle" by submitting a token for consideration. The contract owner is privileged and can set the fee for token creation and designate specific tokens as winners of the meme battle. A token creation fee of 0.1 ether is charged for each new token launch, and a maximum of 100 tokens can be launched per creator. The contract announces the successful creation of a new token and the designation of a meme battle winner.
