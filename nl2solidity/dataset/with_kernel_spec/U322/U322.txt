This contract is a liquid staking vault for a single underlying proof-of-stake asset, holding deposited tokens and issuing a corresponding liquid staking derivative token. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking derivative, the total amount of underlying staked, and per-user balances of the liquid staking derivative. Callers can deposit the underlying asset to receive the liquid staking derivative, and they can redeem the liquid staking derivative for the underlying asset. A designated operator can initiate staking of deposited assets with validators and claim staking rewards, and they can also set the fee percentage applied to claimed rewards. The contract charges a 10% fee on all claimed staking rewards, and the minimum deposit amount for the underlying asset is 0.01 units. The contract announces events for deposits, redemptions, reward claims, and fee percentage changes.
