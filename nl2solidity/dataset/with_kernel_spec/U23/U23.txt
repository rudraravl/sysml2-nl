This contract is a prediction market for coin flips, holding user bets until the outcome is determined. It maintains a record of each user's outstanding bets, the total amount staked on heads, and the total amount staked on tails for each flip, along with a configuration for the house edge and minimum bet. Users can place a bet on heads or tails, and after a flip's outcome is determined, they can claim their winnings or retrieve their losing bet. A designated operator can initiate a new coin flip, record the outcome of a flip, and update the house edge. The house edge is fixed at 2.5% of the winning pot, and the minimum bet for any flip is 100 units of the accepted token. The contract announces when a new flip is initiated, when a bet is placed, and when a flip's outcome is recorded.
