This contract manages a streaming payment system, holding deposited tokens that are continuously disbursed over time. It maintains individual stream records, each detailing the sender, recipient, token type, total amount, start time, and end time. Callers can create new streams, deposit tokens into existing streams, withdraw tokens from streams they are recipients of, and cancel streams they initiated. Only the owner can pause all stream creation and update the fee percentage for new streams. A stream's duration must be at least one minute and at most five years. The contract charges a 0.1% fee on the total amount of each newly created stream, deducted from the sender's deposit. The contract announces stream creations, stream cancellations, and withdrawals to observers.
