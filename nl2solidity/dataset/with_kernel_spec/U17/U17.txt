This contract facilitates cross-chain asset wrapping, holding original ERC-20 and ERC-721 tokens in custody to back their wrapped representations on a different blockchain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited tokens, the total supply of each wrapped asset, and configurable parameters for wrapping and unwrapping. Callers can deposit ERC-20 or ERC-721 tokens to mint wrapped tokens, and they can burn wrapped tokens to withdraw their original assets. A designated operator can pause all wrapping and unwrapping operations, and the owner can update the fee percentage. A 0.5% fee is applied to all unwrapping operations, and the maximum number of unique ERC-20 token types that can be wrapped is limited to 100. The contract announces events for successful deposits, withdrawals, and changes to the fee percentage.
