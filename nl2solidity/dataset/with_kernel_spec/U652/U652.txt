This contract facilitates private transfers of a fungible token by allowing users to deposit and withdraw tokens, obscuring the link between deposits and withdrawals. It custodies deposited fungible tokens. The contract maintains a record of each user's private balance represented by a set of unspent notes, a global total supply of notes, and a configuration for the deposit fee. Callers can deposit tokens, generating new private notes, and can withdraw tokens by spending existing private notes. A designated operator can update the deposit fee. The deposit fee is fixed at 0.05% of the deposited amount. A withdrawal transaction must spend at least two existing private notes. The contract announces an event for every successful deposit and every successful withdrawal.
