This contract is a yield optimization vault that custodies deposited tokens. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, their share of the vault's total value, and the current allocation of vault funds across various external strategies. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their share of tokens, and claim any accumulated rewards. Only a designated operator can rebalance the vault's allocation to different strategies and update the list of approved strategies. A minimum deposit of 100 tokens is enforced, and a 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit, withdrawal, and rebalancing events.
