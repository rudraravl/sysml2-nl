This contract manages a marketplace for non-fungible tokens and custodies deposited tokens and non-fungible tokens awaiting sale or transfer. It maintains a record of listed non-fungible tokens with their prices and sellers, and a balance of fungible tokens held for each user. Callers can list a non-fungible token for sale, purchase a listed non-fungible token, cancel a listing, and withdraw their fungible token balance. A designated operator can pause all listing and purchasing activities and update the marketplace fee percentage. The marketplace charges a 2.5% fee on all successful non-fungible token sales, deducted from the seller's proceeds. A non-fungible token listing remains active for a maximum of 30 days unless purchased or canceled. The contract announces when a non-fungible token is listed, purchased, or a listing is canceled.
