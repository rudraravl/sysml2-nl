This contract is a liquid staking vault for a base asset, holding deposited tokens and issuing a liquid staking derivative in return. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking derivative, a mapping of user balances for the derivative, and a configuration for the current exchange rate between the base asset and the derivative. Callers can deposit the base asset, withdraw their deposited base asset by burning the derivative, and redeem their derivative for the underlying base asset. A designated operator can update the exchange rate and initiate rebalancing operations. The contract imposes a 0.1% withdrawal fee on all base asset withdrawals. The exchange rate can only be updated once every 24 hours. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, and exchange rate updates.
