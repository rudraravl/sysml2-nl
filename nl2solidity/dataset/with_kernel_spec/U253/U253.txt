This contract is a coverage pool for insuring against smart contract vulnerabilities, holding deposited stablecoins as collateral. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance, their active coverage policies, and the total stablecoins available in the pool. Callers can deposit stablecoins, purchase coverage policies specifying a target contract and coverage amount, file claims against active policies, and withdraw their available stablecoin balance. Only a designated operator can approve or reject claims, and only the owner can update the premium rate or add new target contracts for coverage. A minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins is required to purchase a policy, and a 5% fee is deducted from all approved claim payouts. The contract announces when a new policy is purchased, when a claim is filed, and when a claim is approved or rejected.
