This contract manages a stablecoin, USD0, and custodies its underlying collateral, which consists solely of USDC. It maintains individual account balances for USD0, the total supply of USD0, and a record of the current collateralization ratio. Callers can mint new USD0 by depositing USDC, redeem USD0 for USDC, and transfer USD0 between accounts. A designated operator can pause or unpause minting and redemption, and can adjust the fee for minting. The contract ensures that the collateralization ratio never falls below 100%, and a minting fee of 0.1% is applied to all new USD0 minted. The contract emits an event whenever USD0 is minted, redeemed, or transferred, and when the minting fee is updated.
