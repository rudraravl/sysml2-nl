This contract facilitates continuous, real-time token transfers between accounts, holding deposited tokens in escrow until streamed. It maintains a record for each sender detailing their deposited balance, the recipient of their stream, the rate of transfer per second, and the start time of the stream. Callers can deposit tokens into their account, create a new stream specifying a recipient and a rate, update an existing stream's recipient or rate, and withdraw any unstreamed or received tokens. Only the designated owner can pause all streaming activity or update the fee percentage applied to all outgoing streams. A stream's rate must be greater than zero and cannot exceed 1,000 tokens per second. All outgoing stream payments incur a 2.5% fee, deducted from the sender's balance and transferred to a designated fee recipient. The contract emits an event whenever a new stream is created, an existing stream is updated, or tokens are withdrawn.
