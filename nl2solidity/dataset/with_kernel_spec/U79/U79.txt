This contract issues and manages a tokenized representation of short-term U.S. government debt, holding no custodied assets itself but relying on an external system of record for underlying asset ownership. It maintains individual user balances of the tokenized debt, a total supply, and a configurable fee rate for redemptions. Callers can mint new tokens by providing proof of external asset ownership, transfer tokens to other users, and redeem tokens for the underlying asset. A designated operator can pause all minting and redemption operations, and can adjust the redemption fee, which is capped at 0.5% of the redeemed amount. The contract will emit events for all minting, redemption, and transfer actions, and for any changes to the redemption fee.
