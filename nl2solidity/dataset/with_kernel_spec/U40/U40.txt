This contract is a decentralized prediction market that custodies user deposits in a designated base currency. It maintains a record of open markets, each with a unique identifier, a set of possible outcomes, and the total liquidity provided for each outcome, along with individual user positions (outcome chosen, amount staked) within each market. Callers can create new markets, deposit funds into an outcome of an existing market, withdraw their share of funds from a market before it resolves, and claim winnings after a market has resolved. Only a designated operator can resolve a market by declaring a winning outcome and only after a specified resolution time has passed. Market creation requires a minimum initial deposit of 100 units of the base currency, and a 2% fee is applied to all winnings claimed. The contract announces market creation, deposits, withdrawals, market resolution, and winnings claims as events.
