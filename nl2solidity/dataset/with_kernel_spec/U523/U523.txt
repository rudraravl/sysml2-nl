This contract facilitates private transfers of a designated base token, holding deposited tokens in escrow. It maintains a Merkle tree of deposit commitments, a mapping of nullifiers to booleans indicating spent commitments, and a record of the current deposit and withdrawal fees. Callers can deposit tokens by providing a commitment hash, and they can withdraw tokens by providing a valid zero-knowledge proof that corresponds to an unspent commitment and a nullifier. A designated owner can update the deposit fee, update the withdrawal fee, and set the verifier contract address. The deposit fee is fixed at 0.1% of the deposited amount, and the withdrawal fee is fixed at 0.2% of the withdrawn amount. The contract emits an event upon each successful deposit, including the commitment hash and the amount, and an event upon each successful withdrawal, including the nullifier and the recipient.
