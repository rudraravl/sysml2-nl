This contract manages a liquid staking mechanism for a proof-of-stake blockchain, holding deposited native tokens and issuing corresponding liquid staking tokens. It maintains a record of each staker's deposited amount and the amount of liquid staking tokens they hold, along with a global total of staked tokens and a current exchange rate between the native token and the liquid staking token. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw their native tokens by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim rewards accrued from staking. A designated operator can update the staking rewards distribution and set the protocol fee. The minimum deposit amount is 10 native tokens, and the protocol charges a 5% fee on all staking rewards before distribution. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward distribution events to observers.
