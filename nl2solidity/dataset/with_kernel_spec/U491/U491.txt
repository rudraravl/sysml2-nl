This contract manages a decentralized prediction market, holding collateral tokens deposited by users for their predictions. It maintains individual user balances for each market, the total collateral locked per market, and the final outcome for each market. Callers can create new prediction markets, place predictions on market outcomes, claim winnings from resolved markets, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator is privileged to resolve markets by declaring the final outcome, and to pause or unpause the contract. Each prediction market must have a resolution delay of at least 24 hours from its creation. A 1% fee is applied to all market winnings, deducted at the time of claiming. The contract announces market creation, prediction placements, market resolution, and winning claims.
