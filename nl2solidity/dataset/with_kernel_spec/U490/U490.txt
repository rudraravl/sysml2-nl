This contract is a treasury for a decentralized reserve currency, holding a basket of approved collateral tokens. It maintains a global reserve ratio, a list of approved collateral tokens with their individual target weights, and a total supply of the protocol's native token. Callers can deposit approved collateral to mint the native token, redeem the native token for a proportional share of the collateral, and transfer the native token. A designated operator can adjust the target weights of approved collateral tokens, add new collateral tokens to the approved list, and remove existing collateral tokens. The reserve ratio must always be at least 100%, and the maximum number of approved collateral tokens is 10. The contract announces all minting and redemption events, including the amount of native token minted or redeemed and the collateral involved.
