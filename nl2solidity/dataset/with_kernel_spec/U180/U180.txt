This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers by holding wrapped versions of various cryptocurrencies. It maintains a mapping of each user's balance for every wrapped asset, along with a global configuration for each asset's bridging parameters. Callers can deposit an asset to receive its wrapped equivalent, redeem a wrapped asset for its native form on a different chain, and query their wrapped asset balances. Only a designated operator can add new wrapped assets, adjust bridging fees, or pause transfers for a specific asset. The maximum bridging fee for any asset is 0.5% of the transfer amount, and a minimum transfer amount of 0.001 wrapped asset units is enforced for all redemptions. The contract announces events for successful deposits, successful redemptions, and changes to an asset's bridging fee.
