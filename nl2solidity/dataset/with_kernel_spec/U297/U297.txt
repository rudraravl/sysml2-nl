This contract is a yield-bearing token representing fractional ownership of a basket of tokenized short-term government debt instruments. It maintains each user's balance of the yield-bearing token, the total supply of the token, and a record of the current underlying asset value per token. Callers can deposit an approved stablecoin to mint new yield-bearing tokens, and they can redeem their yield-bearing tokens for the underlying stablecoin. An authorized administrator can update the underlying asset value per token, adjust the redemption fee, and pause all deposit and redemption operations. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.1% of the redeemed amount. The contract will emit an event whenever new tokens are minted or existing tokens are redeemed.
