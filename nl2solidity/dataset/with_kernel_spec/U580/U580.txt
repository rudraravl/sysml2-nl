This contract is for tokenizing a share of an institutional money market fund, custodying the fund shares themselves as an off-chain asset. It maintains individual balances for each token holder and a total supply of tokens, along with a configurable daily redemption limit and a list of authorized participants. Callers can deposit fiat currency to mint tokens, redeem tokens for fiat currency, and transfer tokens between authorized participants. A designated operator can pause and unpause all transfers, update the daily redemption limit, and add or remove authorized participants. The daily redemption limit for all participants combined is capped at 1,000,000 units of the underlying asset. All redemption requests are subject to a 24-hour waiting period before processing. The contract announces token transfers, minting events, and redemption requests to observers.
