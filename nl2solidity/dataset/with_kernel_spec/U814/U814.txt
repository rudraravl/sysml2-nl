This contract manages a token launchpad, holding deposited tokens from participants and the newly issued project tokens. It maintains a record of each participant's deposited amount and their allocated share of project tokens, along with the current state of the launch phase. Callers can deposit tokens during the open phase, claim their allocated project tokens after the launch concludes, and withdraw any unspent deposited tokens if the launch fails to meet its minimum threshold. The owner can set the launch parameters, initiate the launch, and finalize it. A launch must raise at least 100,000 units of the deposited token to be considered successful, and the owner can only finalize a launch after a minimum 24-hour participation period. The contract announces when a launch starts, when it ends, and when project tokens are claimed.
