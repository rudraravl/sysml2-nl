This contract is for decentralized insurance underwriting, custodying staked collateral in a designated stablecoin to back insurance policies. It maintains per-policy records including coverage amount, premium paid, expiration date, and claim status, along with a global pool of staked collateral and a list of supported insurance products. Callers can stake collateral, purchase insurance policies for supported products, file claims against their active policies, and withdraw their unstaked collateral. A designated operator can approve or reject claims, add new insurance products, and adjust premium rates for existing products. The maximum coverage amount for any single policy is 10,000 units of the stablecoin. A 5% fee is deducted from all successfully paid claims, which is added to the collateral pool. The contract announces policy purchases, claim filings, claim approvals, and claim rejections.
