This contract manages a social content platform's creator-follower relationships and associated value transfers, holding platform fees and creator earnings. It maintains a record of each creator's active followers, their subscription tiers, and the total value accumulated for each creator. Callers can subscribe to a creator at a specified tier, unsubscribe from a creator, and creators can withdraw their accumulated earnings. Only a designated operator can set the subscription price for each tier and register new creators. A subscription tier must have a minimum monthly price of 0.001 ether, and a 5% platform fee is deducted from all subscription payments. The contract announces when a new subscription is created, when an existing subscription is canceled, and when a creator successfully withdraws funds.
