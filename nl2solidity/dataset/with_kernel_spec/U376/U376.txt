This contract is a marketplace for non-fungible tokens, holding deposited tokens for sale and the proceeds from sales. It maintains a record of listed tokens, their prices, the seller for each listing, and the current balance of marketplace fees collected. Callers can list a non-fungible token for sale, purchase a listed token, cancel their own listing, and withdraw their sales proceeds. Only the owner can set the marketplace fee percentage, which defaults to 2.5% and cannot exceed 10%, and only the owner can withdraw accumulated marketplace fees. A token can only be purchased if its listed price is paid in full, and a listing can only be cancelled by the original seller. The contract announces when a token is listed, when a token is sold, and when a listing is cancelled.
