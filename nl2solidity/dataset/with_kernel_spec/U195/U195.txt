This contract is a wrapper for a stablecoin pegged to a fiat currency, holding the underlying stablecoin as its sole asset. It maintains a total supply of wrapper tokens and a per-account balance of wrapper tokens. Callers can deposit the underlying stablecoin to mint wrapper tokens, and redeem wrapper tokens for the underlying stablecoin. A designated operator is privileged to update the exchange rate between the wrapper token and the underlying stablecoin, and only the owner can set the designated operator. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.1% of the redeemed amount, and the exchange rate can only be updated once every 24 hours. The contract announces when the exchange rate is updated and when tokens are minted or burned.
