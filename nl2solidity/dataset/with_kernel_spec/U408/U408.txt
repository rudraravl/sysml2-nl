This contract is a payment routing hub, holding user-deposited stablecoins for secure transfers. It maintains individual user balances, a list of registered merchants, and a configurable transaction fee. Callers can deposit stablecoins, initiate payments to registered merchants, and withdraw their available balance. The contract owner can register new merchants, update the transaction fee, and pause all payment operations. A maximum of 1000 merchants can be registered, and all payments incur a 0.5% fee, deducted from the sender's balance. The contract announces successful deposits, completed payments, and withdrawals through emitted events.
