This contract establishes a lending market for a specific token, holding deposited tokens as collateral. It maintains individual account balances for deposits and borrows, a global pool of available liquidity, and market-specific configuration parameters like interest rates. Callers can deposit tokens, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding borrows, withdraw their collateral, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can adjust market parameters such as interest rates and collateral factors, and pause borrowing or depositing in emergencies. The maximum collateral factor for any market is 90%, meaning borrowers can borrow up to 90% of the value of their collateral. A 0.5% liquidation fee is applied to the liquidator's reward. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, withdrawals, and liquidations, including the affected accounts and amounts.
