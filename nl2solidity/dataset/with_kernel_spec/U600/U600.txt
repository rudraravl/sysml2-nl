This contract manages concentrated liquidity positions for token pairs, holding deposited tokens and tracking their value. It maintains records of individual liquidity positions, including their token amounts, price ranges, and accumulated fees, alongside global pool state such as current prices and fee growth. Callers can create new liquidity positions, add to existing positions, remove liquidity, collect accumulated fees, and swap tokens within a pool. Only the designated owner can update the protocol-wide fee tier configuration or pause all trading activity. Each liquidity position must specify a price range with a minimum tick spacing of 10 basis points between the upper and lower bounds. A 0.3% fee is charged on all token swaps, which is distributed proportionally to active liquidity providers. The contract announces all liquidity provision events, token swaps, and fee collection actions.
