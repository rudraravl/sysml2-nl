This contract is for managing verifiable attestations and does not custody any tokens. It maintains a registry of unique attestation identifiers, each linked to an issuer's address, a schema identifier, and a cryptographic hash of the attestation data. Callers can issue new attestations, revoke existing attestations they previously issued, and query the status and details of any attestation by its identifier. Only the original issuer of an attestation may revoke it. A maximum of 10,000 attestations can be issued per schema identifier. The contract announces when an attestation is issued and when an attestation is revoked.
