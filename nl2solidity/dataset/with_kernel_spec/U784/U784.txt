This contract is a yield aggregator that accepts and custodies a single designated base token, depositing it into various external yield-generating strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, the total amount deposited in each strategy, and a configuration for each strategy including its current allocation percentage. Callers can deposit the base token, withdraw their deposited amount, and claim any accumulated yield. The owner can add new strategies, remove existing strategies, update the allocation percentage for each strategy, and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The contract will only allow a maximum of 10 active strategies at any given time, and a 0.5% fee is charged on all withdrawals, which is directed to a designated treasury address. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, including the user's address, the amount, and the strategy involved if applicable.
