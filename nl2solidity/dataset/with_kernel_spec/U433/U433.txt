This contract manages a provably fair prediction game where users bet on a multiplier reaching a certain value before a "crash" event. It custodies a stablecoin used for betting and payouts. The contract maintains a global game state including the current round number, the crash multiplier for the previous round, and a pool of stablecoin for house liquidity. For each player, it tracks their current bet amount, their chosen cash-out multiplier, and their stablecoin balance. Callers can place a bet, cash out their active bet, or withdraw their available stablecoin balance. A designated operator can initiate new game rounds and set the outcome of a round. The minimum bet amount is 10 stablecoins, and the house takes a 1% fee on all winning bets. The contract announces the start of a new round, the crash multiplier of a completed round, and player bet placements and cash-outs.
