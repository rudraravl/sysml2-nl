This contract manages player progression and resource ownership within an on-chain RPG, holding player-owned tokens representing in-game items and resources. It maintains individual player records including experience points, current level, and quantities of various item types. Callers can perform actions such as leveling up their character, crafting new items from existing resources, and consuming items for in-game effects. A designated operator is privileged to mint new item tokens and adjust crafting recipes. Players must reach level 5 before they can craft rare items, and crafting a legendary item requires a 10-block delay before the item is delivered. The contract announces player level-ups and successful item crafts to observers.
