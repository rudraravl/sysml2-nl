This contract is a stable-value token vault, custodying a reserve of a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances for the stable-value token and its yield-bearing staked counterpart, as well as a global total supply for each, and a current exchange rate between the staked token and the underlying stable-value token. Callers can deposit the designated stablecoin to mint the stable-value token, stake the stable-value token to receive the yield-bearing staked token, unstake the yield-bearing staked token to redeem the stable-value token, and withdraw the underlying stablecoin by burning the stable-value token. An owner can set the designated stablecoin address and adjust the staking reward rate, which will be capped at 5% annually. The contract announces deposit, withdrawal, stake, and unstake events, including the amount and the affected account.
