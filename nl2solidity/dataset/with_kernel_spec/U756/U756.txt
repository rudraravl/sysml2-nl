This contract is a yield-bearing vault for a single ERC-20 token, holding deposited principal and accruing yield in that same token. It maintains each user's share balance, the total shares issued, and the total underlying token held. Callers can deposit tokens to receive shares, withdraw their deposited tokens by burning shares, and redeem their shares for the underlying token. A designated operator can rebase the vault's total assets to reflect yield generation or losses, and can pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The vault charges a 0.5% withdrawal fee on the underlying tokens, and the operator can only rebase the vault once every 24 hours. The contract announces deposit, withdrawal, and rebase events, including the affected user, amount, and new total assets.
