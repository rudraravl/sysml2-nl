This contract is an automated market maker for two tokens, holding reserves of both tokens in a liquidity pool. It maintains the current reserves of each token, a record of each liquidity provider's share of the pool, and configurable trading fees. Callers can deposit both tokens to provide liquidity, withdraw their share of liquidity, and swap one token for the other. A designated operator can adjust the trading fee and pause all trading operations. The trading fee is fixed at 0.3% of the swapped amount, and the maximum number of liquidity providers is capped at 1000. The contract emits an event whenever liquidity is added, removed, or a swap occurs.
