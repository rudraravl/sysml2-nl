This contract is a vault for a community-owned mutual fund, holding various digital assets deposited by participants. It maintains a record of each participant's share balance, the total asset value held, and a configuration for investment strategies. Participants can deposit supported digital assets to acquire shares, redeem their shares for a proportional amount of the underlying assets, and vote on proposed investment strategies. A designated operator can add new supported digital assets, update investment strategy parameters, and initiate asset rebalancing operations. A 0.5% fee is applied to all redemption operations, and a minimum deposit amount of 100 units of any supported asset is enforced. The contract announces all deposit, redemption, and rebalancing events, including the participant's address, asset type, and amount.
