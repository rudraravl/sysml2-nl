This contract manages concentrated liquidity pools for token pairs, holding deposited tokens as reserves. It maintains per-pool records including token addresses, current prices, and liquidity bin configurations, along with per-user positions detailing their deposited amounts and active bins. Callers can deposit tokens to create or add to a liquidity position, withdraw their liquidity and accrued fees, and swap tokens within a pool. A designated operator can adjust pool parameters like fee tiers and bin widths, and pause trading in an emergency. The maximum number of active bins per pool is 100, and a 0.05% swap fee is applied to all trades. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, and successful swaps, including the amounts involved and the affected user.
