This contract manages a community governance system, holding deposited tokens to enable voting and reward distribution. It maintains individual user balances of locked tokens, records of votes cast per proposal, and a pool of reward tokens for distribution. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their unlocked tokens, create new proposals, vote on active proposals, and claim earned rewards. A designated administrator can initiate new voting rounds, set the reward rate, and pause deposits in emergencies. Each user must lock a minimum of 100 tokens to participate in voting, and proposals remain open for exactly 7 days. The contract emits events when tokens are deposited, votes are cast, and rewards are claimed.
