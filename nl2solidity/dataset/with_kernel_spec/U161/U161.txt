This contract is a marketplace for non-fungible tokens (NFTs), holding NFTs and the native blockchain currency in escrow during sales. It maintains a record of listed NFTs including their seller, price, and status, and tracks the marketplace's accumulated fees. Callers can list an NFT for sale, purchase a listed NFT, cancel their own NFT listing, and update the price of their listed NFT. A designated owner can withdraw accumulated fees and pause all marketplace activity. A listing fee of 0.01 native currency is charged upon successful sale, and the maximum number of NFTs a single address can list concurrently is 10. The contract announces NFT listings, purchases, and cancellations to observers.
