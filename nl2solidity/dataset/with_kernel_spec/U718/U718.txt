This contract facilitates token presales, holding deposited funds and the tokens to be sold. It maintains a record of each participant's purchased token amount and the total funds raised for each presale, along with a global configuration for presale creation. Callers can create a new presale, contribute funds to an active presale, claim purchased tokens after a successful presale, and refund their contributions if a presale fails. The owner is privileged to set the global presale creation fee and to designate operators who can approve or reject new presales. A presale must raise at least 50% of its soft cap to be considered successful, and the presale creation fee is fixed at 0.1 ether. The contract announces when a new presale is created, when a contribution is made, and when tokens are claimed or funds are refunded.
