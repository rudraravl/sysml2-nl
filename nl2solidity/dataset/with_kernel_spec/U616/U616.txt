This liquid staking contract allows users to deposit Ether to receive a liquid staking token representing their staked Ether and its accrued rewards. It maintains a global pool of deposited Ether, the total supply of the liquid staking token, and per-user balances of the liquid staking token. Callers can deposit Ether, withdraw their underlying Ether and accumulated rewards by burning their liquid staking tokens, and transfer their liquid staking tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can update the staking reward rate and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. A 0.1% fee is applied to all withdrawals, and the maximum daily withdrawal amount is capped at 1000 Ether. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward rate updates.
