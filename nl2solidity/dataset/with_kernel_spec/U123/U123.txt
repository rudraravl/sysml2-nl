This contract manages user balances for a crypto-linked debit card service, holding various ERC-20 tokens deposited by users. It maintains individual user account balances for each supported ERC-20 token, along with a global configuration of supported tokens and their associated fees. Callers can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens into their account, withdraw their deposited tokens, and initiate a card payment request that debits their account. A designated operator can add or remove supported ERC-20 tokens, set the withdrawal fee for each token, and approve or reject pending card payment requests. The withdrawal fee for any token is capped at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount. All card payment requests must be approved by a designated operator within 24 hours of submission. The contract emits an event when a deposit is made, when a withdrawal is processed, and when a card payment request is approved or rejected.
