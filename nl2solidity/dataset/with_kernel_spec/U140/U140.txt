This contract is a liquid staking vault that custodies deposited Ether and issues two ERC-20 tokens: one representing the principal deposit and another representing accrued yield. It maintains per-account records of deposited principal, earned yield, and active withdrawals, along with a global configuration for fees and withdrawal delays. Callers can deposit Ether to receive principal tokens, redeem principal tokens for Ether, or initiate a withdrawal of earned yield. The owner can set the deposit fee, adjust the withdrawal delay, and designate operators who can pause deposits. The maximum deposit fee is 0.5%, and the minimum withdrawal delay is 24 hours. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal requests, and successful Ether transfers to observers.
