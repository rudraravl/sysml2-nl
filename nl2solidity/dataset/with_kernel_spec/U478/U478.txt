This contract manages prediction markets for artist hype scores, holding user stakes in an ERC-20 token. It maintains individual user balances, active market configurations including artist pairs and prediction windows, and the current state of each prediction, such as total staked for each outcome. Callers can create a new prediction, stake tokens on an artist to win, and claim winnings after a market concludes. A designated operator can initiate new prediction markets and resolve existing ones by providing the final hype scores. Each prediction market must have a minimum stake of 100 tokens, and a 5% fee is applied to all winnings, directed to a treasury address. The contract emits an event when a new market is created, when a user stakes tokens, and when a market is resolved.
