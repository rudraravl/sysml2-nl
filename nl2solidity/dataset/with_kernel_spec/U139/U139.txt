This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers, holding user-deposited tokens in escrow on the source chain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance and a global configuration for transfer parameters. Callers can deposit tokens, initiate a transfer to the destination chain, and claim transferred tokens on the destination chain. A designated operator is privileged to update the transfer fee and pause all transfer operations. A minimum transfer amount of 100 units of the deposited token is enforced, and a transfer fee of 0.1% is applied to all outgoing transfers. The contract announces events for successful deposits, initiated transfers, and claimed transfers, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
