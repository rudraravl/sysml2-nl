This liquid staking contract issues a non-rebasing receipt token representing staked Ether and its accumulated rewards, holding deposited Ether and any earned staking rewards. It maintains a total supply of the receipt token, a total amount of Ether held, and a conversion rate between the receipt token and Ether, which continuously increases over time. Callers can deposit Ether to receive receipt tokens, and they can redeem receipt tokens for Ether. A designated operator can deposit earned staking rewards into the contract, and a designated operator can pause and unpause all deposit and redemption operations. The contract imposes a 0.1% fee on all redemptions, and the redemption conversion rate is updated at least once every 24 hours. The contract emits an event whenever Ether is deposited, receipt tokens are redeemed, or staking rewards are added.
