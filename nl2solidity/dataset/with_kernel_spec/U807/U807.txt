This contract serves as a cross-chain bridge for a specific fungible token, enabling users to lock tokens on this chain and mint equivalent wrapped tokens on a connected destination chain, or burn wrapped tokens on the destination chain and unlock original tokens here; it custodies the locked fungible tokens. The contract maintains a total supply of locked tokens, a mapping of each user's locked token balance, and a record of all completed bridge operations including a unique identifier and the amount transferred. Callers can initiate a lock operation by depositing tokens, and can request an unlock operation by providing proof of a burn event on the destination chain. A designated operator is responsible for approving unlock operations and setting the bridge fee, which is fixed at 0.1% of the transfer amount, and can only be changed once every 24 hours. The maximum single transfer amount is 1,000,000 tokens. The contract announces events for successful lock operations, successful unlock operations, and changes to the bridge fee.
