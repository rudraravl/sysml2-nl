This contract is a yield farm that custodies deposits of a single designated ERC-20 token. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, a global reward pool of a second designated ERC-20 token, and a configuration for the reward rate. Callers can deposit the designated deposit token, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim their accumulated rewards. Only the owner may set the reward rate, update the designated reward token address, or pause all deposits and withdrawals. The maximum reward rate is 1000 reward tokens per block, and the initial deposit token address is fixed upon deployment. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claim events.
