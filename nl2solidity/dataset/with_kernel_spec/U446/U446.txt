This contract is a decentralized stablecoin protocol that custodies a reserve cryptocurrency and issues two tokens: a stable token pegged to a fiat currency and a reserve token that absorbs volatility. It maintains a global reserve pool of the reserve cryptocurrency, a total supply for both the stable token and the reserve token, and a configuration for various parameters. Callers can mint stable tokens by depositing the reserve cryptocurrency, redeem stable tokens for the reserve cryptocurrency, mint reserve tokens by depositing the reserve cryptocurrency, and redeem reserve tokens for the reserve cryptocurrency. A designated operator is privileged to adjust the oracle price feed for the reserve cryptocurrency and update system parameters. The stable token always maintains a minimum collateralization ratio of 400% by the reserve cryptocurrency, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stable token redemption operations. The contract announces the current reserve ratio, the total supply of both tokens, and the current oracle price of the reserve cryptocurrency.
