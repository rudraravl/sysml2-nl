This contract is a vault for tokenized gold, holding deposits of a specific gold-backed stablecoin. It maintains individual user balances of deposited gold, a total gold collateral pool, and configuration parameters for borrowing and yield strategies. Callers can deposit gold tokens, withdraw their deposited gold, and claim any accrued yield. A designated operator can initiate borrowing against the gold collateral, deploy borrowed capital into approved yield-generating strategies, and redeem positions from those strategies. The contract will enforce a maximum loan-to-value ratio of 70% against the gold collateral. A 0.5% fee will be applied to all yield distributions before they are claimable by users. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield distribution events.
