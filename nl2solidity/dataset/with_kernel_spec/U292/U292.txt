This contract manages token sale allocations for projects, holding deposited funds and the project's sale tokens. It maintains a record of each participant's allocated token amount, their contributed funds, and a global configuration for each sale including the total tokens offered and the minimum required reputation score. Callers can contribute funds to a sale, claim their allocated tokens after the sale concludes, and withdraw any excess contributed funds if their allocation is less than their contribution. A designated operator can start and end sales, set the allocation for individual participants, and withdraw collected funds to the project's designated address. Each sale has a maximum contribution limit of 1000 units of the contribution token per participant. The contract emits an event when a participant successfully contributes funds and when a sale concludes.
