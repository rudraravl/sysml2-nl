This contract serves as a secure, non-custodial cross-chain transfer hub, holding various fungible tokens temporarily during bridging operations. It maintains a record of each user's pending transfers, including the destination chain, recipient address, and token amount, alongside a global configuration for supported tokens and their associated transfer fees. Callers can initiate a transfer by depositing tokens, claim a transfer on the destination chain, or cancel a pending transfer if it has not yet been processed. Only a designated operator can approve or reject cross-chain transfer requests and update the supported token list or transfer fees. Each transfer incurs a flat fee of 0.1% of the transferred amount, and a transfer must be claimed within 72 hours of its approval or it will be automatically cancelled and refunded. The contract announces events for initiated transfers, approved transfers, claimed transfers, and cancelled transfers, including relevant details like the user, token, amount, and status.
