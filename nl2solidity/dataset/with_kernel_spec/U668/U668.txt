This contract is a yield aggregation and execution engine, designed to custody various digital assets provided by users to be deployed across different yield-generating strategies. It maintains individual user balances for each deposited asset, records of active positions within various strategies, and a configurable set of approved yield strategy modules. Callers can deposit supported assets, withdraw their deposited assets, initiate new yield positions by selecting an approved strategy and allocating funds, and close existing positions to reclaim assets. Only a designated operator can approve new yield strategy modules, pause all deposit and withdrawal operations, and update the protocol-wide fee percentage. A maximum of 10 distinct yield strategy modules can be active at any given time. A 0.1% protocol fee is applied to all realized profits from closed yield positions. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, new position openings, position closures, and changes to the protocol fee.
