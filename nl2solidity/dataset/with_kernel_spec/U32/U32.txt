This contract facilitates cross-chain transfers of a stablecoin, holding the stablecoin in escrow on the source chain. It maintains a record of each user's stablecoin balance on the source chain and a global count of stablecoins locked. Callers can initiate a transfer by locking stablecoins, and designated operators can mint stablecoins on a destination chain or burn them on the source chain to complete a transfer. A designated operator is the only entity that can approve minting or burning operations. Each transfer incurs a flat fee of 0.01 stablecoins, deducted from the amount transferred, and a single transfer cannot exceed 1,000,000 stablecoins. The contract emits an event upon stablecoin lock and another upon stablecoin unlock.
