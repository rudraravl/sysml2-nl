This contract facilitates decentralized predictions on future events, holding user-deposited tokens as collateral for prediction outcomes. It maintains individual user balances for each prediction market, records the chosen outcome for each participant, and stores configuration parameters for each market, such as the event description and resolution criteria. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit tokens into an existing market to back a specific outcome, withdraw tokens from a resolved market if their prediction was correct, and dispute market resolutions within a defined window. A designated operator can resolve prediction markets, declaring the winning outcome, and can update market resolution criteria before a market closes. Each prediction market must have at least two possible outcomes and no more than ten, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all winning payouts, which is directed to a treasury address. The contract announces market creation, user deposits, outcome selections, market resolution, and payout distributions through emitted events.
