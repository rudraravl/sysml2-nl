This contract is a wrapped asset issuance mechanism, custodying a reserve of a base asset. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped asset and individual balances for each holder. Callers can mint new wrapped assets by depositing the base asset, and redeem wrapped assets for the base asset. A designated operator can pause all minting and redemption operations. The redemption fee is 0.1% of the redeemed amount. The maximum amount of base asset that can be deposited in a single minting operation is 100 base asset units. The contract announces the total supply of the wrapped asset and individual balance changes for all holders.
