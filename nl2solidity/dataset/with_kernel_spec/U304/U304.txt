This contract facilitates the auction and direct sale of unique digital assets, holding the assets themselves in escrow during active listings. It maintains a record of each asset's current owner, its listing status (direct sale or auction), the current bid and highest bidder for auctions, and any associated sale price or reserve price. Callers can list an asset for direct sale, place bids on active auctions, accept a direct sale offer, finalize an auction once its duration has passed, and withdraw their assets if a sale or auction is unsuccessful. A designated operator can set a platform fee for successful sales and auctions, and also pause or unpause all listing activity. The platform fee is fixed at 2% of the final sale price. Auction durations are fixed at 7 days from the time of listing. The contract announces events for asset listings, bids placed, sales finalized, and asset withdrawals.
