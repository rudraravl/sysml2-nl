This contract is a tokenized representation of real-world environmental assets, specifically carbon allowances, and it custodies no digital assets directly. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and a configurable price floor for the token. Callers can acquire new tokens by depositing an approved stablecoin, transfer tokens to other accounts, and redeem tokens for the underlying real-world asset. A designated operator can adjust the price floor, pause token transfers, and initiate redemption of the underlying assets. The price floor will increase by 0.4% each month. A 0.5% fee is applied to all token redemptions. The contract announces all token transfers and price floor updates.
