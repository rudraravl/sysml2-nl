This contract manages a synthetic asset that tracks the performance of a basket of other digital assets, holding no underlying collateral itself. It maintains a record of each user's synthetic asset balance and the current composition and weighting of the underlying asset basket. Callers can mint new synthetic assets by providing a specified amount of a base currency, redeem synthetic assets for the equivalent value in the base currency, and transfer their synthetic asset balances to other users. A designated operator can update the basket's asset composition and their respective weights, and pause or unpause minting and redemption operations. Minting new synthetic assets incurs a 0.5% fee, payable in the base currency, and the basket composition can be updated a maximum of once every 24 hours. The contract announces transfers of synthetic assets, minting events, redemption events, and changes to the basket's composition.
