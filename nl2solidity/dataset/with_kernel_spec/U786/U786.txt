This contract manages a gaming platform's native utility token, facilitating staking and reward distribution. It maintains individual user stake balances, accumulated rewards, and a global reward pool. Callers can stake tokens, unstake tokens, claim accumulated rewards, and transfer tokens to other users. A designated operator can adjust the staking reward rate and deposit tokens into the global reward pool, while the contract owner can pause all staking and unstaking operations. The minimum staking amount is 100 tokens, and unstaking incurs a 5% fee on the unstaked amount if performed within 7 days of staking. The contract emits events for successful staking, unstaking, reward claims, and token transfers.
