This contract facilitates the transfer of Ether and ERC-20 tokens between a Layer 1 blockchain and a Layer 2 blockchain, holding deposited assets in escrow on Layer 1. It maintains a mapping of Layer 1 transaction hashes to Layer 2 transaction hashes for withdrawals, and a configuration for the Layer 2 system's state root. Callers can initiate a deposit of Ether or ERC-20 tokens to Layer 2, withdraw previously deposited assets from Layer 2 back to Layer 1, and prove the inclusion of a Layer 2 transaction in the Layer 2 state. A designated operator can update the Layer 2 system's state root, and only this operator can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. A withdrawal request must wait a minimum of 7 days after the Layer 2 transaction is proven on Layer 1 before the funds can be claimed. The contract charges a fixed fee of 0.001 Ether for every withdrawal operation, deducted from the withdrawn amount. The contract announces the initiation of a deposit, the successful finalization of a withdrawal, and any updates to the Layer 2 state root.
