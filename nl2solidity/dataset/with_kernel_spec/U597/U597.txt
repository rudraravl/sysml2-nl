This contract manages the registration and ownership of unique, human-readable identifiers, acting as a registry for these identifiers and their associated data. It maintains a mapping of each registered identifier to its current owner, a resolver address, and a creation timestamp, along with a global configuration for registration fees and minimum identifier lengths. Callers can register new identifiers, transfer ownership of their identifiers to another address, and update the resolver address associated with their owned identifiers. The contract owner can set the registration fee, which is a fixed amount of the native blockchain currency, and can also set the minimum allowed length for new identifiers, which must be at least 3 characters. All registration and transfer operations emit an event announcing the identifier, its new owner, and the associated resolver.
