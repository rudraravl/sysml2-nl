This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, holding a reserve of a base asset to back a synthetic asset. It maintains individual account balances for the synthetic asset, a global supply of the synthetic asset, and a record of the current peg ratio between the synthetic asset and the base asset. Callers can mint new synthetic assets by depositing the base asset, redeem synthetic assets for the base asset, and participate in a seigniorage mechanism by staking the synthetic asset to earn newly minted synthetic assets. A designated operator can adjust the peg ratio within a 1% deviation per 24-hour period and can initiate a system-wide rebase event. A 0.5% fee is applied to all redemptions of the synthetic asset, and a minimum of 100 base assets must be deposited to mint new synthetic assets. The contract emits events for all minting, redemption, and rebase operations, including the affected accounts and amounts.
