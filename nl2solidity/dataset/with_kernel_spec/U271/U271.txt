This contract manages a pool of funds to provide coverage against smart contract exploits and other defined risks, holding various digital assets as collateral. It maintains a record of each user's deposited collateral, active coverage policies, and any outstanding claims, alongside global configuration parameters like coverage premiums and claim processing delays. Callers can deposit collateral, purchase coverage policies specifying a covered asset and duration, file claims against active policies, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated administrator can adjust coverage premiums, approve or reject claims, and pause new policy purchases. Coverage policies have a minimum duration of 7 days and a maximum duration of 365 days, and a 2% fee is applied to all policy purchases. The contract emits events when collateral is deposited, a policy is purchased, a claim is filed, and a claim is resolved.
