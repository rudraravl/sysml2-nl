This liquid staking contract custodies deposited Ether and issues a corresponding liquid staking token representing a claim on the staked Ether plus accrued rewards. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, the amount of Ether deposited by each user, and a global staking ratio that determines the amount of liquid staking tokens issued per Ether. Callers can deposit Ether to receive liquid staking tokens, and they can redeem liquid staking tokens to withdraw their share of staked Ether and rewards. The owner can set the staking fee, which is initially 5%, and can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations in an emergency. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 0.01 Ether. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the staking ratio.
