This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, custodying various collateral tokens and a native governance token. It maintains individual user positions including margin, leverage, and open orders, a global pool of liquidity provider assets, and system-wide configuration parameters like trading fees and maximum leverage. Callers can open new positions, close existing positions, add or remove collateral, place limit orders, and swap between supported assets. A designated operator can update asset configurations, adjust trading parameters, and pause trading in emergencies. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all trades. The contract announces position liquidations, new order placements, and collateral adjustments to observers.
