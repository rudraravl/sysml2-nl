This contract manages the on-chain distribution of unique digital collectibles through a gacha-style pack opening mechanism, holding the collectible tokens themselves until they are claimed. It maintains records of available pack types, the probability tables for each collectible within those packs, and the number of unopened packs owned by each user. Callers can purchase packs using an ERC-20 token, open purchased packs to reveal collectibles, and claim revealed collectibles to their wallet. A designated operator can create new pack types, adjust the probability tables for existing packs, and replenish the supply of packs. The contract charges a 5% fee on all pack purchases, which is sent to a treasury address. Each pack opening reveals exactly 5 collectibles. The contract emits an event when a pack is purchased, when a pack is opened, and when a collectible is claimed.
