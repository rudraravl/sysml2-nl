This contract manages a vault for depositing and rebalancing various digital assets, issuing a single fungible receipt token representing a user's share of the vault's total value. It maintains records of each user's deposited assets, their corresponding share of the vault, and the current configuration of the vault's investment strategies. Callers can deposit assets, withdraw their share, and redeem their receipt tokens for underlying assets. A designated operator can upgrade the vault's investment strategy, rebalance assets among strategies, and set fees. The operator may set a maximum of 10 active strategies at any given time, and a 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and strategy upgrade events.
