This contract manages a pooled derivatives trading system, holding various stablecoins and non-stablecoins as collateral. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each account, and global configuration parameters for trading. Callers can deposit collateral, open new positions, close existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. Only a designated operator can adjust global risk parameters, and only the owner can upgrade the contract or pause all trading. A maximum of 10,000 units of any single collateral asset can be deposited into the pool per day, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all position opening and closing operations. The contract announces all deposits, withdrawals, and position changes as events.
