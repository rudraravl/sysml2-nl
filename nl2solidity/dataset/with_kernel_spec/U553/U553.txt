This contract manages a decentralized prediction market, holding deposited collateral tokens for active predictions. It maintains individual user balances, open prediction positions, and global market parameters for each prediction event. Callers can create new prediction events, place bets on outcomes, claim winnings from settled events, and withdraw their available balance. A designated operator can settle prediction events by providing the winning outcome and can adjust the fee percentage. The fee for placing a bet is 1% of the bet amount, and a minimum bet amount of 100 units of the collateral token is enforced. The contract emits an event when a new prediction market is created, when a bet is placed, and when a market is settled.
