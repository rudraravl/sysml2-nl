This contract is a tokenized fund representing fractional ownership of a portfolio of short-term US Treasury bills and repurchase agreements, acting as a digital custodian for these underlying assets. It maintains individual balances for each token holder, a total supply of tokens, and a daily net asset value (NAV) per token. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint new fund tokens, redeem fund tokens for stablecoins, and transfer their fund tokens to other addresses. Only a designated operator can update the daily NAV, pause or unpause deposits and redemptions, and set the redemption fee. A redemption fee of 0.05% is applied to all redemptions, and redemptions are subject to a 24-hour processing delay after the request is submitted. The contract announces all deposit and redemption events, including the amount of stablecoins involved and the number of tokens minted or burned.
