This contract facilitates privacy-preserving transfers of fungible tokens by acting as a temporary custodian for deposited assets. It maintains a Merkle tree of deposit commitments and a record of spent nullifiers, but no per-account balances or user-specific data. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw tokens using a valid zero-knowledge proof, and submit a batch of nullifiers for processing. The contract owner can set the deposit fee, update the Merkle tree verifier, and pause deposits. The deposit fee is fixed at 0.1% of the deposited amount. Withdrawals are subject to a minimum delay of 10 minutes between consecutive withdrawals to the same recipient address. The contract emits events for successful deposits, withdrawals, and nullifier submissions.
