This contract facilitates decentralized token swaps and concentrated liquidity provision, custodying deposited tokens within various trading pools. It maintains records of individual liquidity positions, including deposited token amounts, price ranges, and accumulated fees, alongside global pool configurations like swap fees and tick spacing. Callers can create new trading pools, add or remove liquidity from existing pools, swap tokens, and collect accrued fees. A designated operator can update the protocol's treasury address and modify the base swap fee for new pools. The maximum swap fee for any pool is 1%, and the minimum tick spacing is 1. The contract emits events for all liquidity additions, removals, and token swaps, detailing the affected pool, amounts, and participants.
