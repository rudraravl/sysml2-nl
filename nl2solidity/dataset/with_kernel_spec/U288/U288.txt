This contract is a permissionless price reporting system that custodies two different tokens used for bonding. It maintains a record of the current price for each supported asset pair, the bond amount for that price, and the timestamp of the last price update. Callers can propose a new price for an asset pair by bonding a specified amount of both tokens, and they can dispute an existing price by bonding a larger amount of both tokens at a new price. There are no privileged roles; all operations are permissionless. A new price proposal must bond at least 100 units of each token, and a dispute bond must be at least 10% larger than the current bond. The contract announces new valid prices and dispute events.
