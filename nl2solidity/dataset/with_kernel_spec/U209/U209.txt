This contract is a yield aggregator vault that custodies various ERC-20 tokens deposited by users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount for each supported token, the total amount of each token held in the vault, and a configuration for each yield strategy, including its associated external vault address and a performance fee percentage. Callers can deposit supported tokens into the vault, withdraw their deposited tokens plus accrued yield, claim rewards generated by the vault's strategies, and approve external spending of their deposited tokens. Only a designated owner can add or remove supported tokens, update the external vault address for a strategy, adjust the performance fee for a strategy, or pause and unpause all deposits and withdrawals. The maximum performance fee for any strategy is 10%, and a 0.1% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claim events, including the user's address, the token involved, and the amount.
