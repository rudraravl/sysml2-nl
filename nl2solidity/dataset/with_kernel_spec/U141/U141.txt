This contract manages fractional ownership of real-world assets, specifically real estate properties, and custodies the digital representations of these property fractions. It maintains a record of each property, including its total fractional units and the current owner of each unit, alongside a global configuration for fees and administrative addresses. Callers can purchase fractional units, transfer their owned units to another address, and sell their units back to the system. A designated operator can add new properties, set the total fractional units for a property, and adjust the purchase and sale prices. The contract charges a 1% fee on all unit transfers, and a property must have at least 100 fractional units before any units can be purchased. The contract emits an event whenever fractional units are transferred or a new property is added.
