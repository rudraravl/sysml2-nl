This contract manages a tokenized basket of other tokens, representing a diversified portfolio. It maintains the current composition of the basket, including the quantity of each underlying token held, and the total supply of the basket token. Callers can deposit a specified set of underlying tokens to mint new basket tokens, and they can redeem their basket tokens to receive a proportional share of the underlying tokens. Only a designated operator can adjust the target weights of the underlying tokens within the basket, or pause and unpause all deposit and redemption operations. A deposit fee of 0.1% is applied to all minting operations, and the basket's rebalancing operations are limited to a maximum of 10% change in any single underlying token's weight per day. The contract announces events for all minting, redemption, and rebalancing actions, including the quantities of tokens involved.
