This contract manages a collection of unique digital creature tokens, allowing users to own, breed, and battle them. It maintains individual creature token records, including their unique identifiers, genetic traits, battle statistics, and ownership. Callers can acquire new creature tokens from a designated pool, transfer creature tokens to other users, initiate breeding between two owned creature tokens, and engage their creature tokens in battle simulations. Only a designated operator can mint new creature tokens into the pool and set the breeding fee. A breeding operation requires two creature tokens and costs a fixed fee of 0.005 of the native blockchain currency. Each creature token can participate in a maximum of 7 breeding operations. The contract announces the transfer of creature tokens, the creation of new creature tokens, and the outcome of battle simulations.
