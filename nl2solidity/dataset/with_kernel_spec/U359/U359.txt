This contract manages the creation and evolution of digital life forms, holding no custodied assets directly but tracking ownership of unique digital entities. It maintains a record of each digital entity's attributes, its current evolutionary stage, and its owner, along with a global configuration for creation parameters. Callers can create new digital entities, evolve an existing entity using specific resources, and transfer ownership of their entities. Only a designated operator can adjust the global configuration parameters, such as the initial creation cost and the resource requirements for evolution. New digital entity creation requires a fixed fee of 0.01 ether, and an entity can only evolve a maximum of 5 times. The contract announces when a new digital entity is created and when an existing entity completes an evolutionary stage.
