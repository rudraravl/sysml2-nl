This contract manages a prediction market, holding collateral tokens deposited by participants. It maintains individual participant balances, open positions for each market outcome, and market-specific configuration including resolution status. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit collateral, place bets on market outcomes, redeem winning bets after market resolution, and withdraw their available collateral. Only a designated operator may resolve a market by declaring a winning outcome, and only the contract owner may adjust the operator address or set the market creation fee. A market creation fee of 0.01 collateral tokens is charged for each new market, and a maximum of 10 distinct outcomes are permitted per market. The contract announces market creation, bet placements, and market resolutions.
