This contract establishes a decentralized lending market, holding deposited tokens as collateral and issuing new tokens representing debt. It maintains per-asset pools tracking total deposits, total borrows, and interest rates, alongside per-user records for individual deposits and borrows for each asset. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their deposits, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay their loans, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can adjust interest rate parameters and add or remove supported assets. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 80%, and a liquidation penalty of 5% is applied to the borrower's outstanding debt. The contract announces deposit events, borrow events, repayment events, and liquidation events.
