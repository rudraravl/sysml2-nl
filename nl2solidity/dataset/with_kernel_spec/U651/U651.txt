This contract is a liquid staking derivative for a base asset, custodying deposited base assets and issuing a yield-bearing receipt token. It maintains a total supply of the receipt token, the total amount of base assets staked, and a per-account balance of receipt tokens. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive receipt tokens, redeem receipt tokens for the base asset, and transfer receipt tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the staking rate, which determines the amount of base asset received per receipt token upon redemption. The contract owner can pause all deposit and redemption operations. The maximum deposit amount in a single transaction is 1000 base units. Redemption requests are subject to a 24-hour unbonding period. The contract emits events for every deposit, redemption, and transfer of receipt tokens, and for changes to the staking rate.
