This contract manages a collection of unique digital assets representing in-game items and their associated metadata, holding no custodial funds. It maintains a record of each asset's current owner, its unique identifier, and a URI pointing to its off-chain metadata, alongside a global counter for total assets minted and a mapping of approved operators for each asset owner. Callers can mint new assets, transfer ownership of an asset to another account, approve an operator to manage a specific asset on their behalf, or approve an operator to manage all their assets. Only a designated minter role can create new assets, and only the contract owner can assign or revoke the minter role. A maximum of 10,000 assets can ever be minted by this contract. Each successful asset transfer will emit an event detailing the previous owner, the new owner, and the asset's unique identifier.
