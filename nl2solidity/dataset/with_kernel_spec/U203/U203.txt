This contract manages a treasury of a fungible governance token and a non-fungible asset representing a gaming item, distributing them to eligible participants. It maintains a record of each participant's allocated governance token balance and their owned gaming item identifiers. Callers can claim their allocated governance tokens, deposit gaming items into the treasury, and withdraw gaming items they own. A designated operator can allocate governance tokens to participants, mint new gaming items, and transfer gaming items to participants. The maximum number of gaming items that can be minted is 10,000, and a participant can only claim governance tokens once every 30 days. The contract announces when governance tokens are claimed, when gaming items are deposited, and when gaming items are transferred to participants.
