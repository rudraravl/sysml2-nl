This contract manages a parimutuel prediction market for asset price movements, holding user deposits in an escrow pool. It maintains records of each participant's active predictions, their deposited amount, and the market's current state including the prediction window, settlement window, and the total amount staked on each outcome. Callers can create a new prediction market, stake funds on a specific price outcome, and claim their winnings after a market has settled. Only a designated operator can settle a market by providing the final asset price, and they can also pause new market creation. A market's prediction window must be at least 30 minutes and no more than 24 hours. A 2% fee is deducted from the total winnings of each settled market. The contract emits an event when a new market is created, when funds are staked, and when a market is settled.
