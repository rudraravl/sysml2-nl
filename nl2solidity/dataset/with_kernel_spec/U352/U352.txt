This contract manages prediction markets, holding deposited collateral for active markets. It maintains a record of each market's topic, resolution status, total collateral, and individual user positions (shares in 'yes' or 'no' outcomes). Callers can create new markets, deposit collateral into an existing market to buy outcome shares, sell their outcome shares back for collateral, and withdraw their share of resolved market collateral. A designated market creator is privileged to resolve a market, setting its final outcome. New markets must have a minimum initial collateral deposit of 100 stablecoins, and a 2% fee is applied to all market resolution payouts. The contract announces when a new market is created, when collateral is deposited or withdrawn, and when a market is resolved.
