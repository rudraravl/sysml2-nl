This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, holding a reserve of various collateral assets. It maintains individual user balances for the stablecoin, the reserve token, and collateral deposits, along with system-wide configuration parameters like the current collateralization ratio and fee rates. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing approved collateral, burn stablecoins to redeem underlying collateral, and stake stablecoins to earn reserve tokens. A designated operator can adjust the list of approved collateral assets and their individual collateral factors, and can also update the system's target collateralization ratio. The minimum collateralization ratio for minting is 150%, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin minting operations. The contract emits events for all stablecoin mints, burns, and reserve token distributions.
