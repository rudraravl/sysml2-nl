This contract manages a vault for a yield optimization strategy, holding deposited stablecoins and their corresponding yield-bearing derivatives. It maintains individual user balances of deposited stablecoins and their share of the strategy's total value, along with a global configuration for fees and strategy parameters. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their share of stablecoins, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can rebalance the strategy's assets, update strategy parameters, and initiate emergency withdrawals. The owner can set the operator address and adjust the withdrawal fee, which is capped at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount. All withdrawals are subject to a 24-hour delay after the request is made. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, reward claims, and significant configuration changes.
