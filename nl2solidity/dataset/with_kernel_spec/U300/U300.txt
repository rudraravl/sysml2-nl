This contract facilitates the transfer of an arbitrary ERC-20 token between a Layer 1 blockchain and a Layer 2 scaling solution, holding deposited tokens in escrow on Layer 1. It maintains a record of each user's deposited Layer 1 token balance awaiting transfer to Layer 2, and a mapping of Layer 2 transaction hashes to their corresponding Layer 1 withdrawal status. Callers can deposit tokens for transfer to Layer 2, initiate a withdrawal from Layer 2 to Layer 1, and claim their withdrawn tokens on Layer 1. A designated operator can pause and unpause the contract, and update the Layer 2 messaging contract address. The contract imposes a minimum deposit amount of 100 units of the ERC-20 token and a withdrawal processing fee of 0.01 ETH, paid by the Layer 2 messaging contract. The contract emits an event when tokens are deposited, when a withdrawal is initiated, and when withdrawn tokens are claimed.
