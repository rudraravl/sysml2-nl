This contract is a launchpad for new tokens, holding a reserve of platform tokens to reward participants. It maintains a record of each creator's deployed tokens, their associated market capitalization milestones, and the platform token rewards due. Callers can deploy a new token, claim earned rewards, and stake platform tokens to participate in governance. Only a designated operator can set the market capitalization thresholds for rewards and approve new token deployments. A minimum of 1000 platform tokens must be staked to participate in governance, and rewards are distributed only after a deployed token achieves a market capitalization of at least 1,000,000 units of a stablecoin. The contract announces when a new token is deployed, when rewards are claimed, and when market capitalization milestones are met.
