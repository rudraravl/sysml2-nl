This contract is a stablecoin, holding a reserve of a designated collateral token to back its own issued token. It maintains the total supply of its issued token, the amount of collateral held, and each account's balance of the issued token. Callers can mint new tokens by depositing collateral, burn tokens to redeem collateral, and transfer their issued tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio and pause or unpause all minting and burning operations. The initial collateralization ratio is 1.05, meaning 1.05 units of collateral are required to mint 1 unit of the stablecoin. A 0.1% fee is applied to all token transfers, which is sent to a designated treasury address. The contract announces when tokens are minted, burned, or transferred, and when the collateralization ratio is updated.
