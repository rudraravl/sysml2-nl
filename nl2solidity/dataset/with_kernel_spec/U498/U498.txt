This contract is for an automated yield vault, custodying user-deposited stablecoins. It maintains records of each user's deposited amount, their share of the vault, and the total vault assets, along with configuration parameters for strategy allocation. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their share of stablecoins, and claim accumulated rewards. Only a designated operator can rebalance funds between different yield strategies, update strategy parameters, or pause deposits and withdrawals. The contract charges a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all withdrawals, and users must wait at least 24 hours after a deposit before initiating a withdrawal. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes in strategy allocation.
