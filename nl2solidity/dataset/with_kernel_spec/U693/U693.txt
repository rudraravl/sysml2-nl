This contract facilitates cross-chain token transfers and custodies deposited tokens during the transfer process. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balances, a configuration for supported token addresses on each connected chain, and a log of all initiated and completed transfer requests. Callers can deposit tokens for transfer, initiate a transfer request to a specified destination chain and recipient, and claim transferred tokens on the destination chain once the transfer is finalized. A designated operator is privileged to approve transfer requests, mark transfers as completed on the destination chain, and update the list of supported tokens and their associated chain identifiers. A transfer request must be approved by the operator within 24 hours of its initiation, otherwise it expires and the deposited tokens become reclaimable by the user. A fixed fee of 0.1% of the transfer amount is deducted from each successful transfer and sent to a designated treasury address. The contract announces events for token deposits, transfer requests initiated, transfer requests approved, and transfers completed.
