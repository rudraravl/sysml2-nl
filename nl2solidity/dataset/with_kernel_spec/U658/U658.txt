This contract manages user deposits into yield-generating strategies, holding deposited tokens and strategy-specific receipt tokens. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount for each strategy, the total deposited amount for each strategy, and configuration parameters for each strategy. Callers can deposit tokens into an available strategy, withdraw their deposited tokens and any accrued yield, and claim rewards distributed by the strategies. A designated operator can add new strategies, update strategy configurations, and pause or unpause deposits for a specific strategy. A strategy's maximum deposit capacity is 1,000,000 units of the base token, and a 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claim events.
