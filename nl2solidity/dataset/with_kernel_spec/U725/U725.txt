This contract facilitates access to tokenized real-world assets, holding wrapped versions of these assets as collateral. It maintains a record of each user's deposited collateral, their outstanding loan balance, and a global configuration for interest rates and asset eligibility. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw excess collateral. Only a designated administrator can adjust the interest rate, add or remove eligible collateral types, and pause all borrowing activity. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 70%, and a 0.5% origination fee is applied to all new loans. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit is made, a loan is taken out, or a loan is repaid, including the user's address and the amount involved.
