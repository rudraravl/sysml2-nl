This contract is a collateral market vault, designed to custody an arbitrary ERC-20 token as collateral for various financial obligations. It maintains individual user deposit balances, a global total deposited amount, and a list of approved operators with their associated collateral commitment amounts. Callers can deposit collateral, withdraw their available collateral, and delegate a portion of their deposited collateral to an approved operator. Only the owner can add new operators, remove existing operators, or adjust the maximum collateral an operator can manage. A user's withdrawal amount is capped by their available (undelegated) balance. The owner can set a maximum of 100 approved operators at any given time. The contract emits an event whenever collateral is deposited, withdrawn, or delegated, and when an operator's status or commitment changes.
