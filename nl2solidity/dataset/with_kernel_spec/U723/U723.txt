This contract facilitates the creation and initial distribution of new fungible tokens, holding the newly minted tokens and any collected platform fees. It maintains a record of each launched token's configuration, including its total supply and the address of its creator, and a global platform fee balance. Callers can launch a new token by providing a name, symbol, and initial supply, and can claim their allocated portion of a launched token. Only the designated platform operator can set the platform fee percentage and withdraw accumulated platform fees. A platform fee of exactly 0.08 units of the native currency is charged for each token launch, and the initial supply of any launched token must be between 1,000,000 and 1,000,000,000 units. The contract announces the successful launch of a new token, including its identifier and total supply, and any updates to the platform fee percentage.
