This contract manages a collection of unique digital collectibles, each with a rarity and a set of attributes, and facilitates their distribution through a randomized drawing mechanism. It maintains a registry of all minted collectibles, tracking their current owner, and stores configuration parameters for the drawing process. Callers can participate in a drawing by submitting a payment, claim newly drawn collectibles, and transfer collectibles they own to other accounts. The contract owner can configure drawing parameters such as the price per drawing attempt and the probability distribution of rarities, and can mint new collectibles into the system. Each drawing attempt costs 0.01 ether, and a maximum of 10,000 collectibles can ever be minted. The contract announces when a new collectible has been minted and when a collectible has been transferred.
