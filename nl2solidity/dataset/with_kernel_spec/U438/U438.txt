This contract manages a pool of tokenized short-term debt instruments, representing exposure to US Treasury bills and repurchase agreements. It maintains individual account balances for each user's share of the pooled assets, along with a global configuration for fees and operational parameters. Callers can deposit supported stablecoins into the pool, redeem their shares for stablecoins, and transfer their shares to other accounts. A designated operator can pause and unpause all deposit and redemption operations, and can adjust the redemption fee. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.1% of the redeemed amount. A minimum deposit of 1000 units of stablecoin is required for new deposits. The contract announces all deposit, redemption, and transfer events to observers.
