This contract manages a staking pool for a native blockchain asset, allowing users to deposit and earn yield. It maintains individual user deposit balances, a total staked amount, and a configurable annual percentage rate (APR) for yield calculation. Callers can deposit the native asset into the pool, withdraw their principal and accumulated yield, and claim their accumulated yield without withdrawing principal. Only a designated operator can update the APR based on external staking returns. A minimum deposit of 0.1 units of the native asset is enforced, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and APR updates to observers.
