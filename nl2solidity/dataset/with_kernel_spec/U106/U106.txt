This contract facilitates the transfer of a specific fungible token between two distinct blockchain networks by holding tokens in escrow on one chain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balance and a global configuration for operational parameters. Callers can deposit tokens into the contract, initiate a withdrawal request for their deposited tokens, and claim their tokens after a successful cross-chain transfer. A designated operator can approve withdrawal requests, cancel pending withdrawal requests, and update the fee percentage. The maximum fee percentage is 0.5% of the transferred amount, and a withdrawal request remains pending for at least 30 minutes before it can be approved. The contract emits events when tokens are deposited, when a withdrawal request is initiated, when a withdrawal request is approved, and when the fee percentage is updated.
