This contract establishes a decentralized lending market, holding deposited fungible tokens and issuing corresponding interest-bearing receipt tokens. It maintains per-asset records including total supply, total borrowed, current interest rates, and per-user records of deposits and borrows, alongside global configuration parameters for interest rate models. Callers can deposit fungible tokens, borrow fungible tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding borrows, and withdraw their deposited fungible tokens. A designated administrator can adjust interest rate model parameters and pause borrowing for specific assets. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 75%, and a 0.05% origination fee is charged on all new borrows. The contract announces events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, withdrawals, and changes to interest rate parameters.
