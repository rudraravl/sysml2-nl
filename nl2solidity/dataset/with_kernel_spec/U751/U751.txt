This contract enables users to stake a wrapped Bitcoin asset to earn yield, holding the staked wrapped Bitcoin in escrow. It maintains individual user balances of staked wrapped Bitcoin and accumulated rewards, along with a global pool of rewards awaiting distribution and a configurable staking epoch duration. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin, request a withdrawal of their staked amount, claim their accumulated rewards, and unstake their wrapped Bitcoin after a withdrawal request has been processed. A designated operator can update the reward rate, initiate a new staking epoch, and pause or unpause staking and withdrawals. A minimum deposit of 0.001 wrapped Bitcoin is enforced, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all reward claims. The contract announces when a deposit is made, when a withdrawal is requested, and when rewards are claimed.
