This contract manages the lifecycle of tokenized real-world trade receivables, holding stablecoins deposited by liquidity providers. It maintains a registry of all issued receivable tokens, each linked to its underlying stablecoin collateral, and tracks the total stablecoin balance available for lending. Callers can deposit stablecoins to become liquidity providers, withdraw their stablecoin deposits, and redeem receivable tokens for stablecoins once matured. Designated operators can issue new receivable tokens against verified real-world assets, approve specific receivable tokens for redemption, and pause or unpause all contract operations. The contract imposes a 0.5% fee on all stablecoin withdrawals, and a receivable token can only be redeemed after its maturity date has passed. The contract emits an event whenever stablecoins are deposited, whenever stablecoins are withdrawn, and whenever a new receivable token is issued.
