This contract manages a synthetic dollar, allowing users to mint it against deposited collateral and redeem it. It maintains individual user collateral balances, synthetic dollar balances, and a global collateral pool, along with configuration parameters like the collateralization ratio and stability fees. Callers can deposit collateral, mint synthetic dollars, redeem synthetic dollars by burning them and withdrawing collateral, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated administrator can adjust the stability fee and the minimum collateralization ratio, and can pause all minting and redeeming operations. The initial minimum collateralization ratio is 150%, and the stability fee is 0.5% per year, calculated continuously. The contract emits events for all deposits, mints, redemptions, and withdrawals, as well as for changes to the stability fee or collateralization ratio.
