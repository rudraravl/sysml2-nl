This contract manages a multiplayer game where players collect and risk Ether. It maintains each player's current Ether balance within the game, their total Ether collected across all games, and a global high score for the largest single game collection. Players can enter a new game by depositing Ether, collect additional Ether within the game, attempt to escape a game to claim their collected Ether, or be eliminated from a game, losing their collected Ether. Only a designated game operator can initiate new game rounds and eliminate players. A player's initial deposit to enter a game must be at least 0.01 Ether, and the operator can set a maximum of 10 active games concurrently. The contract announces when a player enters a game, collects Ether, escapes with Ether, is eliminated, and when the global high score is updated.
