This contract facilitates the transfer of an arbitrary fungible token from a source chain to a destination chain, holding locked tokens on the source chain as collateral. It maintains a record of each user's locked token balance and the total amount of tokens locked in the contract. Callers can deposit tokens, initiating a transfer to the destination chain, and can withdraw their locked tokens if the corresponding transfer is cancelled or fails. A designated relayer is authorized to finalize transfers by burning tokens on the destination chain and releasing an equivalent amount from this contract. The contract imposes a minimum deposit amount of 100 units of the fungible token. A 0.1% fee is applied to each successful transfer, deducted from the deposited amount. The contract emits an event upon every deposit and every successful transfer finalization, detailing the sender, recipient, and amount.
