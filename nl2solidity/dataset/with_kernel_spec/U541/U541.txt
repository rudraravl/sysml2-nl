This contract manages a decentralized lottery, holding a stablecoin for jackpot payouts. It maintains the current jackpot amount, the price of a single lottery entry, the total number of entries sold for the current round, and a mapping of entry ticket identifiers to their purchasers. Callers can purchase an entry ticket, which mints a non-fungible token representing their participation, and designated operators can trigger a draw, which selects a winner and transfers the jackpot. The owner can set the entry ticket price and designate new operators. The entry ticket price is fixed at 10 stablecoins, and a maximum of 1000 entries can be sold per round. The contract announces when a new lottery round begins and when a winner is selected.
