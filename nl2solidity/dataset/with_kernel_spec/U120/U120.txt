This contract serves as a cross-chain asset bridge, holding deposited tokens from one chain to facilitate their representation on another. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balance, a total supply of wrapped tokens minted on the destination chain, and a configurable bridge fee percentage. Callers can deposit supported tokens, initiating a transfer, and can withdraw their corresponding wrapped tokens from the destination chain. A designated operator can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The bridge fee is fixed at 0.1% of the transfer amount for all transactions. The maximum single deposit amount is capped at 1,000,000 units of any supported token. The contract emits an event upon successful deposit, indicating the sender, amount, and destination chain identifier, and another event upon successful withdrawal, indicating the recipient, amount, and source chain identifier.
