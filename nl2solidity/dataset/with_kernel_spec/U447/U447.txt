This contract is a liquid staking protocol that custodies staked Ether. It maintains a global pool of staked Ether, a record of each user's deposited Ether, and their corresponding liquid staking token balance. Callers can deposit Ether to receive liquid staking tokens, redeem liquid staking tokens for a pro-rata share of staked Ether, and claim rewards. A designated operator can initiate staking of deposited Ether and unstake Ether from the underlying validator set. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 0.01 Ether and charges a 10% fee on all staking rewards, which is distributed to the protocol's treasury. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward distribution events.
