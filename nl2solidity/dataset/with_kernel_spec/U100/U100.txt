This contract manages the creation and distribution of unique digital collectibles, each representing a generative AI art piece. It maintains a record of each collectible's owner, its unique identifier, and a reference to its off-chain metadata. Callers can mint new collectibles, transfer ownership of their owned collectibles, and approve other accounts to transfer their collectibles. Only a designated operator can mint new collectibles, up to a maximum supply of 10,000 collectibles. A fee of 0.01 ether is charged for each mint operation, payable to the contract owner. The contract announces when a new collectible is minted and when ownership of a collectible changes.
