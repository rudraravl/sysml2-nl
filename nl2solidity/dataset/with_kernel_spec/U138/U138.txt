This contract manages a vault for tokenized real-world assets, holding various ERC-20 tokens representing fractional ownership of physical goods. It maintains a record of each user's deposited assets, their share balance within the vault, and a global configuration for fees and asset whitelisting. Callers can deposit whitelisted ERC-20 tokens, redeem their vault shares for underlying assets, and transfer their vault shares to other accounts. A designated operator can whitelist new ERC-20 assets for deposit, update the redemption fee, and pause or unpause all deposits and redemptions. The maximum redemption fee is 5%, and a minimum of 100 shares must be held to initiate a redemption. The contract emits an event upon each successful deposit, redemption, and share transfer, and when the redemption fee is updated.
