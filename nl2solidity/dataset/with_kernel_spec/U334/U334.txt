This contract manages reputation markets, allowing users to express trust or distrust in specific entities. It maintains individual trust and distrust balances for each entity, along with a global configuration for market parameters. Callers can create a new entity, express trust in an entity, express distrust in an entity, and redeem their trust or distrust for a proportional share of the entity's counter-reputation. The owner can set the market fee, which is initially 0.5% of each trust or distrust expression, and can also pause all market operations. A newly created entity must have a minimum initial trust expression of 100 units. The contract announces when an entity is created, when trust or distrust is expressed, and when trust or distrust is redeemed.
