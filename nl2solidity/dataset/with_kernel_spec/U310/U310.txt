This contract manages the staking of a specific ERC-20 token, holding deposited tokens in custody. It maintains individual user balances of staked tokens, a global total of staked tokens, and a configurable reward rate. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their staked tokens, and claim accrued rewards. A designated operator can update the reward rate and distribute rewards to stakers. The maximum reward rate is 100 tokens per block, and the minimum staking period before withdrawal is 100 blocks. The contract emits an event when tokens are deposited, when tokens are withdrawn, and when rewards are claimed.
