This smart contract establishes a shared orderbook for non-fungible tokens, holding no custody over assets directly but facilitating their exchange. It maintains a record of active listings and bids for specific non-fungible tokens, including the token identifier, the desired price, and the seller or bidder's address. Callers can create new listings, place bids on existing listings, cancel their own listings or bids, and execute a trade when a matching listing and bid are present. A designated operator is privileged to pause all trading activity and to update the royalty enforcement mechanism. The contract imposes a maximum of 100 active listings per unique non-fungible token collection and charges a fixed 0.5% protocol fee on all successful trades, deducted from the seller's proceeds. The contract emits events upon the creation of new listings and bids, their cancellation, and the successful execution of a trade.
