This contract is a collateral vault for a decentralized lending platform, holding various ERC-20 tokens deposited by users. It maintains individual account records for each user's collateral balances and their associated borrowing limits, along with global configuration parameters like interest rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can deposit supported ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their deposited collateral, and borrow against their collateral up to their calculated limit. A designated operator can add or remove supported collateral tokens and adjust global interest rate parameters. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrowed asset is 75%, and a 0.5% origination fee is applied to all new loans. The contract announces deposit and withdrawal events, as well as loan origination and repayment events, including the affected user, asset, and amount.
