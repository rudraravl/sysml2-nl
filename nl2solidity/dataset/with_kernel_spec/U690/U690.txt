This contract mints and burns a stablecoin, backed by a basket of collateral assets held within the contract. It maintains a global reserve ratio, individual collateral deposits for each user, and a list of approved collateral types with their respective target weights. Callers can deposit approved collateral to mint stablecoins, redeem stablecoins for a proportional share of the collateral basket, and transfer their stablecoin balance to others. A designated operator can add or remove approved collateral types, adjust their target weights, and update the external price oracle used for valuation. The total minted stablecoin supply must not exceed 95% of the total value of the collateral held, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all stablecoin redemptions. The contract announces stablecoin minting and burning events, and changes to approved collateral types or their weights.
