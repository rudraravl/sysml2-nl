This contract manages token vesting schedules and streaming payments, holding custody of ERC-20 tokens designated for distribution. It maintains individual vesting schedules for each recipient, including their total allocated amount, the start time of their vesting, and the duration over which tokens vest, alongside a global configuration for the default vesting period. Callers can deposit tokens into the contract for a specific recipient, initiate a new vesting schedule for a recipient, and recipients can claim their vested tokens. Only the contract owner can set the default vesting period and pause or unpause token claims. A vesting schedule must have a duration of at least 30 days, and recipients can claim their vested tokens no more frequently than once every 24 hours. The contract emits an event whenever new tokens are deposited, a vesting schedule is created, or tokens are claimed.
