This contract manages a pool of stablecoins that back a crypto-linked debit card system, allowing users to fund their cards with cryptocurrency and spend fiat. It maintains individual user balances of stablecoins, a global stablecoin reserve, and a record of each user's linked external card identifier. Users can deposit stablecoins into their balance, initiate a fiat spending transaction by specifying an amount and their card identifier, and withdraw any remaining stablecoin balance. A designated operator can approve or reject pending fiat spending transactions, and set the transaction fee. The maximum stablecoin deposit per user is 10,000 stablecoins, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all fiat spending transactions. The contract announces when stablecoins are deposited, when fiat spending transactions are initiated, and when fiat spending transactions are approved or rejected.
