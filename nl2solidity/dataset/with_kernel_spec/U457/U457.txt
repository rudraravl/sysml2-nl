This contract is a capital allocation vault for a decentralized autonomous organization, holding various tokenized real-world assets, interest-bearing deposit receipts, and liquidity provider tokens. It maintains a record of each member's deposited capital, their allocated voting power, and the current configuration of investment strategies. Callers can deposit capital, withdraw their allocated capital, propose new investment strategies, and vote on active proposals. A designated operator can execute approved investment strategies, update the list of approved real-world asset providers, and set the fee for withdrawals. The withdrawal fee is fixed at 0.5% of the withdrawn amount, and new investment strategies require a minimum of 60% of the total voting power to pass. The contract announces when new investment strategies are proposed, when proposals pass or fail, and when capital is deployed or redeemed.
