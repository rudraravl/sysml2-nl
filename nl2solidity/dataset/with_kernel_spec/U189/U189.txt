This contract manages a token launchpad, holding deposited collateral in an escrow for new tokens. It maintains a record of each user's collateral deposits, their allocated new tokens, and the total collateral pooled for each launched token. Users can deposit collateral, claim their allocated new tokens after a launch, and withdraw any unallocated collateral. A designated operator can initiate new token launches, set the collateral requirements for each launch, and finalize the distribution of new tokens. A minimum deposit of 100 units of collateral is required for any participation, and a 2% fee is applied to all claimed new tokens, deducted from the claimed amount. The contract announces the start of new token launches and the completion of token distributions.
