This contract establishes undercollateralized credit lines for borrowers, holding no assets itself. It maintains records of each borrower's outstanding debt, available credit, and associated interest rates, alongside a global configuration for default interest and fee percentages. Callers can borrow funds against their established credit line, repay outstanding principal and accrued interest, and deposit collateral to increase their borrowing capacity. The designated administrator can create new credit lines for approved borrowers, adjust their individual interest rates, and set the global default interest rate and protocol fee percentage. A borrower's maximum available credit is capped at 500,000 units of the underlying asset, and the protocol charges a 0.5% fee on all interest payments. The contract announces when a new credit line is established and when a borrower defaults on their obligations.
