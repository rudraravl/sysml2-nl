This contract creates and manages prediction markets, holding deposited collateral for active markets. It maintains a record of each market's parameters, the total collateral for each outcome, and individual user positions within each market. Callers can create new markets, deposit collateral to back a prediction on a specific outcome, withdraw their share of collateral from a resolved market, and dispute market resolutions. A designated operator can resolve markets and set the fee percentage. Market creation requires a minimum initial collateral deposit of 100 units of the designated collateral token. A 1% fee is applied to all market resolution payouts. The contract announces market creation, deposit events, and market resolution outcomes.
