This contract tokenizes a creator's time, measured in minutes, and custodies Ether from trading fees and redemptions. It maintains a total supply of time tokens for the creator, a current price for each time token, and individual balances of time tokens for each holder. Callers can buy time tokens, sell time tokens, and redeem time tokens directly from the creator. The creator is privileged and can set their initial time token supply, withdraw accumulated Ether, and pause trading. The initial supply of time tokens for a creator must be between 100 and 10,000, and a 5% fee is applied to all buy and sell transactions, distributed to the creator. The contract announces all token transfers and price updates.
