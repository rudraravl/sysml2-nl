This contract manages a stablecoin backed by yield-generating real-world assets, holding tokenized representations of those assets as collateral. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin, a total supply of the stablecoin, and a record of the current collateralization ratio. Callers can deposit approved collateral to mint new stablecoins, redeem stablecoins for the underlying collateral, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. Only a designated operator can approve new collateral types and adjust the maximum minting limit. The contract enforces a minimum collateralization ratio of 105% for all minted stablecoins, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all stablecoin redemptions. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and transfer events, along with changes to the collateralization ratio.
