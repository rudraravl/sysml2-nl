This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral deposits in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each trading pair, and global funding rates. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close positions, add margin to existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can adjust trading pair parameters and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage allowed is 10x, and a 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing actions. The contract announces all position changes, liquidations, and funding rate updates.
