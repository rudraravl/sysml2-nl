This contract is a staking mechanism for a new governance token launch, custodying deposited fungible tokens. It maintains individual user deposit balances, accumulated reward points for each staker, and a global configuration for reward point accrual rates. Callers can deposit fungible tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim their accumulated reward points. The contract owner can set the reward point accrual rate and designate an address to distribute the new governance tokens. A minimum deposit of 100 units of the fungible token is required for each deposit. Reward points accrue at a rate of 1 point per 10 units of deposited fungible token per day. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit is made, a withdrawal occurs, or reward points are claimed.
