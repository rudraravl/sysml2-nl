This contract manages a no-loss prize pool, holding user deposits of a designated base asset and distributing yield-generated prizes. It maintains individual user deposit balances, the total pooled yield, and configuration parameters such as the prize period duration. Callers can deposit the base asset, withdraw their deposited principal, and claim a prize if selected as a winner. Only a designated operator can initiate a new prize round, which involves collecting accumulated yield and selecting a winner from eligible depositors. The minimum deposit amount is 100 units of the base asset, and a prize round must conclude within 7 days of its initiation. The contract announces the start of each new prize round and the identity of the prize winner.
