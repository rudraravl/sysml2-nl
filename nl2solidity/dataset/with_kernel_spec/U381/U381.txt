This contract facilitates charitable donations and manages a pool of matching funds for public goods. It maintains records of individual project registrations, the total amount donated to each project, and the current balance of the matching fund pool. Callers can register new projects, donate to existing projects, and claim matching funds for eligible projects. A designated operator can approve project registrations and disburse matching funds. Donations to a project must be at least 0.01 ether, and matching funds are disbursed at a 1:1 ratio up to a maximum of 10 ether per project per funding round. The contract announces when a new project is registered and when matching funds are disbursed.
