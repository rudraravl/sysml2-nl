This contract manages a euro-pegged stablecoin, holding collateralized assets that back its value. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin, a total supply of the stablecoin, and a record of the current collateralization ratio. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing approved collateral, burn stablecoins to redeem collateral, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. The owner can approve new collateral types, set the collateralization ratio, and pause or unpause minting and burning operations. The minimum collateralization ratio for minting is 100%, and the maximum stablecoin supply is 1,000,000,000 units. The contract announces minting events, burning events, and transfers of stablecoins.
