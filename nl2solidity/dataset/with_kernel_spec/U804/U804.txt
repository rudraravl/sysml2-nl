This contract manages a single-asset lending pool, holding deposited tokens and tracking outstanding loans. It maintains the total amount of tokens deposited, the total amount borrowed, and for each borrower, their current outstanding principal and accrued interest. Callers can deposit tokens into the pool, borrow tokens against a pre-approved credit line, repay their outstanding loans, and withdraw their available deposits. The owner can set the interest rate model, adjust the total credit available to a specific borrower, and pause or unpause borrowing and lending operations. The maximum annual interest rate is 25%, and a borrower's outstanding principal plus accrued interest cannot exceed 110% of their approved credit line. The contract announces deposit events, borrow events, repayment events, and withdrawal events, including the amount and the involved account.
