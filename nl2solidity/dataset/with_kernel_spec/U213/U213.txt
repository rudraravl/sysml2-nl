This contract serves as a cross-chain asset transfer bridge, holding deposited tokens on the source chain until a corresponding withdrawal is initiated on the destination chain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance for each supported token, along with a global total supply for each wrapped asset minted on the destination chain. Callers can deposit tokens, initiate a withdrawal request, and claim wrapped tokens on the destination chain. Only a designated relayer may finalize withdrawal requests and mint or burn wrapped tokens. A deposit must be at least 10 units of the deposited token. The contract charges a 0.1% fee on all withdrawals. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit is made or a withdrawal is processed.
