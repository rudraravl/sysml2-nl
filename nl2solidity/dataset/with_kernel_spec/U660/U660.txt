This contract manages a mutual cover pool, holding deposited collateral in various approved tokens. It maintains records of each user's deposited collateral, active cover policies, and outstanding claims, alongside a global configuration for cover terms and approved collateral types. Callers can deposit collateral, purchase cover for specified protocols, file claims against active policies, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can approve new collateral tokens, adjust cover policy parameters, and resolve claims. A minimum collateralization ratio of 120% is enforced for all active cover policies, and a 5% fee is applied to all cover purchases, directed to a designated treasury address. The contract announces events for all deposits, cover purchases, claim filings, claim resolutions, and withdrawals.
