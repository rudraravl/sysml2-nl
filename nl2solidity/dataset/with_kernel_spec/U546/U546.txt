This contract manages a decentralized insurance pool, holding deposited collateral tokens and issued coverage tokens. It maintains individual user balances for deposited collateral, active coverage policies, and earned rewards, alongside global pool statistics like total collateral, total outstanding coverage, and current premium rates. Callers can deposit collateral, purchase coverage policies, file claims against active policies, and withdraw their available collateral and earned rewards. A designated operator can approve or reject claims, and adjust premium rates within predefined limits. The maximum coverage period for any policy is 365 days, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all collateral withdrawals. The contract announces events for new deposits, policy purchases, claim submissions, claim resolutions, and withdrawals.
