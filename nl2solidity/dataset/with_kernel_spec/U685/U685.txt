This contract manages a perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral in a stablecoin for margin trading. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each trading pair, and global funding rates. Callers can deposit collateral, open and close positions, modify existing positions, and withdraw available margin. A designated operator can adjust risk parameters like initial margin requirements and liquidation thresholds, and also pause trading in an emergency. The maximum leverage offered is 20x, and a liquidation fee of 0.5% of the position value is applied. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, and liquidations.
