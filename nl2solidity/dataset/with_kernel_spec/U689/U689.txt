This contract manages prediction markets, holding user deposits and payouts in an escrow. It maintains individual user balances for each market, the current state of each market (open, closed, resolved), and the chosen outcome for each market. Callers can create new markets, place predictions on open markets, claim winnings from resolved markets, and withdraw their uncommitted funds. A designated operator can resolve markets by declaring the winning outcome and setting payout ratios. The maximum number of active markets at any time is 100. A 0.5% fee is applied to all winning payouts, which is directed to a treasury address. The contract announces market creation, prediction placements, and market resolutions.
