This contract provides a mechanism for users to stake funds against the continued correct operation of a designated external smart contract, holding staked tokens in escrow. It maintains a record of each user's staked amount, the specific external contract they are staking against, and a global pool of rewards for successful attestations. Callers can stake tokens, withdraw their unstaked tokens, and attest to a failure in the designated external contract. A designated operator can declare a failure event for a specific external contract, triggering a payout to successful attestors and a slashing of stakers. The contract owner can set the designated external contract and the reward rate for successful attestations. A minimum stake of 100 units of the staked token is required for any new stake, and a 1% fee is applied to all successful attestor payouts. The contract announces when a new stake is made, when a stake is withdrawn, when a failure attestation is submitted, and when a failure event is declared.
