This contract manages a decentralized exchange's concentrated liquidity pools, custodying deposited tokens for trading pairs. It maintains per-pool records of token reserves, tick ranges, and liquidity depth, alongside per-user records of active positions and their associated liquidity. Callers can deposit tokens to create new liquidity positions, add liquidity to existing positions, remove liquidity from positions, and swap tokens between pairs. Only a designated operator can adjust global trading fees, add new whitelisted token pairs, or pause trading for specific pools. A maximum of 100 distinct liquidity positions are allowed per user, and the protocol charges a fixed 0.25% trading fee on all swaps. The contract announces all liquidity additions, removals, and token swaps as events, including the affected pool, user, and token amounts.
