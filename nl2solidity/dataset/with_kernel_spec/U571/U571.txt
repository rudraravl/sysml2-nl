This contract is a yield-bearing vault for a single designated stablecoin, custodying deposited stablecoins and managing their deployment into an external yield-generating strategy. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance and their share of the total vault, along with the current total stablecoin assets under management and the vault's share price. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their stablecoin balance, and claim any accumulated yield. Only the designated operator can initiate a rebalance of the underlying strategy or update the external yield-generating strategy's address. A minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins is enforced, and withdrawals incur a 0.5% fee. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, each including the user's address and the amount of stablecoins involved.
