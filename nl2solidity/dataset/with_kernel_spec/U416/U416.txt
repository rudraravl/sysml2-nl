This contract is a governance power acquisition vault that custodies various ERC-20 tokens deposited by users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balances and their accumulated governance power, along with a global configuration for accepted deposit tokens and their respective power multipliers. Callers can deposit approved ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim their share of accumulated governance power. A designated operator can add new approved deposit tokens, update existing token power multipliers, and initiate governance power distribution. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 100 units for any approved token and a 24-hour withdrawal delay after a withdrawal request is made. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and governance power distribution events to observers.
