This contract manages a stablecoin pegged to a fraction of the value of Ether, holding Ether and a volatile reserve token to back the stablecoin supply. It maintains the total supply of the stablecoin, the amount of Ether held as collateral, the amount of reserve token held, and a target collateralization ratio. Callers can mint stablecoins by depositing Ether, redeem stablecoins for Ether, or swap stablecoins for the reserve token. A designated operator can adjust the target collateralization ratio and deposit or withdraw the reserve token. The target collateralization ratio must always be between 50% and 100%. Swapping stablecoins for the reserve token incurs a 0.5% fee, which is added to the reserve token pool. The contract emits events when stablecoins are minted, redeemed, or swapped, and when the collateralization ratio is updated.
