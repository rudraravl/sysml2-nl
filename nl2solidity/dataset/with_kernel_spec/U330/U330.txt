This contract manages a decentralized reserve currency, holding a basket of approved collateral assets. It maintains a global reserve ratio, individual user deposit balances, and a list of approved collateral tokens with their current weights. Callers can deposit approved collateral to mint new reserve tokens, withdraw their deposited collateral by burning reserve tokens, and redeem reserve tokens for a proportional share of the underlying collateral assets. A designated operator can add or remove approved collateral tokens, adjust their weights, and update the external price oracle used for valuation. The reserve ratio must always be maintained above 150%, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all collateral withdrawals. The contract announces events for all minting, burning, deposit, and withdrawal operations, as well as changes to approved collateral tokens or their weights.
