This contract manages a decentralized exchange's treasury, holding various reserve assets and its native token. It maintains records of each user's staked native tokens, the total supply of the native token, and the current exchange rates between reserve assets and the native token. Callers can deposit reserve assets to mint native tokens, withdraw reserve assets by burning native tokens, and stake native tokens to earn rewards. A designated operator can update the exchange rates and adjust staking reward parameters. The minimum deposit amount for any reserve asset is 100 units, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, and staking actions to observers.
