This contract is a decentralized reserve currency protocol that custodies a basket of stablecoin assets. It maintains a total supply of its native reserve token, the current backing per reserve token, and the balances of stablecoin assets held in its treasury. Callers can mint new reserve tokens by depositing approved stablecoins, burn reserve tokens to redeem a proportional share of the treasury's stablecoin assets, and stake their reserve tokens to earn rebase rewards. Only the owner can add or remove approved stablecoin assets, adjust the rebase rate, or transfer stablecoins out of the treasury for protocol operations. The minimum stablecoin deposit to mint reserve tokens is 10 units of the deposited stablecoin. The protocol charges a 0.5% fee on all stablecoin redemptions. The contract announces the current total supply of reserve tokens and the current backing per reserve token whenever these values change.
