This contract is a yield aggregator for a lending protocol, holding deposited tokens and their corresponding interest-bearing receipt tokens. It maintains individual user balances of deposited tokens and receipt tokens, a global configuration for approved lending pools, and a record of the current yield-bearing strategy for each supported token. Callers can deposit supported tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, claim accumulated yield, and stake their receipt tokens to earn additional rewards. The owner can add or remove supported tokens, update the yield strategy for a token, and set the platform fee. A maximum of 10 supported tokens can be configured at any time. A 5% platform fee is applied to all claimed yield. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit is made, a withdrawal occurs, yield is claimed, or the platform fee is updated.
