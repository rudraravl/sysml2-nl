This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin protocol, custodying collateral tokens and minted stablecoins. It maintains individual user positions including deposited collateral amounts, minted stablecoin debt, and a global configuration for parameters like collateralization ratios and fees. Callers can deposit collateral, mint stablecoins against their collateral, repay stablecoin debt, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can adjust protocol parameters within predefined bounds and pause minting in emergencies. The minimum collateralization ratio for minting is 150%, and a 0.5% stability fee is applied annually to outstanding stablecoin debt. The contract announces all minting, repayment, and collateral adjustment events, including the user involved and the amounts transacted.
