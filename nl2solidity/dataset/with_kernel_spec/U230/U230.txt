This contract is a yield aggregator vault for a single designated ERC-20 token, holding user deposits of that token and deploying them into various yield-generating strategies. It maintains a total deposited amount, individual user balances, and a record of which strategies are active along with their allocated capital. Callers can deposit tokens into the vault, withdraw their share of tokens, and claim any accumulated rewards. The owner can add new strategies, remove existing strategies, adjust the allocation of capital to strategies, and set the fee percentage applied to profits. A maximum of 10 strategies can be active at any given time, and the profit-sharing fee is capped at 15% of realized gains. The contract announces when a deposit occurs, when a withdrawal occurs, and when a profit harvest has been completed.
