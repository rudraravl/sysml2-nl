This contract manages a registry of digital asset managers and their associated performance data, but it does not custody any digital assets itself. It maintains a record for each registered manager, including their unique identifier, a score representing their historical performance, and a timestamp of their last score update. Callers can register a new asset manager, update an existing manager's performance score, and retrieve a manager's current score and registration details. Only a designated operator can register new managers and update existing manager scores. A manager's score must be a positive integer between 1 and 100, inclusive, and a manager's score can only be updated once every 24 hours. The contract announces when a new manager is registered and when an existing manager's score has been updated.
