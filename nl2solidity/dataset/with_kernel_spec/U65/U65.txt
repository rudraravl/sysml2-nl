This contract manages a non-custodial payment system, facilitating direct token transfers between payers and payees without holding funds itself. It maintains a registry of configured payment processors, each with its supported tokens and associated fees, and tracks the total volume processed by each processor. Callers can initiate a payment by specifying the payee, the token, the amount, and the chosen payment processor, which then triggers a transfer from the caller to the payee. A designated operator can add new payment processors, update their supported tokens and fees, and pause or unpause the entire payment system. Each payment incurs a fixed fee of 0.5% of the transferred amount, payable in the same token as the payment, and the maximum number of supported tokens for any single payment processor is limited to 10. The contract announces successful payments, including the payer, payee, token, amount, and fee collected, and also announces when a payment processor's configuration is updated.
