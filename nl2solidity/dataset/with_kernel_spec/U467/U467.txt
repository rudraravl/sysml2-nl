This contract manages credit lines backed by deposited collateral, enabling users to spend against their crypto assets. It maintains individual user credit positions, including deposited collateral amounts, outstanding debt, and available credit limits, alongside global configuration parameters like interest rates and collateralization ratios. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow against their collateral, repay outstanding debt, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can adjust global interest rates, update approved collateral tokens, and pause all borrowing operations in an emergency. The minimum collateralization ratio for any active credit line is 150%, and the maximum annual interest rate is 20%. The contract emits events for every deposit, borrow, repayment, and withdrawal, and for every change to the global interest rate or collateralization ratio.
