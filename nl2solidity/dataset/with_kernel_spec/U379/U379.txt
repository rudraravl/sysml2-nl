This contract facilitates the creation and management of new token launches, holding the newly minted tokens and any collected base currency. It maintains a record for each launched token, tracking its total supply, the amount of base currency raised, and individual participant contributions. Callers can initiate a new token launch, contribute base currency to an active launch, and claim their proportional share of the launched tokens once the launch concludes. The contract owner can set the base currency for new launches and pause or unpause all launch activity. A launch must raise at least 1000 base currency units to be considered successful, and a 2% fee on all base currency contributions is automatically directed to a designated treasury address. The contract announces when a new token launch begins, when contributions are made, and when a launch concludes.
