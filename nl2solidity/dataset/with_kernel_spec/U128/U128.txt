This contract manages a vault for non-fungible tokens, allowing users to deposit and borrow against them. It maintains individual records of deposited non-fungible tokens, outstanding loan amounts, and accrued interest for each borrower, alongside a global configuration for lending parameters and a pool of fungible tokens available for loans. Callers can deposit non-fungible tokens, borrow fungible tokens against their deposits, repay loans, and withdraw their deposited non-fungible tokens after full repayment. A designated administrator can adjust lending parameters, including the interest rate and the maximum loan-to-value ratio, and can initiate liquidations of undercollateralized loans. The maximum loan-to-value ratio is fixed at 50% of the non-fungible token's appraised value. The annual interest rate for loans is 10%. The contract announces when a non-fungible token is deposited, when a loan is taken out, when a loan is repaid, and when a non-fungible token is withdrawn.
