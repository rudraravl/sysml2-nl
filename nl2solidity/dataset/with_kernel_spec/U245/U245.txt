This contract is for a stablecoin system, minting and burning a synthetic asset pegged to a reference asset, and it custodies reserves of the reference asset. It maintains a total supply of the synthetic asset, individual account balances for the synthetic asset, and the current reserve ratio. Callers can mint new synthetic assets by depositing the reference asset, burn synthetic assets to redeem the reference asset, and transfer synthetic assets between accounts. A designated operator can adjust the target reserve ratio and pause/unpause minting and burning. The initial target reserve ratio is 1.0, meaning one unit of the reference asset backs one unit of the synthetic asset. A 0.5% fee is charged on all redemptions, payable in the reference asset. The contract emits an event whenever synthetic assets are minted, burned, or transferred.
