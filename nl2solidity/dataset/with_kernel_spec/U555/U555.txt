This contract facilitates the secure, non-custodial bridging of a specific external blockchain's native asset onto this blockchain, holding the bridged asset in escrow. It maintains a mapping of each user's deposited amount of the external asset, a total supply of the wrapped token, and a configuration for the bridge fee. Callers can deposit the external asset to mint an equivalent amount of a wrapped token, and they can redeem the wrapped token to withdraw the external asset. A designated operator is privileged to pause and unpause the bridging functionality and to update the bridge fee. The maximum bridge fee is 0.5% of the transaction value, and the minimum deposit amount is 0.0001 units of the external asset. The contract announces deposit and withdrawal events, including the user's address, the amount, and a unique transaction identifier.
