This contract manages a synthetic asset pegged to the value of a blockchain asset, holding the underlying asset in reserve. It maintains a total supply of the synthetic asset, the current reserve balance of the underlying asset, and a global price oracle value for the underlying asset. Callers can mint new synthetic tokens by depositing the underlying asset, burn synthetic tokens to redeem the underlying asset, and stake synthetic tokens to earn rewards. A designated operator can update the oracle price and adjust the reserve ratio. The contract charges a 0.5% fee on all minting and burning operations, and the reserve ratio must always be at least 100% of the synthetic asset's value. The contract emits events for all minting, burning, and oracle price updates, including the amount and the new total supply.
