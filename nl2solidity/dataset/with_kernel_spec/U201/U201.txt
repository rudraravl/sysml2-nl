This contract manages a collectible card game's digital assets, specifically unique game cards and an in-game currency, ensuring their secure custody and transfer. It maintains individual player inventories of cards and currency balances, along with global game configuration parameters like card pack prices and generation rates. Players can purchase card packs using the in-game currency, trade cards with other players, and redeem cards for in-game currency. A designated operator can mint new cards, create new card types, and adjust the price of card packs. Card pack purchases are limited to a maximum of 10 packs per transaction, and a 2% fee is applied to all card trades, payable in the in-game currency. The contract emits events for card transfers, card pack purchases, and changes to card pack prices.
