This contract facilitates the peer-to-peer exchange of non-fungible tokens (NFTs) and fungible tokens, holding deposited items in escrow during active offers. It maintains a record of all active offers, including the items offered, the items requested, and the offer's expiration time, along with a global fee percentage and a designated operator address. Callers can create new offers to exchange specific NFTs for fungible tokens or other NFTs, accept existing offers by fulfilling the requested items, and cancel their own active offers. A designated operator can update the global fee percentage and withdraw accumulated fees. The contract enforces a maximum offer duration of 30 days and applies a 2% fee on all successful exchanges, deducted from the fungible token portion of the trade. The contract emits an event when a new offer is created, when an offer is successfully accepted, and when an offer is cancelled.
