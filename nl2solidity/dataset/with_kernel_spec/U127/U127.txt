This contract manages a pool of stablecoin deposits, acting as a capital buffer for real-world insurance risks. It maintains a total deposited amount, individual user deposit records, and a configuration for risk exposure limits. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their deposited stablecoins, and claim rewards accrued from the underlying risk coverage. A designated operator can initiate new risk tranches, settle risk outcomes, and adjust the maximum allowed capital deployment. The total capital deployed across all active risk tranches cannot exceed 80% of the total deposited capital. A 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, deducted from the withdrawn amount. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and risk tranche settlement outcomes.
