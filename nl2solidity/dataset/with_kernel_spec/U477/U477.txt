This contract facilitates self-custodial peer-to-peer trading of tokenized real-world assets and perpetual futures, holding deposited collateral in various supported cryptocurrencies. It maintains individual account balances, open positions for each trader, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and supported asset lists. Callers can deposit collateral, open new perpetual future positions, close existing positions, withdraw available collateral, and claim funding payments. Only a designated operator can adjust global trading parameters, add new tradable assets, or pause trading in emergencies. A maximum leverage of 20x is enforced for all perpetual future positions, and a trading fee of 0.05% of the notional value is applied to all trades. The contract broadcasts events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, position closures, and funding payment settlements.
