This contract manages a payment network's transaction fees and their distribution to social impact projects. It maintains a global fee percentage, a list of approved social impact projects with their respective funding allocations, and a ledger of all transactions including their associated fees and project contributions. Callers can initiate payments, claim their portion of collected fees if they are a designated project, and query their transaction history. The owner can set the global transaction fee, add or remove social impact projects, and adjust their funding allocations. The global transaction fee is fixed at 0.5% of the payment amount, and 33% of all collected fees are automatically allocated to the approved social impact projects. The contract emits an event when a payment is successfully processed and when funds are claimed by a project.
