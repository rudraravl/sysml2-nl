This contract facilitates cross-chain asset transfers, holding wrapped tokens on behalf of users. It maintains a mapping of original chain asset addresses to their wrapped token equivalents on this chain, and tracks each user's wrapped token balances. Callers can deposit native assets on a source chain to mint wrapped tokens on this chain, or burn wrapped tokens on this chain to release native assets on a destination chain. A designated operator is privileged to register new cross-chain asset mappings and to pause or unpause all transfer operations. Each cross-chain transfer incurs a fixed fee of 0.1% of the transfer amount, and the maximum single transfer amount is capped at 1,000,000 units of any supported asset. The contract announces successful deposits, successful withdrawals, and changes to asset mappings.
