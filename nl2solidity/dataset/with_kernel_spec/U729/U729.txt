This contract facilitates the bridging of an external blockchain's native currency to an EVM-compatible chain, holding the deposited external currency in custody. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance, a total supply of the wrapped token, and a configuration for the bridge fee. Callers can deposit external currency, initiating a bridge transfer, and can withdraw their wrapped tokens, burning them to redeem the underlying external currency. A designated operator can pause and unpause deposits and withdrawals, and can update the bridge fee. The bridge fee is fixed at 0.1% of the deposit amount, and a minimum deposit of 0.001 of the external currency is enforced. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the bridge fee.
