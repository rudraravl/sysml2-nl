This contract facilitates anonymous transfers of a standard fungible token by breaking the link between deposits and withdrawals; it custodies deposited tokens. It maintains a Merkle tree of deposit commitments, a nullifier set of spent commitments, and a configuration for the minimum and maximum deposit amounts. Callers can deposit tokens to generate a commitment, withdraw tokens by providing a valid zero-knowledge proof of a commitment and nullifier, and relay a withdrawal for another user. Only the owner can update the contract's configuration parameters, such as the fee percentage or the maximum deposit amount. The minimum deposit amount is 0.01 tokens, and the maximum deposit amount is 1000 tokens. The contract announces successful deposits, successful withdrawals, and changes to the configuration.
