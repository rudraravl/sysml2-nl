This contract facilitates a decentralized NFT bidding system, holding deposited fungible tokens as collateral for bids. It maintains a record of each user's fungible token balance, active bids for specific NFT collections, and a global configuration for fees and minimum bid amounts. Callers can deposit fungible tokens, place bids on NFT collections specifying a price and quantity, cancel their active bids, and withdraw their fungible token balance. A designated operator can update the fee percentage and the minimum bid amount for any collection. A 0.5% fee is applied to successful bid fulfillments, and the minimum bid amount for any collection must be at least 0.001 of the fungible token. The contract emits events upon successful deposits, bid placements, bid cancellations, and bid fulfillments.
