This contract manages a simple coin flip prediction game, holding user deposits of the native blockchain currency. It maintains individual player balances, a total contract balance, and a record of active bets including the predicted outcome and wagered amount for each. Callers can place a bet on heads or tails, providing a wager, and can claim winnings if their bet was successful. A designated operator can resolve a bet by providing a random outcome, and can withdraw collected fees. The minimum bet amount is 0.01 units of the native currency, and a 2% fee is deducted from all winning payouts. The contract emits an event when a new bet is placed and when a bet is resolved.
