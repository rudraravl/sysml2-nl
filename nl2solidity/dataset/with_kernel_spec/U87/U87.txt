This contract manages a gaming item crafting system, holding deposited fungible tokens and non-fungible tokens used as crafting materials. It maintains a registry of crafting recipes, each specifying required input tokens and the resulting output non-fungible token, along with individual user balances of deposited materials. Callers can deposit fungible and non-fungible tokens, craft items by consuming deposited materials according to a recipe, and withdraw any of their deposited tokens. A designated operator can add new crafting recipes, remove existing recipes, and set the crafting fee. Each crafting recipe has a fixed crafting fee of 50 units of the primary fungible token, and a maximum of 10 distinct crafting recipes can be active at any given time. The contract announces when a new recipe is added, when a recipe is removed, and when a user successfully crafts an item.
