This contract manages a leveraged yield-generating position, holding deposited base tokens and their corresponding yield-bearing tokens. It maintains records of each user's deposited base tokens, their accrued yield, and the configuration parameters for leverage. Callers can deposit base tokens, withdraw their principal and accrued yield, claim rewards from the leveraged position, and repay outstanding leverage. A designated operator can adjust the leverage ratio within predefined bounds and initiate the harvesting and re-investment of yield. The maximum leverage ratio is capped at 2.0x, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all claimed rewards. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claim events.
