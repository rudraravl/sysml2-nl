This contract facilitates private asset withdrawals and custodies deposited tokens. It maintains a Merkle tree of commitment hashes, a record of nullifiers for spent commitments, and a configuration for accepted token types and their associated deposit limits. Callers can deposit supported tokens, generating a cryptographic commitment, and later withdraw assets by providing a valid zero-knowledge proof and a nullifier for a previously unspent commitment. An owner can add or remove supported token types and set the maximum deposit amount for each token, which is capped at 1000 units of the respective token. Withdrawals incur a fixed fee of 0.1% of the withdrawn amount, payable in the withdrawn token, which is sent to a designated treasury address. The contract announces successful deposits and withdrawals, including the token type and amount.
