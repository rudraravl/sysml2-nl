This contract manages a perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral deposited by users to back their trading positions. It maintains individual account balances for deposited collateral, open long and short positions for each user, and a global funding rate for each trading pair. Callers can deposit collateral, open new long or short positions, close existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update the funding rate for each trading pair and adjust the maximum leverage allowed. The maximum leverage for any position is 100x. A 0.1% fee is charged on all position openings and closings. The contract emits an event whenever a new position is opened or closed, and when the funding rate is updated.
