This contract manages leveraged perpetual futures positions, holding collateral in various accepted tokens. It maintains individual account balances for each collateral token, open position details including size, entry price, and liquidation price, and global configuration parameters like maximum leverage and trading fees. Callers can open new positions, increase or decrease existing positions, close positions, deposit collateral, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update global configuration parameters, adjust oracle addresses, and pause trading in emergencies. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 100x. A trading fee of 0.08% of the position's notional value is charged on opening and closing positions. The contract emits events for position openings, closures, collateral deposits, and withdrawals.
