This contract manages a basket of yield-bearing tokens, specifically liquid staking derivatives, allowing users to deposit and withdraw a base currency. It maintains individual balances for each user's share of the basket, along with the current composition of the basket and configuration parameters like fees and rebalancing thresholds. Callers can deposit the base currency to receive basket shares, withdraw their share of the underlying liquid staking derivatives, and claim any accumulated rewards. A designated operator can adjust the basket's composition by swapping underlying assets, and set the deposit and withdrawal fees. The deposit fee is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited amount, and withdrawals are subject to a maximum 2% fee. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, and changes to the basket's composition.
