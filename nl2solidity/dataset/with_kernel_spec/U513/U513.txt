This contract manages player progression and rewards within a decentralized role-playing game, holding no custodial assets directly. It maintains individual player experience points, current character level, and a record of completed quests for each player. Players can initiate a new game, complete quests to earn experience points, and spend experience points to level up their character. A designated game administrator can define new quests, adjust experience point rewards for quests, and reset a player's progress. Quests must have a minimum experience point reward of 10 and a maximum of 1000. The contract emits an event whenever a player levels up or completes a quest.
