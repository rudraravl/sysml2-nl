This contract manages a collection of unique digital character assets and their associated in-game items, holding no custodied value itself. It maintains a record for each character asset, detailing its unique attributes, current experience points, and equipped items, alongside a global registry of all available item types and their properties. Callers can create new character assets, assign experience points to their owned characters, equip or unequip items from their characters, and transfer character assets to other accounts. A designated operator can define new item types and their properties, and also mint initial quantities of these items into the game's ecosystem. A character asset's experience points are capped at 10,000, and equipping an item requires the character to have at least 500 experience points. The contract announces when a new character asset is created, when an item is equipped or unequipped, and when a character asset is transferred.
