This contract manages a credit line collateralized by user-deposited digital assets, enabling users to spend against their crypto holdings without selling them. It maintains individual user accounts, tracking each user's collateral balances, outstanding credit, and accrued interest, alongside a global configuration for interest rates and supported collateral types. Callers can deposit supported digital assets as collateral, borrow stablecoins against their collateral, repay outstanding credit, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can add or remove supported collateral tokens, adjust interest rates, and initiate liquidations of undercollateralized positions. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any collateral asset is 50%, and the annual interest rate for borrowed stablecoins is fixed at 12%. The contract announces when a user's collateral balance changes, when a loan is taken out or repaid, and when a liquidation occurs.
