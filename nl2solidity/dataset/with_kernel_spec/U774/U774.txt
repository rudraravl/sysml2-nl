This contract manages perpetual futures positions, holding collateral in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances for collateral, open long and short positions for each trading pair, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can open new positions, close existing positions, add collateral, withdraw excess collateral, and claim funding payments. A designated operator can adjust trading pair parameters, update oracle prices, and initiate liquidations for undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 10x the collateral provided. A 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing actions, paid in the designated stablecoin. The contract announces all position changes, liquidations, and funding rate updates.
