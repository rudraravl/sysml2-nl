This contract manages a decentralized lending and borrowing market, custodying deposited collateral and borrowed assets. It maintains records for each user's deposits and loans, market-wide interest rate parameters, and a pool of available liquidity for each supported asset. Callers can deposit assets as collateral, borrow assets against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw their available collateral. A designated operator can adjust market parameters, including interest rates and collateral factors, and can pause borrowing or depositing in an emergency. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrowed asset is 80%, and a 0.5% protocol fee is applied to all interest payments. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, borrows, repayments, and changes to market parameters.
