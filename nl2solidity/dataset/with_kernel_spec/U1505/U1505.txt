This contract manages a perpetual futures market for a power-indexed asset, holding collateral in a designated base token. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including long or short status, entry price, and size, and global market configuration such as funding rates and margin requirements. Callers can deposit collateral, open new positions, close existing positions, partially adjust position size, withdraw available collateral, and claim funding payments. A designated operator can adjust the global funding rate calculation parameters and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A minimum initial margin of 10% of the position's notional value is required to open a new position, and a 0.05% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing actions. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, position closures, and liquidations, including the affected account, asset, and amount.
