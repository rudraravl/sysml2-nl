This liquid staking contract allows users to deposit a base asset and receive a liquid staking token representing their staked position, and it custodies the deposited base asset. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, a total amount of the base asset staked, and a record of each user's liquid staking token balance. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive liquid staking tokens, and they can redeem their liquid staking tokens to withdraw the base asset. The owner can set the staking reward rate and can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. A minimum deposit amount of 0.1 units of the base asset is enforced, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the staking reward rate.
