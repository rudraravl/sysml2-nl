This contract is a yield-boosting vault for a specific liquid staking token, holding deposited liquid staking tokens and their associated reward tokens. It maintains individual user balances of deposited liquid staking tokens, a global count of total deposited tokens, and a configuration for the reward distribution strategy. Callers can deposit liquid staking tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim accumulated reward tokens. A designated operator can update the reward distribution strategy and trigger the distribution of accumulated reward tokens. The maximum deposit amount per user is 100,000 liquid staking tokens. A 0.5% fee is applied to all reward token claims. The contract emits an event upon successful deposit, withdrawal, and reward claim, specifying the user, amount, and relevant token.
