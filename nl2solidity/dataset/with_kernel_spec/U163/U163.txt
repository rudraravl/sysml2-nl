This contract manages a decentralized options market, holding collateral tokens deposited by writers and premiums paid by buyers. It maintains a record of each user's collateral balance, their active option positions, and market configuration parameters for each option series. Callers can create new option series, buy options, exercise options, deposit collateral, and withdraw collateral. A designated operator can pause trading for specific option series, and the owner can adjust global trading fees. The maximum number of option series active at any time is 200. A 0.1% fee is charged on option purchases, paid to a treasury address. The contract announces events for new option series creation, option purchases, option exercises, and collateral deposits and withdrawals.
