This contract facilitates liquid staking of a native blockchain asset, holding deposited assets and issuing a liquid staking token in return. It maintains a mapping of each user's deposited native assets, their corresponding liquid staking token balance, and the current exchange rate between the native asset and the liquid staking token. Callers can deposit native assets to receive liquid staking tokens, redeem liquid staking tokens for native assets, and claim rewards accrued from the underlying staking mechanism. Only a designated operator can update the exchange rate between the native asset and the liquid staking token, and only the contract owner can set the redemption fee. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed native assets, and the minimum deposit amount for native assets is 0.1 units. The contract announces deposit events, redemption events, and exchange rate updates.
