This contract facilitates the one-way bridging of a specific ERC-20 token from a source chain to a destination chain, holding a reserve of the bridged token on the source chain. It maintains a mapping of destination chain addresses to the amount of bridged token they are entitled to receive, and a record of each bridge transaction's unique identifier and its status. Callers can initiate a bridge transfer by depositing tokens and specifying a destination address, and can claim their tokens on the destination chain once the transfer is processed. A designated operator is privileged to mark bridge transfers as processed, enabling claims on the destination chain, and can adjust the bridging fee. The maximum amount of tokens that can be bridged in a single transaction is 10,000 tokens, and a 0.5% fee is deducted from each initiated bridge transfer. The contract announces the initiation of a bridge transfer, including the source address, destination address, amount, and a unique identifier, and also announces when a bridge transfer has been successfully processed.
