This contract is a liquid staking vault for a base-layer cryptocurrency, holding deposited amounts of that cryptocurrency. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount and their accumulated rewards, along with a global total of staked funds and a configurable reward rate. Callers can deposit the base-layer cryptocurrency, withdraw their deposited amount and accrued rewards, and claim only their accrued rewards. A designated operator can update the reward rate and trigger the distribution of rewards from the vault's balance to user accounts. The minimum deposit amount is 1 unit of the base-layer cryptocurrency, and the reward rate cannot exceed 0.05% per day. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward claim events, including the user's address and the amount involved.
