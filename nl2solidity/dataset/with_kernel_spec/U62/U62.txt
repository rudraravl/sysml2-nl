This contract manages a perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral funds deposited by users. It maintains individual account balances, open positions with their associated leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and trading fees. Callers can deposit collateral, open new long or short positions, close existing positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can adjust trading fees, update oracle price feeds, and pause trading in emergency situations. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x, and a 0.10% fee is applied to all position opening and closing operations. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, position openings, and position closures, including the account involved, asset, amount, and relevant parameters.
