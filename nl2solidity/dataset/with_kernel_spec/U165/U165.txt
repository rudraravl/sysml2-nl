This contract facilitates a marketplace for unique, graded digital collectibles, holding the digital collectibles themselves in escrow during active listings. It maintains a registry of all listed collectibles, their current asking prices, and the identity of their sellers, along with a configurable platform fee percentage. Callers can list a collectible for sale, purchase a listed collectible, and delist their own active listings. Only a designated operator can adjust the platform fee, which is capped at a maximum of 5% of the sale price. The contract announces when a collectible is listed, when it is successfully sold, and when a listing is canceled.
