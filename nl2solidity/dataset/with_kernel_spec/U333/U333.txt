This contract manages a vault for depositing and staking a single designated ERC-20 token, holding deposited tokens in custody. It maintains individual user balances for deposited tokens, staked amounts, and accumulated rewards, alongside a global reward rate and a total staked token supply. Callers can deposit tokens into the vault, stake their deposited tokens to earn rewards, unstake tokens, and withdraw their available balance. A designated operator can update the reward rate, which is capped at 0.05 tokens per second per staked token, and can pause or unpause staking operations. The contract emits an event whenever tokens are deposited, staked, unstaked, or withdrawn, and when the reward rate is updated. The minimum deposit amount is 100 of the designated ERC-20 token.
