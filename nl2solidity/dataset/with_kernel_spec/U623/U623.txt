This contract manages tokenized real estate assets, representing fractional ownership of properties, and custodies the underlying asset tokens. It maintains a registry of all tokenized properties, each with its unique identifier, total supply of fractional tokens, and a record of each holder's balance. Callers can purchase fractional tokens for listed properties, transfer their fractional tokens to other accounts, and redeem fractional tokens for the underlying asset when a property is liquidated. A designated operator can list new properties, set the fractional token supply for each property, and initiate property liquidations. The maximum number of fractional tokens for any single property is 1,000,000, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all token transfers, payable to the designated operator. The contract announces property listings, token transfers, and property liquidations to observers.
