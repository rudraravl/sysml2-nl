This contract manages a vault for a yield-bearing stablecoin, holding user deposits of an underlying stablecoin and issuing a yield-accruing token. It maintains individual user balances of the yield-accruing token, the total supply of the yield-accruing token, and the current amount of underlying stablecoin held in the vault. Callers can deposit underlying stablecoin to mint the yield-accruing token, withdraw underlying stablecoin by burning the yield-accruing token, and redeem their yield-accruing tokens for the underlying stablecoin after a specified lockup period. A designated operator can transfer underlying stablecoin to and from external yield-generating strategies, and can update the address of the underlying stablecoin. Only the owner can set the designated operator address. A minimum deposit of 100 units of the underlying stablecoin is required for each deposit operation. Withdrawals are subject to a 0.1% fee on the amount withdrawn, which is sent to the owner. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, and redemptions, including the user's address, the amount of underlying stablecoin, and the amount of yield-accruing token.
