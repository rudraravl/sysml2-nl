This contract manages a collection of unique digital assets, each representing a distinct in-game character, and holds a fungible utility token used for in-game transactions. It maintains a record of ownership for each unique digital asset, the current balance of the utility token for each player, and a global configuration for asset creation and modification. Players can acquire new unique digital assets, transfer their owned unique digital assets to other players, and spend utility tokens for in-game actions. A designated operator can mint new unique digital assets, burn existing unique digital assets, and adjust the utility token emission rate. New unique digital assets can only be minted if the total supply of unique digital assets does not exceed 10,000. Each transfer of a unique digital asset incurs a 2% fee, payable in the utility token, which is sent to a treasury address. The contract emits an event whenever a unique digital asset is transferred, minted, or burned, and when a player's utility token balance changes.
