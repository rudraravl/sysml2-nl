This contract facilitates one-way transfers of a specific fungible token from a source chain to a destination chain, holding transferred tokens in escrow on the source chain. It maintains a record of each user's total deposited amount and the total amount bridged to the destination chain. Callers can deposit tokens into the contract and initiate a bridge operation, which locks their tokens and signals the destination chain to mint corresponding tokens. Only a designated operator can finalize a bridge operation, which deducts the bridged amount from the contract's total balance. A minimum deposit of 100 tokens is required for any bridge operation. A 0.1% fee is applied to all bridge operations, deducted from the amount transferred. The contract emits an event upon each successful deposit and another event upon each successful bridge operation, including the sender's address, the amount, and a unique transaction identifier.
