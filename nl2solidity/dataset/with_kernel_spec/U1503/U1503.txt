This contract is a decentralized exchange for two tokens, holding reserves of both tokens in a liquidity pool. It maintains the current reserves of each token in the pool, a trading fee percentage, and the total liquidity tokens minted. Callers can add liquidity by depositing both tokens, remove liquidity by burning their liquidity tokens, and swap one token for the other. The owner can set the trading fee percentage and withdraw accumulated fees. The trading fee is fixed at 0.3% of the swapped amount. Swaps must maintain a product of reserves that is at least 99.7% of the product before the swap, accounting for fees. The contract announces liquidity additions, liquidity removals, and successful token swaps.
