This contract is a privacy mixer that custodies deposited tokens. It maintains a list of unspent deposit commitments, a total deposit amount for each supported token, and a configuration for each mixer instance including its denomination and the token it accepts. Callers can deposit tokens by providing a cryptographic commitment, withdraw tokens by providing a valid proof and a nullifier, and relay a withdrawal for another user. The owner can add new mixer instances, update the fee percentage, and designate new relayers. A deposit must be for the exact denomination configured for that mixer instance, and a withdrawal incurs a 0.1% fee on the withdrawn amount. The contract announces new deposits, successful withdrawals, and changes to the fee percentage.
