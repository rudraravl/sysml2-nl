This contract manages a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, holding collateral deposited by traders. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including their margin and leverage, and global configuration parameters such as funding rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can deposit collateral, open new positions, close existing positions, adjust leverage on open positions, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update global configuration parameters and initiate liquidations for undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 20x, and a 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing volume. The contract announces position openings, position closures, and liquidations.
