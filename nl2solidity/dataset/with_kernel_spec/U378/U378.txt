This contract manages perpetual futures positions, holding collateral tokens deposited by traders. It maintains individual account balances for collateral, open positions including their size and entry price, and a global configuration for each supported trading pair. Callers can open new positions, increase or decrease existing positions, close positions, deposit collateral, withdraw collateral, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can adjust trading pair parameters such as funding rates and liquidation thresholds, and can pause all trading activity. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x, and a 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing actions. The contract announces position openings, position closures, liquidations, and collateral deposits and withdrawals.
