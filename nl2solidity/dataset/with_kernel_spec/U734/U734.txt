This contract manages a token-pegged stablecoin system, holding reserves of a base token and minting a stablecoin token. It maintains the current total supply of the stablecoin, the amount of base token held as reserves, and individual user balances of the stablecoin. Callers can mint stablecoins by depositing the base token, redeem stablecoins for the base token, and transfer stablecoins to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the minting and redemption fees, and can pause or unpause minting and redemption operations. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% and the minting fee is fixed at 0.2%. The contract emits an event whenever stablecoins are minted, redeemed, or transferred, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
