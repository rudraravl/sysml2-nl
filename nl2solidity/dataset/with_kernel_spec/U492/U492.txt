This contract manages a player's in-game virtual land parcels, acting as a secure registry and escrow for these non-fungible digital assets. It maintains records of each player's owned land parcels, their current status (e.g., undeveloped, developed, rented), and any associated upgrade levels. Players can acquire new parcels, develop existing parcels by spending in-game currency, and transfer ownership of their parcels to other players. A designated game administrator can mint new land parcels, set the development cost for each parcel type, and pause all player-initiated transfers in emergency situations. Each land parcel has a unique identifier, and development costs are fixed at 1000 units of in-game currency per level for standard parcels, with a maximum of 5 development levels per parcel. The contract announces when a new land parcel is minted, when a parcel's development level changes, and when ownership of a parcel is transferred.
