This contract manages a virtual world's land parcels, holding non-fungible tokens representing unique land plots. It maintains records of each land plot's owner, its coordinates, and any associated metadata, along with a global configuration for land sales. Callers can purchase available land plots, transfer ownership of their plots, and approve other accounts to manage their plots. A designated operator can list new land plots for sale, set the price for each plot, and update the global sale configuration. The maximum number of land plots an individual account can own is 100, and a 2% royalty fee is applied to all secondary market land transfers, payable to a designated treasury account. The contract announces when a land plot is purchased, when its ownership is transferred, and when its metadata is updated.
