This contract manages a pre-launch token distribution and NFT reward system, holding deposited yield-bearing tokens on behalf of users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, their accumulated share of the distribution token, and the total supply of distribution tokens allocated to the contract. Callers can deposit eligible yield-bearing tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens and any earned distribution tokens, and claim their randomized reward NFTs. A designated operator can set the eligible yield-bearing token address and the distribution token address, and initiate the distribution token release. The contract will only accept deposits of the designated eligible yield-bearing token, and the total supply of distribution tokens available for this contract is capped at 70,000,000 units. The contract emits events when tokens are deposited, withdrawn, reward NFTs are claimed, and when the distribution token address is set.
