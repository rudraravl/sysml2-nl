This contract manages a decentralized lending pool, holding deposited base assets and issuing yield-bearing tokens representing a claim on those assets plus accrued interest. It maintains individual account balances for deposited base assets, issued yield tokens, and accrued interest, alongside a global pool balance and a configuration for interest rates. Callers can deposit base assets, withdraw their deposited base assets and accrued interest, and transfer their yield tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can adjust the interest rate parameters and pause or unpause deposits and withdrawals. The minimum deposit amount is 100 units of the base asset, and the maximum annual interest rate is capped at 15%. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit or withdrawal occurs, detailing the account involved and the amount transferred.
