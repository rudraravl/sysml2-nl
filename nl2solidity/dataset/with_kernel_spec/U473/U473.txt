This contract facilitates the transfer of an arbitrary fungible token between a Layer 1 blockchain and a Layer 2 blockchain, holding deposited tokens in escrow on Layer 1. It maintains a record of each user's deposited token balance awaiting withdrawal on Layer 2, and a mapping of unique message identifiers to their processing status. Callers can deposit tokens to be bridged, initiate a withdrawal of their tokens from Layer 2 back to Layer 1, and claim their withdrawn tokens on Layer 1 after a successful Layer 2 withdrawal. A designated relayer can finalize a Layer 2 withdrawal on Layer 1 by providing proof of the Layer 2 transaction. The owner can set the fee for withdrawals and pause deposits. The maximum deposit amount per transaction is 100,000 tokens, and the withdrawal fee is 0.05% of the withdrawn amount. The contract emits an event when tokens are deposited, when a withdrawal is initiated, and when a withdrawal is finalized.
