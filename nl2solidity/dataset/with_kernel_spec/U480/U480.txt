This contract manages user-owned digital asset accounts, holding various fungible tokens and non-fungible tokens on behalf of individual users. It maintains per-user balances for each fungible token and a list of non-fungible tokens owned by each user. Callers can deposit tokens into their account, transfer tokens to another user's account, and withdraw tokens from their account to an external address. A designated operator can pause all transfer and withdrawal operations. The maximum number of non-fungible tokens a single user account can hold is 100. All withdrawals incur a fixed fee of 0.01 units of the withdrawn token, which is sent to a treasury address. The contract announces successful deposits, transfers, and withdrawals, including the sender, recipient, token type, and amount.
