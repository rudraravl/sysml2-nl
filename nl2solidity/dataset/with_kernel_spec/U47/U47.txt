This contract is a liquid staking protocol for a proof-of-stake blockchain, custodying deposited native tokens and issuing corresponding liquid staking tokens. It maintains a registry of approved validator identities, the total amount of native tokens staked with each validator, and each user's balance of liquid staking tokens. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, unstake their liquid staking tokens to initiate a withdrawal of native tokens, and claim their withdrawn native tokens after a cooling-off period. A designated operator can add new validators to the approved list, remove existing validators, and adjust the staking allocation among approved validators. The cooling-off period for unstaking is 3 days, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all unstaking operations. The contract announces deposit events, unstake requests, and successful withdrawals, including the amounts involved and the associated user addresses.
