This liquid staking contract custodies deposited base tokens and issues liquid staking tokens representing a share of the pooled staked assets. It maintains a global total of deposited base tokens, a global total of issued liquid staking tokens, and a per-user record of their liquid staking token balance. Callers can deposit base tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, and can redeem liquid staking tokens to withdraw an equivalent share of base tokens. The owner can set the staking reward fee and can update the underlying staking strategy. The contract charges a 5% fee on all staking rewards. Redemptions are subject to a 7-day unbonding period. The contract announces deposit events including the depositor's address and the amount deposited, and redemption events including the redeemer's address and the amount redeemed.
