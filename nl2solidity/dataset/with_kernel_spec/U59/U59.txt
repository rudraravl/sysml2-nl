This contract manages a seigniorage-based stablecoin system, holding reserve tokens and newly minted stablecoins. It maintains a total supply for the stablecoin, a redemption pool of reserve tokens, and a per-account stablecoin balance. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing reserve tokens, redeem stablecoins for reserve tokens, and transfer stablecoins between accounts. A designated operator can adjust the target stablecoin price within a 1% deviation band, and can initiate a system-wide redemption pause lasting no more than 24 hours. Redemptions incur a 0.5% fee, and the system will not allow the stablecoin supply to exceed 1,000,000,000 units. The contract announces stablecoin transfers, minting events, and redemption events.
