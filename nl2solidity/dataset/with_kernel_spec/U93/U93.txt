This contract facilitates the creation and auctioning of unique digital assets, holding the digital assets themselves and any associated bid funds. It maintains a record of each digital asset, its current owner, its auction status, the highest bid, and the current reserve price, along with a global platform fee percentage. Callers can mint new digital assets, place bids on active auctions, accept bids to conclude an auction, and withdraw their owned digital assets or successful bid funds. Only the contract owner can adjust the platform fee percentage and designate approved minters. The platform fee is fixed at 10% of the final sale price, and auctions must have a minimum duration of 24 hours. The contract announces when a new digital asset is minted, when a bid is placed, and when an auction concludes.
