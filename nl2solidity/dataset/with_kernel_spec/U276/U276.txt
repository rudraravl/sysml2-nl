This contract manages the creation and sale of digital content items, holding the content's ownership rights until purchased. It maintains a record of each content item, including its current owner, its sale price, and a unique identifier, along with a global fee percentage and a designated fee recipient. Callers can create new content items, transfer ownership of their content items, list their content items for sale at a specified price, purchase listed content items, and delist their content items. Only the contract owner can set the global fee percentage and change the fee recipient. The initial sale price for any content item must be at least 0.001 ether, and the global fee percentage cannot exceed 10%. The contract announces when a new content item is created, when a content item is listed for sale, and when a content item is purchased.
