This contract manages a no-loss scratch card game, holding stablecoin deposits backing player funds and prize pools. It maintains individual player balances, a global prize pool, a recovery ticket pool, and game configuration parameters like card prices and prize probabilities. Callers can purchase scratch cards, reveal card outcomes, claim instant prizes, and redeem recovery tickets for stablecoins. A designated operator can adjust game parameters, replenish prize pools, and pause game operations in emergencies. Each scratch card costs 10 stablecoins, and 55% of cards award an instant prize, with a maximum instant prize of 10,000 stablecoins. The contract emits events for card purchases, prize claims, and recovery ticket redemptions.
