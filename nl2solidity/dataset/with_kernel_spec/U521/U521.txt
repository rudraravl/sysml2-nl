This contract is a liquid staking vault for a proof-of-stake blockchain, holding deposited native tokens and issuing an equivalent amount of a liquid staking derivative token. It maintains a record of each user's deposited native tokens and issued derivative tokens, a total supply of the derivative token, and a current staking yield rate. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive derivative tokens, withdraw native tokens by burning derivative tokens, and claim staking rewards. A designated operator can update the staking yield rate and initiate withdrawals from the underlying staking mechanism. The maximum deposit per transaction is 1000 native tokens. A 0.5% fee is applied to all staking rewards before distribution. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward distribution events.
