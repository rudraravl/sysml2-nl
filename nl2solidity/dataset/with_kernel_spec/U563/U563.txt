This contract manages a decentralized exchange for token swaps and liquidity provision, holding deposited tokens within various trading pools. It maintains records of each user's liquidity positions, the balances of all token pairs within each pool, and global configuration parameters such as trading fees and the address of a designated fee recipient. Callers can deposit tokens to create new liquidity positions, add tokens to existing positions, remove tokens from their positions, and swap one token for another within a pool. A designated owner can update the trading fee percentage, add new token pairs to be traded, and pause all trading and liquidity operations in an emergency. The maximum trading fee is 0.5% of the swapped amount, and the minimum initial liquidity for any new pool must be at least 100 units of each token in the pair. The contract announces events for all token swaps, liquidity additions, and liquidity removals, including the tokens involved and the quantities exchanged.
