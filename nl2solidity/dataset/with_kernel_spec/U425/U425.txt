This contract is a yield vault that custodies stablecoins deposited by users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, the total stablecoins held, and the current yield strategy configuration. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their deposited stablecoins, and claim accumulated yield. A designated operator can update the active yield strategy and adjust the withdrawal fee. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 100 stablecoins and charges a 0.1% fee on all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events.
