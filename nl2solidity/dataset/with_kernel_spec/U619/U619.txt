This contract manages ownership and access permissions for digital land parcels within a metaverse, holding no custodied assets itself. It maintains a record of each parcel's current owner, a list of approved editors for each parcel, and a global configuration for parcel creation fees. Callers can claim an unowned parcel, transfer ownership of a parcel they own, add or remove an editor for a parcel they own, and retrieve ownership or editor information for any parcel. Only a designated operator can set the fee for claiming new parcels and pause or unpause the ability to claim new parcels. A parcel claim fee is fixed at 0.01 ether, and a maximum of 10 editors can be assigned to any single parcel. The contract announces when a parcel has been claimed, when its ownership has been transferred, and when an editor has been added or removed.
