This contract manages a synthetic asset that tracks the value of a major cryptocurrency, enabling users to stake a stablecoin to mint the synthetic asset and redeem the synthetic asset for the stablecoin. It maintains individual user balances of staked stablecoin and minted synthetic assets, along with a global pool of staked stablecoin and the current exchange rate between the stablecoin and the synthetic asset. Callers can stake stablecoin, mint synthetic assets, redeem synthetic assets, and withdraw stablecoin. A designated operator can update the exchange rate and pause or unpause staking and redemption operations. A 0.5% fee is applied to all stablecoin withdrawals, and the maximum daily exchange rate fluctuation is limited to 5% of the previous day's rate. The contract announces successful staking events, successful redemption events, and exchange rate updates.
