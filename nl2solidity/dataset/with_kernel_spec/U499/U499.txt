This contract manages a utility token that grants access to tiered luxury benefits, holding no custodied assets itself. It maintains a record of each account's token balance and a mapping of benefit tiers to specific access requirements. Callers can transfer tokens between accounts, approve spending by other accounts, and burn tokens to redeem benefits. An owner can mint new tokens, set the total supply cap, and configure the benefit tier requirements. The total supply of tokens is capped at 100,000,000 units. Burning tokens for benefits requires a minimum of 1,000 tokens for the lowest tier. The contract announces token transfers, approvals, and benefit redemptions to observers.
