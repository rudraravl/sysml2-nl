This contract facilitates liquid staking of a base asset, issuing a liquid staking token in return, and custodies the deposited base asset. It maintains a global pool of staked base assets, a record of each user's deposited base asset balance, and their corresponding liquid staking token balance. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw their staked base asset by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim rewards accrued from the staking process. A designated operator can update the staking strategy and adjust parameters. The contract imposes a 0.5% fee on all withdrawals, and the minimum deposit amount for the base asset is 0.01 units. The contract announces all deposits, withdrawals, and reward distributions.
