This contract is a launchpad for autonomous agent-driven token launches, custodying the native chain asset for bonding curve liquidity. It maintains a record for each launched token, including its current supply, the bonding curve parameters, and the total native chain asset deposited into its liquidity pool. Callers can create new token launches, deposit native chain assets into a token's bonding curve, withdraw native chain assets from a token's bonding curve, and trade tokens against their respective bonding curves. A designated operator can pause all trading activity and update the global launch fee. New token launches incur a 0.5% fee on initial native chain asset deposits, and each launch is capped at a maximum of 1,000,000 tokens. The contract announces events for new token launches, deposits, withdrawals, and trades, including the token identifier, the amount of native chain asset, and the amount of tokens involved.
