This contract enables the creation and redemption of leveraged tokens, which represent exposure to an underlying asset's perpetual futures price, and custodies the underlying collateral used to back these tokens. It maintains a record of each user's token balances, the total supply of each leveraged token, and a configuration for each token including its leverage factor and associated fees. Callers can mint new leveraged tokens by depositing collateral, redeem existing leveraged tokens for collateral, and transfer their leveraged tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can update the leverage factor for a specific token and adjust the minting and redemption fees. The maximum leverage factor for any token is capped at 10x, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all minting and redemption operations. The contract announces all token minting, redemption, and transfer events, including the token type, amount, and involved addresses.
