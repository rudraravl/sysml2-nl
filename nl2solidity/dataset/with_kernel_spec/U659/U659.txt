This contract facilitates cross-chain token transfers between a source chain and a destination chain, holding locked tokens on the source chain as collateral for minted tokens on the destination chain. It maintains a record of each user's locked token balance, a total supply of minted tokens on the destination chain, and a configurable transfer fee. Callers can initiate a token transfer from the source chain, claim minted tokens on the destination chain after a successful transfer, and redeem minted tokens on the destination chain to unlock tokens on the source chain. A designated operator can pause and unpause transfers, and update the transfer fee. The transfer fee is fixed at 0.1% of the transfer amount, and a minimum transfer amount of 100 units of the token is enforced. The contract emits an event when a transfer is initiated, when tokens are minted, and when tokens are redeemed.
