This contract facilitates decentralized execution of off-chain tasks, holding no custodied assets directly but managing job registrations and keeper assignments. It maintains a registry of available jobs, each with its associated parameters, and a list of registered keepers along with their staked collateral. Callers can register new jobs, update existing job parameters, add themselves as a keeper by staking collateral, signal their readiness to perform a specific job, and claim rewards for successfully completed jobs. Only the contract owner may pause all job executions or set the base reward for job completion. A keeper must stake a minimum of 100 units of the designated collateral token to register, and a job's reward claim must occur within 24 hours of its successful execution. The contract announces job registrations, job updates, keeper registrations, and successful job completions.
