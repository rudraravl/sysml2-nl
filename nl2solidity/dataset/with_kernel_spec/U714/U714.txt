This contract is a cross-chain asset bridge for wrapping and unwrapping tokens, and it custodies wrapped tokens and their underlying assets. It maintains a mapping of original asset addresses to their wrapped token addresses, a total supply for each wrapped token, and a record of each user's wrapped token balances. Callers can deposit an underlying asset to mint its wrapped equivalent, and they can burn a wrapped token to withdraw the underlying asset. A designated operator can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations, and they can update the mapping of original assets to wrapped tokens. A deposit transaction must exceed a minimum value of 100 units of the underlying asset. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit or withdrawal occurs, including the user's address, the asset involved, and the amount.
