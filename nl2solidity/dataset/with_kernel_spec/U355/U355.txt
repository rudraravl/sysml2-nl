This contract facilitates a decentralized exchange for event-based predictions, holding deposited collateral tokens in escrow. It maintains individual account balances for each collateral token, records of active prediction markets including their outcomes, and a global configuration for fees and market parameters. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit collateral into a market, place orders to buy or sell outcome tokens, cancel open orders, and claim their share of settled market proceeds. A designated operator is privileged to settle markets by providing the definitive outcome and to adjust the market creation fee, which is fixed at 0.1% of the total collateral. Each prediction market has a maximum of two possible outcomes, and orders placed must be for a minimum value of 100 units of the collateral token. The contract announces market creation, order placements, order cancellations, and market settlements to observers.
