This contract manages a stable token system, holding a native platform token and a volatile collateral token. It tracks each user's collateral deposits, minted stable tokens, and any accrued interest, along with global reserves of both collateral types and the current collateralization ratio. Users can deposit collateral, mint stable tokens against their collateral, repay stable tokens, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can adjust the collateralization ratio within a range of 100% to 200% and pause minting in emergencies. Minting new stable tokens requires a minimum collateralization ratio of 150%, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all stable token minting operations. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, mints, and repayments, including the user's address, the token amount, and the transaction type.
