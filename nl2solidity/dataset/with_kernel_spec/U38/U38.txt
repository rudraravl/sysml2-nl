This contract manages a vault for a stablecoin, allocating deposited funds across various yield-generating strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance and the total stablecoin held in the vault, along with configuration parameters for approved strategies and their allocation weights. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the vault, withdraw their deposited stablecoins, and claim any accumulated yield. A designated operator can add new yield strategies, update the allocation weight for existing strategies, and initiate rebalancing of funds across strategies. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 100 stablecoins and a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all withdrawals. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit, withdrawal, or strategy rebalance occurs.
