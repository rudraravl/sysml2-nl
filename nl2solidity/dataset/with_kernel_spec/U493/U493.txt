This contract mints and manages unique, non-fungible digital assets, each backed by a quantity of a specified fungible token which it custodies. It maintains a record for each asset, including its unique identifier, its current owner, the fungible token type and quantity backing it, and a set of immutable characteristics. Callers can mint new assets by depositing the required backing tokens, transfer ownership of their assets to other addresses, and burn their assets to reclaim the underlying backing tokens. A designated operator can set the types and quantities of backing tokens required for new asset minting. The contract enforces a maximum of 10,000 unique assets that can ever be minted. Minting an asset requires a deposit of at least 100 units of the specified backing token. The contract announces asset minting, asset transfers, and asset burning events.
