This contract manages a gaming platform's native token, holding a reserve for game rewards and platform operations. It tracks each player's token balance, their accumulated reward points, and the current reward rate configuration. Callers can deposit tokens into the platform, withdraw their tokens, claim accumulated reward points, and stake tokens to earn additional rewards. Only a designated operator can adjust the reward rate, mint new tokens up to a daily cap, and pause all token transfers. The maximum daily token minting is capped at 10,000 tokens, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all token withdrawals. The contract emits events when tokens are deposited, withdrawn, rewards are claimed, and the reward rate is updated.
