This contract facilitates the buying and selling of non-fungible tokens, holding no token custody itself, but rather facilitating direct transfers between buyers and sellers. It maintains a record of active listings, each containing the listed token, its price, and the seller's address. Callers can list a token for sale, cancel an existing listing, and purchase a listed token. A designated operator can update the platform fee percentage and designate an address to receive collected fees. The platform fee is fixed at 2.5% of the sale price for every successful purchase. A listing can only be canceled by the original seller. The contract announces successful purchases and new listings through emitted events.
