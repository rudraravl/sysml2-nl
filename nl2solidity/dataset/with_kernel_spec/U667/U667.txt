This contract is a yield-bearing deposit vault for a wrapped Bitcoin asset, enabling users to earn rewards and participate in network governance. It maintains individual user deposit balances, accumulated rewards for each user, and a global pool of the wrapped Bitcoin asset. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin, withdraw their deposited wrapped Bitcoin and accrued rewards, and delegate their voting power to another address. A designated operator can update the reward rate and initiate reward distributions. The minimum deposit amount for wrapped Bitcoin is 0.001 BTC. Withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period before funds become transferable. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal requests, withdrawal completions, and reward distributions to observers.
