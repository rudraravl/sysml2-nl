This contract manages a decentralized lending and borrowing platform, holding deposited collateral tokens and borrowed asset tokens. It maintains individual account records for each user, tracking their deposited amounts, borrowed amounts, and accrued interest, alongside global configuration parameters for each supported asset. Callers can deposit tokens as collateral, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can add new supported assets, adjust interest rate models, and pause borrowing for specific assets. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset pair is 75%, and a 0.05% liquidation fee is applied to all liquidated positions. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, borrows, and repayments.
