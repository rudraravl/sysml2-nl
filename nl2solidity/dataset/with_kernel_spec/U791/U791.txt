This contract is a multi-chain asset bridge, enabling users to transfer a specific fungible token between an origin chain and a destination chain, and it custodies the bridged tokens on the origin chain. It maintains a record of each user's locked token balance on the origin chain, a global total supply of bridged tokens on the destination chain, and a mapping of transaction nonces to their completion status. Callers can initiate a transfer on the origin chain, claim a transfer on the destination chain, or cancel an unfulfilled transfer on the origin chain. A designated operator can approve a transfer for claiming on the destination chain and can pause or unpause the bridging functionality. Each transfer initiated on the origin chain must be for a minimum of 100 tokens. A transfer claim on the destination chain must occur within 7 days of its approval by the operator. The contract announces when a transfer is initiated, when a transfer is approved, and when a transfer is claimed.
