This contract manages a basket of diverse digital assets, enabling users to invest in predefined strategies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited assets, their shares in various index baskets, and the current composition and value of each index. Callers can deposit assets into an index, withdraw their proportional share of assets from an index, and redeem their index shares for the underlying assets. A designated operator can create new index baskets, adjust the asset allocation within an existing index, and update the fees associated with each index. The contract charges a 0.5% management fee on the total value of assets within each index, and a 0.1% redemption fee when users withdraw assets. The maximum number of distinct assets in any single index is limited to 10. The contract announces events for deposits, withdrawals, index rebalances, and fee updates.
