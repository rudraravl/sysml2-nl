This contract manages a reward distribution system, holding a pool of reward tokens. It maintains a record of each user's accumulated rewards and a global configuration for reward rates. Callers can register for the reward system, claim their accumulated rewards, and view their current reward balance. A designated operator can update the reward rate, add reward tokens to the pool, and pause the reward distribution system. The reward rate cannot exceed 100 units per action. A minimum of 50 reward tokens must be added to the pool in a single transaction. The contract emits an event when rewards are claimed and when the reward rate is updated.
