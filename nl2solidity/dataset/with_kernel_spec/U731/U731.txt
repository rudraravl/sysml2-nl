This contract manages a decentralized messaging network's user registrations and associated service fees. It maintains a record of each registered user's unique identifier, their associated public key, and their current subscription status, along with a total balance of collected fees. Callers can register a new user, update their public key, renew their subscription by paying a fee, and deregister their account. A designated operator can set the subscription fee and withdraw accumulated fees. The subscription fee is fixed at 0.01 units of the native blockchain currency per renewal period, and a user's subscription is valid for 30 days after a successful renewal. The contract emits an event when a user registers, renews their subscription, or deregisters.
