This contract is a liquid staking contract that custodies deposited Ether and issues liquid staking tokens representing staked Ether. It maintains per-account records of deposited Ether, issued liquid staking tokens, and rewards accrued, along with a global configuration for staking parameters and a pool of staked Ether. Callers can deposit Ether to receive liquid staking tokens, redeem liquid staking tokens for Ether, and claim accrued rewards. A designated operator can initiate staking operations with a minimum of 32 Ether per validator and can adjust the staking reward rate, which is capped at 10% annually. The owner can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations and can upgrade the contract logic. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward distribution events.
