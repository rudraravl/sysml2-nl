This contract is a tokenized fund representing ownership in a portfolio of short-duration government securities, and it custodies no assets directly, instead tracking ownership of an off-chain fund. It maintains per-account balances of fund units, a total supply of fund units, and a daily net asset value per unit. Callers can subscribe to the fund by depositing a stablecoin, redeem fund units for stablecoin, and transfer fund units to other accounts. A designated operator can update the daily net asset value and pause or unpause subscriptions and redemptions. Subscriptions and redemptions are processed daily at 5 PM UTC, and a 0.10% fee is applied to all redemptions. The contract announces subscription events, redemption events, and daily net asset value updates.
