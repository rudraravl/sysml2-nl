This contract is a yield-boosting vault that custodies fungible tokens and liquid staking derivative tokens. It maintains individual user balances for each deposited asset, a global tally of total deposits for each asset, and a configuration for various operational parameters. Callers can deposit fungible tokens or liquid staking derivative tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, claim accumulated rewards, and delegate their voting power to a designated address. A designated operator can update the reward distribution schedule and adjust the fee percentage, while the contract owner can pause all deposits and withdrawals, and can transfer ownership to a new address. A withdrawal fee of 0.5% is applied to all withdrawals, and the maximum number of unique assets that can be deposited into the vault is limited to 10. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, reward claims, and delegation changes.
