This contract manages a token launchpad, holding deposited tokens for sale and collected funds from purchases. It maintains a list of active sales, each with its own sale token, payment token, start time, end time, and individual user allocations, along with a global record of total funds raised per sale. Callers can deposit payment tokens to participate in a sale, claim purchased tokens after a sale concludes, and withdraw their unsold deposited payment tokens if a sale fails to meet its minimum target. The owner can create new sales, set sale parameters, and withdraw collected funds from completed sales. Each sale must have a minimum purchase amount of 100 payment tokens and a maximum individual allocation of 10,000 payment tokens. The contract announces when a new sale is created, when a user participates in a sale, and when a user claims tokens.
