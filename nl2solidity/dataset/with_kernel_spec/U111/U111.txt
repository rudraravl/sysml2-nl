This contract manages a liquid staking pool for a base asset, issuing a liquid staking token representing staked principal and accrued rewards. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, the total amount of the base asset staked, and the current exchange rate between the liquid staking token and the base asset. Callers can deposit the base asset to receive liquid staking tokens, and they can redeem liquid staking tokens to withdraw the base asset. A designated operator can initiate staking of the pooled base asset and unstaking operations, and can update the exchange rate based on external oracle data. The contract imposes a 0.5% fee on all withdrawals, and the minimum deposit amount is 0.01 units of the base asset. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, and exchange rate updates.
