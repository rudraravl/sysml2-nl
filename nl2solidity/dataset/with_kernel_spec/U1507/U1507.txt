This contract facilitates borrowing ERC-20 tokens against concentrated liquidity positions, holding deposited ERC-20 tokens and non-fungible tokens representing concentrated liquidity positions as collateral. It maintains records of each user's borrowed amount, deposited collateral positions, and the configuration for each borrowable ERC-20 token, including interest rates and collateral factors. Callers can deposit non-fungible token collateral, borrow ERC-20 tokens, repay borrowed amounts, and withdraw their collateral once all loans are repaid. A designated operator can update interest rate models and collateral factors for specific ERC-20 tokens, and the owner can pause all borrowing and lending activity. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any collateral position is 70%, and a liquidation penalty of 5% is applied to the borrower's outstanding debt during liquidation. The contract announces events for deposits, borrows, repayments, liquidations, and configuration changes.
