This contract provides an isolated lending market for various digital assets, holding deposited collateral and borrowed assets. It maintains per-account records of deposits, borrows, and collateral ratios, along with global pools for each asset and configuration parameters like interest rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can deposit assets as collateral, borrow available assets against their collateral, repay outstanding borrows, withdraw excess collateral, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can add new lendable assets, adjust interest rate models, and set liquidation parameters. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any asset is 75%, and a 5% liquidation penalty is applied to the borrower's position during liquidation. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, withdrawals, and liquidations, including the affected account, asset, and amount.
