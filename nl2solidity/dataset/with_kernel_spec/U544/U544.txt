This contract facilitates the creation and management of synthetic assets, holding collateral tokens as backing. It maintains individual user positions tracking deposited collateral, minted synthetic tokens, and accrued stability fees, alongside global configuration for each synthetic asset type. Callers can deposit collateral, mint synthetic tokens against their collateral, repay synthetic tokens to reduce their debt, withdraw excess collateral, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can pause minting and redemptions, and set stability fees and liquidation thresholds. The minimum collateralization ratio for any synthetic asset is 150%, and a stability fee of 0.5% per year is levied on all outstanding synthetic debt. The contract emits events for all deposits, mints, repayments, withdrawals, and liquidations, including the affected user, asset type, and amount.
