This contract manages structured yield positions, holding user-deposited base tokens and their corresponding yield-bearing receipt tokens. It maintains individual user balances for deposited base tokens and accrued yield, along with a global configuration for strategy parameters and approved external venues. Callers can deposit base tokens, initiate a withdrawal of their principal and accrued yield, and claim their share of realized profits. A designated operator can deploy deposited funds into approved external venues, manage the delta-neutral strategy, and rebalance positions. The contract owner can set the maximum deposit amount to 100,000 base tokens per user, adjust the operator's permissions, and update the list of approved external venues. A 0.5% management fee is deducted from all realized profits before distribution. All withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal requests, and successful profit distributions.
