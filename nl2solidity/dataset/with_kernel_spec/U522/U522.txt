This contract is for managing a treasury of a governance token and facilitating proposal execution. It maintains a record of the total governance token supply, the current voting power allocated to each delegate, and a queue of pending proposals with their associated parameters and state. Callers can delegate their voting power to another address, undelegate their voting power, and submit new proposals for consideration. A designated operator can cancel a pending proposal, queue an approved proposal for execution, and execute a queued proposal after a time lock. The contract imposes a minimum delegation amount of 100 governance tokens. Executing a queued proposal requires a 48-hour time lock after it has been queued. The contract announces when voting power is delegated, when voting power is undelegated, when a new proposal is submitted, when a proposal is canceled, when a proposal is queued, and when a proposal is executed.
