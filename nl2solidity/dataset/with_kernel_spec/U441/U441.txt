This contract is a vault for interest-bearing tokens, specifically designed to hold and manage deposit receipts representing regulated non-fungible tokens (NFTs) backed by real-world assets. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount of the interest-bearing token, the total supply of the vault's own fungible token, and a configuration for the annual interest rate. Callers can deposit interest-bearing tokens to receive vault tokens, withdraw their deposited interest-bearing tokens by burning vault tokens, and redeem their vault tokens for the underlying interest-bearing tokens plus accrued interest. A designated operator can adjust the annual interest rate, which is initially set at 5.00% and can be changed by a maximum of 0.50% in any 24-hour period. The contract emits an event when a deposit or withdrawal occurs, and when the interest rate is updated.
