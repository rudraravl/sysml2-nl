This contract manages a decentralized prediction game, holding user deposits and prize funds in an escrow. It maintains a record of each user's active predictions, the current round's prediction pool, and a configurable fee percentage. Callers can deposit funds to make predictions, claim winnings from past rounds, and withdraw any unspent balance. A designated operator can initiate new prediction rounds and finalize existing ones, distributing prizes. The minimum prediction amount is 0.01 ether, and a 5% fee is applied to all winning payouts. The contract announces the start of new prediction rounds and the results of finalized rounds, including winning numbers and prize distributions.
