This contract manages a token distribution for a new game release, holding a fixed supply of game tokens. It maintains each player's claimable token balance and a global total of tokens distributed. Callers can claim their allocated tokens and transfer their tokens to other players. The contract owner can set the initial allocation for players and pause all token transfers. A maximum of 1,000,000 tokens will ever be distributed, and each player's initial allocation cannot exceed 100,000 tokens. The contract announces when tokens are claimed and when transfers occur.
