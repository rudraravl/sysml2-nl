This contract serves as a diversified investment vehicle, holding various approved ERC-20 tokens in a curated portfolio. It maintains a record of each user's deposited assets, their proportional share of the total portfolio, and the current composition of the portfolio. Callers can deposit ERC-20 tokens, withdraw their share of the underlying assets, and redeem their portfolio tokens for the underlying assets. A designated operator can adjust the portfolio's asset allocation and add or remove approved ERC-20 tokens, while the owner can set the operator and pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. A deposit fee of 0.5% is applied to all incoming assets, and the portfolio must always contain at least 3 distinct ERC-20 tokens. The contract announces all deposits, withdrawals, and portfolio rebalances to observers.
