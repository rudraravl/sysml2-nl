This contract facilitates the funding and launch of scientific research projects, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing them to projects. It maintains a registry of approved projects, each with a funding goal and a deadline, and tracks individual user contributions to each project. Callers can deposit stablecoins into a project, claim their pro-rata share of a successfully funded project's tokens, and withdraw their stablecoin contributions if a project fails to meet its goal. Only a designated administrator can approve new projects, set their funding goals and deadlines, and mark projects as successfully funded or failed. A project must raise at least 10,000 stablecoins to be considered successful, and a 5% platform fee is deducted from all successful project funding distributions. The contract announces when a project is approved, when a contribution is made, and when a project's funding status changes.
