This liquid staking contract mints a synthetic token representing staked assets on an external ledger, and custodies the total supply of this synthetic token. It maintains a record of each user's staked balance, the total amount of assets locked on the external ledger, and a global fee rate. Callers can deposit native assets to receive synthetic tokens, redeem synthetic tokens to initiate an unstaking process on the external ledger, and claim their unstaked native assets once available. A designated operator can update the total amount of assets locked on the external ledger, reflecting changes from the external staking mechanism. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 100 native assets and charges a 0.5% fee on all synthetic token redemptions. The contract announces events for deposits, redemptions, and the completion of unstaking claims.
