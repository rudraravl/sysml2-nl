This contract manages the lifecycle and governance of a decentralized application, holding the application's native governance tokens. It maintains a record of each participant's staked token balance, their voting power, and the current state of active and pending governance proposals, along with a configuration for proposal submission and voting parameters. Callers can stake their tokens to gain voting power, unstake their tokens, submit new governance proposals, vote on active proposals, and execute proposals that have passed. A designated administrator can update core governance parameters like the minimum staking amount or proposal submission fee. Staking requires a minimum of 100 tokens, and unstaking incurs a 2% fee on the unstaked amount. The contract emits events for every token stake, unstake, proposal submission, vote cast, and successful proposal execution.
