This contract facilitates peer-to-peer price protection policies for various crypto assets, holding deposited collateral in an escrow. It maintains individual policy records including asset type, strike price, expiration, and collateral amount, along with a global registry of active policies and a configurable fee structure. Callers can create new policies by specifying an asset, strike price, and duration, deposit collateral to fund a policy, claim protection if the asset price falls below the strike price at expiration, and withdraw their collateral if the policy expires without a claim. An owner role can set the premium fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of the policy's value, and designate allowed collateral tokens. Policies have a minimum duration of 24 hours and a maximum duration of 90 days. The contract emits events when a new policy is created, collateral is deposited, a claim is successfully processed, or collateral is withdrawn.
