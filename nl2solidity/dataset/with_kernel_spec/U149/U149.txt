This contract facilitates interest-free, self-repaying loans by accepting yield-generating collateral and issuing synthetic tokens representing future yield. It maintains individual loan positions tracking collateral deposited, synthetic tokens minted, and accrued yield, alongside global configuration parameters like the collateralization ratio and supported collateral types. Callers can deposit collateral, mint synthetic tokens against their collateral, repay synthetic tokens, and withdraw their collateral once the loan is fully repaid. A designated operator can add new supported collateral types and adjust the collateralization ratio, which must always be between 120% and 200%. The contract charges a 0.5% fee on all minted synthetic tokens, payable to a treasury address. The contract emits events for all loan creations, repayments, and collateral withdrawals, including the borrower's address, the amount of collateral, and the amount of synthetic tokens involved.
