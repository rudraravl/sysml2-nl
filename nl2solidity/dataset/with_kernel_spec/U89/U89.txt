This contract facilitates peer-to-pool lending and borrowing, holding deposited collateral and borrowed assets. It maintains per-user records of deposited collateral, borrowed amounts, and accrued interest, alongside global pools for each supported asset and configuration parameters like interest rates and liquidation thresholds. Callers can deposit collateral, borrow assets against their collateral, repay borrowed amounts, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can add new supported assets, adjust interest rate models, and initiate liquidations for undercollateralized positions. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrow is 75%, and a 0.1% origination fee is applied to all new borrows. The contract emits events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, including the user, asset, and amount.
