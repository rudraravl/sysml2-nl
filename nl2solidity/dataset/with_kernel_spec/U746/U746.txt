This contract facilitates timed auctions for unique digital assets, holding the auctioned assets and the highest bid amount in escrow. It maintains a record for each ongoing auction including the asset being auctioned, its seller, the current highest bidder, the highest bid amount, and the auction's end time. Callers can initiate an auction, place a bid on an active auction, and claim an auctioned asset if they are the winning bidder after the auction concludes. The owner can pause all auctions and set a platform fee percentage. Each auction has a minimum bid increment of 1% of the current highest bid, and a platform fee of 2.5% is deducted from the final sale price of each successful auction. The contract announces when an auction starts, when a new highest bid is placed, and when an auction concludes with a winner.
