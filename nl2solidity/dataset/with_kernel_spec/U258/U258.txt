This contract manages a decentralized liquidity provisioning system for a stablecoin, holding user-deposited stablecoins and their synthetic equivalents. It maintains a record of each user's deposited stablecoin balance, their synthetic stablecoin balance, and a global configuration for fees and operational parameters. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw stablecoins, convert stablecoins to synthetic stablecoins, and convert synthetic stablecoins back to stablecoins. Only a designated operator can adjust the conversion fee, which is capped at 0.5%, and only the owner can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The contract announces events for all deposits, withdrawals, and conversions, including the amount and the involved addresses.
