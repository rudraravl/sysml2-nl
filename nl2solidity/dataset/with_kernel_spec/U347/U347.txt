This contract manages a staking pool for a gaming ecosystem's primary token, holding deposited tokens and distributing rewards. It maintains individual records of each staker's deposited amount, their accumulated rewards, and their chosen lock-up period. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their principal and earned rewards, claim only their earned rewards, and extend their lock-up period. Only a designated operator can update the reward rate and distribute new rewards to the pool. A staker's deposit is locked for a minimum of 90 days, and withdrawing before the lock-up period expires incurs a 10% penalty on the principal. The contract announces when new rewards are distributed and when a staker's lock-up period changes.
