This contract facilitates decentralized perpetual futures trading, holding user collateral in a designated base asset. It maintains individual user account balances, open positions including margin and unrealized profit/loss, and global pool liquidity and funding rates for each trading pair. Callers can open new positions, close existing positions, add or remove margin from their positions, and deposit or withdraw collateral. A designated operator can update oracle prices, adjust funding rates, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage allowed is 100x, and a 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing actions. The contract emits events for all position changes, collateral deposits and withdrawals, and liquidations.
