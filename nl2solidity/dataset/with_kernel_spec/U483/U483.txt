This contract facilitates the tokenized sale of real-world collectible cards, holding the digital representations of these cards as non-fungible tokens. It maintains a registry of each card's unique identifier, its current owner, and its associated physical location for redemption, alongside a global configuration for sale parameters. Callers can list a card for sale, purchase an available card, or initiate a redemption process for a card they own. A designated administrator can add new cards to the registry, update the physical location of a card, or adjust the global sale fee. The contract imposes a 2% fee on all successful card sales, payable in the purchase currency, and a maximum of 1000 unique cards can be registered at any given time. The contract emits an event upon a card being listed for sale, purchased, or redeemed.
