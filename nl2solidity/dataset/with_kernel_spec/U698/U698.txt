This contract is a yield aggregator for a governance-mined token, holding staked governance tokens and their corresponding yield-bearing wrapper tokens. It maintains a global fee rate, a record of each user's deposited governance tokens, and their accumulated claimable yield. Callers can deposit governance tokens, withdraw their deposited governance tokens, claim accumulated yield, and stake their wrapper tokens into the system. Only a designated operator can update the global fee rate, which is capped at 10% of claimed yield, and only the owner can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. A minimum deposit of 100 governance tokens is enforced for new deposits. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, including the user's address and the amounts involved.
