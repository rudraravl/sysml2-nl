This contract manages a fractional reserve lending pool, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing yield-bearing tokens. It maintains individual account balances for deposited stablecoins, a global reserve ratio, and a list of approved yield-generating strategies. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their deposited stablecoins, and claim accrued yield. A designated operator can add new yield-generating strategies, adjust the target reserve ratio, and pause all deposits or withdrawals. The contract enforces a minimum reserve ratio of 10% of total deposits and charges a 0.5% fee on all withdrawals, deducted from the withdrawn amount. It announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and changes to the reserve ratio.
