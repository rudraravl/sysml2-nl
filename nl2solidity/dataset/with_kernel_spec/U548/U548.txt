This contract manages a basket of diverse digital assets, functioning as a decentralized exchange-traded product. It maintains the current allocation of each asset within the basket, the total supply of basket tokens, and the individual balances of basket tokens held by each user. Callers can deposit supported assets to mint new basket tokens, withdraw underlying assets by burning their basket tokens, and rebalance the asset allocations. A designated operator can adjust the target allocation for each asset and add or remove supported assets from the basket. The contract enforces a minimum deposit amount of 0.01 basket tokens and charges a 0.5% fee on all withdrawals, which is distributed to basket token holders. The contract announces when new basket tokens are minted, when basket tokens are burned, and when asset allocations are updated.
