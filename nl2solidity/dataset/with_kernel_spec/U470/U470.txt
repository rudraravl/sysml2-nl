This contract manages a no-loss lottery, holding deposited funds in an interest-bearing vault. It maintains a record of each participant's current deposit amount and the total pooled funds, along with a configurable lottery duration. Callers can deposit funds into the current lottery round, withdraw their deposited funds at any time, and claim their prize if they are the winner of a completed round. A designated operator can start new lottery rounds, select the winner of a completed round, and update the interest-bearing vault address. Each lottery round must last exactly 7 days, and the operator may only select a winner after the round duration has elapsed. The contract announces the start of new lottery rounds, the winner of each round, and the amount of any prize awarded.
