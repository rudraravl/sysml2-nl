This contract is a vault for non-fungible tokens representing graded collectibles, holding them securely while facilitating their trade. It maintains a record of each vaulted non-fungible token's unique identifier, its current owner, and its listed sale price, alongside a global fee percentage for all transactions. Callers can deposit non-fungible tokens they own into the vault, list their vaulted non-fungible tokens for sale at a specified price, purchase listed non-fungible tokens from other users, and withdraw non-fungible tokens they own from the vault. Only a designated operator can set the global transaction fee, which is capped at a maximum of 5%. Each purchase transaction incurs the global transaction fee, which is deducted from the sale price and transferred to a designated treasury address. The contract announces events for non-fungible token deposits, listings, purchases, and withdrawals, including the non-fungible token's identifier, the involved parties, and the transaction amount.
