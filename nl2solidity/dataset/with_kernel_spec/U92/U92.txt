This contract manages a decentralized prediction market, holding collateral tokens deposited by users. It maintains records of open markets, individual user positions within each market, and the total collateral locked per market. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit collateral into existing markets, trade outcome tokens, and withdraw their share of settled markets. A designated operator can resolve markets, determining the winning outcome, and can adjust the market creation fee. Market creation requires a minimum collateral deposit of 100 units of the designated collateral token, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all outcome token trades, directed to the protocol treasury. The contract announces market creation, market resolution, and all outcome token trades.
