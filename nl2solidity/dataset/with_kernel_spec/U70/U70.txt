This contract facilitates a peer-to-peer coverage market, holding deposited collateral for active coverage policies. It maintains a record of each user's deposited collateral, the active coverage policies they hold, and the policies they have underwritten, along with a global configuration for coverage terms and fees. Callers can deposit collateral, request new coverage, underwrite existing coverage requests, and claim against their active coverage policies. A designated operator can adjust the global coverage terms, including the maximum coverage duration and the claim assessment period, and can also pause or unpause the entire contract. The maximum coverage duration for any policy is 365 days, and a flat 0.5% fee is applied to all successful coverage claims. The contract emits an event upon the creation of a new coverage policy, the successful underwriting of a policy, and the approval or rejection of a claim.
