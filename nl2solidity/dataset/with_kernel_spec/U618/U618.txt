This contract is a risk coverage provider, holding deposited collateral tokens to back against potential smart contract exploits. It maintains individual coverage policies for each user, recording their covered protocols, coverage amount, and active period, alongside a global pool of staked collateral and a configuration for risk parameters. Callers can deposit collateral, purchase new coverage policies, extend existing policies, claim against an active policy, and withdraw their unstaked collateral. The owner can adjust coverage pricing, add or remove supported protocols, and pause all coverage purchases. A minimum coverage purchase is 100 units of the collateral token, and claims are subject to a 7-day review period before payout. The contract announces new coverage purchases, policy expirations, and successful claim payouts.
