This contract manages a staking pool for a specific fungible token, enabling users to deposit and earn rewards. It maintains individual user deposit balances, a total staked amount for the pool, and a configurable reward rate. Callers can deposit tokens into the pool, withdraw their staked tokens, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can update the reward rate and pause/unpause deposits and withdrawals. The maximum reward rate is 10% per day, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, which is sent to a treasury address. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, reward claims, and reward rate updates.
