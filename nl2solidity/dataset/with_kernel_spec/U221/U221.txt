This contract facilitates decentralized ETH staking by pooling user deposits and pairing them with operator-supplied ETH to create new validators. It maintains a record of each user's deposited ETH, the total pooled ETH, and the current exchange rate between deposited ETH and the liquid staking token, along with a list of registered node operators and their associated validator states. Callers can deposit ETH to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw their deposited ETH and accrued rewards by burning liquid staking tokens, and node operators can register their nodes, deposit their required ETH, and claim rewards from their validators. Only a designated owner can set the protocol fee, pause deposits, or update the exchange rate oracle address. A minimum deposit of 0.01 ETH is required for users, and node operators must supply exactly 16 ETH per validator. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, new validator creations, and changes to the protocol fee.
