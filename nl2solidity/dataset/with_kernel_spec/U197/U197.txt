This contract manages a decentralized lending pool for a single fungible token, holding deposited tokens and issuing interest-bearing receipt tokens. It maintains individual balances for each user's deposits and borrowed amounts, the total supply of receipt tokens, and a global interest rate model configuration. Callers can deposit tokens to receive receipt tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens by burning receipt tokens, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay borrowed tokens, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can update the interest rate model parameters and pause borrowing and depositing in emergencies. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any borrow is 75% of the collateral's value, and a 5% liquidation penalty is applied to the borrower's collateral value during liquidation events. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, including the affected user, token amounts, and new balances.
