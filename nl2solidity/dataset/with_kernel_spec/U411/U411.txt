This contract manages a synthetic asset that tracks the price of a target asset, holding a reserve of a base stablecoin to back the synthetic asset. It maintains an account balance for each user of the synthetic asset, a total supply of the synthetic asset, and a global reserve of the base stablecoin. Callers can mint new synthetic assets by depositing base stablecoins, burn synthetic assets to redeem base stablecoins, and transfer synthetic assets between accounts. A designated operator can adjust the minting and burning fees, and only the owner can pause all operations. The minting fee is fixed at 0.1% of the deposited base stablecoins, and the burning fee is 0.1% of the redeemed base stablecoins. The contract emits an event whenever synthetic assets are minted, burned, or transferred.
