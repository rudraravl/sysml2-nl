This contract manages a pool of stablecoin tokens to facilitate instant, on-chain settlements for off-chain digital asset spending. It maintains a total stablecoin balance, a record of each user's available spending limit, and a configuration for settlement fees. Callers can deposit stablecoins to increase their spending limit, initiate a spending settlement request, and withdraw their available balance. A designated operator can approve or reject settlement requests, and only the owner can update the settlement fee percentage or transfer ownership. Each settlement request incurs a 0.5% fee on the settled amount, and a user's total spending limit cannot exceed 10,000 stablecoins. The contract announces successful deposits, approved settlements, and rejected settlements.
