This contract manages a prediction market where users stake an ERC-20 token on the outcome of real-world events. It maintains a record of each user's staked amount, their chosen outcome for each event, and the total staked amount for each outcome. Callers can create a new prediction event, stake tokens on an outcome, withdraw their stake if an event is cancelled, and claim their winnings after an event concludes. Only a designated operator can create new prediction events, mark an event as cancelled, or declare the winning outcome of an event. Each prediction event must have at least two possible outcomes and a maximum of five. A 5% fee is deducted from all winnings before distribution. The contract announces when a new prediction event is created, when a user stakes tokens, and when an event's outcome is declared.
