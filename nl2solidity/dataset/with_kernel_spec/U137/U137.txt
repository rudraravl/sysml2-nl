This liquid staking contract allows users to deposit an underlying asset and receive a liquid staking token in return, representing their staked position and accruing rewards. It maintains individual user deposit balances, the total amount of underlying asset staked, and a configurable exchange rate between the underlying asset and the liquid staking token. Callers can deposit the underlying asset, withdraw their staked underlying asset, and redeem their liquid staking tokens for the underlying asset. A designated operator can update the exchange rate and set the staking fee. The staking fee is fixed at 10% of generated rewards. Withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period after a withdrawal request is initiated. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal requests, and exchange rate updates.
