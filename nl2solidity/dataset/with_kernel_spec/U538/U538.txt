This contract manages a vault for automated options strategies, holding deposited underlying tokens and minted options tokens. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance, the total assets under management, and configuration parameters for the current strategy. Callers can deposit underlying tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and initiate a strategy rollover. A designated operator can initiate new strategy auctions and settle expired options. The owner can set the fee for strategy rollovers, which is capped at 0.5% of the gross premium, and can update the address of the external options exchange. Withdrawals are subject to a 7-day delay after the request is made. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and strategy rollover completions.
