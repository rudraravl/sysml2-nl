This contract manages a yield-bearing stablecoin, holding a reserve of an approved underlying stablecoin to back its issuance. It maintains a total supply of the issued stablecoin, individual balances for each user, and a global reserve balance. Callers can deposit the underlying stablecoin to mint the yield-bearing stablecoin, transfer their stablecoin balances to other users, and redeem the yield-bearing stablecoin for the underlying stablecoin. A designated operator can adjust the annual yield rate and add or remove approved underlying stablecoin types. The contract enforces a minimum deposit of 100 units of the underlying stablecoin and charges a 0.1% redemption fee on all redemptions. The contract announces all minting, transfer, and redemption events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
