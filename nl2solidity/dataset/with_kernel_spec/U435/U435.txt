This liquid staking contract allows users to deposit a base asset and receive a liquid staking token representing their staked position, while also holding the deposited base assets in escrow. The contract maintains a record of each user's deposited base asset balance, their corresponding liquid staking token balance, and a global pool of staked base assets, alongside configuration parameters like the staking fee and the reward rate. Callers can deposit base assets to mint liquid staking tokens, withdraw their staked base assets by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim accumulated rewards. The owner can set the staking fee, adjust the reward rate, and designate an operator who can pause deposits and withdrawals in emergencies. A staking fee of 5% is applied to all rewards distributed, and a minimum deposit of 0.01 base assets is required for staking. The contract announces events for successful deposits, withdrawals, reward claims, and changes to the staking fee or reward rate.
