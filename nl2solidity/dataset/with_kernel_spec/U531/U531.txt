This contract manages a social token system, holding a reserve of the system's native token. It tracks each user's token balance, their share of the total supply, and a global fee percentage. Callers can create new social tokens, buy and sell existing social tokens, and claim their accumulated rewards. Only a designated operator can adjust the global fee percentage, which is capped at 5%, and pause or unpause trading for all social tokens. A 0.5% fee is applied to all buy and sell operations, distributed proportionally to existing social token holders. The contract emits an event whenever a new social token is created, a buy or sell operation occurs, or the global fee percentage is updated.
