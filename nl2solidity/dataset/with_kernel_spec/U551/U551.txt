This contract manages a liquid staking pool for a proof-of-stake blockchain, holding deposited native tokens and issuing corresponding liquid staking tokens. It maintains a record of each user's staked balance and the total amount of native tokens held in the pool, along with a global exchange rate between native tokens and liquid staking tokens. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw their native tokens by burning liquid staking tokens, and claim staking rewards. A designated operator can update the exchange rate based on validator performance and rebalance the staked assets among validators. A 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, and the minimum deposit amount is 0.1 native tokens. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward distribution events.
