This contract manages a collection of non-fungible tokens (NFTs), each representing a share of staked Ether, and custodies the underlying staked Ether. It maintains a record for each NFT of its unique identifier, its current owner, and the amount of staked Ether it represents, along with a global total of all staked Ether held. Callers can mint new NFTs by depositing staked Ether, transfer ownership of their NFTs, and burn their NFTs to redeem the underlying staked Ether. A designated operator can pause or unpause all minting and redemption operations. The minimum amount of staked Ether required to mint a new NFT is 0.1 Ether. The contract emits an event whenever an NFT is minted, transferred, or burned, including the NFT's identifier and the relevant addresses.
