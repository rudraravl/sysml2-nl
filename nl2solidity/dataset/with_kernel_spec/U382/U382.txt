This contract manages a decentralized algorithmic stablecoin, holding collateral in a basket of yield-bearing assets. It maintains individual user balances of the stablecoin, a global reserve pool of collateral, and a configuration for various protocol parameters. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing approved collateral, redeem stablecoins for their underlying collateral, and stake stablecoins to earn yield. A designated operator can adjust the collateral asset whitelist and update oracle prices, while the owner can set global fees and pause operations. The redemption fee is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed amount, and the maximum minting capacity is capped at 100,000,000 stablecoins. The contract announces all minting, redemption, and staking events, including the amounts and involved addresses, to observers.
