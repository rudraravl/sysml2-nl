This contract manages the creation, ownership, and sale of unique digital artworks, holding the artwork tokens themselves. It maintains a record of each artwork's creator, current owner, and its sale status, along with a global platform fee percentage. Callers can create new artworks, transfer artwork ownership, list artworks for sale at a fixed price, bid on listed artworks, accept bids, and withdraw their bids. Only a designated platform operator can mint new artwork tokens and set the platform fee, which is capped at 15%. When an artwork is sold, the platform collects a 5% fee from the sale price. The contract announces artwork creation, ownership transfers, and successful sales to observers.
