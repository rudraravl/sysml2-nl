This contract manages a decentralized identity credentialing system, custodying no assets directly but tracking user progress and achievements. It maintains per-account records of earned credentials, associated metadata, and a global configuration for credential definitions. Callers can claim new credentials, view their credential history, and transfer ownership of their identity. The owner can define new credential types, update existing credential metadata, and pause or unpause the entire system. A maximum of 10,000 unique credential types can be defined, and a fee of 0.01 ether is charged for claiming each credential. The contract announces events for credential claimed, credential definition updated, and system paused/unpaused.
