This contract manages a vault for depositing and withdrawing a single designated stablecoin, which it then allocates to an external yield-generating strategy. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance and the total amount of stablecoin currently held within the vault. Users can deposit stablecoins into the vault and withdraw their share of the vault's assets, while a designated operator can initiate rebalancing of the vault's assets with the external strategy and adjust the fee charged on withdrawals. Only the owner can change the designated operator or upgrade the contract. A 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, and withdrawals are subject to a 24-hour delay after initiation. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit or withdrawal occurs, and when the fee percentage is updated.
