This contract manages a pool of tokenized real-world assets, specifically private credit investments, allowing investors to deposit stablecoins and receive yield-bearing receipts. It maintains individual investor balances of deposited stablecoins and issued receipt tokens, along with the total value of assets under management and a configuration for investment strategies. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the pool, withdraw their stablecoin principal and accrued yield, and redeem their receipt tokens for the underlying assets when investments mature. A designated operator can approve new investment opportunities, initiate asset purchases with deposited stablecoins, and distribute returns from matured investments. A 0.5% management fee is applied annually to the total assets under management, and a minimum deposit amount of 1000 stablecoins is enforced for all new deposits. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and the completion of investment distributions.
