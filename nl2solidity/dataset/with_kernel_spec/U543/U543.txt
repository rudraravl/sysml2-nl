This contract facilitates the launch of new tokens, holding deposited launch tokens and raised capital in a pool. It maintains records of each participant's deposited launch tokens, their allocated share of the raised capital, and the current launch phase. Callers can deposit launch tokens, commit funds to a launch, claim their allocated new tokens after a successful launch, and withdraw their unspent committed funds if a launch fails. A designated operator can initiate a new launch, set the launch parameters including the target raise amount and the launch token to new token ratio, advance the launch through distinct phases (deposit, commitment, distribution), and finalize or cancel a launch. A launch must raise at least 50% of its target capital to be considered successful, and a 2% fee on all raised capital is automatically transferred to a designated treasury address upon successful launch finalization. The contract announces events for new launch initiations, participant deposits, fund commitments, and successful or failed launch finalizations.
