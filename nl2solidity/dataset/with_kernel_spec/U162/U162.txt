This contract facilitates scheduled, recurring token transfers, acting as an escrow for the tokens designated for future payments. It maintains individual payment schedules for each sender, including the recipient, amount, frequency, and start time, along with a total balance of tokens held in escrow. Callers can create a new payment schedule, deposit tokens into an existing schedule, withdraw available tokens from a schedule if the recipient has not claimed them, and cancel their own active schedules. A designated operator can pause all transfers in an emergency and update the fee percentage. Each new payment schedule incurs a one-time setup fee of 0.05 ether, and a maximum of 100 active schedules are permitted per sender. The contract announces when a new schedule is created, when tokens are deposited, when a payment is executed, and when a schedule is cancelled.
