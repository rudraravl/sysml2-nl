This contract is a decentralized exchange for swapping stable value tokens, holding reserves of two distinct stablecoins. It maintains a record of each token's current balance within the pool and a configurable swap fee. Callers can deposit pairs of stablecoins to provide liquidity, withdraw their share of deposited tokens, and swap one stablecoin for another. A designated owner can adjust the swap fee, which is initially set to 0.05%, and can pause all swapping operations. The contract will always maintain a minimum of 1000 units of each stablecoin in its reserves. It announces all deposit, withdrawal, and swap events, including the tokens involved and the amounts transferred.
