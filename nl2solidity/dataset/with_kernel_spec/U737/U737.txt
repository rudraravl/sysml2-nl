This contract manages prediction pools for event outcomes, holding US dollar stablecoins deposited by participants. It maintains a record of each participant's deposits and withdrawals, the current state of each prediction pool including its total staked amount and outcome selections, and global configuration parameters such as fees. Callers can create new prediction pools, join an existing pool by staking stablecoins on a specific outcome, withdraw their staked amount if the pool is still open, and claim their winnings after a pool's outcome has been determined. Only a designated operator can finalize a pool's outcome and set the fee percentage. A pool must have at least 100 US dollar stablecoins staked before it can be finalized, and the operator fee is fixed at 2% of the total winnings. The contract announces when a new pool is created, when a participant joins a pool, when a participant withdraws from a pool, and when a pool's outcome is finalized.
