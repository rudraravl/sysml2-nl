This contract manages a decentralized exchange for tokenized real-world assets, holding various asset tokens and a native governance token. It maintains a registry of approved asset tokens, per-user balances for each token, and configurable trading pair parameters including fees and liquidity. Callers can deposit and withdraw approved asset tokens, create new trading pairs, provide and remove liquidity, and swap tokens within established pairs. A designated operator can approve new asset tokens, adjust trading pair fees within a range of 0.05% to 0.50%, and pause all trading operations. The contract enforces a maximum of 100 distinct trading pairs and requires that all new trading pairs are created with an initial liquidity provision of at least 100 units of each asset in the pair. It announces events for deposits, withdrawals, liquidity additions, liquidity removals, and all token swaps, including the tokens involved and the quantities exchanged.
