This contract manages a social platform's native token, facilitating user engagement and rewarding participation. It maintains individual user balances, a global treasury balance, and a configurable fee structure. Callers can deposit tokens into the platform, transfer tokens to other users, stake tokens to earn rewards, and withdraw their unstaked tokens. Only a designated operator can set the staking reward rate and adjust the platform's operational fees. A 5% fee is applied to all token transfers between users, and the staking reward rate is capped at 0.1% per day. The contract announces successful deposits, transfers, stakes, and withdrawals, along with any fee deductions.
