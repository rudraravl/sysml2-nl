This contract is a non-custodial lending protocol for prediction market shares, facilitating leveraged positions and yield generation. It custodies USDC supplied by lenders and prediction market shares deposited as collateral by borrowers. The contract maintains a global pool of available USDC, individual borrower positions tracking collateral, borrowed amount, and accrued interest, and a global configuration for interest rates and liquidation parameters. Callers can supply USDC to the lending pool, withdraw their supplied USDC and accrued interest, deposit prediction market shares as collateral, borrow USDC against their collateral, repay borrowed USDC, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can adjust interest rate models and liquidation thresholds, and pause borrowing or lending in emergencies. The maximum loan-to-value ratio for any position is 80%, and a 0.5% liquidation fee is applied to the liquidator. The contract emits events for all deposits, withdrawals, borrows, repayments, and liquidations, including the affected accounts and amounts.
