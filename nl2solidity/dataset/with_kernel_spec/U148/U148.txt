This contract manages a pool of stablecoin deposits, allowing users to invest in tokenized real-world assets. It maintains individual user balances of deposited stablecoins and records of their proportional shares in various asset portfolios, along with a global configuration for investment strategies. Callers can deposit stablecoins into the pool, redeem their stablecoin balance, and initiate an investment into a specific asset portfolio which converts their stablecoin balance into portfolio shares. Only a designated operator can approve new asset portfolios for investment and adjust the stablecoin deposit fee, which is capped at 0.5% of the deposited amount. The contract emits an event whenever a deposit is made, an investment is executed, or a redemption occurs, including the user's identity and the amount involved.
