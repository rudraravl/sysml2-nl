This contract manages a decentralized exchange's concentrated liquidity positions, holding deposited tokens for users. It maintains records of each user's liquidity positions, including the token pair, price range, and deposited amounts, along with global configuration parameters like the protocol fee and the designated operator address. Callers can deposit tokens to create new liquidity positions, increase existing positions, decrease positions, or withdraw their tokens entirely. The designated operator can adjust the protocol fee, which is capped at 0.5% of swapped volume, and can update the address of an external price oracle. All new liquidity positions must specify a price range where the upper bound is at least 1.1 times the lower bound. The contract announces events for all deposits, withdrawals, and fee changes.
