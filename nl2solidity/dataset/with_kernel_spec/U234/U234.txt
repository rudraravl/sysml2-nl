This contract manages a treasury of Ether and a native protocol token, facilitating the accumulation of Ether through token sales. It maintains a total supply of the native token, individual account balances for the native token, and the current price of the native token in Ether. Callers can purchase the native token by sending Ether, and they can sell the native token to receive Ether back. A designated operator can adjust the native token's price, and can withdraw accumulated Ether from the treasury. The contract enforces a minimum purchase amount of 0.01 Ether. All sales of the native token incur a 0.5% fee, deducted from the Ether returned to the seller. The contract emits events for every token purchase, token sale, and Ether withdrawal from the treasury.
