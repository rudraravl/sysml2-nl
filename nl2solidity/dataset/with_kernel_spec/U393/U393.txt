This contract serves as a simple, non-custodial smart wallet for individual users, allowing them to manage their own digital assets on the blockchain. It maintains a record of each user's owned tokens and their associated allowances for other contracts. Callers can deposit supported tokens, transfer tokens to other addresses, approve spending limits for other contracts, and revoke existing approvals. Only the designated owner of the contract can add new supported token addresses to the whitelist. A user's total token deposits cannot exceed 100 ether for any single token type, and a transfer of tokens incurs a flat fee of 0.001 ether, deducted from the transferred amount. The contract announces all successful token deposits, transfers, and approval changes.
