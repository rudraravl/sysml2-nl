This contract manages a decentralized perpetuals trading platform, holding user collateral in a designated stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including leverage and margin, and global configuration parameters for each supported trading pair. Callers can open new positions, close existing positions, deposit collateral, withdraw available collateral, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. A designated operator can add new trading pairs, adjust trading fees, and pause all trading activity. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x, and a 0.05% trading fee is applied to all trades. The contract announces position openings, position closures, and liquidations to observers.
