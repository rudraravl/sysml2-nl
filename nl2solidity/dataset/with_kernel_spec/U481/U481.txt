This contract manages user deposits of a specified ERC-20 token, acting as a gateway to external yield-generating strategies. It maintains individual user balances of deposited tokens and a total supply of shares representing their proportional ownership in the pooled assets. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and redeem their shares for underlying tokens. A designated operator can initiate rebalancing operations to allocate funds to external strategies and harvest yield from those strategies. The contract imposes a 0.5% withdrawal fee on all redemptions. A minimum deposit amount of 100 units of the deposited token is enforced. The contract emits an event when a deposit or withdrawal occurs, and when the operator rebalances funds or harvests yield.
