This contract manages unique, human-readable domain names ending in ".meta" and custodies the registration fees. It maintains a registry of registered domain names, their associated owner addresses, and their expiration timestamps. Callers can register a new domain, renew an existing domain, transfer domain ownership, and set a resolver address for their domain. A designated operator can set the registration and renewal fees, and update the base resolver contract address. A new domain registration costs 0.01 ether per year, and renewals cost 0.005 ether per year. The contract emits an event when a domain is registered, renewed, or transferred.
