This contract manages a deflationary token and its backing reserve of three distinct fungible tokens. It maintains a total supply of the deflationary token, individual balances for each token holder, and the current balances of the three reserve tokens held in the contract. Callers can acquire the deflationary token by depositing any of the three reserve tokens, and they can burn their deflationary tokens to redeem a proportional share of the reserve. A designated operator can initiate a reserve yield distribution event, which adds reserve tokens to the contract, and can also adjust the reserve distribution checkpoint threshold. Only the designated operator can update the reserve distribution checkpoint threshold, which must always be a positive integer and can only be increased. The contract will emit an event when a reserve yield distribution occurs, specifying the amount of each reserve token added.
