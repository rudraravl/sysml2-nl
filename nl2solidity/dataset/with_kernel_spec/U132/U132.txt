This contract is a bridge for transferring a specific wrapped asset between an EVM chain and an external, non-EVM blockchain, holding the wrapped asset in escrow. It maintains a total supply of the wrapped asset on the EVM chain, and for each user, it tracks their individual balance of the wrapped asset. Callers can deposit the wrapped asset into the contract to initiate a transfer to the external chain, and they can request a withdrawal of the wrapped asset from the contract, which requires a corresponding deposit on the external chain. A designated operator can mint new wrapped assets on the EVM chain after verifying a deposit on the external chain, and they can burn wrapped assets from the EVM chain after verifying a withdrawal request. The contract will not allow a single mint operation to exceed 1000 units of the wrapped asset. All withdrawal requests will incur a fixed fee of 0.01 units of the wrapped asset, deducted from the withdrawal amount. The contract announces when wrapped assets are minted, burned, deposited, or withdrawn.
