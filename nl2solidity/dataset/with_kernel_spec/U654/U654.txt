This contract manages a decentralized perpetual exchange, holding collateral deposited by traders and liquidity providers. It maintains individual trader positions including margin, leverage, and profit/loss, as well as global pools for each supported asset, and configuration parameters like funding rates and fees. Callers can open new positions, increase or decrease existing positions, close positions, deposit collateral, withdraw collateral, and add or remove liquidity from the pools. A designated operator can update oracle prices, adjust funding rates, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x. A 0.1% trading fee is applied to all position opening and closing operations. The contract announces position openings, position closures, liquidations, and collateral deposits and withdrawals.
