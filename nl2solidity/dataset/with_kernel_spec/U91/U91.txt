This contract manages user investments in a diversified portfolio of underlying assets, holding various ERC-20 tokens representing those assets. It maintains individual user balances for each portfolio, the current allocation percentages for each asset within the portfolio, and a global fee percentage for withdrawals. Users can deposit ERC-20 tokens into the portfolio, withdraw their share of the portfolio's assets, and rebalance the portfolio's asset allocations. Only a designated operator can rebalance the portfolio's asset allocations and update the withdrawal fee. The withdrawal fee is capped at 5% of the withdrawn amount, and rebalancing operations can only be performed once every 24 hours. The contract emits events for every deposit, withdrawal, and portfolio rebalancing action, including the affected user, asset, amount, and the new allocation percentages.
