This contract manages a synthetic yield token, representing diversified exposure to various yield-generating strategies, and custodies underlying yield-bearing assets. It maintains each user's balance of the synthetic token, the total supply of the synthetic token, and a list of approved underlying asset strategies with their current allocations. Callers can deposit approved underlying assets to mint new synthetic tokens, redeem synthetic tokens for a proportional share of the underlying assets, and transfer synthetic tokens to other users. Only a designated operator can add or remove approved underlying asset strategies, adjust the allocation of assets to each strategy, and set the redemption fee. A redemption fee of 0.5% is applied to all redemptions, and the total allocation across all strategies must always sum to 100%. The contract announces events for token transfers, deposits, redemptions, and changes to strategy allocations.
