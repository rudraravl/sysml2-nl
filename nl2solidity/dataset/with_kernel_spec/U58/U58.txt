This contract manages a prediction market where users can bet on the outcome of social media trends, holding deposited tokens as collateral. It maintains individual user balances, active prediction markets with their associated stakes and outcomes, and a global configuration for fees and market parameters. Callers can create new prediction markets, deposit tokens into their account, place bets on open markets, claim winnings from resolved markets, and withdraw their available balance. A designated administrator can resolve markets, set the global fee percentage, and pause all betting activity. New prediction markets must have a minimum stake of 100 tokens, and a 2% fee is applied to all winning payouts. The contract emits an event when a new market is created, when a bet is placed, and when a market is resolved.
