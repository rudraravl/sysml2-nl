This contract is a cross-chain bridge for transferring a specific wrapped token between a Layer-1 network and a Layer-2 network, holding the Layer-1 native token as collateral. It maintains a record of each user's deposited Layer-1 native tokens awaiting transfer to Layer-2, and a total supply of the wrapped token on Layer-2. Callers can deposit Layer-1 native tokens to receive wrapped tokens on Layer-2, and burn wrapped tokens on Layer-2 to withdraw Layer-1 native tokens. A designated operator can pause all deposits and withdrawals, and can update the address of the Layer-2 bridge contract. The maximum single deposit amount is 10,000 Layer-1 native tokens, and a 0.1% fee is charged on all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events including the depositor's address, the amount, and the Layer-2 recipient address, and withdrawal events including the Layer-1 recipient address and the amount.
