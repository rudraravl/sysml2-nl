This contract enables creators to mint and manage unique digital assets, acting as a registry for these assets and their associated metadata. It maintains a record of each digital asset's creator, current owner, and a URI pointing to its off-chain metadata, along with a global fee configuration. Callers can mint new digital assets, transfer ownership of their owned assets, and approve other accounts to transfer their assets. Only the designated owner of the contract can set the global minting fee and designate an operator account. The minting fee is fixed at 0.005 Ether per asset, and a creator can mint a maximum of 100 assets per transaction. The contract announces when a new digital asset is minted, when its ownership changes, and when an approval for transfer is granted.
