This contract implements a decentralized exchange with a central limit order book, custodying deposited tokens for trading. It maintains individual account balances, active buy and sell orders, and global configuration parameters such as trading fees and minimum order sizes. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw tokens, place new limit orders, cancel existing orders, and fill existing orders. A designated operator is privileged to adjust the trading fee percentage and to pause or unpause trading for all pairs. The maximum trading fee is 0.2% of the trade value, and the minimum order size for any token pair is 100 units of the base token. The contract announces order placements, order cancellations, and trade executions to observers.
