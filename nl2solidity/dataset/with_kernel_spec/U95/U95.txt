This contract serves as a deposit vault for native blockchain currency and a wrapped version of a prominent cryptocurrency, holding these assets on behalf of users. It maintains individual user deposit balances for both asset types, a total deposited amount for each asset, and a configurable fee percentage. Callers can deposit native blockchain currency, deposit wrapped cryptocurrency, and withdraw their deposited assets. Only the contract owner can set the deposit fee, which must be between 0% and 5% inclusive, and only the contract owner can pause and unpause deposits. A withdrawal fee of 0.1% is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and fee updates to observers.
