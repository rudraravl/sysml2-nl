This contract is a liquid staking token that custodies staked native tokens and issues an equivalent amount of a liquid staking derivative token. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking derivative token, the total amount of native tokens staked, and a per-user balance of the liquid staking derivative token. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive liquid staking derivative tokens, and they can redeem liquid staking derivative tokens for native tokens. A designated operator can initiate a rebase operation to adjust the redemption rate based on staking rewards. Only the owner can set the designated operator. The redemption rate for liquid staking derivative tokens to native tokens can never decrease. A minimum deposit of 0.1 native tokens is required. The contract announces the current total supply of the liquid staking derivative token and the current redemption rate.
