This contract manages a decentralized autonomous organization's treasury, holding various ERC-20 tokens and potentially other digital assets. It maintains records of approved investment strategies, each with a unique identifier, and tracks the current allocation of treasury funds across these strategies. Callers can propose new investment strategies, deposit funds into existing strategies, withdraw funds from strategies, and redeem their share of strategy returns. Only a designated operator can approve new strategies, pause or unpause existing strategies, and set the maximum allowable capital for any single strategy. A new strategy proposal must be open for community review for at least 48 hours before it can be approved. The maximum capital allocated to any single strategy cannot exceed 25% of the total treasury value. The contract announces events for strategy approvals, fund deposits, fund withdrawals, and strategy pauses.
