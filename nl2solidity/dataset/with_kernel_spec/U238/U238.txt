This contract manages a yield-bearing stablecoin, holding deposited stablecoins and distributing yield. It maintains individual user balances of the base stablecoin and a yield-bearing derivative, a global total supply for both tokens, and a configuration for yield distribution. Callers can deposit stablecoins to receive the yield-bearing token, redeem the yield-bearing token for the base stablecoin, and claim accrued yield. A designated operator can adjust the yield distribution rate and pause/unpause deposits and redemptions. The maximum annual yield distribution rate is 10%, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all redemptions. The contract announces deposit, redemption, and yield claim events, including the amounts involved and the user's address.
