This contract facilitates the secure bridging of a specific wrapped Bitcoin asset from a source chain to a destination chain, holding the wrapped Bitcoin in escrow on the source chain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount, the total amount of wrapped Bitcoin locked in the contract, and a configurable bridging fee. Callers can deposit wrapped Bitcoin into the contract, initiate a bridge transfer to the destination chain, and claim their bridged assets on the destination chain once confirmed. Only a designated operator can approve bridge transfers and update the bridging fee, which is capped at 0.5% of the transfer amount. A minimum deposit of 0.001 wrapped Bitcoin is required for any transfer. The contract announces events for successful deposits, initiated bridge transfers, and completed claims.
