This smart contract facilitates liquid staking for a base network's native token, holding deposited native tokens and issuing a corresponding liquid staking derivative token. It maintains a record of each user's deposited native tokens and issued liquid staking derivative tokens, along with the current exchange rate between the two tokens and a configuration for various operational parameters. Callers can deposit native tokens to receive liquid staking derivative tokens, withdraw their deposited native tokens by burning liquid staking derivative tokens, and claim rewards accrued from staking operations. A designated operator can update the staking rewards distribution and adjust the exchange rate, while the contract owner can set the operator, modify the deposit fee, and pause or unpause the contract's core functionalities. The deposit fee is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited amount, and the minimum deposit amount is 100 native tokens. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and exchange rate updates to observers.
