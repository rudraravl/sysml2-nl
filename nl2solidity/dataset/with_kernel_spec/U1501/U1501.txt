This contract manages collateralized debt positions, holding various approved digital assets as collateral and issuing a stablecoin against them. It maintains individual user accounts tracking deposited collateral, issued stablecoin debt, and liquidation status, alongside global configuration for supported collateral types and their associated parameters. Callers can deposit approved collateral, borrow stablecoin against their collateral, repay stablecoin debt, and withdraw excess collateral. A designated operator can initiate liquidations on undercollateralized positions, and an owner can add new collateral types, adjust collateralization ratios, and set liquidation fees. Each collateral type must have a minimum collateralization ratio of 110% and a liquidation fee of 5% of the liquidated debt. The contract announces events for all deposits, borrows, repayments, withdrawals, and liquidations, including the affected user, asset, and amount.
