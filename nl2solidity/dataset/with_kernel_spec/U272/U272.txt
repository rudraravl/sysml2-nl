This contract is a yield-earning vault that custodies deposited ERC-20 tokens. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance, the total assets under management, and a list of approved earning strategies. Callers can deposit supported tokens, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim earned yield. A designated operator can add new earning strategies, remove existing strategies, and adjust the allocation of assets to different strategies. The contract will support a maximum of 10 active earning strategies at any given time. A 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield distribution events.
