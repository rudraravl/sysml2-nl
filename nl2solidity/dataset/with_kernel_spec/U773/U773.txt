This contract is a liquid restaking vault, holding deposited SOL tokens and issuing a yield-bearing liquid restaking token (LRT). It maintains a total supply of the LRT, the amount of SOL deposited by each user, and a global restaking yield rate. Callers can deposit SOL to receive LRT, redeem LRT for SOL, and transfer LRT to other accounts. A designated operator can update the restaking yield rate and set a withdrawal fee. The withdrawal fee is capped at 0.5% of the redeemed SOL. Redemptions are subject to a 7-day unbonding period. The contract announces deposit, withdrawal, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
