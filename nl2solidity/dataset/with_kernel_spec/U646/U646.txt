This contract facilitates the fractional ownership and management of high-value physical assets, holding wrapped tokens representing these assets. It maintains a registry of asset fractions, recording the owner and quantity for each fractionalized asset, alongside a global configuration for fees and operational parameters. Callers can deposit wrapped asset tokens to fractionalize them, transfer ownership of fractions, and redeem fractions for the underlying wrapped asset. Only a designated operator can register new assets for fractionalization and set the redemption fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of the redeemed value. A minimum of 100 fractions must be held to initiate a redemption request. The contract emits events upon asset fractionalization, fraction transfers, and successful redemptions.
