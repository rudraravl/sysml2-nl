This contract is a concentrated liquidity management vault for a decentralized exchange, designed to custody and actively manage two distinct tokens within a single liquidity position. It maintains individual user deposit records, the current state of the aggregated liquidity position, and configurable management parameters. Callers can deposit pairs of tokens, withdraw their share of deposited tokens and accumulated fees, and claim accrued rewards. A designated operator can rebalance the concentrated liquidity position, adjust the fee tier for new positions, and update the external price oracle used for rebalancing decisions. The maximum number of rebalances allowed within a 24-hour period is 5. A 0.1% performance fee is deducted from all claimed rewards. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and rebalance operations to observers.
