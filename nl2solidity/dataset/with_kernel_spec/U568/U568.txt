This contract facilitates credit delegation, allowing lenders to delegate their unused borrowing capacity to borrowers, and custodies no assets directly. It maintains per-lender records of delegated credit lines, per-borrower records of active loans, and a global configuration for fees and limits. Callers can delegate credit, borrow against delegated credit, repay loans, and withdraw delegated credit. Only the owner can set the delegation fee, adjust the maximum delegation amount, or pause new delegations. The maximum delegation amount for any single lender is 100,000 units of the underlying asset, and a 0.5% delegation fee is applied to each new delegation. The contract announces events for new delegations, loan originations, loan repayments, and delegation withdrawals.
