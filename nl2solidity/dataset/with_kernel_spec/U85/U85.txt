This contract facilitates the creation and management of distributed validator clusters for Ethereum staking, custodying deposited Ether and associated validator credentials. It maintains a registry of active validator clusters, each with its designated operators, the amount of Ether staked by each participant within the cluster, and the current operational status of the cluster. Callers can create new validator clusters, deposit Ether into existing clusters, withdraw their staked Ether and earned rewards from completed clusters, and operators can attest to validator duties. Only the owner can set the global staking fee, pause all deposits, or add new designated operators to the system. Each validator cluster must have a minimum of 4 operators and a maximum of 7 operators, and a staking fee of 10% of all earned rewards will be applied to withdrawals. The contract announces when a new validator cluster is created, when Ether is deposited into a cluster, and when rewards are distributed.
