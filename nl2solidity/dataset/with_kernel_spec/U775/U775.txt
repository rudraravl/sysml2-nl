This contract manages a social engagement token, which represents a user's commitment to a specific content creator, and custodies the native blockchain currency deposited by users. It maintains a record of each user's deposited balance for each creator, a total supply of engagement tokens for each creator, and a global configuration for the token's price per creator. Callers can deposit native currency to acquire engagement tokens for a chosen creator, transfer their engagement tokens to other users, and withdraw their underlying native currency by burning their engagement tokens. A designated operator can set the price of engagement tokens for any creator, and can pause all deposits and withdrawals. The price of engagement tokens for a creator must be at least 0.001 native currency per token. A 0.5% fee is applied to all withdrawals, deducted from the native currency returned to the user. The contract announces when engagement tokens are minted, transferred, or burned, and when native currency is deposited or withdrawn.
