This contract is a token locker, designed to custody various fungible tokens for a specified duration. It maintains records of each locked token, including the token's address, the amount locked, the unlock time, and the beneficiary of the locked tokens. Callers can deposit tokens into the locker, withdraw tokens once their lock duration has expired, and extend the lock duration for existing deposits. The contract owner is privileged to set a deposit fee, which is fixed at 0.5% of the deposited amount, and to designate up to five trusted addresses as operators who can initiate emergency withdrawals in extreme circumstances. All token deposits must have a minimum lock duration of 24 hours and a maximum of 365 days. The contract announces when tokens are deposited, when tokens are withdrawn, and when a lock duration is extended.
