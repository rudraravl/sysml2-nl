This contract is a yield-generating stablecoin vault that custodies a single designated stablecoin. It maintains individual user deposit balances, a total deposited amount, and a current yield rate. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their deposited stablecoins, and claim accumulated yield. Only the owner can set the yield generation strategy, adjust the yield rate, and pause deposits. A minimum deposit of 100 units of the designated stablecoin is enforced, and a 0.5% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, including the user's address and the amount involved.
