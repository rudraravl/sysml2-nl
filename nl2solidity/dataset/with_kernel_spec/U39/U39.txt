This contract facilitates the transfer of an arbitrary fungible token from an origin chain to a destination chain, holding deposited tokens in escrow on the origin chain. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount for each token, and a mapping of canonical token addresses between chains. Callers can deposit tokens, initiate a withdrawal request for tokens, and finalize a withdrawal on the destination chain. Only a designated relayer can finalize withdrawal requests on the destination chain, and only the owner can update the mapping of canonical token addresses. A withdrawal request must wait a minimum of 7 days before it can be finalized. A fee of 0.1% is applied to all finalized withdrawals, deducted from the withdrawn amount. The contract announces when tokens are deposited, when a withdrawal request is initiated, and when a withdrawal is finalized, including the sender, recipient, token, and amount.
