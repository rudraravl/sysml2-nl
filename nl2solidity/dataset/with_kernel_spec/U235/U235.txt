This contract is a yield vault for trusted real-world asset strategies, holding deposited stablecoins. It maintains individual deposit records for each user, tracking their principal and accrued yield, and a global record of the total assets under management within the vault. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their principal and accrued yield, and claim any available rewards. Only a designated operator can update the vault's strategy parameters and initiate yield harvesting. A minimum deposit amount of 100 stablecoins is enforced for all new deposits. The vault emits an event whenever a deposit or withdrawal occurs, and when new yield is harvested.
