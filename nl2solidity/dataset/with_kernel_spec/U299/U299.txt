This contract manages a decentralized reserve currency, holding various collateral assets to back a native stablecoin. It maintains individual account balances for the stablecoin, records of deposited collateral, and global configuration parameters like target collateral ratios and stability fees. Callers can mint new stablecoin by depositing approved collateral, burn stablecoin to redeem collateral, and transfer stablecoin between accounts. A designated operator can adjust the target collateral ratio within a predefined range, add or remove approved collateral types, and update stability fees. The initial target collateral ratio is 150%, and the maximum stability fee is 0.5% per year. The contract announces events for stablecoin minting, burning, and transfers, as well as changes to collateral types or configuration parameters.
