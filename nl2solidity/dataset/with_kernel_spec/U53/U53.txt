This contract is a payment gateway for a crypto debit card, holding user-deposited stablecoins to back card spending. It maintains individual user balances, a total outstanding balance for all cards, and a configurable fee structure. Callers can deposit stablecoins, withdraw their available balance, and initiate a card top-up request. Only a designated operator can approve card top-up requests and update the fee percentages. A minimum deposit of 100 stablecoins is required for card activation, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all card top-up transactions. The contract announces successful deposits, withdrawals, and card top-ups, along with the associated amounts and user identifiers.
