This contract manages unique digital monster assets, each with elemental properties and combat statistics, and facilitates their capture and evolution. It maintains a record for each player, detailing their owned monsters, their monster's current experience points, and their monster's elemental type. Players can capture new monsters, initiate combat between their monsters and other players' monsters, and evolve their monsters once they reach sufficient experience. A designated operator can mint new monster types and adjust the combat effectiveness of existing monster types. Capturing a monster costs a fixed fee of 100 units of the native currency, and a monster must accumulate 500 experience points before it can be evolved. The contract announces when a new monster is captured, when a monster evolves, and the outcome of each combat encounter.
