This contract manages a decentralized stablecoin system, holding a reserve asset to back the stablecoin. It maintains the total supply of the stablecoin, the reserve balance, and a mapping of user stablecoin balances. Callers can mint new stablecoins by depositing the reserve asset, redeem stablecoins for the reserve asset, and transfer stablecoins between accounts. A designated operator can adjust the stablecoin's target peg, and only the owner can pause all minting and redemption operations. The stablecoin's total supply is capped at 1,000,000 units, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all redemption operations, deducted from the reserve asset. The contract announces all stablecoin transfers and changes to the reserve balance.
