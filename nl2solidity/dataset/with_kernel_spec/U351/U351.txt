This contract manages a digital gold vault, holding wrapped gold tokens that represent physical gold. It maintains individual user balances of deposited wrapped gold, a total supply of its own yield-bearing token, and a global yield rate configuration. Callers can deposit wrapped gold to receive yield-bearing tokens, redeem yield-bearing tokens for wrapped gold, and transfer their yield-bearing tokens to other accounts. A designated operator can update the global yield rate, and the owner can pause all deposits and redemptions. The maximum yield rate that can be set is 10% annually, and a 0.5% redemption fee is applied to all withdrawals of wrapped gold. The contract announces when new wrapped gold is deposited, when wrapped gold is redeemed, and when the yield rate is updated.
