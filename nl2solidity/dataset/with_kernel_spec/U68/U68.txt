This contract manages a gaming asset marketplace, holding digital item tokens and the native platform currency. It maintains individual balances for each player, a global listing of available items with their prices, and a configurable transaction fee. Players can list items for sale, purchase listed items, and withdraw their earnings. A designated operator can set the transaction fee and pause all trading activity. The maximum transaction fee is 5%, and all item listings are valid for a maximum of 30 days. The contract emits events for item listings, purchases, and withdrawals.
