This contract manages unique, non-fungible digital identities, acting as a registry for these names and their associated data. It maintains a mapping of registered names to their owners, a mapping of names to their configuration records, and a global counter for the total number of names issued. Callers can register an available name, transfer ownership of a name they own, update the configuration records for a name they own, and renew the registration of an owned name. The contract owner can set the registration fee, designate operators who can pause and unpause name registrations, and withdraw collected fees. Each name registration costs 0.01 ether and is valid for a period of one year. The contract emits an event when a name is registered, when its ownership is transferred, and when its configuration records are updated.
