This contract is a fund management protocol that custodies various fungible tokens. It maintains per-account records of deposited tokens, a list of approved investment strategies, and a global configuration for fees and operational parameters. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw their share of the fund, and propose new investment strategies. Only the owner can approve new investment strategies and set the management fee. The maximum management fee is 2%, and withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period. The contract announces deposit and withdrawal events, including the account involved and the amount of tokens transferred.
