This contract is a liquid staking vault for a proof-of-stake blockchain, holding deposited native blockchain currency. It maintains a record of each user's deposited amount and their corresponding liquid staking token balance, along with a global pool of staked assets and configuration parameters like the staking reward rate and withdrawal unbonding period. Callers can deposit native currency to receive liquid staking tokens, withdraw their staked assets after an unbonding period, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can initiate staking operations on the underlying blockchain and update the staking reward rate, while the owner can pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. The minimum deposit amount for native currency is 0.1 units, and the unbonding period for withdrawals is fixed at 7 days. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal requests, and successful reward distributions.
