This contract manages the creation and distribution of unique digital trading cards, holding no custodial assets itself. It maintains a record of all minted cards, including their unique identifiers, associated artwork hashes, and current owner, along with a total supply count for each card type. Callers can mint new cards, transfer ownership of cards to other accounts, and burn cards they own. A designated operator can define new card types, set the maximum supply for each card type, and pause all minting operations. The maximum supply for any single card type is capped at 10,000 units. A fee of 0.001 ether is charged for each card minted, payable to a treasury address. The contract emits an event whenever a new card is minted, transferred, or burned, and when the minting operations are paused or unpaused.
