This contract manages concentrated liquidity positions within a decentralized exchange, holding deposited tokens on behalf of users. It maintains records of individual liquidity positions, including token amounts, price ranges, and accumulated fees, along with global pool configurations such as fee tiers and active tick. Callers can deposit tokens to create new liquidity positions, add to existing positions, remove liquidity, and claim accumulated fees. A designated operator can adjust global protocol parameters like the maximum number of fee tiers and the base fee rate for new pools. The contract enforces a maximum of 5 distinct fee tiers per token pair. All liquidity removal operations incur a 0.05% protocol fee on the withdrawn token value. The contract emits events for all new position creations, liquidity adjustments, and fee claims.
