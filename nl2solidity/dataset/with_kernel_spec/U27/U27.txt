This contract manages a pool of a single fungible token, enabling users to deposit and borrow against it. It maintains individual deposit balances, outstanding loan amounts per borrower, and a global configuration for interest rates and collateral requirements. Callers can deposit tokens into the pool, withdraw their deposited tokens, borrow tokens against their collateral, repay outstanding loans, and liquidate undercollateralized loans. A designated operator can adjust the borrowing interest rate and the collateralization ratio. The maximum borrowing interest rate is 15% annually, and the minimum collateralization ratio for new loans is 150%. The contract emits an event when a deposit is made, a loan is taken out, a loan is repaid, or a liquidation occurs.
