This contract manages a decentralized vault infrastructure, holding deposited tokens and issuing corresponding vault shares. It maintains records of each user's deposited token balances, their issued vault shares, and the total supply of vault shares for each distinct vault. Callers can deposit tokens into a specified vault, withdraw their deposited tokens by redeeming vault shares, and transfer their vault shares to another address. A designated operator is privileged to create new vaults and to update the underlying asset address for an existing vault. Each vault must maintain a minimum of 100 base tokens as a reserve, and the total supply of vault shares for any single vault cannot exceed 1,000,000 shares. The contract announces to observers when new vaults are created, when tokens are deposited, and when tokens are withdrawn.
