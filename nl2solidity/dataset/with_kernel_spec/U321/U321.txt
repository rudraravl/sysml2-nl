This contract manages a decentralized savings protocol, holding user deposits of various fungible tokens. It maintains individual user balances for each deposited token and a global configuration for allowed tokens and their associated parameters. Callers can deposit tokens into their account, withdraw their deposited tokens, and claim accrued yield. A designated administrator can add new supported tokens, update token parameters, and pause or unpause the protocol. Deposits are limited to a maximum of 10,000 units of any single token per user. A 0.1% withdrawal fee is applied to all withdrawals, deducted from the amount withdrawn. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and yield claim events, including the user's address, the token involved, and the amount.
