This contract is a liquid staking protocol that custodies an underlying proof-of-stake asset, allowing users to deposit the asset and receive a liquid staking token representing their staked position. It maintains a total supply of the liquid staking token, a mapping of user balances for the liquid staking token, and a record of the total underlying asset deposited and staked. Callers can deposit the underlying asset, withdraw their deposited underlying asset by burning the liquid staking token, and claim staking rewards. A designated operator can initiate staking and unstaking operations with the underlying asset, and can set the protocol fee up to a maximum of 10%. The owner can upgrade the contract and pause all deposit and withdrawal operations. Withdrawals are subject to a 7-day unbonding period after the request is made. The contract announces deposit events, withdrawal events, and reward distribution events.
