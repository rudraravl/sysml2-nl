This contract issues and manages tokenized representations of real-world equity shares, holding no assets itself but relying on an off-chain custodian for the underlying shares. It maintains a record of each user's balance of these tokenized shares and a global configuration for operational parameters. Callers can mint new tokenized shares by providing proof of underlying share acquisition, transfer their tokenized shares to another account, and burn tokenized shares upon redemption of the underlying equity. Only a designated operator can mint or burn tokenized shares, and only the contract owner can set the operator or adjust the minting fee. A minting fee of 0.1% is applied to all new tokenized share issuances, and the maximum number of tokenized shares that can be minted in a single transaction is 1,000,000. The contract announces all minting, burning, and transfer events, including the sender, recipient, and amount.
