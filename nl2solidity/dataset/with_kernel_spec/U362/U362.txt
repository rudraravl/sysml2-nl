This contract manages token launches, holding deposited tokens from participants and the project's launch tokens. It maintains a record of each participant's deposited funds and their allocated launch tokens, along with the current launch phase and configuration parameters. Callers can deposit funds during the deposit phase, claim their allocated launch tokens after the launch concludes, and withdraw any unspent deposited funds if the launch is canceled. A designated operator can initiate new launches, set launch parameters including the token to be launched and its total supply, transition between launch phases, and cancel a launch. The maximum individual deposit is capped at 1000 units of the deposit token. A launch must proceed through deposit, allocation, and claim phases in that strict order. The contract emits an event when a new launch is initiated, when a participant deposits funds, and when a participant claims tokens.
