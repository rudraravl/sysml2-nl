This contract manages user deposits and perpetual futures positions, holding collateral tokens in escrow. It maintains individual account balances, open positions including their size and entry price, and global configuration parameters like funding rates and margin requirements. Callers can deposit collateral, open new positions, close existing positions, modify leverage, and withdraw available collateral. A designated operator can update oracle prices, adjust funding rates, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. The maximum leverage allowed for any position is 50x, and a 0.05% fee is charged on all position opening and closing trades. The contract announces all position opening, closing, and liquidation events to observers.
