This contract facilitates peer-to-peer trading of tokenized assets by holding user deposits and managing open orders. It maintains individual account balances for each token, a global order book for each trading pair, and per-user open order records. Callers can deposit tokens, withdraw tokens, place new limit orders, cancel existing orders, and fill existing orders. A designated operator can pause trading activity and update trading pair configurations. The maximum number of open orders per user is 100. A 0.1% fee is charged on all filled order volume, paid by the taker. The contract emits an event when an order is placed, cancelled, or filled, and when tokens are deposited or withdrawn.
