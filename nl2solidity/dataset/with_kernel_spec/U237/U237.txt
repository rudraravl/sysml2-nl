This contract is a tokenized money market fund, representing shares in a pool of short-term, high-quality USD instruments. It maintains individual account balances for token holders and a total supply of tokens, reflecting the net asset value of the underlying assets. Callers can deposit stablecoins to mint new tokens, redeem tokens to withdraw stablecoins, and transfer tokens between accounts. Only a designated operator can adjust the exchange rate between the stablecoin and the token, and only the owner can pause all deposit and redemption operations. A minimum deposit amount of 100 stablecoins is enforced for all new deposits, and a 0.1% redemption fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces token transfers, deposit events, and redemption events to observers.
