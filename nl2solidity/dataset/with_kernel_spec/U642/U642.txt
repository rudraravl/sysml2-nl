This contract facilitates the creation and management of individual user-controlled vaults for trading non-fungible tokens, holding deposited Ether or wrapped Ether. It maintains a record of each vault's owner, its Ether and wrapped Ether balances, and a mapping of approved non-fungible token collections for trading. Callers can create new vaults, deposit Ether or wrapped Ether into their own vaults, withdraw Ether or wrapped Ether from their own vaults, and place signed bids for non-fungible tokens. The contract owner can set a global trading fee and designate a trusted relayer for processing signed bids. The maximum trading fee is 2% of the bid amount, and a minimum of 0.01 Ether must be deposited to activate a new vault. The contract emits events for vault creation, deposits, withdrawals, and successful non-fungible token purchases.
