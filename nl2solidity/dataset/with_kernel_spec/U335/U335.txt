This contract establishes a decentralized money market for lending and borrowing fungible tokens, holding deposited tokens as collateral. It maintains individual account records for each user's deposits, borrows, and accumulated interest, along with global pools for each supported token and configuration parameters like interest rates. Callers can deposit tokens, borrow tokens against collateral, repay borrowed tokens, withdraw their deposits, and liquidate undercollateralized positions. Only a designated operator can add new supported tokens, adjust interest rate models, and pause borrowing or depositing for specific tokens. A borrower's total outstanding debt, including interest, cannot exceed 80% of the value of their deposited collateral, and a 0.5% fee is applied to all successful liquidations, paid to the liquidator. The contract announces events for every deposit, borrow, repayment, withdrawal, and liquidation, including the affected user, token, and amount.
