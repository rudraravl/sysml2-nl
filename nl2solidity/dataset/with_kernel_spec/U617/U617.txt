This contract facilitates trustless exchange of two different fungible tokens, holding reserves of both tokens in a liquidity pool. It maintains the current quantity of each token in the pool and a cumulative price for each token. Callers can add liquidity by depositing both tokens in proportion to the current reserves, remove liquidity by burning their liquidity tokens and receiving a proportional share of the reserves, and swap one token for another. The contract owner can update the fee percentage charged on swaps. Swaps incur a 0.3% fee, which is added to the liquidity pool. The contract emits an event whenever liquidity is added, removed, or a swap occurs, detailing the tokens involved and their amounts.
