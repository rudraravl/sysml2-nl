This contract manages a collection of unique digital art tokens and facilitates their exchange, holding the tokens themselves in escrow during sales. It maintains a registry of all minted art tokens, their current owner, and their listing status, along with a global fee percentage and a record of all sales. Callers can mint new art tokens, list their owned tokens for sale at a specified price, purchase listed tokens, and withdraw their sales proceeds. Only a designated administrator can set the minting fee, update the global sales fee percentage, or pause all sales. The minting fee is fixed at 0.01 ether per token, and the global sales fee is 2.5% of the sale price. The contract emits an event upon each successful token mint, each token listing, and each token sale.
