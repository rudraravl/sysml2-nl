This contract manages a decentralized fund for community-driven initiatives, holding various fungible tokens contributed by participants. It tracks individual participant balances, the total fund balance for each token, and proposal states including voting results and funding allocations. Participants can deposit fungible tokens into the fund, propose new initiatives requiring funding, vote on active proposals, and withdraw their share of tokens from successfully funded proposals or their unallocated deposits. A designated operator can approve proposals for voting, execute successful proposals to disburse funds, and set the fee for proposal submission. A proposal must receive at least 51% 'yes' votes to pass, and a 0.01 ether fee is charged for each new proposal submitted. The contract announces when new proposals are created, when votes are cast, and when proposals are executed.
