This contract manages a stablecoin system, minting and burning a pegged token backed by a basket of collateral assets. It maintains individual user balances of the stablecoin, records of collateral deposited by each user, and a global configuration for acceptable collateral types and their associated parameters. Users can deposit approved collateral to mint the stablecoin, redeem stablecoin for their deposited collateral, and transfer their stablecoin holdings to other accounts. A designated operator can add or remove approved collateral types, adjust the collateralization ratio for each type, and pause or unpause minting and redemption operations. The stablecoin must always maintain a minimum 150% collateralization ratio across all outstanding tokens. A 0.1% fee is charged on all stablecoin redemptions. The contract broadcasts events for all minting, burning, transfer, and collateral deposit/withdrawal actions.
