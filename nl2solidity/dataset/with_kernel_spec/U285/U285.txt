This contract creates and manages wrapped tokens, where each wrapped token is backed by a basket of other fungible or non-fungible tokens. It maintains a record of each wrapped token's unique identifier, its underlying collateral assets, and its current owner. Callers can create new wrapped tokens by depositing collateral, transfer ownership of existing wrapped tokens, and redeem wrapped tokens to retrieve their underlying collateral. A designated operator can set the fee for creating wrapped tokens, which is fixed at 0.5% of the total collateral value, and can add or remove supported collateral types. The contract will emit an event whenever a new wrapped token is created, transferred, or redeemed.
