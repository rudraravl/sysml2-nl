This contract manages algorithmic trading strategies, holding user-deposited collateral tokens to execute trades on decentralized exchanges. It maintains per-strategy records including the strategy's current configuration, its allocated capital, and its performance metrics. Callers can deposit collateral into a strategy, withdraw their share of a strategy's capital, create a new trading strategy, update an existing strategy's parameters, or pause a strategy. Only a designated operator can create new strategies or update strategy parameters. A strategy's maximum allowable slippage for any trade is 0.5%, and a 0.1% fee is applied to all withdrawals. The contract announces strategy creation, strategy updates, and trade executions to observers.
