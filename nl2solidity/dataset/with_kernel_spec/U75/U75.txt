This contract tokenizes real estate ownership, representing fractional interests in specific properties as fungible tokens. It maintains a record of each property's total token supply, the current owner of each token, and a mapping of property identifiers to their associated legal documentation hashes. Callers can purchase tokens for available properties, transfer their owned tokens to another address, and redeem their tokens for an equivalent share of the property's rental income. A designated operator can mint new tokens for a property up to a predefined cap of 1,000,000 tokens per property, and can also burn tokens. The owner can set a one-time property onboarding fee of 0.5% of the initial token issuance value, which must be paid before any tokens for that property can be minted. The contract emits events for token transfers, token minting, token burning, and property onboarding.
