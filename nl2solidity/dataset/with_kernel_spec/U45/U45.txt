This contract is an automated vault that custodies a liquid staking derivative token and earns rewards from an external yield-generating protocol. It maintains a record of each user's deposited liquid staking derivative tokens, their share of the vault's total assets, and the current configuration parameters. Callers can deposit liquid staking derivative tokens, withdraw their share of the vault's assets, and claim accumulated rewards. A designated operator can update the external yield-generating protocol's strategy and adjust the vault's fee structure. The maximum fee for withdrawals is 0.5% of the withdrawn amount, and a minimum deposit of 0.01 liquid staking derivative tokens is enforced. The contract announces deposit, withdrawal, and reward claim events to observers.
