This contract serves as an oracle to determine the ETH-equivalent value of various assets, primarily for use on Arbitrum. It maintains a mapping of assets to their corresponding Chainlink ETH-denominated price feeds and a threshold for how old a price can be before it's considered stale for each feed. The contract also tracks the Arbitrum sequencer's uptime using a dedicated Chainlink feed. The owner can set or update the price feed and its staleness threshold for any asset, emitting an event upon each update. Callers can query the contract to get the ETH value of a specified amount of an asset. Before returning a price, the contract verifies that the Arbitrum sequencer is operational and has been for at least a grace period, and that the asset's price is positive and not older than its configured staleness threshold. If any of these checks fail, the query will revert with a specific error.
