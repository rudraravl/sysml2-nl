This contract facilitates a decentralized lending platform for conditional tokens, connecting lenders and borrowers. It maintains records of loan offers, borrow requests, and active loans, tracking their status, interest rates, collateral, and participants. Callers can accept borrow requests or loan offers, either directly or by filling an order on a connected exchange. Borrowers can repay their loans, and both borrowers and designated refinanciers can refinance existing loans. Lenders can "call" a loan after its minimum duration, potentially leading to an auction if the underlying question's price isn't yet available, or seizing collateral if it is. The contract supports cancelling loan offers and borrow requests. Access control is managed through roles, with a specific role for refinanciers. The contract charges a protocol fee on each loan, directed to a configurable recipient. It emits events for matched proposals, loan repayments, refinancings, loan calls, and loan transfers, allowing off-chain services to monitor loan lifecycles. The contract is pausable and reentrancy-guarded, ensuring secure operations.
