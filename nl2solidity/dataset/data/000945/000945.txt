This contract implements a decentralized governance system, allowing token holders to propose and vote on changes to the protocol. It maintains a record of proposals, including their state (pending, active, canceled, defeated, succeeded, queued, expired, executed), the actions they entail, and the votes cast for each. Users can create proposals if they hold enough voting power, which is derived from their holdings in a governance token, a distributor contract, and a safety module. Once a proposal is active, token holders can cast "yes," "no," or "abstain" votes. A proposal succeeds if it meets a minimum quorum of "yes" votes and has more "yes" than "no" votes, after which it can be queued and eventually executed through an associated Timelock contract. The contract also features a "Break Glass Guardian" who can initiate emergency actions to return governance control to a designated address, and a "Governance Return Guardian" who can update this emergency address. Various parameters like quorum, proposal threshold, and voting periods can be adjusted by the Timelock. Events are emitted for all significant actions, such as proposal creation, vote casting, and state changes, enabling off-chain monitoring.
