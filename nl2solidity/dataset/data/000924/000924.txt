This contract provides a set of utility functions for verifying digital signatures on the Ethereum blockchain using the Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA). It does not maintain any persistent state on-chain. Its primary purpose is to recover the address of the signer from a given message hash and its corresponding signature. Callers can provide signatures in various formats: a full 65-byte signature, or separate `r`, `s`, and `v` components, including an optimized `vs` format. The contract includes safeguards to prevent signature malleability by enforcing specific ranges for the `s` and `v` values. It also offers helper functions to format messages into the standard Ethereum Signed Message hash (EIP-191) or Typed Data hash (EIP-712) before signature verification. There are no access control mechanisms, events, fees, or upgradeability features, as it's a pure utility library.
