This contract provides a public interface for reading storage slots from any contract on the blockchain. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather acts as a utility to expose the underlying storage of other contracts. Callers can retrieve the value of a single storage slot by providing its 32-byte identifier, or they can fetch a contiguous range of slots by specifying a starting slot and the number of slots to read. Additionally, callers can request the values of an arbitrary list of specific storage slots. All operations are read-only and do not alter the state of any contract. There are no access controls, fees, or events associated with these operations. This contract is designed to be inherited by other contracts, allowing them to offer a standardized way for external entities to inspect their storage.
