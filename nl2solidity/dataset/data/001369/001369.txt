This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token with several custom features. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual token balances for each address, and allowances for delegated transfers. The contract also tracks an owner, who has special permissions. A key feature is a transaction tax mechanism, which is initially set to 10% and reduces to 0% after a certain number of buy transactions. This tax is collected into the contract and can be swapped for ETH via a Uniswap router, with the resulting ETH sent to a designated tax wallet. The contract enforces maximum transaction amounts and maximum wallet holdings to prevent large buys or whale accumulation, though these limits can be removed by the owner. Trading is initially disabled and must be explicitly started by the owner. The owner can also trigger a manual swap of collected tokens for ETH and transfer the ETH to the tax wallet. All token transfers and approvals emit standard ERC-20 events.
