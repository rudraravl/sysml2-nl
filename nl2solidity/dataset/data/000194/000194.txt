This contract is a mock ERC20 token designed for testing purposes. It maintains a name, symbol, and decimal count for the token, along with the standard ERC20 balances for all addresses. Callers can mint new tokens to any address and burn tokens from any address, effectively increasing or decreasing the total supply and specific account balances. Beyond standard ERC20 functionality, it also provides a suite of utility functions for safely interacting with other tokens and transferring ETH, including methods to transfer all of a specific token from one address to another, or to transfer all of a token held by the contract itself. These utility functions are primarily for demonstrating safe transfer patterns rather than core token functionality. No specific access control is implemented for minting and burning, meaning any caller can perform these actions. The contract emits standard ERC20 Transfer and Approval events when token balances or allowances change.
