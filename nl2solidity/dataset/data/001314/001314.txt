This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "GENI" with the symbol "GeniBot", which can be transferred between accounts and approved for spending by third parties. It maintains a record of each account's token balance and the total supply of tokens. The contract also incorporates an ownership mechanism, where the deployer is initially the owner and can transfer this ownership to another address or renounce it. A key feature is a maximum total supply limit of 10 million tokens, preventing the total number of tokens from exceeding this cap. The owner has the exclusive ability to mint new tokens, increasing the total supply and assigning them to a specified address, provided the maximum supply is not exceeded. Additionally, any token holder can burn their own tokens, or burn tokens from another account if they have been granted sufficient allowance, reducing the total supply. All token transfers, approvals, and ownership changes emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring.
