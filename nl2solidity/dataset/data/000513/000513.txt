This contract provides a robust role-based access control system, allowing other contracts to manage permissions for different addresses. It maintains a mapping of roles, identified by unique `bytes32` values, to the addresses that possess them. Each role also has an associated admin role, which controls who can grant or revoke that specific role. The contract supports querying whether an address has a particular role, granting roles to addresses, and revoking roles from addresses. Only an account holding a role's designated admin role can grant or revoke it. A special `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` exists, which is its own administrator. Users can also renounce their own roles. Important events are emitted whenever a role is granted, revoked, or when a role's admin is changed, enabling off-chain monitoring of access control modifications. The contract also supports the ERC-165 standard for interface detection.
