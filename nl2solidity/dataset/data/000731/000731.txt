This contract provides a utility library for performing arithmetic operations on unsigned 112-bit fixed-point numbers, specifically in a Q112.112 format. It allows callers to convert a standard unsigned 112-bit integer into this fixed-point representation by multiplying it by a constant scaling factor (2^112). Additionally, it offers a function to divide a fixed-point number by a standard unsigned 112-bit integer, returning the result also in the fixed-point format. The contract does not maintain any state, nor does it have any access control or emit events. Its sole purpose is to facilitate precise division and encoding of numbers within a specific fixed-point system for other contracts that might require such calculations.
