This contract acts as a secure holding vault, specifically designed to store lpETH tokens during a staking cooldown period. It maintains a record of the designated staking vault address and the specific lpETH token it manages. The primary operation allowed is the withdrawal of lpETH tokens to a specified recipient and amount. This withdrawal function is strictly permissioned, allowing only the pre-configured staking vault to initiate transfers. Any other caller attempting to withdraw tokens will be rejected. The contract does not emit any events. Its core purpose is to provide a controlled and secure temporary storage for lpETH tokens, ensuring only the authorized staking vault can release them.
