This contract acts as a permissioned wrapper for an underlying ERC20 token, facilitating its use within a Uniswap V4 pool manager. It maintains the address of the underlying permissioned token, the Uniswap V4 pool manager, an allowlist checker contract, and flags indicating whether swapping is enabled and which wrappers or hooks are permitted to interact with it. The contract allows authorized wrappers to deposit underlying tokens and mint an equivalent amount of this contract's ERC20 tokens to the pool manager. Users can deposit underlying tokens into this contract for verification purposes. The contract owner can update the allowlist checker, enable/disable swapping, and manage allowed wrappers and hooks. Crucially, the contract ensures that only the pool manager can hold its ERC20 tokens, automatically unwrapping them back to the underlying token when transferred from the pool manager to another address. Ownership of the contract cannot be renounced.
