This contract serves as a price oracle, providing time-weighted average prices from Uniswap V3 pools. It maintains a reference to a configuration contract that stores the specific Uniswap V3 pool, the quote token, and the required observation cardinality for the pool. Callers can query the contract for the price of a base token in terms of the configured quote token, specifying the amount of the base token. The contract will return the equivalent amount of the quote token based on a time-weighted average price over a configured period. Before querying prices, a caller can invoke a function to adjust the Uniswap V3 pool's observation cardinality to ensure sufficient data for accurate TWAP calculations. The contract also allows querying the timestamp of the oldest available observation in the configured pool. If a price query encounters an "OLD" error from Uniswap V3, indicating the requested period is too far in the past, the contract automatically adjusts the period to the oldest available observation and retries the query.
