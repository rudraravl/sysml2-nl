This contract provides a library for performing advanced mathematical operations, specifically exponentiation (x^y) and logarithms (log_base(arg) and natural logarithm ln(a)), on fixed-point numbers with 18 decimal places. The contract does not maintain any state itself, but offers pure functions that take 18-decimal fixed-point numbers as input and return results in the same format. It handles both positive and negative exponents for natural exponentiation and ensures arguments for logarithms are positive. The core mechanics involve converting arbitrary base operations to natural logarithm and exponentiation, utilizing Taylor series expansions for precision, and decomposing numbers into sums of precomputed constants to handle large values efficiently. Intermediate calculations are performed with higher precision (20 or 36 decimal places) to minimize rounding errors before converting back to 18 decimals for the final result. The contract includes bounds checks to prevent overflows and ensure valid input ranges for its mathematical functions. There are no access controls, events, fees, upgradeability, or integrations with other protocols as it is a purely mathematical utility library.
