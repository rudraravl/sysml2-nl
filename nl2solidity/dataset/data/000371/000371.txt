This contract facilitates a flash loan arbitrage strategy across Uniswap V3 pools. It maintains references to a Uniswap V3 router, factory, and WETH9 address established at deployment. The primary operation is initiated by `initFlash`, which requests a flash loan of specified amounts of two tokens from a Uniswap V3 pool. During the flash loan callback, `uniswapV3FlashCallback` is executed. This function attempts to profit from the borrowed tokens by performing two single-asset swaps through the Uniswap V3 router, using two different pools with specified fees. After these swaps, the contract repays the flash loan, including fees, to the original pool. Any remaining tokens from the arbitrage are then transferred as profit to the caller who initiated the `initFlash` function. The contract ensures that the flash loan is only repaid if the arbitrage is profitable enough to cover the borrowed amounts and fees. All token transfers and approvals are handled internally.
