This contract manages protected listings of ERC721 tokens, allowing users to deposit NFTs and receive a fungible token in return, effectively taking a loan against their NFT collateral. It tracks each listing, including the owner, the collection, the specific token ID, and the amount of fungible tokens taken. Callers can create multiple listings simultaneously, transfer ownership of an existing listing, adjust their position by increasing or decreasing their debt, and unlock a listing by repaying the loan and associated fees. If a listing's collateral health deteriorates, it can be liquidated by a keeper, initiating a dutch auction for the NFT and rewarding the keeper. The contract maintains a record of withdrawable assets for unlocked listings and uses checkpoints to track interest rates and time for calculating listing health and fees. All operations are guarded against re-entrancy, and important actions like listing creation, transfer, unlock, asset withdrawal, debt adjustment, liquidation, and checkpoint creation emit events for off-chain monitoring.
