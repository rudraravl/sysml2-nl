This contract manages token and Ether payments, acting as a payment hub. It maintains the address of a WETH9 contract, which is set at deployment and cannot be changed. Callers can instruct the contract to unwrap WETH9 held within it, converting it to native Ether and sending it to a specified recipient, provided a minimum amount is met. Similarly, it allows sweeping any ERC20 token held by the contract to a recipient, again requiring a minimum amount. Any Ether received by the contract can be refunded to the caller. The contract also includes an internal payment mechanism that can handle WETH9 deposits and transfers, or transfer other ERC20 tokens either from its own balance or by pulling from a designated payer to a recipient. The contract only accepts direct Ether deposits from the WETH9 contract.
