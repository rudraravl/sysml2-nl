This contract establishes a robust, two-tiered access control system, maintaining a set of roles: `GUARDIAN_ROLE`, `ADMIN_ROLE`, and `PAUSER_ROLE`. It tracks the number of active guardians to ensure at least one always exists. The contract can be paused and unpaused, affecting any inheriting contracts. Guardians have the highest authority, able to change the administrator for any role except their own, and are the only ones who can unpause the contract. Admins, along with guardians and pausers, can pause the contract. The contract explicitly prevents any role from being renounced by its holder, ensuring that roles can only be managed by a guardian. An initial guardian is set during deployment, and this guardian controls the default admin role, preventing privilege escalation. Events are emitted when roles are granted or revoked, and when the contract is paused or unpaused.
