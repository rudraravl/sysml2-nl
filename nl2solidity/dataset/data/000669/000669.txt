This contract facilitates the transfer of ERC20 token rewards from a designated vault address to a user. It maintains a record of the `REWARDS_VAULT` address, set immutably at deployment, along with an `incentivesController` and `rewardsAdmin`. The primary operation is `performTransfer`, which allows only the `incentivesController` to initiate a transfer of a specified `amount` of a particular `reward` token from the `REWARDS_VAULT` to a `to` address. The contract also provides a view function, `getRewardsVault`, to retrieve the address of the rewards vault. This design ensures that reward distribution is controlled by a trusted entity and funds are pulled from a pre-configured source.
