This contract provides a set of mathematical functions designed for precise calculations involving fixed-point decimal numbers, specifically "wad" (18 decimal places) and "ray" (27 decimal places). It doesn't maintain any on-chain state or have any access control mechanisms, as its sole purpose is to offer utility functions for other contracts. Callers can perform multiplication and division operations on both wad and ray values, with all results rounded half-up to the nearest whole unit of their respective precision. Additionally, it provides functions to convert values between wad and ray formats, also with rounding. The contract is purely a library of stateless mathematical operations, optimized for gas efficiency using assembly, and does not emit any events or integrate with other protocols.
