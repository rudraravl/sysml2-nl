This contract establishes a foundational governance system, allowing for the creation, voting, and execution of proposals. It maintains a record of each proposal, including its proposer, voting start and duration, and whether it has been executed or canceled. Callers can propose new actions by specifying target addresses, values, and calldata, along with a description. Once proposed, others can cast votes for or against the proposal, optionally providing a reason and additional parameters, or even using a signed message. The contract tracks the state of each proposal, moving from pending to active, then potentially to succeeded, queued, defeated, canceled, or executed, based on voting outcomes and time. Only specific functions, designated as `onlyGovernance`, can be executed through successful proposals, ensuring all critical changes are subject to the governance process. The contract emits events for proposal creation, queuing, execution, and cancellation, enabling off-chain monitoring of the governance lifecycle. It also supports receiving ETH, though this is disabled if an external executor is configured.
