This contract acts as a bridge for a specific ERC-7802 compliant token, facilitating its movement between different chains. Upon deployment, it permanently records the address of the ERC-7802 token it will manage. The contract maintains no internal token balance; instead, it orchestrates cross-chain transfers by interacting directly with the designated token. When tokens are sent from this chain, the contract instructs the associated ERC-7802 token to perform a crosschain burn from the sender's address for the specified amount. Conversely, when tokens are received onto this chain, the contract instructs the ERC-7802 token to perform a crosschain mint to the recipient's address for the given amount. All operations are mediated through the ERC-7802 token's own cross-chain functionalities, ensuring that the token's supply is correctly managed across chains.
