This contract defines a mintable and burnable ERC20 token that integrates with a rewards system. It maintains the total supply of the token and individual user balances. Callers can mint new tokens to a specified account or burn tokens from an account. Both minting and burning operations trigger an interaction with a designated rewards controller if one is configured, passing the affected account, the total supply before the operation, and the account's balance before the operation. This allows the rewards system to adjust incentives based on these token movements. The contract is initialized with a reference to a main pool, the token's name, symbol, decimals, and the address of the rewards controller.
