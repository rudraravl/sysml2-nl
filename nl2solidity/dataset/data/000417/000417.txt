This contract provides a library of unsafe mathematical operations for use by other contracts. It does not maintain any state itself, nor does it define any access control or emit events. The library offers two primary functions: one to calculate the ceiling of a division, and another to perform a multiplication followed by a division, effectively calculating `floor(a * b / denominator)`. Both functions are designed for internal use by other contracts and explicitly do not perform any checks for overflow, underflow, or division by zero, leaving such validations to the calling contract. This design prioritizes gas efficiency by omitting safety checks, assuming the caller will handle input validation.
