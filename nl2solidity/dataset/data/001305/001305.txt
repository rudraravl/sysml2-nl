This contract implements a cryptocurrency token with standard ERC-20 functionalities, including token transfers, approvals, and balance queries. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks individual token balances for each address. The contract incorporates a tax mechanism on transfers, with separate buy and sell taxes that can change over time. It also enforces maximum transaction amounts and maximum wallet sizes to prevent large holdings or trades. A unique feature is a transfer delay, limiting transfers to one per block for certain transactions. The contract integrates with a Uniswap V2 router to facilitate automatic token swaps for ETH when a certain token balance threshold is met within the contract. An owner address has special permissions to open trading, remove transaction limits, add or remove bot addresses (which are prevented from trading), and update the marketing address. The designated tax wallet can also reduce the final buy and sell taxes and manually trigger token swaps and ETH transfers from the contract. Several initial token allocations are distributed to specific addresses upon deployment. Events are emitted for token transfers and approvals, as well as for updates to the maximum transaction amount.
