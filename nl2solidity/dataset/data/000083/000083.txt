This contract extends an ERC-20 token to enable gasless transfers and receives through signed authorizations. It maintains a record of nonces for each authorizer, organized by a 192-bit key and a 64-bit sequence, allowing for independent parallel authorization streams. Users can authorize transfers of their tokens to another address, or authorize another address to receive tokens on their behalf, by providing a signed message containing the transfer details, a validity period, and a unique nonce. These authorizations can be executed by anyone, effectively paying the gas for the original authorizer. Additionally, an authorizer can cancel a specific authorization by signing a cancellation message. The contract verifies signatures using a robust signature checking mechanism and ensures nonces are used sequentially within their respective keys. The `authorizationState` function allows checking if a specific nonce has been consumed.
