This contract establishes a basic access control mechanism, designating a single account as the "owner" with exclusive permissions for certain operations. Upon deployment, the contract's creator automatically becomes this owner. The contract maintains a record of the current owner's address. Any caller can query the current owner's address. The owner can transfer ownership to a new address, provided the new address is not the zero address, or they can renounce ownership entirely, which permanently removes all owner-restricted functionality. Every change in ownership, including the initial assignment, transfer, or renunciation, triggers an event indicating the previous and new owner addresses. Functions restricted to the owner will revert if called by any other address.
