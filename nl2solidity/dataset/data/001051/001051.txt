This contract serves as a Balancer pool price oracle, providing a reliable and updatable price for a specific Balancer pool. It maintains the current calculated price, a "safe" price (the previous current price), and the timestamp of the last update. The contract is initialized with the Balancer Vault, a Chainlink oracle for individual token prices, the target Balancer pool, and parameters for update frequency and price staleness. Only an authorized Keeper can trigger price updates, which calculate the pool's price based on its constituent tokens' Chainlink prices and pool weights. The contract implements access control, with an admin role for general management and a manager role specifically for authorizing contract upgrades. It also provides a status check to determine if the reported price is valid and not stale, and will revert if an invalid or stale price is requested.
