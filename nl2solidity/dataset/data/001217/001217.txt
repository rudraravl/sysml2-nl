This contract serves as a financial model for calculating interest rates and accrued interest within a lending protocol. It maintains immutable minimum and maximum interest rate bounds, set at deployment, which define the range for its linear rate curve. Callers can query the instantaneous interest rate based on the total amount of assets borrowed and the total assets held by a pool. Additionally, the contract can compute the total interest accrued over a period, given the last update timestamp, total borrows, and total assets. All calculations, including utilization, rate factor, and accrued interest, are consistently rounded up to favor the protocol. There are no access controls, events, or external integrations; its sole purpose is to provide these interest rate calculations.
