This contract implements a pausable, ownable ERC-20 compliant token called CIFCoin. It maintains a total supply of tokens and individual balances for each address. The contract owner, set at deployment, has special privileges, including the ability to pause and unpause all token transfers, transfer ownership to another address, and mint new tokens until minting is explicitly finished. The owner can also freeze and unfreeze individual addresses, preventing them from sending or receiving tokens. Users can transfer tokens to other addresses, and approve other addresses to spend tokens on their behalf. Approved spenders can then transfer tokens from the approving address. Any token holder can burn their own tokens, reducing the total supply, or burn tokens from an address they are approved to spend from. The contract also includes functions for the owner to perform direct token exchanges between two addresses and to drain any ERC-20 tokens accidentally sent to the contract. All significant actions, such as transfers, approvals, freezing, unfreezing, minting, and ownership changes, emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract also allows for receiving Ether, which the owner can withdraw.
