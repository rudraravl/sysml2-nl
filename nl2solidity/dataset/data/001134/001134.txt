This contract implements a basic fungible token, tracking the total supply and individual balances for each address. It also maintains a record of allowances, permitting one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. Users can transfer their own tokens to another address, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens. Approved spenders can then transfer tokens from the approving user to any recipient. The contract emits events for every token transfer and approval, allowing off-chain applications to monitor token activity. It includes internal functions for minting new tokens and burning existing ones, which adjust the total supply and individual balances accordingly. There are no access control mechanisms for these internal mint/burn functions; their usage would be defined by a contract inheriting this one.
