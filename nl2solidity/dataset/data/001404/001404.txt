This contract implements a token sale for "Manor Token" (MT) with a dynamic pricing model and a referral system. It tracks the total supply of MT, individual token balances, and the total ETH deposited. The contract also maintains an owner address and a realtor address, both with special permissions. Callers can purchase MT by sending ETH, either specifying the exact amount of ETH to spend or the exact number of tokens to buy, with prices increasing as more tokens are sold. A referral mechanism awards a portion of purchased tokens to a referrer and the realtor. Once the sale ends, either by reaching a time limit or a hard cap on deposited ETH, the owner can withdraw all collected ETH and claim all remaining tokens. Users can also claim their accumulated referral rewards after the sale ends. Token transfers between users incur a 5% burn, effectively reducing the total supply and increasing the value of remaining tokens. The contract emits events for token transfers, approvals, and purchases, allowing off-chain services to monitor its activity.
