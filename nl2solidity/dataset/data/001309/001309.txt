This contract implements an ERC-20 token named "APE LORD" with a fixed supply of 1,000,000 tokens, each having 18 decimal places. The contract tracks token balances for each address and allows for transfers between accounts, as well as approvals for third parties to spend tokens on behalf of an owner. The contract is owned by the deployer, who has the exclusive ability to enable trading for the token. Initially, trading is disabled, and only the owner can transfer tokens. Once trading is enabled, general token transfers are permitted, subject to complex internal logic that appears to implement anti-bot or anti-manipulation measures during the initial trading phase, involving specific addresses like the router and the trading pair. The contract emits standard ERC-20 `Transfer` and `Approval` events for all relevant operations, and an `OwnershipTransferred` event when the contract's owner changes or renounces ownership.
