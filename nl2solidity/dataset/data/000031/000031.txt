This contract implements a vesting wallet designed to hold and release native currency (Ether) and ERC-20 tokens to a designated beneficiary over a specified period. Upon deployment, it records the beneficiary, a start timestamp, and a vesting duration. The contract maintains the total amount of Ether and each ERC-20 token that has been released to the beneficiary. Any Ether or ERC-20 tokens sent to this contract are subject to the defined vesting schedule, meaning that if vesting has already begun, a portion of new deposits may become immediately releasable. Only the beneficiary (owner) can call the `release` function to withdraw vested Ether or ERC-20 tokens. The contract emits `EtherReleased` and `ERC20Released` events whenever funds are withdrawn, indicating the token address and amount. The vesting schedule is linear, calculating the vested amount based on the elapsed time since the start timestamp relative to the total duration.
