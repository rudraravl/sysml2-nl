This contract manages a rewards distribution system for NFT positions, allowing users to earn a specific rewards token based on their staked assets. It tracks individual NFT balances for various asset types (identified by a unique hash combining pool, asset, and debt status) and calculates rewards based on a dynamic rate and duration. The contract maintains the total supply of each asset type for reward calculation, the last time rewards were updated, the reward rate, and the period finish time. Users can query their earned rewards and claim them, with the contract transferring the rewards token directly to the NFT owner. A special role, `REWARDS_ALLOCATOR_ROLE`, is required to notify the contract of new reward amounts, which then updates the reward rate and period. The contract also includes a mechanism to boost a user's balance based on their voting power in a separate staking token, up to a maximum boost requirement. It prevents re-entrant calls for reward claiming and emits an event when rewards are paid.
