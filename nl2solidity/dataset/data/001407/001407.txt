This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token with additional features. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual token balances for each address, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of other addresses. The contract has an owner who can perform specific administrative actions. Upon deployment, the total supply is minted to the deployer's address, and a designated "head" address, the contract itself, and the owner are excluded from transaction fees. The owner can initiate the token's trading by creating a liquidity pair on Uniswap V2, linking the token to WETH. Transfers between addresses are subject to a fee mechanism, though currently set to zero, which can be modified. A unique "heart" mapping allows the owner to set a value for an address, which, if greater than zero, would impose a fee on transfers from that address. The contract also includes a mechanism to swap a portion of its own token balance for ETH via Uniswap, sending the ETH to the "head" address. Ownership can be transferred to a new address by the current owner.
