This contract provides a robust role-based access control system, allowing other contracts to manage permissions for different addresses. It maintains a mapping of roles, identified by unique `bytes32` values, to the addresses that possess them, along with an associated administrative role for each. Callers can query whether an account has a specific role using `hasRole`. Roles can be granted or revoked by an account holding the role's designated admin role, ensuring a hierarchical permission structure. A special `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` exists, which is its own administrator. Users can also `renounceRole` to voluntarily give up a role they hold. Important state changes, such as roles being granted, revoked, or an admin role being changed, emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract also supports the ERC-165 interface for introspection.
