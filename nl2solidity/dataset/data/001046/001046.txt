This contract operates as a lending pool, allowing users to deposit underlying tokens and receive shares, or borrow tokens via approved credit managers. It tracks the total amount of underlying assets, the total borrowed amount, and individual debt limits for each credit manager. Users can deposit and mint shares, or withdraw and redeem shares, with an optional referral code. Deposits and withdrawals incur a fee, and a withdrawal fee is also applied. The contract maintains an interest rate model and updates interest accruals based on liquidity changes. Only whitelisted credit managers can borrow funds, and they are responsible for repaying debt. The contract can be paused by an administrator, and certain operations can be locked, though whitelisted addresses can bypass the lock. It emits events for deposits, withdrawals, borrows, and referrals, and handles losses by burning treasury shares.
