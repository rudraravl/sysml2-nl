This contract implements a Wrapped Ether (WETH) token, allowing users to convert native Ether (ETH) into an ERC20-compatible token and vice-versa. It maintains the standard ERC20 state, tracking token balances for each address. Callers can deposit ETH into the contract, which in turn mints an equivalent amount of WETH tokens to their address. Conversely, users can burn their WETH tokens to withdraw an equivalent amount of ETH back to their address. The contract also allows direct ETH transfers to its address, which automatically triggers a deposit operation. There are no special access controls; anyone can deposit or withdraw. An event is emitted for every token transfer (mint or burn) as per ERC20 standards. A notable mechanic is the direct ETH transfer handling and a specific error for failed ETH withdrawals.
