This contract creates a wrapped ERC-20 token, allowing users to convert an existing underlying ERC-20 token into a new, equivalent token. It maintains the address of the underlying token and its own total supply and individual balances, just like a standard ERC-20. Callers can deposit the underlying token to mint an equal amount of the wrapped token into a specified account, or burn the wrapped token from their own balance to withdraw the underlying token to a specified account. The contract prevents itself from being the recipient of deposits or withdrawals. It also includes an internal mechanism to recover underlying tokens that might accumulate in the contract without corresponding wrapped tokens being minted, which could happen if the underlying token has rebasing mechanics. The contract inherits standard ERC-20 functionality, including events for transfers and approvals.
