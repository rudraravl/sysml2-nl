This contract provides a set of utility functions for reading specific state variables from Uniswap V4 pools. It allows any caller to retrieve the current square root price (`sqrtPriceX96`) and the current tick of a specified Uniswap V4 pool by providing the pool manager address and the pool's unique ID. Additionally, it offers a function to calculate the accumulated fee growth within a specific tick range of a Uniswap V4 pool, given the pool manager, pool ID, current tick, and the lower and upper bounds of the tick range. Finally, it enables retrieval of the last recorded fee growth values for a unique Uniswap V4 position, identified by the pool manager, pool ID, and a unique position ID. All operations are read-only and do not alter any on-chain state.
