This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token, named "SUSHI.ZIPPO.IO" with the symbol "SUSHI" and 18 decimal places. It tracks the total supply of tokens and individual account balances, along with allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and transfer tokens from accounts they have been approved to spend from. The contract also includes functionality for staking and unstaking tokens, where only designated "stakers" can perform these operations. Upon deployment, 10,000 SUSHI tokens are minted to the deployer, who is also automatically designated as a staker. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing external applications to monitor token activity.
