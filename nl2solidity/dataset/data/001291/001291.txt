This contract provides a mechanism for a designated guardian to recover ERC20 and ERC721 tokens that are accidentally sent to the contract. It maintains a single state variable: a `recoveryAddress`, which is the destination for rescued tokens. The contract allows a guardian, identified by a specific role, to set this `recoveryAddress` and subsequently initiate the rescue of either ERC20 or ERC721 tokens. When rescuing ERC20 tokens, the guardian specifies the token address and an amount, and the contract transfers up to the available balance to the `recoveryAddress`. For ERC721 tokens, the guardian specifies the token address and `tokenId`, transferring the specific NFT. All rescue operations are restricted to the guardian role and cannot occur if the contract is paused. Events are emitted for setting the recovery address and for each successful token rescue, providing an auditable log of these critical operations.
