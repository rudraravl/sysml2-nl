This contract serves as a risk assessment module for a lending protocol, determining the health and liquidation parameters of user positions. It maintains a reference to a central registry, a pool contract, and a risk engine, along with a fixed liquidation discount. Callers can query the health of a specific position, retrieve detailed risk data including total asset value, total debt value, and the minimum required asset value to remain healthy. The contract also allows for validation of liquidation attempts, ensuring that only unhealthy positions are liquidated and that liquidators do not seize assets exceeding the allowed discount. It also provides functions to validate if a position has bad debt. The contract relies on external oracle services, accessed via the risk engine, to determine asset values in ETH. It updates its internal references to the pool and risk engine from the registry, enabling flexible configuration.
