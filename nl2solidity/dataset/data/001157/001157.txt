This contract manages the core logic for a lending pool, handling the initialization and configuration of asset reserves, and calculating user account data. It maintains a mapping of all reserve data, including configuration parameters like loan-to-value, liquidation thresholds, and caps for borrowing and supplying. It also tracks a list of active reserve addresses. Callers can initialize a new reserve by providing its asset address, interest rate strategy, oracle, and configuration parameters, which are then validated to ensure consistency and prevent immediate liquidations. The contract also allows for the minting of accrued reserve factor to a treasury address, transferring funds and adjusting supply shares. Furthermore, it provides a function to calculate a user's overall financial health within the pool, returning their total collateral, total debt, available borrowing power, liquidation threshold, loan-to-value ratio, and health factor, all denominated in a base currency. Important events are emitted when reserves are initialized or when collateral configurations are changed, allowing off-chain systems to monitor the pool's state.
