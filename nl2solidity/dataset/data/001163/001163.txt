This contract provides a utility library for performing percentage calculations with two decimal places of precision, effectively representing percentages from 0.00% to 100.00%. It maintains no state on-chain itself, but offers two pure functions: `percentMul` to calculate a percentage of a given value, and `percentDiv` to divide a value by a percentage. Both operations incorporate rounding, where values of 0.5 or greater are rounded up, and values less than 0.5 are rounded down. The contract is designed for efficiency, utilizing assembly for optimized gas usage in its mathematical operations. There are no access controls, events, or integrations with other protocols, as it serves purely as a mathematical helper.
