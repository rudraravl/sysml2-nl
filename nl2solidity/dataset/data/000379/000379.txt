This contract serves as a utility library for calculating liquidity amounts in a decentralized exchange context, specifically for Uniswap V3-like concentrated liquidity pools. It does not maintain any on-chain state itself. Its primary operations involve computing the amount of liquidity generated from specific token amounts within a defined price range, or conversely, determining the token amounts required for a given amount of liquidity. Callers can provide either token0, token1, or both, along with price boundaries (square root of prices) to calculate the corresponding liquidity. The contract also offers functions to calculate the token0 and token1 amounts that a given liquidity represents within a specific price range. All functions are pure, meaning they do not modify state, and are designed for internal use by other contracts. There are no access control mechanisms, events, fees, or upgradeability features as it's a stateless library for mathematical computations.
