This contract provides a library of mathematical operations for fixed-point decimal numbers, specifically designed for calculations involving 18 decimal places. It defines two data structures, `Exp` and `Double`, to represent these fixed-point numbers, with `Exp` using a single 18-decimal scale and `Double` using a 36-decimal scale for intermediate precision. The contract does not maintain any state itself but offers pure functions for addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of both fixed-point numbers and unsigned integers, ensuring arithmetic safety by checking for overflows and underflows. It also includes utility functions for comparing fixed-point numbers, checking if an `Exp` is zero, and truncating an `Exp` to a whole number. There are no access control mechanisms, events, or integrations with other protocols, as its sole purpose is to provide robust mathematical utilities for other contracts.
