This contract serves as a read-only data provider for a lending pool, allowing external callers to query specific information about reserves and their configurations. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather retrieves data directly from a specified `IPool` contract. Callers can request details about a particular asset's reserve configuration, including its decimals, loan-to-value ratio, liquidation thresholds, reserve factor, and whether it's enabled for collateral or borrowing, or if it's frozen. Additionally, it provides the address of the interest rate strategy contract associated with a given asset within a pool. All functions are view-only, meaning they do not alter any on-chain state, and there are no access control mechanisms or events emitted, as its sole purpose is to serve as a data query interface.
