This contract implements a comprehensive library of mathematical functions primarily designed for use in decentralized finance protocols, particularly those interacting with Uniswap V3. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather provides pure functions for calculations. The contract offers general arithmetic operations like finding the minimum or maximum of two numbers, calculating absolute values, and identifying the most significant nibble of a number. Crucially, it includes specialized functions for Uniswap V3 tick math, such as computing approximate ticks for a given token amount, determining maximum liquidity per tick, and calculating the square root price at a specific tick. Furthermore, it provides functions to determine token amounts for a given liquidity chunk and vice versa, and includes various casting utilities for different integer types, with built-in overflow/underflow checks. The contract also features a robust `mulDiv` function for precise multiplication and division, guarding against common arithmetic errors. All operations are internal and pure, meaning they do not modify blockchain state and are intended to be called by other contracts.
