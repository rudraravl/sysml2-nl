This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token named "NUWA" with symbol "NUGUA" and 9 decimal places, featuring a total supply of 100,000,000 tokens. It incorporates a reflection mechanism where a portion of transaction fees is distributed among token holders, and another portion is sent to a designated fee wallet. The contract maintains balances for each address, allowances for spending, and flags to exclude addresses from fees. It also tracks a list of "bot" addresses and implements a cooldown period for transfers. The contract owner has special privileges, including setting the standard tax, removing transaction limits, and enabling/disabling the cooldown. A key function allows the owner to open trading, which initializes a Uniswap V2 pair for the token and adds liquidity. During transfers, if trading is open and certain conditions are met, a portion of tokens held by the contract may be swapped for ETH via Uniswap, and that ETH is sent to the fee wallet. The contract also enforces maximum transaction amounts and maximum wallet sizes for non-owner transfers when trading is active.
