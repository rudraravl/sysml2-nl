This contract manages a system of role-based access control and address books for a lending protocol. It maintains a mapping of address book owners to a further mapping of grantees and their assigned roles, represented as a bitmask. Each address book owner can register or unregister addresses for specific roles within their own address book. Additionally, a special global address book, owned by the contract itself, is managed by administrators or guardians, allowing them to approve and revoke "Originator" and "Servicer" roles. Only the contract's guardian can approve these global roles, while either an administrator or guardian can revoke them. All registrations and unregistrations, both local and global, emit events to signal these changes. Callers can query whether a specific address holds a particular role within any given address book.
