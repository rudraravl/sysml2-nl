This contract serves as a specialized price oracle for the RLP token, providing a gas-optimized and safety-capped exchange rate against USD. It inherits core functionality from a general-purpose capped rate contract, which manages the application of upper and lower bounds to the reported price. The contract maintains its current exchange rate, along with configurable parameters for these caps and the source from which it fetches raw price data. Callers can query the current RLP/USD exchange rate, which is always guaranteed to be within the defined safety limits. The contract fetches the raw RLP price from an external RLP price oracle and then applies its capping logic. A specific multiplier of 1e9 is enforced for the rate, and any deviation during deployment will cause the contract to revert.
