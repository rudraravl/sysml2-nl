This contract is an upgradeable ERC721 token representing brand NFTs for a tokenized community. It maintains a mapping from a lowercase brand ticker to its unique token ID and tracks the total number of brand NFTs minted. Callers can mint new brand NFTs by providing a ticker (3 to 5 characters, English alphabet only) and a recipient address. Upon successful minting, a new token is created, its ID is recorded against the lowercase ticker, and the total supply increases. The contract prevents duplicate brand names by ensuring a ticker hasn't been used before, and it internally converts all tickers to lowercase for consistent lookup. There are no specific access controls beyond the standard ERC721 ownership and transfer mechanisms, and no explicit events are emitted beyond those inherited from the ERC721 standard.
