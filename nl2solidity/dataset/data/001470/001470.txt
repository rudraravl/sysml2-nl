This contract implements a Carchain Coin (CCC) ERC-20 token with a fixed maximum supply. It tracks the total supply of tokens and individual balances, along with a list of vesting schedules for specific beneficiaries. Upon deployment, an initial token supply is minted to the deployer, partners, first supporters, and a future token sale address. A portion of tokens is also minted to the contract itself to fund various vesting schedules for company reserves and team members, which unlock at predefined dates over several years. Only the contract's administrator can mint additional tokens for "Vehicle Tokenization" and "DocuVerify" initiatives, provided the total supply remains below the hard-capped maximum. Any beneficiary with vested tokens can query their currently claimable amount and then call a function to claim these tokens, which are transferred from the contract to their address. Each successful claim emits a `TokensReleased` event. The contract also supports meta-transactions, allowing gasless transactions for users.
