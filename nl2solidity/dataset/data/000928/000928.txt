This contract provides a set of mathematical utility functions for signed 256-bit integers. Its purpose is to offer common operations like finding the maximum or minimum of two numbers, calculating their average without overflow, and determining the absolute value of a signed number. The contract maintains no state on-chain; all its functions are pure computations that take input parameters and return a result. There are no access controls, permissions, or events, as it simply performs calculations. It does not integrate with other protocols, involve fees, or have upgradeability mechanisms. Its core mechanic is to safely handle signed integer arithmetic, particularly for the average function which employs a specific formula to prevent overflow.
