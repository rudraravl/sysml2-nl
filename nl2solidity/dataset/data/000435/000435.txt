This contract acts as a forwarder for Permit2 approvals, enabling other contracts to receive token allowances on behalf of a user. Upon deployment, it immutably records the address of the official Permit2 contract it will interact with. Callers can initiate a single token allowance approval or a batch of approvals by providing the owner's address, the Permit2 permit data, and a valid signature. The contract then attempts to execute the corresponding permit function on the designated Permit2 contract. If the Permit2 call fails, for instance due to a front-running attempt, the contract gracefully catches the error and returns the reason without reverting the entire transaction, preventing denial-of-service attacks on multi-call operations. There are no access control mechanisms; any caller can attempt to forward a permit. The contract does not maintain any internal token balances or state beyond the Permit2 address.
