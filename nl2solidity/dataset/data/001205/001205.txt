This contract creates bridged ERC1155 tokens on a Layer 2 network, representing original tokens from a remote chain. It stores the address of a special `InfernalRiftBelow` contract, which is the only entity permitted to perform critical operations. The contract maintains the remote chain ID and the original token's address, along with a mapping of token IDs to their unique URIs. It also tracks whether it has been initialized. Once deployed, the `InfernalRiftBelow` contract can initialize it by setting the royalty percentage, the remote chain ID, and the remote token address. Subsequently, only `InfernalRiftBelow` can mint new tokens to a recipient, assigning a URI to each token ID. The `InfernalRiftBelow` contract can also claim any accumulated royalties, either in native ETH or specified ERC20 tokens, and transfer them to a designated recipient. The contract implements the ERC1155 and ERC2981 standards, allowing for royalty payments on secondary sales.
