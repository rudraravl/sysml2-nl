This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token, representing a fungible asset with a fixed name, symbol, and decimal precision. It maintains the total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Users can transfer tokens they own to others, or, if approved, transfer tokens on behalf of another user. Token holders can also grant spending allowances to other addresses, which can be revoked or modified. A unique feature is the `permit` function, allowing users to sign an off-chain message authorizing a spender, which can then be submitted on-chain to update the allowance without the owner directly sending a transaction. All token transfers and allowance approvals emit corresponding events for external monitoring. The contract itself has no special owner or administrative privileges; all operations are governed by standard ERC-20 rules and the `permit` signature mechanism.
