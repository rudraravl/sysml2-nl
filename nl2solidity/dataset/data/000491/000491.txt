This contract provides a set of secure mathematical operations for unsigned 256-bit integers, specifically focusing on addition, subtraction, multiplication, and finding the maximum of two numbers. Its purpose is to prevent common arithmetic errors like integer overflow and underflow, which can lead to critical vulnerabilities in smart contracts. It doesn't maintain any state on-chain itself, but rather offers functions that other contracts can use safely. Callers can perform these operations by providing two unsigned integers as input, receiving the calculated result or causing a transaction to revert if an overflow or underflow would occur. There are no access control mechanisms, as these are pure functions intended for general use. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability features, or integrations with other protocols; it solely provides robust arithmetic.
