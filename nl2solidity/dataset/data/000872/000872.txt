This contract implements an ownable smart wallet designed to hold assets and interact with other contracts on behalf of its owner. It maintains a single owner address and a record of which external addresses are approved to transfer ownership of the wallet. The contract is initialized with an owner, who can then execute arbitrary calls to any target contract, optionally sending Ether with the call. Only the current owner can initiate these executions. Ownership can be transferred to a new address, but this action is restricted to either the current owner themselves or an address that has been explicitly approved by the owner for such transfers. When ownership is transferred by an approved address, that approval is automatically revoked. Changes in transfer approval status are broadcast via an event. The wallet can receive Ether directly.
