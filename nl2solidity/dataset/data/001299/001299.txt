This contract implements an ERC-20 token named "Arbirdrum" with the symbol "ARBIRD" and 9 decimal places, featuring a total supply of 1 billion tokens. It maintains balances for each address and tracks allowances for token transfers. The contract owner, set at deployment, has special privileges. A key feature is a transaction fee mechanism, where a percentage of tokens from transfers (unless the sender is exempt) are collected by the contract. These collected tokens are periodically swapped for ETH, with a portion used to add liquidity to a WETH/ARBIRD pair on a decentralized exchange and another portion sent to a designated marketing fee receiver. The contract also enforces a maximum wallet holding limit and a maximum transaction amount for non-exempt addresses. The owner can adjust these limits and the fee percentages. Important events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and automatic liquidity additions.
