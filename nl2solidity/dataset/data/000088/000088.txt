This contract extends a standard ERC-20 token to include a temporary approval mechanism. It maintains both a standard allowance and a separate temporary allowance for each owner-spender pair. Callers can set a temporary allowance for a spender over their tokens, similar to a regular approval, but this temporary allowance is designed to be consumed and automatically reduced as tokens are spent. When checking the total allowance a spender has, the contract sums both the persistent and temporary allowances. When tokens are spent, the contract prioritizes consuming the temporary allowance first, reducing it accordingly, before drawing from the persistent allowance. This allows for one-time or limited-use approvals without affecting the long-term persistent allowance. No events are emitted for temporary allowance changes.
