This contract provides a reentrancy guard mechanism to prevent unauthorized reentrant calls. It does not maintain any persistent state on-chain; instead, it utilizes transient storage, meaning its state is only valid for the duration of a single transaction. The contract offers two primary modifiers: `nonReentrant` and `nonReentrantView`. The `nonReentrant` modifier prevents a function from being called if the contract is already executing another `nonReentrant` function, ensuring that critical operations complete atomically. The `nonReentrantView` modifier provides a similar check for view functions, preventing them from reading inconsistent state during an ongoing reentrant call. If a reentrant call is detected, the contract will revert with a `ReentrancyGuardReentrantCall` error. This contract is designed to be inherited by other contracts to secure their functions against reentrancy attacks.
