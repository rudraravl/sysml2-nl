This contract acts as a registry for "safes" and "stamps," managing their lifecycle through different statuses. It maintains a record of each safe's status (unused, pending, or used), its associated stamp (token address and ID), and the start block and timestamp of its pending phase, along with a duration. The contract also tracks which safe is associated with a given stamp. Only accounts with the `SAFE_ROLE` can initiate a safe's pending status or mark it as used. When a safe transitions to pending, it records the associated stamp and emits a `MorphStarted` event. Once the pending duration has passed, a `SAFE_ROLE` account can mark the safe as used, emitting a `MorphFinished` event. Safes in a pending state expire after a fixed `EXPIRATION_TIME` if not marked as used. The contract integrates with an external configuration contract to manage roles and retrieve addresses.
