This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "RatedXToken" with the symbol "RTDX". It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks the balance of RTDX tokens held by each address. Callers can view the total supply, their own balance, and the allowance granted to other addresses. They can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and transfer tokens on behalf of an approved sender. The contract also provides functions to atomically increase or decrease an allowance. Upon deployment, an initial supply of tokens is minted and assigned to the deployer. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing off-chain applications to monitor token activity.
