This contract functions as a giant liquidity pool designed to manage and distribute ETH for staking across various liquid staking networks, specifically targeting MEV and fees. It maintains a record of an associated Giant LP token, which represents shares in this pool, and tracks idle ETH within the contract. Users can deposit ETH to stake it against multiple BLS keys in different staking funds vaults, receiving LP tokens in return. The contract allows users to claim accumulated rewards from these vaults, rotate their LP tokens if the underlying ETH isn't staked, and retrieve unused ETH from the staking funds vaults back into the pool. It automatically updates accumulated ETH per LP share and processes reward claims during LP token transfers and withdrawals. The contract also prevents new depositors from claiming previously accrued ETH by setting their claimed amount to the current maximum.
