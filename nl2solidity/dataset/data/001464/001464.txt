This contract implements a timelock mechanism for scheduling and executing operations, often used to introduce a delay before critical changes to other contracts. It maintains a minimum delay period and tracks the scheduled timestamp for each unique operation, identified by a hash. Operations can be single transactions or batches of transactions. Callers with the 'proposer' role can schedule operations, specifying a target address, value, calldata, an optional predecessor operation, a unique salt, and a delay. The scheduled operation must adhere to the contract's minimum delay. Callers with the 'executor' role can then execute operations once their scheduled delay has passed and any predecessor operations are complete. Proposers can also cancel pending operations. The contract emits events for scheduled, executed, and cancelled operations, as well as when the minimum delay is modified. The contract itself can receive and hold Ether, which can be transferred as part of executed operations. The minimum delay can only be changed by the timelock contract itself, requiring a scheduled and executed operation.
