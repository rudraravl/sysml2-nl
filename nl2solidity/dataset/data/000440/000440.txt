This contract manages Uniswap V4 liquidity positions, extending the base PositionManager to incorporate permissioned tokens. It maintains state about liquidity positions as NFTs, including their associated pool keys and whether they have subscribers. The contract allows for the unwinding of a liquidity position, burning its NFT, and routing the underlying currencies to the original liquidity provider (LP) or, if permissioned, to an administrator, emitting a `CurrencyUnwound` event for each currency. It also enables users to withdraw ERC-6909 claims on the PoolManager, transferring the underlying currency to a specified recipient and emitting a `ClaimWithdrawn` event. Crucially, it restricts the transfer of position NFTs entirely. When minting or increasing liquidity in a position, the contract verifies that at least one of the involved currencies is permissioned and that the recipient is allowed to hold liquidity for those currencies, rejecting pools without any verified permissioned adapters. It also ensures that associated hooks are allowed for permissioned currencies. The contract integrates with a `PermissionsAdapterFactory` to identify and interact with permissioned tokens, and handles the wrapping and unwrapping of permissioned tokens during payment and sweeping operations.
