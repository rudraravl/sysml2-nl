This contract acts as a proxy for an ERC-20 token, allowing it to function as a lending market token (MToken) within the Moonwell protocol. It maintains state regarding its current administrative address and the address of its underlying logic implementation. Callers can interact with the contract to mint and redeem MTokens, borrow and repay underlying assets, and liquidate positions. It also supports standard ERC-20 operations like transfers and approvals. An administrator, initially the contract deployer, has exclusive control over updating the underlying logic implementation, setting the Comptroller, interest rate model, reserve factor, and managing pending administrators. All operational functions, except for administrative changes, are delegated to an external implementation contract, enabling upgradeability. The contract emits a "NewImplementation" event when its logic implementation is updated.
