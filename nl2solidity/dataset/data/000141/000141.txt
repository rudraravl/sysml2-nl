This contract provides utility functions for interacting with transient storage, a temporary, transaction-scoped storage area on the blockchain. It does not maintain any persistent state itself. The contract allows callers to read from and write to specific transient storage slots, treating them as various data types: addresses, booleans, bytes32 values, unsigned 256-bit integers, and signed 256-bit integers. There are no access control mechanisms, fees, or events emitted by this contract; its sole purpose is to offer a structured way to manage temporary data within a single transaction's execution context, which is automatically cleared at the transaction's end.
