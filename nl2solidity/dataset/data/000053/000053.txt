This contract extends a voting system to track historical delegation and voting unit balances. It maintains a record of an account's delegate at various points in time and also stores snapshots of an account's voting unit balance over time. Callers can query the delegate of any account at a specific past timepoint and retrieve the voting unit balance of any account at a specific past timepoint. When an account delegates its voting power, the contract records the new delegate and the current time. Similarly, when voting units are transferred between accounts, the contract updates the historical balance records for both the sender and receiver, reflecting their new voting unit totals at the current time. This contract is designed to be integrated with token contracts where the token transfer logic first updates balances, then calls this contract to record the voting unit movement.
