This contract acts as a generic receiver for ERC721 non-fungible tokens. Its sole purpose is to accept any incoming ERC721 token transfer, regardless of the sender, the token's origin, or its unique identifier. It maintains no internal state beyond its existence on the blockchain. The only operation it performs is a callback function, `onERC721Received`, which is invoked when an ERC721 token is safely transferred to it. This function always returns a specific selector value, signaling to the sending contract that the transfer was successfully received and acknowledged. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms, as its function is purely to be a passive recipient for ERC721 tokens.
