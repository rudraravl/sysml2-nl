This contract acts as a token collector and streaming mechanism for an ecosystem reserve, managing ERC20 tokens and native ETH. It maintains a record of unique streaming agreements, each identified by an ID, detailing the sender (always the contract itself), recipient, deposit amount, token type, and the start and stop times for the stream. A designated "Funds Admin" role, granted by a default administrator, has exclusive control over creating new streams, approving token transfers, and directly transferring tokens or ETH to any address. Both the Funds Admin and the stream's recipient can withdraw available funds from a stream or cancel it entirely, with any remaining balance returned to the contract upon cancellation. The contract tracks the next available stream ID and the remaining balance within each active stream. Important events are emitted when streams are created, funds are withdrawn, or streams are canceled, allowing off-chain systems to monitor activity. The contract incorporates re-entrancy protection for withdrawal and cancellation operations.
