This contract acts as a central hub for managing lending and borrowing activities across various markets. It maintains a list of supported markets, each with its own collateral factor, borrow cap, and supply cap. The contract tracks which markets an account has entered, allowing it to calculate an account's overall liquidity and potential shortfall. Users can enter and exit markets, provided they meet certain conditions like not having outstanding borrows or insufficient collateral. The contract also defines rules for minting, redeeming, borrowing, repaying, liquidating, seizing, and transferring tokens, ensuring that these actions adhere to established market parameters and user liquidity. Key parameters like close factor, collateral factor, liquidation incentive, and price oracle can be updated by an authorized administrator. Various events are emitted to signal changes in market status, account membership, and policy adjustments, enabling off-chain monitoring. The contract incorporates mechanisms to pause specific actions globally or per market, and it integrates with a reward distribution system to incentivize participants.
