This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token with additional features for managing token distribution, transaction fees, and liquidity. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract has an owner who can manage certain parameters. It implements a "max wallet" and "max transaction" limit to prevent large holdings or transfers by non-excluded addresses. A portion of tokens from transfers (unless excluded) can be collected as a fee, which is then swapped for BNB and sent to a designated marketing wallet. This swap and liquidity process is triggered periodically based on a counter and a threshold. The contract integrates with a Uniswap V2 router to facilitate token swaps for BNB. Key events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and when the swap and liquify mechanism is activated or executed.
