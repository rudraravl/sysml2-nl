This contract extends a standard governance system, enabling token holders to override votes cast by their delegates. It maintains a record of votes for each proposal, including "against," "for," and "abstain" counts, and tracks whether a specific voter has cast a regular vote or an override vote, along with the weight of any overridden votes. Callers can cast a regular vote through their delegate, or directly cast an override vote for a proposal, either by calling a function or providing a signed message. An override vote reduces the delegate's vote weight and applies the token holder's vote directly. The contract emits events when a delegate's vote is reduced due to an override and when an override vote is successfully cast. Quorum is reached if "for" and "abstain" votes meet a threshold, and a proposal succeeds if "for" votes strictly exceed "against" votes. This contract integrates with a token that supports extended voting functionalities, allowing it to query past balances and delegate assignments.
