This contract manages a token-based pledge mining system where users can stake a specific ERC20 token (Token A) to earn rewards in another ERC20 token (Token B). The contract maintains a mapping of pledge configurations based on duration types, which determine the reward scale. Users can pledge Token A for a chosen duration, initiating a mining record that tracks the pledge amount, creation time, and potential earnings. The contract allows users to claim accumulated Token B income, and to either temporarily pause or resume the accumulation of rewards for a specific pledge. Once a pledge's duration expires, users can remove their original Token A pledge along with any remaining accumulated Token B income. Only the contract owner can stop the entire mining operation, which also allows them to withdraw any remaining Token A and Token B held by the contract. All fund transfers are protected against re-entrancy, and important actions like pledging, receiving income, and removing pledges emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract does not accept direct ETH transfers.
