This contract serves as a price oracle, providing asset prices to other protocols. It maintains a record of an administrator address, which controls critical functions, and stores both manually overridden prices for specific assets and Chainlink oracle feeds mapped to token symbols. When queried for an asset's price, the contract first checks for an administrator-set override; if none exists, it retrieves the price from the configured Chainlink feed. All prices are returned scaled to 1e18 decimals. The administrator can set or update asset prices, configure Chainlink feeds for token symbols, and transfer administrative control to a new address. Significant actions, such as posting a new price, setting a new administrator, or configuring a new Chainlink feed, trigger corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract ensures that Chainlink prices are positive and from a completed round, and it handles decimal adjustments to standardize output.
