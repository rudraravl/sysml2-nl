This contract provides a set of mathematical functions for calculating liquidity amounts in a decentralized exchange context, specifically for concentrated liquidity pools. It does not maintain any on-chain state, nor does it have any access control or emit events. Its sole purpose is to offer pure, deterministic computations. Callers can use it to determine the amount of liquidity generated from a given quantity of token0 or token1 within a specified price range, or to calculate the maximum liquidity from both tokens at current and boundary prices. Conversely, it can also compute the amounts of token0 and token1 required to achieve a certain liquidity amount within a price range. All calculations involve square root prices and handle potential overflows during downcasting to 128-bit integers.
