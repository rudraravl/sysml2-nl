This contract manages a lottery pool, maintaining a list of participants and distributing winnings. It tracks current lottery candidates, previous lottery winners, and the total earnings in USD for each user. It also records the total payments made in both USD and MATIC. The contract allows an authorized "repoint" address, set at deployment, to add participants with a specified number of tickets to the current lottery pool. The repoint can also trigger a distribution, which randomly selects winners from the current candidates, transfers a calculated share of the contract's balance to each winner, updates their total USD earnings, and then resets the candidate and user ticket lists for a new lottery round. The contract also provides a function for the repoint to withdraw all funds in an emergency. Callers can query the current lottery candidates, the previous lottery winners, and the number of tickets held by a specific user. The contract accepts direct ETH/MATIC payments.
