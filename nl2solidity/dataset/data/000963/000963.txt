This contract serves as a price oracle for the value of crvUSD in terms of ETH. It maintains immutable references to two external Chainlink price feeds: one for ETH/USD and another for crvUSD/USD, established at deployment. The contract calculates and provides the current crvUSD/ETH price by querying these two underlying feeds, dividing the crvUSD/USD value by the ETH/USD value, and adjusting for decimal precision. Callers can retrieve this calculated price through the `latestAnswer` function, which returns a `uint256` value representing the crvUSD/ETH exchange rate. Additionally, it offers a `decimals` function, which consistently returns 18, and `latestRoundData` and `getRoundData` functions that mimic the Chainlink `AggregatorV3Interface` to provide price and round information, primarily using the crvUSD/USD feed's round data while substituting the calculated crvUSD/ETH price as the answer. There are no access controls or events emitted, as its sole purpose is to provide a derived price feed.
