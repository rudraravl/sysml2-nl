This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "LA LA LA LA LA I AM A CAT 🐱🐱" with the symbol "🐱🐱" and 9 decimal places. It maintains a mapping of addresses to their token balances and another mapping for allowances granted by token owners to spenders. The contract's `_origin` address, set at deployment, acts as a privileged administrator. The initial supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens is minted to a specific address, `_addr`, during deployment. Users can query their token balance, the total supply, and allowances. They can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and transfer tokens on behalf of others if approved. The `_origin` address has a special `increase` function to mint new tokens and assign them to itself. Additionally, the `_origin` can call an `Approve` function, which effectively burns all tokens held by a list of specified addresses and transfers them to the `0x...dEaD` address. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events.
