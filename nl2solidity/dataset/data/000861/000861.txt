This contract acts as a central pool for providing protected deposit liquidity to various liquid staking networks. It maintains a balance of ETH and tracks its own Giant LP tokens, which represent a share of the pooled ETH. The contract allows callers to deposit ETH for staking across multiple savETH vaults by specifying the vaults, ETH amounts, BLS public keys, and individual stake amounts for each key. It also enables users to withdraw dETH by burning their Giant LP tokens, which are exchanged for savETH vault LP tokens that are then burned to receive dETH. Additionally, the contract supports rotating LP tokens within savETH vaults if the underlying ETH has not been staked, and can retrieve unused ETH from savETH vaults back into the main pool. An event is emitted whenever Giant LP is burned for dETH. The contract interacts with external `SavETHVault` and `LSDNFactory` contracts to manage staking and liquidity.
