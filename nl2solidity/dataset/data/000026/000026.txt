This contract serves as a bridge for ERC-721 tokens, enabling their transfer between different blockchain networks by taking custody of the assets. It maintains a record of the specific ERC-721 token it manages, established at the contract's deployment. Callers can initiate a cross-chain transfer of a token they own or are approved to manage using the `crosschainTransferFrom` function, specifying the token's current owner, the destination chain and address, and the token's ID. The contract verifies that the caller has the necessary ERC-721 approval (either as the owner, an approved operator, or an approved address for the specific token) before processing the transfer. When a token is sent across chains, the contract takes custody of it by transferring it from the original owner to itself. Conversely, when a token is received on this chain, the contract releases custody by transferring it from itself to the designated recipient.
