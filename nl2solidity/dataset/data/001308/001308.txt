This contract facilitates fixed-price sales of ERC721 NFTs using signed messages from sellers. It maintains a nonce for each seller, which can be incremented to invalidate all their outstanding sale offers. The primary operation is `finalize`, where a buyer provides a signed sale offer from the seller, the NFT, the ERC20 token for payment, and the agreed-upon price and end time. The contract verifies the seller's signature and that the sale's end time has passed. If the NFT supports the ERC2981 royalty standard, it calculates and transfers the royalty amount to the designated receiver. Finally, it transfers the remaining payment to the seller and the NFT to the buyer. A `LogFinalize` event is emitted upon successful completion, detailing the sale. Sellers can call `cancelSale` to increment their nonce, effectively invalidating any previous signed sale offers. The contract also includes standard ownership management, allowing the owner to transfer or claim ownership.
