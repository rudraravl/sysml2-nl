This contract allows users to stake Ether (ETH) in exchange for a corresponding amount of a specific Dcoin token, and later withdraw their staked ETH, receiving a portion of their Dcoin back while the remainder is burned. The contract maintains records of each user's staked ETH and Dcoin. Users can stake ETH by calling a function that calculates the required Dcoin based on current prices from an external oracle, then transfers the Dcoin from the user to the contract. To withdraw, users retrieve their staked ETH, and a percentage of their Dcoin is burned, with the rest returned to them. The contract owner has special permissions to set the burn rate, update the oracle address, and withdraw any accumulated ETH or Dcoin tokens from the contract. All staking and withdrawal actions, as well as owner withdrawals, emit events to track these operations.
