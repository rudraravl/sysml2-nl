This contract serves as a registry for EVM-compatible blockchain network information, primarily focusing on RPC URLs. It maintains a mapping of chain aliases (like "mainnet" or "goerli") to their corresponding network details, including name, chain ID, and an RPC URL. It also tracks chain IDs to aliases for reverse lookups and stores default RPC URLs for various chains. Callers can query chain information using either a chain alias or a chain ID. Upon the first query, the contract initializes a set of predefined chains with their default RPC URLs. Callers can also explicitly set or update chain information, including their RPC URLs. When retrieving chain data, the contract prioritizes RPC URLs provided during a `setChain` call, then attempts to fetch from an external configuration or environment variable, and finally falls back to its internal default RPC URL if `fallbackToDefaultRpcUrls` is enabled. There are no access control mechanisms; all internal functions are accessible to any caller of the contract. No events are emitted.
