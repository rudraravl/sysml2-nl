This contract implements a privacy-preserving deposit and withdrawal system, similar to a mixer. It maintains a Merkle tree of deposited commitments and a record of spent nullifiers. Callers can deposit a fixed denomination of Ether, which generates a unique commitment and adds it as a leaf to the Merkle tree. Subsequently, users can withdraw funds by providing a valid zero-knowledge proof that demonstrates they possess a secret corresponding to a committed leaf, without revealing which specific leaf it is, and that the associated nullifier has not been used before. Withdrawals incur a platform fee and an optional relayer fee, both deducted from the withdrawn amount. The contract ensures that each deposit commitment is unique and that each nullifier can only be used once to prevent double-spending. Access to modify platform parameters is restricted to the current platform address, or any address if it's not yet set. All deposit and withdrawal actions, along with the Merkle root, emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract also incorporates a reentrancy guard to protect against malicious reentrant calls during fund transfers.
