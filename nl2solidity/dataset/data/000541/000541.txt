This contract serves as a central registry for Aave market addresses providers, allowing for their identification and management. It maintains a mapping between unique numerical IDs and the addresses of these providers, as well as a list of all registered provider addresses. The contract is owned, and only the owner can register new addresses providers with a unique ID or unregister existing ones. Callers can query the contract to retrieve a list of all registered provider addresses, find a provider's ID given its address, or find a provider's address given its ID. Successful registration or unregistration of a provider emits an event, indicating the provider's address and its associated ID. This contract acts as an indexing service, providing a canonical way to discover and reference different Aave markets.
