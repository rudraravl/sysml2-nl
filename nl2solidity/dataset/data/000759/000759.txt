This contract serves as a helper for interacting with a GMX-like decentralized exchange, specifically for managing short positions. It maintains immutable references to the market token, long token, short token, their respective Chainlink price feed addresses, and an oracle. The contract can retrieve its own position key, fetch detailed position properties from a data store, and calculate the collateral amount currently locked in its position. It can also determine the maximum gas limit for order execution callbacks and estimate the position's value including profit and loss in terms of the collateral token, using Chainlink prices with a small tolerance. The contract is designed to facilitate the creation of market increase and decrease orders for short positions, calculate the size delta for these orders, and provides placeholders for canceling orders and claiming funding fees.
