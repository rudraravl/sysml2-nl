This contract acts as an intermediary for users to interact with a GMX staking ecosystem. It doesn't maintain any internal state beyond the GMX tokens temporarily held during staking operations. Callers can stake a specified amount of GMX tokens, which involves transferring them to the contract, approving the reward tracker, and then initiating the staking process through a reward router. Conversely, users can unstake their GMX tokens via the same reward router. The contract also allows users to claim various rewards, including GMX and WETH, and to stake GMX and multiplier points, all through the reward router. Additionally, it provides a function to query the amount of GMX currently staked by the contract within the reward tracker and to delegate voting power of GMX DAO tokens to another address. All operations are initiated by the caller, with no specific access control beyond standard transaction requirements.
