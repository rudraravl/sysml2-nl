This contract manages the configuration of E-Mode categories and their associated assets within a lending protocol. It does not maintain its own state but rather interacts with an external `PoolConfigurator` and `Pool` contract to modify their configurations. Callers can create new E-Mode categories, specifying their loan-to-value (LTV), liquidation threshold, liquidation bonus, a descriptive label, and whether they are isolated, along with initial collateral and borrowable assets. They can also update existing E-Mode categories, adjusting these parameters, and enable or disable assets as collateral or borrowable within specific E-Mode categories, or set them as LTV-zero. All operations require at least one update or creation and ensure that E-Mode categories have valid liquidation thresholds. The contract automatically finds the first available E-Mode category ID for new creations. There are no explicit access control mechanisms within this contract itself, implying that access control is handled by the external `PoolConfigurator` contract. No events are emitted directly by this contract.
