This contract is an ERC721 non-fungible token (NFT) collection called "Doorcam Dogs" (DCD) that also functions as a payment splitter. Upon deployment, it establishes a set of payees and their respective shares for any incoming funds. Callers can mint new DCD NFTs by sending Ether to the contract, provided the total supply of 10,000 tokens has not been reached and the sent amount meets the current minting cost of 3 Ether. Each successful mint increases the total supply, assigns the new token to the caller, and emits a Minted event with the token's owner, URI, and mint time. The contract maintains the ownership of each token, the total supply, and the current minting cost. Any of the designated payees can claim their share of the Ether or ERC20 tokens held by the contract, which are distributed proportionally based on their pre-defined shares, and this action emits a PaymentReleased or ERC20PaymentReleased event. The contract also supports standard ERC721 operations like transfers, approvals, and checking balances.
