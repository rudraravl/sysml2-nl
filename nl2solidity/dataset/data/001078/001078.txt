This contract acts as a specialized gauge for distributing tokens to a designated recipient on the Polygon network. It maintains the address of a Polygon Root Chain Manager, a Polygon ERC20 Predicate, and a recipient address. The contract is initialized with a relative weight cap and the recipient's address. Its primary function is to facilitate the transfer of a specific BAL token to the pre-configured recipient on the Polygon chain whenever a minting action occurs. Before transferring, it approves the Polygon ERC20 Predicate to spend the minted amount of BAL. Only the contract itself can trigger this transfer as part of its internal minting process. It provides read-only functions to retrieve the recipient, the Polygon bridge manager, and the Polygon ERC20 predicate addresses.
