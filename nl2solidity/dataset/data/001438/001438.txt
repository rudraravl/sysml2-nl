This contract functions as a token wallet, allowing users to deposit, withdraw, and transfer ERC20 tokens. It maintains internal balances for each token and user, and also tracks approvals for spending tokens on behalf of others. Users can deposit tokens into the contract, which are then held internally. They can also withdraw their tokens, or transfer them to other users within the contract's internal balance system. Importantly, the contract enables off-chain signed messages to authorize token transfers and approvals, with an optional "Relaying King" mechanism for certain operations, where a designated address must execute the transaction. These signed messages can include a relayer reward and an expiration block number, and each signature can only be used once. Events are emitted for deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and approvals, providing a clear record of all token movements and authorizations.
