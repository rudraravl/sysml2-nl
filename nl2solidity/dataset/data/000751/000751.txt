This contract facilitates interactions with a GLV vault, specifically for depositing and withdrawing liquidity using WETH and USDC tokens. It maintains references to the WETH, USDC, and GLV tokens, as well as the GLV router. Users can initiate a deposit of USDC into the GLV vault by specifying the USDC amount and a minimum expected GLV token amount in return. This operation requires the caller to approve the contract to transfer their USDC and also to pay an execution fee in ETH. Similarly, users can initiate a withdrawal of liquidity from the GLV vault, specifying minimum WETH and USDC amounts they expect to receive. This operation also requires an execution fee in ETH. Both deposit and withdrawal functions return a unique key to identify the created order. The contract is designed to receive execution fee refunds from the GMX protocol, although the mechanism for this is not fully implemented. There are no explicit access control mechanisms beyond the `msg.sender` for initiating deposits and withdrawals. No events are emitted.
