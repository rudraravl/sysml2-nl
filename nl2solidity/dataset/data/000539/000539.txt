This contract serves as a centralized access control manager, maintaining a registry of various administrative and operational roles within a larger system. It stores a reference to an `IPoolAddressesProvider` contract, which is used to initialize the contract's primary administrator. The contract defines several distinct roles: `POOL_ADMIN`, `EMERGENCY_ADMIN`, `RISK_ADMIN`, `FLASH_BORROWER`, `BRIDGE`, and `ASSET_LISTING_ADMIN`. The contract allows the designated `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` to assign or revoke any of these roles to specific addresses, and to change the administrator for any given role. Any caller can query whether a particular address holds a specific role. All role assignments and revocations are recorded through events, enabling off-chain monitoring of permission changes.
