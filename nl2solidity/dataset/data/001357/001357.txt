This contract acts as a distributor for both native Ether and ERC20 tokens, allowing multiple designated payees to claim their proportional share of funds sent to the contract. Upon deployment, the contract is configured with a list of payees and their respective share amounts, which cannot be changed later. The contract maintains records of the total shares, the amount of Ether and each ERC20 token released to date, and the amount released to each individual payee. Any caller can send Ether or ERC20 tokens to the contract. The contract owner is the only entity permitted to trigger the `release` function for a specific payee, which transfers their accumulated share of either Ether or a specified ERC20 token. Events are emitted when a payee is added, when Ether or ERC20 payments are released, and when Ether is received, allowing off-chain systems to track distributions.
