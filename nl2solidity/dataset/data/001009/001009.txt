This contract acts as a governor for managing Blast-related yield operations and general administrative tasks. It maintains a single state variable, `feeTo`, which designates an address to receive collected fees. Upon deployment, it initializes this `feeTo` address and configures default claimable yields for itself. The contract allows designated operators to claim native and gas yields from specified contract addresses, directing these yields to the `feeTo` address. The contract owner has exclusive control over setting a new `feeTo` address, triggering a `LogFeeToChanged` event, and can also execute arbitrary calls to the Blast precompile or perform general purpose transactions to any address with a specified value and data. All functions include checks to prevent the use of a zero address where applicable.
