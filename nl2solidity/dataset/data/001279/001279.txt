This contract manages the lifecycle of multiple loans, acting as a double-entry ledger for each. It tracks loan-specific data such as status, due dates, and terms, along with the borrower, servicer, and originator for each loan. The contract holds the loan currency, which is an ERC20 token. Loans are created by an approved originator or an admin, and an NFT representing the loan is minted to the investor. Funds are committed by the investor, disbursed to the borrower (with an optional origination fee withheld), and then the loan becomes active. Servicers or admins can update loan details, accrue interest, charge miscellaneous fees, and apply borrower payments through a waterfall mechanism that allocates funds to various accounts. Servicers can withdraw accumulated fees across multiple loans in a single transaction, and can also return funds to the contract. Investors can withdraw their principal and interest. The contract uses reentrancy guards for fund transfers and emits events for significant state changes, allowing off-chain systems to monitor loan activity.
