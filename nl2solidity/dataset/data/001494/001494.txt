This contract manages a reward pool for users holding specific NFTs and ZBC tokens. It tracks individual user information, including their NFT count, accumulated bonus rewards, and whether they qualify as a "pooler" based on their ZBC holdings and market value. The contract maintains system-wide state such as the total number of NFTs, the total amount of rewards settled and debt accrued, and timestamps for reward distribution cycles. Users can withdraw their accumulated USDT rewards, which are calculated daily based on their NFT holdings and the available pool balance. The contract also updates user NFT counts when transfers occur, adding or removing users from the active pool as their NFT balance changes. Only an administrator can configure the addresses of associated tokens and other system contracts. Reward distribution and withdrawals are protected by a re-entrancy guard, ensuring that only one operation can occur at a time. Events are emitted for reward withdrawals and pool reward distributions, allowing off-chain monitoring of these key activities.
