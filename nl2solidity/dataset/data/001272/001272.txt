This contract implements a standard ERC721 Non-Fungible Token (NFT) with additional enumeration capabilities, allowing for tracking of individual tokens and their owners. It maintains the name and symbol of the NFT collection, the owner of each specific token ID, the total supply of tokens, and approval states for individual tokens or for all tokens owned by an address. Callers can approve another address to manage a specific token or all their tokens, and transfer tokens between addresses. Transfers can be "safe" to ensure the recipient contract is prepared to receive NFTs. The contract also allows querying the owner of a token, the balance of tokens an address holds, and enumerating tokens by their index or by owner and index. All transfers and approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract includes internal mechanisms for minting new tokens and managing token ownership and indexing efficiently.
