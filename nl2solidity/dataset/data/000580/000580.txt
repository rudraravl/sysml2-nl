This contract defines a mintable and burnable ERC-20 token designed to integrate with the Aave protocol's incentive system. It maintains the standard ERC-20 state of total supply and individual user balances. Callers can mint new tokens to a specified account or burn existing tokens from an account. Crucially, after any minting or burning operation, the contract interacts with an Aave Incentives Controller, if one is configured, to potentially apply or adjust incentives for the affected account based on the token's total supply and the user's balance before the operation. This ensures that incentive calculations are updated in real-time with token supply changes. The contract itself does not directly expose mint or burn functions to external callers; these are intended for internal use by inheriting contracts.
