This contract facilitates the distribution of ERC20 tokens to a predefined list of recipients based on a Merkle tree. It stores the address of the token being distributed, a Merkle root representing the valid claims, and a designated funder. The contract maintains a record of which claims have already been processed using a bitmap. Claimants can individually request their tokens by providing their claim details and a valid Merkle proof, which is verified against the stored Merkle root. The funder, and only the funder, has the ability to trigger multiple claims in a single transaction or to reclaim any undistributed tokens from the contract. Successful claims emit a `FundsClaimed` event, while funder actions emit `ReclaimFunds` or `BatchClaimTriggered` events, allowing off-chain systems to track the distribution process.
