This contract provides a set of utility functions for interacting with a Silo protocol, primarily focused on calculating financial metrics. It does not maintain any state itself but rather queries external Silo, configuration, interest rate model, and share token contracts. Callers can use it to determine the flash loan fee for a given token and amount, retrieve total assets and shares for different asset types (protected, collateral, debt) including accrued interest, and fetch fee details along with their receivers. It also offers functions to calculate the total collateral and debt assets with interest, considering DAO and deployer fees for collateral. There are no specific access controls or events emitted by this contract, as its purpose is purely computational and informational, relying on external contracts for actual state management and permissions. The contract handles potential errors gracefully when querying interest rate models, preventing transaction reverts.
