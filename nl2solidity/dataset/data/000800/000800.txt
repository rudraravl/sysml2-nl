This contract functions as a simple treasury designed to hold Ether, which can only be withdrawn by a designated governance address. Upon deployment, the contract permanently records the address of the governance entity. The contract maintains its Ether balance as its primary state. The sole operation available to callers is the `withdraw` function, which allows the governance address to transfer the entire Ether balance of the treasury to a specified recipient. Any attempt to call `withdraw` from an address other than the designated governance will be rejected. The contract also allows direct Ether deposits through its `receive` function. If a withdrawal fails for any reason, the transaction will revert. There are no events emitted, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms.
