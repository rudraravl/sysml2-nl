This contract acts as a central vault for ERC721 tokens, enabling their conversion into ERC20 "Collection Tokens" and facilitating various operations around these tokenized collections. It maintains a mapping of ERC721 collection addresses to their corresponding ERC20 Collection Token contracts, and tracks whether a collection has been initialized for liquidity pooling. Callers can deposit ERC721 tokens to mint ERC20s, redeem ERC20s to receive ERC721s, or swap one ERC721 for another within the same collection. An approved manager can perform an unbacked deposit of ERC20 tokens or withdraw any ERC721 token from the vault. The contract owner can create new collections, update metadata for existing Collection Tokens, set external contracts for listings, collection shutdown, and tax calculation, and pause/unpause all activity. Important events are emitted for collection creation, initialization, sunset, token deposits, redemptions, and swaps, as well as updates to linked external contracts. The contract integrates with a LockerManager for manager permissions, a Listings contract to identify listed tokens, a CollectionShutdown contract for sunsetting collections, and a TaxCalculator for tax computations. It prevents re-entrancy and ensures operations are not performed on paused collections or non-existent collections.
