This contract serves as a foundational building block for other contracts that intend to receive simple flash loans from an Aave Pool. It doesn't perform any direct operations itself, but rather establishes an immutable link to the Aave `PoolAddressesProvider` and the Aave `Pool` contract upon deployment. This allows any contract inheriting from it to easily interact with the Aave protocol's core lending pool. The contract maintains two key pieces of state: the address of the `PoolAddressesProvider` and the address of the `Pool` itself. It does not define any functions for callers to directly invoke, nor does it implement any access control, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms. Its sole purpose is to provide a standardized way for flash loan receivers to obtain and store the necessary Aave protocol addresses.
