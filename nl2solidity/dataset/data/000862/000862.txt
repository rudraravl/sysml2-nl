This contract implements an upgradeable ERC-20 token with permit functionality, designed to serve as a Liquid Staking (LP) token. It tracks a designated deployer address, which is the only entity permitted to mint new tokens to a recipient or burn existing tokens from a recipient. The contract also maintains an optional integration with a `transferHookProcessor` that can execute custom logic both before and after any token transfer. Additionally, it records the timestamp of the last interaction for any address involved in a token transfer. The contract can report the address of its associated liquid staking manager by querying its deployer. This design allows for controlled issuance and destruction of LP tokens, with the flexibility to integrate custom transfer-related operations and track user activity.
