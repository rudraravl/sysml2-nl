This contract serves as a vault for a single ERC20 token, designated at the time of its creation. It maintains a record of the specific token it manages. The primary operation allowed is for the contract's owner to grant approval to a specified target address to spend a certain amount of the token held within the vault. Only the designated owner of the contract has the authority to call this approval function; all other callers will be rejected. There are no events emitted by this contract, nor does it involve any fees, complex math, or integrations beyond the standard ERC20 interface. Its purpose is solely to hold and, under owner's direction, approve spending of a single ERC20 token.
