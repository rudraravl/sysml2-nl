This contract implements a standard non-fungible token (NFT) system, allowing for the creation, ownership, and transfer of unique digital assets. It maintains a record of each token's owner, the total number of tokens held by each address, and approvals for token transfers. Callers can query the name and symbol of the token collection, retrieve the owner of a specific token ID, and check the balance of any address. Users can approve another address to spend a specific token on their behalf, or grant blanket approval to an operator for all their tokens. Tokens can be transferred between addresses, with an option for safe transfers that ensure the recipient contract is prepared to receive NFTs. Internal functions allow for the minting of new tokens to an address and the burning of existing tokens, both of which emit a Transfer event. The contract also supports the ERC165 interface for introspection.
