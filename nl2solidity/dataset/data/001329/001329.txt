This contract manages a token-based airdrop where participants pay a fixed ticket price to join. It maintains the address of the airdropped token, the total shares available, the individual ticket price, and the deadline for joining. It tracks each participant's shares and the amount of tokens already released to them. The contract owner can modify the end date, total shares, and even transfer ownership. Participants can join by paying the ticket price in the specified token before the deadline, which records their shares and emits a `PayeeAdded` event. After the deadline, participants can call the `release` function to claim their proportional share of the total airdrop, which is calculated based on their initial contribution relative to the total collected. This action emits a `PaymentReleased` event. The contract ensures that participants can only join once and can only claim their release after the airdrop period has ended and if they haven't already claimed.
