This contract provides a library for performing fixed-point arithmetic with 18 decimal places of precision, represented by an `Exp` struct containing a `mantissa`. It offers functions to create an `Exp` from a numerator and denominator, and to perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division operations between `Exp` values or between an `Exp` and a scalar unsigned integer. All operations are designed to handle potential arithmetic errors, returning a `MathError` enum along with the result. Notably, multiplication of two `Exp` values includes rounding to the nearest integer rather than simple truncation. The contract does not maintain any state itself; all operations are pure functions that take inputs and return outputs. There are no access control mechanisms, events, fees, or upgradeability features, as it serves purely as a mathematical utility.
