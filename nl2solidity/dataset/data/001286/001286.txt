This contract acts as a module for Safe accounts, enabling whitelisted delegates to execute specific, pre-approved function calls on behalf of a Safe. It maintains a global registry of trusted function calls, identified by a target address and function selector, and a per-Safe mapping of authorized delegates. Guardians can add new trusted calls, individually or in batches, and administrators or guardians can remove them. A Safe or its guardian can add a delegate for that specific Safe, while a Safe or its administrator can remove a delegate. Delegates can then execute single or batched trusted calls on a Safe, provided the call's target and selector are globally trusted and the sender is an authorized delegate for that Safe. All state changes, such as adding/removing trusted calls or delegates, emit corresponding events. The contract also includes mechanisms for pausing operations and recovering funds, managed by guardians and an admin.
