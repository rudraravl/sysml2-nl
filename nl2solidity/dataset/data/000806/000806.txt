This contract facilitates flash loans of a single, pre-specified ERC-20 token. Upon deployment, it permanently records the address of the ERC-20 token it will manage. The primary operation allows any caller to request a flash loan of a specified amount of this token. The contract immediately transfers the requested amount to the caller, then expects the caller to execute a predefined action. After this action, the contract verifies that the full loan amount, plus any associated fees (implicitly, by checking if the balance is less than before the loan), has been repaid to the contract. If the balance is insufficient after the caller's action, the entire transaction is reverted. There are no explicit access controls beyond the `flashLoan` function being externally callable, and no events are emitted. The contract does not integrate with other protocols beyond the ERC-20 standard and a custom `IFlashLoanReceiver` interface for the callback.
