This contract provides a collection of mathematical utility functions for unsigned 256-bit integers. It does not maintain any state on-chain, nor does it have any access control or permissions, as all its functions are pure and operate solely on their input parameters. Callers can perform various arithmetic operations, including finding the maximum or minimum of two numbers, calculating their average, and performing ceiling division. More complex operations include high-precision multiplication and division (`mulDiv`), square root calculations (`sqrt`), and logarithmic calculations in bases 2, 10, and 256 (`log2`, `log10`, `log256`). Many of these functions offer overloaded versions that allow specifying a rounding direction (down, up, or towards zero) for the result. There are no events emitted, fees, or integrations with other protocols, as its sole purpose is to provide robust mathematical computations.
