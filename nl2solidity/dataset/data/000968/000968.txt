This contract serves as a price oracle, providing the value of USDT in terms of WETH. It achieves this by integrating with two external Chainlink price feeds, one for WETH/USD and another for USDT/USD, which are specified at deployment and remain immutable. The contract maintains no mutable state of its own beyond these initial configurations. Callers can query the `latestAnswer` function to retrieve the current USDT price in WETH, calculated by dividing the USDT/USD price by the WETH/USD price, with appropriate scaling to maintain 18 decimal places. The contract also exposes a `decimals` function, which consistently returns 18, and `latestRoundData` and `getRoundData` functions, which primarily relay information from the underlying USDT/USD Chainlink feed while providing the calculated USDT/WETH price as the answer. There are no access controls, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms.
