This contract is a staking platform where users can deposit a specific BEP20 token, `swaprum`, to earn rewards in another BEP20 token, `rewardToken`. The contract maintains a single staking pool. It tracks the amount of `swaprum` each user has deposited and their accumulated reward debt. The contract owner can set a maximum amount of `swaprum` a user can stake. Rewards are distributed per block, with the rate determined by `rewardPerBlock` and the pool's allocation points, which are fixed at deployment. The reward distribution period is defined by `startBlock` and `bonusEndBlock`. Users can deposit `swaprum` up to the limit, withdraw their staked `swaprum` and claim earned `rewardToken`, or perform an emergency withdrawal that forfeits any pending rewards. The contract emits events for deposits, withdrawals, emergency withdrawals, and changes to a reference percentage. Only the contract owner can adjust the staking limit.
