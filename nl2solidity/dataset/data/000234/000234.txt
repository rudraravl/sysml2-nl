This contract implements a tokenized vault that adheres to the ERC-4626 standard, allowing users to deposit an underlying ERC-20 asset and receive vault shares in return, or redeem vault shares for the underlying asset. It maintains the total supply of vault shares and the balance of the underlying asset it holds. Callers can deposit a specific amount of the underlying asset to mint shares, or mint a specific amount of shares by depositing the required assets. Conversely, they can withdraw a specific amount of the underlying asset by burning shares, or redeem a specific amount of shares for the underlying asset. The contract provides preview functions to estimate the shares received or assets withdrawn for a given input. It also defines maximum limits for deposits, mints, withdrawals, and redemptions, with default behavior allowing unlimited operations unless overridden. Each deposit and withdrawal operation emits a corresponding event, `Deposit` or `Withdraw`, detailing the participants, asset amounts, and share amounts. The contract uses fixed-point math for precise conversions between assets and shares and includes a mechanism to mitigate inflation attacks using virtual shares.
