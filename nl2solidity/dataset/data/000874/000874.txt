This contract acts as a syndicate for managing and distributing ETH rewards from transaction tips to participants in the Stakehouse protocol. It maintains state regarding registered "knots" (validator keys), priority stakers, and the accumulated ETH rewards for both "free-floating" sETH stakers and "collateralized SLOT" owners. The contract owner has exclusive rights to register and de-register knots, add priority stakers, and update the priority staking end block. Any caller can stake sETH against registered knots, unstake their sETH, and claim their accrued ETH rewards. Collateralized SLOT owners can also claim their share of ETH rewards. The contract ensures that ETH rewards are accurately calculated and distributed, preventing re-entrancy during fund transfers. Events are emitted for significant actions like contract deployment, ETH updates, knot registration/de-registration, priority staker additions, and staking/unstaking/claiming of ETH, allowing off-chain systems to track the syndicate's activity.
