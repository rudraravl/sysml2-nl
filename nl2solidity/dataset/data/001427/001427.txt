This contract implements a fungible token called "Unity ExaGBP" with a fixed total supply of 100,000,000,000,000,000,000 units and 2 decimal places, represented by the symbol "eGBP". It maintains a record of each address's token balance and tracks allowances, which permit one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. The contract owner at deployment receives the entire initial token supply. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, or approve another address to transfer a specified amount of their tokens. A unique feature is that direct token transfers incur a transaction fee, calculated as the square root of one-tenth of the transfer amount (minimum 1 unit), which is sent to a predefined fee address (0xbba92e4a0642ce1c33da73d9012a296dfe0f076b). Transfers from an approved spender do not incur this fee. The contract also supports an `approveAndCall` function, allowing a caller to approve a spender and then immediately trigger a function call on the spender's contract, passing additional data. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events. The contract rejects any direct Ether sent to it.
