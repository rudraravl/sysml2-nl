This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token with a total supply of 1 trillion tokens and 9 decimal places. It maintains balances for all token holders, tracks allowances for spending, and manages several fee parameters. The contract features a transfer mechanism that applies a dynamic fee on transactions, which is split between a designated fee address and a marketing wallet. It integrates with Uniswap V2 for liquidity provision and token swaps. A cooldown mechanism is in place to restrict frequent buying and selling, especially during an initial "buy limit" period. The contract owner has control over enabling trading, adding initial liquidity to Uniswap, and adjusting the cooldown feature. Additionally, the owner can manually trigger token swaps for ETH and send accumulated ETH to the fee and marketing addresses. The contract emits events for token transfers, approvals, and updates to key parameters like the maximum buy amount, cooldown status, and fee rates.
