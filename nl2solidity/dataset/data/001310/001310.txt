This contract implements an ERC-20 token with a fixed total supply, named "ARABIAN SHARBI" ($ASHARBI), featuring a dividend distribution mechanism and anti-bot measures. The contract tracks token balances and allowances for transfers, similar to standard ERC-20 tokens. It maintains state for various fee percentages (reflection, liquidity, marketing) for both buy and sell transactions, along with accumulated fee counts. A `DividendDistributor` sub-contract manages dividend payments in a separate token (Sharbi) to holders based on their share. The contract also defines maximum transaction and wallet amounts, and implements an anti-sniping bot mechanism during an initial launch period. The owner can set various parameters, including fee percentages, maximum transaction/wallet limits, and enable/disable trading. Fees are collected in the contract and periodically swapped for ETH, with a portion used for liquidity provision and the remainder distributed as dividends or sent to a marketing wallet. Transfers trigger fee calculations, updates to dividend shares, and processing of dividend distributions.
