This contract provides a set of mathematical utility functions for signed integers. It does not maintain any on-chain state or track any permissions, as all its operations are pure computations. Callers can use this contract to determine the maximum or minimum of two signed numbers, calculate their average, or find the absolute unsigned value of a signed number. It also includes a generic ternary function for conditional evaluation. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or integrations with other protocols. The contract is designed to offer standard signed math operations that are not natively available in the language, focusing on gas efficiency and correctness for these specific calculations.
