This contract implements an ERC20 token designed to represent shares in a liquidity pool. It maintains the token's name, symbol, total supply, and individual account balances, along with allowances for spending. The contract is initialized once by its owner, setting the address of the associated liquidity pool. Only the designated owner can later update a `liquidityTracker` address, which is a separate contract that receives notifications about token movements. The core functionality allows the linked liquidity pool to mint new tokens to any account or burn tokens from any account. Standard ERC20 operations like `transfer`, `transferFrom`, `approve`, `increaseAllowance`, and `decreaseAllowance` are available to all token holders. Crucially, any token transfer, mint, or burn operation triggers a call to the `liquidityTracker` (if set), notifying it of tokens being added or removed from specific addresses. Ownership of the contract can be transferred through a two-step nomination and acceptance process. An event is emitted when the `liquidityTracker` is updated.
