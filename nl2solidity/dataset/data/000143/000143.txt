This contract provides foundational utilities for implementing EIP-712 typed structured data hashing and signing. Its purpose is to generate a unique domain separator and compute the final message digest for off-chain signatures, preventing replay attacks across different domains or chains. The contract stores immutable `name` and `version` strings, along with a cached domain separator, chain ID, and contract address to optimize repeated calculations. Callers can retrieve the EIP-712 domain parameters, including the name, version, chain ID, and verifying contract address. The core functionality involves an internal function to compute the EIP-712 domain separator and another to combine this separator with a provided struct hash to produce the final typed data hash, which can then be signed off-chain. There are no access controls, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms directly within this contract; it's designed as a base for other contracts to inherit and extend.
