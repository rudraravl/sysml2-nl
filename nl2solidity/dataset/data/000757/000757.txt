This contract facilitates interactions with a GMX staking ecosystem. It maintains no internal state itself, instead acting as a proxy to external GMX-related contracts. Callers can stake a specified amount of GMX tokens, which involves interacting with the GMX token contract and a reward router. Conversely, they can unstake GMX tokens through a similar process. The contract also allows users to claim any accumulated rewards from the reward router. Users can query their currently staked GMX amount, which is retrieved from an external reward tracker. Finally, the contract enables users to delegate their voting power to another address through the GMX DAO governance token contract. All operations are permissionless, and no fees are charged by this contract.
