This contract implements a timelock mechanism, allowing for delayed execution of administrative actions. It maintains a configurable delay period, a minimum and maximum allowed delay, a grace period for execution, and tracks a designated administrator and a pending administrator. The contract also keeps a record of all transactions that have been queued for future execution. The administrator can queue a transaction for execution at a specified future time, which must be at least the current delay period away. The administrator can also cancel a previously queued transaction. Once the scheduled execution time has passed, and within the grace period, the administrator can execute the transaction. Additionally, the administrator can immediately execute certain transactions without a delay. The contract emits events for changes in the administrator, pending administrator, delay, and for queuing, canceling, and executing transactions, allowing off-chain systems to monitor its state. Only the current administrator can queue, cancel, or execute transactions, and only the pending administrator can accept the admin role. The contract itself can set its own delay and pending administrator, implying a self-governing mechanism for these critical parameters.
