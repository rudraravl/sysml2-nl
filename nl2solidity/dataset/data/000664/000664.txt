This contract enables users to delegate their governance power, either for voting or proposing, to another address. It maintains a record of each user's chosen delegatee for both voting and proposition power, along with a delegation mode indicating whether they have delegated either or both types of power. Users can delegate both voting and proposition power simultaneously to a single address, or delegate each type independently. Delegation can be performed directly by the user or via a meta-transaction signed by the delegator, allowing a third party to submit the transaction on their behalf, provided it's within a specified deadline. The contract tracks the current governance power of any user, which includes their own balance and any delegated power they receive, scaled down for efficiency. Changes in token balances, such as transfers, automatically adjust the delegated power of affected delegatees. The contract emits an event whenever a delegation changes, providing transparency for off-chain services.
