This contract manages the distribution of airdrops to eligible recipients, primarily for tokens received by the contract itself. It maintains a record of valid Merkle roots, each associated with a specific claim type (ERC20, ERC721, ERC1155, or native currency), and tracks which individual claims within those distributions have already been processed. The contract owner can register new airdrop distributions by providing a Merkle root and claim type, which emits an `AirdropDistributed` event. The owner can also set an address for ERC1271 signature validation and initiate arbitrary external calls to claim airdrops on behalf of the contract, protected by a reentrancy guard. Any authorized claimant can then claim their allocated airdrop by providing a Merkle proof, which is validated against the registered Merkle root. Upon successful validation, the contract marks the specific claim as processed and transfers the corresponding assets (ERC20, ERC721, ERC1155, or native currency) to the recipient, emitting an `AirdropClaimed` event. The contract prevents double-claiming and ensures that only valid Merkle roots are used.
