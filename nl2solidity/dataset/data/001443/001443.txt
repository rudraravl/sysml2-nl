This contract manages a streaming payment system for builders, where funds are released over time. It maintains a record of each builder's streaming cap and the last time they withdrew funds. The contract owner, initially the deployer, has exclusive control over adding new builders, updating their streaming caps, and can transfer or renounce ownership. Builders can query their currently unlocked amount, which accrues based on their cap and a fixed frequency (defaulting to 30 days). Any builder with an active stream can withdraw their unlocked funds, provided the contract holds sufficient balance. The contract emits events for ownership transfers, builder additions, builder cap updates, and successful withdrawals, allowing off-chain systems to track these activities. The contract is designed to receive Ether, which funds the streaming payments.
