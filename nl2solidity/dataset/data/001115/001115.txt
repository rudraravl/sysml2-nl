This contract acts as a pseudo-minter for bridged Balancer (BAL) tokens on a child chain, distributing them to users based on their activity in liquidity gauges. It maintains a list of allowed gauge factories and tracks the total amount of BAL tokens already distributed to each user from specific gauges. The contract's owner can add or remove gauge factories from this allowlist, emitting `GaugeFactoryAdded` or `GaugeFactoryRemoved` events respectively. When a user requests their BAL, the contract queries the relevant gauge for the total amount they should receive, subtracts what has already been distributed, and transfers the difference to the user. This process involves checkpointing the user's activity in the gauge and verifying that the gauge originates from an allowed factory. Fees are collected from the distributed amount before transfer. The contract does not mint new tokens but rather distributes pre-existing bridged BAL.
