This contract defines a library of standard error messages, specifically for panic conditions in Solidity. It does not maintain any state, nor does it have any callable functions or operations. Its sole purpose is to provide a set of predefined `bytes` constants, each representing a specific type of panic error, such as assertion failures, arithmetic overflows, division by zero, or out-of-bounds array access. There are no access controls, events, fees, or integrations; it simply offers a standardized way to encode common panic reasons for use by other contracts, allowing for consistent error handling and off-chain interpretation of critical failures.
