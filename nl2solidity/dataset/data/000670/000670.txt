This contract acts as a strategy for distributing rewards by staking an underlying token into a designated staking contract. It stores the addresses of an incentives controller, a rewards administrator, the specific staking contract, and the underlying token that will be staked. The contract allows only the incentives controller to initiate a transfer operation, which involves staking a specified amount of the underlying token into the staking contract on behalf of a recipient. The rewards administrator has exclusive control over managing the contract's approval for spending the underlying token by the staking contract, enabling them to renew or revoke this approval. The contract also provides read-only functions to retrieve the addresses of the staking contract and the underlying token.
