This contract facilitates swapping tokens between different "vaults" by leveraging Curve's StableSwap pools. It maintains an `owner` address, which has special permissions, and keeps track of a constant `registry` address for interacting with Curve's registry. Users can initiate swaps either directly or by providing an EIP-2612 permit signature, allowing the contract to spend their vault tokens. The core functionality involves withdrawing tokens from a source vault, removing liquidity from a corresponding Curve pool, adding liquidity to a target Curve pool, and finally depositing the resulting tokens into a destination vault. A small portion of the swapped tokens can be optionally donated to the contract owner. The contract emits an `Orgin` event to track the source of a swap. It also provides functions to estimate the output of a swap without executing it. The owner can transfer ownership of the contract to a new address.
