This contract serves as an oracle providing the price of a Pendle LP token, integrating with Chainlink for base asset pricing and a Pendle PT Oracle for additional validation. It stores the Chainlink aggregator address, a stale period for price validity, the Pendle market address, a TWAP window, and the Pendle PT Oracle address. Callers can query the `spot` price of the LP token, which is derived by multiplying the validated Chainlink price of the underlying asset by the LP to asset rate from the Pendle market. The contract also provides a `getStatus` function to check the overall validity of the oracle's price. Access control is managed by an admin and a manager role; the admin can grant roles, and only a manager can authorize contract upgrades, provided the current oracle status is valid. The contract is upgradeable, allowing for future enhancements.
