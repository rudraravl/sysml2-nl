This contract implements a custom ERC-20 token, named PDEX, with additional access control features. It maintains a ledger of token balances for each address, and tracks allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract has a designated owner who can mint new tokens, manage a whitelist and blacklist of addresses, and set a "safe owner" address. Token transfers are subject to checks against these lists: addresses on the blacklist cannot send tokens, and transfers involving whitelisted addresses have special handling. The owner can perform multiple transfers at once and also pre-approve certain addresses on a decentralized exchange router. The contract also includes standard ERC-20 functions like `totalSupply`, `balanceOf`, `approve`, and `transferFrom`, all of which emit appropriate events for off-chain monitoring. It uses a SafeMath library to prevent arithmetic overflows, enhancing security.
