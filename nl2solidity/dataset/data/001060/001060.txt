This contract extends a base `PositionAction` to specifically manage leveraged positions within a CDP vault, likely interacting with Pendle-related liquidity pools. It maintains no unique state itself, relying on the inherited `PositionAction`'s state and external vault and pool contracts. Callers can initiate deposits and withdrawals of collateral into and from a specified vault, and also increase or decrease the leverage of a position. Increasing leverage involves an optional auxiliary action, followed by depositing all available collateral of a specific token into the vault. Decreasing leverage first withdraws collateral from the vault, then performs an optional auxiliary action to convert the withdrawn tokens, returning the final amount. Access control is inherited from the base contract, and no specific events are emitted by this contract directly, though underlying vault and pool operations would emit their own. The contract integrates with external `ICDPVault` and `IPoolAction` interfaces, and uses `SafeERC20` for secure token interactions.
