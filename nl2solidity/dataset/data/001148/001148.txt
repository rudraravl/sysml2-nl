This contract serves as a core component of a decentralized lending pool, enabling users to interact with various assets for lending, borrowing, and liquidation. It maintains state regarding individual user configurations, asset balances within the pool, and total supplies of each asset. Users can supply assets to the pool, withdraw their supplied assets, borrow assets against their collateral, and repay their outstanding loans. Additionally, the contract supports liquidation of undercollateralized positions and flash loans for arbitrage or other capital-efficient operations. Access to these functions is generally open to any caller, but internal logic and reentrancy guards prevent malicious re-execution. Before and after each major operation, the contract can trigger external hooks, allowing for custom logic or integrations with other protocols. The contract also tracks a reserve factor and flash loan premium, which are managed by an associated factory contract.
