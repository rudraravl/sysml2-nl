This contract provides a set of utility functions for calculating a user's financial position within a lending protocol. It does not maintain any state itself, but operates on data provided by other components of the protocol, such as reserve configurations, E-Mode categories, and user-specific configurations. The primary operation is `calculateUserAccountData`, which computes a user's total collateral and debt in a base currency, their average Loan-To-Value (LTV), average liquidation threshold, and health factor. It also offers `calculateAvailableBorrows` to determine the maximum amount a user can borrow based on their collateral and existing debt. Access to these functions is unrestricted as they are pure or view functions, performing calculations without altering any on-chain state. There are no events emitted, as the contract is purely for computation. The contract integrates with external price oracles to fetch asset prices and interacts with scaled balance tokens to retrieve user balances and debt. It handles E-Mode configurations to adjust LTV and liquidation thresholds for specific user categories.
