This contract creates a wrapped ERC-20 token that represents a share in a money market. It records the underlying asset, the market's controller, its interest rate model, and an initial exchange rate, along with standard ERC-20 token details like name, symbol, and decimal precision. The contract is initialized with these parameters and an administrator address. Initially, the contract creator temporarily holds administrative privileges during the setup process, but immediately after initialization, the specified administrator address takes over permanent control. This administrator is responsible for managing the token within the broader money market system. The contract itself does not define any further operations beyond its initial setup, relying on its inherited functionality for all other interactions.
