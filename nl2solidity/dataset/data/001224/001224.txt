This contract acts as a fixed-price oracle, providing a way to determine the equivalent value in Ether for a specified amount of any ERC-20 token. Upon deployment, it permanently stores a single, immutable price, representing the fixed value of one unit of an asset in Ether, scaled to 18 decimal places. Callers can query the contract by providing an ERC-20 token address and an amount of that token. The contract then calculates the corresponding Ether value by multiplying the provided amount by its stored fixed price and dividing by the token's decimal precision. This calculation always rounds down, which has a neutral effect whether used for debt or asset valuation. There are no access controls, events, or upgradeability features; its sole purpose is to provide this fixed-price conversion on demand.
