This contract manages the core supply and withdrawal functionalities within a lending protocol, tracking user deposits and their collateral status. It maintains detailed records of all supported assets, including their interest rates and liquidity, alongside individual user configurations indicating which assets they have supplied or borrowed, and which are designated as collateral. Callers can supply assets to the protocol, receiving corresponding aTokens, or withdraw previously supplied assets by burning their aTokens. Users can also designate or revoke an asset as collateral, subject to health factor validations to prevent undercollateralization. Additionally, users can set their "eMode" category, which influences borrowing power and collateral requirements. All supply and withdrawal actions, as well as changes in collateral status and eMode, emit events to keep off-chain systems updated. The contract ensures that all operations adhere to strict validation rules, preventing actions that would compromise the protocol's solvency or a user's health factor.
