This contract provides utility functions for reading and calculating information about a trading position within a decentralized exchange or similar financial protocol. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather operates on data structures representing a `Position`, `Market`, and `Price` that are passed into its functions. Its primary purpose is to compute various financial metrics for a given position, including associated fees, the execution price, and different forms of profit and loss (PnL) in USD, both capped and uncapped, and after accounting for price impact. There are no access control mechanisms as it's a stateless utility library. It does not emit events, handle fees directly, or integrate with other protocols beyond consuming their data structures. It serves as a computational helper, providing a structured `PositionInfo` output containing all calculated details.
