This contract provides utility functions for managing and distributing fees within a liquidity book system. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather operates on data structures passed to it. Its primary purpose is to assist in calculating and updating fee distributions, particularly in the context of flash loans. One function, `flashLoanHelper`, verifies that the correct amount of tokens was received during a flash loan and updates the total fees and protocol fees accordingly, reverting if an insufficient amount was received. Another function, `getTokenPerShare`, calculates the amount of tokens per share for a given total supply, considering both total fees and protocol fees. There are no access control mechanisms as it's a library, and it emits no events. It relies on external helper contracts for safe casting and token operations.
