This contract provides a utility for performing 512-bit multiplication and division operations on unsigned 256-bit integers, ensuring full precision even when intermediate products exceed 256 bits. It does not maintain any on-chain state or track any specific accounts. Callers can use two main functions: `mulDiv`, which calculates the floor of `(a * b) / denominator`, and `mulDivRoundingUp`, which calculates the ceiling of the same operation. Both functions require the denominator to be non-zero and will revert if the final result overflows a 256-bit unsigned integer. There are no access controls, permissions, or events emitted, as its sole purpose is to provide robust mathematical computations for other contracts. The contract is not upgradeable and does not integrate with other protocols; it is a pure mathematical library.
