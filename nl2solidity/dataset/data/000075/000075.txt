This contract implements a fungible token following the ERC-20 standard. It maintains a record of token balances for each address and tracks allowances, which permit one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. The contract stores the token's name, symbol, and a fixed decimal precision of 18. Callers can query the total supply, their own balance, or the allowance granted to a spender. They can transfer tokens directly from their balance, approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens, or transfer tokens from an account they have been approved to spend from. Token transfers and allowance changes emit corresponding events, allowing off-chain applications to track all token movements. The contract includes internal functions for minting and burning tokens, which are intended to be used by derived contracts to manage the token's supply. All operations include robust checks to prevent common errors like insufficient balances, invalid addresses, or exceeding allowances, reverting transactions when these conditions are not met.
