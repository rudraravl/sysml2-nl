This contract implements a standard ERC20 token with additional burn functionality. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks individual token balances for each address, along with allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Callers can query the token's name, symbol, decimals, total supply, and any account's balance or allowance. Token holders can transfer their tokens to another address, approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens, increase or decrease an existing allowance, and transfer tokens on behalf of another address if they have sufficient allowance. Additionally, token holders can burn their own tokens, reducing the total supply, or burn tokens from another account if they have been granted an allowance to do so. All token transfers, approvals, and burns emit corresponding events for off-chain tracking. The contract also includes an ownership mechanism, where the deployer is initially the owner, and this ownership can be transferred to another address.
