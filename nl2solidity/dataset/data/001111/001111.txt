This contract serves as a repository for various constant values used across a larger system. It does not maintain any mutable state, nor does it offer any functions or operations for callers to interact with. Its sole purpose is to define and expose fixed numerical values, such as a maximum timelock duration, a minimum timelock duration, a maximum queue length for markets, and a maximum allowable fee. There are no access controls, events, or complex mechanics like fees, math, or upgradeability associated with this contract, as it is purely a data store for immutable constants.
