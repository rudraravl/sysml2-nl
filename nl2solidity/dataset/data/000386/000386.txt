This contract provides a utility for calculating the value of a Uniswap V3 non-fungible liquidity position. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather queries external Uniswap V3 contracts to retrieve necessary information. Callers can query the total value of a specific liquidity position, identified by its unique token ID, at a given square root price. This total value includes both the principal amounts of token0 and token1 that would be returned if the position were burned, and any accumulated fees for both tokens. The contract also allows for separate queries to determine just the principal amounts or just the accumulated fees. All calculations are performed as view functions, meaning they do not alter the blockchain state. The contract integrates with the Uniswap V3 NonfungiblePositionManager, Uniswap V3 Pools, and various Uniswap V3 libraries for mathematical operations and address computation. There are no access control mechanisms as all functions are public view functions. No events are emitted.
