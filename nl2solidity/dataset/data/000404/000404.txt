This contract provides a mechanism for managing a temporary, transaction-specific lock. It doesn't maintain any persistent state on the blockchain. Its sole purpose is to allow a caller to transiently mark a specific "lock slot" as either unlocked or locked within the context of a single transaction. Callers can also check the current transient state of this lock slot. This mechanism is designed to be used for temporary, in-transaction control flow, such as re-entrancy protection, where the lock state is reset at the end of the transaction. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability features, as its utility is limited to transient state management within a single execution.
