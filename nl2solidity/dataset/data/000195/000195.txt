This contract implements a standard ERC721 non-fungible token, representing unique digital assets. It maintains a collection name "Mock ERC721" and symbol "MERC721", and for each token, it tracks its owner and a generic URI "aaa". Callers can mint new tokens to a specified address, burn tokens they own, or burn tokens from the zero address. Tokens can be transferred between addresses, with the caller needing to be approved or the owner of the token being transferred. The contract also allows querying the balance of tokens an address holds and the owner of a specific token ID. Before and after any token transfer, including minting and burning, the contract emits `BeforeTokenTransfer` and `AfterTokenTransfer` events, respectively, detailing the `from` address, `to` address, and `id` of the token involved. There are no special fees or upgradeability mechanisms.
