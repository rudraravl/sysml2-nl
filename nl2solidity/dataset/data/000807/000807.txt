This contract issues unique digital tokens, specifically NFTs, to users who purchase them. It maintains a record of how many NFTs have been minted in total and tracks the balance of each user's NFTs. The core functionality allows any user to buy an NFT by sending Ether to the contract. The price of an NFT is fixed at 100 USD, but the contract dynamically calculates the equivalent Ether amount required for purchase by querying an external exchange for the current Ether-to-USD price. If the exact Ether amount is not sent, the purchase fails. The contract integrates with an external "BadExchange" to determine the Ether price of one USD, which it then scales to find the Ether cost of an NFT. There are no special access controls beyond the standard ERC721 ownership and transfer mechanisms for the NFTs themselves. No events are explicitly emitted beyond those inherent to ERC721 minting.
