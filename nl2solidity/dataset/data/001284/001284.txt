This contract functions as a portfolio vault designed to manage and value loan-backed NFTs. It maintains state regarding its underlying asset token, a share token representing ownership in the vault, and references to external contracts for loans, loan NFTs, and a loan exchange. Key state variables track the vault's Net Asset Value (NAV) through a paginated computation process, including a cursor for progress, pending NAV, last finalized NAV, and its update time. It also tracks pending and claimable assets/shares for asynchronous deposits and redemptions. Callers can trigger NAV updates, approve deposits and redemptions, collect cashflows from loans, fund loans, manage which loans are included in NAV calculations, and interact with a loan exchange to buy or sell loan NFTs. Access control is managed through roles: a `GUARDIAN_ROLE` for critical configurations, `PORTFOLIO_MANAGER` for loan management, and `INVESTOR_MANAGER` for approving investor requests. Important events are emitted for NAV updates, deposit/redemption approvals, and configuration changes. The contract prevents re-entrancy, ensures NAV is fresh for sensitive operations, and includes mechanisms to rescue mistakenly sent tokens.
