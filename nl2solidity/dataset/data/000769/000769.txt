This contract facilitates the creation of a decentralized exchange limit order to swap USDC for WETH. It maintains no internal state beyond the addresses of external contracts it interacts with. Any caller can invoke the `createLimitOrder` function, providing the amount of USDC they wish to swap and the maximum acceptable WETH price. Upon invocation, the contract first transfers the specified USDC amount from the caller to itself, along with an execution fee in native currency. It then forwards both the execution fee and the USDC to a designated order vault. Finally, it creates a limit swap order on an external exchange router, specifying the desired output WETH amount based on the provided USDC amount and maximum price, and setting itself as the receiver of the swapped WETH. The contract also includes a fallback function to receive native currency, presumably for execution fee refunds.
