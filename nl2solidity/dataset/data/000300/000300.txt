This contract provides a set of utility functions for safely transferring both native ETH and ERC20 tokens between addresses, primarily focusing on robust error handling and ZKsync compatibility. It does not maintain any persistent state itself, but rather orchestrates transfers and approvals. Callers can initiate standard ETH transfers, transfer all ETH held by the contract, or attempt "force" transfers that create a temporary vault contract if the initial transfer fails, ensuring the ETH reaches its destination even if the recipient is a contract that reverts. For ERC20 tokens, it supports `transferFrom` (requiring prior approval), direct `transfer` from the contract's balance, and `approve` operations, including a retry mechanism for tokens like USDT that require resetting approval to zero before setting a new one. All functions are internal, meaning they are intended to be called by other contracts, not directly by external users. Important events are emitted when a single-use ETH vault is created during a force transfer, allowing off-chain services to track these fallback mechanisms. The contract guards against various transfer failures by reverting with specific error messages.
