This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "Doge Eats Ramen" with a fixed total supply. It tracks token balances for each address and allows standard ERC-20 operations like transferring tokens and approving spending. The contract owner has special privileges, including the ability to open trading on a Uniswap V2 pair, set launch phases, and manage a list of "bot" addresses. Transfers are subject to a variable fee structure, which changes based on whether the contract is in a "duringLaunch" or "duringLaunchPhase2" state. A portion of these fees is sent to two designated wallets, and another portion is burned, reducing the total supply. When tokens are bought from the Uniswap pair, a cooldown mechanism can be enabled, restricting subsequent buys for a short period. The contract also includes functionality to automatically swap a portion of its token balance for ETH and distribute it to the fee wallets. The owner can manually trigger these swap and send operations.
