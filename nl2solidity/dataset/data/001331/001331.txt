This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token with additional features for managing trading and fees. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract's owner has special privileges, including the ability to open trading, remove transaction limits, and manage certain fee parameters. Transfers between non-owner addresses may incur a dynamic tax, which is collected by the contract and can be swapped for ETH and sent to a designated tax wallet. Trading is initially closed and must be opened by the owner, which also initializes a Uniswap V2 liquidity pair. The contract also includes anti-bot measures and transfer delay mechanisms. Important events are emitted for token transfers and approvals, as well as for ownership changes.
