This contract serves as a read-only information provider for Cream Finance lending positions. It does not maintain any internal state beyond constant addresses for ETH and cETH. Callers can query the contract to retrieve various details about a user's Cream loan, including their collateral-to-debt safety ratio, the addresses and amounts of their collateral and borrowed assets (denominated in ETH), and individual token balances. Additionally, the contract provides functions to fetch current prices and collateral factors for specified cTokens, as well as comprehensive information about cTokens including supply/borrow rates, exchange rates, market liquidity, and total supply/borrow. All functions are public and view-only, meaning anyone can access this information without needing special permissions or paying transaction fees, and no state changes occur on-chain. The contract integrates with Cream Finance's core contracts to retrieve real-time data.
