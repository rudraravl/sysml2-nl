This contract acts as a remote executor, enabling a designated cross-chain controller to relay instructions and execute transactions on its behalf. It maintains two key pieces of state: the address of the cross-chain gateway it uses for communication, and the interoperable address of its authorized controller. Only messages originating from the configured gateway and controller are processed. The contract allows the controller to reconfigure the gateway and controller addresses, but this operation can only be initiated by the contract itself, ensuring that reconfigurations are a result of a valid instruction from the controller. Upon any update to the gateway or controller, a `CrosschainControllerSet` event is emitted. The contract processes incoming messages by decoding a payload that specifies a call type (single, batch, or delegatecall) and an execution type, then performs the corresponding on-chain operation. Any attempt by an unauthorized entity to interact with the contract's restricted functions will result in an `AccessRestricted` error.
