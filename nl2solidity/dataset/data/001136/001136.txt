This contract defines a data structure called `LiquidityChunk` which efficiently stores information about a specific range of liquidity within a concentrated liquidity automated market maker (AMM). Each `LiquidityChunk` packs a lower tick, an upper tick, and a liquidity amount into a single 256-bit unsigned integer. The contract provides functions to create a new `LiquidityChunk` from its constituent parts, and to retrieve the individual lower tick, upper tick, and liquidity values from an existing chunk. It also offers operations to add liquidity to a chunk, add or update its lower tick, and add or update its upper tick. All these operations are pure functions, meaning they do not modify any on-chain state; they simply compute and return a new `LiquidityChunk` value. There are no access control mechanisms, events, or integrations with other protocols, as this contract is solely a utility for data packing and unpacking.
