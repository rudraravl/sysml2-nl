This contract manages fee distribution, incentives, and bad debt repayment within the WiseLending ecosystem. It maintains a list of supported pool tokens, tracks which of these are Aave tokens and their underlying assets, records the total bad debt across the system, and individually for each position (identified by an NFT ID), and stores accumulated incentive amounts for designated incentive owners and beneficial users. The contract also tracks a proposed incentive master for ownership transfers. A designated master address has extensive control, including setting bad debt repayment incentives, configuring Aave token flags, adjusting pool fees in WiseLending, and managing the list of supported pool tokens. The incentive master can increase incentive amounts for two specific incentive owners, who can then claim these incentives. Users can claim accumulated incentives for specific tokens. The contract allows for claiming fees from WiseLending, either for individual pools or in bulk, which are then distributed to beneficial users or used for bad debt repayment. Beneficial users, designated by the master, can claim their allocated fees. Users can repay bad debt for a specific position, optionally receiving an incentive from the collected fees, or repay without an incentive. All significant actions, such as fee changes, incentive increases, and bad debt repayments, emit events for off-chain monitoring.
