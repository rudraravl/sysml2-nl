This contract manages a token distribution system, allowing for the controlled release of rewards based on Merkle proofs. It maintains a name, a designated reward token, and tracks two Merkle cycles: a current, approved cycle and a pending, proposed cycle, along with the root of the previous cycle. It also stores claimed amounts per user and position, a list of rewards, and distribution details including cycles per distribution, blocks per distribution, and a vesting schedule. The contract allows an owner to pause operations and manage lists of addresses authorized to propose and approve new Merkle roots, as well as addresses authorized to distribute rewards. Callers can query the contract for various cycle and distribution parameters.
