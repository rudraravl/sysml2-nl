This contract provides a robust role-based access control system, allowing for fine-grained permissions management. It maintains a mapping of roles, identified by unique `bytes32` values, to the accounts that possess them, and also tracks an administrative role for each defined role. Callers can query if an account has a specific role using `hasRole`. Roles can be granted to or revoked from accounts by calling `grantRole` or `revokeRole`, respectively, but only if the caller possesses the administrative role assigned to the target role. An account can also voluntarily renounce a role it holds using `renounceRole`. The contract emits `RoleGranted`, `RoleRevoked`, and `RoleAdminChanged` events to signal changes in role assignments or administrative relationships, enabling off-chain monitoring. The `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` is a special role that acts as the initial administrator for all other roles and is its own administrator.
