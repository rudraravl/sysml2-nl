This contract creates a wrapped version of an existing ERC20 token, allowing users to convert between the underlying token and its wrapped representation. It maintains a record of the underlying token's address and tracks the total supply of the wrapped token. Callers can deposit a specified amount of the underlying token, which is then transferred from their account to the contract, and in return, they receive an equivalent amount of the wrapped token minted to their address. Conversely, users can burn their wrapped tokens to withdraw the corresponding amount of the underlying token, which is then transferred from the contract back to their account. There are no specific access controls beyond the standard ERC20 transfer and approval mechanisms for the underlying token, and no events are explicitly emitted by the wrapping and unwrapping operations. The contract also includes a recovery mechanism to mint wrapped tokens if the contract holds more underlying tokens than its current wrapped token supply.
