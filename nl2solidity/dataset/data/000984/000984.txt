This contract serves as a base for managing interactions with the Pendle protocol, specifically for power farming. It tracks various Pendle-related addresses and configurations, including the Pendle token, vePendle, voter contracts, and an oracle hub. The contract maintains a list of active Pendle markets and information about their associated child contracts and compounding strategies, including reserved amounts for compounding and reward tokens. It also stores a public `exchangeIncentive` value. The contract allows for receiving ETH, and its owner can change the `exchangeIncentive`. It provides internal mechanisms to synchronize supply for individual Pendle markets and a public function to synchronize all active markets. The contract emits events for various actions such as receiving ETH, changing the exchange incentive, withdrawing liquidity, exchanging rewards, adding new Pendle markets, updating reward tokens, changing mint fees, locking Pendle, claiming Arbitrum rewards, increasing reserved amounts for compounding, claiming vote rewards, and withdrawing locks. Access to certain functions is restricted to the contract owner or specific child contracts, and some operations are limited to the Arbitrum network.
