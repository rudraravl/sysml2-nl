This contract implements a set of mathematical functions to manage and calculate interest for financial quotas. It doesn't maintain any on-chain state itself but provides pure and view functions for other contracts to use. The core purpose is to compute cumulative interest indices based on a non-compounding rate over time, calculate accrued interest on a given quoted amount, determine changes in revenue based on a quota rate and change, and bound quota increases to a predefined limit. There are no access controls, events, or direct integrations with other protocols, as it serves as a utility library for calculations. The contract focuses on precise arithmetic for financial operations, utilizing fixed-point numbers (RAY) and handling potential overflows or underflows through careful casting and unchecked blocks where appropriate.
