This contract implements an ERC-20 compatible token named "CD1CAPUSDC" with the symbol "cd1capUSDC". It maintains the standard ERC-20 state, including token balances for each address, total supply, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract is initialized with a designated "vault" address, which is permanently stored. Only this vault address has the exclusive permission to mint new tokens to any recipient or burn existing tokens from any account, provided the recipient or account is not the zero address. All other standard ERC-20 operations like transferring tokens between users, approving spending, and checking balances are available to any caller. The contract emits `TokenMinted` and `TokenBurned` events when tokens are created or destroyed by the vault, in addition to the standard ERC-20 `Transfer` and `Approval` events. The contract also has an owner, initially the deployer, who can transfer ownership to another address or renounce it.
