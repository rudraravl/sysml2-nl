This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token called "MOSAIC" with a total supply of 100 million tokens. It incorporates a reflection mechanism where a portion of each transaction is burned, increasing the value of remaining tokens for holders. The contract tracks token balances for all addresses, including a separate accounting for "excluded" addresses that do not participate in the reflection. It also manages allowances for token spending. A key feature is a trading cooldown period of 35 seconds for transfers between non-owner and non-Uniswap pair addresses, which can be enabled or disabled by the contract owner. The owner can also exclude or include addresses from the reflection mechanism and update the associated Uniswap V2 pair address. All token transfers and approvals emit standard ERC-20 events.
