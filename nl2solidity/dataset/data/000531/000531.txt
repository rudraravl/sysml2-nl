This contract implements a Wrapped Ether (WETH) token, allowing users to convert native Ether into an ERC-20 compatible token and back. It maintains each user's WETH balance and tracks allowances granted to other addresses for spending WETH on their behalf. Users can deposit Ether to mint WETH tokens, which are credited to their balance, or withdraw WETH to receive native Ether back, burning the tokens. Any Ether sent directly to the contract without calling a specific function will automatically trigger a deposit. Users can also transfer their WETH to others or approve another address to spend a specified amount of their WETH, which can then be transferred by the approved address. All deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The total supply of WETH tokens is always equal to the total Ether held by the contract.
