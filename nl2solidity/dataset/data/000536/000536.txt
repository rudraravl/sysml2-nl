This contract serves as a data provider for a lending protocol, offering a centralized point to query various pieces of information about reserves and user positions within the protocol's main Pool. It maintains a reference to an `IPoolAddressesProvider` contract, which it uses to locate the primary `IPool` contract. Callers can retrieve a list of all supported reserve tokens and their symbols, including special handling for MKR and ETH, or a list of all associated aTokens. The contract also provides detailed configuration data for any given asset, such as its decimals, loan-to-value ratio, liquidation thresholds, and various flags indicating its status (e.g., active, frozen, borrowing enabled). Furthermore, it allows querying specific reserve data like current liquidity and borrow rates, total supply of aTokens, and total outstanding debt. For individual users, it can fetch their balances of aTokens, stable debt, and variable debt, along with their stable borrow rates and collateral status. All functions are read-only, providing information without altering any on-chain state, and there are no access control mechanisms beyond the inherent immutability of the `ADDRESSES_PROVIDER` set at deployment.
