This contract acts as a router for cross-chain token swaps, integrating with the Stargate protocol and a SmartFinanceSwap contract. It maintains the addresses of the Stargate router and the associated SmartFinanceSwap contract, along with a mapping of Stargate pool IDs to their corresponding token addresses. The primary operation is `sendSwap`, which is exclusively callable by the SmartFinanceSwap contract. This function initiates a cross-chain token swap via Stargate, requiring an ETH payment for cross-chain messaging fees, and emits a `CrossChainMessageSent` event. The contract also implements `sgReceive`, a callback function invoked by the Stargate router upon successful cross-chain token transfer. This function transfers the received tokens to the SmartFinanceSwap contract and then calls its `receivePayload` function, emitting a `CrossChainMessageReceived` event. The contract owner can configure the Stargate router, SmartFinanceSwap address, and pool-to-token mappings. The owner can also pause and unpause the contract, and withdraw any ERC20 tokens or ETH held by the contract.
