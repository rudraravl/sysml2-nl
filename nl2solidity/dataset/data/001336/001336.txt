This contract serves as the official deposit mechanism for the Ethereum 2.0 beacon chain. It maintains a Merkle tree of all deposits, tracking the `deposit_count` and the `branch` nodes of the tree. Callers can submit a deposit by invoking the `deposit` function, providing their public key, withdrawal credentials, signature, and a pre-calculated `deposit_data_root` hash, along with a minimum of 1 ETH. The contract verifies the provided data against the sent ETH and reconstructs the `deposit_data_root` to ensure integrity. Upon a successful deposit, the contract updates its internal Merkle tree, increments the `deposit_count`, and emits a `DepositEvent` containing all relevant deposit information. Callers can query the current Merkle root of all deposits using `get_deposit_root` and the total number of deposits using `get_deposit_count`. The contract also implements the ERC-165 interface, allowing external systems to verify its supported interfaces. There are no special access controls; anyone can deposit, and all view functions are publicly accessible.
