This contract acts as a transaction monitoring and logging tool for a Gnosis Safe. It maintains a mapping of transaction hashes to nonces for tracking purposes. When a transaction is about to be executed by the Safe, the contract records detailed information about it, including the destination, value, data, operation type, gas parameters, signatures, and the executor, emitting a `TransactionDetails` event. It also stores the transaction's nonce. After the transaction's execution, it emits a `GasUsage` event, indicating whether the transaction was successful and clearing the stored nonce for that hash. Similarly, for module-initiated transactions, it records the transaction details and the module involved, emitting a `ModuleTransactionDetails` event. The contract does not revert on its fallback function, ensuring it doesn't interfere with Safe upgrades.
