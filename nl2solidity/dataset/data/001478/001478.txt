This contract implements a BEP20-compatible token with several additional features. It maintains a total supply of tokens and individual balances for each address. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, and approve other addresses to spend tokens on their behalf, which can then be transferred using `transferFrom`. The contract owner has special permissions: they can mint new tokens up to the predefined total supply, pause and unpause all transfers, and manage two lists of addresses: "pairs" and "blacks". When the contract is not paused, transfers are restricted if either the sender or receiver is on the "blacks" list. When paused, only addresses on the "pairs" list can perform transfers. The contract also includes a fallback function that rejects any direct Ether transfers. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, and ownership changes are also logged.
