This contract provides a robust access control mechanism, allowing the definition of various roles and the assignment of accounts to those roles. It maintains a record of which accounts hold which roles, and importantly, it enables the enumeration of all accounts associated with a specific role. Callers can query the total number of members for any given role, retrieve a specific member by its index, or fetch an array containing all members of a role. The contract internally manages the addition and removal of accounts from roles, ensuring that the enumerable lists are always consistent with the access control state. This enumeration feature is primarily intended for off-chain querying due to potential gas costs if used within state-changing transactions.
