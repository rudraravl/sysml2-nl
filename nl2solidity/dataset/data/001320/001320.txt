This contract implements a token with a dividend distribution mechanism, rewarding holders with another token, MAGIC. The contract maintains balances for each token holder, allowances for spending, and various fee and exemption settings. Key operations include standard ERC20 token transfers, approvals, and balance queries. The contract owner has elevated privileges to manage authorizations, transfer ownership, and configure dividend distribution criteria.

A notable mechanic is the automatic distribution of MAGIC tokens as dividends. When transfers occur, a portion of the transaction amount is collected as fees. These fees are then used to buy MAGIC tokens, which are subsequently distributed among eligible token holders based on their share of the total supply. The contract also features anti-whale measures like maximum transaction and wallet limits, which can be exempted for specific addresses. It integrates with a DEX router to facilitate token swaps for dividend distribution and liquidity management. Events are emitted for token transfers and approvals, allowing off-chain services to track token activity.
