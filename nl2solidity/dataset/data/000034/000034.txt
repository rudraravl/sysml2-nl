This contract implements a timelock mechanism for executing operations, ensuring a minimum delay before actions can be performed. It maintains a record of scheduled operations, their states (unset, waiting, ready, done), and a minimum delay period. Callers can propose single or batched operations, which are identified by a unique hash, specifying target addresses, values, and calldata, along with an optional predecessor operation and a salt. Only accounts with the `PROPOSER_ROLE` can schedule operations, which become executable after the defined delay. Operations can be canceled by accounts with the `CANCELLER_ROLE` before execution. Once an operation's delay has passed, accounts with the `EXECUTOR_ROLE` can execute it, triggering the specified calls. The contract emits events for scheduled, executed, and canceled operations, as well as changes to the minimum delay. The minimum delay itself can only be updated by the timelock contract through a scheduled and executed operation, preventing immediate changes to this critical parameter. The contract also supports receiving ERC721 and ERC1155 tokens.
