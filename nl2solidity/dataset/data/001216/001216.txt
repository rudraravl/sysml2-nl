This contract defines a Kinked Rate Model, which calculates interest rates based on asset utilization. It stores four immutable parameters at creation: a minimum interest rate, two different slopes for calculating interest (one for utilization below an optimal point and one for utilization above it), and the optimal utilization percentage itself. The contract does not maintain any mutable state. Callers can query the instantaneous borrow interest rate for a given total amount of borrows and total assets, or calculate the total interest accrued over a period given a last update timestamp, total borrows, and total assets. There are no access controls; anyone can query these functions. The contract uses fixed-point arithmetic for precision and defines a constant for seconds in a year to standardize rate calculations.
