This contract manages a token staking and reward distribution system. Users can stake a specific LP token for varying lock durations to earn rewards, which are distributed from a pool of multiple ERC-20 tokens. The contract tracks each user's staked balance, locked earnings, and unlocked tokens. Staked tokens are locked for a set period, and early withdrawal of earned tokens incurs a penalty, with a portion burned and the remainder distributed to other lockers. The contract owner has extensive control over system parameters, including setting reward tokens, lock periods, multipliers, and treasury addresses. Certain addresses, designated as "minters," can add new reward tokens. Users can enable auto-compounding of rewards and set their preferred slippage. The contract emits events for significant actions like locking, withdrawing, and reward payments, allowing off-chain monitoring. It includes mechanisms for pausing operations and recovering accidentally sent ERC-20 tokens.
