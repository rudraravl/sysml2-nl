This contract implements a lending platform where users can collateralize assets and borrow tokens. It maintains state for lending pools, borrow pools, and user positions, which are represented by NFTs. Users can deposit and withdraw both ETH and ERC20 tokens, either into public pools that earn interest or into "solely" pools that keep funds private and do not earn interest but guarantee withdrawal. Borrowing is also supported for ETH and ERC20 tokens, requiring collateral. All deposit, withdraw, and borrow operations are tied to a specific NFT ID, representing a user's position. The contract enforces health checks on positions after state changes to ensure solvency and uses a "syncPool" mechanism to update borrow rates and share prices based on pool utilization and a Lending Automated Scaling Algorithm (LASA). Access control is in place for certain "on behalf" functions, restricted to an AaveHub. Events are emitted for solely deposits and withdrawals, allowing off-chain monitoring. A notable mechanic is the ability to use lending shares to pay back debt, and the contract caps share price increases to prevent excessive APRs within a single transaction.
