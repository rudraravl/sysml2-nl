This contract acts as an oracle for GMX, providing price and market information. It stores the addresses of the GMX DataStore, GMX Synthetic Reader, and a Chainlink oracle upon deployment. Its primary function is to retrieve the amount of tokens received from a swap within a GMX market. Callers provide details about the market, including the market token, index token, long token, short token, the input token, and the amount of the input token. The contract then consults the Chainlink oracle to get the current prices for the index, long, and short tokens, and uses the GMX Synthetic Reader to calculate the output amount. The contract ensures that all provided addresses are valid and that the input token is either the long or short token of the specified market.
