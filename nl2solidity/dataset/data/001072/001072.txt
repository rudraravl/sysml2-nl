This contract is an ERC-4626 compliant vault designed to automatically compound rewards from an Aura Finance reward pool. It allows users to deposit an underlying asset, receiving shares in return, and to withdraw their assets by redeeming shares. The contract tracks the total assets it manages within the Aura reward pool. Two administrative roles, `VAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and `VAULT_CONFIG_ROLE`, control configuration parameters like incentive rates for claimers and lockers, and oracle addresses. Anyone can trigger the `claim` function to collect accumulated rewards (BAL and AURA) from the Aura pool, which are then partially distributed as incentives to the caller and a designated locker rewards address, with the remaining rewards converted to the underlying asset and redeposited into the Aura pool. This compounding action increases the vault's total assets, benefiting all share holders. The contract emits a `Claimed` event upon successful reward compounding. The contract relies on external Chainlink and custom price oracles to determine token values for reward conversion.
