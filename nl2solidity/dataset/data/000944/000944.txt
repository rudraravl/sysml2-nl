This contract implements a fungible token, named "WELL" with 18 decimal places, and a fixed total supply of 5 billion tokens. It maintains records of token balances for each address and tracks allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. Users can transfer tokens, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and perform transfers on behalf of approved owners. The contract also incorporates a delegation mechanism, allowing token holders to delegate their voting power to another address, either directly or via a signed message. It tracks historical voting power at different block numbers, enabling queries for past vote balances. All token transfers, approvals, and delegation changes emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract uses EIP-712 for signed approvals and delegations, enhancing security and user experience.
