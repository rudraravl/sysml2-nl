This contract implements an ERC-20 compatible token named "ETHWitcher" with the symbol "ETHWitcher" and 18 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks individual token balances for each address. The contract also manages allowances, permitting one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. The deployer of the contract is designated as the owner, with exclusive rights to certain administrative functions. A notable mechanic is a 6% tax applied to most token transfers, which is directed to a designated ETH wallet. However, transfers initiated by the contract owner are exempt from this tax. Additionally, there's a "bot address" check that limits the transfer amount for specific addresses if their balance falls within a certain range. The owner can also trigger a "buy back" mechanism, which increases the total supply and the owner's balance, and can prepare the contract for a presale by adjusting a fee parameter. Ownership can be transferred to a new address or renounced entirely. All token transfers and allowance approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring.
