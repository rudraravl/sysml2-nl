This contract provides a suite of common mathematical utility functions for unsigned 256-bit integers. It doesn't maintain any on-chain state or involve access control; all operations are pure computations. Callers can perform basic arithmetic like finding the maximum or minimum of two numbers, calculating their average, or performing ceiling division. More complex operations include a high-precision multiplication and division function (`mulDiv`) that handles potential overflows and offers different rounding modes (down, up, or toward zero), and a square root function (`sqrt`) that also supports various rounding options. There are no events emitted, fees, or integrations with other protocols. The contract is designed to be a stateless library of mathematical tools.
