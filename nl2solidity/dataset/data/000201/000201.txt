This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token with an additional permit functionality. It maintains a total supply of tokens, and tracks individual token balances for each address, as well as allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Upon deployment, the entire initial token supply is assigned to the deployer. Callers can transfer tokens they own to other addresses, or transfer tokens from an address that has granted them an allowance. They can also approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens. The contract also supports a `permit` function, allowing a token holder to authorize a spender to move their tokens off-chain using a signed message, which is then verified on-chain, updating the allowance without requiring a direct transaction from the token holder. All token transfers and allowance approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring.
