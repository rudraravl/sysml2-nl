This contract acts as a factory for creating new liquid staking derivative networks, designed to integrate with a simulated Stakehouse ecosystem. It maintains references to several mock Stakehouse components, including an account manager, transaction router, universe, slot registry, and a savETH registry, along with a mock dETH token. Upon deployment, it initializes these mock components and configures the mock transaction router with the slot registry and universe. The contract allows authorized callers to deploy new liquid staking derivative networks, optionally specifying a commission and whether to deploy a house gatekeeper, and injects itself as the dependency injector for these new networks. It ensures the mock Stakehouse dependencies are correctly linked within the ecosystem it creates.
