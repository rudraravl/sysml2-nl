This contract acts as a SuperPool, aggregating multiple underlying pools that hold a specific ERC20 asset. It functions as an ERC20 token itself, representing shares in the SuperPool. The contract maintains the total assets it controls, a fee percentage, a fee recipient address, and a maximum capacity for total assets. It also manages ordered queues for depositing and withdrawing assets across its underlying pools, along with individual caps for each pool. The contract owner has administrative control, including pausing operations, adding/removing pools, modifying pool caps, reordering queues, and setting the SuperPool's overall asset cap and fee recipient. Fee updates are subject to a timelock mechanism, requiring a delay before activation and a deadline for acceptance. An 'allocator' role, distinct from the owner, can reallocate assets between underlying pools. Users can deposit assets to receive shares or mint shares directly, and similarly withdraw assets or redeem shares. All deposit and withdrawal operations accrue fees, which are minted to the fee recipient. Important events are emitted for pool additions/removals, cap changes, fee updates, allocator status changes, and all deposit/withdrawal actions, allowing off-chain monitoring. The contract includes reentrancy guards and various checks to prevent invalid operations, such as exceeding caps or attempting zero-value transfers.
