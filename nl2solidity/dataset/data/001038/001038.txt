This contract functions as an ERC-4337 Paymaster, enabling users to sponsor gas fees for their UserOperations and withdraw funds. It maintains a record of ETH available for withdrawal by each user and tracks nonces to prevent replay attacks on withdrawal requests. The contract owner has privileged access to manage deposits and withdrawals from the associated EntryPoint contract, including staking and unstaking. Users can initiate withdrawals by providing a signed request from the contract owner, specifying the asset (currently only ETH), amount, a unique nonce, and an expiry. The contract validates these requests, ensuring sufficient balance and preventing replayed or expired requests. After a UserOperation, the contract transfers the remaining sponsored funds, including any unspent gas, back to the user. An event is emitted for each validated withdrawal.
