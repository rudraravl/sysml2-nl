This contract acts as a module for Gnosis Safe accounts, enabling authorized parties to perform token swaps and manage gas tanks on behalf of a Safe. It maintains a record of gas tank balances for each Safe, a list of designated administrators, and a list of "ScoreLab" addresses. It also tracks allowances for specific token pairs, defining the maximum amount and expiration time for a swap. Any address can deposit Ether into a Safe's gas tank, emitting a Deposit event. Only ScoreLab addresses can initiate refunds from a Safe's gas tank, emitting a Refund event, or execute various types of token swaps (multi-swaps, mega-swaps, simple swaps, Uniswap swaps, and 0x swaps) through the Augustus Swapper. These swaps require a pre-approved allowance for the token pair, which can be set by any caller for their own Safe. Administrators can manage other administrators and ScoreLab addresses.
