This contract acts as a governance mechanism that integrates with a separate TimelockController contract to manage proposal execution. It maintains the address of the associated TimelockController and maps proposal identifiers to their corresponding timelock operation IDs. Callers can query the current state of a proposal, including whether it's queued or executed by the timelock, and retrieve the estimated execution time for queued proposals. The core operations involve queuing a successful proposal to the timelock, which schedules its execution after a minimum delay, and executing a previously queued proposal through the timelock. Proposals can also be canceled, which, if already queued, will cancel the corresponding timelock operation. The contract itself is the only entity permitted to update the linked TimelockController address, ensuring that changes to the timelock are subject to governance proposals. An event is emitted whenever the TimelockController address is changed.
