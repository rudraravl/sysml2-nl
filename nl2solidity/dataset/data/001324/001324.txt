This contract provides a utility for verifying digital signatures. It does not maintain any state on-chain. Callers can generate a unique message hash by providing a timestamp. This message hash can then be used in conjunction with a digital signature and the expected signer's address to verify if the signature is valid and was indeed produced by that specific signer for the given timestamp. The contract exposes a public function to perform this verification, returning a boolean indicating success or failure. There are no access controls, fees, or events associated with its operations. Its primary purpose is to offer a reusable signature verification mechanism.
