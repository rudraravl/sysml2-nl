This contract implements a restricted ERC20 token, representing shares in a vault, where transfers are controlled by a role-based access system. It maintains the token's name, symbol, total supply, and individual balances, along with specific roles: `GUARDIAN_ROLE` for overall administration, `WHITELISTER_ROLE` for managing shareholder eligibility, `SHAREHOLDER_ROLE` for transfer permissions, and `MINTER_ROLE`/`BURNER_ROLE` for issuing and destroying tokens. A designated vault address holds the `MINTER_ROLE` and `BURNER_ROLE` to manage the token supply, and an underlying asset address is tracked. Callers can query the token's name, symbol, and balances, and transfer tokens between eligible addresses. Only addresses with the `MINTER_ROLE` can create new tokens, and only those with the `BURNER_ROLE` can destroy them. Transfers between addresses are restricted; both the sender and recipient must possess the `SHAREHOLDER_ROLE`, unless the transfer is a mint (from address zero) or burn (to address zero). The contract also provides functions to detect and explain transfer restrictions, and allows a guardian to update the associated vault. Important events are emitted when the vault address is updated. The contract also includes a mechanism to rescue accidentally sent tokens to a predefined recovery address.
