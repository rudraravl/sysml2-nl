This contract extends a governance system to enable flexible voting on proposals. It maintains a record for each proposal, tracking the total votes cast "Against," "For," and "Abstain," along with the amount of voting power each individual voter has utilized. Callers can query the current vote distribution for any proposal and check if a specific account has already voted. The core functionality allows voters to cast their votes in two ways: a standard "nominal" vote where all remaining voting power is assigned to a single option (Against, For, or Abstain), or a "fractional" vote. In a fractional vote, a voter can distribute their voting power across the Against, For, and Abstain options by providing specific weights. This is particularly useful for smart contract delegates managing voting power from multiple sources. The contract ensures that a voter cannot exceed their available voting power for a proposal and that quorum is reached if the combined "For" and "Abstain" votes meet the required threshold. A proposal succeeds if "For" votes outnumber "Against" votes.
