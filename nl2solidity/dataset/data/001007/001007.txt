This contract serves as a vault for various ERC-20 tokens, including wrapped Ether, enabling users to deposit, withdraw, and transfer token shares. It maintains a record of each user's token shares and the total shares and amounts for each token, using a rebase mechanism to manage the share-to-amount ratio. The contract supports flash loans, allowing borrowers to temporarily access tokens for a fee, and integrates with external strategies to generate yield. The owner of the contract can set a target percentage of a token's total balance to be invested in a strategy and can queue and activate new strategies after a time delay. Profits and losses from strategies are reflected in the token's total amount. All deposits, withdrawals, transfers, flash loans, and strategy-related actions emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract includes access control for certain operations, allowing only the owner to manage strategies and master contracts to approve transfers on behalf of users.
