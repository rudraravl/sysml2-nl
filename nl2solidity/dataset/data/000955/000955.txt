This contract defines the core structure and interfaces for an MToken, a token representing a share in a lending pool. It tracks various financial metrics like total supply, total borrows, and reserves, along with individual user balances for tokens and outstanding borrows. The contract maintains an administrator who can manage critical parameters such as the interest rate model, reserve factor, and the overarching Comptroller contract that governs inter-MToken operations. It also supports a pending administrator mechanism for secure admin role transfers. Users can mint new MTokens by supplying underlying assets, redeem MTokens for underlying assets, borrow underlying assets, and repay their borrows. Liquidation of undercollateralized loans is also supported. All significant financial actions and administrative changes emit events, allowing off-chain systems to monitor the market's state. The contract is designed with an upgradeable architecture, allowing its core logic to be updated through a delegate pattern managed by the administrator.
