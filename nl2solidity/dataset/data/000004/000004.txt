This contract establishes a robust access control system centered around a single, highly privileged `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`. It maintains the current administrator's address and a configurable delay period for critical administrative changes. The contract enforces a two-step process for transferring the `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` to a new address, requiring the current administrator to initiate the transfer and the new administrator to explicitly accept it after a specified delay has passed. This delay can also be changed by the current administrator, again with a scheduled waiting period. Regular role management functions like `grantRole` and `revokeRole` are restricted for the `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` to prevent accidental or malicious changes. Events are emitted when a default administrator transfer or delay change is scheduled, accepted, or canceled, allowing off-chain systems to track these critical state transitions. The contract ensures that only one account holds the `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` at any given time, and it cannot be managed by other roles.
