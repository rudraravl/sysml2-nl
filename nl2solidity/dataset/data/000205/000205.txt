This contract implements an ERC20-compliant token with an added pause mechanism. It maintains the standard token balances and allowances, along with a single owner address and a boolean flag indicating whether the token operations are active. The contract is initialized with a total supply, and the deployer becomes the owner. The owner can pause all token transfers and approvals by calling `stop()`, or resume them by calling `start()`. When paused, any attempts to `approve()`, `transfer()`, or `transferFrom()` will fail. Only the designated owner has permission to pause or unpause the contract; all other callers are rejected. The contract does not emit any specific events for pausing or unpausing, but standard ERC20 events for transfers and approvals are emitted when those operations successfully occur.
