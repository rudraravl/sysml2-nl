This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token called "ETHDRIVE" with a total supply of 100 billion tokens. It incorporates a reflection mechanism, where a portion of transaction fees is distributed to holders, and also includes marketing, liquidity, and burn fees. The contract maintains the token balances of all addresses, allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others, and tracks addresses excluded from fees or rewards. It also records the last purchase and sell times for each wallet, and has a configurable "standartValuation" period. The contract owner can set buy and sell fee rates, enable or disable trading, and manage the transfer functionality. It integrates with Uniswap V2 for liquidity provision and swapping, and includes a mechanism to automatically swap a portion of collected fees for ETH and add it to the liquidity pool. The contract emits standard ERC-20 Transfer and Approval events, and an OwnershipTransferred event when the contract ownership changes. The owner can transfer ownership to another address but cannot renounce it.
