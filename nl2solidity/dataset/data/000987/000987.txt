This contract implements a standard fungible token, similar to an ERC-20 token. It maintains a total supply of tokens, along with individual balances for each address and allowances that permit one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. Callers can query the token's name, symbol, decimals, total supply, and any address's balance. They can transfer tokens they own to another address, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens. Approved spenders can then transfer tokens from the owner's balance to another address. Allowances can be increased or decreased. All token transfers and allowance changes emit corresponding events for off-chain tracking. The contract prevents transfers to or from the zero address, burning more tokens than an account holds, or spending more than an approved allowance.
