This contract extends the ERC6909 standard to enable a specific burning mechanism for tokens. It maintains a record of token balances for different token types (identified by `id`) held by various addresses, and also tracks allowances granted by token holders to operators for specific token types. The primary operation this contract enables is the burning of a specified `amount` of a particular `id` token from a `from` address. This burn can be initiated by the `from` address itself, or by an authorized operator. An operator is either explicitly designated by the `from` address or holds a sufficient allowance for that specific token type. If an allowance is used, it is reduced by the burned amount, unless it's an unlimited allowance. The contract does not define how tokens are minted or transferred, focusing solely on the burning process within the ERC6909 framework.
