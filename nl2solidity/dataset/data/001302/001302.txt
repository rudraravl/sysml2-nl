This contract implements a standard ERC20 token, allowing for the creation and management of a fungible digital asset. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks individual token balances for each address. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other accounts to spend their tokens on their behalf, and then transfer tokens from an approved account. The contract also supports increasing or decreasing existing allowances to mitigate race conditions. All token movements, including transfers and approvals, emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract uses safe math operations to prevent common arithmetic overflow and underflow vulnerabilities, ensuring robust and predictable behavior.
