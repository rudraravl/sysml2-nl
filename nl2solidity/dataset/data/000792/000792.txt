This contract defines data structures and utility functions for calculating and representing detailed information about a trading position within a decentralized exchange. It maintains no on-chain state itself, but rather provides a blueprint for how position-related data, including its properties, associated fees, and various profit and loss metrics, should be organized and interpreted. Specifically, it defines structures for an `ExecutionPriceResult` to capture price impact and the final execution price, a comprehensive `PositionInfo` that aggregates position details, fees, and PnL, and a `GetPositionInfoCache` to hold market data, collateral token prices, and funding rates necessary for these calculations. The contract offers no direct callable functions, access control, or events, serving purely as a data definition and calculation helper for other contracts that manage actual trading positions.
