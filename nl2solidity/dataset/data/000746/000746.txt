This contract provides a set of mathematical utility functions for common operations. It does not maintain any state on-chain, nor does it have any owners, access control, or events. Its sole purpose is to offer pure functions for calculating the maximum of two 24-bit signed integers, the minimum of two 24-bit signed integers, and the integer square root of an unsigned 256-bit integer. Callers can invoke these functions to perform these specific mathematical computations, receiving the result directly. There are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or integrations with other protocols.
