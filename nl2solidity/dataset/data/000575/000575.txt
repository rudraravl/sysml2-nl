This contract is a non-transferable stable debt token designed to track user borrowing positions at a stable interest rate within a lending protocol. It maintains the principal balance for each user, their individual stable borrowing rate, the timestamp of their last interaction, the overall average stable rate across all borrowers, and the timestamp of the total supply's last update. The contract can be initialized by a designated Pool contract, setting its underlying asset, an incentives controller, and ERC20 metadata. Only the Pool contract is authorized to mint new debt tokens to a user, which updates the user's principal balance, their stable rate, and the global average stable rate, emitting a Mint event. Similarly, only the Pool can burn debt tokens, reducing a user's principal and adjusting the global average rate, emitting a Burn event. Users can query their current debt balance, their stable rate, and the last update time. The contract explicitly disallows all standard ERC20 transfer and approval functions, ensuring the debt tokens remain non-transferable.
