This contract acts as an upgradeable proxy, allowing for the future modification of its underlying logic without changing its on-chain address. It is initialized with a designated administrator address during deployment. The contract maintains the address of this administrator and the address of the implementation contract it delegates calls to. Any call made to this proxy contract is forwarded to the current implementation contract. The administrator has exclusive control over updating the implementation contract to a new version. This design enables seamless upgrades to the contract's functionality over time, ensuring that the contract can adapt to new requirements or fix bugs without requiring users to migrate to a new address.
