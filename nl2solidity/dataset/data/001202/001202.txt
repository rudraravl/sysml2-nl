This contract facilitates the shutdown and liquidation of NFT collections deemed illiquid, distributing the proceeds to token holders. It maintains parameters for each collection undergoing shutdown, including the total votes cast, the required quorum, and the Sudoswap pool address used for liquidation. Users can initiate a shutdown process for a collection if its total supply is below a certain threshold, then vote by depositing their collection tokens into the contract. Once a quorum of votes is reached, the contract owner can execute the shutdown, transferring the collection's NFTs from a Locker contract to a newly created Sudoswap pool for liquidation. The contract receives ETH from these liquidations, which then becomes claimable by voters proportionally to their deposited tokens. The contract owner can pause or unpause operations, and a Locker manager can prevent a collection from being shut down. Important events are emitted for each stage of the shutdown process, from initiation to claim.
