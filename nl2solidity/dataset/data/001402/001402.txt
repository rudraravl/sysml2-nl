This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token with dynamic transaction taxes and anti-whale mechanisms, designed for initial launch and subsequent trading. It maintains a total supply, individual token balances, and allowances for spending. The contract owner has special privileges, including the ability to open trading, remove transaction limits, and manually trigger token swaps for ETH. Transfers between non-owner addresses are subject to variable buy and sell taxes, which decrease after a certain number of transactions. These taxes are collected into the contract and can be swapped for ETH, which is then sent to a designated tax wallet. There are also limits on the maximum transaction amount and maximum wallet size for non-excluded addresses, and a transfer delay mechanism that restricts transfers to one per block for new holders. The contract integrates with Uniswap V2 for liquidity provision and token swapping. Events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and updates to the maximum transaction amount.
