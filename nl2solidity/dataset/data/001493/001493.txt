This contract implements a BEP20-compatible token with a fixed total supply, named "DiamondHand" with symbol "DHand" and 9 decimal places. It incorporates a reflection mechanism where a portion of each transaction is taxed and distributed proportionally to all token holders, increasing their balances without direct transfers. The contract maintains the total supply, individual token balances in a reflective format, and allowances for spending. It features access control, allowing the contract owner to set the tax fee, maximum transaction amount, and maximum wallet token holdings. The owner can also exclude specific addresses from transaction fees and has the ability to temporarily lock and unlock ownership of the contract. Transfers are subject to a maximum transaction limit and a maximum wallet holding limit, with exceptions for the owner and a specific address. A dynamic anti-dumping multiplier is applied to fees based on the time elapsed since the contract's start, increasing the effective tax for early sales. All token transfers and approvals emit standard BEP20 events.
