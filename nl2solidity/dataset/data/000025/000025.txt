This contract acts as a bridge for a specific ERC-20 token, enabling its transfer between different blockchain environments. It maintains custody of the designated ERC-20 token. The contract is initialized with the address of the ERC-20 token it will manage. Callers can initiate a "send" operation, which involves transferring a specified amount of the ERC-20 token from their account to the bridge contract, effectively locking it. Conversely, a "receive" operation allows the contract to transfer a specified amount of the custodied ERC-20 token to a designated recipient, effectively unlocking it. The contract ensures that transfers are handled safely using a secure ERC-20 library. There are no explicit access control mechanisms beyond the internal nature of the send and receive operations, implying they are invoked by a higher-level bridging logic. No events are explicitly emitted by this contract.
