This contract implements a basic fungible token, similar to an ERC-20, but with a key difference: operations will return `false` on failure instead of reverting the transaction. The contract maintains a total supply of tokens, and tracks the balance of each token holder, along with allowances granted by holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Upon deployment, the entire initial supply is assigned to the deployer. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens. A `transferFrom` function allows approved addresses to move tokens on behalf of the owner. Additionally, a `mint` function allows anyone to increase the total supply and assign new tokens to a specified address. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events to allow off-chain tracking of token activity. There are no fees, upgradeability, or integrations with other protocols.
