This contract implements a simple flash loan mechanism, allowing users to borrow a specified amount of a single asset for a very short duration, typically within a single transaction. The contract does not maintain any persistent state itself, but rather operates on external reserve data passed into its functions. Callers can initiate a flash loan by providing the asset, the amount, a receiver address, and additional parameters. The contract first validates the request, then transfers the requested amount to the receiver. The receiver must then execute an arbitrary operation and, crucially, return the borrowed amount plus a calculated premium to the contract within the same transaction. If the repayment, including the premium, is not successfully transferred back to the contract, the entire transaction will revert. A `FlashLoan` event is emitted upon successful completion, detailing the receiver, initiator, asset, amount, and premium. The contract relies on external data structures for reserve state and supply information, which it updates to reflect the loan and repayment.
