This contract provides a set of utility functions for interacting with other addresses on the blockchain, primarily focusing on secure and robust low-level calls. It does not maintain any persistent state itself. Callers can reliably send Ether to a recipient, ensuring the transaction succeeds or reverts with a clear error, and perform general function calls, static calls, or delegate calls to other contracts. These operations include an option to transfer Ether alongside the call. The contract ensures that calls to non-contract addresses are explicitly flagged and that any revert reasons from target contracts are propagated. It also includes helper functions to verify the success of low-level calls, bubbling up revert reasons or indicating a generic failure. There are no access control mechanisms as all functions are internal utilities.
