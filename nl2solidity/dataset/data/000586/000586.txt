This contract establishes a foundational access control mechanism, designating a single account as the "owner" with exclusive permissions. Upon deployment, the contract records the deployer's address as the initial owner. It maintains the current owner's address as its primary state. Callers can query the current owner's address. The owner has the ability to transfer ownership to a new address, which must not be the zero address, or to renounce ownership entirely, leaving the contract without an owner. Any change in ownership, including the initial assignment, is broadcast via an event, indicating both the previous and new owner. Functions within other contracts that inherit this one can restrict their execution to only the current owner.
