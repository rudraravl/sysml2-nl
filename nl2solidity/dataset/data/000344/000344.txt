This contract implements a Uniswap V3 liquidity pool, enabling decentralized exchange between two tokens. It maintains the current price, tick, and liquidity of the pool, along with historical data for oracle functionality. Users can provide liquidity by calling `mint`, specifying a price range (tickLower, tickUpper) and an amount of liquidity. This operation requires the user to deposit the corresponding amounts of token0 and token1, which are calculated based on the current price and the specified range. Liquidity providers can also `burn` their positions to remove liquidity and receive their share of the underlying tokens and accumulated fees. The `collect` function allows liquidity providers to claim accrued fees and tokens from their positions. The core `swap` function facilitates token exchanges, where callers specify the direction (zeroForOne), the amount to swap, and a price limit. Swaps update the pool's price, liquidity, and fee growth. The contract also supports flash loans via `flash`. Access to `mint`, `burn`, `collect`, `swap`, and `flash` is protected by a re-entrancy guard. The contract emits events for `Mint`, `Burn`, `Collect`, `Swap`, `Flash`, `Initialize`, and `IncreaseObservationCardinalityNext` to track key operations and state changes. The contract is initialized by a factory, which sets immutable parameters like tokens, fee, and tick spacing.
