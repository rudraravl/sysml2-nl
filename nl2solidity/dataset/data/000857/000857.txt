This contract facilitates the creation and management of LP tokens for ETH deposits into validator staking pools. It maintains a record of the total number of unique LP tokens issued, the maximum and minimum ETH amounts allowed per validator stake, and a mapping between BLS public keys (representing validators) and their corresponding LP tokens. Users can rotate their staked ETH from one LP token to another, provided both associated BLS keys are in the `INITIALS_REGISTERED` lifecycle status and the old LP token has not been interacted with recently. This rotation involves burning the specified amount of old LP tokens and minting new ones. The contract emits events for ETH withdrawals, LP token burning, and both new and existing LP token issuance, allowing off-chain systems to track these actions. It integrates with a `StakehouseAPI` and `IDataStructures` to check validator lifecycle statuses and uses an `LPTokenFactory` to deploy new LP tokens.
