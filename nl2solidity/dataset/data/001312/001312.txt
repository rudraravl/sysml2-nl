This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "UP" with the symbol "$UP". It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks the balance of each token holder, along with allowances granted by token owners to other addresses for spending their tokens. Upon deployment, the contract mints a fixed initial supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens to the address that deployed it. Any token holder can transfer their tokens to another address, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens on their behalf. The approved spender can then transfer those tokens from the owner to another address. The contract emits `Transfer` events whenever tokens are moved and `Approval` events when allowances are set or changed, allowing off-chain applications to monitor token activity. There are no special access controls beyond standard ERC-20 permissions, and no fees, upgradeability, or external integrations are present.
