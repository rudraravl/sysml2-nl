This contract acts as a financial ledger for a system of loans, tracking balances and transactions for each loan in a specified ERC20 currency. It maintains a count of all loans created, the immutable currency used across all loans, and detailed account balances for each loan, along with a chronological record of all financial entries. Callers can query the balance of any specific account within a loan, retrieve a normalized balance (where certain accounts are presented as positive values), and fetch individual or a range of entries for a given loan. Access to modify the ledger is restricted to authorized guardians. The contract facilitates deposits of the currency into a loan's cash account from an external address and withdrawals from a loan's cash account to an external address, recording each as a ledger entry. It also supports internal transfers between accounts within a loan. Every new entry created emits an event, detailing the transaction.
