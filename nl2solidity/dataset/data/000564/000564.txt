This contract provides a library for performing percentage calculations with two decimal places of precision, where 100.00% is represented by a factor of 10,000. It does not maintain any state on-chain. Callers can compute a percentage of a given value using `percentMul`, which multiplies a value by a percentage, or `percentDiv`, which divides a value by a percentage. Both operations round results, with values of 0.5 or greater rounding up, and less than 0.5 rounding down. There are no access controls, permissions, or events, as it is a purely mathematical utility. The contract includes overflow checks to prevent erroneous calculations. It does not integrate with other protocols or have upgradeability mechanisms.
