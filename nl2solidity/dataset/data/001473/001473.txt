This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "MYAX" with the symbol "MYAX". It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks the balance of MYAX tokens held by each address. Callers can view the total supply, their own balance, and the name, symbol, and decimal precision of the token. Token holders can transfer MYAX tokens to other addresses, and they can approve other addresses (spenders) to transfer a specified amount of their tokens on their behalf. Spenders can then execute these approved transfers. The contract emits `Transfer` events when tokens are moved between accounts and `Approval` events when allowances are set or changed, allowing off-chain applications to track all token movements and approvals. Upon deployment, an initial supply of MYAX tokens is minted and assigned to the address that created the contract.
