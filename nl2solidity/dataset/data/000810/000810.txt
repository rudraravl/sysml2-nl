This contract manages user balances, allowing deposits and withdrawals. It maintains a record of each user's balance. Users can deposit Ether into the contract, increasing their recorded balance. They can also initiate a withdrawal of a specified amount, which reduces their balance and transfers the Ether to them. Additionally, the contract supports withdrawals authorized by an off-chain signature, where a user signs a message indicating an amount to withdraw, and any caller can then submit this signature to execute the withdrawal on behalf of the signer. If a withdrawal is attempted for an amount exceeding the user's balance, the transaction will revert with an error indicating the insufficient funds. The contract does not emit any events for these operations.
