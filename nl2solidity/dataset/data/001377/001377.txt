This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token called "Shibarium Art" (SHIBART) with a fixed total supply and a reflection mechanism. It tracks token balances for each address, along with allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract has an owner who can control various parameters. It integrates with a Uniswap V2 router to facilitate token swaps for ETH and maintain liquidity. A key feature is its dynamic transaction fees, which can differ for buys and sells, and are collected into the contract to be swapped for ETH and sent to a marketing address. The contract also enforces maximum transaction amounts and wallet size limits to prevent large-scale manipulation, and includes a bot blacklist. Trading can be enabled or disabled by the owner, and certain addresses can be excluded from fees. The owner can also manually trigger token swaps and ETH transfers from the contract. Ownership can be transferred or renounced, and all significant actions like transfers and approvals emit events.
