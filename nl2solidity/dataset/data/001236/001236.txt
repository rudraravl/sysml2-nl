This contract serves as an authorized mechanism for updating borrow rates within a linked liquidity protocol. It maintains a record of the last time each token's borrow rate was updated. The contract is initialized with the address of the liquidity protocol it manages, a maximum allowed percentage change for rate updates, and a cooldown period between updates for any given token. Only two designated team multisig addresses are permitted to call the rate update functions. Callers can update borrow rates for either version 1 or version 2 rate models, providing the token address and new rate at utilization kinks. Before applying any update, the contract verifies that the rate model version matches, the cooldown period has passed for that token, and the proposed rate change does not exceed the allowed percentage. Successful updates trigger an event logging the token, old rates, and new rates.
