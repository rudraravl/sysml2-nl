This contract manages multiple lending pools, each identified by a unique ID, allowing users to deposit assets, borrow against them, and repay debt. It maintains state for each pool, including its asset type, current interest rate model, deposit and borrow totals (in both assets and shares), and whether it's paused. The contract also tracks individual user deposits in shares and position-specific borrow shares. Only the contract owner can set default fees, minimum borrow/debt amounts, and update the registry and fee recipient addresses. Pool owners can pause their specific pools, set caps, and propose new rate models, which undergo a timelock period before activation. Deposits and withdrawals are handled by any caller, while borrowing and repaying debt are restricted to a designated position manager. Interest accrues on borrowed assets, and fees are collected. All significant actions, like pool initialization, deposits, withdrawals, borrows, and repayments, emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract integrates with a registry for module addresses, a risk engine for valuations, and an oracle for asset prices. It uses share-based accounting for deposits and borrows, and includes re-entrancy guards by updating state before transfers.
