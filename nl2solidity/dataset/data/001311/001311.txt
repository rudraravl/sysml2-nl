This contract implements a BEP20-compatible token with integrated dividend distribution and anti-whale mechanisms. The token, named "WTFUCK TOKEN" with symbol "$WTFUCK", has a fixed total supply and maintains balances for each address. It tracks allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract owner has extensive control, including setting transaction and wallet size limits, exempting addresses from fees or limits, adjusting various fees (liquidity, marketing, rewards, extra sell fee), and changing fee recipient addresses. A dividend distribution system is integrated, allowing holders to receive rewards in a specified reward token (ARB). Dividends are processed periodically or on demand, and the contract can automatically swap a portion of its token balance for ETH and add liquidity, as well as distribute ETH to marketing wallets and the dividend distributor. A blacklist feature allows the owner to prevent specified addresses from participating in transfers. Trading can be enabled or disabled by the owner, and a "rugNPCs" function allows the owner to blacklist all addresses that have transferred tokens to the contract.
