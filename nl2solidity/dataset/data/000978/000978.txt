This contract manages the creation and configuration of lending pools for various tokens. It maintains detailed state for each pool, including its borrowing rates, collateral factors, maximum deposit limits, and internal algorithm parameters. Only a designated master address can create new pools, set their parameters, or verify isolation pools. New pools are initialized with specific multiplication and collateral factors, and their borrowing rate algorithms are configured with initial pole values, delta, and bounds. A special function exists for creating "Curve Pools," which additionally interact with an external security module. The contract also allows the master to update existing pool parameters like collateral factors and maximum deposit amounts, and to adjust the borrowing rate algorithm's stepping direction and bounds. All pool creation and parameter adjustments are restricted to the master address, ensuring centralized control over the lending ecosystem.
