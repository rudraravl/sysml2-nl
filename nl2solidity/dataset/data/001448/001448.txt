This contract serves as a simple fee collector, designed to signal when fees should be gathered for a specific asset. It does not hold any funds itself, nor does it manage any internal state. The contract exposes a single external function, `collectFees`, which any caller can invoke. When `collectFees` is called with an asset address and an amount, it emits a `FeeShouldCollect` event. This event communicates to off-chain systems or other contracts that a specified amount of a particular asset is due for collection, but the contract itself does not perform the collection or transfer of these fees. There are no access controls, special permissions, or complex mechanics like upgradeability, security guards, or integrations; its sole purpose is to emit this notification.
