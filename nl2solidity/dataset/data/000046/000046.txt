This contract extends a standard governance mechanism by integrating with an AccessManager, allowing the governor to execute operations with time delays. It maintains a configurable base delay for all operations and an execution plan for each proposal, detailing whether individual actions within a proposal are routed through the AccessManager and their associated delays. Callers can propose actions, which are then evaluated to determine the total delay required based on the base delay and any additional delays imposed by the AccessManager for specific target functions. The contract allows the governor to ignore AccessManager restrictions for certain functions, which can be configured by a governance proposal. Once a proposal is queued, some of its operations might be scheduled with the AccessManager, potentially making them cancellable by AccessManager guardians. When executed, operations are either directly called or routed through the AccessManager, with a check to ensure the required delay has passed and, for AccessManager-controlled operations, that the correct nonce is used. Events are emitted when the base delay is changed or when AccessManager ignore settings are updated.
