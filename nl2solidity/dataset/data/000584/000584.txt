This contract provides a foundational utility for other contracts by offering a standardized way to retrieve the sender of a transaction and its associated data. It maintains no persistent state of its own. Its primary operations are two internal, view-only functions: one to return the address of the transaction's sender and another to return the raw transaction data. There are no external functions for callers to interact with directly, nor does it implement any access control, events, fees, or integrations. Its purpose is purely to abstract away the direct use of `msg.sender` and `msg.data`, particularly to facilitate compatibility with meta-transaction systems where the account initiating the transaction might differ from the logical sender.
