This contract serves as an on-chain oracle for token prices, specifically providing the price of a given token denominated in Wrapped Ether (WETH). It maintains a single piece of state: the address of a pool factory, which is set once during initialization. Callers can query the price of any token by providing its address, and the contract will retrieve this information by interacting with the designated pool factory to find the relevant swap pool, then querying that pool for the token's price in WETH. Additionally, the contract allows anyone to retrieve the address of the configured pool factory. The contract is designed to be upgradeable, meaning its implementation logic can be changed in the future without deploying a new contract address.
