This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "Mew Inu" with the symbol "MEW" and 18 decimal places, having a fixed total supply of 70 billion tokens. Upon deployment, all tokens are minted and assigned to the contract creator. Token holders can view their balance, transfer tokens to other addresses, and approve other addresses to spend a specified amount of their tokens on their behalf. Approved spenders can then transfer tokens from the approving address to a recipient. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing off-chain applications to track these activities. The contract creator initially holds all tokens and has no special privileges beyond that of a regular token holder, as the `Ownable` contract's `transferOwnership` function is incorrectly set to `private`, effectively making the `newComer` variable (initialized to the deployer) the immutable owner for the `onlyOwner` modifier, but no functions actually use this modifier.
