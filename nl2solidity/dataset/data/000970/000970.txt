This contract serves as a price oracle for the WstETH token, providing its value in ETH. It maintains immutable references to an external WstETH token contract and an external Chainlink price feed for stETH to ETH. Upon deployment, it stores the decimal counts for both WstETH and the stETH price feed, and calculates their difference. Callers can query the contract for the current ETH value of WstETH, which is derived by multiplying the stETH equivalent of 1 WstETH (obtained from the WstETH contract) by the latest stETH/ETH price from the Chainlink feed, then adjusting for decimal differences. The contract also exposes the standard 18 decimals for its own price output and allows callers to retrieve specific historical round data or the latest round data directly from the underlying stETH/ETH price feed, along with the calculated WstETH price. There are no fees, special access controls, or upgradeability mechanisms; it's a read-only data aggregator.
