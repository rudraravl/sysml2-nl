This contract serves as a verification check to ensure that the owners of specific incentive gauges are designated growth multisig addresses. It stores configuration data for a Silo protocol instance, including its name, and tracks whether the check was skipped, along with the address and owner of any gauge that fails the check. Callers can query the contract for a descriptive name of the check, a success message, or an error message if the check fails. The primary operation involves executing the check, which retrieves configured gauges for protected, collateral, and debt share tokens from a hook receiver. If no gauges are configured, the check is skipped and considered successful. Otherwise, it verifies that the owner of each configured gauge is the predefined growth multisig address. If any gauge owner does not match, the check fails, storing the non-compliant gauge and its owner.
