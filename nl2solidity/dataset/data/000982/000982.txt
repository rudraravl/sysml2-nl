This contract acts as a manager for a leveraged yield farming strategy, allowing users to enter and exit positions. It maintains state regarding a minimum deposit amount, whether the farm is active or shut down, and maps unique key IDs to underlying Wise Lending NFTs, also tracking whether a position uses Aave. Users can enter the farm by providing WETH or ETH, specifying leverage and an allowed spread, which opens a leveraged position and reserves a unique key ID for them. The contract mints new Wise Lending NFTs as needed or reuses available ones. Users can exit the farm by providing their key ID, which closes their position and returns the underlying NFT to an available pool. The contract owner has special permissions to change the minimum deposit amount and to shut down or reactivate the farm, preventing new entries. All ETH sent directly to the contract is forwarded to a master address. Key events are emitted for farm entries, exits, minimum deposit changes, farm status changes, and ETH receipts, allowing off-chain monitoring. Users can also manually adjust their positions by paying back or withdrawing shares, with a check to ensure the debt ratio remains acceptable after withdrawals.
