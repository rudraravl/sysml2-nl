This contract facilitates the migration of two existing tokens, $NFTX and $FLOOR, into a new $FLAYER token. It stores the addresses of these three ERC20 tokens and defines fixed conversion ratios for $NFTX and $FLOOR to $FLAYER. Callers can initiate a swap by specifying a recipient address and indicating whether they wish to burn their $NFTX, $FLOOR, or both. The contract will then take the caller's entire balance of the selected tokens, burn the $FLOOR tokens, transfer the $NFTX tokens to a burn address, calculate the corresponding $FLAYER amount based on the predefined ratios, and transfer the new $FLAYER tokens to the specified recipient. An event is emitted for each successful swap, detailing the amounts of $NFTX, $FLOOR, and $FLAYER involved. The contract owner has the exclusive ability to pause and unpause the swap functionality, preventing or allowing migrations as needed.
