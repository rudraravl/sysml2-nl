This contract acts as a central gateway for a Paydefi system, implementing the Diamond Standard (EIP-2535) for upgradeability. It maintains a record of whitelisted swap providers and stores a global protocol fee rate, along with token-specific protocol fees. The contract's primary purpose is to serve as a proxy, forwarding all incoming calls to various "facet" contracts that contain the actual business logic. It supports standard ERC-165, ERC-173 (ownership), IDiamondCut, and IDiamondLoupe interfaces. The contract owner, set during deployment, has exclusive control over upgrading the contract's functionality by adding, replacing, or removing facets, and can transfer ownership. Any received Ether is accepted. The contract emits an event whenever its structure is modified via a diamond cut, allowing off-chain systems to track changes.
