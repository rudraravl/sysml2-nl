This contract is a yield farming platform that allows users to stake ERC-20 LP tokens to earn MEOW tokens. It maintains a list of staking pools, each associated with a specific LP token, an allocation point determining its share of MEOW rewards, and tracking variables for reward calculation. For each user, the contract records their staked amount and their accumulated reward debt. The contract owner, or a designated governance contract, can add new staking pools and adjust their MEOW allocation points. Users can deposit LP tokens into a pool, which updates their staked amount and automatically harvests any pending MEOW rewards. Similarly, users can withdraw their staked LP tokens, also triggering a reward harvest. An emergency withdrawal function allows users to retrieve their staked LP tokens without claiming pending rewards. The contract calculates MEOW rewards based on a per-block rate, with a bonus multiplier for an initial period, and mints MEOW tokens to the contract itself for distribution. A special provision exists for a developer fee, which can be claimed by the developer address after a vesting period. All deposits, withdrawals, and emergency withdrawals emit events for off-chain monitoring.
