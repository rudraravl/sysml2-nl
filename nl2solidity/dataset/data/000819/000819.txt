This contract serves as the core of a decentralized governance system, managing proposals and voting. It maintains a record of each proposal, including its voting start and end times, and whether it has been executed or canceled. Users can propose new actions by providing target addresses, values, and calldata, which are then hashed to create a unique proposal ID. Once a proposal is active, users can cast votes, either directly or via signed messages, with optional reasons and parameters. The contract tracks the state of each proposal (pending, active, succeeded, defeated, executed, canceled) and allows for the execution of successful proposals, which involves calling the specified target contracts with their respective data and values. A special `onlyGovernance` modifier restricts certain functions to be callable only through successful governance proposals, ensuring that critical operations are subject to community approval. Events are emitted for proposal creation, execution, and vote casting, enabling off-chain monitoring of the governance process. The contract also includes mechanisms to receive ERC721 and ERC1155 tokens, and a `relay` function to recover mistakenly sent assets.
