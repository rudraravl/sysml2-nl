This contract facilitates the exchange between StableR INRS tokens and USDC tokens, aiming to peg the INRS token's value to the Indian Rupee. It maintains the contract owner's address, the addresses of the StableR INRS and USDC tokens, and a mapping of token balances held by the contract. The contract also stores the current exchange rate between INRS and USDC, which is primarily determined by Chainlink oracle price feeds for INR/USD and USDC/USD. Users can deposit MATIC or other ERC20 tokens into the contract. The owner has exclusive permission to withdraw any deposited MATIC or ERC20 tokens, including INRS and USDC, from the contract. The owner can also manually set the INRS/USDC price, though it's typically updated using the Chainlink oracle. The core functionality allows users to exchange StableR INRS for USDC and vice versa, with the exchange rate derived from the oracle feeds. The contract does not emit any specific events beyond the standard ERC20 transfer events.
