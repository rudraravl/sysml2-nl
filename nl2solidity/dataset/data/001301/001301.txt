This contract manages boost ratios for users participating in a reward system, influencing how their rewards are calculated. It maintains a record of the contract owner, additional administrators, and whether the contract is currently paused. Crucially, it stores individual boost ratios for each user, along with global base, volume, TVL (Total Value Locked), and auto-compounding boost ratios. The contract interacts with an external rewarder to update user rewards and a compounder to check user auto-compounding status. The owner or an administrator can pause or unpause the contract, add or remove administrators, and set the addresses of the rewarder and compounder. They can also adjust the global boost ratios for volume, TVL, and auto-compounding. A key operation allows administrators to set specific boost ratios for multiple users simultaneously, which triggers an update of their pending rewards. The contract also includes a mechanism for the owner to recover accidentally sent ETH or ERC20 tokens. Important events are emitted when ownership is transferred, the contract is paused/unpaused, administrators are updated, or boost ratios are changed.
