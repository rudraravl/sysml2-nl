This contract facilitates a peer-to-peer betting game where two participants, Side A and Side B, can wager an equal amount of Ether against each other. The contract maintains records of proposed bets, including the proposer's address, the wagered amount, and the time of proposal, and separately tracks accepted bets, storing the acceptor's address, acceptance time, and a secret random number. Side A initiates a bet by providing a commitment (a hash of a secret random number) and an Ether stake. Side B can then accept the bet by matching the Ether stake and providing their own secret random number. Once both parties have committed, Side A reveals their secret random number, which, when combined with Side B's number, determines the winner. The contract then transfers the total wagered amount to the winner and emits events for bet proposal, acceptance, and settlement, allowing off-chain services to track the game's progress. Only the original proposer (Side A) can reveal their random number to settle the bet.
