This contract provides flash loan functionality for an ERC-20 token. It allows any caller to request a temporary loan of the contract's own token, which is minted on demand and sent to a designated receiver. The contract tracks the total supply of its token. Callers can query the maximum available flash loan amount and the associated fee for a given loan. To perform a flash loan, a caller specifies a receiver, the token (which must be the contract's own token), the loan amount, and arbitrary data. The receiver must implement a specific interface to handle the loan. After the receiver processes the loan, they must return the original loan amount plus any calculated fee to the contract, which then burns the minted tokens. If the fee is non-zero and a fee receiver is specified, the fee portion is transferred to that receiver; otherwise, the entire amount is burned. The contract enforces that the loan amount does not exceed the maximum allowed and that the receiver correctly processes the loan.
