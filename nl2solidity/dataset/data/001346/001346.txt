This contract provides utility functions for validating minting operations for a digital asset, likely an NFT, based on different sale phases and user eligibility. It defines a `Pass` structure to store details about each asset type, including its supply limits, minting prices for public, whitelist, and hodler participants, staking points, and a Merkle root for whitelist verification. The contract offers two primary validation functions: `ValidatePublicMint` checks public sale conditions, ensuring the requested amount doesn't exceed supply or individual limits, and calculates the correct payment, applying a discount for "hodlers" based on the number of tokens they hold. `ValidatePresaleMint` handles presale validations, verifying against supply and individual limits, and then either confirms whitelist eligibility using a Merkle proof and the corresponding whitelist price, or applies a special hodlers price if not whitelisted. Both functions ensure the exact payment amount is provided. The contract does not maintain any state itself; it purely offers logic for external contracts to use for minting validation.
