This contract serves as a base for managing credit delegation, allowing users to grant borrowing power to others. It maintains a record of how much a delegator has allowed a specific delegatee to borrow, associated with an underlying asset. Callers can directly approve a delegation amount for another address, or they can use a signed message to delegate borrowing power, which includes a nonce to prevent replay attacks and a deadline for validity. Any user can query the current borrow allowance between a delegator and a delegatee. The contract emits an event whenever a borrow allowance is delegated, including the delegator, delegatee, underlying asset, and the new allowance amount. It includes mechanisms for upgradeability and EIP712 signature verification.
