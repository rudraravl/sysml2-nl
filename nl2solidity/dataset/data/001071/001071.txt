This contract manages a system of delegated permissions, allowing users to authorize others to act on their behalf or to manage their permissions. It maintains two mappings: one to track which callers are permitted to act for a given owner, and another to track which agents are permitted to modify an owner's permissions. Users can grant or revoke permission for a specific caller to act on their own behalf, or they can designate an agent to manage their permissions. An agent, or the owner themselves, can then grant or revoke permission for a caller to act on that owner's behalf. Attempts by unauthorized parties to modify permissions will be rejected. The contract emits events whenever a permission is modified or an agent is set, allowing off-chain systems to monitor changes. Callers can query the contract to check if a specific address has permission to act for another.
