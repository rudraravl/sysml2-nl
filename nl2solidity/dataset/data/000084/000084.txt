This contract implements an ERC-20 token with integrated voting and delegation capabilities, similar to Compound's COMP token. It maintains the standard ERC-20 token balances for each address, and additionally tracks the historical voting power of each account through a system of checkpoints. Users can delegate their voting power to another address, either directly or by providing a signed message. The contract allows querying an account's current voting power and its voting power at a past block number. When tokens are transferred, the contract automatically adjusts the associated voting power. A critical constraint is that the total supply of tokens is capped at `type(uint208).max` to prevent voting power calculation overflows, and exceeding this cap will cause transactions to revert. The contract emits events related to delegation changes, allowing off-chain services to monitor voting power dynamics.
