This contract serves as a validator for lending and borrowing proposals within a larger PredictDotLoan system. It maintains immutable references to the core PredictDotLoan contract, a Conditional Tokens contract, the ERC20 loan token, and a NegRisk adapter, all established at deployment. The primary operation is `validateProposal`, which takes a proposal, a potential taker, and a fulfillment amount, and returns an array of validation codes indicating any issues. This function performs a comprehensive set of checks, including proposal expiration, ensuring the lender is not also the borrower, verifying the proposal's signature, validating the fulfillment amount against the total loan, checking if the proposal's salt is valid and not already used or cancelled, confirming the nonce is current, and ensuring the collateralization ratio and interest rate are within acceptable bounds. It also verifies that the underlying position is tradeable and that the question price associated with the collateral is available and not resolved. The contract does not modify state; it only performs read-only validations against external contracts and its own internal logic.
