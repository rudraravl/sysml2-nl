This contract provides a utility for counting initialized ticks within a Uniswap V3 pool. Its sole purpose is to determine the number of active tick positions that would be crossed during a swap between two specified tick values, `tickBefore` and `tickAfter`. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather queries the `IUniswapV3Pool` contract for its tick bitmap data. The main operation is `countInitializedTicksCrossed`, which takes the pool instance and the two tick boundaries as input, returning the total count of initialized ticks between them. The logic carefully handles edge cases where `tickBefore` or `tickAfter` themselves are initialized, adjusting the count based on the swap direction to accurately reflect gas-incurring ticks. There are no access controls, fees, or events, as this is a pure utility library.
