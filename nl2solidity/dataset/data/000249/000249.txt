This contract provides a set of utility functions for working with ECDSA signatures on the Ethereum blockchain. Its purpose is to recover the address of the signer from a given message hash and signature, and to generate canonical hashes of signatures. The contract does not maintain any state. Callers can use various `recover` functions to determine the signer of a message, accepting signatures in different formats, including standard 65-byte (r, s, v) and EIP-2098 short form (r, vs). There are also `tryRecover` variants that return the zero address on failure instead of reverting. Additionally, `toEthSignedMessageHash` functions are available to compute the EIP-191 compliant hash of a message, and `canonicalHash` functions provide a standardized hash of a signature, ensuring non-malleability by adjusting `s` and `v` values. All functions are pure or view, meaning they do not modify the blockchain state, and there are no specific access control mechanisms or events emitted. The contract includes an `InvalidSignature` error for recovery failures.
