This contract defines a cross-chain fungible token that extends standard ERC-20 functionality, enabling token transfers between different blockchain networks. It maintains the standard ERC-20 state of token balances and allowances. Callers can initiate a cross-chain transfer of their own tokens, which involves burning the specified amount from their balance on the current chain. Additionally, an authorized account can perform a cross-chain transfer on behalf of another user, provided they have sufficient ERC-20 allowance, also resulting in a burn of the tokens from the `from` address. When tokens are received on this chain from another network, the contract mints the corresponding amount to the recipient's address. The contract itself does not directly handle cross-chain communication but integrates with an abstract bridge mechanism to facilitate these operations.
