This contract serves as a cross-chain bridge for a specific ERC-1155 token, enabling its transfer between different blockchain networks by taking custody of the tokens on the source chain. Upon deployment, it permanently stores the address of the ERC-1155 token it manages. Callers can initiate cross-chain transfers of single or multiple token IDs and amounts by calling `crosschainTransferFrom`, specifying the sender, the destination chain and address, the token IDs, and their respective values. An optional data field can be included for the destination chain's receiver. The contract verifies that the caller is either the `from` address or an approved operator for the `from` address's tokens. When a transfer is initiated, the contract locks the specified tokens by transferring them from the sender to itself. Conversely, upon receiving a cross-chain message, it unlocks tokens by transferring them from its custody to the designated recipient. The contract only accepts incoming ERC-1155 tokens if the transfer originated from the managed token itself and the operator was this bridge contract.
