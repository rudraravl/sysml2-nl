This contract implements an ERC20 token with additional functionalities for permissioned minting, burning, and flash loans. It maintains the token's name, symbol, total supply, and individual account balances. The contract also tracks a configurable fee amount for flash loans and a designated address to receive these fees. Any external caller can mint new tokens to a specified account or burn existing tokens from an account. It supports the ERC20 Permit extension, allowing gasless approvals. The contract also enables flash loans, where a user can borrow tokens without collateral, provided they return the borrowed amount plus a predefined fee within the same transaction. The fee amount and its receiver are internal configurations.
