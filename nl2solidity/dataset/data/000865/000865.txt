This contract acts as a central manager for a liquid staking network, coordinating various components and operations. It maintains state regarding the network's DAO, a specific Stakehouse, a Syndicate for fees, and a brand NFT. It tracks whitelisting status for node runners, maps node runners to their associated smart wallets, and records which smart wallet owns each staked BLS public key. The contract allows the DAO to update its address, adjust a revenue commission, and manage node runner whitelisting and banning. Node runners can register BLS public keys by depositing ETH, which creates or utilizes a smart wallet, and can later withdraw this ETH if the KNOT isn't staked. Node runners can also rotate their EOA representative for their smart wallet and claim rewards from the Syndicate. The DAO has privileged functions to execute operations as a smart wallet, deregister KNOTs, restore shares for rage quits, and rotate node runners. The contract emits events for significant actions like whitelisting changes, smart wallet creation, ETH transfers, KNOT staking, and reward claims, and includes reentrancy protection for certain operations.
