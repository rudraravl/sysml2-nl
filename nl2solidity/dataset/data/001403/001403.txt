This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "Shibaku Inu" with a fixed total supply and a reflection mechanism. It tracks token balances for each address, along with allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract has an owner who can control various parameters. It integrates with a Uniswap V2 router to facilitate token swaps for ETH, automatically swapping a portion of collected fees. A key feature is its dynamic transaction fees, which are applied to both buys and sells, with separate rates for each. These fees are split between a reflection mechanism that redistributes tokens to holders and a tax that is sent to designated development and marketing wallets. Certain addresses, including the owner and the contract itself, can be excluded from these fees. The contract enforces maximum transaction amounts and maximum wallet sizes, which can be adjusted by the owner. Trading can be enabled or disabled by the owner, and the owner can also manually trigger token swaps and ETH transfers from the contract's balance. All token transfers and approvals emit standard ERC-20 events.
