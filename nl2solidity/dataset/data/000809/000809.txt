This contract allows users to deposit Ether and later withdraw their deposited amount. It maintains a record of each user's deposited Ether balance. Any user can deposit Ether into the contract, which increases their individual balance. Subsequently, any user can initiate a withdrawal of their entire recorded balance. During withdrawal, the contract first attempts to send the user's balance to their address. If this transfer fails, the transaction is reverted. Only after a successful transfer is the user's balance within the contract reset to zero. There are no access controls beyond the user's own balance, no fees, and no events are emitted. The contract is vulnerable to reentrancy attacks because it performs an external call before updating the user's balance to zero.
