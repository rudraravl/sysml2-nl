This contract provides a utility for safely adjusting a 128-bit unsigned integer representing a liquidity amount by a signed 128-bit integer delta. Its sole purpose is to perform this arithmetic operation while preventing overflow or underflow. Callers can provide an initial liquidity value and a signed delta, and the contract will return the new liquidity value. It ensures that if the delta is negative, the resulting liquidity does not underflow below zero, and if the delta is positive, the resulting liquidity does not overflow the maximum 128-bit unsigned integer value. If either an underflow or an overflow would occur, the operation will revert, protecting the integrity of the liquidity value. There are no state variables, access controls, events, or external integrations; it's a pure mathematical helper.
