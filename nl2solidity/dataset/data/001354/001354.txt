This contract implements an ERC-20 token named "Shiba X" with the symbol "SHIBX" and 8 decimal places, maintaining a fixed total supply of 1,000,000 tokens. It tracks token balances for each address and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract features an ownership model, allowing the deployer to control certain functionalities. A key mechanic is a transaction tax, initially 6% and reducing to 4% after 40 transactions involving the liquidity pool. This tax is collected in tokens and can be swapped for ETH and sent to a designated tax wallet. The contract also enforces maximum transaction amounts and maximum wallet sizes to prevent large holdings or trades, which the owner can remove. It integrates with Uniswap V2 to facilitate trading, allowing the owner to open trading by creating a liquidity pair and adding initial liquidity. The owner can also manually trigger token swaps for ETH and send accumulated ETH to the tax wallet. Certain addresses, including the owner and the contract itself, are excluded from fees. Additionally, the owner can mark addresses as "bots" to prevent them from transacting.
