This contract provides a utility for efficiently querying on-chain data. It maintains no persistent state itself, acting purely as a read-only gateway. Callers can retrieve the current block's timestamp or the Ether balance of any given address. Its primary function, `multicall`, allows a single transaction to execute multiple arbitrary read-only calls to other contracts. For each sub-call, it records whether the call succeeded, the gas consumed, and any returned data. There are no access controls, and all functions are publicly available for anyone to use. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or integrations with other protocols.
