This contract provides an on-chain oracle for tracking historical price and liquidity data. It maintains an array of observations, each recording a block timestamp, a cumulative tick value, and a cumulative seconds-per-liquidity value. The contract is initialized with a single observation, and its storage capacity can be expanded by any caller. New observations are written to the array, overwriting the oldest entry once the array is full. Callers can query historical data by providing a timestamp or an array of timestamps, receiving interpolated cumulative tick and seconds-per-liquidity values. The contract ensures that observations are written at most once per block and handles timestamp rollovers. There are no explicit access control mechanisms or fees, and no events are emitted. The contract primarily focuses on efficient storage and retrieval of time-weighted average data.
