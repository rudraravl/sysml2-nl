This contract represents a liquidity pool for two ERC-20 tokens, enabling decentralized token swaps and liquidity provision. It maintains the current reserves of `token0` and `token1`, along with cumulative price accumulators for time-weighted average price (TWAP) calculations and a `kLast` value representing the product of reserves after the last liquidity event. Callers can add liquidity by depositing both tokens via the `mint` function, receiving liquidity tokens in return, or remove liquidity by burning liquidity tokens via the `burn` function, receiving a proportional share of the underlying tokens. The `swap` function allows users to exchange one token for another, with a 0.3% fee taken from the input amount, which is added to the reserves. An `initialize` function, callable only once by the factory that deploys the contract, sets the two tokens for the pair. The contract emits `Mint`, `Burn`, `Swap`, and `Sync` events to track all significant state changes. A re-entrancy guard (`lock` modifier) protects critical functions. The contract also includes `skim` and `sync` functions to manage token balances and reserves.
