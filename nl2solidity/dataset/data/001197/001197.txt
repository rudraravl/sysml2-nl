This contract facilitates Uniswap V4 pool management and fee distribution for various collections. It maintains a record of registered collections, their associated Uniswap pool keys, and specific parameters like fees and initialization status. Callers can register new collections, which generates a pool key but doesn't initialize the pool. Subsequently, the designated locker can initialize a collection by providing initial liquidity, establishing a full-range position. The contract allows for depositing fees for a collection, which are then distributed between the Uniswap pool to incentivize liquidity and an optional beneficiary. Fees are collected in ETH equivalent and can be split based on a defined fee structure. The contract implements several Uniswap V4 hooks to manage dynamic fees, distribute fees before liquidity changes, and potentially front-run swaps using collected fees to optimize trades. Access to registration and initialization functions is restricted to a designated "locker" address. Events are emitted for pool state updates, fee changes, and fee collection/distribution, allowing off-chain services to monitor activity.
