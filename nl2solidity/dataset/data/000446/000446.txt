This contract serves as a read-only interface for querying state information from a Uniswap V4 pool manager. Its primary purpose is to provide off-chain clients with convenient access to various data points about liquidity pools and positions. Upon deployment, it permanently records the address of the `IPoolManager` it will query. Callers can retrieve current pool parameters like the square root price, current tick, and protocol/LP fees for a given pool. They can also fetch detailed information about specific ticks, including gross and net liquidity, and fee growth outside those ticks. Furthermore, the contract allows querying global fee growth, total liquidity for a pool, and tick bitmap data. It also provides functions to retrieve liquidity and fee growth information for individual positions, identified either by owner, tick range, and salt, or by a unique position ID. There are no state-changing operations, access controls, or events, as it's purely a data retrieval mechanism.
