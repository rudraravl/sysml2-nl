This contract serves as a centralized registry for important addresses and constants related to the Pendle ecosystem on the Ethereum Mainnet. It maintains a set of immutable addresses for key Pendle contracts, including the vePENDLE contract, the PENDLE token, the PENDLE router, the Voter contract, the Voter Rewards Claimer, and the WISE Oracle Hub. It also stores a constant representing the maximum cardinality for a specific operation, which is 180. The contract does not have any functions or operations that can be called by external users; its sole purpose is to provide a reliable and consistent source for these critical on-chain identifiers. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms, as it is a static data store.
