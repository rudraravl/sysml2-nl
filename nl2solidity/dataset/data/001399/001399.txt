This contract implements a BEP-20 compatible token with a deflationary and reflection mechanism, designed for trading on decentralized exchanges like Uniswap. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual balances for holders, and allowances for spending. A key feature is its dynamic transaction fees, which can vary for buys and sells, with a portion of these fees being reflected back to holders and another portion directed to designated development and marketing addresses. The contract also incorporates anti-bot measures, including the ability to block specific addresses from trading and enforce maximum transaction and wallet size limits. The contract owner has extensive control over these parameters, including setting fees, managing bot lists, and enabling or disabling trading. The contract integrates with a Uniswap V2 router to facilitate automatic liquidity provision and token swaps for ETH, with events emitted for standard token transfers and approvals, as well as ownership changes.
