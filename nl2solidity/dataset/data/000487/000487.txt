This contract acts as a security guard for a Gnosis Safe, restricting delegate calls to a single, pre-defined target address. Upon deployment, it immutably stores this allowed target address. The contract maintains no other state. Its primary function is to intercept and validate transactions originating from the Safe, specifically checking the `checkTransaction` and `checkModuleTransaction` methods. If a transaction attempts a delegate call to any address other than the allowed target, the contract will revert the transaction, preventing its execution. This access control mechanism ensures that the Safe can only perform delegate calls to a trusted, specified contract. There are no events emitted, and the contract does not handle any fees or upgradeability. It includes a fallback function that does not revert, designed to prevent locking the Safe in case of future upgrades.
