This contract manages NFT listings, allowing users to create, modify, transfer, and cancel them. It maintains a record of each NFT listing, including its owner, creation timestamp, duration, and a floor price multiplier. Listings can be of two types: liquid or dutch, each with specific duration constraints. The contract interacts with a `Locker` contract to manage the underlying ERC721 tokens and an `IProtectedListings` contract for specific liquidation listings. When a listing is created, the contract receives the NFT, deposits it into the `Locker`, and distributes an ERC20 token to the lister, while also collecting a tax. Only the listing owner can modify or transfer their listings. Cancelling a listing returns the NFT to the owner and refunds any unused tax, burning the corresponding ERC20 token. The contract emits events for all significant actions, such as listing creation, modification, transfer, cancellation, and fee capture. It includes reentrancy protection and access control for certain functions, like creating liquidation listings which are restricted to the `ProtectedListings` contract.
