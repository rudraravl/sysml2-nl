This contract manages borrowing data within a decentralized exchange environment, specifically for a DEX type identified by a constant value. It tracks the total raw borrow amounts for two tokens within various pools, along with the total borrow shares for each pool. For individual users, the contract stores detailed borrow data, including whether a user is permitted to borrow, their current debt amount and shares, their previous debt ceiling, the timestamp of their last processing, and parameters governing debt ceiling expansion and limits. This includes the percentage and duration for expanding the debt ceiling, as well as base and maximum debt ceiling values. The contract does not define any external functions, implying its state variables are intended to be read and potentially modified by other contracts that inherit from it, providing foundational data storage for a borrowing system.
