This contract manages approvals for "master contracts" to interact with user funds, likely within a larger ecosystem like BentoBox. It maintains a record of which master contracts are approved by which users, and also tracks a whitelist of master contracts that can be approved without a signed message. The contract owner can add or remove master contracts from this whitelist. Any contract can register itself as a protocol. Users can approve or revoke a master contract's access to their funds either directly, if the master contract is whitelisted, or by providing a signed EIP-712 message. Each approval or whitelisting action emits an event, allowing off-chain systems to monitor these state changes. Signatures are protected against replay attacks by incorporating a nonce and the chain ID.
