This contract provides utility functions for calculating interest, designed to be integrated into other contracts. It does not maintain any state itself, nor does it have any direct callers or access control mechanisms; its functions are intended for internal use by other contracts. The primary operations are calculating linear and compounded interest. The linear interest calculation takes a rate and a last update timestamp, returning the accumulated interest based on the time elapsed since the last update, assuming a 365-day year. The compounded interest calculation, also taking a rate and a timestamp, uses a binomial approximation to efficiently compute compounded interest over time, with an optional current timestamp parameter for flexibility. This approximation prioritizes gas efficiency over perfect mathematical precision, slightly underpaying liquidity providers and undercharging borrowers. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability, or security guards within this contract itself, as it serves purely as a mathematical library.
