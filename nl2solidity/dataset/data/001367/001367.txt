This contract implements a standard ERC-20 compliant token named "DeFiner" with the symbol "FIN" and 18 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of tokens, which is fixed at deployment, and tracks the balance of each token holder. The contract also records allowances, enabling one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. Callers can transfer tokens directly to another address, approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens, and transfer tokens from an address that has granted an allowance. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing external applications to monitor token activity. The contract ensures that transfers and allowances do not exceed available balances and uses safe arithmetic operations to prevent overflows and underflows. The initial total supply is minted to the contract deployer.
