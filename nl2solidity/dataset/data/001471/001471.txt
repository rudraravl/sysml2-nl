This contract serves as a factory for creating and managing vote proxy contracts, enabling a secure separation of voting power from the funds used for voting. It maintains a record of which "cold" (secure) and "hot" (active) wallets are linked to specific vote proxies, and also tracks pending link requests. Callers can initiate a link between a cold and hot wallet, which the hot wallet then approves to create a new VoteProxy contract. This VoteProxy allows the hot wallet to perform voting actions on behalf of the cold wallet's locked tokens. The contract also allows for breaking existing links, provided no funds are currently locked within the associated VoteProxy. Important events are emitted for link requests and confirmations, allowing off-chain services to monitor the creation and management of these voting proxies.
