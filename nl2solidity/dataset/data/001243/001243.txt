This contract provides a mechanism to retrieve the exchange rate between two tokens, specifically designed for Layer 2 environments. It maintains configuration details for Chainlink price feeds and a separate Chainlink feed to monitor the Layer 2 sequencer's uptime. Callers can query the current exchange rate through several functions, each of which first verifies that the Layer 2 sequencer is operational and its data is valid before fetching the exchange rate from the configured Chainlink feeds. The contract ensures that all exchange rate queries are contingent on the sequencer's health, providing a robust and reliable price oracle for Layer 2 applications.
