This contract serves as a foundational security mechanism, preventing unauthorized execution of its functions through `delegatecall`. It establishes an immutable record of its original deployment address upon creation. Any subsequent attempt to call a function marked with its special modifier via `delegatecall` will be rejected, ensuring that the function's logic always executes within the context of the contract's own storage and state. This protection is enforced by comparing the current contract address with its original deployment address. If they differ, an error is immediately raised, preventing further execution. This mechanism is primarily for internal use by other contracts that inherit from it, providing a robust guard against a common vulnerability in smart contract interactions.
