This contract implements a BEP-20 compatible token with a deflationary and reflection mechanism, designed to manage tokenomics and trading behavior. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual balances for each address, and allowances for spending on behalf of others. The core functionality revolves around token transfers, which are subject to dynamic fees (reflection and marketing taxes) that vary based on whether the transaction is a buy or a sell. These fees are automatically collected and distributed, with a portion reflected back to holders and another sent to a designated marketing address. The contract integrates with a Uniswap V2 router to facilitate automatic token swaps for ETH to fund the marketing wallet. Access control is managed by an owner, who can set trading enablement, adjust transaction and wallet size limits, modify fee percentages, and manage a blacklist of "bot" addresses. The owner can also manually trigger token swaps and ETH transfers to the marketing address. Important events are emitted for token transfers and approvals, as well as for ownership changes. The contract prevents re-entrancy during swaps and includes safeguards against common arithmetic overflows.
