This contract acts as a proxy, allowing its underlying logic to be upgraded via a beacon pattern, with an added mechanism to permanently disable upgrades. It stores the address of a beacon contract, which in turn points to the current implementation logic, and an administrator address. Callers can query the current implementation, the beacon, and the administrator. The designated administrator can change the administrator address, or crucially, revoke the beacon. Revoking the beacon permanently freezes the contract's logic to its current implementation, making it immutable thereafter, and also clears the administrator and beacon addresses. The administrator can also revoke their own administrative privileges, leaving the contract without an admin. All administrative actions and beacon updates emit events to signal these changes.
