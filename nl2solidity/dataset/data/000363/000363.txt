This contract facilitates the migration of liquidity from Uniswap V2 to Uniswap V3. It maintains immutable references to the Uniswap V3 factory, the WETH token, and the Uniswap V3 non-fungible position manager. The primary operation is `migrate`, which allows a caller to burn their V2 liquidity tokens from a specified V2 pair. A percentage of the resulting V2 tokens (token0 and token1) are then used to mint a new V3 position with user-defined parameters, including fee tier, tick range, and minimum amounts. Any remaining V2 tokens or unspent V3 desired amounts are refunded to the caller, with an option to convert WETH refunds to native ETH. The contract also supports general purpose multicall functionality and permits self-approvals for ERC20 tokens. Only the WETH contract can send Ether directly to this contract.
