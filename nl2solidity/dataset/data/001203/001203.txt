This contract facilitates the bridging of ERC721 and ERC1155 tokens from an L1 blockchain to an L2 blockchain, and their return. It maintains references to an Optimism Portal, an L1 Cross Domain Messenger, and a Royalty Registry, along with the address of its L2 counterpart, InfernalRiftBelow. Users can initiate a bridge of ERC721 or ERC1155 tokens by calling `crossTheThreshold` or `crossTheThreshold1155` respectively, which escrows the tokens in this contract and sends a message via the Optimism Portal to the L2. The contract also allows for tokens to be returned from L2 to L1 via `returnFromTheThreshold`, but only if the call originates from the L1 Cross Domain Messenger and the L2 InfernalRiftBelow contract. Additionally, it enables the claiming of royalties on L2 for L1 collections that comply with ERC2981, provided the caller is the registered royalty receiver. Events are emitted for bridge initiations, finalizations, and royalty claim starts, as well as when the L2 InfernalRiftBelow address is set. The L2 InfernalRiftBelow address can only be set once.
