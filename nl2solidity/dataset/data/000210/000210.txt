This contract implements a basic fungible token, similar to an ERC20, with a fixed name "Token" and symbol "TKN", and 18 decimal places. It maintains the total supply of tokens and tracks individual token balances for each address, along with allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Upon deployment, the contract's creator receives the initial token supply. Callers can query the total supply, an address's balance, or an allowance. Token holders can transfer tokens directly to another address, or approve another address to spend a specific amount on their behalf. Approved spenders can then transfer tokens from the grantor's balance to another address. The contract also includes a `mint` function, which increases both the total supply and a specified user's balance. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract uses `uint96` for internal balance and allowance storage, ensuring arithmetic safety by reverting on overflows or underflows, and handles `uint256` inputs by checking against the `uint96` maximum.
