This contract provides utility functions for safely interacting with ERC-20 tokens. Its purpose is to facilitate reliable token transfers by wrapping standard `transfer` and `transferFrom` operations. The contract itself does not maintain any state beyond its own code. Callers can invoke two main internal operations: one to perform a `transfer` of a specified amount of a given token from the contract's balance to a recipient, and another to perform a `transferFrom` operation, moving a specified amount of a token from a designated sender to a recipient. Both operations ensure that the token contract call is made and handle potential return values gracefully, preventing common issues with non-standard ERC-20 implementations. There are no external functions, access controls, or events; its functionality is purely internal and intended to be inherited and used by other contracts.
