This contract implements a standard ERC20 token with additional support for EIP-2612 permit functionality. It maintains the total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances granted by token holders to other addresses. Callers can query the token's name, symbol, and decimals, as well as the total supply, any account's balance, and any allowance. Token holders can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and transfer tokens on behalf of another address if they have sufficient allowance. The EIP-2612 permit function allows token holders to sign an approval off-chain, which can then be submitted on-chain by anyone to set an allowance. All transfers and approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract includes error handling for common issues like insufficient balance or allowance, and overflow/underflow conditions. It also has a special integration with the Permit2 protocol, allowing for infinite allowances to the canonical Permit2 address.
