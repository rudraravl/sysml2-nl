This contract acts as a vault for managing ETH deposits and rewards within a liquid staking network. It maintains a record of the total shares representing issued LP tokens and tracks the associated liquid staking network manager. Callers can deposit ETH for staking against specific BLS public keys, either individually or in batches, receiving LP tokens in return. Users can burn their LP tokens to withdraw unstaked ETH, provided a minimum time has passed since their last interaction. The contract allows users to claim accumulated ETH rewards for LP tokens associated with BLS keys that have minted derivatives. The liquid staking network manager has exclusive permission to withdraw ETH from the vault for staking purposes and to initiate unstaking for BLS keys no longer part of the syndicate. Important events are emitted for ETH deposits, withdrawals, and ERC20 token recovery by an administrator. The contract integrates with a Syndicate contract for reward claiming and utilizes a reentrancy guard for secure fund transfers.
