This contract implements a decentralized investment platform where users can deposit funds to earn rewards and refer others for additional income. It maintains state for each user, including their deposited tokens, referral information, various reward balances, and withdrawal history. The contract tracks total investments, withdrawals, and the overall contract balance. Users can deposit funds, either initially with a referrer or as a re-deposit, which allocates a portion to the contract balance, distributes tokens based on a dynamic price, and potentially triggers referral bonuses and PSI (Platform Share Index) distribution to all investors. The contract owner, designated as the "initiator," can update weekly rewards based on business volume. Users can perform two types of withdrawals: "working withdrawals" based on accumulated referral and PSI income, and "non-working withdrawals" which allow for periodic withdrawals of a percentage of their token and weekly reward balance, subject to time and frequency constraints. The initiator also has a function to transfer funds out of the contract. Events are emitted for deposits, PSI distribution, and both types of withdrawals, allowing external systems to monitor activity.
