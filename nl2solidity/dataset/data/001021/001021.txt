This contract provides a set of mathematical operations for fixed-point numbers with 18 decimal places, treating `10^18` as the unit `ONE`. It maintains no on-chain state itself. Callers can perform multiplication with floor or ceiling rounding, division with floor or ceiling rounding, and calculate the reciprocal of a number with floor or ceiling rounding. Additionally, it offers a power function that calculates `target` raised to the power of `e`, always rounding down. All functions are pure computations, meaning they do not modify any state or interact with other contracts, and there are no access control mechanisms, events, or fees associated with their use. The contract is designed as a utility library for other contracts requiring precise decimal arithmetic.
