This contract extends a standard governance mechanism by integrating with a Compound Timelock, ensuring that all successful proposals undergo a mandatory delay before execution. It maintains a reference to the associated Compound Timelock contract. Callers can propose actions, which, upon successful voting, are queued into the timelock with a calculated execution time. Once queued, these actions can be executed through the timelock or cancelled if still within the grace period. The contract also allows for the governor to accept administrative control of the timelock and provides a mechanism for the governance itself to update the linked timelock contract. Only the governance system can initiate a timelock update. Important events are emitted when the timelock address is changed. The contract ensures that all proposal executions are routed through the timelock, meaning any assets or permissions must be held by the timelock itself, not the governor contract.
