This contract provides a comprehensive suite of pure functions for date and time manipulation, operating on Unix timestamps and Gregorian calendar dates. It does not maintain any on-chain state itself, but rather offers utilities for other contracts to perform calculations such as converting between timestamps and date components (year, month, day, hour, minute, second), determining if a year is a leap year, finding the number of days in a given month, or identifying the day of the week. Callers can also add or subtract various time units (years, months, days, hours, minutes, seconds) from a timestamp, and calculate the difference between two timestamps in these same units. The contract includes checks to ensure that input dates and timestamps fall within supported ranges, preventing overflows or underflows. There are no access control mechanisms as all functions are pure and do not modify state, and no events are emitted as there are no state changes to report.
