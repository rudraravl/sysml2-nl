This contract serves as a factory for deploying and managing Panoptic Pools, which are linked to specific Uniswap V3 pools. It maintains a record of all deployed Panoptic Pools, mapping each to its corresponding Uniswap V3 pool and risk engine. Any caller can request the deployment of a new Panoptic Pool by providing the underlying Uniswap V3 pool's token addresses, fee tier, a risk engine, and a custom salt value. Upon deployment, the contract also creates two associated Collateral Tracker contracts and issues a unique NFT to the deployer, representing ownership of the newly created Panoptic Pool. A `PoolDeployed` event is emitted with details of the new pool and its trackers. Callers can query the address of a Panoptic Pool given its Uniswap V3 pool and risk engine. Additionally, a helper function allows users to "mine" for a Panoptic Pool address with a desired rarity (number of leading zeros) by iterating through salt values. The contract integrates with a Semi-Fungible Position Manager (SFPM) to initialize new pools and utilizes a cloning mechanism for efficient deployment of new Panoptic Pools and Collateral Trackers.
