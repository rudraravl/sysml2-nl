This contract implements a basic non-fungible token (NFT) system, allowing users to create, own, and trade unique digital assets. It maintains a record of each token's owner, its metadata (name, link, and price), and the total supply of tokens. Users can create new tokens, becoming their initial owner, and update the metadata of tokens they own. Tokens can be transferred directly by the owner or through an approval mechanism where an owner grants another address permission to take ownership. A special "buytoken" function allows users to purchase tokens by sending Ether, with a portion of the sale price going to an administrator and the rest to the previous owner, and the token's price automatically increases after each sale. An administrator, set at deployment and changeable only by the current administrator, has specific privileges, including receiving a fee from token sales. Key events are emitted for token transfers and approvals, enabling off-chain tracking of token activity.
