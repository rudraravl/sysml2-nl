This contract implements a fungible token standard, allowing for the management of multiple distinct token types, each identified by a unique ID. It tracks the balance of each token ID for every owner address, as well as allowances granted by owners to spenders for specific token IDs, and operator approvals allowing a spender to manage all token IDs for an owner. Callers can query balances, allowances, and operator status. Owners can approve a specific amount of a token ID for a spender, or grant/revoke operator status to a spender. Tokens can be transferred directly by the owner, or by an approved spender or operator on behalf of the owner. The contract supports minting new tokens by transferring from a zero address, and burning tokens by transferring to a zero address. All transfers, approvals, and operator status changes emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract includes checks to prevent transfers from or to the zero address, and to ensure sufficient balances and allowances before transactions.
