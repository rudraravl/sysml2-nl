This contract enables a "Safe" account to delegate the ability to transfer ERC-20 tokens and ERC-721 NFTs to specific "delegate" addresses, within predefined limits and durations. It maintains a record of authorized delegates for each Safe, along with separate allowances for ERC-20 tokens (amount and expiration) and ERC-721 NFTs (boolean permission and expiration) for transfers between a Safe and a designated recipient. Safes, or authorized guardians, can add or remove delegates and set these allowances. Delegates can then initiate transfers of approved tokens or NFTs from the Safe to the specified recipient, provided the allowance is sufficient and unexpired. The contract emits events for delegate additions/removals and allowance settings. It also includes a mechanism for an admin or guardian to recover funds in emergencies, and can be paused.
