This contract implements a multi-signature wallet, referred to as a Safe, designed to securely manage assets. It maintains a list of authorized owners and a threshold, which is the minimum number of owners required to approve a transaction. The Safe tracks a unique nonce for each transaction to prevent replay attacks and stores approved message and transaction hashes. Callers can propose and execute transactions, which involve specifying a recipient, value, data, operation type, gas limits, and a refund receiver. Before execution, the contract verifies that the required number of owner signatures are provided, supporting various signature types including standard ECDSA, contract signatures (EIP-1271), and pre-approved hashes. It also integrates with optional "guards" that can perform pre- and post-transaction checks. Transaction costs, including gas fees, are handled by the contract, allowing payment in native currency or specified tokens. The contract is designed to be used as a singleton for proxy contracts, and its setup function initializes owners, threshold, and optional modules.
