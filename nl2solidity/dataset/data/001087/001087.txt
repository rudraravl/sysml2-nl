This contract facilitates the wrapping and unwrapping of Compound v2 cTokens, allowing users to convert between underlying assets and their cToken representations. It maintains no internal state, acting purely as a relayer for these operations. Callers can either `wrapCompoundV2`, providing a cToken, sender, recipient, and amount of underlying tokens to deposit, or `unwrapCompoundV2`, providing the same parameters for cTokens to redeem. Both functions are payable to support potential ETH transfers in a multicall context. The contract handles token approvals and transfers to and from the Compound protocol, ensuring the correct amounts are moved. It integrates with Compound v2 by calling the `mint` and `redeem` functions on cToken contracts, validating their success. No specific access control is implemented beyond the standard external function calls, and no events are emitted by this contract itself. The contract relies on an external `IBaseRelayerLibrary` for internal token handling and chained references.
