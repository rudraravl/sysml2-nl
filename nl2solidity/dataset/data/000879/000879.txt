This contract serves as a specialized vault for managing liquid staking operations, primarily interacting with dETH tokens and LP tokens. It maintains references to a dETH token contract, a SavETH registry, and an account manager, which are initialized through a dependency injector linked to a liquid staking manager. The contract allows users to query their ETH balance, the contract's own ETH balance, and the balance of specific LP tokens associated with BLS public keys for a given user. It also provides functions to retrieve the total supply of an LP token by its BLS public key, a user's dETH token balance, and the lifecycle status of a BLS public key from the account manager. This contract extends core vault functionalities, enabling detailed state inspection for testing and operational oversight within the liquid staking ecosystem.
