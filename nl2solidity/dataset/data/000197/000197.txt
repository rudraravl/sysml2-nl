This contract serves as a mock for a token that explicitly rejects all incoming Ether transfers. It maintains no internal state. Any attempt to send Ether to the contract, whether through a direct transfer or by calling a non-existent function, will result in the transaction reverting with a specific error message. There are no callable functions, events, or access controls, as its sole purpose is to prevent Ether from being held within it. This contract is designed to simulate a scenario where a token or contract should never receive native currency, providing a clear and immediate rejection for any such attempts.
