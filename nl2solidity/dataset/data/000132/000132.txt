This contract provides two distinct rate-limiting mechanisms, each managing independent entries identified by a unique `bytes32` key. The first, a RefillingBucket, models a token bucket that refills linearly over a defined window, allowing bursts up to its capacity. Callers can query the used and available quantities for a specific key, attempt to consume a quantity, or consume a quantity, which will revert if insufficient. The bucket can be reset for a key, and its global capacity and window settings can be updated. The second mechanism, a SlidingWindow, caps cumulative consumption over a rolling time window. It tracks consumption history for each key, allowing queries for used and available quantities, and operations to try consuming or consuming a quantity, reverting on insufficiency. This window can also be reset for a key, and its global limit and window settings can be updated. Both mechanisms share a common error type, `RateLimitExceeded`, for when consumption attempts fail due to insufficient capacity or limit.
