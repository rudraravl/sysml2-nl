This contract serves as a utility for calculating various financial metrics related to listings, specifically taxes and interest rates. It maintains two constant values: a `UTILIZATION_KINK` representing a utilization rate threshold, and a `FLOOR_MULTIPLE_KINK` for a listing floor multiple. Callers can compute the tax required for a listing by providing its collection address, floor multiple, and duration; this calculation adjusts the floor multiple for high values to promote certain listings. The contract also calculates an annual interest rate for protected listings based on a given utilization rate, exhibiting a "hockey puck" style curve where the rate increases slowly until the `UTILIZATION_KINK` and then rapidly accelerates. Furthermore, it can determine a compounded factor given a previous factor, utilization rate, and time period, and finally, it can calculate a compounded amount from an initial principle and two checkpoints, effectively applying the compounded interest. All functions are pure or view, meaning they do not alter the contract's state, and there are no access control mechanisms or events emitted.
