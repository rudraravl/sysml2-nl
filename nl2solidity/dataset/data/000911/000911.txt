This contract implements a standard fungible token, compliant with the ERC20 specification, allowing for the creation and management of a digital asset. It maintains a record of each account's token balance and tracks allowances, which permit one account to spend tokens on behalf of another. Upon deployment, the token is assigned a fixed name and symbol. Callers can query the total supply, their own balance, or the allowance granted to another address. Token holders can transfer tokens directly to other addresses, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens, which can then be transferred via `transferFrom`. Allowances can be increased or decreased atomically to mitigate common front-running attacks. All token transfers and allowance changes emit corresponding events, enabling off-chain applications to monitor the token's activity. The contract itself does not define a mechanism for creating new tokens or destroying existing ones; these operations are expected to be implemented in a derived contract.
