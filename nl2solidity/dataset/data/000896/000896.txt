This contract implements a flexible role-based access control system. It maintains a mapping of roles, identified by unique `bytes32` values, to the addresses that possess them, and also tracks an administrative role for each defined role. Callers can query whether a specific address has a given role using `hasRole`. The core operations involve granting and revoking roles: `grantRole` assigns a role to an address, and `revokeRole` removes it. Both of these actions can only be performed by an address holding the administrative role for the role being modified. Additionally, an address can `renounceRole` to voluntarily give up a role it holds. The contract emits `RoleGranted`, `RoleRevoked`, and `RoleAdminChanged` events to signal changes in role assignments and administrative relationships, allowing off-chain systems to track permissions. The `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` is a special role that acts as the initial administrator for all other roles and is its own administrator.
