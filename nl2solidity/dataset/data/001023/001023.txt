This contract provides a pricing model for a decentralized exchange, specifically implementing a Proactive Market Making (PMM) algorithm. It does not maintain any state on-chain itself, but rather operates on a provided `PMMState` structure, which encapsulates the current reserves of base and quote tokens, an initial price, and a constant related to the pool's liquidity curve. The core functionality involves calculating the amount of tokens received when selling either base or quote tokens, considering the current market conditions represented by the `RState` (whether the price ratio is at, above, or below the initial price). There are two main operations: `sellBaseToken` and `sellQuoteToken`, which take the current `PMMState` and the amount of tokens being sold, returning the amount of tokens received and the new `RState`. The contract also includes helper functions to adjust the target reserves and calculate the mid-price based on the PMM model. There are no access control mechanisms or events, as it's a pure library for price calculation.
