This contract implements a standard ERC-20 compatible token named "F-ELON" with the symbol "FUE" and 0 decimals. It maintains a total supply of 1,000,000,000,000 tokens, initially assigned to a specific address upon deployment. The contract tracks token balances for each address and allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend a specified amount of their tokens, and transfer tokens from an account that has granted them an allowance. An additional function allows a token holder to approve a spender and then immediately trigger a function call on the spender's contract, passing data along with the approval. The contract also includes an ownership mechanism, allowing the deployer to transfer ownership to another address, which then needs to accept it. The current owner can recover any other ERC-20 tokens accidentally sent to this contract. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract uses safe math operations to prevent overflow and underflow vulnerabilities.
