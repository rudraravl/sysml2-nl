This contract extends a standard multi-token contract, enabling token holders to permanently remove tokens from circulation. It maintains the balances of various token IDs for different accounts, as defined by the underlying multi-token standard. Callers can perform two main operations: burning a specific quantity of a single token ID from a designated account, or burning multiple quantities of different token IDs from a designated account in a single transaction. Access control dictates that a caller can only burn tokens from an account if they are either the owner of that account or have been explicitly approved to manage tokens for that account. No specific events are emitted by this contract beyond those inherited from the base multi-token standard, nor are there any fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or external integrations defined here. The core mechanic is the irreversible reduction of token supply for specified IDs and accounts.
