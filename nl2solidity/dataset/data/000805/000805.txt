This contract implements a decentralized exchange for swapping between a designated WETH token and a single other ERC20 token, referred to as the pool token. It maintains reserves of both WETH and the pool token, and also issues its own ERC20 liquidity tokens to users who provide liquidity. Users can deposit both WETH and the pool token to receive liquidity tokens, or burn liquidity tokens to withdraw a proportional share of the underlying WETH and pool token reserves. The contract allows for two types of swaps: `swapExactInput`, where a user specifies the amount of input token they wish to provide, and `swapExactOutput`, where a user specifies the exact amount of output token they wish to receive. Pricing for swaps is determined by the current reserves of each token in the contract. There are no explicit access controls beyond standard ERC20 permissions for transfers, and no events are emitted for liquidity provision, withdrawal, or swaps. The contract calculates swap fees internally, but there are no explicit fees collected or distributed.
