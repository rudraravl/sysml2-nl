This contract acts as a proposer for a specific "Smart Wallet Checker" contract, enabling a two-step ownership transfer process. It maintains an immutable reference to the `SMART_WALLET_CHECKER` contract, which is determined at deployment based on the current blockchain and a predefined registry. The contract's primary function is to queue actions for the `SMART_WALLET_CHECKER`. Any proposed action, represented as raw calldata, is added to an internal queue, targeting the `SMART_WALLET_CHECKER` with a zero ETH value. Access control is managed through a two-step ownership mechanism, meaning a new owner must first be proposed and then accept ownership before gaining control. This ensures a secure and deliberate transfer of administrative privileges. The contract does not emit any specific events beyond those inherited from its base `TwoStepOwnableProposer` functionality.
