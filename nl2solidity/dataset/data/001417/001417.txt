This contract implements a standard fungible token, named "Shiba Poop" with the symbol "SHIBAP" and 18 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks the balance of each token holder. Upon deployment, the entire initial supply is assigned to the contract creator. Callers can query the total supply, their own token balance, or the amount of tokens they have approved another address to spend on their behalf. They can transfer tokens directly to another address, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens. Approved addresses can then transfer tokens from the original owner to another recipient. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing off-chain applications to monitor token activity. The contract uses safe math operations to prevent common overflow and underflow vulnerabilities.
