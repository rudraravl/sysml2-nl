This contract functions as a Wrapped Ether (WETH) token, allowing users to convert native Ether into an ERC20-compatible token and vice-versa. It maintains a standard ERC20 token balance for each user, representing their wrapped Ether. Callers can deposit native Ether into the contract, which then mints an equivalent amount of WETH tokens to their address. Conversely, users can burn their WETH tokens to withdraw an equivalent amount of native Ether back to their address. The contract also allows direct Ether transfers to its address to trigger a deposit. All deposits and withdrawals emit distinct events, `Deposit` and `Withdrawal` respectively, to track these conversions. There are no special access controls; any user can deposit or withdraw. The contract leverages a safe transfer library to ensure secure Ether transfers during withdrawals.
