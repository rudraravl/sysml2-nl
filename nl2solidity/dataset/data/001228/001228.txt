This contract serves as a specialized lending and borrowing platform for conditional tokens traded on predict.fun's CTF exchange, specifically operating on the Blast network. It inherits functionalities from two other contracts, `BlastNativeYield` and `PredictDotLoan`, combining their capabilities. The contract is initialized with several key addresses: a recipient for protocol fees, the primary CTF exchange, a negative risk CTF exchange, adapters for UMA conditional tokens (both standard and negative risk), and an address finder. An owner address is also set during deployment, likely for administrative control. The contract itself does not introduce new state variables or functions beyond what it inherits, primarily acting as an aggregation point for the functionalities provided by its parent contracts, enabling conditional token lending and borrowing with Blast native yield integration.
