This contract defines a set of standardized action codes for various operations within a decentralized finance ecosystem, primarily focused on liquidity management and swapping. It doesn't maintain any state itself, nor does it have any callable functions or access control. Its sole purpose is to provide a common vocabulary of integer constants representing actions like increasing or decreasing liquidity, minting or burning positions, executing different types of swaps, donating funds, and settling or taking positions. It also includes deprecated actions, explicitly warning against their use due to vulnerability to sandwich attacks, and defines actions for wrapping/unwrapping assets and specific permissioned pool operations like subscribing or unsubscribing. There are no events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms, as it's purely a library of definitions.
