This contract serves as a vault for a specific ERC20 token, managing deposits and withdrawals, and tracking a "quarter rent" amount. It maintains the total supply of tokens held, the current quarter rent, a designated multisig address for critical operations, and a record of approved external contracts that can interact with it. Access is controlled through roles: a `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` and a `DEV_ROLE`. Only addresses with the `DEV_ROLE` can approve or revoke external contracts, and update the `TokenInfo` contract which dictates supported tokens. Only the `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` can set the multisig address. Approved contracts can deposit and withdraw tokens, reset the quarter rent, and update a timestamp. The multisig address has the unique ability to withdraw all tokens from the vault. All token transfers are protected against re-entrancy.
