This contract serves as a registry for vaults, allowing for their management and aggregation of user data across them. It maintains a list of registered vault addresses and a mapping for quick lookup. Upon deployment, the deployer is granted both the default administrator role and a specific vault manager role. Only accounts with the vault manager role can add new vaults to the registry or remove existing ones. Adding a vault emits a `VaultAdded` event, and removing one emits a `VaultRemoved` event. Callers can retrieve the complete list of registered vaults for iteration, and can also query the total debt for a specific user by aggregating their debt across all registered vaults. The contract ensures that only unique vaults can be registered and prevents attempts to remove non-existent vaults.
