This contract is a reward pool designed to distribute TOMB tokens to users who stake other ERC20 tokens. It maintains a list of staking pools, each associated with a specific ERC20 token, an allocation point determining its share of rewards, and tracking information for reward calculation. For each user, it records the amount of staked tokens and their accumulated reward debt. The contract defines a TOMB token address and a pool start time. Reward distribution occurs over predefined epochs, with specific TOMB per second rates for each epoch. Only an authorized operator can add new staking pools or adjust existing pool allocation points. Users can deposit their ERC20 tokens into a chosen pool to earn TOMB rewards, or withdraw their staked tokens, which also claims any pending rewards. An emergency withdrawal function allows users to retrieve their staked tokens immediately, foregoing any pending rewards. The contract also includes a mechanism for the operator to recover unsupported ERC20 tokens, with a safeguard preventing recovery of TOMB or staked pool tokens within a certain period after farming ends. Events are emitted for deposits, withdrawals, emergency withdrawals, and reward payments.
