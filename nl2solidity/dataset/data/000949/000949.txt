This contract implements an interest rate model for a lending protocol, calculating borrow and supply rates based on market utilization. It stores a constant representing the approximate number of timestamps per year, along with a `multiplierPerTimestamp` and `baseRatePerTimestamp` which define the linear interest rate curve. These parameters are set once upon deployment, derived from initial annual rates, and an event is emitted to signal their values. Callers can query the contract to determine the current borrow rate and supply rate for a given market, providing the market's cash, total borrows, and reserves. The contract internally calculates the utilization rate, which is the ratio of borrows to the total liquidity available. There are no access control mechanisms; all functions are publicly accessible for querying. The contract does not hold any funds or manage any state beyond its initial parameters.
