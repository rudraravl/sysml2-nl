This contract, named `ReentrancyVictim`, allows users to deposit Ether and later withdraw their deposited balance. It maintains a record of each user's Ether balance. Users can call the `deposit` function to send Ether to the contract, which is then added to their individual balance. To retrieve their funds, users call the `withdrawBalance` function, which attempts to send their entire recorded balance back to them. After the transfer attempt, the user's balance within the contract is reset to zero. There are no specific access controls beyond the implicit permission for any address to deposit and withdraw their own funds. No events are emitted to track deposits or withdrawals. The contract's design is vulnerable to reentrancy attacks because it performs an external call to transfer funds before updating the user's balance to zero.
