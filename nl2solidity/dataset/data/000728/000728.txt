This contract serves as a factory for creating and managing trading pair contracts for two ERC-20 tokens. It maintains a record of all created pairs and allows for the retrieval of a specific pair's address given its constituent tokens. The contract stores an address designated to receive fees (`feeTo`) and an address authorized to set this fee recipient (`feeToSetter`). Any caller can request the creation of a new trading pair by providing two distinct token addresses. The contract ensures tokens are sorted by address to prevent duplicate pairs and deploys a new pair contract using a deterministic address generation method. Once created, the pair's address is stored and an event is emitted. Only the `feeToSetter` address can update the `feeTo` address or transfer the `feeToSetter` role to another address.
