This contract allows users to deposit Ether into it and later withdraw their deposited amount. It maintains a record of the total Ether deposited by all users and individual deposit amounts for each user. Callers can deposit Ether using the `deposit` function, which adds the sent Ether to their individual balance and the contract's total deposits. Users can retrieve their deposited Ether using the `withdraw` function, which reduces their individual balance and the total deposits before transferring the Ether back to them. There are no access controls, so any address can deposit or withdraw their own funds. The contract includes an assertion that the contract's Ether balance must exactly match the sum of all recorded deposits before a withdrawal can occur, which is a critical mechanic. No events are emitted.
