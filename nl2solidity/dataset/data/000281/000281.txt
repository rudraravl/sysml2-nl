This contract provides a reentrancy guard mechanism to prevent malicious re-entrant calls to functions. It does not maintain any persistent state beyond a single storage slot used internally for its guard logic. Callers cannot directly interact with this contract; instead, other contracts can inherit from it to utilize its `nonReentrant` and `nonReadReentrant` modifiers. The `nonReentrant` modifier prevents a function from being called again while it is already executing, reverting with a `Reentrancy()` error if detected. The `nonReadReentrant` modifier offers similar protection for view functions, ensuring they are not re-entered. The contract intelligently adapts its reentrancy detection method based on the chain ID, using transient storage on mainnet for efficiency and regular storage on other chains. There are no events emitted, and no fees or upgradeability features are present.
