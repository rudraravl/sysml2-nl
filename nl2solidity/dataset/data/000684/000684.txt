This contract provides utility functions for interacting with a specific CREATE2 factory. Its purpose is to facilitate the deterministic deployment of other contracts using the CREATE2 opcode, ensuring that a contract's address can be pre-calculated based on a salt and its creation bytecode. It maintains no state itself, acting purely as a library of helper functions. Callers can compute the future address of a contract that would be deployed by the designated CREATE2 factory, either by providing a salt and the full bytecode or a salt and the hash of the bytecode. Additionally, callers can initiate the deployment of a contract through the factory, given a salt and the contract's bytecode. The contract also includes a function to check if a given address already has code deployed to it. There are no access control mechanisms, events, or fees associated with these operations; it simply provides tools for CREATE2 deployments.
