This contract implements a custom ERC-20 token with additional access control and transfer restrictions. It maintains balances for each token holder and tracks allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract's deployer has special privileges, including the ability to mint tokens to any address, set specific addresses as "trusted" for transfers, configure transfer limits (minimum, maximum, and frequency per address), and blacklist addresses from receiving tokens. Transfers are subject to these configurable limits and may be blocked if the sender or receiver is a blacklisted address, or if the transfer exceeds the allowed frequency or value. The contract also includes logic to identify specific liquidity pool addresses based on provided factory and token addresses, which can influence transfer behavior. Token approvals allow a designated spender to transfer tokens from an owner's balance. Events are emitted for all token transfers and approvals, enabling off-chain monitoring of token activity.
