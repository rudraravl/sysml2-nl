This contract enables a system where external contracts can subscribe to receive notifications about specific tokenized positions. It maintains a record of which subscriber contract is associated with each unique token ID. Callers can subscribe a new contract to a token ID, provided they are approved for that token and the system's PoolManager is in a locked state. If a token already has a subscriber, the subscription will fail. Upon successful subscription, the contract notifies the new subscriber and emits a `Subscription` event. Callers can also unsubscribe a token, which removes the associated subscriber, attempts to notify the former subscriber (if it has code), and emits an `Unsubscription` event. The contract defines a minimum gas limit for unsubscribe notifications to prevent denial-of-service attacks. Internal functions allow the parent contract to notify subscribers about position burns and liquidity modifications.
