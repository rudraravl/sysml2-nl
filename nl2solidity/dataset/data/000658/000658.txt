This contract is an Aave AToken with added delegation functionality, specifically designed to manage voting and proposition power. It tracks the delegated voting and proposition balances for each user, alongside their general token balance and delegation mode. When tokens are transferred, minted, or burned, the contract automatically adjusts these delegated balances to reflect the change in ownership. This ensures that a user's voting and proposition power accurately reflects their token holdings, even as tokens move between accounts. The contract relies on an underlying AToken implementation for core token functionalities like balance management, and integrates with a pool, rewards controller, and treasury for its broader Aave ecosystem role. It also includes a mechanism for tracking nonces for each user, likely for signature-based operations.
