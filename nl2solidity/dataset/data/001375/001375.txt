This contract serves as a time-locked vault for a single ERC721 Non-Fungible Token (NFT), specifically designed for a Uniswap V3 LP token. The contract stores the address of the NFT contract it interacts with, the address of the designated owner ("blurr"), a timestamp for when the lock-up period ends, and boolean flags indicating if an NFT is currently locked and if the withdrawal timer has been initiated. Initially, the contract owner is set to the deployer. The owner can lock an NFT by transferring it to the contract, after which no other NFT can be locked. The owner can then trigger a 14-day withdrawal timer. Once this timer expires, the owner can withdraw the locked NFT. The owner also has the ability to cancel the withdrawal timer before it expires, effectively resetting the lock-up. Additionally, the owner can transfer ownership of the contract to a new address. All operations are restricted to the designated owner.
