This contract serves as an upgradeable proxy for an ERC721 token, allowing for future modifications to its logic without changing the contract address. Upon deployment, it initializes an underlying ERC721 implementation contract with a specified name and symbol, effectively creating a new NFT collection. All calls made to this proxy contract are automatically delegated to the designated implementation contract, ensuring that the NFT collection's functionality, such as minting, transferring, and managing ownership, is handled by the current implementation. The contract stores the address of its current implementation in a specific storage slot, which can be queried by anyone. This design enables the contract to maintain its identity while its operational logic can be updated, providing flexibility for evolving project requirements.
