This contract serves as a base for quoting prices on a Uniswap V4 pool manager. Its purpose is to facilitate the estimation of swap outcomes without actually executing the trade. It maintains a reference to the Uniswap V4 Pool Manager. Callers can initiate a simulated swap operation by providing pool details, the direction of the trade (zeroForOne), the exact amount to be swapped, and any associated hook data. The contract then attempts to perform this swap through the Pool Manager. Access to its core functionality is restricted to the contract itself, ensuring that external calls cannot directly trigger these internal operations. If a simulated swap succeeds, it indicates an unexpected outcome and reverts; otherwise, it bubbles up the revert reason, which might contain the quoted amount or an error. An important mechanic is its liquidity check: if the actual amount swapped differs from the specified amount, it signals insufficient liquidity in the pool.
