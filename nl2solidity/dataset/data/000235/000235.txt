This contract implements a multi-token standard, allowing for the management of various fungible tokens identified by a unique `id`. It tracks the balance of each token `id` held by individual addresses, as well as allowances for specific spenders to transfer tokens on behalf of an owner, and operator approvals for an address to manage all tokens of another. Callers can transfer their own tokens, or transfer tokens from another address if they have sufficient allowance or are an approved operator. Owners can grant allowances for specific token `id`s to a spender, or approve an operator to manage all their tokens. The contract emits `Transfer` events for all token movements, `Approval` events when allowances are set, and `OperatorSet` events when operator permissions are changed. It includes mechanisms to prevent balance overflows and reverts on insufficient balances or permissions. The contract also provides functions to query token metadata like name, symbol, decimals, and URI, which are expected to be implemented by inheriting contracts.
