This contract serves as a factory for deploying new "gauge-like" incentive controllers. It maintains a record of all gauge-like contracts it has successfully created. Any external caller can request the creation of a new gauge-like contract by providing an owner address, a notifier address, and a share token address. Upon successful creation, the factory stores the address of the newly deployed gauge-like contract and emits an event indicating its creation, allowing off-chain systems to track new instances. The factory itself does not hold any funds or implement complex access control beyond allowing anyone to create new gauge-like contracts.
