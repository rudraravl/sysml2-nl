This contract extends a governance system to define quorum based on a fraction of the total supply of a voting token. It stores a single piece of state: a quorum numerator. Upon deployment, this numerator is initialized to a specified value. Callers can query the current quorum numerator and the fixed quorum denominator (which is always 100). The contract calculates the required quorum for any given block number by multiplying the token's past total supply by the stored numerator and dividing by the denominator. The quorum numerator can be updated, but only through a successful governance proposal, and the new numerator must not exceed the denominator. Each update to the quorum numerator emits an event, `QuorumNumeratorUpdated`, detailing both the old and new values, allowing off-chain systems to track changes in quorum requirements.
