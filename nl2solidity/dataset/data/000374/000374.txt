This contract serves as a read-only utility for querying Uniswap V3 pools. Its sole purpose is to retrieve detailed information about "populated" ticks within a specific tick bitmap word of a given Uniswap V3 pool. It maintains no internal state. Any caller can invoke its single function, `getPopulatedTicksInWord`, providing the address of a Uniswap V3 pool and a tick bitmap index. The contract then queries the specified pool to identify which ticks are active within that word, calculates their precise tick values, and fetches their corresponding gross and net liquidity. The function returns an array of structures, each containing the tick value, its net liquidity, and its gross liquidity. There are no access controls, fees, or events emitted, as it acts purely as a data aggregator from external Uniswap V3 pools.
