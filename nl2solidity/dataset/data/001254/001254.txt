This contract serves as a read-only resolver for a Fluid Liquidity protocol, providing functions to query revenue and various financial metrics. It maintains an immutable reference to the core `IFluidLiquidity` contract, which it queries for all its state. Callers can retrieve the address designated to collect revenue. The contract allows users to query the uncollected revenue for a specific token or for all tokens listed in the liquidity protocol. It also offers several functions to calculate revenue, exchange prices, and total/user-specific supply and borrow amounts, all of which can be simulated at a specified future timestamp. These simulation functions are purely mathematical calculations and do not alter any on-chain state. There are no access control mechanisms; all functions are publicly accessible for querying. No events are emitted, and there are no fees or upgradeability features.
