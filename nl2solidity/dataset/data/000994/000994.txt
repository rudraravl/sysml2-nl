This contract serves as a foundational component for a lending protocol, managing various state variables related to lending and borrowing pools. It maintains records of total assets within pools, pseudo-total amounts, total bare tokens, and total deposit and borrow shares for each pool token. It also tracks user-specific lending and borrowing shares associated with NFTs, and pure collateral amounts. Callers can query these various pool and user-specific data points. Access to modify certain pool parameters, such as setting pool fees, is restricted to a designated fee manager. The contract includes internal functions for validating input parameters, preventing re-entrancy, and managing the increase or decrease of various pool and user-specific values. It also integrates with an external security contract to verify NFT ownership and with an AaveHubLite contract for re-entrancy checks.
