This contract defines a library for managing "ticks," which are specific price points within a liquidity pool, primarily for decentralized exchanges. It maintains a mapping of tick indices to detailed information about each initialized tick, including its gross and net liquidity, fee growth metrics for both tokens outside the tick, cumulative tick values, and timestamps. Callers can calculate the maximum liquidity allowed per tick based on a given tick spacing, retrieve fee growth accumulated within a specified tick range, and update a tick's liquidity and associated metrics. A tick can also be "crossed," which updates its outside fee growth and other cumulative values when the pool's price moves past it. Finally, ticks can be cleared, effectively removing them from the pool's active state. The contract does not implement any access control or emit events, as its functions are intended to be called internally by other contracts managing the overall liquidity pool.
