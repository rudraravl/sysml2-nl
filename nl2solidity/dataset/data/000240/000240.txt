This contract provides a utility for encoding and decoding data using the Base58 standard. It does not maintain any on-chain state, nor does it have any access control mechanisms or emit events. Callers can use two primary functions to convert byte arrays or 32-byte words into their Base58 string representation, and two corresponding functions to convert Base58 strings back into their original byte array or 32-byte word format. The contract includes error handling to revert transactions with a `Base58DecodingError` if an invalid character or an overflow is encountered during the decoding process. There are no fees, upgradeability features, or integrations with other protocols; it solely focuses on the Base58 conversion logic.
