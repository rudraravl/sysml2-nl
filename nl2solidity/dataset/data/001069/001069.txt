This contract provides a utility for recovering ERC20 tokens that have been inadvertently sent to it. It does not maintain any persistent state beyond the standard contract address and balance. The sole operation callers can perform is to trigger the recovery of a specified ERC20 token. This function is designed to be called internally by other contracts, transferring a given amount of a specified ERC20 token from this contract's balance to the address that initiated the internal call. There are no access controls, as the function is internal and relies on the calling contract's permissions. Each successful recovery emits a `Recovered` event, detailing the token's address and the amount transferred, allowing off-chain systems to track these operations. The contract does not implement any fees, upgradeability, or direct integrations with other protocols.
