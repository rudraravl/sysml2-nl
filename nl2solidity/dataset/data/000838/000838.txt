This contract extends a standard ERC721 non-fungible token to incorporate a voting mechanism. It tracks the voting power of each address, where each NFT held by an address represents one vote. Users can delegate their voting power to another address, or to themselves, allowing them to participate in governance decisions. The contract automatically updates an address's voting power whenever an NFT is transferred, ensuring that the voting balance accurately reflects current ownership. Changes in delegation and voting power are broadcast through events, enabling off-chain systems to monitor and react to these updates. The core purpose is to link NFT ownership directly to a flexible and delegatable voting system.
