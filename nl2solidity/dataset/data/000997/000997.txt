This contract serves as an oracle helper, providing price data for various tokens by integrating with external price feeds like Chainlink and Uniswap V3 TWAPs. It maintains state for registered price feeds, their associated token decimals, and information about Uniswap V3 pools used for TWAP calculations, including whether a pool is a direct TWAP or a derivative. Callers can retrieve the latest USD value of a token using its TWAP, and the contract validates these prices against Chainlink aggregators, ensuring they remain within acceptable min/max bounds and do not deviate significantly from TWAP values if both are available. It also includes mechanisms to check the liveness of Chainlink feeds by recalibrating and monitoring their heartbeats, and can detect if an Arbitrum sequencer is dead. Only internal functions are exposed for adding new oracle configurations and managing the heartbeat recalibration process.
