This contract is a payment splitter designed to distribute received Ether and ERC20 tokens among a predefined group of payees based on a share system. Upon deployment, the contract is initialized with a list of payee addresses and their corresponding share amounts, which cannot be changed thereafter. The contract maintains the total shares across all payees, the total Ether released, and for each ERC20 token, the total amount released. It also tracks the individual shares and released amounts for each payee. Any caller can send Ether to the contract, which is recorded, and any ERC20 token can be transferred to it. Payees can call the `release` function to claim their proportional share of the Ether or a specific ERC20 token held by the contract, based on their assigned shares and the total amount received. This action triggers a transfer of funds to the payee and emits an event indicating the release. The contract employs a pull payment model, meaning payees must actively request their funds.
