This contract establishes a loyalty system for DegenBrains, tracking user tiers based on their holdings of a specific ERC20 token, vDBX, and any staked balance in an external staking contract. The contract maintains a mapping of tier levels to the minimum vDBX balance (including staked amounts) required for each tier. The contract owner can update these tier requirements, and each update emits an event to signal the change. Any caller can query the requirement for a specific tier or determine the loyalty tier of any user by providing their address. The user's tier is calculated by summing their vDBX balance and their staked balance from the integrated staking contract, then comparing this total against the defined tier requirements, returning the highest tier achieved.
