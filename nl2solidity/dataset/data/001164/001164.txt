This contract manages NFT positions representing liquidity in a lending protocol. It maintains a mapping of unique NFT identifiers to the specific lending pool they are associated with, and tracks a counter for the next available NFT ID. Users can mint a new NFT position for a given lending pool, receiving a unique token representing their stake. The contract allows users to supply various assets, including ETH, to a lending pool, and to borrow or withdraw assets from a pool, also supporting ETH. Users can also repay borrowed assets, again including ETH. All asset operations are performed by transferring tokens to or from the contract, or by wrapping/unwrapping ETH. An administrator role has the exclusive permission to sweep any accidentally sent ETH or ERC20 tokens from the contract to a specified address. The contract is designed to be upgradeable and includes re-entrancy protection.
