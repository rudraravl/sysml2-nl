This contract acts as a callback handler for a GMX-related system, primarily interacting with a specified vault and WETH. It maintains a mapping of unique keys to refund addresses, allowing for the registration of a single refund account per key. The contract's main operations are callback functions triggered by an authorized GMX order handler for events like refunding execution fees, after order execution, after order cancellation, and after an order is frozen. These callbacks currently do not perform any on-chain state changes or transfers. Additionally, an authorized caller can transfer WETH tokens held by the contract to any destination. Access to the callback functions is strictly limited to a predefined GMX order handler, while the WETH transfer function is restricted by a general authorization mechanism. The contract stores the WETH address and the vault address upon deployment.
