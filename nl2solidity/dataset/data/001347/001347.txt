This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "The Shiba Cult" (TSC) with built-in liquidity and reflection mechanisms. It tracks token balances for all holders and manages allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract has an owner who can configure various parameters, including transaction limits, wallet limits, and fee exclusion lists. During active trading, a portion of each transaction is collected as a fee, which is then used to buy back tokens and add liquidity to a Uniswap V2 pair, with the remaining ETH distributed to two designated fee wallets. A cooldown mechanism can be enabled to prevent rapid buying and selling, and a bot-detection system can block specific addresses from trading. The contract also allows the owner to manually trigger token swaps for ETH and distribute collected ETH to the fee wallets. Important events are emitted for token transfers and approvals.
