This contract is a specialized version of a Syndicate contract, designed for testing purposes within a larger Stakehouse ecosystem. It maintains references to a mock Stakehouse Universe and a mock Slot Registry, which are created and stored internally. The contract can be initialized by a contract owner, who also provides a priority staking end block, a list of priority stakers, and BLS public keys for syndicate knots. Once initialized, it behaves like a standard Syndicate contract, but all interactions with the Stakehouse Universe and Slot Registry are routed through the internal mock instances, rather than external, production-ready contracts. This allows for isolated testing of Syndicate functionality without requiring a full Stakehouse deployment.
