This contract defines a specific interest rate strategy for a lending protocol. Its purpose is to establish a fixed borrow rate for a reserve. The contract maintains no mutable state of its own; all relevant parameters are inherited and set during its deployment. Callers cannot perform any operations on this contract directly, as it contains no public or external functions beyond its constructor. Access control is not applicable as there are no modifiable states or callable functions. No events are emitted. The core mechanic is that it initializes an inherited interest rate strategy with a fixed base borrow rate and zero values for other rate components like variable and stable rate slopes, effectively making the borrow rate constant. This contract integrates with a larger lending protocol by providing a concrete implementation of an interest rate strategy.
