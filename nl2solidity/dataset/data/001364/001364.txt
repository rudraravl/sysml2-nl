This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token called "Aries-ZodiacToken" with the symbol "ARI♈" and 8 decimal places, allowing for standard token transfers and approvals. It maintains individual token balances for all addresses and tracks the total supply of tokens. The contract also features a token sale mechanism where users can send Ether to receive tokens, with a configurable exchange rate (ICORatio) and a bonus (AIRDROPBounce). The contract owner has exclusive control over enabling/disabling token purchasing, setting the ICO parameters (ICORatio, AIRDROPBounce, MINfinney), and withdrawing any Ether sent to the contract. The owner can also withdraw any other ERC-20 tokens accidentally sent to this contract. Users can burn their own tokens, reducing their balance and the total supply. Important events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and token burns, allowing off-chain applications to monitor activity.
