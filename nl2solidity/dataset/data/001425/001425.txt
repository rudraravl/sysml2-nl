This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "SUNDAY PUMP" with the symbol "PUMPDAY" and 9 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of 1,000,000 tokens. The contract tracks token balances for each address, as well as allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. It incorporates a reflection mechanism where a portion of each transaction's value is distributed among token holders, and another portion is sent to a dedicated contract address for eventual conversion to ETH and distribution to development and marketing addresses. The contract owner has special permissions to enable/disable trading, set transaction and wallet size limits, block/unblock bot addresses, and adjust the fee percentages for buys and sells. Transfers are subject to maximum transaction and wallet size limits, and certain addresses can be excluded from fees. The contract automatically swaps a portion of its token balance for ETH via Uniswap when a threshold is met, distributing the ETH to the development and marketing addresses. Ownership can be transferred or renounced. Events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and ownership changes.
