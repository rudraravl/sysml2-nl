This contract creates a new fungible token, named "icashprosto" with the symbol "CASH", adhering to the ERC20 standard. Upon deployment, it mints an initial supply of 1,000,000 tokens to the deployer's address. The contract maintains the total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and transfer tokens from accounts they have been approved to spend from. The contract also includes an access control mechanism where the deployer is initially designated as the owner. Only this owner can mint new tokens to any specified address, increasing the total supply. The owner can also transfer ownership to another address or renounce it entirely. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events for off-chain tracking.
