This contract provides a set of mathematical utility functions for performing multiplication and division operations on unsigned 256-bit integers, specifically designed to handle potential overflows that might occur with standard Solidity arithmetic. It does not maintain any state on-chain. The primary operations are `fullMul`, which multiplies two numbers and returns the full 512-bit result as two 256-bit parts (low and high), and `mulDiv`, which multiplies two numbers and then divides the 512-bit result by a third number, returning a single 256-bit result. There are no access controls, permissions, or events, as it's a stateless library of pure functions. The contract is explicitly labeled as not for production use, indicating it's an experimental or illustrative implementation of these mathematical mechanics.
