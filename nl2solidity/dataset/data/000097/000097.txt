This contract defines a standard for non-fungible tokens (NFTs) that can be permanently removed from circulation. It maintains a record of unique tokens, each identified by a `tokenId`, and tracks their current owner. The primary operation available is `burn`, which allows the current owner of a specific `tokenId`, or an approved operator for that token, to destroy it. Once burned, the token ceases to exist within the contract, effectively removing it from the blockchain. There are no fees, special mathematical operations, or explicit upgradeability mechanisms defined within this contract; its sole purpose is to add the burn functionality to an existing ERC721 token implementation. No events are emitted by this specific burn operation.
