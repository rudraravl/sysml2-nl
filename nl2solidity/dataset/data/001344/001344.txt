This contract implements a deflationary token named DogsRUs (DOGSRUS) with a fixed total supply and a reflection mechanism. It tracks token balances for all holders and allowances for spending on behalf of others. A 3% tax is applied to most transfers, with 3% of the transferred amount being redistributed proportionally to all existing token holders, effectively increasing their balances over time. The contract owner can set a maximum transaction amount to prevent large transfers and can also exclude specific addresses from the reflection mechanism, meaning their balances won't grow from reflections. The contract emits standard ERC20 `Transfer` and `Approval` events for all token movements and allowance changes. The owner can transfer ownership to another address or renounce it.
