This contract serves as a registry for Aave market addresses providers, allowing for the indexing and management of different Aave markets. It maintains a mapping between addresses of `PoolAddressesProvider` contracts and unique numerical IDs, as well as the reverse mapping, and a dynamic list of all registered provider addresses. The contract is owned, meaning only the designated owner can register new addresses providers with a unique ID or unregister existing ones. Callers can query the contract to retrieve the list of all registered provider addresses, find the ID associated with a given provider address, or find the provider address associated with a given ID. Registering or unregistering a provider emits an event, `AddressesProviderRegistered` or `AddressesProviderUnregistered` respectively, including the provider address and its ID. The contract ensures that IDs are non-zero and unique, and that providers are not registered multiple times.
