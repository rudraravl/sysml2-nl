This contract manages credit delegation for a specific underlying asset, allowing users to grant borrowing power to others. It maintains a record of how much borrowing allowance each delegator has granted to each delegatee. Callers can directly approve a specific amount of borrowing allowance for a delegatee, or revoke all delegation from a specific delegator. Additionally, a delegator can sign an off-chain message to delegate borrowing power, which another party can then submit on-chain, provided the signature is valid and not expired. The contract tracks nonces for each delegator to prevent replay attacks on signed delegations. All changes to borrowing allowances emit an event, including the delegator, delegatee, the underlying asset, and the new allowance amount. The contract also provides a view function to query the current borrowing allowance between any two users.
