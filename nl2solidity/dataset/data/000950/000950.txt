This contract serves as a money market token (mToken) for a specific underlying ERC-20 asset, enabling users to supply, borrow, and manage their positions within a lending protocol. It maintains state regarding the underlying asset's address, a Comptroller contract for protocol rules, and an Interest Rate Model for calculating interest. Callers can supply the underlying asset to the market, receiving mTokens in return, or use a `mintWithPermit` function for a single-transaction supply if the underlying token supports EIP-2612. Users can redeem their mTokens for the underlying asset, either by specifying the mToken amount or the desired underlying amount. Borrowing the underlying asset is also supported, as is repaying one's own borrow or repaying on behalf of another borrower. A liquidation mechanism allows liquidators to repay a borrower's debt and seize their collateral. Only the designated `admin` can sweep accidentally transferred ERC-20 tokens, excluding the underlying asset itself. The contract includes robust internal mechanisms for handling ERC-20 transfers, accommodating both standard and non-standard token implementations to prevent common transfer-related issues.
