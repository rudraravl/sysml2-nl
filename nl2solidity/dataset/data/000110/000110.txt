This contract provides a collection of utility functions for manipulating arrays in memory or storage. It does not maintain any persistent state itself, but operates on arrays passed to its functions. Callers can sort arrays of `uint256`, `address`, or `bytes32` types, either using a default ascending order or a custom comparison function. It also offers functions to search sorted `uint256` arrays for specific elements, returning their index or the array's length if not found. Additionally, the contract provides operations to extract portions of arrays into new ones (slice), modify arrays in place by removing initial elements (splice), and replace elements within an array with elements from another array. All functions are pure or view, meaning they do not alter the blockchain state, and are designed for internal use by other contracts.
