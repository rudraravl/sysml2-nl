This contract serves as a secondary module for a Fluid Vault, handling complex operations related to debt absorption and liquidity rebalancing. It maintains records of absorbed liquidity, including both collateral and debt, and tracks various vault-specific variables internally. The contract offers two main functions: `absorb`, which liquidates bad debt positions in ticks and branches above a specified maximum ratio, updating the vault's state to reflect the absorbed amounts and emitting a `LogAbsorb` event; and `rebalance`, which adjusts the vault's supply and borrow amounts in external liquidity protocols to match its internal state, only callable by a designated rebalancer address. Both functions are designed to be invoked via delegatecall from the main Vault CoreModule contract, and direct calls are explicitly disallowed. The `rebalance` function also implements a re-entrancy guard.
