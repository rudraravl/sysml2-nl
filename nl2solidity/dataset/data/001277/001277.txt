This contract manages a lending and borrowing system with a focus on leveraged positions and liquidation mechanics. It tracks global vault statistics like total supply, total borrow, and the number of active positions. Crucially, it maintains detailed information about individual user positions, including whether they are for borrowing or supplying, their specific tick (a price level), and the amounts of supplied collateral and "dust" debt. The contract also manages a complex system of "ticks" and "branches" to handle liquidations, recording whether a tick is liquidated, the branch where it was liquidated, and associated debt factors. It stores various configurable parameters such as supply and borrow rate magnifiers, collateral factors, liquidation thresholds, and penalties. The contract also keeps track of exchange prices for different tokens and the address of a designated rebalancer. It does not expose any direct functions for users to interact with, implying that its state is updated by other contracts or privileged external calls.
