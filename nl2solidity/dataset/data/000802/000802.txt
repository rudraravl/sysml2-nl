This contract functions as a secure vault for ERC20 tokens, allowing users to deposit and withdraw specific supported tokens. It maintains a record of which ERC20 tokens are allowed for interaction and tracks the individual balance of each supported token held by every user. Upon deployment, the contract is initialized with a list of tokens it will support, and only these tokens can be used for deposits and withdrawals. Users can deposit a specified amount of a supported token, which increases their recorded balance within the contract and transfers the tokens from their account to the vault. Conversely, users can withdraw their entire balance of a supported token, which resets their recorded balance to zero and transfers the tokens back to them. There are no special access controls beyond the initial supported token configuration, and no events are emitted. The contract ensures that only supported tokens are interacted with, reverting if an unsupported token is used.
