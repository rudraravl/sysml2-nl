This contract implements a standard fungible token, named "Token" with symbol "TKN" and 18 decimal places, tracking the total supply and individual account balances. Upon creation, the entire initial supply is assigned to the deployer. Users can transfer tokens directly to another address or permit a third party to spend a specified amount on their behalf. The contract maintains a record of each user's balance and any allowances granted to other addresses. All transfers and approvals trigger corresponding events, allowing external systems to monitor token movements. Additionally, a special `mint` function allows new tokens to be created and assigned to a specified address, increasing the total supply. There are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or integrations with other protocols.
