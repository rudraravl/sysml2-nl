This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "Lily Blossom" with the symbol "NARI", designed to integrate with Uniswap V2. It maintains balances for token holders, allowances for spenders, and the total supply. Key state variables include the Uniswap V2 router and pair addresses, a marketing wallet, and various fee percentages for buys and sells. It also tracks maximum transaction and wallet amounts, and whether trading and swapping are enabled. The contract owner has exclusive control over enabling trading, updating fee percentages, adjusting transaction and wallet limits, and excluding addresses from fees or transaction limits. Transfers between non-excluded addresses incur fees, which are split between a marketing wallet and a liquidity pool. When the contract accumulates enough tokens from fees, it automatically swaps them for ETH and adds liquidity to the Uniswap pool. Important events are emitted for ownership transfers, fee exclusions, AMM pair settings, marketing wallet updates, and swap-and-liquify operations.
