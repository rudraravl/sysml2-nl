This contract is designed to calculate the reserves of a decentralized exchange (DEX) pool, specifically for tokens that are expected to maintain a peg, like stablecoins. It stores the address of the DEX pool, whether the quote token is token0, and a fixed percentage buffer applied to reserve calculations. This buffer, set at deployment, must be greater than zero. The contract provides two main operations: retrieving collateral reserves and retrieving debt reserves. When calculating collateral reserves, the contract reduces the raw reserves by the predefined percentage buffer. Conversely, when calculating debt reserves, it increases the raw reserves by the same percentage buffer. This mechanism aims to provide a safer, more conservative estimate of reserves for pegged assets by adjusting their value.
