This contract provides a utility for intentionally consuming a specified amount of gas during a transaction, without causing the transaction to revert. Its primary purpose is to facilitate Contract Secured Revenue (CSR) by allowing a contract to incur a predictable gas cost. The contract does not maintain any state. Callers can invoke three distinct functions, `burnPure`, `burnView`, and `burn`, each designed to burn an approximate amount of gas specified by an input parameter. There is no access control; any caller can use these functions. No events are emitted. The core mechanic involves performing computationally intensive operations, such as repeated hashing or static calls, within an assembly block to achieve the desired gas consumption. The contract is designed to be a library, meaning its functions are intended to be called by other contracts.
