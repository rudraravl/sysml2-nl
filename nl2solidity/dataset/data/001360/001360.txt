This contract implements an ERC-20 compatible token named "BONE" with a fixed supply, initialized at deployment by the contract creator, who is designated as the `_owner`. It tracks token balances for each address and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract includes custom transfer logic: transfers are restricted based on a whitelist and blacklist, and the owner can manage these lists. Specifically, the owner can add multiple addresses to a whitelist, allowing them to transfer tokens, or add addresses to a blacklist, preventing transfers. The owner can also perform a `multiTransfer` to distribute tokens to multiple recipients and grant an infinite allowance to a specific router for some of these recipients. Additionally, the owner can change a `_safeOwner` address. All arithmetic operations are protected against overflows and underflows. The contract emits standard ERC-20 `Transfer` and `Approval` events for transparency.
