This contract serves as a mathematical utility for converting between "ticks" and "square root prices" within a specific financial model, likely for a decentralized exchange or liquidity pool. It does not maintain any state on-chain. Callers can use two primary functions: `getSqrtPriceAtTick` to calculate a fixed-point Q64.96 square root price given an integer tick, and `getTickAtSqrtPrice` to determine the greatest tick value corresponding to a provided square root price. Both functions enforce strict bounds on their inputs, reverting with `InvalidTick` or `InvalidSqrtPrice` if the values fall outside the acceptable range. There are no access control mechanisms as this is a pure library for calculations. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability, or direct integrations with other protocols; its purpose is solely to provide these specific mathematical conversions.
