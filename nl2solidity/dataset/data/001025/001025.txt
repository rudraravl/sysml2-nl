This contract serves as a router for interacting with a decentralized exchange, facilitating the creation of liquidity pools, adding and removing liquidity, and swapping tokens. It maintains immutable references to a Wrapped Ether (WETH) contract and a factory contract responsible for deploying new liquidity pools. Users can create new token-to-token or ETH-to-token liquidity pools by providing initial token amounts and pool parameters, receiving LP shares in return. Liquidity can be added to existing pools, either with two tokens or one token and ETH, with options for adjusted or unsafe (direct) transfers, and removed by burning LP shares to receive underlying tokens or ETH. The contract supports complex token swaps across multiple liquidity pools, including direct token-to-token, ETH-to-token, and token-to-ETH routes. All operations involving ETH automatically handle WETH wrapping and unwrapping. Crucially, all transactions are subject to a deadline to prevent front-running and slippage protection by requiring a minimum expected output amount. The contract performs decimal validation for token pairs to ensure compatibility and prevent issues.
