This contract serves as a factory for creating and managing lending pools, acting as a central registry and configuration hub. It maintains a record of all deployed pools, their current implementation, a designated treasury address, and various protocol-wide parameters like reserve factors, flash loan premiums, and liquidation fees. Any caller can initiate the creation of a new lending pool, specifying initial parameters and administrative roles for that pool. The contract owner, who can be transferred through a two-step process, has exclusive control over updating the core pool implementation, changing the configurator contract, setting the treasury address, and adjusting all protocol-level fee percentages. All significant changes to the contract's state, including pool creation and parameter updates, emit events to allow off-chain monitoring.
