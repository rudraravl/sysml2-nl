This contract manages a liquidity pool for two tokens, X and Y, enabling swaps and liquidity provision. It maintains the current reserves of token X and Y, an active price bin, and various fee parameters. Callers can swap tokens, providing either X or Y and receiving the other, with the contract automatically traversing price bins to fulfill the order. Liquidity providers can deposit both tokens into specific price bins, receiving LP tokens in return. The contract also supports flash loans, allowing users to borrow tokens for a single transaction. Access to initialization is restricted to a factory contract, while other operations are generally public. Important events are emitted for swaps, liquidity additions, flash loans, and composition fees, allowing off-chain systems to track activity. The contract incorporates an oracle to track price and volatility, and uses a reentrancy guard for security. Fees are collected during swaps and flash loans, with a portion going to the protocol and the remainder distributed to liquidity providers.
