This contract manages a token rewards system, distributing a specified reward token to users who stake a designated staking token. It maintains the addresses of the reward token, the staking token, and the period over which rewards are distributed. The contract tracks each user's staked balance, their earned rewards, and the total supply of staked tokens. The contract owner can set the reward distribution, notify the contract of new rewards, and recover accidentally sent ERC20 tokens. Users can deposit staking tokens to earn rewards, withdraw their staked tokens, and claim their accumulated rewards. All interactions are protected by re-entrancy guards and utilize safe math operations. Important events are emitted for reward rate updates, reward deposits, and user staking/withdrawal actions, allowing off-chain systems to monitor the reward program.
