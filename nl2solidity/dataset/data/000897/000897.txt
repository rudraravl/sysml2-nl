This contract establishes a two-step ownership transfer mechanism. It maintains a single owner address, which is initially the deployer, and a separate "pending owner" address. The current owner can initiate an ownership transfer to a new address, which becomes the pending owner. This action emits an event indicating the previous and new potential owner. The transfer is only finalized when the designated pending owner explicitly accepts ownership. Until accepted, the original owner retains control. Once accepted, the pending owner becomes the new owner, and the pending owner state is cleared. This two-step process prevents accidental or forced ownership changes, ensuring the new owner is aware and willing to take control.
