This contract extends a standard ERC20 token to support cross-chain operations, acting as a bridgeable token. It maintains the standard ERC20 token balances and allows for the creation and destruction of tokens under specific conditions. The primary operations are `crosschainMint` and `crosschainBurn`, which increase and decrease the token supply, respectively. These functions are strictly controlled by a `onlyTokenBridge` modifier, meaning only a designated token bridge address, determined by an internal `_checkTokenBridge` function, can execute them. Each successful mint or burn operation emits a `CrosschainMint` or `CrosschainBurn` event, respectively, detailing the recipient/sender, the amount, and the bridge address that initiated the action. The contract also supports the ERC165 interface for introspection, indicating its support for the IERC7802 standard.
