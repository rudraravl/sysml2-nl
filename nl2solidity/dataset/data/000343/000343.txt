This contract serves as a factory for creating and managing Uniswap V3 liquidity pools. It maintains a record of its current owner, a mapping of supported fee amounts to their corresponding tick spacings, and a lookup table for deployed pools based on their token pair and fee. The owner, initially the deployer, can transfer ownership to another address. Any caller can create a new liquidity pool by providing two token addresses and a supported fee amount; the contract ensures the tokens are unique, ordered, and that a pool with the exact parameters doesn't already exist. The owner can also enable new fee amounts and their associated tick spacings, subject to certain bounds. All ownership changes, fee amount activations, and pool creations emit events for off-chain monitoring.
