This contract is a staking vault for liquidity pool tokens, designed to manage deposits and withdrawals with an optional cooldown period. It maintains a record of each user's cooldown status, including when their cooldown ends and the amount of underlying assets they have staked. The contract owner can set a cooldown duration, which, when active, disables standard ERC4626 `withdraw` and `redeem` functions, instead requiring users to initiate a cooldown via `cooldownAssets` or `cooldownShares`. Once the cooldown period concludes, or if the cooldown duration is set to zero, users can claim their assets using `unstake`. If the cooldown duration is zero, the contract behaves as a standard ERC4626 vault. The contract prevents re-entrancy during deposits and withdrawals and ensures a minimum share amount is maintained to guard against donation attacks. Updates to the cooldown duration are announced via an event.
