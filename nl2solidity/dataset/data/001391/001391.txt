This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "Gazillion" with the symbol "GG" and 9 decimal places, maintaining balances for each address and tracking allowances for spending on behalf of others. The contract has an owner, initially the deployer, who can perform privileged operations. A unique feature is a 5% tax on all transfers, where 5% of the transferred amount is sent to the contract itself, and the remaining 95% reaches the recipient. The contract integrates with Uniswap V2, allowing the owner to add liquidity to a Uniswap pair for the token and WETH, and to enable trading. Once trading is open, a portion of the tokens held by the contract (accumulated from the transfer tax) can be automatically swapped for ETH via Uniswap, and this ETH is then sent to a designated marketing address. The contract also allows the creator to mark certain addresses as "pairings," which affects when trading can occur for transfers involving those addresses. The contract emits standard ERC-20 Transfer and Approval events.
