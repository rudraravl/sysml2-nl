This contract serves as a dedicated collector for protocol fees within a larger system, specifically designed to offload tasks from a main Vault contract. It stores and manages two types of fee percentages: a swap fee and a flash loan fee, both represented as 18-decimal fixed-point numbers. Any tokens collected as protocol fees are held by this contract. Authorized entities can withdraw collected fees for specified tokens and amounts to a designated recipient. The contract also allows authorized users to update the swap fee percentage and the flash loan fee percentage, subject to predefined maximum limits. Changes to these percentages emit corresponding events. Access control for all operations is delegated to an external authorizer associated with the immutable Vault contract, ensuring only approved callers can modify settings or withdraw funds. The contract includes re-entrancy protection for withdrawal operations.
