This contract facilitates the gradual adjustment of several key parameters within a larger system, specifically focusing on range, threshold, and center price values. It maintains state for ongoing shifts for these parameters, including their starting values, target values, durations, and timestamps. Callers can trigger calculations for the current values of these shifting parameters. The contract is designed to be called via a delegatecall mechanism, ensuring its functions are executed within the context of another contract. When a shift completes, the contract updates the relevant system variables and clears the shift's data. The center price shift additionally integrates with an external pricing oracle to determine its target value, allowing for dynamic adjustments based on external market conditions.
