This contract facilitates the sale of unique digital land tokens, allowing users to purchase them using MATIC. It maintains a list of available land tokens held by a designated wallet, a base price for land, and factors that adjust prices based on district and individual land properties. The contract also tracks whether the sale is active, a wallet to receive sale proceeds, and an optional VIP presale contract for automatic wallet additions. An owner can activate/deactivate the sale, set various price parameters (including a static MATIC price or dynamic pricing via Uniswap), and manage the addresses of integrated contracts. Users can buy listed land tokens by sending the exact MATIC price, which is calculated dynamically based on the base price, MATIC's USD value, and land-specific factors. Upon successful purchase, the land token is transferred to the buyer, the MATIC is sent to the designated owner wallet, and a `BuySuccess` event is emitted. The contract also allows the owner to transfer any held MATIC or custom ERC20 tokens.
