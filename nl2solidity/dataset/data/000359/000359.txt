This contract provides a utility library for performing a specific mathematical operation: division with rounding up. Its sole purpose is to calculate the ceiling of a division between two unsigned 256-bit integers. It maintains no state on-chain and has no callable functions or access control mechanisms, as it is designed to be used internally by other contracts. There are no events emitted, fees, or upgradeability features. The contract explicitly notes that it does not perform overflow or underflow checks and that division by zero will result in unspecified behavior, requiring calling contracts to handle these conditions externally.
