This contract extends an ERC20 token to include a voting mechanism, allowing token holders to delegate their voting power. It tracks the current delegate for each token holder and maintains a historical record of voting power for both individual delegates and the total supply at different block numbers. Users can explicitly delegate their votes to another address, or they can sign an off-chain message to delegate their votes, which can then be submitted on-chain by anyone. The contract emits events when a delegate changes and when a delegate's voting power is adjusted. It provides functions to query the current voting power of an address, its past voting power at a specific block, and the total voting power of the token at any given block. The contract ensures that queries for past voting power cannot be made for future block numbers and includes safeguards against arithmetic overflows and underflows when updating voting power.
