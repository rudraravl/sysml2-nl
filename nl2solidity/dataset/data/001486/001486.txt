This contract acts as a helper for a primary "store" contract, facilitating the purchase of tokens (NFTs) and managing certain administrative functions on behalf of the store. It maintains the address of the associated store contract and a time offset for transaction validity. Callers can buy individual tokens or packages of tokens by providing a signed message from the contract owner, specifying the token IDs, price, payment token (ETH or ERC20), receiver, and a timestamp. The contract verifies the signature and timestamp, collects the specified payment (transferring ETH or ERC20 tokens), and then instructs the linked store contract to mint the tokens to the designated receiver. The contract owner has exclusive control over administrative functions, including updating the store address, withdrawing ERC20 tokens or ETH held by the contract, setting the transaction offset, and proxying calls to the store contract for actions like editing claiming addresses, royalty fees, token URIs, recovering ERC20s, transferring ownership, and batch minting or minting next tokens.
