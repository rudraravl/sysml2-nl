This contract defines a library of pure functions for calculating and validating protocol fees within a larger system, likely a decentralized exchange or liquidity pool. It does not maintain any on-chain state itself. Its primary purpose is to provide utility functions for managing two distinct protocol fees, one for each direction of a swap (zero-for-one and one-for-zero), ensuring they do not exceed a predefined maximum of 0.1%. Callers can retrieve individual fee values from a packed representation, validate if a combined fee is within acceptable limits, and calculate a total swap fee by combining a protocol fee with a liquidity provider fee. There are no access controls, events, or upgradeability mechanisms, as it serves purely as a stateless calculation utility.
