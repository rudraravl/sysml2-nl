This contract is an ERC4626-compliant vault that allows users to deposit an underlying asset into various lending pools within the ZeroLend ecosystem. It maintains state regarding the vault's asset, its name and symbol, a configurable fee and its recipient, a timelock duration for certain administrative changes, and a list of approved lending pools with their individual supply caps. The contract also tracks pending changes for the timelock and pool caps, and manages a supply and withdraw queue for assets across pools. Callers can deposit assets to receive vault shares, or mint shares directly, and conversely, withdraw assets or redeem shares. These operations can also be performed with slippage protection and a deadline. The contract has a multi-role access control system: an owner can set the skim recipient, modify the fee and its recipient, and submit changes to the timelock; curators can submit changes to pool supply caps and initiate market removals; and allocators manage the supply and withdraw queues and reallocate assets between pools. Guardians, curators, and the owner can revoke pending changes. Important events are emitted for all significant state changes, such as setting fees, updating queues, and reallocating assets. A notable mechanic is the timelock, which delays the activation of certain critical parameter changes, and a skimming function allows the designated recipient to withdraw any arbitrary ERC20 tokens accidentally sent to the contract.
