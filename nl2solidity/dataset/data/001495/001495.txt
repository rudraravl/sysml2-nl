This contract implements a standard ERC-20 compliant token, named "GIEToken", which allows for the creation, transfer, and management of digital tokens. Upon deployment, the contract initializes with a specified name and symbol, and mints a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens to the deployer, who becomes the initial owner. The contract maintains a record of each address's token balance and tracks allowances, which permit one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. Key operations include transferring tokens between accounts, approving other addresses to spend a specific amount of tokens, and transferring tokens on behalf of an approved owner. The contract owner has special permissions to mint new tokens, increasing the total supply, or burn existing tokens, decreasing the total supply. Ownership can be transferred to a new address by the current owner, or renounced entirely. All token transfers and allowance changes emit events, allowing off-chain applications to monitor token activity.
