This contract serves as a basic receiver for ERC-1155 tokens, allowing it to hold these tokens. It doesn't maintain any specific state beyond what's inherent to being an ERC-1155 token holder. The primary operation it performs is responding to incoming ERC-1155 token transfers, both for single tokens and batches of tokens. When an ERC-1155 token or batch of tokens is sent to this contract, it acknowledges receipt by returning the appropriate selector for the `onERC1155Received` or `onERC1155BatchReceived` function, respectively. There are no explicit access controls or permissions for these receiving functions; any ERC-1155 token transfer mechanism can interact with them. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, special mathematical operations, upgradeability features, or integrations with other protocols. Its sole purpose is to be a compliant recipient for ERC-1155 tokens, allowing them to be stored within the contract.
