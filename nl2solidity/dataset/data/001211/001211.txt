This contract serves as a central risk engine for a lending protocol, managing loan-to-value (LTV) ratios and integrating with external risk modules and oracles. It maintains the minimum and maximum global LTV bounds, a mapping of assets to their respective price oracles, and the LTV for each asset within specific pools. It also tracks pending LTV updates for pool-asset pairs, including their proposed LTV and when they become valid. The contract owner can set global LTV bounds, assign oracles to assets, and update the addresses of the core Pool and RiskModule contracts. Pool owners can propose LTV updates for assets within their pools, which are subject to a 24-hour timelock before they can be accepted and become active, and must be accepted within 72 hours. Accepted and rejected LTV updates, oracle assignments, and LTV bound changes all emit events. The contract also provides functions to query position health, validate liquidations, and retrieve risk data by delegating these calls to the configured RiskModule.
