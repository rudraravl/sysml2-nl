This contract facilitates interacting with a decentralized exchange to open and close leveraged long positions on ETH using WETH as collateral. It maintains no internal state beyond its immutable oracle address, instead relying on external GMX-like contracts for all position tracking and order management. Any caller can initiate a long position by providing WETH and an execution fee, specifying the desired leverage and WETH amount. The contract then transfers the WETH and fee to the exchange's order vault and creates a market increase order. Similarly, any caller can initiate closing an existing long position by providing an execution fee; the contract retrieves the current position details and creates a market decrease order to close the entire position. The contract also provides view functions for any caller to retrieve the unique key for its position, fetch the full details of that position, and calculate the profit and loss for a given position key and ETH price. The contract is designed to receive ETH, likely for execution fee refunds from the exchange.
