This contract serves as a foundational implementation for managing fees and collections within a larger ecosystem, specifically interacting with a Locker contract and a native token (like WETH). It tracks a designated beneficiary address and a royalty percentage determining how collected fees are split between the beneficiary and a pool. It also maintains minimum and maximum thresholds for fee donations. Callers can claim accumulated fees if they are the beneficiary, provided the beneficiary is not a pool. The contract owner has exclusive control over setting the beneficiary, their royalty percentage, and the donation thresholds. Important events are emitted for fee distribution, beneficiary updates, and threshold changes. The contract is designed to be extended, with several core functions like `depositFees` and `registerCollection` requiring concrete implementations in derived contracts. It includes reentrancy protection for fee claims.
