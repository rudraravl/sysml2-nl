This contract manages the distribution of BTRFLY tokens as rewards to various recipients. It maintains a list of recipients, each with an associated reward rate, and tracks the BTRFLY token contract address, a treasury contract responsible for minting rewards, and an epoch length defining the reward distribution frequency. The contract also stores information about pending adjustments to reward rates for each recipient. Any caller can trigger the `distribute` function, which, if the current block number has passed the `nextEpochBlock`, will iterate through all active recipients, calculate their rewards based on the total supply of BTRFLY and their individual rates, and instruct the treasury to mint and send these rewards. After each distribution, the contract checks if any rate adjustments are scheduled for that recipient and applies them until a target rate is reached. The contract owner, referred to as the "policy", has exclusive control over adding or removing recipients and setting up or modifying reward rate adjustments. The policy can also transfer ownership to a new address through a two-step process.
