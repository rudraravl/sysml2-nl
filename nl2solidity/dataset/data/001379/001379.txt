This contract implements an ERC-20 token with advanced tokenomics, including reflection, automated liquidity provision, and anti-bot features. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual token balances for each address, and allowances for spending on behalf of others. The contract tracks two types of fees: a reflection fee that redistributes tokens to holders and a tax fee that sends tokens to a designated development/marketing address. These fees can vary for buy and sell transactions. The contract also stores a list of addresses excluded from fees and a list of "bot" addresses that are blocked from transacting. The owner can enable or disable trading, set maximum transaction and wallet size limits, and configure the fee percentages. The contract automatically swaps a portion of its collected tax tokens for ETH via a Uniswap router and sends the ETH to the development/marketing address when a certain token threshold is met. The owner can manually trigger these swap and send operations. The contract emits standard ERC-20 transfer and approval events, along with an ownership transfer event.
