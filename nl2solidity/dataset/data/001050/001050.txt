This contract serves as a base for receiving flash loans, providing common functionality for borrowers. It records the address of a specific flash lending contract at its creation. Callers can implement the `onFlashLoan` function to receive tokens from a standard ERC3156 flash loan, or the `onCreditFlashLoan` function to receive internal credit from a specialized credit flash loan. After receiving a flash loan, the contract includes an internal helper function to approve the flash lender to pull back the borrowed amount in the underlying token, ensuring repayment. It maintains no other state beyond the flash lender's address and does not emit any events. There are no access control mechanisms or fees implemented within this base contract; these would be handled by the specific flash loan implementation.
