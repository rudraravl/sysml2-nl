This contract implements a standard ERC721 non-fungible token, allowing for the creation, ownership, and transfer of unique digital assets. It maintains state for each token's owner, an optional single approved address for each token, and a global approval status for an operator to manage all tokens belonging to a specific owner. Callers can query the owner of a token, the balance of tokens held by an address, and check approvals. Owners can approve another address to manage a specific token or all their tokens, and transfer tokens to other addresses. Transfers can be "safe" to ensure the recipient contract is aware of ERC721 tokens. The contract emits `Transfer`, `Approval`, and `ApprovalForAll` events to signal significant state changes. It includes internal functions for minting and burning tokens, and for attaching auxiliary and extra data to owners and tokens respectively, which are not directly exposed to external callers. The contract is designed for efficiency, with some internal optimizations and checks for common ERC721 errors like non-existent tokens or zero address transfers.
