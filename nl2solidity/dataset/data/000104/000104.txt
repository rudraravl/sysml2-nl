This contract extends a standard non-fungible token (NFT) to enable voting capabilities, where each NFT held by an address represents one voting unit. It tracks the voting power of token holders and allows them to delegate their votes to another address. The contract maintains the ownership of NFTs and the delegated voting power for each address. Callers can mint and transfer NFTs, which automatically updates the voting power of the involved addresses. When an NFT is transferred, the voting units are moved from the previous owner to the new owner. Similarly, when NFTs are minted, the new owner gains voting units. The contract emits events whenever a delegate's voting power changes, allowing off-chain systems to monitor voting dynamics. There are no explicit fees or upgradeability mechanisms described.
