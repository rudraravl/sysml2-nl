This contract provides mathematical utility functions for calculating token amounts based on liquidity and square root price ratios, primarily for automated market maker (AMM) protocols. It does not maintain any state on-chain. Callers can use `getAmount0Delta` to determine the amount of token0 required or produced when moving between two specified square root price ratios with a given liquidity, and `getAmount1Delta` for the equivalent calculation for token1. Both functions allow for rounding up or down, which is crucial for ensuring correct token transfers in AMM contexts. There are no access controls, events, or fees associated with these pure mathematical operations. The contract's purpose is to offer precise calculations for liquidity management within a decentralized exchange environment.
