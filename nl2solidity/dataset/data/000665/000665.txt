This contract acts as a centralized manager for reward emissions, maintaining a mapping of specific rewards to their designated emission administrators. It also stores a reference to a Rewards Controller contract, which handles the actual reward distribution logic. The contract owner has exclusive permission to set the Rewards Controller, assign or update the emission administrator for any given reward, and set a claimer for a user. Each emission administrator can configure assets for their assigned reward, set its transfer strategy, define its oracle for price feeds, and adjust its distribution end time. Additionally, an emission administrator can modify the emission rate per second for assets associated with their managed reward. An event is emitted whenever an emission administrator is updated.
