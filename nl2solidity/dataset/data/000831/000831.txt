This contract serves as a foundational building block for any other contract that needs to receive ERC-1155 tokens. Its primary purpose is to declare that it is capable of handling ERC-1155 token transfers, specifically by implementing the `onERC1155Received` and `onERC1155BatchReceived` functions (though these specific functions are not detailed here, their existence is implied by the interface). The contract maintains no internal state beyond its inherent ability to signal its compatibility. There are no operations callers can perform directly on this contract; it simply provides a standard interface for other contracts to interact with when sending ERC-1155 tokens. Access control is not applicable as it's an abstract interface implementation. No events are emitted by this contract itself. Its notable mechanic is its adherence to the ERC-165 standard, allowing other contracts to query its support for the ERC-1155 receiver interface.
