This contract provides a set of utility functions for safely interacting with ERC-20 tokens and native Ether. Its purpose is to facilitate reliable token transfers, token approvals, and Ether transfers within other smart contracts. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather acts as a helper for other contracts. Callers can initiate a transfer of ERC-20 tokens from a specified address to a recipient, transfer ERC-20 tokens directly from their own balance to a recipient, approve another address to spend a certain amount of a specific ERC-20 token, or transfer native Ether to a recipient. All operations include robust error handling, reverting if the underlying token or Ether transfer fails. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms implemented within this utility contract.
