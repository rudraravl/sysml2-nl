This contract serves as a permanent configuration registry for a dual-silo lending protocol, storing all immutable parameters for two distinct lending pools (silos) identified by a unique `SILO_ID`. It maintains various addresses for tokens, share tokens, oracles, and interest rate models, along with numerical parameters like fees, maximum loan-to-value ratios, and liquidation thresholds for each silo. The contract also tracks which silo a borrower has designated as their collateral silo. Callers can retrieve comprehensive configuration data for either silo, including details relevant for solvency checks, withdrawals, or borrowing operations. It allows authorized silo contracts to set a borrower's collateral silo and facilitates interest accrual for one or both silos. The contract ensures that only designated debt share tokens can trigger debt transfer logic, and it includes re-entrancy protection.
