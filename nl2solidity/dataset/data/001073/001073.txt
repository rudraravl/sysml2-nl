This contract serves as a centralized registry for various blockchain addresses, associating a human-readable string key with a specific address. It maintains a mapping of these string keys to their corresponding addresses. Callers can retrieve the address associated with any of the predefined keys, such as "WETH", "BALANCER_VAULT", or "USDC". The contract itself does not perform any operations beyond providing these address lookups; it simply stores and exposes these constant values. There are no functions to modify the stored addresses, nor any access control mechanisms, events, or complex mechanics like fees or upgradeability. Its sole purpose is to offer a consistent and easily referenceable source for important contract and token addresses within a specific ecosystem.
