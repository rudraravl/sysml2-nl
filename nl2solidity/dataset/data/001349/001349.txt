This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "Brother Shiba" with a fixed total supply. It tracks token balances for all holders and allows for standard ERC-20 operations like transferring tokens and managing allowances. A key feature is a reflection mechanism: a portion of each transfer is burned, reducing the total supply and increasing the value of remaining tokens for holders. The contract owner has special privileges, including the ability to set a maximum transaction amount to prevent large transfers and to exclude specific addresses from the reflection mechanism, effectively making them immune to the burn. All token transfers and allowance approvals emit standard ERC-20 events.
