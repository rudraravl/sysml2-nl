This contract extends a governance system to define how many votes are needed for a proposal to pass, known as the quorum. It maintains a historical record of a "quorum numerator," which is a component of the quorum calculation. Callers can query the current quorum numerator or its value at any past point in time. The contract calculates the total quorum required for a given timepoint by multiplying the token's total supply at that time by the stored numerator and dividing by a fixed denominator, which defaults to 100. The quorum numerator can only be updated through a governance proposal, ensuring that changes are approved by the community. When the numerator is updated, an event is emitted, logging both the old and new values. The contract enforces that the quorum numerator can never exceed the denominator, preventing invalid quorum fractions.
