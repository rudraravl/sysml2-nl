This contract provides mathematical utilities for converting between "ticks" and "square root prices" in a system that models prices using a base of 1.0001. It does not maintain any on-chain state; all its operations are pure computations. The contract offers two primary functions: `getSqrtRatioAtTick`, which takes an integer tick value and returns a fixed-point Q64.96 number representing the square root of the price at that tick, and `getTickAtSqrtRatio`, which performs the inverse operation, taking a Q64.96 square root price and returning the corresponding integer tick. Both functions enforce bounds on their inputs and outputs, reverting if a tick is outside the defined minimum and maximum range or if a square root price is outside its valid range. There are no access control mechanisms, fees, or events, as its sole purpose is to provide these specific mathematical conversions for other contracts to utilize.
