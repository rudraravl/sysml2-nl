This contract is a vault that manages deposits and withdrawals of a specific asset, adhering to the ERC-4626 standard. It maintains a queue of external pools where assets can be supplied and withdrawn, and tracks the total assets held, the last recorded total assets, and a performance fee. Callers can query the maximum amount of assets they can deposit or mint shares for, and the maximum amount of assets or shares they can withdraw or redeem. The contract calculates these maximums by considering the available liquidity across the configured supply and withdrawal queues, applying any supply caps, and accounting for accrued performance fees. These calculations involve converting between assets and shares, with careful rounding to ensure accuracy. The contract also provides a revision number for tracking updates.
