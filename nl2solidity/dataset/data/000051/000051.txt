This contract extends a governance system to incorporate a "super quorum" mechanism, allowing proposals that achieve a higher threshold of votes to advance more quickly. It maintains a historical record of the super quorum numerator, which is a fraction of the total token supply. Callers can query the current super quorum numerator or its value at a specific point in time. The super quorum itself is calculated based on this numerator and the total token supply at a given time. The super quorum numerator can be updated, but only through a governance proposal, ensuring that changes are approved by the community. When updated, the new super quorum numerator must be less than or equal to the quorum denominator and greater than or equal to the standard quorum numerator. Similarly, the standard quorum numerator cannot be set higher than the current super quorum numerator. An event is emitted whenever the super quorum numerator is updated, providing transparency for off-chain monitoring.
