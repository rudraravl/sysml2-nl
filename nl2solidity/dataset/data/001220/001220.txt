This contract implements a multi-token standard, allowing users to manage balances of various token IDs. It tracks the balance of each token ID for every owner, and also maintains allowances for specific spenders to transfer tokens on an owner's behalf, as well as operator approvals that grant full control over all of an owner's tokens. Owners can transfer their own tokens, approve other addresses to spend a specific amount of a specific token ID, or designate an address as an operator to manage all their tokens. Approved spenders or operators can then transfer tokens from the owner's balance. The contract emits events for every token transfer, approval, and operator status change, enabling off-chain systems to track all activity. It also supports the ERC165 interface for introspection.
