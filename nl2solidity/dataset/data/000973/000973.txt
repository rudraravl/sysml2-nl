This contract acts as a manager or proxy, forwarding calls to other pre-deployed contracts: an `InvariantUser` contract and an `InvariantHandler` contract. It stores the addresses of these two contracts, initialized during deployment, and also allows setting the address of a `Tester` contract after deployment. Callers can trigger various deposit, withdrawal, borrow, and payback operations on the `InvariantUser` contract, specifying amounts, token indices, and NFT IDs. Additionally, it provides functions to initiate deposit/withdraw loops and a borrow/payback loop on the `InvariantHandler` contract. There are no access control mechanisms implemented within this contract; any external caller can invoke its public functions, which in turn call the respective functions on the linked contracts. No events are emitted by this contract itself, and it does not manage any internal state beyond the addresses of the linked contracts.
