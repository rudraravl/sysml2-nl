This contract implements an ERC-4626 compliant vault, allowing users to deposit an underlying ERC-20 asset and receive "shares" in return, which are themselves ERC-20 tokens. The contract maintains the address of the underlying asset and its decimal precision. Callers can deposit assets to mint shares, or mint a specific amount of shares by providing the corresponding assets. Conversely, users can redeem their shares to withdraw the underlying assets, or withdraw a specific amount of assets by burning shares. The contract provides preview functions to estimate the shares received for a given asset deposit, or assets received for a share redemption. It also includes `maxDeposit`, `maxMint`, `maxWithdraw`, and `maxRedeem` functions to indicate the maximum amounts available for these operations for a given user. All deposit and withdrawal operations emit `Deposit` and `Withdraw` events, respectively, detailing the caller, receiver, owner, asset amount, and share amount involved. The contract manages internal transfers of the underlying asset and share tokens, and incorporates a mechanism to mitigate inflation attacks by adjusting the share-to-asset conversion rate using a configurable decimal offset.
