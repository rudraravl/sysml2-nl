This contract provides a set of mathematical utility functions for signed 256-bit integers. It does not maintain any state on-chain, nor does it have any specific purpose beyond offering these calculations. Callers can compute the maximum of two signed integers, the minimum of two signed integers, the average of two signed integers (rounded towards zero), and the absolute unsigned value of a signed integer. There are no access controls, permissions, or events, as all operations are pure computations. This contract is designed to be a foundational utility, offering common signed math operations that might be missing from the core Solidity language, without integrating with other protocols or implementing any complex mechanics like fees or upgradeability.
