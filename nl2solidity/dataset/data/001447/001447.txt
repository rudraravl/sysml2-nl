This contract acts as a meta-transaction forwarder, enabling users to interact with other contracts without directly paying for gas. It maintains a record of nonces for each sending address to prevent replay attacks. A user wishing to perform a transaction constructs a "ForwardRequest" containing the intended recipient, value, gas limit, data, and their current nonce. This request is then signed off-chain by the user. Any third party, known as a relayer, can then submit this signed request to the forwarder's `execute` function. The forwarder verifies the signature against the user's address and nonce, increments the nonce, and then executes the requested transaction on behalf of the original user, forwarding any attached value and gas. The contract ensures the relayer provides sufficient gas for the forwarded call and reverts if the signature is invalid or the nonce is incorrect.
