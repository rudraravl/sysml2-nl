This contract serves as a mock implementation for a StakeHouse Universe, primarily for testing or development purposes. It maintains state regarding the active status of "KNOTs" identified by their BLS public keys, and the associated StakeHouse address for each KNOT. Callers can update the active status of a KNOT and set its associated StakeHouse address. The contract provides read-only functions to retrieve information about KNOTs and StakeHouses, such as the number of StakeHouses (always zero), the StakeHouse at a given index (always zero address), and detailed KNOT information by coordinates or BLS public key. Most queries return default, hardcoded values, except for the KNOT's active status and associated StakeHouse, which reflect the state set by the `setIsActive` and `setAssociatedHouseForKnot` functions. There are no access controls, fees, or events implemented.
