This contract provides a set of safe mathematical operations for unsigned integers, designed to prevent common arithmetic errors like overflow, underflow, and division by zero. It does not maintain any on-chain state itself. Callers can perform multiplication, division, addition, subtraction, and a combined add-then-subtract operation. Each function takes two or three unsigned integer inputs and returns a tuple indicating whether a mathematical error occurred and the result of the operation. The possible errors are `NO_ERROR`, `DIVISION_BY_ZERO`, `INTEGER_OVERFLOW`, and `INTEGER_UNDERFLOW`. There are no access controls, events, or special mechanics as its sole purpose is to offer robust mathematical utility functions for other contracts to use.
