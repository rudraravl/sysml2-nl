This contract serves as a vault for managing staked ETH and its derivatives, specifically dETH and savETH, in a liquid staking ecosystem. It maintains state regarding the associated liquid staking manager, a unique index ID for the vault's savETH, and details about dETH for each "Knot" (validator), including its savETH balance and whether it has been withdrawn. Users can deposit ETH for staking, either individually or in batches, by specifying BLS public keys and amounts, provided the keys are registered and not banned. The contract issues LP tokens in return for these deposits. Callers can burn these LP tokens, either individually or in batches by BLS key or LP token address, to claim either the original ETH (if not yet staked and after a 30-minute cooldown) or dETH (if derivatives have been minted). The liquid staking manager has exclusive permission to withdraw ETH for staking to a specified smart wallet. Events are emitted to signal dETH redemptions and ETH withdrawals for staking. The contract prevents re-entrancy during fund transfers.
