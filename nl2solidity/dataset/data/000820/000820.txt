This contract defines a standard interface for a governance system, compatible with Compound's Governor Bravo. It doesn't maintain any state itself but outlines how a compliant governance contract would store and manage proposals. Callers can query the current quorum needed for proposals, retrieve detailed information about any past or present proposal by its ID, and fetch a voter's specific receipt for a given proposal. The primary operations involve creating new proposals with target addresses, values, function signatures, and call data, queuing proposals that have passed their voting period, and executing queued proposals once their waiting period has elapsed. Additionally, a proposer can cancel their own proposal under certain conditions. This interface ensures interoperability with systems expecting the Governor Bravo standard for decentralized autonomous organizations.
