This contract extends a Governor to persistently store the details of proposed governance actions. It maintains a list of all proposal identifiers and a mapping from each proposal ID to its specific details, including the target addresses, associated Ether values, function calls, and a hash of the proposal's description. When a new proposal is created, its full details are recorded. Callers can then queue, execute, or cancel a proposal by simply providing its unique ID, rather than needing to re-submit all the original parameters. The contract also allows querying the total number of stored proposals and retrieving the full details of any proposal by its ID or by its sequential index in the stored list. If a query is made for a non-existent proposal, the transaction will revert.
