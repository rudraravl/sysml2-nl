This contract serves as a decentralized oracle for retrieving USD-denominated asset prices and converting them to an equivalent ETH value. It maintains immutable references to a Chainlink Arbitrum sequencer uptime feed and a Chainlink ETH/USD price feed, along with a mapping of assets to their respective Chainlink USD price feeds and their associated stale price thresholds. The contract owner can set or update the Chainlink USD price feed for any asset, specifying a threshold for how old a price update can be before it's considered invalid, and this action emits an event. Callers can query the equivalent ETH value of a given amount of any supported asset. This query involves checking the L2 sequencer's health, ensuring it's not down or within a grace period after recovery, and then fetching the asset's USD price and the ETH/USD price, both with checks for staleness and non-positive values, before performing the conversion.
