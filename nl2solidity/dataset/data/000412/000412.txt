This contract serves as a read-only utility for interacting with an external PoolManager contract, providing functions to retrieve specific state variables from various pools and positions managed by that PoolManager. It does not maintain any state of its own, nor does it have any access control or permissions. Callers can query pool-specific data such as the current price, tick, and fees, or retrieve detailed information about individual ticks including liquidity and fee growth. It also allows fetching global fee growth for a pool, total liquidity, and tick bitmap data. Furthermore, callers can retrieve comprehensive details about liquidity positions, either by providing owner, tick bounds, and a salt, or directly by a position ID, including liquidity and fee growth within that position. The contract also offers a calculated fee growth inside a specific tick range, derived from global and tick-specific fee growth values. All operations are read-only and do not alter any on-chain state.
