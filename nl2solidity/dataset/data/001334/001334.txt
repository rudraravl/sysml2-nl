This contract facilitates a trustless swap of Ether for another asset, likely Monero, between two parties, Alice (the contract owner) and Bob (the claimer). It holds the Ether in escrow, which is deposited upon creation. The contract tracks two public keys derived from secrets, one for Bob to claim the Ether and one for Alice to refund it, along with two timeouts. Alice can signal that she has seen the other asset locked by calling `set_ready` within the first timeout. If she does, Bob can claim the Ether using his secret until the second timeout. If Alice doesn't call `set_ready` or if the second timeout passes, Alice can refund the Ether to herself using her secret. The contract ensures that only the designated claimer can claim and only the owner can refund or set the ready state. All state changes and claims/refunds emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract uses an internal Ed25519 library to verify the secrets against the provided public keys before releasing funds.
