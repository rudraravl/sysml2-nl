This contract facilitates profit-maximizing token swaps on a Uniswap V2-like decentralized exchange. It maintains references to the factory and router contracts of the exchange. The primary operation, `swapToPrice`, allows any caller to swap an amount of either token A or token B to align the on-chain price with an externally provided "true price" ratio. The contract calculates the optimal trade direction and input amount to maximize profit based on current reserves and the true price. Callers must pre-approve the contract to spend the token they intend to swap. The contract then transfers the calculated input amount from the caller to itself, approves the router to spend this amount, and executes a `swapExactTokensForTokens` transaction through the router, sending the output tokens to a specified recipient. The contract enforces that both components of the true price ratio are non-zero and that at least one maximum spend limit is provided.
