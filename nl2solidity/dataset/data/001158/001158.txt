This contract implements the core logic for managing a lending pool's reserve. It maintains the state of a specific reserve, including its liquidity index, borrow index, and interest rates. Callers can initialize a reserve by providing an interest rate strategy address, and the contract will set initial liquidity and borrow indexes. The contract automatically updates the reserve's state, including indexes and accrued treasury amounts, whenever an interaction occurs or time passes. It also allows for the accumulation of instantaneous income, such as flash loan fees, into the liquidity index. Interest rates are dynamically updated based on liquidity added or taken, total debt, and a reserve factor, by interacting with an external interest rate strategy contract. All updates to interest rates and reserve data emit an event, allowing off-chain services to track changes. The contract uses various mathematical utilities for precise calculations and guards against re-entrancy by updating state before transfers.
