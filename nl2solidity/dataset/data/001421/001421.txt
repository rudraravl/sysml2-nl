This contract implements a standard ERC20 token named "IPChainToken" with the symbol "IPChain" and 18 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks the balance of each token holder, as well as allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Upon deployment, the entire initial supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens is minted and assigned to the contract creator. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend a specific amount of their tokens, and transfer tokens on behalf of another address if an allowance has been granted. The contract emits `Transfer` events when tokens are moved and `Approval` events when allowances are set or modified, allowing off-chain applications to track token activity. All arithmetic operations are protected against overflows and underflows to ensure secure token accounting.
