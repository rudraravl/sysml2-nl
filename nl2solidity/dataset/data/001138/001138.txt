This contract defines a data structure called `OraclePack` which efficiently stores and manages price oracle data within a single 256-bit word. It maintains eight recent price observations as "residuals" relative to a `referenceTick`, four Exponential Moving Averages (EMAs) at different timescales (spot, fast, slow, eons), a timestamp, and a lock mode. The core functionality revolves around packing and unpacking this data, allowing for the retrieval of individual components like EMAs, residuals, reference tick, and timestamp. It includes a mechanism to update the EMAs based on a new price observation and a time delta, ensuring that EMAs converge towards the new tick while preventing excessive convergence after periods of inactivity. A crucial feature is the ability to insert new price observations, which involves updating the residuals and maintaining their sorted order via an `orderMap`, and rebasing the `referenceTick` if the new observation is too far from the current reference. It also incorporates a `clampTick` function to prevent manipulation by limiting new observations to a certain deviation from the last recorded tick. The contract does not have external access control or emit events; its purpose is purely for internal data management and calculation within a larger system.
