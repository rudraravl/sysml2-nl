This contract serves as a delegated implementation for an ERC-20 token wrapper, allowing for upgradeability of its logic. It maintains no unique state beyond what it inherits from its parent contract, acting purely as a blueprint for behavior. Callers can trigger two primary operations: `_becomeImplementation` and `_resignImplementation`. Both functions are restricted to the contract's designated administrator, ensuring only authorized parties can initiate or revoke its role as an active implementation. These functions are designed to be called by a delegator contract to switch the underlying logic it uses, though the current implementation of these functions does not perform any specific actions beyond an access control check. No events are emitted, and there are no fees or direct integrations with other protocols.
