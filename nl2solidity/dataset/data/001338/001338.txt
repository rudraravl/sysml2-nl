This contract acts as a privileged proxy for managing conduits through a designated conduit controller. Its sole purpose is to allow a single, immutable "conduit creator" address, set at deployment, to remotely invoke specific functions on other conduit controller contracts. The contract itself does not maintain any state beyond this initial creator address. The authorized conduit creator can initiate the creation of new conduits on a specified controller, providing a unique key and an initial owner for the new conduit. Additionally, the creator can initiate or cancel ownership transfers for existing conduits managed by a given controller, by specifying the conduit's address and the new potential owner. All operations are strictly restricted to the designated conduit creator; any other caller will be rejected with an `InvalidConduitCreator` error.
