This contract provides a utility for managing and retrieving deployment addresses of "Silo Incentives Controllers" associated with specific share tokens within a Silo protocol configuration. It doesn't maintain any on-chain state itself, but rather interacts with an external key-value storage system to persist and retrieve deployment addresses. Callers can save a deployment address for a given chain and share token, or retrieve a previously saved address. The contract constructs a unique, human-readable name for each deployment based on the associated Silo configuration, asset symbols, and share token type (Collateral, Protected, or Debt). This naming convention ensures clarity and organization of stored addresses. There are no access control mechanisms or events emitted, as its primary purpose is to serve as a helper library for deployment management.
