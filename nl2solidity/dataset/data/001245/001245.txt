This contract provides a mechanism to retrieve a conversion price directly from a decentralized exchange (DEX) pool's storage. It is specifically designed for use with vaults that have no borrow limits. The contract maintains no mutable state of its own; instead, it reads a specific slot from an external DEX contract to extract the relevant pricing information. Callers can query two distinct functions, `_getDexReservesConversionPriceOperate` and `_getDexReservesConversionPriceLiquidate`, both of which perform the same operation: they read a raw value from the DEX's storage, then apply a series of bitwise operations to parse and scale this value into a usable conversion price, representing the amount of one asset per unit of another, scaled to 1e27. There are no access control mechanisms, events, or fees associated with these operations, as its sole purpose is to provide a read-only price oracle by directly interpreting the DEX's internal data structure.
