This contract creates a non-fungible token (NFT) collection named "AstroWorld Test" with the symbol "ASTRO", adhering to the ERC721 standard. It tracks ownership of unique tokens, allowing users to query their balance of tokens, identify the owner of a specific token, and retrieve metadata like the token's name, symbol, and a unique URI for each token. Callers can approve other addresses to transfer their tokens, either for a single token or for all tokens they own. Tokens can be transferred between addresses, with a "safe" transfer option that verifies if the recipient contract is designed to handle ERC721 tokens. The contract maintains a mapping of token owners and approved addresses. The primary operation is the `AstroTestPass` function, which allows any caller to mint a new, unique "AstroWorld Test" token and assign it to themselves, automatically setting a default IPFS URI for the newly minted token. The contract emits `Transfer` and `Approval` events to signal changes in token ownership and approval status.
