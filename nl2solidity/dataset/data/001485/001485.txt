This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token named "EggToken" with the symbol "EGG", and includes a robust governance delegation system. The contract maintains a total supply of EGG tokens and individual balances for each address, along with allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract owner, initially the deployer, has the exclusive ability to mint new EGG tokens to any address. Beyond standard ERC-20 functionalities like transferring tokens and managing allowances, the contract allows token holders to delegate their voting power to another address, either directly or via a signed message, enabling off-chain signature delegation. It tracks historical voting power through checkpoints, allowing queries for an account's votes at any past block number. Changes in delegation or voting power emit events, facilitating off-chain monitoring of governance activities. The contract also includes standard access control for ownership and uses SafeMath for secure arithmetic operations.
