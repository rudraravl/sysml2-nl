This contract manages a raffle for unique digital puppy collectibles, which are ERC721 tokens. It maintains a list of active participants, the duration of the raffle, the entrance fee, and an address designated to receive fees. Participants can enter the raffle by providing a list of addresses and paying the entrance fee for each, which must be exact. Duplicate entries are prevented. A participant can request a refund for their entry, removing them from the active player list and returning their entrance fee. After a set duration and with a minimum number of participants, the contract owner can trigger a winner selection. The winner receives 80% of the collected funds and a newly minted puppy NFT with a randomly determined rarity. The remaining 20% of the funds are transferred to the designated fee address. The contract owner can change the fee address. Events are emitted when players enter, are refunded, or when the fee address changes.
