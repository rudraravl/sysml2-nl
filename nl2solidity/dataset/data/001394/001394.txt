This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "DONT BUY" with the symbol "COPYTRADERS" and 9 decimal places. It maintains a total supply and tracks individual token balances, along with allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract incorporates a reflection mechanism where a portion of each transaction is distributed to existing token holders, and also applies buy and sell taxes. These fees are dynamically set and can differ for buying and selling operations, with a portion directed to a development address and a marketing address. The contract integrates with Uniswap V2 for liquidity and includes anti-bot measures, transaction limits, and wallet size restrictions. An owner can enable/disable trading, block/unblock specific addresses, adjust fee percentages, and modify transaction and wallet size limits. The contract also allows the owner to manually trigger token swaps for ETH and send accumulated ETH to the fee addresses. Ownership can be transferred or renounced, and important actions like transfers and approvals emit corresponding events.
