This contract provides a utility for calculating accumulated swap fees within a specific liquidity range of a Uniswap V3 pool. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather performs calculations based on provided parameters. The primary operation is to calculate the total AMM swap fees, for both token0 and token1, that a given amount of liquidity has earned within a defined price tick range. This calculation considers the current price tick of the Uniswap pool and the lower and upper ticks of the liquidity chunk. The contract interacts with a Uniswap V3 pool to retrieve fee growth data. The result of the fee calculation is returned as a packed value, indicating the fees for each token. There are no access controls, events, or special mechanics like fees or upgradeability, as it functions purely as a stateless calculation library.
