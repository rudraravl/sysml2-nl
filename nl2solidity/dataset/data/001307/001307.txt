This contract implements a custom ERC-20 token named "Satoshi AI" with the symbol "SATAI" and 10 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract's owner, set at deployment, has special privileges to manage a "liquidity" status for specific addresses. The owner can add or remove addresses from this liquidity list. Token transfers and approvals are restricted: neither the sender, recipient, approver, nor spender can be on the liquidity list. The contract emits standard ERC-20 Transfer and Approval events for all token movements and allowance changes, and an OwnershipTransferred event when the contract's owner changes. The owner can also renounce ownership, transferring it to the zero address.
