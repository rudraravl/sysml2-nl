This contract serves as a comprehensive data structure library for a decentralized exchange (DEX) system, specifically designed to manage and track various states and configurations related to liquidity, pricing, and user interactions. It defines several complex data structures, including `DexState` to store dynamic DEX parameters like prices, timestamps, and share totals; `ShiftData` and `CenterPriceShift` for managing price and range adjustments over time; `Configs` to hold all configurable parameters of the DEX, such as fees, ranges, and utilization limits; and `SwapLimitsAndAvailability` which aggregates current liquidity, available amounts for swapping, and utilization limits. Additionally, it defines `UserSupplyData` and `UserBorrowData` to detail individual user's supply and borrow positions, including limits and expansion parameters. The contract itself does not implement any executable logic or state-changing functions; its sole purpose is to provide a standardized schema for other contracts within the DEX ecosystem to store and retrieve structured data, ensuring consistency and interoperability across different components.
