This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "El Protocol" with a fixed total supply and a reflection mechanism. It tracks token balances for each address, along with allowances for spending on behalf of others. The contract maintains several fee parameters for buys and sells, including a reflection fee and a tax fee, which are applied during transfers. It also stores addresses for a development fund and a marketing fund, and integrates with a Uniswap V2 Router to facilitate token swaps for ETH. Key state variables include the current trading status, whether an automated swap is in progress, and configurable limits for transaction amounts and wallet sizes. The contract owner has exclusive control over setting trading status, managing bot blacklists, adjusting fee percentages, and modifying transaction and wallet size limits. The owner can also manually trigger token swaps for ETH and send accumulated ETH to the marketing address. Transfers are subject to various checks, including maximum transaction and wallet size limits, and a bot blacklist. Fees are dynamically applied based on whether the transfer is a buy or sell transaction, with certain addresses being exempt from fees. A portion of the fees is reflected back to token holders, while another portion is sent to the contract itself to be swapped for ETH and distributed to the marketing address. Events are emitted for token transfers and approvals, as well as for changes in ownership.
