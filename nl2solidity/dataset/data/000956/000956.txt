This contract acts as a multi-asset reward distributor for a lending protocol, managing the distribution of various ERC-20 tokens as incentives for supplying and borrowing assets within different markets. It maintains state for each market, including an array of emission configurations, each tied to a unique reward token, its owner, emission rates for supply and borrow, and an end time. The contract also stores the address of the associated Comptroller and a designated pause guardian. Callers can query current owners of emission configurations, retrieve all configurations for a given market, or get a specific configuration. Users can also view their outstanding rewards across all markets or for a specific market. The Comptroller's administrator, typically a timelock, can add new emission configurations, sweep accidentally sent tokens, set a new pause guardian, or adjust the global emission cap. The Comptroller itself can trigger updates to market supply and borrow indices, and disburse rewards to suppliers and borrowers. A designated pause guardian can pause all token emissions, preventing transfers, but accruals continue; only the Comptroller's administrator can unpause. The contract emits events for new configurations, rescued funds, pause guardian changes, and emission cap updates. It incorporates re-entrancy protection and ensures that emission speeds do not exceed a predefined cap.
