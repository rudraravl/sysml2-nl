This contract manages collateralized debt positions (CDPs), allowing users to deposit collateral and borrow against it. It maintains individual user positions, tracking their collateral and debt, along with global vault parameters like total debt, debt floor, and liquidation ratios. Users can deposit and withdraw collateral tokens, and borrow or repay underlying tokens. The contract integrates with an oracle for collateral pricing and a pool for managing credit. Access control is enforced through roles, allowing specific administrators to configure vault parameters and pause operations. Important events are emitted for position modifications, parameter changes, and liquidations. A key mechanic is the liquidation process, where an unsafe position's collateral can be sold at a discounted price to repay debt, with a penalty applied. The contract also manages accrued interest and integrates with a reward controller.
