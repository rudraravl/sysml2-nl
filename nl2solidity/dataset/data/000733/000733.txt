This contract serves as a router for interacting with a Uniswap V2 decentralized exchange, facilitating liquidity provision and token swaps. It maintains immutable references to the Uniswap V2 factory contract and the Wrapped Ether (WETH) token address. Users can add liquidity to existing or new token pairs, either with two ERC-20 tokens or one ERC-20 token and native ETH, receiving liquidity tokens in return. They can also remove liquidity, burning their liquidity tokens to reclaim underlying assets, with options for permit-based approvals. Furthermore, the contract enables various token swap operations, allowing users to exchange exact amounts of tokens for minimum output, or vice-versa, across multiple token pairs, including direct ETH interactions. All operations include a deadline parameter to prevent stale transactions. The contract does not emit custom events but relies on events emitted by the underlying Uniswap V2 pair contracts. It does not implement any fees or upgradeability mechanisms directly.
