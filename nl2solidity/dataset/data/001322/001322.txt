This contract suite implements a decentralized exchange (DEX) system, similar to Uniswap V2, allowing users to swap ERC-20 tokens and provide liquidity. The core component is the `MoxieSwapFactory`, which manages the creation of unique `MoxieSwapPair` contracts for any two distinct ERC-20 tokens. Each `MoxieSwapPair` contract acts as an automated market maker (AMM) for its specific token pair, holding reserves of both tokens and issuing liquidity tokens (MoxieSwapERC20) to liquidity providers. Users can add or remove liquidity to earn a share of trading fees, or swap tokens against the pair's reserves. The factory maintains a record of all created pairs and allows an authorized `feeToSetter` to designate an address (`feeTo`) to receive a portion of trading fees, and a `migrator` address for potential liquidity migration. All operations involving token transfers and liquidity calculations are protected against common arithmetic overflows. Key events are emitted for pair creation, liquidity minting/burning, and token swaps, enabling off-chain monitoring.
