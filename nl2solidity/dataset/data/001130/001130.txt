This contract provides a suite of mathematical utilities and helper functions primarily for managing positions and liquidity within a Uniswap V3-like environment. It does not maintain any on-chain state itself, acting purely as a stateless library. Callers can perform operations such as incrementing a pool identifier, counting leading zero hex characters in an address, and retrieving an ERC20 token's symbol. Key functionalities include updating a homomorphic hash that tracks a user's open positions and their leg counts, and checking for duplicate token IDs within an array. The contract also offers advanced oracle calculations to compute median observed prices and Time-Weighted Average Prices (TWAPs) from Uniswap V3 pools, along with functions to determine liquidity chunks, tick ranges, and associated notional values for option positions. There are no access control mechanisms, fees, or upgradeability features as it's a pure utility library.
