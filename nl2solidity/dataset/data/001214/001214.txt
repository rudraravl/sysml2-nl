This contract serves as a factory for creating SuperPools, which are financial instruments designed to aggregate assets. It maintains a record of a fixed underlying POOL address, established at deployment, and tracks which SuperPools were deployed by this factory. Callers can deploy new SuperPools by providing an owner, the asset type, a fee recipient, a fee percentage, a maximum capacity, an initial deposit amount, a name, and a symbol. The contract ensures that if a fee is specified, a valid fee recipient is provided, and that a minimum amount of initial shares from the deposit are burned. Upon successful deployment, ownership of the new SuperPool is transferred to the specified owner, and a `SuperPoolDeployed` event is emitted, detailing the new SuperPool's parameters. The factory facilitates the initial deposit and burning of shares within the newly created SuperPool.
