This contract implements a minimal on-chain governance system. It maintains a record of proposals, each identified by a unique ID, storing the target address and call data for the proposed action, the total votes received, and whether it has been executed. It also tracks which addresses have voted on each proposal. The contract is initialized with a specific governance token and a quorum threshold. Callers can propose new actions, which increments a proposal counter and stores the details. Any address can vote on an active proposal, and their vote weight is determined by their current balance of the governance token. Once a proposal has accumulated enough votes to meet the quorum, any caller can trigger its execution, which marks the proposal as executed and attempts to perform the proposed action on the target address. The contract prevents double-voting and re-execution of proposals.
