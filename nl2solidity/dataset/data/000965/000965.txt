This contract serves as a price oracle for Pendle LP tokens, providing their value in USD. It maintains immutable references to a specific Pendle market, an external price feed for an asset (expected to be ETH) in USD, and another oracle for Pendle PT tokens, along with a configured TWAP duration and a descriptive name. The core functionality is the `latestAnswer` function, which calculates the LP token's USD value by combining the external asset price feed's latest data with a time-weighted average price (TWAP) from the Pendle PT oracle, ensuring specific cardinality and observation age requirements are met. It also exposes a `decimals` function and a `latestRoundData` function to conform to common oracle interfaces, returning the calculated price. There are no access control mechanisms or events; its purpose is solely to provide a calculated price.
