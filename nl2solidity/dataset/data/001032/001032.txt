This contract establishes a staking mechanism where users can deposit a designated staking token to earn multiple reward tokens. Stakers can choose to lock their tokens for a fixed duration to receive a boosted reward rate. The contract maintains records of each user's unlocked and locked staking token balances, along with individual lock details including amount and unlock time. It also tracks the total supply of both unlocked and boosted locked tokens, and the minimum amount required for a lock. Owners can add new reward tokens, set a minimum lock amount, and recover accidentally sent tokens (excluding the staking token or excess staking token). Operators are responsible for distributing new reward amounts for each token and processing expired user locks, which moves locked tokens to the unlocked balance. Users can stake tokens, optionally locking them for a boost, or lock existing unlocked balances. They can withdraw unlocked tokens, claim accumulated rewards, or withdraw tokens along with their rewards. The contract can be paused and unpaused by the owner, preventing most user interactions during a paused state. Key events are emitted for staking, locking, unlocking, withdrawing, reward additions, and reward payments, allowing external systems to monitor contract activity.
