This contract is an ERC-20 token representing shares in a Pendle Power Farm, allowing users to deposit and withdraw underlying Pendle LP assets. It tracks the total underlying LP assets, both currently held and those awaiting distribution, along with a minting fee and the last time the contract interacted with the Pendle protocol. The contract is initialized once with the underlying Pendle market, a Pendle controller, and token metadata. Only the designated Pendle Power Farm Controller can change the minting fee. Callers can deposit exact amounts of LP assets to receive shares, or withdraw exact shares or exact amounts of LP assets. A `manualSync` function is available for general updates. When depositing, a portion of the minted shares is taken as a fee and sent to the controller. The contract ensures that the share price grows monotonically and within a defined limit, and it manages the distribution of rewards from the underlying Pendle market over time. Various events are implicitly emitted by the underlying ERC-20 standard for transfers and approvals.
