This contract implements a standard fungible token, tracking balances for individual addresses and the total supply of tokens. It stores the token's name, symbol, and decimal precision. Users can transfer tokens to other addresses, and approve other addresses to spend tokens on their behalf. The contract also supports gasless approvals through a permit function, allowing users to sign off-chain messages to authorize spending without directly interacting with the blockchain. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events for external monitoring. The contract includes internal functions for minting new tokens and burning existing ones, which adjust the total supply and individual balances accordingly. There are no special access controls beyond standard token operations; any holder can initiate transfers or approvals for their own tokens.
