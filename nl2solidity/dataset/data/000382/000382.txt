This contract serves as a utility library for interacting with Uniswap V3 pools, providing functions to retrieve and calculate price-related data. It does not maintain any state itself but rather queries external Uniswap V3 pool contracts. Callers can consult a Uniswap V3 pool to obtain time-weighted arithmetic mean tick and harmonic mean liquidity over a specified historical period. It also allows for calculating the amount of a quote token received for a given base token amount at a specific tick. Additionally, callers can determine the oldest observation's age for a pool, retrieve the block-starting tick and liquidity, compute a weighted arithmetic mean tick from an array of tick-weight pairs, and calculate a "synthetic" tick representing the chained price across a sequence of tokens and their corresponding pairwise ticks. There are no specific access control mechanisms or events emitted, as it's designed as a pure utility library.
