This contract serves as a Position Manager for Uniswap V4, enabling users to manage their liquidity positions through a non-fungible token (NFT) interface. It maintains state about each unique liquidity position, including its associated pool key, lower and upper ticks, and the current liquidity. Users can mint new liquidity positions, receiving an ERC721 NFT representing their share, or increase/decrease existing liquidity. The contract also allows for burning positions, which removes liquidity and transfers the underlying assets. All operations are subject to access control, requiring the caller to be the NFT owner or an approved address. It integrates with Uniswap V4's PoolManager for core liquidity operations and Permit2 for gasless approvals. The contract includes re-entrancy protection and supports multicall functionality for batching operations. It also handles native token wrapping/unwrapping and provides mechanisms for settling and taking currency balances from the pool.
