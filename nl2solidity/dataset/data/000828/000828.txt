This contract implements a pull-payment system, allowing a payer to set aside funds for a recipient to withdraw later. It maintains a record of outstanding payments for each address within an internal escrow mechanism. Any caller can initiate a payment by specifying a recipient and an amount, which is then held in escrow. Recipients, or any other address on their behalf, can query their current outstanding balance and then withdraw these accumulated payments. The contract does not impose any access control on withdrawals, allowing anyone to trigger a payment to a designated recipient. All deposits and withdrawals are recorded as events by the underlying escrow, providing transparency into the payment lifecycle. This design prioritizes security by preventing direct pushes of funds, mitigating reentrancy risks for the payer.
