This contract implements a standard ERC-20 token named "VENDETTA" with the symbol "VEN" and 18 decimal places. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. Upon deployment, the contract mints an initial supply of 111,222,333 tokens and assigns them to the deployer's address. Callers can query the total supply, their own balance, or another account's balance. They can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend a specific amount of their tokens, and transfer tokens from an account that has granted them an allowance. The contract emits `Transfer` events when tokens move between accounts and `Approval` events when allowances are set or changed, allowing off-chain applications to track all token activity. There are no special access controls beyond standard ERC-20 mechanics; any token holder can initiate transfers or approve spending.
