This contract extends a standard multi-token contract to enable cross-chain transfers. It allows users to send tokens from one chain to another by burning tokens on the source chain and minting them on the destination chain. Callers can initiate these transfers for single or multiple token IDs and amounts, specifying the source address, the destination chain address, and optional data. The contract ensures that the caller is authorized to spend tokens on behalf of the `from` address, leveraging the underlying multi-token allowance mechanism. When tokens are sent, they are burned from the sender's balance. When tokens are received on the destination chain, they are minted to the recipient's address. This contract does not maintain any unique state beyond what is inherited from its base multi-token and bridge functionalities, and it does not emit any specific events beyond those from its parent contracts. There are no fees or special security guards implemented directly within this contract.
