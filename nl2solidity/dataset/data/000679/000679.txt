This contract provides a utility for deploying a specific type of Aave oracle. Its sole purpose is to facilitate the creation and deployment of an `AaveOracle` contract on the blockchain. The contract itself does not maintain any state after deployment; it's a stateless factory. Callers can invoke a single internal function, `_deployAaveOracle`, which takes two parameters: the desired number of decimals for the oracle's price feed and the address of the Aave `PoolAddressesProvider`. Upon execution, this function deploys a new `AaveOracle` instance, configured with the provided `PoolAddressesProvider` and a scaling factor derived from the `oracleDecimals`, and returns the address of the newly deployed oracle. There are no access controls or events emitted, as its function is purely to instantiate another contract. This contract does not handle any fees, integrate with other protocols directly beyond deploying the Aave Oracle, or have upgradeability mechanisms.
