This contract serves as a specialized liquid staking manager, designed to integrate with a broader StakeHouse ecosystem, primarily for testing or development environments. It maintains records of whether specific BLS public keys are considered part of its liquid staking network and if any are banned. Callers can initialize the contract by providing addresses for various ecosystem components like a DAO, syndicate factory, LP token factory, and others. During initialization, it retrieves critical StakeHouse dependency addresses (account manager, transaction router, universe, and slot registry) from a provided factory. Users can explicitly mark BLS public keys as part of the network or ban them. The contract also overrides the creation of its associated SavETH and StakingFunds vaults, instantiating mock versions for testing purposes. It provides read-only functions to check the network status or ban status of a BLS public key and to retrieve the addresses of the integrated StakeHouse components.
