This contract creates a new fungible token compliant with the ERC-20 standard, allowing for transfers, approvals, and balance checks. Upon deployment, it establishes a fixed total supply of tokens, minting the initial amount to the deployer's address. The contract also defines the token's name, symbol, and the number of decimal places for its representation. A unique feature is that a fee, specified during deployment, is paid to a designated receiver address for a service named "StandardERC20" at the time of the contract's creation. The contract maintains a record of each address's token balance and the allowances granted to other addresses for spending tokens on their behalf. Token holders can transfer tokens to others, approve third parties to spend their tokens, and increase or decrease these allowances. All token transfers and allowance changes emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring.
