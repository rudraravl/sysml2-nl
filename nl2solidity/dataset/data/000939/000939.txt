This contract serves as a central control and storage unit for a lending protocol, managing critical parameters and state for various financial operations. It maintains records of an administrator, a pending administrator, and the active and pending implementation addresses for the core logic. It tracks an oracle for asset pricing, global liquidation parameters like close factor and incentive, and a list of all supported markets, each with its collateral factor and membership status for individual accounts. The contract also stores per-account lists of assets they are currently using as collateral. It implements a pause mechanism, allowing a designated guardian to temporarily halt transfers, seizures, minting, and borrowing for specific markets or globally. Additionally, it manages guardians and caps for both borrowing and supplying assets, and integrates with a reward distribution mechanism. The contract also includes a reentrancy guard.
