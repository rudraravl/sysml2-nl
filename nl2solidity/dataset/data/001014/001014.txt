This system comprises three distinct contracts: a router, a factory, and a cauldron, each designed to integrate with the Blast network's yield mechanism. The router and factory contracts are initialized with a designated governor address, which is then configured to claim Blast gas yields. Similarly, the cauldron contract is also initialized with a governor address, which cannot be the contract itself, and it blacklists the Blast yield precompile address. During its initialization, the cauldron also configures the governor to claim Blast gas yields. The primary purpose of these contracts is to enable their respective functionalities while ensuring that Blast gas yields generated through their operations are directed to a specified governor address.
