This contract implements a custom ERC-20 token named PEPO. It maintains a total supply of tokens and tracks individual token balances for each address. The contract allows token holders to transfer tokens to other addresses and to approve other addresses to spend tokens on their behalf. A special "dev address" is designated at deployment. The contract owner has the ability to set a Uniswap router and pair address, which is used to identify transfers involving the liquidity pool. When tokens are transferred, if the sender is not the contract owner, a 5% burn fee is applied, meaning 5% of the transferred amount is sent to a burn address, and the remaining 95% is sent to the recipient. Additionally, transfers to the Uniswap pair from addresses other than a pre-approved address are limited by a maximum transaction amount. The contract owner can also burn tokens from their own balance and set the maximum transaction amount. Ownership can be transferred or renounced. Key events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and when the Uniswap router is updated.
