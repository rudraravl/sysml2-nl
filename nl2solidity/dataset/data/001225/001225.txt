This contract serves as a price oracle, providing the value of a specific asset in ETH. It maintains the asset's address, its decimal precision, and unique identifiers for its USD price feed and the ETH/USD price feed. The contract stores the latest USD prices for both the asset and ETH, along with a timestamp indicating when these prices were last updated. Any external caller can trigger an update to these prices, which fetches the latest values from an integrated Redstone data service. Once updated, the contract can calculate the ETH equivalent of a given amount of the tracked asset. This calculation will revert if the stored prices are older than a predefined stale price threshold, ensuring only recent data is used. The contract does not emit events.
