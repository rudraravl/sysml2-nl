This contract implements a standard ERC-20 fungible token with enhanced access control and lifecycle management. It maintains the token's name, symbol, total supply, and individual account balances. Callers can transfer tokens, approve spending by other accounts, and burn their own tokens, reducing the total supply. Crucially, the contract defines three roles: a `MINTER_ROLE` to create new tokens, a `PAUSER_ROLE` to halt and resume all token transfers, and a `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` with the power to grant or revoke these other roles. The deployer of the contract automatically receives all three roles. The contract emits standard ERC-20 transfer and approval events, as well as events for role assignments and revocations, and for pausing/unpausing transfers. This allows for a flexible and controlled token economy.
