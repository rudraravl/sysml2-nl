This contract is a yield-bearing token that wraps an underlying Aave aToken, converting its rebasing yield into a non-rebasing value accrual for its holders. It maintains its own name, symbol, and total supply, and tracks the underlying aToken it holds. Callers can deposit the underlying aToken to mint this token, and redeem this token to withdraw the underlying aToken. It also supports standard ERC-20 operations like transfers and approvals, including permit functionality. The contract can be paused by an authorized emergency administrator, preventing transfers and reward claims, and can be unpaused by the same role. A designated ACL admin has the ability to rescue excess underlying assets from the contract, beyond what is required to back the existing tokens. All state-changing operations are guarded against re-entrancy.
