This contract provides a governance system that allows participants to propose and vote on changes to the system, following a "Bravo" compatibility standard. It maintains a record of each proposal, including its proposer, the actions it entails (target addresses, values, function signatures, and calldata), and the current vote counts for, against, and abstaining. Callers can submit new proposals, which are then identified by a unique ID. Once a proposal is created, participants can cast their votes, which are recorded along with their voting weight. The contract tracks whether a voter has already participated in a specific proposal. Proposals can be queued and executed once they pass, or canceled by the original proposer or if the proposer's voting power falls below a threshold. The contract determines if a proposal has reached quorum and succeeded based on its internal voting rules, where only "for" votes contribute to quorum and "for" votes must strictly exceed "against" votes for success. It also provides functions to retrieve detailed information about any proposal, including its current state and voting results.
