This contract provides utility functions for interacting with a contract's storage and simulating calls. It allows any caller to retrieve a specified number of 32-byte words from the contract's storage, starting at a given offset. Additionally, it enables any caller to simulate a `delegatecall` to another target contract with a provided payload, ensuring that the simulation reverts and returns the outcome of the delegatecall, including any return data or error messages. The contract itself does not maintain any state beyond its code, acting purely as a functional library. There are no access controls, fees, or events; all operations are publicly accessible.
