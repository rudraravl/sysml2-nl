This contract serves as a dedicated storage layer for a lending pool, maintaining critical data about reserves, user configurations, and protocol parameters. It stores detailed information for each reserve, including its configuration and dynamic data, and tracks individual user settings, such as their borrowing and collateral status. The contract also maintains a list of all active reserves, categorized by an internal identifier, and manages eMode categories for users, allowing for differentiated risk parameters. Key protocol-wide settings are stored, such as the bridge protocol fee, flash loan premiums (both total and protocol-specific), and a limit on stable rate borrowing. The contract also keeps a count of the total number of reserves ever activated within the system.
