This contract provides a set of mathematical functions for performing multiplication and division with enhanced precision, specifically designed to handle intermediate values that might exceed the standard 256-bit integer limit without losing accuracy. It doesn't maintain any on-chain state; all its operations are pure computations. Callers can utilize two primary functions: `mulDiv`, which calculates the floor of `(a * b) / denominator`, and `mulDivRoundingUp`, which calculates the ceiling of the same expression. Both functions ensure that the final result fits within a 256-bit unsigned integer and will revert if the denominator is zero or if the final result overflows. There are no access control mechanisms, events, fees, or upgradeability features, as its sole purpose is to offer robust mathematical utilities for other contracts to integrate and use.
