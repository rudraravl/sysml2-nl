This contract manages deposits and withdrawals of various tokens, tracking user and global balances, with the ability to lock deposited funds. It maintains a state that can be idle, opened, or closed, and records the contract owner, a designated fee recipient, and a token registry. Users can deposit supported tokens, optionally locking them immediately, or later lock previously unlocked deposits. Users can also withdraw their unlocked tokens. The contract owner has extensive control, including setting the fee recipient, managing supported tokens and their deposit caps, changing the bootstrapper address, and pausing/unpausing operations. The owner can also open and close the contract for deposits, claim gas and token yields, and rescue unsupported tokens. All significant actions, such as deposits, withdrawals, state changes, and administrative updates, emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract integrates with Blast-specific functionalities for yield and points.
