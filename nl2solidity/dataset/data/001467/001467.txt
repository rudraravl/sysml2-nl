This contract implements a fungible token, named "NOPT" with the symbol "NOPT" and 18 decimal places, adhering to the BEP20 standard. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual token balances for all addresses, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of other addresses. Upon deployment, the contract mints an initial supply of 1000 tokens and assigns them to the deployer, who also becomes the contract's owner. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend their tokens, and transfer tokens on behalf of an approved sender. The contract owner has the exclusive ability to mint new tokens, increasing the total supply and assigning them to themselves. Ownership can be transferred to another address or renounced entirely by the current owner. All token transfers and allowance changes emit corresponding events for off-chain monitoring. The contract incorporates safety checks for arithmetic operations to prevent overflows and underflows.
