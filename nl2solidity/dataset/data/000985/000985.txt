This contract serves as a utility and information provider for managing interactions with Pendle markets, specifically focusing on compounding and reward distribution. It maintains internal state related to active Pendle markets and their associated reward tokens, including information about reserved amounts for compounding and last processed indices. Callers can query the contract for details about specific Pendle markets, such as the reward tokens associated with them, the length of the active markets list, and internal compounding data like reserved amounts and last indices. It also provides functions to calculate expiry times based on weeks, determine the amount of tokens to send based on a reward value and an exchange incentive, and retrieve position data from a Pendle lock contract. The contract integrates with an Oracle Hub to fetch token prices and conversions, ensuring accurate calculations for rewards. It also includes internal checks for valid price feeds and compares reward token lists using cryptographic hashes.
