This contract serves as a helper for interacting with a decentralized exchange, specifically for managing short positions. It maintains immutable references to the market token, long token, short token, and their respective Chainlink oracle addresses, along with an oracle contract. It tracks the decimals for the long and short tokens. The contract allows callers to retrieve details about its current position, including the collateral amount and the position's value with profit and loss calculated in terms of the collateral token. Callers can create market increase orders for short positions by providing an execution fee and the amount of the long token to add, which involves calculating the size delta and approving token transfers. Similarly, callers can create market decrease orders for short positions, specifying an execution fee, the amount of long token to remove, a receiver for the tokens, and a callback contract with a gas limit. It also enables the cancellation of existing orders and the claiming of funding fees for specified markets and tokens. All interactions with the exchange router and data store are facilitated through predefined interfaces.
