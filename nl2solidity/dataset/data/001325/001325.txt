This contract manages the minting and ownership of a fixed supply of ERC-1155 tokens, representing digital collectibles. It maintains a total supply limit, tracks the number of tokens minted, and distinguishes between public and private minting allocations. The contract records how many tokens each wallet has minted publicly and whether a wallet has participated in the private mint. It also stores a designated address for receiving payments, a specific ERC-20 token address for payment, and a signing address for private mint verification. Callers can mint tokens publicly, paying a set cost in the specified ERC-20 token, or mint a single token privately if they provide a valid signature from the designated signer and haven't minted privately before. Only the contract owner can enable or disable minting, adjust the minting cost, change the payment token or recipient addresses, reset a wallet's public mint count, transfer remaining private mints to the public pool, and whitelist specific addresses to bypass trading restrictions. Token transfers are generally restricted until trading is enabled by the owner, though whitelisted addresses can bypass this. Both public and private mints emit distinct events to signal successful token creation.
