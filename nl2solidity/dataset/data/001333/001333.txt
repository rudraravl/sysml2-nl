This contract is a multi-reward staking platform where users can stake a specific ERC20 token to earn multiple different ERC20 reward tokens. The contract maintains the total supply of staked tokens, individual user balances, and detailed reward data for each configured reward token, including its distributor, duration, rate, and accumulated rewards per user. The contract owner can add new reward tokens, set their distributors, and update reward durations, while a designated controller can also add new rewards. Users can stake their tokens, withdraw them, and claim all accumulated rewards across all configured reward tokens. The contract incorporates re-entrancy protection for critical functions and can be paused by the owner, preventing most operations. Important events are emitted for staking, withdrawals, reward payments, and changes to reward configurations, allowing off-chain monitoring.
