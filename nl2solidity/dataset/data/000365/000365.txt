This contract facilitates adding liquidity to Uniswap V3 pools. It maintains a reference to a Uniswap V3 factory and handles payments for liquidity provision. The primary operation is `addLiquidity`, which allows a caller to specify two tokens, a fee tier, a recipient for the liquidity, a tick range, and desired amounts of each token to provide. The contract calculates the corresponding liquidity amount and then calls the target Uniswap V3 pool's `mint` function. During this process, the contract acts as a callback receiver for the Uniswap V3 pool, handling the payment of the owed tokens from the original caller to the pool. It ensures that the actual amounts provided meet minimum thresholds to guard against price slippage. Access to these functions is internal, meaning they are intended to be called by other contracts within the same system, not directly by external users.
