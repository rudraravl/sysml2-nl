This contract extends a standard ERC20 token to allow for gasless approvals of token transfers. It maintains a record of nonces for each token holder, which are unique, incrementing numbers used to prevent replay attacks on signed messages. The primary operation is `permit`, which allows a token holder to authorize a spender to transfer a specific amount of their tokens by providing a cryptographic signature, rather than sending a transaction themselves. This signature must be valid, not expired by a deadline, and correspond to the token holder. Upon successful verification, the contract updates the allowance for the spender. The contract also exposes a `nonces` function to retrieve the current nonce for any address and a `DOMAIN_SEPARATOR` function, both crucial for constructing and verifying off-chain signatures. There are no fees, and access control is limited to signature validation.
