This contract facilitates the wrapping and unwrapping of Silo share tokens, allowing users to convert between an underlying asset and its interest-bearing share token representation within a Silo liquidity pool. It maintains no internal state, acting purely as a stateless utility for these operations. Callers can invoke `wrapShareToken` to deposit an underlying asset into a Silo and receive share tokens, or `unwrapShareToken` to redeem share tokens from a Silo and receive the underlying asset. Both functions are payable to accommodate potential ETH transfers in a multi-call context. There are no specific access controls beyond the standard Ethereum transaction permissions. The contract interacts with external `IShareToken`, `ISilo`, and `IERC20` contracts to perform its operations. It also utilizes a chaining mechanism to pass results between calls, identified by `outputReference`. No fees are charged by this contract, and it does not implement upgradeability or security guards beyond standard Solidity practices.
