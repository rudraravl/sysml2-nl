This contract manages multiple types of fungible tokens, identified by a unique ID, allowing users to hold and transfer these tokens. It maintains a record of each user's balance for every token ID, and tracks allowances granted by token owners to other addresses (spenders) for specific token IDs. Additionally, it records whether an address has been designated as an operator by another address, granting broad transfer permissions. Users can transfer their own tokens, approve a spender to transfer a specific amount of a token ID on their behalf, or designate an address as a full operator to manage all their tokens. Spenders can transfer tokens from an owner's balance if they have sufficient allowance, and operators can transfer any token from the owner's balance. All token transfers, approvals, and operator assignments emit events to signal these actions on-chain. The contract also provides internal functions for minting new tokens and burning existing ones, which also emit transfer events.
