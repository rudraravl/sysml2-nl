This contract manages a system for tracking and delegating voting power, essential for decentralized governance. It maintains a record of which address has delegated its voting units to another, and the historical voting power of each delegate and the total supply of votes at any given block number. Callers can delegate their voting units to a chosen representative or to themselves, either directly or by signing a message off-chain that another party can submit on their behalf. The contract allows querying the current voting power of any delegate and their historical voting power at a specific past block, as well as the total historical voting supply. Changes in delegation or voting unit distribution trigger events, `DelegateChanged` and `DelegateVotesChanged`, enabling off-chain systems to monitor the state. The contract is designed to be integrated with other contracts that define the actual voting units, such as token contracts, and it guards against querying future voting states.
