This contract implements a custom ERC-20 token named "AKASH.NETWORK" with the symbol "AKT" and 18 decimal places. It maintains balances for all token holders and tracks allowances for spending tokens on behalf of others. The contract is initialized with a total supply of 80,000 tokens, all minted to the deployer, who is automatically designated as both an owner and an administrator. Owners have exclusive control over adding or removing other owners, administrators, and general users, as well as the ability to deposit (effectively burn) tokens from any account or add work (mint tokens) to any account. Administrators can also be added or removed by owners. A notable mechanic is that users, once designated, are prevented from transferring any tokens. All token transfers and approvals emit standard ERC-20 events, allowing off-chain applications to monitor token movements and authorizations.
