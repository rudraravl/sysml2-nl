This contract creates a fungible ERC20 token designed to represent a collection of ERC721 NFTs. It maintains the token's name, symbol, and a custom denomination value. The contract owner, set during initialization, has exclusive permission to mint new tokens to any address, update the token's name and symbol (emitting a `MetadataUpdated` event), and manage other administrative functions. Any token holder can burn their own tokens or burn tokens from another account if they have been granted an allowance. The contract also supports ERC20 Permit and ERC20 Votes functionalities, allowing for gasless approvals and enabling voting power based on token holdings. This contract is designed to be upgradeable, with its constructor preventing direct initialization to ensure it's used via a proxy.
