This contract acts as a gateway for interacting with a lending pool using Wrapped Ether (WETH) and native ETH. It maintains immutable references to the WETH contract and the core lending pool. Users can deposit native ETH, which the contract wraps into WETH and then deposits into the pool, receiving aTokens in return. They can also withdraw their WETH reserves from the pool, which the contract unwraps back to native ETH and sends to the user. Repaying WETH loans is supported by sending native ETH, which the contract converts to WETH before repaying the debt in the pool. Borrowing WETH is also possible, with the borrowed WETH being unwrapped and sent as native ETH to the borrower. A special withdrawal function allows users to approve the contract to withdraw on their behalf using an ERC712 permit signature. The contract owner has special permissions to recover accidentally sent ERC20 tokens or native ETH. All interactions with the lending pool are facilitated through the contract, which handles the WETH wrapping and unwrapping. The contract restricts direct ETH transfers to only come from the WETH contract itself, and all other fallback calls are rejected.
