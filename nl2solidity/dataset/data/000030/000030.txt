This contract facilitates the cross-chain transfer of non-fungible tokens (NFTs) by acting as a bridge between different blockchain networks. It maintains a record of sent and received cross-chain transfers through unique identifiers. Callers can initiate a cross-chain transfer of an NFT by specifying the sender, the destination interoperable address (including the target chain and recipient address), and the NFT's unique identifier. Upon sending, the contract ensures the NFT is handled appropriately on the source chain (e.g., locked or burned) and emits an event detailing the transfer. When a cross-chain message is received, the contract processes the payload to identify the original sender, the recipient on the current chain, and the NFT's identifier. It then ensures the NFT is handled appropriately on the destination chain (e.g., minted or unlocked) and emits an event confirming the reception. The contract integrates with a cross-chain messaging system to send and receive data, and it requires specific implementations for handling the NFT on both the sending and receiving ends of a transfer.
