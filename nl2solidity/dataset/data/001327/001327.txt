This contract implements an ERC-1155 compliant token standard, allowing for the creation, management, and transfer of multiple token types. It maintains a mapping of token IDs to individual token holders and their respective balances, along with a record of operator approvals for managing tokens on behalf of other users. The contract supports single token and batch operations for minting new tokens, burning existing tokens, and transferring tokens between addresses. It includes mechanisms for safe transfers, which verify that recipient contracts are capable of handling ERC-1155 tokens, and emits `TransferSingle`, `TransferBatch`, and `ApprovalForAll` events to track all significant token movements and approval changes. The contract also incorporates an ownership mechanism, allowing a designated owner to control certain privileged functions, and emits an `OwnershipTransferred` event when the owner changes.
