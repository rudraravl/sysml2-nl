This contract manages the core supply and withdrawal functionalities within a lending protocol, tracking user deposits and their status as collateral. It maintains a comprehensive record of all reserves, including their configurations and current interest rates, alongside individual user configurations detailing their supplied and borrowed assets. Callers can supply assets to a reserve, which mints corresponding aTokens and potentially enables the asset as collateral for the first time. They can also withdraw assets by burning aTokens, with the option to withdraw their entire balance. Additionally, the contract allows users to explicitly enable or disable an asset as collateral, subject to health factor checks to prevent undercollateralization. All supply, withdrawal, and collateral status changes trigger events, providing transparency for off-chain monitoring. The contract enforces strict validation rules to ensure the integrity of the lending system, particularly regarding health factors and loan-to-value ratios, to safeguard against insolvency.
