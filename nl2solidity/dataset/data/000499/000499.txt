This contract provides utility functions for encoding and decoding method handler information into a single `bytes32` value. It doesn't maintain any on-chain state or offer any callable operations directly. Its purpose is to pack an address, a boolean indicating if a method is static, and optionally a 4-byte selector into a compact format, and then to unpack this information. The encoding differentiates between static and non-static methods by setting the first byte of the `bytes32` value. This allows for efficient storage or transmission of handler details, enabling other contracts to interpret and route calls based on the packed data. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms, as it's a stateless utility.
