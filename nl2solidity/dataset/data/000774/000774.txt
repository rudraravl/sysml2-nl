This contract enables users to execute a complex trading strategy involving a long position on ETH, coupled with automatic stop-loss and take-profit orders. It integrates with an external exchange router and an oracle for price data. The contract maintains references to WETH, USDC, the exchange router, and an oracle instance, which are set during deployment. The primary operation is `createTakeProfitAndStopLossOrders`, where a caller provides desired leverage and a USDC amount. The contract first transfers the specified USDC from the caller to itself, then sends an execution fee in WETH and the USDC collateral to an order vault via the exchange router. It then creates three distinct orders: a market increase order to long ETH, a stop-loss decrease order to close the position if ETH drops to 90% of its current price, and a limit decrease order to take profit if ETH rises to 110% of its current price. Each order creation involves sending an additional execution fee. The contract also has a payable `receive` function, allowing it to accept ETH, presumably for refunding execution fees. There are no explicit access controls beyond the caller initiating the `createTakeProfitAndStopLossOrders` function. No events are emitted.
