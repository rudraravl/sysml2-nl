This contract enhances a governance system by allowing voters to sign their votes off-chain, using a unique nonce for each proposal. It maintains a record of nonces for each voter, keyed by the lower 192 bits of the proposal ID, ensuring that a signed vote for a specific proposal can only be used once. When a vote is submitted with a signature, the contract first attempts to validate it against the proposal-specific nonce. If successful, it consumes that nonce. If not, it falls back to validating the signature against a traditional, globally incrementing nonce. This mechanism supports both standard and extended voting with reasons and additional parameters, providing flexibility for off-chain voting while preventing replay attacks for individual proposals.
