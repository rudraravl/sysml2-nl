This contract serves as an abstract helper for interacting with the Aave lending protocol, specifically designed to manage positions through an associated NFT system. It maintains a mapping of underlying asset addresses to their corresponding Aave token addresses and tracks a re-entrancy guard state. Callers can reserve a new position NFT for themselves, deposit exact amounts of underlying assets into Aave via a specific NFT, withdraw exact amounts of underlying assets or shares from Aave via an NFT, and borrow exact amounts of underlying assets from Aave via an NFT. The contract also provides a utility function to retrieve the current Aave pool APY for a given asset. All state-changing operations are protected by a re-entrancy guard, preventing concurrent calls that could lead to unexpected behavior. It also validates that tokens used for operations are recognized by the WISE_LENDING system.
