This contract provides a set of mathematical functions for performing precise calculations with fixed-point decimal numbers, specifically "wad" (18 decimal places) and "ray" (27 decimal places). It does not maintain any on-chain state; all operations are pure functions. Callers can multiply and divide wad values, multiply and divide ray values, and convert between wad and ray representations. Most operations round results half-up by default, but specific functions are available for floor and ceiling rounding for ray multiplication and division. The contract ensures that calculations do not overflow standard `uint256` limits and reverts if an overflow would occur or if division by zero is attempted. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms, as its sole purpose is to offer robust mathematical utilities for other contracts.
