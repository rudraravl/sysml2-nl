This contract extends a standard fungible token, enabling token holders to permanently remove tokens from circulation. It maintains the total supply of the token and individual account balances, as defined by the underlying token standard. Callers can initiate two primary operations: `burn`, which destroys a specified amount of tokens from their own balance, and `burnFrom`, which destroys a specified amount of tokens from another account's balance, provided the caller has been granted sufficient allowance by that account. Both operations reduce the total supply and the respective account's balance. There are no specific access control mechanisms beyond the allowance system for `burnFrom`, and no events are explicitly defined within this contract, though the underlying token standard would emit transfer events for these operations. There are no fees, upgradeability features, or integrations with other protocols.
