This contract implements a BEP-20 compatible token with several anti-whale and anti-bot mechanisms, along with a tax system on transfers. The contract maintains a total supply of tokens, individual account balances, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of other users. It also tracks a designated liquidity pair address, a router for decentralized exchange interactions, and several addresses for receiving collected fees. Key functions include standard token transfers, approvals for spending, and querying balances. Transfers are subject to various checks: a maximum transaction amount, a maximum wallet holding limit, and a trading-allowed flag. Certain addresses, including the contract itself, fee receivers, and the deployer, can be exempted from fees and these limits. The contract applies a variable tax on transfers, which differs for buys (from the liquidity pair), sells (to the liquidity pair), and regular transfers. A portion of these fees is used to automatically swap tokens for ETH and add liquidity to the trading pair, while other portions are sent to marketing and development wallets. The contract also includes a mechanism to burn a small amount of tokens during transfers. The contract owner has special permissions to start trading, set fee exemptions, and manage bot addresses.
