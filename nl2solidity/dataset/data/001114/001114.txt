This contract acts as an adaptor for a "stakeless gauge" system, facilitating the checkpointing process by an authorized entity. It maintains the address of a specific LINK token contract and a designated `checkpointer` address. The primary operation allows the `checkpointer` to trigger a `checkpoint` on a specified gauge contract, potentially sending Ether along with the call. After the checkpoint operation, any remaining Ether or LINK tokens held by this adaptor contract are automatically returned to the caller. The contract owner has the exclusive right to update the `checkpointer` address, and this change is broadcast via an event. The contract also allows receiving Ether directly.
