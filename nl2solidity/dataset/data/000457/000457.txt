This contract provides a set of pure mathematical functions for calculating liquidity amounts within a concentrated liquidity automated market maker system, similar to Uniswap V3. It does not maintain any on-chain state or store any funds. Its purpose is to compute the amount of liquidity that can be provided given token amounts and price ranges. Callers can use three main operations: `getLiquidityForAmount0` to determine liquidity from a specified amount of token0 within a price range, `getLiquidityForAmount1` for the same calculation using token1, and `getLiquidityForAmounts` which calculates the maximum liquidity obtainable given amounts of both token0 and token1, a current pool price, and a price range. All functions are internal and pure, meaning they do not modify state and only perform calculations based on their inputs. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms as it's a stateless utility library.
