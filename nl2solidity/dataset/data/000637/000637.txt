This contract provides a set of mathematical utility functions for calculating scaled amounts and balances related to aTokens and vTokens, which are integral to a lending protocol. It does not maintain any state on-chain itself, but rather offers pure computations. Callers can use these functions to determine the correct scaled amounts for minting, burning, or transferring aTokens, and for minting or burning vTokens, as well as calculating actual balances from scaled ones. Each function takes an amount and an index (either `liquidityIndex` for aTokens or `variableBorrowIndex` for vTokens) and returns a calculated scaled amount or balance. The contract implements specific rounding rules (floor or ceil) for each operation, consistently rounding in favor of the protocol to prevent under-accounting of debt or over-accounting of assets, aligning with ERC-4626 token standards. There are no access controls, permissions, or events, as it's a stateless library of mathematical operations.
