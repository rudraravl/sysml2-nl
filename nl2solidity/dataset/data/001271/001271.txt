This contract manages a lending protocol, enabling users to queue requests for borrowing ETH against staked ETH (stETH) and subsequently claim those loans. It maintains state regarding the maximum Loan-To-Value (LTV) ratio, a list of authorized addresses, guardians, and an allowlist of users who can interact with the system, along with a flag indicating if the allowlist is active. The owner can set the maximum LTV, manage authorized addresses and guardians, and unpause the protocol. Authorized addresses can add or remove users from the allowlist. Guardians can pause the protocol. Users can queue a borrow request, specifying the recipient for the borrowed ETH and the amount of stETH to queue, and later claim their borrowed ETH by providing the request ID. Events are emitted for all significant state changes, including LTV updates, queueing and claiming actions, and modifications to authorization, guardianship, and allowlist status, as well as pausing and unpausing the protocol. The contract can be paused by a guardian, preventing further operations, and unpaused by the owner.
