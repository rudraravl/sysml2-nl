This contract provides a set of utility functions for performing cryptographic operations on the BLS12-381 curve, leveraging precompiled contracts for efficiency. It doesn't maintain any persistent state on its own, but rather acts as a stateless library for complex mathematical computations. Callers can perform fundamental BLS operations such as adding two points on either the G1 or G2 curves, executing multi-scalar multiplication on G1 or G2 points, and checking the validity of a pairing between G1 and G2 points. Additionally, it offers functions to map field elements (Fp and Fp2) to points on the G1 and G2 curves, respectively, and a specialized function to compute a G2 point from a given message using a hash-to-curve algorithm. All operations are permissionless, meaning any caller can invoke these functions. The contract signals failures in any of these cryptographic operations by reverting with specific custom errors, indicating which precompile call failed. There are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or direct integrations with other protocols beyond the reliance on the underlying BLS precompiled contracts.
