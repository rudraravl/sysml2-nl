This contract is an NFT that supports cross-chain transfers. It maintains the standard ERC721 state of token ownership, allowing tokens to be owned by addresses on this chain or transferred to other chains. Callers can initiate a cross-chain transfer of an NFT they own or are approved to transfer by calling `crosschainTransferFrom`, specifying the current owner, the destination chain and address encoded as bytes, and the token ID. When a token is sent cross-chain, it is effectively burned on this chain. Conversely, when a token is received from another chain, it is minted on this chain to the specified recipient. The contract manages the underlying bridging logic for these cross-chain operations, ensuring that tokens are correctly locked and unlocked across different chains.
