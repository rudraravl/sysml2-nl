This contract provides a library for validating token amounts against predefined slippage limits, ensuring that transactions meet expected minimum outputs or do not exceed maximum inputs. It maintains no persistent state on-chain. Callers can invoke two primary functions: `validateMinOut` to confirm that received token amounts (for token0 and token1) are at least specified minimums, and `validateMaxIn` to verify that spent token amounts do not surpass specified maximums. Access to these functions is unrestricted, as they are pure computations. The contract emits no events. Its core mechanic involves comparing a provided `BalanceDelta` (representing token changes) against user-defined `amount0Min`/`amount1Min` or `amount0Max`/`amount1Max`, reverting with `MinimumAmountInsufficient` or `MaximumAmountExceeded` errors if the conditions are not met. It handles potential negative deltas from hooks by reverting in `validateMinOut` if a negative delta is encountered, and by only checking negative deltas in `validateMaxIn`, effectively ignoring positive deltas for maximum input checks.
