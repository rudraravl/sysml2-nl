This contract implements an ERC-4626 compliant tokenized vault designed to hold a specific underlying ERC-20 asset. Its primary purpose is to manage deposits and mint shares for a single designated address, the `ONLY_DEPOSITOR`, which is set at deployment. The contract maintains the `ONLY_DEPOSITOR` address and the balances of the underlying asset and vault shares. Callers can deposit the underlying asset to receive vault shares or mint shares directly, but only the `ONLY_DEPOSITOR` is permitted to perform these actions. Any attempt by another address to deposit or mint will be rejected. The contract inherits standard ERC-20 and ERC-4626 functionalities, including share tracking and asset conversion, and uses a fixed 18-decimal offset for share price calculations to prevent manipulation.
