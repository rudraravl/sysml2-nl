This contract defines a Jump Rate Model for calculating interest rates in a lending market. It maintains several key parameters: a `baseRatePerTimestamp`, a `multiplierPerTimestamp`, a `jumpMultiplierPerTimestamp`, and a `kink` utilization point. These parameters are set once upon deployment, converting annual rates into per-timestamp rates, and are immutable thereafter. The contract provides functions to calculate the `utilizationRate` of a market based on its cash, borrows, and reserves. It then uses this utilization rate to determine the `getBorrowRate` and `getSupplyRate` per timestamp. The borrow rate increases linearly with utilization until the `kink` point, after which it increases at a steeper rate defined by the `jumpMultiplierPerTimestamp`. The supply rate is derived from the borrow rate, utilization, and a `reserveFactorMantissa`. A `NewInterestParams` event is emitted upon deployment, signaling the initial configuration of the interest rate model. The contract is purely a mathematical model and does not hold any funds or have any access control beyond its initial deployment.
