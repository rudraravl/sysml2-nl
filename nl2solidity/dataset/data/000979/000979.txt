This contract facilitates leveraged yield farming positions, allowing users to open, manage, and close positions. It tracks the debt ratio of each position, identified by a unique NFT ID, to assess its health. Users can query approximate net APY for a position setup and the new borrow rate for a given borrow amount. The contract enables partial liquidation of unhealthy positions by external liquidators and provides internal functions for users to manually repay borrowed shares or withdraw their lent shares. It integrates with a lending protocol to manage borrowing and lending, and uses flash loans for opening and closing leveraged positions. Positions can be opened with or without Aave integration, and the contract ensures a minimum deposit amount and limits maximum leverage. It registers NFT IDs with the lending protocol for farm use and emits an event upon registration.
