This contract defines the interface for a governance system, allowing participants to propose, vote on, and execute changes. It tracks the state of proposals, which can be Pending, Active, Canceled, Defeated, Succeeded, Queued, Expired, or Executed. Callers can create new proposals by specifying target addresses, values, calldata, and a description, which triggers a ProposalCreated event. Votes can be cast on active proposals, optionally with a reason or additional parameters, and can even be submitted via a cryptographic signature, all emitting VoteCast or VoteCastWithParams events. A proposal's creator can cancel it while it's in the Pending state. Successful proposals can be executed, leading to a ProposalExecuted event. The contract maintains information about the voting delay, voting period, and the minimum quorum required for proposals to pass, and can determine an account's voting power at a specific timepoint.
