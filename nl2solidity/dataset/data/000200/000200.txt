This contract implements a standard fungible token, named "Token" with symbol "TKN" and 18 decimal places. It maintains a total supply and tracks the balance of tokens held by each address, as well as allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. Upon deployment, the entire initial token supply is assigned to the deployer. Callers can transfer tokens to other addresses, approve other addresses to spend a specific amount of their tokens, and transfer tokens on behalf of another address if an allowance has been granted. The contract also allows for the creation of new tokens, increasing the total supply and assigning them to a specified address. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, enabling off-chain tracking of token movements.
