This contract acts as a reward distribution mechanism for syndicate earnings, specifically designed to distribute ETH to users holding LP shares. It maintains a record of the total accumulated ETH per LP share, scaled by a large precision factor, the total ETH claimed by all users, and the last total rewards observed. For each user and token, it tracks the amount of ETH they have already claimed. Callers can preview the amount of ETH they are eligible to claim based on their LP balance and the current accumulated rewards. The contract allows users to distribute their due ETH rewards to a specified recipient address, provided they have an LP balance. This process updates the claimed amount for the user and the global total claimed, then transfers the ETH, emitting an event upon successful distribution. The contract also internally updates the accumulated ETH per LP share whenever new rewards are received, emitting an event for each new batch of ETH processed. It can receive ETH directly, which contributes to the total rewards available for distribution.
