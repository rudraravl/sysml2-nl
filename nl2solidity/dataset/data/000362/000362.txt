This contract acts as a router for executing token swaps on Uniswap V3. It maintains the addresses of the Uniswap V3 factory and the wrapped Ether (WETH9) contract, established at deployment. Callers can perform two main types of swaps: "exact input" where they specify the amount of tokens to send and receive at least a minimum amount of output tokens, and "exact output" where they specify the desired output amount and pay no more than a maximum input amount. Swaps can be single-hop or multi-hop, traversing multiple pools. The contract handles token transfers for these swaps, including paying for intermediate hops in multi-hop swaps. It also supports "self-permit" functionality, allowing callers to approve token spending in a single transaction. All operations are subject to a deadline to prevent stale transactions. There are no explicit fees charged by this contract, nor are there any upgradeability mechanisms.
