This contract serves as a comprehensive access control system, managing permissions for various roles and addresses. It maintains state regarding which addresses hold specific roles, the associated delays for granting or revoking access, and the delays for administrative actions on target contracts. Callers can query whether a specific address has permission to execute a given function on a target contract, including the immediate outcome and any associated time delay. They can also retrieve detailed information about an address's access to a role, such as when access was granted or pending delay changes. The contract allows for querying administrative restrictions, including whether an action is restricted, the role ID of the administrator, and the execution delay. It also provides utilities to hash execution identifiers and extract specific data from call payloads. The contract is initialized with an initial administrator and includes a mechanism to ensure authorized calls.
