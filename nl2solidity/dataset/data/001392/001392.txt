This contract is an ERC-20 token that also incorporates ownership functionality. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual token balances for each address, and allowances for spending tokens on behalf of other addresses. The contract owner, initially the deployer, has exclusive control over transferring ownership to another address or renouncing it entirely. Any token holder can transfer their tokens to another address, approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens, or transfer tokens on behalf of an approved sender. The contract supports standard ERC-20 operations like checking balances, total supply, and allowances. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing off-chain applications to track token movements and changes in spending permissions.
