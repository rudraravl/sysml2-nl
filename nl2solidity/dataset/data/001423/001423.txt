This contract implements a custom ERC-20 token with integrated liquidity management and an owner-controlled tax mechanism. The token maintains a name, symbol, and a fixed total supply, with 9 decimal places. Upon deployment, the entire token supply is minted to the contract creator, and a Uniswap V2 liquidity pair is automatically created between this new token and WETH using a provided router address. The contract tracks token balances for all addresses and manages allowances for token transfers. The contract owner can set a transfer tax, which is applied to most token transfers, with the collected tax tokens being sent to the contract itself. A portion of these collected tokens can be automatically swapped for ETH and added as liquidity to the Uniswap pair, a process that is triggered under specific conditions related to the contract's token balance and internal flags. The contract also includes internal mechanisms to handle specific transfer scenarios, such as large transfers or transfers involving the Uniswap pair, which can bypass or modify the standard tax and liquidity operations. The owner has the ability to transfer ownership or renounce it.
