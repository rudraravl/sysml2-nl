This contract implements a basic fungible token, tracking balances for individual addresses and a total supply. Upon deployment, the creator receives the initial total supply of tokens. Callers can transfer tokens they own to other addresses, or approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens on their behalf. Approved spenders can then transfer tokens from the approving address to any destination. The contract also allows for the creation of new tokens, increasing the total supply and assigning them to a specified address. All token transfers and approvals emit events, allowing off-chain applications to track token movements and authorizations. The contract ensures that transfers and allowances do not exceed available balances or approved amounts, preventing overdrafts.
