This contract implements a BEP-20 token named "BICASSO AI" with the symbol "BICASSO", featuring custom tax mechanics and anti-bot measures. It tracks token balances for each address, allowances for spending, and the total supply. The contract maintains state variables for a Uniswap-V2 compatible router and liquidity pair, a flag to prevent re-entrant swaps, and booleans to control trading and fee-swapping. It also stores a genesis block number, a deadblock period, thresholds for transaction and wallet amounts, and designated marketing and development wallet addresses. Separate tax rates are stored for buy and sell transactions, along with a total tax percentage for each. Certain addresses can be excluded from fees or marked as bots. Callers can transfer tokens, approve spending, and query balances and allowances. The contract owner has extensive control, including enabling/disabling trading and swapping, setting tax rates, updating wallet addresses, modifying router and pair addresses, marking addresses as bots or fee-exempt, and adjusting transaction and wallet limits. The owner can also rescue any ERC20 tokens or ETH accidentally sent to the contract. During token transfers, a percentage of the amount is collected as a fee, which is then used to perform automated swaps for ETH and distribute funds to marketing and development wallets, and potentially add liquidity to the pair. Important events include `Transfer` and `Approval` for token operations, and `OwnershipTransferred` for owner changes. The contract includes a re-entrancy guard for its internal swap function and a mechanism to identify and block addresses that interact with the contract during a "deadblock" period as bots.
