This contract acts as a factory for deploying new ERC20 token contracts. It maintains a record of deployed token symbols and their corresponding contract addresses. The contract owner is the only entity permitted to deploy new tokens. To deploy a token, the owner provides its symbol and the bytecode of the ERC20 contract to be created. Upon successful deployment, the contract stores the new token's symbol-to-address mapping and emits an event, `TokenDeployed`, indicating the symbol and address of the newly created token. Any caller can query the contract to retrieve the address of a deployed token by providing its symbol.
