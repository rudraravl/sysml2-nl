This contract serves as a foundational component for other contracts that wish to interact with the Blast L2 network's native yield and points system. Upon deployment, it establishes a connection to the Blast network by configuring its yield and gas modes to be claimable, using addresses provided by an `AddressFinder` contract and a `Governor` contract. It inherits functionality related to Blast points, allowing it to participate in the Blast points program. The contract itself does not maintain any unique state beyond its initial configuration and inherited points-related data. Its primary purpose is to set up the necessary Blast configurations for any inheriting contracts, enabling them to claim native yield and accrue Blast points. There are no direct callable functions for external users on this specific contract; its operations are primarily initialization and providing a base for other contracts. No events are emitted directly by this contract, and there are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or security guards implemented here.
