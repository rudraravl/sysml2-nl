This contract implements a weighted multi-signature scheme, allowing a set of authorized signers to collectively approve actions based on a predefined threshold. It maintains a list of authorized signers, each with an assigned weight, and a global threshold. The contract is initialized with an initial set of signers, their corresponding weights, and the required threshold. Callers can add new signers, remove existing signers, modify individual signer weights, and update the overall threshold. Any change to signer weights or the threshold must ensure that the total weight of all signers remains greater than or equal to the threshold. When a signer's weight is changed, an event is emitted. The contract ensures that all provided weights are positive and that arrays for signers and weights are of matching lengths during updates. It also validates that the threshold is always reachable by the combined weight of all authorized signers.
