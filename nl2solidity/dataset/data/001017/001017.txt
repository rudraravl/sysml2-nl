This contract provides a suite of utility functions for interacting with Cauldron V2 contracts, which are lending platforms. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather reads and computes information based on the state of an associated Cauldron contract. Its primary purpose is to calculate various financial metrics related to user positions within a Cauldron, such as their borrow amount, collateral value, loan-to-value ratio, health factor, and potential liquidation price. It also offers functions to determine the interest accrued on borrowed funds, the exchange rate of collateral via an oracle, and the specifics of a liquidation, including the collateral a liquidator would receive and the MIM (Magic Internet Money) required. There are no direct operations callers can perform on this contract; instead, its functions are designed to be called by other contracts or off-chain services to query Cauldron V2 data. Access to these functions is public, as they are read-only and do not alter any state. No events are emitted by this contract, and it does not handle any fees or direct integrations, serving purely as a data computation layer for Cauldron V2 interactions.
