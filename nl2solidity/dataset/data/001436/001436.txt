This contract manages a time-locked distribution of a specific ERC-20 token to a designated recipient. It stores the address of the ERC-20 token, the recipient's address, the initial lock duration, and subsequent time bumps. It also tracks the total amount of tokens deposited and four distinct boolean flags for each of four potential withdrawal periods, indicating whether that period's tokens have been unlocked and whether they have been sent. A depositor can transfer tokens into the contract once. The designated recipient can trigger a withdrawal by sending a zero-value transaction to the contract or by directly calling the `sendOut` function. Upon withdrawal, the contract checks if any of the four time-based locks have expired. If so, it transfers a predefined amount of tokens for each expired and unsent period to the recipient, updating the corresponding sent flags. If all deposited tokens are sent, the contract self-destructs. Callers can query the contract to see the total deposited balance, the contract's creation time, the current time, and the status of each payment and unlock period.
