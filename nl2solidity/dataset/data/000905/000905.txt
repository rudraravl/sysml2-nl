This contract extends a Governor, providing functionality to determine the voting quorum as a fraction of the total token supply. It maintains a historical record of the quorum numerator, which is used in conjunction with a fixed denominator (defaulting to 100) to calculate the quorum. Callers can query the current quorum numerator or its value at a specific historical timepoint. The contract also allows for the quorum numerator to be updated, but this operation is restricted to calls made through a governance proposal. A `QuorumNumeratorUpdated` event is emitted whenever the quorum numerator changes, providing a transparent log of these adjustments. The quorum itself is calculated by multiplying the token's past total supply by the current or historical quorum numerator and dividing by the denominator.
