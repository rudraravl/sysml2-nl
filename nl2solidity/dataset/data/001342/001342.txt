This contract implements a standard fungible token, named "SHIBAMARS" with the symbol "SHIBAMARS" and 18 decimal places, representing a fixed total supply of 100 quadrillion tokens. It tracks the balance of tokens held by each address and maintains a record of allowances, permitting one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. Upon deployment, all initial tokens are assigned to the contract creator. Callers can query the total supply, check any address's token balance, and see the amount an address has approved another to spend. They can transfer tokens directly from their own balance, approve another address to spend a specific amount of their tokens, and transfer tokens from an approved address's balance. All token transfers and approvals emit corresponding events, allowing external applications to monitor these activities. The contract incorporates safeguards against common arithmetic overflows and underflows to ensure secure calculations.
