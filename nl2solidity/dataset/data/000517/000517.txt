This contract implements a fungible token that adheres to the ERC-20 standard. It maintains a total supply of tokens, individual token balances for each address, and allowances that permit one address to spend tokens on behalf of another. Upon deployment, the token is initialized with a name and symbol, and a fixed decimal precision of 18. Callers can transfer tokens directly to another address, query their own balance or the total supply, and check or modify allowances for other addresses to spend their tokens. The contract emits `Transfer` events whenever tokens move between accounts (including minting and burning, which are internal operations not directly exposed to external callers), and `Approval` events when allowances are set or changed. All operations include checks to prevent transfers to or from the zero address, and to ensure sufficient balances or allowances exist for requested operations.
