This contract serves as a deployment helper for configuring risk parameters of multiple assets within a lending pool. It maintains no internal state beyond its owner, which is established at deployment. The primary operation is `configureReserves`, which allows the contract's owner to batch-configure various risk parameters for a list of assets on a specified `PoolConfigurator` contract. These parameters include collateral settings like loan-to-value, liquidation thresholds, and bonuses, as well as borrowing and flash loan capabilities, supply caps, and reserve factors. Only the contract's owner can invoke this function, ensuring controlled setup of the lending pool's assets. No events are emitted by this contract, as its purpose is a one-time setup.
