This contract serves as a utility for reading and calculating various market-related data points. It does not maintain any persistent state on-chain itself, but rather provides functions to compute and return information based on external market data. Callers can query for detailed market information, including properties of a specific market, borrowing factors for long and short positions, base funding values, and the next projected funding amounts. It also calculates and returns virtual inventory details, which include virtual pool amounts for long and short tokens, and virtual inventory for positions. The contract also indicates whether a market is currently disabled. All operations are read-only and do not alter any on-chain state; therefore, there are no access control mechanisms, events, or fees associated with its use.
