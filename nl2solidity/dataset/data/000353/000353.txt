This contract provides a suite of pure mathematical functions for calculating price and liquidity changes within a decentralized exchange context, specifically designed for concentrated liquidity pools. It does not maintain any on-chain state itself, acting purely as a stateless library. Callers can use its functions to determine the next square root price given an amount of token0 or token1 input or output, with precise rounding mechanisms for each scenario. Additionally, it offers functions to calculate the amount of token0 or token1 required to cover a given liquidity position between two specified square root prices, handling both positive and negative liquidity changes. There are no access controls or permissions, as all functions are `pure` and operate solely on their input parameters. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or direct integrations with other protocols; it serves as a foundational mathematical utility.
