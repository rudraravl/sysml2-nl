This contract serves as a security and calculation helper for a lending protocol, providing various read-only functions to assess the health and state of individual lending positions identified by an NFT ID. It does not maintain its own state but interacts with external `WISE_LENDING`, `WISE_ORACLE`, and `POSITION_NFTS` contracts to retrieve necessary data. Callers can query the total collateral for a position, both weighted by collateral factors and unweighted, and similarly retrieve total borrowed amounts, with options for updated values considering interest accrual over an interval. It also offers functions to check if a position can be liquidated, calculate liquidation fees and percentages, and verify if a withdrawal would lead to bad debt. Access control is primarily handled by the `POSITION_NFTS` contract, as some functions require the caller to be the owner of a specific NFT position. Important mechanics include calculations involving `PRECISION_FACTOR_E18` for fixed-point arithmetic and checks against `BORROW_PERCENTAGE_CAP` and `BAD_DEBT_THRESHOLD` to determine position health and liquidation eligibility.
