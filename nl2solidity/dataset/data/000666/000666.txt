This contract manages the distribution and claiming of rewards to users based on their participation in various assets. It maintains a record of which addresses are authorized to claim rewards on behalf of other users, the specific transfer strategy for each reward token, and a price oracle for each reward to facilitate UI display. An emission manager has exclusive control over configuring reward assets, setting transfer strategies, and updating reward oracles. Users can claim their accrued rewards directly, or an authorized claimer can claim on their behalf, with the option to claim a specific amount of a single reward or all available rewards across multiple assets. All reward claims are transferred via a designated transfer strategy, and important actions like setting claimers, installing transfer strategies, and updating reward oracles emit events for off-chain monitoring.
