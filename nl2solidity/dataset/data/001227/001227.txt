This contract serves as a foundational component for other contracts that intend to receive Blast points. Upon deployment, it initializes itself by locating the official Blast Points contract and the Blast Points Operator contract using a provided address finder. It then configures the identified Blast Points contract to recognize the Blast Points Operator as its authorized operator. This setup ensures that subsequent contracts inheriting from this base can interact with the Blast points system through the designated operator, streamlining the process of earning and managing Blast points. The contract itself does not maintain any state beyond its initial configuration, nor does it expose any functions for callers to interact with after deployment. Its sole purpose is to establish this initial connection and authorization within the Blast points ecosystem.
