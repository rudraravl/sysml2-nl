This contract is a utility library designed to assist a Panoptic Pool in managing external interactions, particularly token approvals and collateral tracking. It does not maintain any state itself but provides functions for other contracts to call. Callers can instruct the contract to approve a SemiFungiblePositionManager and two CollateralTrackers to spend an unlimited amount of two specified ERC20 tokens. This approval mechanism differs based on whether a Uniswap V4 PoolManager is used, either by directly approving ERC20 tokens or by setting operators on the PoolManager. Additionally, the contract offers functions to dynamically generate descriptive names and symbols for collateral tokens based on their underlying ERC20 tokens, Uniswap pool fees, and a given prefix, and to retrieve the decimals of an ERC20 token. It also includes a function to settle amounts during a liquidation process, which involves adjusting and accumulating premiums for specific token positions and burning collateral tokens via the CollateralTrackers. This settlement process emits events to track premium settlements.
