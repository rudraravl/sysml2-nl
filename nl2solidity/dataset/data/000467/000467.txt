This contract provides a utility for scoring Ethereum addresses based on a "vanity" metric and comparing them. It does not maintain any on-chain state or store any information. Callers can query the contract to obtain a vanity score for any given address, or to compare two addresses and determine which one has a higher vanity score. The scoring mechanism awards points for leading zero nibbles, specific patterns of the nibble '4' at the beginning and end of the address, and individual occurrences of the nibble '4'. There are no access controls, fees, or events; all functions are pure and can be called by anyone without affecting the blockchain state. The contract is designed solely for off-chain analysis of address aesthetics.
