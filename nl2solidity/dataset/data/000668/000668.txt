This contract defines data structures for managing a rewards distribution system. It tracks configurations for various reward programs, including the emission rate, total supply, distribution end time, and associated assets and reward tokens. It also stores references to a transfer strategy and a price oracle for the reward token. The contract maintains user-specific data, such as their accrued rewards and a liquidity index, along with overall reward data including the global index, emission rate, and last update timestamp. For each asset, it keeps a record of all associated reward tokens, their individual reward data, and the asset's decimal precision. This comprehensive data model allows for precise tracking and calculation of rewards within a multi-asset, multi-reward ecosystem.
