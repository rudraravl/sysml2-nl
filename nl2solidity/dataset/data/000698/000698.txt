This contract serves as a deployment mechanism for a specific set of Aave V3 protocol libraries. Upon deployment, it immediately creates and stores the addresses of four distinct logic contracts: FlashLoanLogic, LiquidationLogic, PoolLogic, and SupplyLogic. These addresses are permanently recorded within the contract's state, accessible through a structured report. The deployment of these logic contracts utilizes a deterministic creation process, ensuring their addresses are predictable based on a fixed salt and their bytecode. There are no external functions for callers to interact with, nor any access control mechanisms, as its sole purpose is to deploy and record these library addresses at construction time. No events are emitted, and no fees are involved. The contract essentially acts as a registry for these specific Aave V3 library instances, making their addresses available for other components of the Aave V3 protocol to reference.
