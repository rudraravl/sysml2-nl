This contract provides a peer-to-peer marketplace for trading Loan NFTs. It maintains a record of active sale offers, each identified by a unique ID, detailing the seller, a specific buyer, the price in a designated ERC20 token, a deadline, and the list of Loan NFT IDs included in the offer. The contract is initialized with references to the Loan NFT contract and the main Loans contract, which defines the currency for transactions. Sellers can create offers, locking their Loan NFTs to the exchange and specifying a buyer, price, and deadline. Only the designated buyer can accept an offer, which atomically transfers the Loan NFTs to them and the specified ERC20 payment to the seller. Both buyer and seller must be registered as Investors in the associated Loans contract's authentication system for the transaction to proceed. Sellers can cancel their own offers, unlocking the associated Loan NFTs. A designated guardian role can also force-cancel any offer, serving as a recovery mechanism. All significant actions, such as offer creation, acceptance, and cancellation, emit events for off-chain monitoring. The contract includes reentrancy protection and allows an administrator or guardian to set a maximum number of loans per offer.
