This contract extends an ERC-20 token to incorporate a voting and delegation system. It tracks the voting power of each account and the total supply over time using a series of checkpoints, which are snapshots taken at specific block numbers. Users can delegate their voting power to another address, either directly by calling a function or by providing a signed message. The contract maintains a record of who each account has delegated to. Callers can query the current voting power of an account or its voting power at a past block number, as well as the total token supply at a past block. When tokens are transferred, minted, or burned, the voting power of the relevant accounts and the total supply are adjusted and new checkpoints are recorded. Events are emitted when a delegation changes or when an account's voting power is updated, allowing off-chain systems to monitor these changes.
