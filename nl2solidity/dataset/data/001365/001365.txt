This contract implements an ERC-20 compliant token called "EdexStore" with the symbol "EDX" and 8 decimal places, designed for a token distribution event. It maintains balances for all token holders and tracks allowances for token transfers on behalf of others. The contract has a designated owner who initially receives a large portion of the total supply. The owner can transfer ownership, finalize the token distribution, distribute tokens to individual participants or multiple participants in batches, update the token-to-Ether exchange rate, and withdraw any Ether or foreign tokens held by the contract. Users can acquire tokens by sending Ether to the contract, with potential bonuses based on the amount of Ether sent and the current time period relative to predefined deadlines. There's also a limited airdrop mechanism where users can claim a small amount of tokens if they haven't claimed before and the airdrop target hasn't been reached. Token holders can transfer their tokens, and approved third parties can transfer tokens on behalf of a holder. The owner can also burn or add to the total supply of tokens. Various events are emitted to signal important actions like token transfers, approvals, distribution events, and updates to the token exchange rate.
