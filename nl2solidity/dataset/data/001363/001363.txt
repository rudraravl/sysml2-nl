This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token with a fixed total supply and a reflection mechanism. It tracks token balances for each address, along with allowances for spending by other addresses. The contract owner has special privileges, including setting trading status, modifying transaction and wallet size limits, adjusting buy and sell fees, and excluding addresses from fees. Fees are applied to transfers, with separate rates for buys and sells, and a portion of these fees is reflected back to token holders, while another portion is sent to designated development and marketing addresses. The contract integrates with Uniswap V2 to facilitate automatic token swaps for ETH when a certain token balance threshold is met, and the collected ETH is then distributed to the development and marketing addresses. Key events are emitted for token transfers, approvals, and ownership changes.
