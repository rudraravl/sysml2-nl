This contract implements a multi-signature wallet designed to manage funds and administrative changes with a robust, multi-group approval system. It maintains a record of pending and executed transactions, as well as a distinct set of pending and executed owner-related transactions. The contract categorizes owners into three groups: Dev, A, and B, each with their own list of addresses. A `requiredFromEachGroup` threshold, set during deployment, dictates the minimum number of confirmations needed from each owner group for any transaction to be executed. Callers can submit new transactions, which are then confirmed by owners. Once the required number of confirmations from each group is met, the transaction can be executed, transferring the specified value to a destination address. Similarly, owners can propose adding new owners to any of the three groups, which also requires multi-group confirmation before the new owner is officially added. The contract emits events for submissions, confirmations, revocations, and executions of both regular and owner-related transactions, as well as for deposits and owner additions, allowing off-chain systems to track its state. It also includes re-entrancy protection to safeguard against malicious re-entrant calls during fund transfers.
