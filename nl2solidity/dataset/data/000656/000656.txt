This contract serves as a dedicated storage component for a lending pool, maintaining all critical data related to reserves, user configurations, and protocol parameters. It records detailed information for each reserve, including its specific data, and tracks individual user configurations, such as their borrowing and collateral settings. The contract also maintains a list of all active reserves and defines various eMode categories, assigning a specific eMode category to each user. Key protocol-wide parameters are stored, such as the flash loan premium. Additionally, it manages permissions for designated position managers to enable collateral or switch eModes on behalf of users.
