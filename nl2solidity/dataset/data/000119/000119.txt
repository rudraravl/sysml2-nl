This contract provides a foundational utility for other contracts, specifically designed to abstract away the direct use of `msg.sender` and `msg.data`. Its primary purpose is to offer a more robust way to identify the true originator of a transaction, especially in scenarios involving meta-transactions where the account paying for gas might differ from the logical sender. It maintains no persistent state itself. The contract exposes two internal, view functions: `_msgSender()` to retrieve the sender's address and `_msgData()` to retrieve the transaction's calldata. There are no external functions for callers to interact with directly, and thus no access control or events. It also includes an internal `_contextSuffixLength()` function, which currently returns zero, suggesting a potential future extension for handling additional context information. This contract is intended to be inherited by other contracts, providing them with a standardized and potentially meta-transaction-aware way to access sender and data information.
