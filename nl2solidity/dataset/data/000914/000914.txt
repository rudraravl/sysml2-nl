This contract implements a tokenized vault conforming to the ERC-4626 standard, allowing users to deposit an underlying ERC-20 asset and receive "shares" in return, which are themselves ERC-20 tokens. The contract maintains the balance of the underlying asset it holds and the total supply of its own share tokens. Callers can deposit assets to mint shares, or mint a specific amount of shares by providing the corresponding assets. Conversely, users can redeem their shares to withdraw the underlying assets, or withdraw a specific amount of assets by burning the corresponding shares. The contract provides functions to preview the asset-to-share conversion rates for all operations, and it handles asset transfers securely. The contract emits `Deposit` and `Withdraw` events for all asset and share movements, allowing off-chain systems to track vault activity. The contract's share token decimals are derived from the underlying asset's decimals, with an optional offset. It incorporates a mechanism to mitigate inflation attacks by using virtual assets and shares, making such attacks economically unfeasible.
