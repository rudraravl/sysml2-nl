This contract establishes a fixed-rate interest model for calculating accrued interest on borrowed assets. Upon deployment, it permanently stores a single, immutable interest rate, provided during creation and scaled to 18 decimal places. It also maintains a constant representing the number of seconds in a year for calculation purposes. Callers can query the contract to determine the total interest accrued since a specific timestamp, given the total amount borrowed. This calculation always rounds up in favor of the protocol. Additionally, callers can retrieve the current fixed interest rate at any time. There are no access control mechanisms; all functions are publicly accessible for reading the rate and calculating interest. No events are emitted.
