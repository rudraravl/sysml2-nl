This contract implements an ERC721 non-fungible token with added functionality for signature-based approvals, commonly known as "permit" functionality. It maintains the standard ERC721 state of token ownership and approvals, alongside a record of used nonces for each token owner to prevent signature replay attacks. Callers can transfer tokens, approve a specific address to manage a single token, or approve an address to manage all their tokens. Additionally, owners can authorize an address to manage a specific token or all their tokens by providing a signed message, which is verified against the owner's address and a deadline. If the deadline has passed, the operation will fail. All approval changes, whether through direct calls or signed permits, emit standard ERC721 Approval or ApprovalForAll events. The contract ensures that only the token owner or an authorized operator can approve other addresses.
