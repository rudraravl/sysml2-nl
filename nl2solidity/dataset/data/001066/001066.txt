This contract manages the distribution of rewards to users who stake tokens in various vaults. It maintains a list of registered tokens, each with an associated vault that tracks its total supply, allocation points, and accumulated rewards per share. The contract also stores user-specific information for each token, including their staked amount, reward debt, and last claim time. Reward emissions can be set at a fixed rate or follow a predefined schedule. The contract has an owner who can start emissions, add new reward pools, update allocation points, and set the reward rate. It integrates with an `IEligibilityDataProvider` to determine user eligibility for rewards, with different modes for checking eligibility. Users can claim their pending rewards, which are then vested into a `MultiFeeDistribution` contract. The contract also includes mechanisms for recovering accidentally sent ERC20 tokens and for exempting certain addresses from eligibility checks. Events are emitted for significant actions like reward rate updates, balance changes, and eligibility status changes.
