This contract provides a robust, configurable oracle for Layer 2 environments, designed to deliver exchange rates while accounting for sequencer liveness. It maintains internal configurations for up to four distinct data sources, which can include other Fluid Oracles, Redstone, or Chainlink, along with the address of a sequencer uptime feed. Callers can query the current exchange rate for general operations or for liquidation purposes. Before returning any exchange rate, the contract verifies that the Layer 2 sequencer is operational and its data is valid. This ensures that the provided rates are reliable even in the presence of sequencer outages. The contract does not implement any access control beyond standard view function restrictions, and it does not emit custom events or charge fees.
