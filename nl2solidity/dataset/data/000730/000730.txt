This contract provides a utility library for common mathematical operations. It does not maintain any on-chain state, nor does it have any callable functions or operations for external accounts. It implements two pure functions: one to determine the minimum of two unsigned integers, and another to calculate the integer square root of an unsigned integer using the Babylonian method. There are no access controls, events, fees, or upgradeability mechanisms, as it is purely a collection of stateless mathematical helpers intended for internal use by other contracts.
