This contract facilitates a claim mechanism for CELDA tokens, allowing users to mint a specific amount of tokens based on a signed message. It maintains state regarding whether the claim process is active, the maximum total supply of CELDA, the maximum amount a single user can claim, and the address of the authorized signer. It also tracks which nonces have been used by each user to prevent replay attacks, and the total amount of CELDA claimed by each address. Callers can claim CELDA by providing a signature, the desired amount, a unique nonce, additional data, and an expiration timestamp. The contract verifies the signature against a predefined signer address, ensures the claim is active, within limits, not expired, and that the nonce hasn't been used before. Upon successful verification, the specified amount of CELDA is minted to the caller, and a `ClaimedCelda` event is emitted. The contract owner has exclusive control over activating/deactivating the claim, setting the signer address, adjusting the maximum claim amount, and withdrawing any accidental ETH or other ERC20 token transfers sent to the contract.
