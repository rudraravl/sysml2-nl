This contract implements a time-locked, role-based access control system, designed to manage privileged operations with a built-in delay. It maintains a minimum delay period for operations, a list of addresses authorized to propose operations (proposers), and a list of addresses authorized to execute operations (executors). An administrator address is also tracked, which has ultimate control over roles. Callers can schedule operations to be executed after the minimum delay, cancel scheduled operations, and execute operations once the delay has passed. Access to these functions is strictly controlled by the assigned roles. The contract emits events for role grants, revocations, and when operations are scheduled, cancelled, or executed, allowing off-chain systems to monitor its state. It also supports the ERC-1155 receiver interface, enabling it to receive ERC-1155 tokens.
