This contract extends a core MagicLP functionality to integrate with Blast-specific yield mechanisms. It maintains a `feeTo` address, which receives claimed yields, and a mapping of `operators` who can perform certain actions. A `BlastTokenRegistry` is immutably set at construction to determine which tokens are eligible for native yield. The contract allows authorized callers to claim gas and token yields, and to update token claimability settings. An owner can also set the `feeTo` address and manage `operators`. Only the contract's owner or designated operators can initiate yield claims, and only the owner can configure the `feeTo` address or operator status. Important events are emitted when the `feeTo` address or operator status changes, or when yields are claimed, providing transparency for off-chain monitoring.
