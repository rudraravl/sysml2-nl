This contract dynamically adjusts the borrow rate for a specific borrow token within a liquidity protocol, based on the supply rate of a designated supply token. It maintains immutable references to the liquidity and reserve contracts, the supply and borrow tokens, and configurable rate buffers for different rate models (Kink1 and Kink2), along with a minimum percentage difference to trigger updates. Only authorized rebalancers, as determined by the reserve contract, can initiate a rebalance operation. The `rebalance` function calculates new borrow rates by adding the configured buffer to the current supply token lending rate, then updates the liquidity contract if the change exceeds the minimum update difference. A `LogUpdateRate` event is emitted upon successful rate adjustments, detailing the old and new rates for transparency. Callers can also query the current calculated borrow rates and the percentage difference from the current on-chain configuration.
