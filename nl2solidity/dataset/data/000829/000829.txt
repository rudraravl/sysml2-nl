This contract extends a standard multi-token interface, enabling the destruction of tokens. It maintains the balances of various token IDs for different accounts. Callers can initiate the destruction of a specific quantity of a particular token ID from a designated account, or destroy multiple token IDs and quantities simultaneously from an account. Crucially, a caller can only burn tokens if they are the owner of the tokens in the specified account, or if they have been explicitly approved to manage all tokens for that account. The contract does not introduce any new state variables beyond what the underlying multi-token standard maintains. It emits events inherited from the base standard when tokens are burned, allowing off-chain services to track token supply changes. There are no fees, upgradeability mechanisms, or integrations with other protocols defined within this specific extension.
