This contract implements a deflationary ERC-20 token named "PolyMind" with a total supply of 100,000,000 tokens. It maintains balances for all token holders, allowances for spending, and tracks addresses excluded from transaction fees and those blacklisted. The contract charges a tax fee, a liquidity fee, and a burn fee on most transfers. The owner, initially the deployer, can manage these fees, blacklist addresses, and transfer ownership. The contract also defines a maximum transaction amount. All token transfers, including those involving excluded or blacklisted addresses, are subject to these fees, which are then reflected, sent to a burn address, or used for liquidity. Key events are emitted for ownership transfers and token movements.
