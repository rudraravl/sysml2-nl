This contract serves as a receiver for ERC-1155 and ERC-721 tokens, primarily for use with Safe contracts. It maintains no internal state. Its main operations are responding to `onERC1155Received`, `onERC1155BatchReceived`, and `onERC721Received` calls, which are standard callbacks from ERC-1155 and ERC-721 token contracts when tokens are transferred. All these functions simply return a predefined magic value, indicating successful receipt of the tokens, without performing any other actions or storing any information. Access to these functions is restricted by an `onlyFallback` modifier, implying that they are intended to be called only as part of a fallback mechanism or by a specific, authorized caller, though the exact nature of `onlyFallback` is external to this contract. No events are emitted, and there are no fees, upgradeability, or direct integrations beyond the token callback interfaces.
