This contract implements a timelock mechanism for executing operations, ensuring a minimum delay before actions can be performed. It maintains a minimum delay period and the state of various proposed operations, which can be "Unset", "Waiting", "Ready", or "Done". Callers can propose operations with a specified delay, which then enter a waiting period. Once the delay has passed, the operation becomes ready for execution. Operations can also be canceled before execution. The contract uses a role-based access control system: an ADMIN_ROLE can manage other roles, a PROPOSER_ROLE can propose operations, an EXECUTOR_ROLE can execute them, and a CANCELLER_ROLE can cancel them. Important events are emitted for proposed, executed, and canceled operations, as well as when the minimum delay is updated. A notable mechanic is the ability to specify a predecessor operation that must be executed before the current one. The contract also allows for the timelock itself to set the minimum delay, and guards against re-entrancy during execution.
