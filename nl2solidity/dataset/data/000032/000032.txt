This contract is a specialized vesting wallet designed to distribute tokens or Ether to a beneficiary over time, incorporating a cliff period. It maintains the beneficiary's address, the start time of the vesting, the total duration, and a specific cliff timestamp. The primary operation is to calculate the amount of vested funds available at any given time. Crucially, no funds can be released to the beneficiary until the cliff timestamp has passed. Before the cliff, the contract reports zero vested funds, regardless of the overall vesting schedule. After the cliff, it behaves like a standard vesting wallet, releasing funds proportionally over the remaining duration. The contract is initialized with the cliff duration, and it will revert if this duration exceeds the total vesting period. There are no explicit access control mechanisms beyond the inherent vesting schedule, and no events are emitted by this specific extension.
