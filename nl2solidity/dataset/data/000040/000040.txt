This contract extends a standard Governor to prevent last-minute quorum manipulation by extending proposal deadlines. It maintains a configurable `_voteExtension` duration and records `_extendedDeadlines` for individual proposals. When a proposal reaches quorum, if its current deadline is earlier than the current time plus the `_voteExtension`, the contract automatically extends the deadline to ensure sufficient time for other voters to react. This extension is a one-time event per proposal. Only the governance executor can modify the `_voteExtension` parameter, which is bounded by a maximum value. Events are emitted when a proposal's deadline is extended and when the `_voteExtension` parameter is changed, allowing off-chain systems to track these critical governance lifecycle updates.
