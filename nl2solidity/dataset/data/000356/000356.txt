This contract provides a utility for efficiently tracking the initialized state of discrete "ticks" within a larger system, where ticks are spaced at regular intervals. It maintains a mapping of integer positions to 256-bit words, where each bit within a word represents the initialized status of a specific tick. Callers can flip the initialized state of a given tick, ensuring the tick adheres to the predefined spacing. Additionally, the contract allows callers to search for the next initialized tick within a limited range (up to 256 ticks away) from a starting tick, either to the left (less than or equal to) or right (greater than) of the starting point. This search returns both the next tick's value and a boolean indicating if it is indeed initialized. There are no access control mechanisms, fees, or events; its purpose is purely to manage and query this packed tick state.
