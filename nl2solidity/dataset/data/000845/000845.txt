This contract acts as a simple escrow, holding Ether deposited by its owner on behalf of various payees until they are ready to be withdrawn. It maintains a record of the Ether balance for each unique payee address. The contract owner is the only entity permitted to deposit Ether into the escrow for a specific payee, or to initiate a withdrawal of a payee's accumulated balance. When Ether is deposited, a "Deposited" event is emitted, indicating the payee and the amount. Similarly, when Ether is withdrawn, a "Withdrawn" event is emitted, detailing the payee and the amount transferred. The contract does not charge any fees and directly transfers the full amount to the payee upon withdrawal.
