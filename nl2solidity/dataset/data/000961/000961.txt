This contract acts as a router for interacting with the Moonwell protocol using native Ether. It maintains references to a WETH9 contract and a Moonwell mToken contract, established at deployment. Callers can deposit Ether into Moonwell by sending Ether to the contract, which then wraps it into WETH and mints the corresponding mToken, transferring the newly minted mToken to a specified recipient. Conversely, callers can redeem mTokens for Ether by transferring mTokens to the contract, which then redeems them from Moonwell, unwraps the WETH back to Ether, and sends the Ether to a specified recipient. The contract pre-approves the mToken contract to spend an unlimited amount of WETH upon deployment. There are no access control mechanisms beyond standard Solidity `msg.sender` for transfers, and no events are emitted.
