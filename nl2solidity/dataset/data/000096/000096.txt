This contract implements a Non-Fungible Token (NFT) standard, allowing for the creation and management of unique digital assets on the blockchain. It maintains a record of each token's owner, the total number of tokens held by each address, and approvals for individual tokens or for all tokens owned by a specific address. Callers can query the token's name and symbol, retrieve the owner of a specific token, check an address's token balance, and get the URI for a token's metadata. Owners can approve other addresses to manage their tokens, either for a single token or for all their tokens, and transfer tokens between addresses. Both standard and "safe" transfers are supported, with the latter including a check to ensure receiving contracts can handle NFTs. Important events are emitted for token transfers, individual token approvals, and approvals for all tokens, enabling off-chain applications to track token activity. The contract handles basic error conditions such as invalid owners, non-existent tokens, and insufficient approvals.
