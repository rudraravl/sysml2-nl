This contract extends a standard governance mechanism by integrating a TimelockController, ensuring a mandatory delay for all approved proposals before execution. It maintains a reference to the TimelockController and maps proposal IDs to their corresponding timelock operation identifiers. Callers can query the state of a proposal, which now reflects the status within the TimelockController (queued, executed, or canceled). Proposals are queued to the TimelockController, which schedules their execution after a minimum delay. Once the delay passes, the contract can trigger the execution of the proposal via the TimelockController. Proposals can also be canceled, which attempts to cancel the corresponding operation in the TimelockController. The contract's TimelockController can be updated, but only through a governance proposal, ensuring controlled changes. A `TimelockChange` event is emitted when the TimelockController address is modified.
