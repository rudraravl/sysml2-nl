This contract implements a decentralized exchange liquidity pool, acting as an automated market maker for a pair of base and quote tokens. It maintains reserves of these two tokens, along with internal pricing parameters like 'I' and 'K', a liquidity provider fee rate, and a reference to a fee rate model. Callers can swap tokens by selling either the base or quote token, receiving the other in return, with fees applied and a `Swap` event emitted. Users can provide liquidity by buying shares, depositing both base and quote tokens to receive LP tokens, or remove liquidity by selling shares, burning LP tokens to withdraw base and quote tokens, both emitting corresponding events. The contract also supports flash loans, allowing users to borrow tokens and repay them within the same transaction, emitting a `FlashLoan` event. The contract owner has administrative control to rescue accidentally sent tokens (excluding the base and quote tokens), update pricing parameters (`I`, `K`, `lpFeeRate`), and adjust reserves, emitting a `ParametersChanged` event. It includes reentrancy protection and tracks cumulative prices for TWAP calculations.
