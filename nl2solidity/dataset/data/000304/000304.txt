This contract provides a utility for checking delegation rights on the ZKsync network, specifically interacting with the canonical Delegate Registry V2. It does not maintain any state itself, but rather queries an external registry to determine if one address (`to`, the delegate or "hot wallet") has been granted specific rights by another address (`from`, the grantor or "cold vault"). Callers can query for general delegation rights, delegation rights for a specific contract, or delegation rights for specific ERC-721, ERC-20, or ERC-1155 tokens, optionally specifying additional `rights` data. All functions are view-only, meaning they do not alter the blockchain state. The contract allows for an override of the default Delegate Registry V2 address through a specific storage slot, enabling flexible testing or deployment scenarios. There are no access control mechanisms, fees, or events emitted by this contract, as its sole purpose is to provide read-only access to delegation information.
