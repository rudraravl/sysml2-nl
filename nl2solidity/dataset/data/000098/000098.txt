This contract is an extension of an ERC-721 non-fungible token, specifically designed to facilitate the efficient minting of large batches of tokens. It maintains a record of token ownership for consecutively minted tokens and tracks which of these tokens have been burned. The primary operation is the ability to mint a consecutive range of tokens to a specified recipient, but this batch minting is strictly restricted to the contract's construction phase. During construction, individual token minting is disallowed, but it becomes available after construction. A maximum batch size of 5000 tokens is enforced to ensure compatibility with off-chain indexing services. The contract emits a `ConsecutiveTransfer` event when a batch of tokens is minted. It also handles the burning of consecutively minted tokens by marking them as burned. Access control is implicit: only the contract's constructor can perform batch mints, and individual mints are only allowed after construction.
