This contract is a utility library providing various mathematical operations for unsigned 256-bit integers. It offers functions for basic arithmetic like addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, including "try" variants that return a success flag to indicate overflow or division by zero, and "saturating" variants that cap results at maximum or minimum values instead of overflowing. The contract also implements more complex operations such as 512-bit addition and multiplication, ceiling division, and a precise `mulDiv` function for calculating `(x * y) / denominator` with full precision and various rounding options. Additionally, it includes functions for modular exponentiation (`modExp`), modular inverse (`invMod`), and square root (`sqrt`). The contract does not maintain any state; all its functions are pure or view, operating solely on input parameters. Access control is not applicable as it's a library of pure functions. It integrates with a precompiled contract for modular exponentiation and uses assembly for performance-critical sections.
