This contract creates a fungible token with a fixed supply, similar to standard ERC20 tokens, but with a unique fee mechanism. Upon deployment, it establishes the total token supply and a fixed fee amount that will be applied to certain transfers. The contract maintains the balance of tokens for each address and tracks allowances granted by token holders to other addresses for spending their tokens. The primary operation is `transferFrom`, which allows an approved third party or the token holder themselves to move tokens from a source address to a destination address. When `transferFrom` is executed, the specified fee is deducted from the transferred amount and sent to the zero address, effectively burning it from circulation, while the remaining tokens reach the destination. Two `Transfer` events are emitted for each `transferFrom` call: one for the net amount transferred to the destination and another for the fee sent to the zero address, allowing off-chain services to track both the token movement and the fee burning.
